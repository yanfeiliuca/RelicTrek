Commission Armor & Jawblade — Two Powerful Pieces From a Ship Most Hunters Never Board

1. Why You Need This

Somewhere in the Windward Plains, a ship appears. It doesn't announce itself. It doesn't show up on your quest board. Most hunters never see it. That ship — the Support Ship — carries a merchant named Santiago, and Santiago sells Commission Tickets. Those tickets unlock two things the game's smithy otherwise never shows you: the Commission Armor set, with Agitator Lv5 pre-loaded, and the Jawblade, a Great Sword with Protective Polish and Focus built in. Agitator Lv5 grants +20 attack and +15% affinity when the monster is enraged — which in High Rank, is almost always.

3. Optimal Route

This route guides you to finding the Support Ship and acquiring Commission Tickets for both the armor set and Jawblade Great Sword. Total estimated time: ~2–4 hours depending on ship RNG.

Step 1 — Unlock the Support Ship

1The Support Ship becomes available in High Rank. It appears anchored at a fixed location in Windward Plains — northeastern coast, near the large rock arch. Check there first when entering the area.

Step 2 — Trigger Support Ship Appearances More Often

2Go to any village and select "Material Retrieval." When choosing the category, pick "Misc" or "Equipment." This increases the chance of the Support Ship appearing on your next expedition to Windward Plains.

Step 3 — Find Santiago and Buy Commission Tickets

3Board the ship and speak to Santiago (the merchant near the stern). Commission Tickets appear under the "Equipment Materials" category. Buy as many as you need — you'll need approximately 6–8 for the full armor set, plus 2 more for the Jawblade.
4💎 Bonus stop: Santiago also sells rare endemic life bait that isn't available anywhere else in the game. Stock up while you're here.

Step 4 — Craft Commission Armor and Jawblade

5Both appear at Gemma's smithy immediately after you acquire your first Commission Ticket.

4. Danger Zones & Bonus Loot

⚠ Danger Zones

⚠ Risk 1 — Support Ship Is NOT Guaranteed

The Support Ship is NOT guaranteed every expedition. Some hunters go 5–6 expeditions without seeing it. Be patient — increasing retrieval categories helps but doesn't guarantee it.

⚠ Risk 2 — Agitator Requires Monster Rage

Agitator Lv5 requires the monster to be enraged. Against calm or flinching monsters, the bonus is inactive.

💎 Bonus Loot Along the Route

💎 Bonus — Underwater Cave Near Ship Anchor Point

The ship's anchor point in Windward Plains has a nearby underwater cave (dive from the rock arch area) containing a chest with Wyvern Gem — a universal crafting material.

5. Skills & Set Summary

Commission Armor α — Full Set Skills

  • Agitator Lv5 — +20 attack and +15% affinity when the monster is enraged. Active for the majority of every High Rank hunt.
  • Divine Blessing Lv2 — Chance to reduce incoming damage by 30%. Adds survivability to an offensive-focused set.
  • Quick Sheathe Lv2 — Faster weapon sheathing. Pairs well with Great Sword hit-and-run tactics.
  • Maximum Might Lv1 — Affinity boost when stamina is full. Benefits the Jawblade's charged slashes.
  • Tool Specialist Lv1 — Reduces mantle cooldown. Useful for extending Rocksteady or Evasion Mantle uptime.
  • Earplugs Lv1 — Reduces the effect of weak monster roars.

Jawblade (Great Sword)

  • Protective Polish Lv2 — Prevents sharpness loss for a duration after sharpening. Ideal for maintaining white sharpness during extended combos.
  • Focus Lv2 — Increases charge speed for Great Sword charged slashes. Reduces True Charged Slash charge time significantly.

6. Build Recommendations

Commission Armor + Jawblade Synergy Build

The Commission Armor and Jawblade are designed to work together as a complete Great Sword package. Agitator Lv5 provides the raw attack and affinity, while the Jawblade's Focus Lv2 and Protective Polish Lv2 handle sharpness and charge time management.

  • Weapon: Jawblade (Protective Polish Lv2 + Focus Lv2 built-in)
  • Armor: Full Commission Armor α set (5 pieces, Agitator Lv5)
  • Charm: Challenger Charm II (pushes Agitator to Lv7 if desired)
  • Key Decorations: Critical Boost ×3, Weakness Exploit ×2, Quick Sheathe ×1 (stacks with set's built-in Quick Sheathe Lv2)

The Jawblade's built-in Focus Lv2 and Protective Polish Lv2 mean you don't need to slot those skills — freeing up decoration slots for pure damage. Combined with Agitator Lv5's near-permanent +20 attack and +15% affinity, this is one of the most efficient mid-to-late High Rank Great Sword setups available before transitioning to full Artian builds.
