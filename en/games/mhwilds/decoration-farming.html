1. Quick Answer

Decorations in Monster Hunter Wilds are jewels slotted into armor and weapon decoration slots (sizes Level 1, 2, and 3) to add skill points to your build. The fastest path to a complete endgame decoration setup has two distinct phases: first, craft all single-skill decorations directly at the smithy using monster materials — no RNG involved. Second, farm the Melding Pot using Gold Melding Tickets for dual-skill [3] jewels, which are RNG but pack two skills into one slot.

The most reliable source of Gold Melding Tickets is the Nightflower Pollen pipeline: gather Nightflower Pollen from Ephemeral Blossoms in Windward Plains during a full moon, then trade it to NPC Sekka in Suja for Gold Melding Tickets. Wait until HR 40 before committing serious farming time — Rarity 6 decorations, the tier that matters for endgame builds, do not appear in the pool before that rank.

2. Requirements & Unlock Progression

Melding Pot — Unlocks at Chapter 3-3 (Through the Dusk)

The Melding Pot is located in Suja, Peaks of Accord, managed by NPC Vio. It unlocks automatically during Chapter 3-3. At this stage you can meld Level 1 decorations only.

📍 Suja, Peaks of Accord 🔓 Auto-unlocks: Chapter 3-3
Glowing Orb Melding — Unlocks at Chapter 5-1 (Stone Cold Vortex)

After completing Chapter 5-1, you unlock the ability to meld Tier-1 Relics and use Glowing Orb items — granting access to Level 1 or Level 2 decorations from the Melding Pot.

📋 Requires: Chapter 5-1 completion
Level 2 Decoration Melding + Ancient Orbs — Unlocks at HR 100

At Hunter Rank 100, Decoration Slot 2 unlocks, and you gain access to Ancient Orbs for melding — enabling high-tier Rarity decoration melding at the Melding Pot.

⚠ Requires: HR 100 🎁 Unlocks: Ancient Orb melding
Start Serious Farming at HR 40
Rarity 6 decorations — the tier used in endgame builds — only begin appearing in the reward pool at HR 40+. Farming decorations before reaching this rank yields lower-tier jewels that will be replaced quickly.

3. Farming Routes

Route A — Craft Single-Skill Decorations at the Smithy (Zero RNG)

Single-skill decorations are craftable deterministically at the smithy. Each one requires specific monster materials — typically from the monster whose gear shares that skill. This is the fastest way to fill your first pass of damage skills without relying on RNG. Craft all the single-skill versions of your priority decorations (Weakness Exploit, Critical Boost, Attack Boost, Critical Eye) before spending any Melding Points.

Route B — Nightflower Pollen → Gold Melding Tickets (Most Reliable for RNG Decos)

Nightflower Pollen is gathered from Ephemeral Blossom plants in Windward Plains. Trading it to Sekka in Suja converts it into Gold Melding Tickets, which feed into the Melding Pot's Wyverian Melding for high-rarity jewels. This is the most reliable repeatable pipeline for dual-skill decoration farming.

Nightflower Pollen Locations — Windward Plains

Ephemeral Blossoms (the source plants) appear in Area 10 (eastern edge near exit to Riverside Trail) and Area 3 (northern area of the map). Both nodes must be harvested during a Full Moon. The plants appear during Fallow or Plenty weather conditions.

📍 Area 10 — near Riverside Trail exit 📍 Area 3 — northern area 🌕 Condition: Full Moon only 🌿 Weather: Fallow or Plenty
Fastest Pollen Route Setup
Place Pop-up Camps in both Sector 3 and Sector 10 of Windward Plains. Position the Sector 3 camp as close as possible to the Ephemeral Blossom node to minimise travel time. Fast travel between the two camps to collect both nodes in a single expedition run.

Route C — Investigation Quest Bonus Rewards

Investigations list their potential decoration rewards before you accept them. At HR 40+, High Rank Investigations begin awarding Rarity 6 decorations in their bonus reward slots. Check the Investigation board regularly and prioritise quests with Gold reward slots — these have the highest probability of yielding rare jewels as a secondary drop alongside the hunt's normal rewards.

4. Step-by-Step Progression

1. Reach Chapter 3-3 to unlock the Melding Pot at Suja. From this point you can meld Level 1 decorations — useful but not yet endgame-relevant.
2. Complete Chapter 5-1 to unlock Glowing Orb melding (Level 1 and 2 decoration access at the Melding Pot).
3. Push to HR 40 before farming decorations seriously. Below this rank, Rarity 6 decorations — the tier that matters — will not appear in the reward pool.
4. Go to the smithy and craft all single-skill priority decorations using monster materials. Start with Weakness Exploit (Tenderizer) 3, Critical Boost 3, Attack Boost 4. These cost materials, not RNG.
5. Monitor the weather in Windward Plains. When a Full Moon is active during Fallow or Plenty weather, launch an expedition and fast travel to your Pop-up Camp in Sector 3 or Sector 10 to harvest Ephemeral Blossoms.
6. Trade your Nightflower Pollen to Sekka in Suja, Peaks of Accord for Gold Melding Tickets.
7. Take your Gold Melding Tickets to Vio at the Melding Pot → Wyverian Melding. Use the tickets to roll for high-rarity dual-skill jewels. The pool is RNG, so repeat across multiple sessions to build your collection.
8. Continue pushing toward HR 100. At this rank, the Melding Pot unlocks Ancient Orbs and Decoration Slot 2, significantly expanding the high-tier decoration melding options available.

5. Warnings

Don't Farm Decorations Before HR 40
Rarity 6 jewels — the endgame tier — are gated behind HR 40. Time spent farming decorations before hitting this milestone produces lower-rarity jewels that will be replaced. Get to HR 40 first, then begin serious farming.
Don't Skip Smithy Crafting and Go Straight to Melding
Single-skill decorations are fully craftable with no RNG at the smithy. Spending Melding Points trying to roll a single-skill Weakness Exploit jewel from the Melding Pot is a waste of tickets — craft it directly. Reserve Melding Points for dual-skill [3] decorations that cannot be crafted deterministically.
Nightflower Pollen Is Time-Gated by Full Moon Cycles
Ephemeral Blossoms only appear under a Full Moon. You cannot force this condition — you need to wait for the in-game calendar to cycle. Set up your Pop-up Camps in advance so you can collect pollen immediately when the moon phase is right.

7. Common Mistakes

Grinding Melding Pot Before Crafting Single-Skill Decos

Many players spend Gold Melding Tickets trying to RNG their way into a Weakness Exploit or Critical Boost jewel — both of which are craftable at the smithy with zero RNG. Craft single-skill priority decorations first. Save every Gold Melding Ticket for dual-skill [3] jewels that have no craftable equivalent.

Spending Mystery Orb Melding Points on Low-Tier Rolls

Mystery Orbs cost 4 Melding Points each and only produce Level 1 decorations. Glowing Orbs cost 10 Melding Points but can produce Level 1 or 2 decorations. Once you unlock Glowing Orb melding after Chapter 5-1, stop spending on Mystery Orbs — the per-point return is far lower.

Missing Nightflower Pollen Harvests

Players who don't set up Pop-up Camps in Windward Plains Sector 3 and 10 in advance often miss full moon windows entirely — the harvest node requires travel time you don't have if you're camping elsewhere. Set the camps up once and leave them there permanently.

Ignoring Decoration Slot Type When Equipping

Armor Decorations (Defense, Elemental Resistances) and Weapon Decorations (Attack, weapon properties) are not interchangeable — they fit different slot types. Equipping a jewel in the wrong slot type is not possible, but it's easy to mistake which decoration type a slot accepts when building a loadout.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

When should I start farming decorations in Monster Hunter Wilds?
Wait until Hunter Rank 40 before committing serious time to decoration farming. Before HR 40, Rarity 6 decorations — the tier used in endgame builds — do not appear in the reward pool. Below that rank you will only collect lower-tier jewels that will be quickly replaced.
What is the fastest way to farm decorations?
The two-phase approach is fastest overall: first, craft all single-skill priority decorations at the smithy using monster materials (zero RNG). Second, farm the Nightflower Pollen pipeline in Windward Plains during full moon cycles to earn Gold Melding Tickets, then use those at the Melding Pot's Wyverian Melding for dual-skill [3] jewels.
Where do I find Nightflower Pollen?
Nightflower Pollen is harvested from Ephemeral Blossom plants in Windward Plains — specifically Area 10 (near the Riverside Trail exit) and Area 3 (northern area). The plants only appear during a Full Moon, during Fallow or Plenty weather. Set up Pop-up Camps in both sectors and fast travel between them when the moon is full.
Which decorations should I prioritize farming?
For endgame damage builds, the top priority is Weakness Exploit (Tenderizer) Lv 3, Critical Boost Lv 3, Attack Boost Lv 4, and Critical Eye Lv 4+. All single-skill versions are craftable at the smithy without RNG. After these are covered, focus Melding Points on dual-skill [3] jewels for slot efficiency.
What is the Melding Pot and when does it unlock?
The Melding Pot is a facility in Suja, Peaks of Accord managed by NPC Vio. It unlocks automatically during Chapter 3-3 (Through the Dusk), starting with Level 1 decoration melding. After Chapter 5-1, you gain access to Glowing Orbs for Level 1–2 decos. At HR 100, Ancient Orb melding and Decoration Slot 2 unlock.
Can I craft decorations without RNG?
Yes — single-skill decorations are fully craftable at the smithy using specific monster materials. The recipe is deterministic and produces the exact jewel you choose. Only dual-skill decorations (which hold two skills in one slot) require the Melding Pot and are subject to RNG.
