1. Why You Need This

There is a Great Sword in Monster Hunter Wilds that is a fish. Not fish-themed. Not fish-shaped. It is a frozen fish. Specifically a Speartuna, and its final upgrade — the Freezer Speartuna — is one of the most recognizable weapons in the entire game for anyone who's seen another hunter wield it mid-hunt. The stats aren't record-breaking, but the Punishing Draw Lv3 skill built into it makes it a legitimate pick for draw-attack Great Sword playstyles. Most importantly: you get it by fishing in a specific spot that most hunters never find. This is one of those things you see once and immediately need to know how to get.

2. Materials

You need only 1 Speartuna Fin for the base Frozen Speartuna. The upgrade chain uses standard ore materials available at any High Rank smithy.

3. Optimal Route

STEP 1 — Unlock fishing in High Rank

1Speak to Kanya at Scarlet Forest Base Camp after obtaining a Fishing Rod (available from the camp shop in Chapter 3+). Complete her introductory fishing quest to unlock advanced fishing spots.

STEP 2 — Navigate to Scarlet Forest Area 17

2Area 17 is in the northeastern section of Scarlet Forest, past the large amber tree cluster. It's off the main patrol route — most hunters only pass through during specific quests. Look for the fishing icon on your minimap once you're close.
💎 Bonus

Area 17 also has a rare bird endemic life spawn that doesn't appear anywhere else in Scarlet Forest. Catching it completes a specific field research entry.

STEP 3 — Fish for the Speartuna

3Equip your fishing rod at the fishing spot. The Speartuna is a large fish — it appears as a long silver shimmer in the water. Use the standard lure. It may take 3–5 attempts to hook one. When it bites, follow the QTE prompt carefully — it fights back harder than regular fish.

STEP 4 — Upgrade at the smithy

4After getting the Speartuna Fin, the Frozen Speartuna I appears at Gemma's smithy. Upgrade it to Frozen Speartuna II then Freezer Speartuna using standard High Rank ore materials.

4. Danger & Bonus

⚠ Danger — Nearby Monster Patrol

Area 17 has a Scarlet Forest Apex Monster patrol nearby. Don't get distracted while fishing.

💎 Bonus — Unique Idle Animation

The Freezer Speartuna has a unique idle animation where your hunter holds the frozen fish like a regular object. It's the best conversation starter in any multiplayer lobby.

5. Stats & Skills

Freezer Speartuna (Final Form)

  • Type: Great Sword
  • Attack: Competitive mid-tier (not the highest, but respectable)
  • Skills: Punishing Draw Lv3, Slugger Lv2
  • Sharpness: Blue (upgradeable to White with Handicraft)
  • Best playstyle: Draw attack Great Sword — every sheathe-and-slash cycle benefits from Punishing Draw
