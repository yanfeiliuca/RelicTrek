1. Why You Need This

Here's something most Monster Hunter Wilds players never discover: the single strongest armor set and weapon class in the game aren't unlocked by hunting the final boss. They're locked behind a side quest that's easy to miss, and materials that drop from a mechanic the game barely explains.

The Artian set — both armor and weapons — sits at the top of virtually every endgame tier list. The armor's signature skill, Slayer Lv5, boosts wound damage and is the backbone of late-game melee builds. The Artian weapon line offers the highest raw damage ceiling of any craftable weapon, with a reinforcement system that lets you customize bonus skills.

Most players hit High Rank, see "Artian" mentioned in build guides, try to find it at the smithy, and come up empty. That's because crafting it requires three specific steps the game never walks you through. Here's the complete path.

3. Optimal Route

This route is structured in three phases. Phase 1 unlocks the prerequisite side quest. Phase 2 farms the core materials. Phase 3 completes the crafting at the smithy. Total estimated time: 3–5 hours depending on drop luck.

Phase 1 — Unlock the Side Quest (~30 min)

1Reach HR 100+. The side quest that unlocks Artian crafting only appears after you hit Hunter Rank 100. Focus on tempered investigations to rank up efficiently.
2Travel to Suja, the Peaks of Accord and speak to Fabius (the elderly researcher near the camp's scroll table). He gives the quest "Echoes of the Forgotten Forge." This quest does NOT appear on the main quest board — you must speak to him directly.
3💎 Bonus stop: While in Suja, check the supply cache behind Fabius's tent. It contains Armor Sphere+ ×3 — useful for upgrading any armor set you're currently wearing.

Phase 2 — Farm Materials (~2–4 hours)

4Frenzy Shards: Hunt any Frenzied Monster in the Iceshard Cliffs. Frenzied Yian Kut-Ku and Frenzied Nerscylla are the easiest targets. Each hunt yields 2–4 Shards. You need ~15 Shards for the full armor set.
5Frenzy Crystals: Hunt Gore Magala in the Iceshard Cliffs. This is the ONLY source. Gore Magala is a Tier 7 threat — bring Nulberries (Frenzy status cure) and a weapon with high Elderseal. Each hunt yields 1–2 Crystals. You need ~8 Crystals.
6💎 Bonus stop: During Gore Magala hunts, gather the glowing resource nodes in the Iceshard Cliffs. They frequently contain Pure Dragon Blood and Elder Dragon Bone — both used in high-tier charm crafting.
7Ancient Weapon Fragments: Hunt Tier 3 Tempered Monsters (Tempered Arkveld, Tempered Gore Magala, or Tempered Apex monsters). These are the hardest hunts in the game. Each investigation reward box has a ~15% chance to contain a fragment. You need 3 Fragments per weapon.
8Hunter Symbol III: Hunt Tempered Apex monsters (Uth Duna, Rey Dau, Nu Udra, Jin Dahaad) at Threat Level 7+. These drop from the quest reward box, not carves. Prioritize investigations with gold reward slots.

Phase 3 — Craft at the Smithy (~15 min)

9Return to any base camp smithy. If you've completed "Echoes of the Forgotten Forge," the Artian Forge tab will now appear in the crafting menu.
10Craft each armor piece individually (5 pieces). Then select your Artian weapon type and use the Reinforce option to apply Ancient Weapon Fragments — each fragment adds a random bonus skill. Re-roll until you get your desired skills.

4. Danger Zones & Bonus Loot

⚠ Danger Zones

🔴 Risk 1 — Gore Magala Frenzy Buildup

Gore Magala's Frenzy attacks build up a debilitating status. If the bar fills, you take increased damage and lose natural recovery. Counter: bring 5+ Nulberries. Aggressively attack during Frenzy mode — landing hits reduces the buildup meter. Equip the Antivirus skill (Lv3) if available.

⚠ Risk 2 — Tempered Monster One-Shots

Tier 3 Tempered Monsters can one-shot hunters below 400 defense. Before attempting these hunts, ensure your current armor is fully upgraded with Armor Spheres and you have Health Boost Lv3 active. Bring Max Potions (craft during hunt with Mandragora + Catalyst).

⚠ Risk 3 — Wasted Ancient Weapon Fragments

The Artian weapon reinforcement system consumes 3 Ancient Weapon Fragments per use and the resulting bonus skills are random. Without save-scumming (save before reinforcing, reload if you don't get the desired skill), you can burn 9–12 Fragments on a single weapon. Turn off auto-save before reinforcing.

💎 Bonus Loot Along the Route

💎 Bonus 1 — Pure Dragon Blood (Iceshard Cliffs)

The glowing gathering nodes in the Iceshard Cliffs spawn Pure Dragon Blood at a ~30% rate during Frenzied Monster quests. This rare material is used in the Dragonking Eyepatch — the best helm piece to pair with 4-piece Artian armor.

💎 Bonus 2 — Decoration Farming (Tempered Investigations)

While farming Ancient Weapon Fragments from Tier 3 Tempered Monsters, you'll collect high-rarity Decorations from the investigation reward boxes. Keep an eye out for Critical Boost, Weakness Exploit, and Handicraft jewels — these are the best decorations to slot into Artian armor and weapons.

Full Set Skills

  • Slayer Lv5 — +45% wound damage. Wounds created on monsters last 50% longer before healing.
  • Critical Draw Lv3 — +100% affinity on draw attacks. Perfect for Great Sword and Long Sword.
  • Partbreaker Lv3 — +30% part damage. Parts break 30% faster. Synergizes with Slayer's wound creation.
  • Frenzy Resistance Lv2 — Reduces Frenzy buildup by 50%. Makes Gore Magala farming significantly safer.
  • Handicraft Lv2 — Extends weapon sharpness gauge by 20 hits. Pairs well with the Artian weapon's high raw damage.

Artian Weapons

Artian Weapons use a unique Reinforcement system instead of traditional upgrade trees. Each weapon starts at Rarity 7 with high base raw damage. You then use 3 Ancient Weapon Fragments at the Artian Forge to reinforce it to Rarity 8 — this process adds 2 random bonus skills from a pool of 12 possible skills. The skills can be re-rolled by consuming another 3 Fragments. The weapon maintains a raw damage ceiling 8–12% higher than any non-Artian alternative in its class, making it the definitive endgame choice for Great Sword, Long Sword, Switch Axe, and Charge Blade.

6. Build Recommendations

Build A — Wound Punisher (Great Sword)

  • Weapon: Artian Great Sword (Reinforced: Critical Draw + Focus)
  • Armor: Full Artian Set (5 pieces)
  • Charm: Exploiter Charm II (Weakness Exploit Lv2)
  • Key Decorations: Critical Boost ×3, Focus ×2, Quick Sheath ×2

This build maximizes Slayer's wound damage bonus. Use draw attacks (charged slash) to create wounds with Critical Draw's 100% affinity, then focus the wounded spot. Partbreaker Lv3 ensures parts break quickly, creating more wound opportunities. Best against slow, large monsters with accessible wound zones (Arkveld, Jin Dahaad, Uth Duna).

Build B — Infinite Sharpness (Long Sword)

  • Weapon: Artian Long Sword (Reinforced: Master's Touch + Handicraft)
  • Armor: Artian Helm + Mail + Coil + Greaves (4 pieces) + Dragonking Eyepatch
  • Charm: Master's Charm III (Critical Eye Lv3)
  • Key Decorations: Tenderizer ×3, Critical Boost ×2, Protective Polish ×1

This build sacrifices the full 5-piece set bonus for the Dragonking Eyepatch's Critical Eye Lv3. Combined with Master's Touch on the weapon, you maintain white/purple sharpness indefinitely on critical hits. Use the Long Sword's Spirit Helm Breaker on wounds for massive burst damage amplified by Slayer Lv4 (from 4-piece set). Best against agile, medium-sized monsters (Gore Magala, Rey Dau, Nu Udra).
