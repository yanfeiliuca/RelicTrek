1. Why You Need This

Heroics Lv3, Geologist Lv3, Maximum Might Lv2 — on a set you can unlock through three completely different trade routes depending on which region you've explored most. The Azuz armor is the best example of Monster Hunter Wilds' hidden trade economy: the same armor is accessible via Dogard (ore trades), Yabran (gem trades), or Gawdygog (the universal coin trader), meaning no matter where you are in the game, there's a path to this set. Most hunters never find any of the three paths. Heroics Lv3 alone — boosting attack when HP is low but not as extreme as Heroics Lv5 — makes this a surprisingly comfortable damage set for players who prefer safety margins.

3. Optimal Route

Recommended: Route A (Mining focus) — fastest and most repeatable

STEP 1 — Farm Fine Antimite Ore

1Go to Oilwell Basin. Mine outcrops in Areas 3, 4, and 5. Fine Antimite Ore appears in dark-colored ore nodes (different from standard silver Antimite).
💎Bonus: Oilwell Basin Area 4 has a viscous pool with a submerged ore node only accessible when the oil level drops during specific weather. It yields Purified Antimite Ore — a rare variant.

STEP 2 — Trade for Azuz Tanned Leather

2Trade Fine Antimite Ore with Dogard for Azuz Tanned Leather. Repeat until you have 8–10 leather for a full set.

STEP 3 — Craft at the smithy

3Craft full Azuz set at smithy. The set appears once you have Azuz Tanned Leather in your inventory.

4. Danger Zones & Bonus Loot

⚠ Danger Zones

⚠ Risk 1 — Bnahabra Nests

Oilwell Basin's mining areas have Bnahabra nests nearby. They inflict Bleeding if they grab you — bring Antiseptic Stone.

💎 Bonus Loot Along the Route

💎 Bonus 1 — Purified Antimite Ore

Oilwell Basin Area 4 has a viscous pool with a submerged ore node only accessible when the oil level drops during specific weather. It yields Purified Antimite Ore — a rare variant used in multiple high-rank upgrades.

💎 Bonus 2 — All Trade Sets via Route C

Route C (Ancient Wyvern Coins via Gawdygog) unlocks access to ALL village trade sets simultaneously. If you're farming coins anyway for other sets, Azuz is essentially free.

5. Skills Summary

  • Heroics Lv3 — attack boost at low HP, safer than Lv5
  • Geologist Lv3 — extra items from mining and gathering
  • Maximum Might Lv2 — affinity boost when stamina is full
