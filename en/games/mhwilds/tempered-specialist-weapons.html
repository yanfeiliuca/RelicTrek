1. Why You Need These

Most hunters build one weapon and use it everywhere. That's fine until you hit the hardest content. Two weapon lines — Lagiacrus and Seregios — are the answer to specific endgame problems that generic Artian weapons can't fully solve. Lagiacrus weapons carry the highest Thunder damage in the game, making them the definitive choice for the Arch-Tempered Uth Duna fight and any future Thunder-weak monsters. Seregios weapons have a unique mechanic — they self-sharpen on dodge, making them ideal for evasion-heavy weapons like Dual Blades, Light Bowgun, and Bow that hate stopping to sharpen mid-hunt. Neither weapon line is obvious to build — both require hunting Tempered variants that only appear under specific conditions.

3. Optimal Route

For Lagiacrus Weapons

1Unlock Tempered Lagiacrus by reaching HR 40+ and completing the Investigation quest that appears in Iceshard Cliffs.
2In each hunt, break the tail for Lagiacrus Lash, and target the back fins for Electrode+ drops.
3Complete 4–5 hunts for a full weapon.
💎Bonus: Iceshard Cliffs has an underwater section in Area 11 where Lagiacrus drags the fight. The underwater cave has 3 bonus harvest nodes not visible from the surface.

For Seregios Weapons

1Unlock Tempered Seregios via Investigation quests at Wildspire Waste (HR 35+).
2Focus on breaking scales on the body — Seregios Pinecone is the rarest material and only drops from specific scale breaks.
3Complete 4–6 hunts for a full weapon.
💎Bonus: Wildspire Waste Area 7 has a sand drift that reveals a buried chest after Seregios's flight patterns disturb the sand. Contains Seregios Shard — useful for weapon upgrades.

4. Danger Zones & Bonus Loot

⚠ Danger Zones

⚠ Risk 1 — Tempered Lagiacrus Thunder Roar

Tempered Lagiacrus's Thunder roar can stun you mid-combo. Always bring Earplugs or Stun Resistance.

⚠ Risk 2 — Seregios Scale Buildup

Seregios attacks cover your character in scales. If you don't shake them off quickly (roll 3 times), they deal recurring damage.

💎 Bonus Loot Along the Route

💎 Bonus 1 — Budget Thunder Build

Lagiacrus weapon + Lagiacrus armor pieces (2–3 pieces for Thunder Attack bonus) is a surprisingly strong budget set before you can fully build Artian.

5. Weapon Highlights

Lagiacrus

  • Best weapon types: Switch Axe, Hammer
  • Key feature: Highest Thunder damage in the game, Bolt Boost mechanic

Seregios

  • Best weapon types: Dual Blades, Bow, Light Bowgun
  • Key feature: Self-sharpening on dodge, Razor Sharp built-in

Neither of these weapon lines replaces Artian weapons for general use. But in their specific fights — Lagiacrus against Thunder-weak monsters, Seregios on evasion-heavy builds — they outperform everything else by a significant margin.
