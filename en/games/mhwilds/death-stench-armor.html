1. Why You Need This

Resentment Lv5. If you've spent any time reading endgame DPS guides for Monster Hunter Wilds, you've seen this skill listed at the top. It grants up to +25 attack when you have recoverable (white) health — which in most high-rank hunts means it's permanently active. The armor that carries Resentment Lv5 as a full-set skill is the Death Stench set. The game gives it an ominous name and then hides it completely — the key material, Sinister Cloth, doesn't drop from any monster. It comes from a trade system most players never interact with.

3. Optimal Route

This route unlocks the Death Stench armor set through Material Retrieval or Kunafa trading. Total estimated time: ~3–4 hours depending on retrieval and gathering luck.

Step 1 — Unlock Kunafa Material Retrieval

1Send the Kunafa NPC on a retrieval mission from the village. This unlocks after Chapter 3. Check back after completing 2–3 quests — retrieval missions run in the background.

Step 2 — Farm Great Windward Aloe (faster alternative)

2Go to Windward Plains. In Areas 11, 12, and 13, look for rare blue-glowing plants along the cliff walls. Great Windward Aloe is a rare harvest — run the full loop of all three areas, then fast-travel back to camp and repeat.
3💎 Bonus stop: Windward Plains Area 12 has a hidden alcove (behind the large rock formation near the cliff edge) containing a chest with Windward Aloe Seed — plant it at camp to grow your own supply.

Step 3 — Trade with Kilama for Sinister Cloth

4Find Kilama at Kunafa village. Trade Great Windward Aloe for Sinister Cloth. Each trade yields 1 cloth.

Step 4 — Craft Full Death Stench Set

5You need approximately 8–10 Sinister Cloths for a full set. Plan for 3–4 retrieval or trade runs.

4. Danger Zones & Bonus Loot

⚠ Danger Zones

⚠ Risk 1 — Resentment Requires Recoverable Health

Resentment only works with recoverable health. If you're using skills that convert white health (like some Palico abilities), Resentment may be inactive during those windows.

⚠ Risk 2 — Material Retrieval Is Slow

If you rely only on retrieval without actively farming Windward Aloe, expect 1–2 real-time hours of waiting.

💎 Bonus Loot Along the Route

💎 Bonus — Hidden Fishing Spot in Kunafa

Kunafa village has a hidden fishing spot (left of the main bridge) that yields Kunafa Eel — a rare ingredient boosting attack in camp meals.

5. Skills & Set Summary

Death Stench Armor α — Full Set Skills

  • Resentment Lv5 — +25 attack when recoverable HP exists. Almost always active in High Rank hunts where chip damage is constant.
  • Ambush Lv3 — Bonus damage on first hit from stealth. Pairs well with opening strategies.
  • Stun Resistance Lv2 — Reduces stun buildup, keeping you in the fight longer.
  • Intimidator Lv2 — Reduces the chance small monsters will attack you during hunts.

6. Build Recommendations

Best Weapon Pairings for Death Stench

Resentment Lv5's near-permanent +25 attack boost benefits weapons with high motion values that can capitalize on every point of raw attack. These weapons extract the most value from the full Death Stench α set:

  • Great Sword — True Charged Slash has the highest motion value in the game. +25 attack from Resentment Lv5 scales multiplicatively with charged attacks. The Ambush + Resentment combo makes wake-up TCS hits devastating.
  • Hammer — Charged big bang combos benefit immensely from flat attack boosts. Hammer's natural mobility means you can stay aggressive while managing recoverable health.
  • Lance — Chip damage from blocking keeps Resentment active permanently. Ambush Lv3 works well with Lance's counter-thrust opening on sleeping monsters.
