Artian Weapon Crafting Guide — Parts, Elements & Farming Route

1. Quick Answer

Artian Weapons are Monster Hunter Wilds' top-tier endgame weapons, crafted by combining three Artian Parts — Blades, Discs, Tubes, or Devices — obtained from hunting Tempered Monsters. Each weapon type uses a specific combination of these parts. The element of the finished weapon is determined by whichever element appears on the majority of the three parts you use. After crafting, you reinforce the weapon five times to stack stat bonuses across Attack, Affinity, Sharpness, and Element slots.

Only Rarity 8 parts are worth targeting for endgame builds. These drop from 6-star and 7-star Tempered Monsters including Gore Magala and Arkveld. The fastest route is running Investigations that feature two Tempered Monsters back to back — you can collect up to 20 parts per quest when a secondary monster is present.

2. Requirements

Quest: Wyvern Sparks and Rose Thorns

Complete this Chapter 4 main story quest, which requires hunting the Guardian Fulgur Anjanath and a Tempered Lala Barina. Upon completion, you receive the Damaged Weapon Shard — this unlocks Artian Tier I crafting at the smithy.

You must reach HR 41+ to encounter 6-star and 7-star Tempered Monsters. The Melding Pot alternative at Suja Village requires HR 100.

3. Where to Get Artian Parts

Primary Route — Tempered Monster Investigations

Artian Parts drop as bonus rewards at the end of hunts against Tempered Monsters in High Rank. The part rarity scales with the monster's star rating: 6-star and 7-star Tempered Monsters are the source of Rarity 8 parts, which are the only tier worth farming for endgame.

Highest Efficiency — Apex Tempered Investigations

5-star Tempered Apex Monster Investigations are the most material-efficient option. These are harder than standard Tempered hunts but yield more parts per quest. When a secondary Tempered Monster is present in the same Investigation, you can receive up to 20 parts in a single run.

Best Investigation Setup
Look for Investigations that feature both Gore Magala and Arkveld. Running these back to back is the community-recommended R8 farming route — two high-tier Tempered targets in one quest maximises parts per time spent.

Alternative Route — Melding Pot (HR 100+)

Once you reach HR 100, the Melding Pot at Suja Village unlocks the "Meld Relics" option. This lets you craft any specific Rarity 8 Artian Part for 120 Melding Points per piece. Use this if you need one or two specific part types to complete a weapon and don't want to rely on RNG drops.

4. Step-by-Step Crafting Walkthrough

1. Complete the Chapter 4 quest Wyvern Sparks and Rose Thorns to unlock Artian crafting. Collect the Damaged Weapon Shard reward.
2. Identify your weapon type and note the required part combination from the table in Section 6. For example, Long Sword requires 1× Blade + 2× Tube.
3. Decide your target element (Fire, Thunder, Water, Ice, Dragon) or go raw (no element). All three parts should share the same element to get maximum elemental power on the finished weapon. If you want raw, element doesn't matter — just pick any parts.
4. Run Tempered Monster Investigations, prioritising Gore Magala + Arkveld multi-target quests. Collect R8 parts until you have the three you need.
5. Go to the smithy (Gemma) → Forge Equipment → Artian Weapons. Select your three parts in the required combination. The weapon is created instantly with no additional materials.
6. Before reinforcing, test the reinforcement seed. Craft a throwaway Artian weapon of any type → check what 5 bonuses it would receive → if the pattern is good, proceed with your real weapon. Quit without saving if the seed is bad (see Section 7 for the seed system explanation).
7. Collect Reinforcement materials — Drearisite, Specklite, Argecite, and Oricalcite — from Festival Shares at the Smelting Foundry in the Everforge. Speak to Roqul or collect from Nata at camp.
8. Reinforce your Artian Weapon five times at Gemma → Reinforce/Dismantle Artian Weapons. Each reinforcement adds one bonus to the weapon's stats (Attack, Affinity, Sharpness, Element, or Defense).

5. Warnings

Nothing Is Missable — But Materials Are Finite Per Session
Artian Weapons have no time-locked or missable unlock conditions. However, Reinforcement materials from Festival Shares refresh on a schedule — if you burn through them on a bad seed roll, you will need to wait for the next festival cycle to collect more.
Don't Reinforce Without Checking the Seed First
The game uses a fixed seed system to assign reinforcement bonuses. The seed does not change between weapons crafted in the same session. If you reinforce a weapon and get five Defense bonuses, the next weapon you craft in that session will also produce poor results. Always test the seed with a dummy weapon before reinforcing your target.
Rarity 6 and 7 Parts Are Not Worth Farming
R6 and R7 Artian Weapons have lower base attack, fewer decoration slots, and no white sharpness. Only craft R6/R7 weapons temporarily if you need them to test reinforcement seed patterns.

7. Common Mistakes

Farming Rarity 6 or 7 Parts for Endgame

R6 and R7 Artian Weapons underperform at endgame — lower attack, fewer decoration slots, no white sharpness. Target 6-star and 7-star Tempered Monsters from the start to get R8 parts directly.

Mixing Elements When Building Elemental Weapons

Using one Fire part, one Thunder part, and one Water part produces a weapon with no element at all. To maximise elemental output, use three parts of the same element. At minimum, ensure two of three parts share the target element.

Reinforcing Without Testing the Seed

The game assigns reinforcement bonuses based on a seed that is fixed for your current crafting session. Craft a cheap R6 or R7 dummy weapon first, check the reinforcement preview, and only proceed to reinforce your real R8 weapon if the pattern is favourable. If not — quit without saving to reset the seed.

Ignoring Investigation Bonus Reward Slots

Investigations have Bronze, Silver, and Gold bonus reward slots. Gold slots have the highest probability of producing R8 Artian Parts. Always check the Investigation reward table before queuing — a quest with three Gold slots is far more efficient than one with only Bronze.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock Artian Weapons in Monster Hunter Wilds?
Complete the Chapter 4 main story quest "Wyvern Sparks and Rose Thorns," which requires hunting a Guardian Fulgur Anjanath and a Tempered Lala Barina. The quest reward includes a Damaged Weapon Shard that unlocks Artian crafting at the smithy.
Which Tempered Monsters drop Rarity 8 Artian Parts?
Rarity 8 parts drop from 6-star and 7-star Tempered Monsters: Gore Magala, Arkveld, Uth Duna, Jin Dahaad, Rey Dau, and Nu Udra. Arkveld is the highest-tier target and is considered the core farming monster for efficient R8 farming.
Can I choose which element my Artian Weapon has?
Yes. The element of the finished weapon is determined by whichever element appears most among the three parts you select. Use three parts of the same element for maximum elemental power. For a raw (no element) weapon, the element of the parts doesn't matter.
What is the best reinforcement pattern (god roll) for Artian Weapons?
For raw damage builds (Greatsword, Hammer, Long Sword), the ceiling is 5× Attack (+25 raw). For critical hit builds, 3× Attack + 2× Affinity is the standard target. For elemental builds, aim for 2–3× Element + 2× Attack. The "balanced" pattern of 3× Attack + 1× Affinity + 1× Sharpness is widely accepted as good enough and much more achievable than a perfect 5× Attack roll.
What is the seed system and how do I use it to fish for better rolls?
The game assigns reinforcement bonuses using a fixed seed for each crafting session. Craft a cheap dummy Artian weapon (R6 or R7), inspect its reinforcement preview — this shows what bonuses your next weapon will receive. If the pattern is poor, quit to the main menu without saving to reset the seed. Only reinforce your target R8 weapon when the seed produces the bonuses you need.
Is there an alternative to farming Tempered Monsters for Artian Parts?
Yes. Once you reach HR 100, the Melding Pot in Suja Village unlocks a "Meld Relics" option that lets you craft any specific Rarity 8 Artian Part for 120 Melding Points each. This removes RNG from the equation but requires a substantial Melding Point investment.
