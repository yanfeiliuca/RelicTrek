1. Why You Need This

Peak Performance Lv5. Constitution Lv5. Foray Lv5. All on one armor set. The Guild Ace Alpha set is the kind of skill combination that build theorists dream about — and virtually no one talks about, because the unlock requirement sounds daunting: defeat a Tempered Apex Monster in a Tier 7+ quest. The Dragonking Eyepatch — the iconic head piece from earlier Monster Hunter games — comes from the same source, and its single piece grants Counterstrike Lv3, a skill that gives a 15-second attack boost every time you're knocked back. Together, these two items from the same currency (Hunter Symbol III) represent some of the highest skill density available from single-slot gear in the entire game.

3. Optimal Route

STEP 1 — Reach Hunter Rank sufficient for Tier 7 quests

1Tier 7 Tempered Apex quests unlock after reaching HR 50+. Focus on completing optional High Rank quests to raise your HR efficiently.

STEP 2 — Identify the best Tier 7 Tempered Apex targets

2Not all Tempered Apex Monsters are equal in farming efficiency. Best targets for Hunter Symbol III with manageable difficulty:
3Tempered Apex Arzuros — lowest HP of Tier 7 Apex targets.
4Tempered Apex Lagombi — fast hunt, predictable patterns.
💎 Bonus

Tier 7 quests also drop Apex Bone and Apex Carapace — rare materials for late-game armor upgrades. Pick everything up.

STEP 3 — Farm Hunter Symbol III

5Each Tempered Apex Monster kill gives 1–2 Hunter Symbol IIIs. You need approximately 8 for the full Guild Ace set + 1 for the Eyepatch. Plan for 5–6 hunts.

STEP 4 — Craft Dragonking Eyepatch first

6Go to Gemma's smithy. Craft the Dragonking Eyepatch (1 Hunter Symbol III) first — it works immediately in any build that benefits from Counterstrike Lv3. Then craft the Guild Ace pieces as you accumulate more symbols.

4. Danger Zones & Bonus Loot

⚠ Danger Zones

⚠ Risk 1 — Tempered Apex Damage

Tempered Apex Monsters hit significantly harder than regular Tempered Monsters. Bring your best defensive setup for the first few attempts.

⚠ Risk 2 — Peak Performance Incompatibility

Peak Performance Lv5 requires full health to activate. Don't use it in builds that rely on low-health skills like Heroics.

💎 Bonus Loot

💎 Budget Mixed Set Strategy

Dragonking Eyepatch + any weapon with strong affinity + Counterstrike Lv3 = a powerful budget mixed set for mid-endgame while you farm Artian materials.

5. Skills Summary

Guild Ace Alpha — Full Set Skills

  • Peak Performance Lv5 — +25 attack when health is full.
  • Constitution Lv5 — -50% stamina consumption for evading, blocking, and dashing.
  • Foray Lv5 — +20% attack and +15% affinity when attacking a monster affected by an abnormal status.

Dragonking Eyepatch — Single Piece Skill

  • Counterstrike Lv3 — +25 attack for 15 seconds after being knocked back. The most skill-dense head piece in the game.
