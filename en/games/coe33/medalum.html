Medalum — Gestral Arena Reward, Passives, and Golgra Route

1. Quick Answer

Medalum is a Physical Maelle weapon with Defense C and Agility D scaling. The direct route is to select Maelle for the Gestral Village arena's fourth duel and win with her.

If another character wins the final duel, Medalum is still recoverable: Maelle can earn it by defeating Golgra in a separate one-on-one challenge.

The weapon supports a specific playstyle rather than every Maelle build. At level 4 it starts Maelle in Virtuose stance; levels 10 and 20 add Burn effects while she remains in Virtuose.

2. What Medalum Actually Does

Element: Physical · Scaling: Defense C / Agility D

Weapon levelVerified effect
4Maelle starts battle in Virtuose stance.
10Burn applied while Maelle is in Virtuose is doubled.
20Burn damage is doubled while Maelle is in Virtuose.

The level-4 effect removes an opening stance transition and makes Virtuose-compatible actions available immediately. The later effects support Burn-focused rotations.

Actual damage and turn order still depend on weapon level, attributes, skills, Pictos, Luminas, enemy defenses, and the current patch. Medalum does not by itself guarantee a one-turn kill or that Maelle acts before every enemy.

3. Prerequisites

Path A — arena reward:

  • Reach the Gestral Village (mandatory story location in Act 1)
  • Have Maelle in your party (she should be there by this point in the story)
  • Select Maelle for the fourth duel and win with her

Path B — Golgra recovery:

  • Complete the Gestral Village tournament with any character
  • Speak with Golgra (the village chief) after the tournament to unlock the duel option
  • Use Maelle for the duel
  • Prepare for a harder solo fight than the arena finale

4. Acquisition Routes

Path A — Gestral Arena

When you arrive in the Gestral Village, speak with Golgra (the chef/village chief) to enter the tournament. The tournament has four rounds of 1v1 duels.

The first three duels can be completed with any available character. For the fourth duel against The Stranger, select Maelle.

Defeat The Stranger as Maelle. A cutscene plays. You receive Medalum as the tournament reward.

Reward Rule

The character who wins the final duel receives the character-specific reward, or an upgrade when that reward is already owned. Medalum is awarded from this route only when Maelle wins.

Path B — Golgra Recovery Duel

If another character won the arena finale:

1Return to the Gestral Village at any point
2Speak with Golgra
3Challenge her to a duel using Maelle
4Win → receive Medalum

Golgra is a substantially harder solo opponent than the arena finale. Improve Maelle's weapon, attributes, skills, and defensive plan before returning if the duel is not currently manageable.

Shorter Recovery

If a save from before the fourth arena duel is available, replaying that duel with Maelle is usually the shorter route.

5. Hazard Guide

Final Duel Must Be Won by Maelle

Selecting Maelle is not enough; she must defeat The Stranger to receive Medalum from the arena.

Golgra Is a Recovery Route

The Golgra duel is harder and has no party support. Treat it as a second route, not evidence that Medalum was permanently missed.

Patch-Sensitive Build Claims

Prominent Maelle damage setups have changed after launch. Evaluate Medalum through its current stance and Burn effects rather than fixed one-shot rankings.
