Medalum — The Tournament Weapon Most Players Walk Away Without

1. Why You Need It

The Gestral Village tournament is not optional. It's a mandatory story event — you can't advance without completing it. But what the game never tells you is that the weapon you walk away with depends entirely on which character you choose for the final round.

If you choose Gustave, you receive Sakaram — a weapon you've almost certainly already picked up in the Ancient Sanctuary.
If you choose Lune, you receive Trebuchim — same situation.
If you choose Maelle, you receive Medalum — a weapon that scales off Defense and Agility in a way that enables Maelle's most powerful build configurations, and which is significantly harder to obtain any other way.

Medalum allows Maelle to start in Virtuose stance without the preliminary setup normally required. This changes her entire combat pattern: she can open with full offensive output instead of spending turns building toward it. Combined with Burn setups and the Mezzo Forte skill, Medalum builds generate massive sustained damage and AP recovery simultaneously.

Most players run the tournament with whoever they feel like playing, get a duplicate weapon or upgrade material, and never understand why their Maelle build never quite clicks in the mid and late game.

2. What Medalum Actually Does

Element: Physical · Scales with: Defense + Agility · Power at Lv. 5: 150

PassiveEffect
Virtuose StartMaelle begins every battle already in Virtuose stance — no setup turn required
Defense-to-damage scalingInvesting points in Defense increases both survivability AND damage output simultaneously

Why this matters for builds:
Standard Maelle builds require spending early turns to enter Virtuose stance before the big damage kicks in. Medalum eliminates that requirement entirely. Every battle starts at full power. This is especially significant in fights where the first few turns are the most dangerous — Maelle can be aggressive immediately instead of carefully setting up.

The Defense scaling is equally important: it lets you build Maelle as a durable fighter who also hits hard, rather than forcing a choice between offense and survival.

For the famous "Maelle one-shot" build: Medalum's Virtuose Start is what makes it viable — the build's full damage potential is available from turn one, making it possible to end encounters before enemies act.

3. Prerequisites

Path A (tournament — best option):

  • Reach the Gestral Village (mandatory story location in Act 1)
  • Have Maelle in your party (she should be there by this point in the story)
  • Remember: only the final round of the tournament matters — you can switch characters for any earlier round

Path B (Golgra duel — backup option):

  • Complete the Gestral Village tournament with any character
  • Speak with Golgra (the village chief) after the tournament to unlock the duel option
  • Use Maelle for the duel
  • Recommendation: wait until Act 2 or later — Golgra is a difficult solo fight early on

4. Optimal Route

Path A — The Tournament (First Playthrough)

When you arrive in the Gestral Village, speak with Golgra (the chef/village chief) to enter the tournament. The tournament has four rounds of 1v1 duels.

The first three rounds don't matter for this weapon. Use whoever you like — Gustave, Lune, Verso, anyone. The opponents are manageable at this point and the choice of character has no bearing on the final reward.

For the fourth and final round only: Select Maelle. She faces "The Stranger" — who turns out to be Sciel. The fight is 1v1 and the opponents at this stage are not particularly difficult for a prepared Maelle.

Defeat The Stranger as Maelle. A cutscene plays. You receive Medalum as the tournament reward.

Note

Even if Maelle loses the fourth round, a member of Expedition 33 wins the overall tournament and someone gets a weapon — but if Maelle doesn't win, you won't get Medalum this way.

After the tournament, speak with Golgra again. She'll agree to a separate 1v1 duel. This is how to get Medalum if you already used another character for the final round — but it's a harder fight, especially early on. More on this in the hazard guide.

Path B — The Golgra Duel (If you missed the tournament)

If you used Gustave or Lune for the final tournament round:

1Return to the Gestral Village at any point
2Speak with Golgra
3Challenge her to a duel using Maelle
4Win → receive Medalum

Golgra can be fought three times across the game (Gestral Village, Sacred River, Dark Gestral Arena), but you don't need to fight her at all locations — just challenge her after the tournament and win with Maelle.

Recommended

Don't attempt the Golgra duel with a low-level Maelle. She one-shots characters in early Act 1 if you don't have strong parry timing or access to Painted Power (unlocked after Act 2). The Golgra duel is manageable in Act 2+.

5. Hazard Guide

Tournament (Path A)

The fourth round opponent is Sciel. She's a fair fight for Act 1 standards. Focus on parrying her attacks and maintaining your AP. There's no particularly dangerous attack to watch for — consistent, clean play wins it.

Golgra Duel (Path B, early attempt)

Warning — Golgra Early

Golgra hits hard. Very hard. In early Act 1, she can eliminate Maelle in one or two attacks if you're not parrying successfully. The fight is 1v1 with no support from the rest of the party.

If you want Medalum quickly and missed the tournament: use a save from before the tournament's final round. It's worth replaying the last fight with Maelle rather than attempting the Golgra duel at low level.

If you're in Act 2 or beyond: the Golgra duel becomes manageable. Build Maelle for maximum Defense scaling, bring strong healing consumables, and parry everything.
