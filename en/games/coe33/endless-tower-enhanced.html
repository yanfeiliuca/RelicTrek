1. Why You Need It

The base game Endless Tower offered a strong challenge for players who had already conquered the main story, Simon, and Clea. But when the Thank You Update released on December 12, 2025, it added something that made even veteran players reconsider their builds: four enhanced variants of the game's most iconic bosses, placed in a new section of the Endless Tower.

These are the hardest fights currently in Clair Obscur: Expedition 33. Players who cleared the original Endless Tower without difficulty are finding these enhanced variants significantly harder. The attack patterns are extended, the damage output is substantially increased, and new phase transitions add complexity that did not exist in the original encounters.

Each enhanced boss drops at least one exclusive item — Pictos, weapons, or cosmetics — unavailable from any other source in the game, including the standard Endless Tower. If you want a complete collection, this content is mandatory. There is no alternative farming route for these rewards.

2. What the Enhanced Bosses Are

The four enhanced bosses are variants of iconic main game encounters. Each has been redesigned with additional attack phases triggered at specific HP thresholds, increased damage multipliers requiring better defensive strategies than the original, new ability combinations that reference the original encounter while adding novel patterns, and unique rewards exclusive to that enhanced difficulty.

Unlike the standard Endless Tower where you climb floors with increasing difficulty, the enhanced section is a gauntlet where each boss must be defeated in sequence. There is no retreating to resupply between fights — your party must survive all four encounters in a single run.

3. Prerequisites

Content Unlocks

  • The Thank You Update (DLC) installed — released December 12, 2025
  • Main story completed (Paintress defeated)
  • Original Endless Tower accessed (standard floors cleared)
  • Enhanced section unlocks after reaching a specific floor in the standard tower

Character Preparation

  • Party level 90+ — characters below level 80 will find the damage output unmanageable
  • Level 33 weapons (maximum) — Perfect Chroma Catalysts from Flying Manor sub-bosses are required for this final upgrade tier
  • Second Chance Lumina on every party member — non-negotiable for the enhanced bosses
  • AP regeneration Lumina optimized — longer fights demand sustained output without running dry
  • Full inventory of healing items and revival consumables

Build Considerations

Burn-stacking builds that are effective in the Clea fight tend to be less efficient against the enhanced bosses due to their phase-based mechanics and frequent status-cleanse transitions. Prioritize raw burst damage and AP regeneration over damage-over-time strategies.

4. Optimal Route — Preparation and Execution

Pre-Run Preparation

1Max your weapons. All six party members should have Level 33 weapons before entering the enhanced tower. Farm Perfect Chroma Catalysts from the four Flying Manor sub-bosses (Goblu, Lampmaster, Évêque, Dualliste).
2Optimize Lumina setups. AP regeneration is more critical than in any other fight in the game. Each character needs a dedicated AP recovery Lumina plus survival Lumina (Second Chance is mandatory).
3Review elemental matchups. The enhanced versions have modified elemental interactions compared to their standard counterparts. Assign weapons and Pictos to cover all elemental weaknesses across your party.
4Stock consumables. Fill your inventory with high-tier healing items. You cannot restock between fights.
📋 Critical — AP Regeneration Priority

In the standard Endless Tower, burst damage is king. In the enhanced section, sustained AP output matters more — fights last 2-3x longer, and running out of AP mid-fight is a guaranteed wipe. Allocate at least two Lumina slots per character to AP management.

The Enhanced Tower Gauntlet

5Enter the Endless Tower and clear the standard floors to unlock the enhanced section portal.
6Activate the Expedition Flag at the enhanced section entrance — this is your only checkpoint for the entire gauntlet.
7Fight the four enhanced bosses in sequence. Each defeated boss drops its exclusive reward immediately — you keep rewards even if you wipe on a subsequent boss.
8Upon clearing all four, you receive a bonus completion reward exclusive to the enhanced gauntlet.

5. Hazard Guide — Combat Strategy

The Thank You Update's enhanced boss content is designed around perfecting the parry and burst damage mechanics simultaneously. The combination of parry precision (to avoid HP drain from missed blocks), concentrated burst windows (the enhanced bosses are more mobile between burst opportunities), and AP management (longer fights require sustained output) creates a skill ceiling above anything in the base game.

Core Combat Principles

⚠ Rule #1 — Parry Is Not Optional

Every missed parry in the enhanced fights drains significantly more HP than in the standard tower. Parry precision directly determines whether you survive long enough to reach a burst window. Practice the enhanced attack patterns — they are longer and faster than the originals.

⚠ Rule #2 — Burst Windows Are Shorter

The enhanced bosses recover from stagger states faster than their standard counterparts. You have approximately 60-70% of the burst window you are accustomed to. Land your highest-damage abilities first — do not save them for the end of the window.

⚠ Rule #3 — AP Runs Out

In the standard tower, most fights end before AP becomes a concern. Not here. Every enhanced boss fight lasts long enough that AP starvation is a real threat. Monitor your AP bar constantly — if it dips below 30%, switch to AP-conserving basic attacks until your regeneration Lumina restores reserves.

Team Composition Recommendations

Community-tested approach: High-damage Maelle builds with Sciel support (the configuration used for the Clea fight) remain effective against some enhanced bosses, but the extended fight duration makes AP regeneration more critical than in the Clea encounter. Consider bringing a dedicated AP-battery character in place of a second DPS if you find yourself running dry mid-fight.

Specific enhanced bosses favor different compositions. Review each boss's elemental properties before engaging — the enhanced versions have modified elemental interactions compared to their standard counterparts. A composition that shreds one enhanced boss may be nearly useless against the next.

💎 Pro Tip — Reward Persistence

Each enhanced boss drops its exclusive reward immediately upon defeat. Even if you wipe on a subsequent boss, you keep the rewards from defeated bosses. Do not feel pressured to clear the entire gauntlet in one attempt — collect rewards incrementally and return when your build is stronger.
