1. Why You Need It

Weapons in Expedition 33 can be upgraded from Level 1 to 33 at the Curator in camp. Grandiose Chroma Catalysts handle the climb from 1 to 32. The final step — Level 32 to 33 — requires something rarer: a Perfect Chroma Catalyst.

Upgrading a weapon to Level 33 nearly doubles its base power. A weapon that hits for 4,000 at Level 32 will hit for over 8,000 at Level 33, before factoring in stat bonuses and passive traits. For the endgame builds that define Expedition 33's ceiling — Simoso builds for Verso, Medalum builds for Maelle, elemental builds for Lune — maxing weapons is the final multiplier.

There are exactly 10 Perfect Chroma Catalysts in a single playthrough, all from specific encounters. Most are locked behind the game's hardest optional content. This guide maps them all.

💡 Note

Simoso (Verso's hidden endgame sword, obtained from Simon) arrives already at Level 33. It is the only weapon in the game that requires no Perfect Chroma Catalyst to reach maximum level.

3. Optimal Collection Order

Plan your route to minimize backtracking:

Phase 1 (Act 3 start, immediately accessible)

  • #8 Serpenphare (world map, flying required)
  • #10 Trash-Can Man (Lumière, Act 3)
  • #9 Painting Workshop Lampmaster (world map dungeon)

Phase 2 (Flying Manor cleared)

  • #1–4 (all four Flying Manor bosses) — also provides Grandiose Chroma Catalysts from every floor

Phase 3 (Renoir's Drafts + The Abyss)

  • #5 Chromatic Création (requires Paint Break from Sastro quest — do this first)
  • #6 Simon / The Abyss (the hardest boss in the game — get #5 first since it's easier)

Phase 4 (Post-game optional)

  • #7 Painted Love / Endless Tower Stage 11 (do after story completion)

4. Hazard Guide (Key Fights)

Chromatic Création (Renoir's Drafts, #5)

Accessed only after breaking the blue-vein rock in Renoir's Drafts with the Paint Break ability. The Chromatic Création is a challenging optional boss. Recommended level 85+.

Simon (#6 — 2 Perfect Chroma Catalysts)

Simon is the hardest boss in the game. He grants TWO Perfect Chroma Catalysts — worth targeting for this reason alone alongside his weapon drop (Simoso). Full strategy in the Simoso guide.

Painted Love (#7)

The Endless Tower Stage 11 final boss. Full strategy in the Endless Tower guide.

Serpenphare (#8)

A large Nevron boss on The Continent overworld. Recommended level 75+. The fight has clear visual telegraph attacks — learn the patterns and it's manageable before tackling Flying Manor or Renoir's Drafts.
