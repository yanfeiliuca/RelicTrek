Lost Gestrals — 9 Small Creatures Spread Across the Continent, and the Ability They Unlock

1. Why You Need It

At some point in Act 1, a portly Gestral named Sastro appears at your camp. He's searching for something — 9 young Gestrals who've gotten lost somewhere across The Continent. He asks you to find them.

The game gives you no map markers. No quest tracker. No indication of which areas they're in. You're told they exist and that's it.

Most players find the first one or two by accident and forget about the quest. But finding all 9 does something that matters for exploration throughout the rest of the game: it unlocks the Paint Break ability. After finding roughly 4 Gestrals and returning to Sastro, he teaches you how to destroy the blue-veined rock formations scattered across The Continent's world map and dungeons. These blue rocks have been sitting in your path since the beginning of the game — blocking items, sealing shortcuts, hiding secrets. Paint Break opens all of them.

The Gestrals themselves are all on the world map overworld — not inside dungeons or caves. They stand in fixed positions and don't require combat to interact with. But several are on islands or floating areas that require Esquie's swimming ability, and a few require the flying ability. The full 9 can't be gathered until Act 3.

3. All 9 Gestral Locations

Act 1 Reachable (no special ability needed)

Gestral 1 — Outside Esquie's Nest. After visiting Gestral Village and heading toward Esquie's Nest, the first Lost Gestral stands just south/outside the entrance.

Gestral 2 — Stone Wave Cliffs (east side). As you approach the Stone Wave Cliffs entrance, look for a path to the right that leads to a Bourgeon enemy. Behind the Bourgeon, in a small alcove to the northeast.

Act 2 Reachable (requires Esquie swimming)

Gestral 3 — White Tree island (northwest). From the first Gestral Beach, swim around to the northwest. On the west side of the island with the White Tree portal.

Gestral 4 — Southeast of Monoco's Station. Southeast of Monoco's Station south entrance, near a merchant with pots. This one unlocks Paint Break when returned to Sastro.

Gestral 5 — North shore of Coastal Cave island. North shore of the island with Coastal Cave (northeast of Forgotten Battlefield). Requires coral swimming after Old Lumiere.

Gestral 6 — Red trees island northwest of Old Lumiere. Island with red trees. Behind 2 Mimes in the center, near mountains among Gestral jars.

Act 3 Reachable (requires Esquie flying)

Gestral 7 — Floating island near the Endless Tower. Fly to the floating island near the Endless Tower (northwest of Stone Wave Cliffs).

Gestral 8 — Northwest of Endless Night Sanctuary portal. Southeast of Sirene's area. Northwest of the Endless Night Sanctuary portal, near a group of trees.

Gestral 9 — Between two green trees south of Monoco's Station. North from Forgotten Battlefield exit, between two green trees in a snowy tree area.

4. Optimal Route

Don't rush this quest. The Gestrals span three acts and require multiple traversal abilities.

Act 1: Find Gestral 1 (Esquie's Nest) and Gestral 2 (Stone Wave Cliffs) naturally during story progression. Return to Sastro.

Early Act 2 (after getting swim ability): Find Gestrals 3, 4, 5, 6 during world map exploration. Return to Sastro to unlock Paint Break after the 4th.

Immediately use Paint Break: Return to every area where you saw blue-vein rocks and break them. This opens items and paths sealed since Act 1.

Act 3: Get Gestrals 7, 8, 9 once you have the flying ability. All three require flying. Return to Sastro for final rewards and the trophy.

5. Hazard Guide

Most Lost Gestrals have no combat encounter — you walk up and interact. A few have enemies nearby (the Bourgeon near Gestral 2, the Mimes near Gestral 6) that you can either fight or route around.

The main challenge is simply knowing where they are. None are inside dungeons or caves — they're all on the overworld — but several are on islands that are easy to miss during normal exploration.

Don't Forget to Return to Sastro

The Paint Break ability only unlocks when you speak to Sastro after finding the 4th Gestral. You won't receive it automatically from finding the Gestral alone.
