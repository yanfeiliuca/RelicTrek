1. Why You Need It

The Endless Tower is the game's endgame gauntlet — 11 stages, 33 trials, every fight harder than the last. The name is a slight lie: there are exactly 33 encounters, and they end.

What they end with is Painted Love.

Renoir and the Paintress share a single health bar of approximately 25 million HP. Both are present in the arena simultaneously, both attack, both use the full suite of abilities you learned to survive across the entire game — Void Cross, Black Hole, Swift Attack, Chroma Waves. But they're one target. AoE attacks hit both. Burn and Stun debuffs track independently on each. Everything you've learned about fighting them separately applies here, at the same time, and for 25 million HP.

Clearing all 33 trials earns you: every floor's Pictos reward (most valuable are Stage 6 onward), a Perfect Chroma Catalyst from Painted Love, the Clair outfit for Verso, Music Record #32 (Clea), and Maelle's Ponytail White Haircut from the Fading Woman after the final fight. You also unlock the "Endless" achievement.

The 1.5.0 update added four bonus super-boss portraits that unlock inside the tower after you clear all 33 normal trials — Clea Unleashed and Simon the Divergent Star among them, each dropping another Perfect Chroma Catalyst. The tower isn't empty after you finish it.

You don't have to clear all 33 trials in one session. The tower saves your progress. Come back.

2. What You Earn

Per-floor rewards (every single trial): Grandiose Chroma Catalyst × 1, Colour of Lumina × 1, Lumina Point × 1 (on success).

Stage-boss rewards (every 3rd floor): Additional Pictos — exclusive to that stage, growing rarer and more powerful as stages increase.

3. Prerequisites

  • Act 3 started — Esquie's flying ability required to reach the tower
  • Recommended level: 70+ to start, 90+ for Stage 8 onward
  • All 6 party slots filled and leveled — later stages demand diverse builds
  • Simoso equipped on Verso if available — the final boss has 25 million HP and Light-damage output matters
  • Cheater Lumina highly recommended — extra turns dramatically affect sustained fights

4. Optimal Route

Step 1 — Find the Endless Tower

1Open the world map. The tower is southwest, visible as a large golden circle with a "C" inside, southwest of Stone Wave Cliffs and the Crimson Forest area. Fly there with Esquie. Land and approach the base.
💎 Before Entering

Check the abandoned camp just outside the entrance → Colour of Lumina. Go under the rightmost staircase at the tower entrance and head north → Visages outfit (Verso cosmetic).

Enter through the giant doorway. Pass through two portals to reach the tower proper. Activate the Endless Tower — Entrance Expedition Flag.

Step 2 — Stages 1–7 (Learning the ramp-up)

2Each stage has 3 trials. The first trial of each stage contains normal Nevron enemies. The second is harder enemies. The third is a boss encounter with a Pictos reward. Stages 1–5 are manageable at level 70. Stage 6 begins the transition to endgame-level difficulty. Collect every Grandiose Chroma Catalyst and Colour of Lumina — these accumulate significantly across 33 floors. You do not need to complete all 33 in one session. Leave and return whenever needed.

Step 3 — Stage 8 onward (Boss marathons)

3From Stage 8, trials stop featuring standard enemies and become back-to-back boss encounters — multiple major bosses in a single fight. Each is a remixed, stronger version of bosses from the main game and optional content.
⚠ Stage 11 — The Hardest Sequence

Trial 31: Chromatic Moissoneuse + Mask Keeper + Dualliste
Trial 32: Chromatic Clair Obscur + Lampmaster + Création
Trial 33: Painted Love

Step 4 — Painted Love

4Full strategy in Section 5. After defeating Painted Love, speak with the Fading Woman near the canvas to receive Maelle's Ponytail Haircut and trigger the "Endless" achievement.

5. Painted Love: Hazard Guide

Recommended preparation: Level 90+, optimized endgame build, Cheater Lumina on all characters, Simoso on Verso, Medalum on Maelle.

Core mechanic: Renoir and the Paintress are one fight with one shared health bar (~25 million HP). They fight simultaneously — each takes a separate action per turn.

Key Rules

⚔ Rule 1 — Hit Both with AoE

AoE damage counts twice — once for each boss — against the shared HP pool. This is the most efficient damage strategy.

⚔ Rule 2 — Debuffs Track Independently

Apply Burn to one, Stun to the other — each keeps its own debuff status. A Burn on the Paintress doesn't prevent a Stun on Renoir.

⚔ Rule 3 — Know Your Attack History

They use the full combined moveset of both bosses: Void Cross, Black Hole, Swift Attack, Chroma Waves. You've survived all of these before. Apply the same timing here.

⚔ Rule 4 — Eliminate Renoir's High-Damage Attacks First

Renoir tends to deal more raw damage. If you can focus Burn and Break effects on him while maintaining DPS, the Paintress becomes the easier remaining portion.

Strategy: Burst Approach

Verso (Simoso) + Maelle (Medalum) + Sciel or Lune as support. Use Cheater for double turns on your main DPS. Apply Burn early via Maelle's fire skills, then stack Verso's Light damage through Simoso passives for sustained high output. Both bosses share HP — every Burn tick and every Light strike counts twice via AoE.
