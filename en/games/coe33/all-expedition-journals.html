1. Why You Need It

Expedition Journals are documents left by members of previous Expeditions — the ones that came before Expedition 33. There are 49 in total, scattered across every region of the game. They tell the stories of other expedition members who faced the same fate as your party faces now, and they provide the most detailed available lore about the world's history.

Beyond lore, complete journal collection is required for 100% completion and specific achievements. Finding all 49 unlocks a full picture of what previous Expeditions discovered before being numbered away by the Paintress — the journals are the game's deepest narrative layer, and most players who skip them miss the richest world-building the game has to offer.

2. What You're Collecting — Distribution by Act

The 49 journals are distributed across every act and region. Understanding the breakdown by act is essential for efficient collection.

ActRegionsNotes
Act 1Gestral Village, Sirene dungeon, Stone Wave Cliffs, coastal exploration zonesAll accessible with no special abilities
Act 2Old Lumiere, Inside the Monolith, Falling Leaves, Sinister Cave, underwater areasSeveral require Esquie swim ability
Act 3The Reacher, Crimson Forest, Flying Manor, post-Paintress areas, Thank You Update zonesNeed Esquie fly; some need dive for Verso's Drafts

Several journals are inside Manor Doors — the Renoir Journal, Aline journal, and others are obtained through the Manor Door system (see Manor Complete Route guide for full access). The game's collection menu has a built-in journal tracker — check it after each major area to identify which journals have been found versus remaining.

3. Prerequisites

  • Esquie swim ability — required for underwater journals in Act 2 and Act 3 coastal zones
  • Esquie fly ability — required for Act 3 Crimson Forest and high-altitude journal locations
  • Esquie dive ability — required for Verso's Drafts DLC journals (Thank You Update content)
  • Manor Door access — several journals are behind Manor Doors; see Manor Complete Route guide
  • In-game collection tracker — use after each area to confirm no journals remain before moving on

4. Optimal Route — Act-by-Act Checklist

Follow this act-by-act checklist approach to ensure zero journals are missed. Total estimated time: varies by thoroughness — a dedicated run with area-by-area tracker verification is the most reliable method.

Act 1 — The Opening Regions

1Sweep Gestral Village thoroughly — interior rooms, side paths, and NPC-adjacent areas.
2Navigate the Sirene dungeon — check all branching corridors and dead-end rooms.
3Explore Stone Wave Cliffs — focus on elevated ledges and hidden alcoves.
4Sweep all coastal zones accessible on foot — no special abilities needed for Act 1 journals.

Act 2 — The Mid-Game Expansion

5Explore Old Lumiere thoroughly — check building interiors and side streets.
6Enter Inside the Monolith dungeon — this has a branching structure. Do not take the direct path; explore every fork. Several journals are in side corridors easily missed by players rushing the main route.
7Sweep the Falling Leaves area and Sinister Cave.
8With Esquie's swim ability unlocked, return to underwater areas — several journals are on submerged ledges and in underwater caves.

Act 3 — Endgame and Post-Game

9Climb The Reacher — check all levels and side platforms.
10With Esquie's fly ability, sweep the Crimson Forest — several journals are on high ledges reachable only by flight.
11Explore the Flying Manor area and adjacent zones.
12After defeating the Paintress, sweep all post-Paintress areas — new journals appear in previously inaccessible zones.
13Enter Verso's Drafts (Thank You Update DLC) — requires Esquie dive. At least one journal is exclusive to this zone.
📋 Note — Manor Door Journals

Several journals (Renoir Journal, Aline Journal, and others) are accessed through the Manor Door system. See the Manor Complete Route guide for the full path to each door. Open these before finishing Act 3 to ensure none are locked behind story progression.

5. Hazard Guide — High-Miss Locations

These are the locations where journals are most frequently missed by players — even thorough explorers.

⚠ High-Miss Zone — Monolith Dungeon Branching Paths

The Monolith dungeon has a branching structure with several side corridors. Many players take the direct path to the boss and miss 2-3 journals in the side branches. Explore every fork before pressing forward.

⚠ High-Miss Zone — Act 3 Fly-Only Areas

Several journals in the Crimson Forest and adjacent Act 3 zones are on high ledges reachable only with Esquie's fly ability. If you do not have fly unlocked, return to these areas after unlocking it — the journals will not appear until fly is available.

⚠ High-Miss Zone — Verso's Drafts Dive Journal

At least one journal is exclusive to Verso's Drafts, the Thank You Update DLC zone. This requires Esquie's dive ability. Players who skip the DLC content will miss this journal permanently.

Golden rule: check the in-game collection tracker after every major area. The tracker distinguishes between journals found and journals remaining per region. Do not leave an area until the tracker confirms zero remaining for that zone.
