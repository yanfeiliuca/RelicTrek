Simon and Alicia — The Two Secret Bosses Hidden Deeper Than Clea

1. Why You Need It

Clea is the most visible secret boss in Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 — but she is not the only one. Two more secret bosses exist in Act 3, each requiring specific conditions to trigger. Simon is found in The Abyss, an area accessible only through Renoir's Drafts — the underwater region that itself requires Esquie's dive ability. Alicia is found at the top of The Reacher in a solo fight, but only if Maelle's Relationship Level is at 7, and only after defeating the Paintress.

Neither is mentioned in the main story or hinted at by any NPC. Players who do not specifically seek the secret boss content will complete the game without ever seeing either of them. Simon is widely considered the most challenging secret boss in the entire game — harder than Clea, harder than the Endless Tower, harder than anything in the Thank You Update.

If you're going for 100% completion or simply want to experience everything the game hides from you, these two fights are non-negotiable.

3. Prerequisites

For Simon (The Abyss)

  • Act 3 started
  • Esquie dive ability unlocked (Relationship Level 6)
  • Access to Renoir's Drafts underwater region
  • Party level 85-90+ recommended
  • Fully upgraded weapons (Level 33) strongly advised

For Alicia (The Reacher)

  • Defeat the Paintress (main story completion required)
  • Maelle's Relationship Level 7 questline started
  • Maelle built for sustained solo combat — no party support in this fight

4. Optimal Route

Simon — The Abyss Route

1Unlock Esquie's dive ability at Relationship Level 6. Travel to the open water between Sirene and Visages.
2Enter Renoir's Drafts through the underwater portal. Navigate through the first section of enemies.
3Locate the large glowing pillar at the center of the underwater space. The opening in the pillar leads to The Abyss.
4Head to the cliff face inside The Abyss and drop down to find Simon's arena.
5Activate the Expedition Flag: Simon save point. Prepare your party, then approach the center.
💎 Bonus — Renoir's Drafts Pickups

While navigating Renoir's Drafts, check upper ledges for grapple points — they lead to Music Records, weapons, and Pictos obtainable nowhere else.

Alicia — The Reacher Route

1Complete the main story by defeating the Paintress.
2Ensure Maelle's Relationship Level 7 quest is active (check your relationship tracker).
3Return to The Monolith and climb to the top again — "Chromatic Clair Obscur" and Alicia will be waiting.
4Engage Alicia — this is a SOLO fight with Maelle only. No party members can assist.
📋 Note — Chromatic Clair Obscur

In addition to Alicia, defeating the Paintress and returning to the Monolith triggers a Chromatic Clair Obscur encounter at the top — a further secret fight that can be considered a companion encounter.

5. Hazard Guide — Simon Fight Strategy

Recommended: Level 90+ party with Second Chance Lumina equipped on every character. Simon punishes mistakes harder than anything else in the game.

Phase 1 — The Measured Onslaught

⚠ Phase 1 Threats

Simon opens with a 3-hit combo, escalating to a 5-hit combo, then a 6-hit long combo. He also uses an HP-to-1 attack that leaves your character at critical health regardless of defense. Rule: Parry over Dodge in Phase 1 — parrying builds Perfection rank, essential for survival.

Phase 2 — Spectral Annihilation (~60% HP)

🔴 Phase 2 Threats

Simon erases your active party — all three front-line members are removed. He summons a spectral phantom sword that attacks independently, and his combos extend to 8 hits. Critical: any downed party member left unattended is permanently erased from the fight. Revive immediately or lose them for good.

Phase 3 — The Red Phantom (Below 25% HP)

🔴 Phase 3 Threats

Simon's phantom sword turns red and deals massively increased damage. Combos reach 12 hits, and he wipes your party again. This is a DPS race — burn him down before he burns through your reserve members.

Alicia Fight Notes

Build Maelle specifically for this encounter rather than relying on party synergies. Sustained solo damage output and self-sustain are critical — Maelle must be able to survive and deal damage without any support. Equip her with the best available Lumina for AP regeneration and survivability.
