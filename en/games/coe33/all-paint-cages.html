1. Why You Need It

Paint Cages are collectible items scattered across the Continent that serve as upgrade materials for weapons and Pictos. They are required for 100% completion. Visually, Paint Cages appear as small, glowing cage-like objects — easy to overlook given the density of other collectibles in the environment.

Every Paint Cage you collect brings you closer to upgrading your weapons and Pictos to their maximum potential. In the late game, the difference between a partially upgraded character and a fully upgraded one is dramatic — and Paint Cages are a critical part of the upgrade economy.

2. What You're Collecting — Distribution by Act

Paint Cages are distributed across all three acts following predictable regional patterns. Understanding the distribution by act is the key to efficient collection.

ActRegionsRequirements
Act 1Gestral Village, Stone Wave Cliffs, coastal paths, dungeon corridorsNo special abilities required — accessible from the start
Act 2Sirene dungeon, Old Lumiere, Monolith branching paths, island locationsSome require Esquie swim for island locations
Act 3Crimson Forest, Flying Manor areas, post-Paintress zones, Verso's Drafts DLCSeveral require Esquie fly; Verso's Drafts requires Esquie dive

The in-game collection menu tracks Paint Cages per area — this is your most reliable tool. After clearing each dungeon, check the tracker to confirm no Cages remain before moving on.

3. Prerequisites

  • No special abilities required for Act 1 — all Cages in the opening regions are accessible from the start
  • Esquie swim ability — required for Paint Cages on Act 2 island locations and some coastal zones
  • Esquie fly ability — required for Act 3 Crimson Forest and Flying Manor adjacent areas
  • Esquie dive ability — required for Verso's Drafts DLC Paint Cages (Thank You Update content)
  • In-game collection tracker — use after each dungeon to verify zero remaining Cages for that area

4. Optimal Route — Act-by-Act Sweep

Follow this act-by-act sweep strategy. The core principle: thorough exploration of every ground-level area before advancing to the next act.

Act 1 — The Opening Sweep

1Thoroughly explore Gestral Village — interiors, side paths, behind buildings.
2Sweep the Stone Wave Cliffs — check elevated ledges and hidden alcoves.
3Walk all coastal paths — Paint Cages are often tucked into shoreline corners.
4Explore dungeon corridors — Paint Cages frequently appear in side rooms off the main path.

Act 2 — Expanding the Search

5Navigate the Sirene dungeon — check all branching corridors and side rooms.
6Explore Old Lumiere — building interiors and side streets contain Cages.
7Enter the Monolith dungeon — this has branching paths with Cages in side corridors. Do not take the direct route — explore every fork.
8With Esquie swim unlocked, return to island locations — several Cages are on offshore islands.

Act 3 — The Final Sweep

9With Esquie fly unlocked, sweep the Crimson Forest — focus on high-altitude ledges.
10Explore Flying Manor and adjacent zones — new Cages appear in post-story areas.
11Enter Verso's Drafts DLC (Thank You Update) with Esquie dive — additional Paint Cages exclusive to this zone.
📋 Critical Habit — Check the Tracker

After clearing each dungeon or major area, check the in-game collection tracker. It shows Paint Cages per area. Do not advance to the next zone until the tracker confirms zero remaining Cages for the current area.

5. Hazard Guide — Why Paint Cages Are Easy to Miss

⚠ Visual Density — Environmental Camouflage

Paint Cages are small, glowing cage-like objects that are easy to overlook given the density of other collectibles and environmental detail. Unlike journals or records, they do not have a distinct sound cue or visual flare — they blend into the environment. Slow, methodical exploration of every room and corridor is the only reliable method.

⚠ Dungeon Speed-Run Penalty

Dungeons are the highest-density locations for Paint Cages, but they are also the areas players are most likely to rush through. Side rooms in dungeons frequently contain 1-2 Cages. If you take the direct path to the boss, you will miss them. Every dungeon should be treated as a thorough sweep, not a speed run.

⚠ Tracker Discipline

The per-area tracker is your best defense against missed Cages. But it only works if you check it. After each dungeon — before you leave — open the collection menu and confirm that the Paint Cage count for that area shows zero remaining. If any remain, retrace your steps before moving on.
