5 Gestral Beaches — Hidden Coastal Spots With Rare Items and Secret Encounters

1. Why You Need to Find These Beaches

The Continent in Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 has a coastline that most players never explore. The main story pulls you inland — toward Sirene, Visages, the Monolith — and the beaches sit at the edges of the map, invisible on the default compass. Five hidden coastal areas exist across all three acts, and every single one contains something you cannot get anywhere else.

Rare Pictos. Secret Gestral encounters. Early-game materials that trivialize Act 2 bosses. A hidden merchant who sells items unavailable from any other vendor in the game. If you are doing a completionist run — or if you want an edge that makes the mid-game dramatically easier — these beaches are mandatory stops.

The game never points you toward any of them. No NPC mentions the coastline. No quest marker appears. You either explore the edges of the map on your own, or you follow a guide like this one.

2. What Each Beach Contains

Each of the 5 Gestral beaches is a self-contained mini-zone with its own theme, enemies, and reward pool. They are not connected — you travel to each independently as you progress through the acts.

3. Prerequisites

  • Act 1 beaches (2): No special abilities required — accessible on foot from the starting region and Sirene
  • Act 2 beaches (2): Esquie's swim ability (Relationship Level 6) required to access underwater cave entrances
  • Act 3 beach (1): Esquie's fly ability required to reach the cliffside cove from elevated ledges
  • Recommended party level: Act 1 beaches — any level. Act 2 beaches — 40+. Act 3 beach — 70+

4. Optimal Route — Beach by Beach

Act 1 — Shimmering Shore

1From the starting area, follow the coastal path heading east. Look for a break in the cliff wall where the path narrows and descends toward the water.
2At the shore, defeat the Gestral sentinel guarding the central rock formation. It drops an early rare Picto not available from any Act 1 merchant.
3Search the tide pools along the eastern edge — one contains a Tier 1 weapon variant with slightly higher base stats than the standard version.

Act 1 — Tidebreak Cove

1From Sirene, head south along the outer wall until you find a narrow cliffside path descending toward the water.
2The descent has several hidden ledges — drop carefully rather than sliding all the way down, as the unique accessory is on an intermediate ledge.
3At the bottom, a sealed chest requires solving a simple environmental puzzle (align the three coastal obelisks). Contains a Lumina fragment.

Act 2 — Sunken Spire Beach

1Unlock Esquie's swim ability (Relationship Level 6). Travel to the open water between Sirene and Visages.
2Dive underwater and look for a cave entrance on the eastern side of the submerged spire formation.
3Surface inside the cave to reach the hidden beach. Defeat the unique Gestral variant here — it guards a mid-game Picto and a cache of Tier 2 catalysts.

Act 2 — Merchant's Rest

1Swim around the eastern cliffs of Visages. Behind the outermost rock formation, find a small submerged tunnel.
2The tunnel surfaces inside a sheltered cove with a lone Gestral merchant sitting by a campfire.
3This merchant's inventory is entirely unique — items here are not sold by any other vendor in the game. Buy everything. The merchant relocates in Act 3 and the inventory changes.
💎 Critical — Merchant's Rest Inventory

The Merchant's Rest vendor sells a Lumina variant that is not available from any other source until NG+. If you miss this merchant in Act 2, you lock yourself out of that Lumina for the entire playthrough. Check the inventory thoroughly before leaving.

Act 3 — Cliffside Sanctuary

1After unlocking Esquie's fly ability, return to the Act 3 coastal region. Look for elevated ledges along the cliff faces — the sanctuary is only reachable by flying from above.
2The cove contains a secret encounter with an enhanced Gestral variant — significantly harder than the Act 2 beach enemies.
3Rewards include an endgame Lumina, Tier 3 catalysts, and a unique cosmetic item for Esquie.

5. Hazard Guide — Beach Enemies and Environmental Dangers

⚠ Gestral Sentinels

Each Act 1 beach is guarded by a Gestral sentinel — a stronger variant of the standard field enemies. They hit harder than anything else in their respective act. Do not engage at minimum level. Come back 5-10 levels above the area recommendation.

⚠ Underwater Navigation

The Act 2 beach cave entrances are easy to miss underwater. The submerged spire tunnel in particular is partially obscured by kelp. Use the photo mode camera to scan ahead if you cannot find the entrance.

⚠ Cliffside Sanctuary — Fall Damage

The Act 3 beach requires precise flying between elevated ledges. Missing a landing can cause fall damage or force you to restart the approach from sea level. Save before attempting the flight path.
