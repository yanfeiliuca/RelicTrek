Sinister Cave — One Cave, Three Hidden Paths, Four Items Most Players Never Find

🕐 Route ~45 min⚔️ Boss difficulty 3/5📍 North of Sirene (small island)⚠️ Esquie swimming required💎 4 items: Elerim + Corderon + Chalium + Nocturne📅 Updated 2026-05-31

1. Why You Need It

The first time you step into the Sinister Cave, you see something you can't reach. There's a glowing item on a ledge across the entrance — visible, obvious, completely inaccessible from where you're standing. Most players note it, move on, and never figure out how to get there.

That item is the Nocturne Pour Lumiere music record. And the cave doesn't just hide that. It hides three weapons — one for Lune, one for Sciel, one for Maelle — on separate paths, each requiring you to take a different route through the same dark cave.

The cave has two primary branches from a central fork. The left path ends quickly at Elerim (Lune's Earth weapon), on the ground in front of a petrified Nevron. The right path eventually leads to a grapple point, a cliff drop to a dead Gestral Merchant with Corderon (Sciel's Dark weapon), and further still — a hidden hole in the cave floor that drops you onto that unreachable ledge with the Nocturne. The boss — Chromatic Chalier — sits in the deepest part of the right branch, before the grapple point, dropping Chalium (Maelle's weapon) on defeat.

Most players take the left path, find Elerim, take the right path partway, fight the boss, and leave. Two weapons out of four. The other two require knowing exactly where to look.

3. Prerequisites

  • Reached Act 2 (earliest point to access Sinister Cave)
  • Esquie's underwater swimming ability unlocked (required to swim to the island north of Sirene)
  • Bring a party capable of defeating the Chromatic Chalier — she has the Berserk status and gains +5% damage per turn; weak to Light

4. Optimal Route (100% Completion)

The Sinister Cave entrance is on a small island north of Sirene. Swim there with Esquie and enter.

First: activate the Entrance Expedition Flag. Note the glowing item visible on the ledge to the right of the entrance — you can't reach it now, but you will later.

Route A — Left Branch → Elerim

1From the central open area, take the left fork. Follow the left wall. The cave narrows into tunnels — hug the left wall throughout.
2You'll reach a small chamber with a glowing petrified Nevron slumped against the rock wall. The Elerim weapon (Earth element, Lune) is on the ground in front of it.

Nothing else is down this route. Grab Elerim and backtrack to the central fork.

Route B — Right Branch → Boss → Corderon → Nocturne

1Take the right fork. Continue forward along the right path. You'll pass a lantern on a sloped path.
2Don't grapple yet. Keep going past the grapple point on the ground level. The path opens into a larger area — the arena for the Chromatic Chalier boss fight.
⚔️ Chromatic Chalier

Has Berserk status — damage increases by +5% each turn. Weak to Light. Defeat her to receive Chalium (Maelle weapon).

3Return to the grapple point after the fight. Use it to climb up the cliff face. From the top, grapple across the gap to reach the upper path. Follow the path forward.
4You'll see a lantern on a ledge ahead. Look down from the right edge — there's a drop point to a ledge below with a dead Gestral Merchant standing beside it. Drop down. The Gestral can't be spoken to (they didn't survive the weapon's curse), but Corderon (Dark element, Sciel) is in front of them.
Curse Warning

Corderon inflicts Curse on its own wielder — the dead Gestral Merchant next to it apparently learned this the hard way. Plan your build accordingly.

5Climb back up to the grapple level. From here, instead of dropping back down to the boss arena, continue north along the upper path. Follow until you reach what looks like a dead end. Look down. There's a hole in the cave floor — it's narrow and easy to miss unless you're looking straight down. Drop through it. You land on a narrow ledge.

This is the ledge with the item you saw from the entrance. Pick up Nocturne Pour Lumiere (Music Record, no. 20 of 33).

Drop off the ledge. You're back in the entrance passageway. Turn left, reach the Expedition Flag, and exit.

5. Hazard Guide

Chromatic Chalier

The Berserk status is the main danger. If the fight goes long, the Chalier's damage can reach levels that are difficult to sustain through. Bring a Light-element attack and end the fight efficiently. Lune (with the Elerim you just picked up) works well here as a Light-element user. Sciel's Dark weapons also work if you have them.

The Hole in the Floor (Route B, Third Item)

This is the only part of the cave where players consistently get stuck. After reaching the dead end at the top of Route B, the hole looks like nothing — it's a small dark patch in the cave floor. If you're moving fast, you'll walk right past it. Stop at the dead end and look straight down at the ground in front of you. The hole is there.

On Landing From the Hole

You'll drop onto a narrow ledge. Use the brake button (R1 on PlayStation) when you land to avoid rolling off the edge.
