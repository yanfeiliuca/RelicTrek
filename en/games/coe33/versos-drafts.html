1. Why You Need It

At The Game Awards 2025, when Sandfall Interactive accepted the Game of the Year award, the CEO announced a free DLC on the spot as a thank-you to players. That DLC is Verso's Drafts — a completely new zone born from Verso's childhood imagination, full of candy, toys, carousels, and Nevrons that want to end you.

The zone is accessible from Act 3. It sits southeast of Lumière, portal accessible near the giant Eiffel Tower landmark. It takes roughly 5 hours to fully clear, contains 6 Expedition Flags, a sub-zone called Root of All Evil with the final boss Osquio, and a hidden area behind a giant Gingerbread Door that requires assembling a Birthday Cake from three pieces scattered across the zone.

The reward pool includes 17 new Pictos added to the game's total, new weapons for every character, new haircuts and outfits including full Osquio outfits for all party members, and Endless Tower super-boss paintings that activate after you clear the tower's normal 33 trials.

Most players find Verso's Drafts and wander around until Nevrons delete their party. This is how to not do that.

3. Prerequisites

  • Act 3 started
  • Esquie's flying ability unlocked
  • Level 90+ strongly recommended — Verso's Drafts enemies are endgame-tier
  • Paint Break ability (from Sastro's Lost Gestral quest) helps break blue rocks in the area
  • Without the Painted Power Pictos (removes 9,999 damage cap), your damage will be capped and fights will take much longer

4. Optimal Route

Step 1 — Reach Verso's Drafts

1On the world map, fly southeast toward Lumière. Look for the giant Eiffel Tower landmark. The portal to Verso's Drafts is to the left of it. Enter. Activate the Open Playground Expedition Flag immediately on arrival.

Step 2 — First area (Open Playground → Reverie Path)

2Descend from the entry point. First enemy groups drop Pictos rewards including Longer Break. Follow the stream toward the Reverie Path Expedition Flag. You'll find Najabla, a Gestral merchant. Buy from her normal inventory (Chroma Catalysts, Colour of Lumina, rare Pictos, Esquie-themed haircuts). For Bonbim (Lune weapon), defeat her in a duel first, then buy it from the expanded inventory. Continue past Najabla along the water to find a waterfall. Behind the waterfall is a cave — the Chromatic Barbasucette boss. Defeat it to receive Sucetton (Sciel weapon). After the boss fight, cross the xylophone bridge, defeat nearby enemies, then navigate the platforming area to find the Gradient Overcharge Pictos at the end.

Step 3 — Gestral Baths area

3Proceed to the Gestral Baths Expedition Flag.
💎 Empowered Healer Pictos

Right after the Gestral Baths flag, look for boards/platforms off to the right side. Platform across to find this Pictos on a ledge.

💎 Chic Haircut for Verso

Find the hopscotch number puzzle. Step on tiles in order. At the top, step on the Esquie tile to receive the haircut.

💎 Esquie Statues Puzzle (Piece of Cake 1 of 3)

Find the 6 orange Esquie statues in the Gestral Baths area. Switch exactly 3 of them to blue — not more, not fewer. A nearby Half-baked Lifeguard NPC will reward you with the first Piece of Cake + Double Bun Haircut for Monoco.

Step 4 — Piece of Cake 2 and 3

4Piece of Cake 2 — Toy Boat quest (Reverie Path): In the Reverie Path area, find the Toy Boat quest. Complete it to receive the second Piece of Cake.
Piece of Cake 3 — Gestral Carousel: Find the Gestral Carousel. Ride it with all 5 party members (one at a time). Once all 5 have ridden, you receive the third Piece of Cake.

Once you collect all three Pieces of Cake, they automatically combine into the Birthday Cake.

Step 5 — Open the Gingerbread Door

5The Gingerbread Door with googly eyes is in the woodland area near the Gestral Baths. Bring the Birthday Cake to the door and offer it. The door opens. Inside, head up the steps to the left to enter the house. Go through the door on the right to reach a platform with a grand piano — pick up Sucreso (Verso weapon). Continue inside the Treehouse to find the Licornapieds Station Expedition Flag and the Licornapieds boss (drops Frenzy Pictos).

Step 6 — Root of All Evil (final boss area)

6Navigate to the Root of All Evil sub-zone. Osquio is the final boss of the DLC. After Osquio is defeated, a cutscene plays. A new merchant appears in front of the arena — buy the remaining Osquio Outfits and Weapons for all party members.

5. Hazard Guide

General

All enemies in Verso's Drafts are endgame-tier. If you're not seeing numbers above 9,999, get the Painted Power Pictos from Sprong (the ocean boss) before entering seriously.

Chromatic Barbasucette (waterfall cave)

Found in the alcove behind the Reverie Path waterfall. Standard Chromatic boss mechanics. No specific trigger required — just enter the cave.

Osquio (final boss, Root of All Evil)

A long endurance fight. Prioritize AoE damage, keep party healthy throughout the fight, and use Cheater Lumina for extra turns. Osquio is challenging but predictable once you learn the attack patterns.

⚠ Navigation Note — Gingerbread Door

Many players spend time looking for the door without having all three Pieces of Cake first. Do all three cake steps before returning to the door. The door will not open otherwise.
