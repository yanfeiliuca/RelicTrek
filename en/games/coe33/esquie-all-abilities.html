1. Why You Need It

Esquie is not just the party's world-map mount. Every major late-game hidden route in Expedition 33 is locked behind one of Esquie's traversal gates: coral swimming, deep-water access, flight, or the Paint Break route tied to Sastro's Lost Gestrals quest.

If you unlock these abilities in the wrong order and never backtrack, the game quietly leaves whole zones untouched: The Abyss, Flying Manor, Endless Tower, Sinister Cave, and several Perfect Chroma Catalyst routes.

This page is the cleanup plan: when each gate becomes useful, what it opens, and which older markers you should revisit before pushing deeper into Act 3.

Route Note

Paint Break is not technically an Esquie movement mode, but it belongs in the same route because it is the other major map gate. Treat it as part of your traversal checklist.

3. Prerequisites

  • Reach Gestral Village and Esquie's Nest naturally through the story.
  • Complete Old Lumiere before doing the Act 2 island sweep; several routes need coral swimming.
  • Start Sastro's Lost Gestrals quest early and return to camp after the 4th Gestral to unlock Paint Break.
  • Keep a written or mental list of blue-vein rocks, unreachable islands, and floating structures you saw earlier.
  • Before entering final Act 3 superboss routes, make sure both flying and Paint Break are available.

4. Optimal Route

1Act 1: find the first two Lost Gestrals as you pass Esquie's Nest and Stone Wave Cliffs. Do not try to force a full cleanup yet; the map is still heavily gated.
2Early Act 2: after Old Lumiere, use coral swimming to collect Lost Gestrals 3-6 and clear nearby coastal routes such as The Crows and Sinister Cave.
3After Gestral 4: return to Sastro immediately. Paint Break only unlocks after you report back; the game does not grant it automatically.
4Immediate backtrack: revisit blue-vein rocks and blocked side paths before your memory of them fades. This is where many hidden items become available.
5Act 3 flight: sweep floating routes in one pass: Floating Cemetery, Flying Manor, Endless Tower, and the final three Lost Gestrals.
6Deep-water cleanup: once underwater portals are available, route Renoir's Drafts and The Abyss for Simoso and catalyst rewards.

5. Hazard Guide

Do Not Forget Sastro

Finding the 4th Lost Gestral is not enough. You must report back to Sastro to receive Paint Break. Missing this step is the most common reason players think blue-vein rocks are still locked.

Do Not Save All Backtracking for the End

By late Act 3, the number of open routes becomes too large to remember cleanly. Backtrack once after coral swimming, once after Paint Break, and once after flight.

Do Not Enter Secret Boss Routes Underprepared

Traversal access does not mean combat readiness. The Abyss, Flying Manor, and Endless Tower are all accessible before many parties are ready to clear them safely.
