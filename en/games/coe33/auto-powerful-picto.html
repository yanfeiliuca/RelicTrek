Auto Powerful Picto — The Dying Warrior's Final Gift

1. Why You Need It

In Act 3, on a floating island the game never sends you to, a lone Chalier — one of the armored Nevron warriors you've fought dozens of times — is waiting for something different.

He doesn't want to fight you to the death because you stumbled into his territory. He's been thinking about it. He wants one last worthy opponent, one final battle fought with intention, and then he wants to die with dignity before the urge to fight and hurt consumes him completely. He asks you to choose a weapon for him. And when the fight is over and he's defeated, he asks you to fulfill his wish.

If you agree — if you grant the Chalier what he asked for — he gives you the Auto Powerful Picto.

Auto Powerful is, in Maxroll's assessment, "the best Picto in the game, hands down — so good it's practically mandatory." At the start of every battle, it automatically applies the Powerful buff to the character who has it equipped. That buff lasts for three full turns, amplifying all damage output without any action cost. Every fight, every battle, the first three turns hit harder before you've even done anything.

The Floating Cemetery doesn't appear on any quest log. No NPC mentions it by name. It sits west of Monoco's Station, above the ocean, invisible until you fly over it. Most players who finish the game never land there.

2. What Auto Powerful Actually Does

Picto type: Active/Passive
How to equip it: Standard Picto slot (one of three per character)
Lumina unlock: Win four battles with it equipped → learn as a permanent Lumina passive

EffectDescription
Auto PowerfulAt the start of every battle, automatically applies the Powerful buff for 3 turns — no action required
Powerful buffAmplifies the character's damage output for the duration

Why it's considered mandatory:
The Powerful buff normally requires a character to spend an action to apply it. Auto Powerful removes that cost entirely. Three turns of free damage amplification means your opening burst hits harder, your AP isn't spent on setup, and your team can focus purely on attack rotation from turn one. Stacked with offensive Luminas and high-damage builds, those first three turns often determine whether a hard fight ends quickly or drags on.

Who benefits most: Any character with a high-damage opening rotation — particularly Verso (Simoso builds), Maelle (Medalum one-shot builds), and Lune (elemental burst builds). In practice, the best characters in the game benefit from it, which is why it's recommended for everyone.

3. Prerequisites

  • Act 3 has started — the Floating Cemetery requires Act 3 access
  • Esquie's flying ability unlocked — required to reach the floating island
  • Optional: Complete the White Nevron quest chain (Blanche) before coming here if you want both the chain's final reward AND Auto Powerful Picto — both are possible in one playthrough

4. Optimal Route

Step 1 — Reach the Floating Cemetery

Open the world map. Locate Monoco's Station. Fly west from there with Esquie. You should see a floating island in the air — the Floating Cemetery. Land on it and enter through the portal.

Unlock the Floating Cemetery – Entrance Expedition Flag.

Step 2 — Collect items before the fight

Don't go straight to the Chalier. Instead:

  • Take the wooden walkway to the right of the main path and use the grapple point. Climb the handholds and use the next grapple to reach the attic of the building → pick up Colour of Lumina
  • Drop into the building interior
  • Before approaching the Chalier inside the mansion, exit left through the archway and pick up another Colour of Lumina
  • In the back of the room, grab the Recoat from the cabinet

Step 3 — Find the Chalier

Follow the left path from the entrance down past the pile of dead expeditioners. You'll find the Chalier standing there. Speak with him.

He'll ask you to choose his weapon: sword, spear, or hammer. Your choice affects only which unique attack he uses during the fight. The spear and hammer tend to be the easiest to deal with. Choose whatever you prefer.

He tells you to meet him inside the building.

Step 4 — Fight the Chalier

Enter the building and face the Chalier. This fight is not particularly difficult compared to the rest of Act 3 content — he fights with intent, not desperation. Focus on parrying his combos and bring the fight to a clean finish.

On defeat, he drops a Longer Shell Pictos upgrade (Lv. 19).

Step 5 — ⚠️ The Critical Choice

Speak with the defeated Chalier. He explains why he wanted this fight. He was losing control of his instinct to harm — and he wanted to end things on his own terms, while he still could.

He asks you to kill him.

You must choose: Grant his request — or refuse.

→ Grant his request: He thanks you and gives you the Auto Powerful Picto before he falls.
→ Refuse: He's disappointed. You leave. When you come back, he's taken his own life and left 11,680 Chroma on the ground.

Choose to grant his request. The Picto is the relic.

Step 6 — Collect remaining items

After the Chalier quest is resolved, head to the back and exit to the right of the building → follow the deck → grapple across the gap to a small floating island → climb handholds → grapple to the mansion from the left side → make your way to the front of the mansion → pick up the final Colour of Lumina.

Then return to the world map.

5. Hazard Guide

The only real danger here isn't the Chalier — it's the choice.

The fight itself is manageable for anyone who has reached Act 3. The Chalier is strong but not a superboss; he has a limited combo pattern and telegraphs his attacks clearly.

The actual risk:

  • Choosing to spare him means you walk away with Chroma instead of the Picto. The Picto is gone from that save file.
  • Killing other White Nevrons before visiting Blanche affects the chain quest reward — but does NOT affect your ability to get Auto Powerful. You can grant the Chalier's wish and still complete the White Nevron chain's final reward, as long as you visit Blanche first.
One-Sentence Rule

Fight him, beat him, and when he asks — say yes.
