1. Why You Need It

North of Spring Waters, directly off the main path as you enter The Continent overworld for the first time, there's a small cave with an Expedition Flag at the entrance. Most players walk past it. The few who enter immediately discover that the single enemy inside — the Chromatic Abbest — is dramatically stronger than anything they've encountered in Act 1.

The cave is not labeled dangerous at this point. There's no warning. You just walk in, and a fluffy pink boss with an orb in its forehead tries to one-shot your party.

Wait until level 20–30. Come back. Defeat the Chromatic Abbest. Walk out with the First Offensive Pictos.

At only 5 Lumina Points, First Offensive is one of the most cost-efficient damage Pictos in the game. It gives the first hit any character deals each fight a 50% damage bonus. That's it. 50% more damage on your opening strike, guaranteed, for 5 LP.

The drawback is symmetrical: the first hit your character receives also deals 50% more damage to them. In practice, this means First Offensive is best paired with shields, defensive Luminas, or builds that act before enemies do — all of which are exactly what endgame builds are built around anyway.

In the famous endgame one-turn builds documented by the community (combining First Offensive + Auto Powerful + Cheater + high-damage weapons), First Offensive is a foundation piece. It's accessible in the first act of the game, and most players don't know the cave exists.

2. What First Offensive Does

Pictos: First Offensive · Stats bonus when equipped: Speed ↑, Critical Rate ↑ · Active effect: First hit dealt = +50% damage. First hit received = +50% damage. · Lumina cost: 5 LP (among the cheapest in the game for this power level)

SynergyEffect
+ Shields / Shell LuminasAbsorb the incoming first hit — negating the drawback entirely while keeping the +50% bonus
+ Speed investmentAct before enemies → your +50% hit lands before they can hit you
+ Auto PowerfulPowerful buff amplifies the already-boosted first strike further
+ One-shot buildsFirst Offensive + Simoso + Cheater + Glass Canon is a documented endgame build

Why the 5 LP cost matters: Most strong damage-boosting Luminas cost 8–15+ LP. First Offensive delivers one of the highest per-hit damage bonuses in the game for 5 LP — leaving LP budget for other offensive Luminas simultaneously.

3. Prerequisites

  • Reached The Continent overworld (available from Act 1 progression)
  • Level 20–30+ recommended — the Chromatic Abbest hits for 1,500+ per attack and can one-shot Act 1 characters
  • Sciel in party (Dark damage dealer) — Chromatic Abbest is weak to Dark primarily, Light secondarily
  • Can be reached without any Esquie abilities — on foot from The Continent portal

4. Optimal Route

Step 1 — Find the Abbest Cave

1From the portal entrance to The Continent (overworld), follow the path to the left. The Abbest Cave is immediately north of Spring Waters — a small cave with an Expedition Flag at the entrance, shortly after you first enter the overworld. Activate the Abbest Cave — Entrance Expedition Flag.

Step 2 — Assess the threat

2The cave contains a single enemy: the Chromatic Abbest. Its health is approximately 85,000 HP. If you're arriving in Act 1 at level 10–15, this fight is almost certainly not survivable. Note the location and return later. Return when your party reaches level 20–30+. The fight is manageable at this level and becomes straightforward from level 40 onward.

5. The Chromatic Abbest Fight: Hazard Guide

Weakness: Dark (primary), Light (secondary) · Resistance: Physical · Weak Point: Glowing orb in the center of its forehead — Free Aim shots deal massive damage here

Recommended party: Sciel (Dark skills: Phantom Blade, Twilight Slash, Grim Harvest) as primary DPS. Maelle can supplement with Percée on Marked targets (especially in Virtuose Stance). Bring healing items.

Attack Patterns

⚠ Ground Combo (2 hits)

Approaches the party slowly, leans back (first strike), then repeats the same motion noticeably faster (second strike). The timing shift between slow and fast is the main risk.

⚠ Air Combo (3 hits)

Leaps upward then slashes downward three times. Wait until the attack blade has fully formed before pressing parry — the timing is slightly later than you expect.

⚠ Ice Pillar Spell (3 eruptions)

Casts a spell that shoots three ice pillars from the ground. Parry on the second pillar's disappearance, not the third. This counterintuitive timing catches many players.

⚠ Headbutt / Wind Blade (1-2 hits)

Winds up and attacks once or twice. Straightforward timing.

Key Strategy

  • Prioritize the weak-point orb with Free Aim shots for accelerated break-bar buildup
  • Apply Dark damage consistently with Sciel
  • Keep a defensive character ready to revive — the boss takes multiple turns per round, and Sciel at low health is vulnerable
  • The fight is long if you avoid the orb; it's manageable if you target it

6. En-Route Bonus Items

The Abbest Cave has only the boss and the flag — no other items inside the cave itself.
