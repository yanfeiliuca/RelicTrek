1. Why You Need It

The existing Flying Manor & Clea guide focuses on the boss chain and the locked door. This page is the broader sweep route: the order to clear the manor without leaving side rooms, catalysts, weapons, and post-boss rewards behind.

Most players find the floating glass pyramid, fight a few enemies, and leave because the central door looks decorative. It is not. The Manor is a structured gauntlet: clear four trials, reopen the center, collect everything, then decide whether your party is ready for Clea.

3. Prerequisites

  • Act 3 started and Esquie's flying ability unlocked.
  • Party level 75+ for the outer Manor sweep; 85+ recommended before Clea.
  • All six party members geared. The Manor does not demand six characters every fight, but Clea punishes narrow builds.
  • At least one reliable breaker, one sustained healer, and one damage dealer who can exploit boss openings after parries.
  • Save at every Expedition Flag before committing to the next boss room.

4. Optimal Route

1Land at the glass pyramid. From the Forgotten Battlefield region, fly toward the large floating glass structure west of a Painting Workshop entrance. Activate the first flag before exploring.
2Sweep the central plaza first. Note the locked central door, side corridors, balcony routes, and any NPC markers. Do not rush into a wing until you understand how to return to the plaza.
3Clear the four trial wings. The bosses can be handled in flexible order. After each one, return through the plaza and check whether a reward path or shortcut opened.
4Collect pre-Clea rewards. Before the center door, do another loop through the rooms you passed quickly: upper ledges, side stairs, and any suspicious dead ends.
5Open the central door. Once all four trials are down, the door is no longer a mystery prop. Enter only after saving and setting your Clea party.
6Use the dedicated Clea guide for the fight. The boss specifics are already covered in Flying Manor & Clea; this page keeps the full-area route clean.

5. Hazard Guide

Do Not Treat the Door as Decoration

The central door is the Manor's main gate. If it stays locked, one of the four trials is still uncleared or a return path has been missed.

Do Not Spend Catalysts Before Choosing Builds

The Manor gives rare upgrade materials. Decide which character-specific weapons actually support your party before pushing anything to level 33.

Clea Is a Separate Difficulty Spike

Finishing the four wings means you can reach Clea; it does not mean your party is ready. If your parry timing is shaky, leave, upgrade, then return.
