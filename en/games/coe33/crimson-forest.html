1. Why the Crimson Forest Matters

The Crimson Forest is one of the densest endgame zones in Clair Obscur: Expedition 33. Accessible only after acquiring Esquie's fly ability in Act 3, this area is packed with high-value collectibles — Paint Cages, Paint Spikes, rare Lumina drops, and a secret mini-boss — concentrated into a relatively compact space.

Most players rush through the Crimson Forest en route to the next story objective and miss 60-70% of what the zone actually contains. The canopy layers, vertical cliffs, and hidden cave systems mean that simply walking the main path reveals less than half the available loot. You need to fly to elevated ledges, drop into hidden ravines, and backtrack through previously inaccessible areas once you have the full Act 3 movement toolkit.

If you are preparing for the Paintress, Simon, Clea, or the Endless Tower, the Crimson Forest is where you stock up on endgame resources. Skip it, and you enter those fights with fewer tools than the developers expected.

3. Prerequisites

  • Act 3 main story progressed to the point where the Crimson Forest becomes accessible
  • Esquie's fly ability unlocked — mandatory. Without flight, roughly half the zone's content is unreachable
  • Recommended party level: 70+ (field enemies hit hard; the secret mini-boss is tuned for 80+)
  • Weapons at Level 20-28 (Tier 3 materials should already be stockpiled)
  • At least one character with elemental coverage for the forest's Gestral variants (they have modified elemental resistances)

4. Optimal Route — Full Clear Path

Entry and Ground-Level Sweep

1Enter the Crimson Forest from the main Act 3 path. Activate the Expedition Flag at the entrance — this is your fast-travel anchor.
2Follow the main path north, clearing field enemies. Collect ground-level Paint Cages (4 are visible from the main road).
3At the first major fork, take the left path toward the ravine. You will find the entrance to a hidden cave system behind a destructible wall.

Canopy Layer — Flight Required

4Return to the forest entrance and switch to Esquie. Fly to the elevated ledges visible along the eastern cliff face.
5The first elevated ledge contains 2 Paint Spikes and a rare Lumina drop from a unique enemy variant.
6Continue flying north along the canopy. The second cluster of elevated platforms contains 2 Paint Cages and a Paint Spike.
7The highest platform — reachable only from the northernmost launch point — holds a Lumina not found elsewhere in the zone.

Secret Mini-Boss

8From the central canopy platform, drop down into the ravine below (do not glide — drop straight down into the narrow crevice).
9At the bottom of the ravine, activate the hidden Expedition Flag. The mini-boss arena is directly ahead.
10Defeat the secret mini-boss for an exclusive Picto and Tier 3 catalyst drops. This boss does not respawn — one kill per playthrough.
💎 Mini-Boss Reward

The secret mini-boss drops the Crimson Bloom Picto — a unique Picto only available from this encounter. It does not appear in any merchant inventory or as a field drop. One chance per playthrough.

Gestral Beach Entrance

11From the northernmost canopy platform, fly east toward the coastline. A hidden cave entrance in the cliff face leads to the Cliffside Sanctuary — one of the 5 Gestral beaches.

5. Hazard Guide — Enemies and Environmental Threats

⚠ Forest Gestral Variants

The Crimson Forest Gestral variants have modified elemental resistances compared to standard Act 3 enemies. Fire-based attacks are less effective here — the forest's theme reduces fire damage. Switch to Light or physical damage Pictos before engaging.

⚠ Canopy Flight — Fall Damage

The canopy platforms require precise landings. Missing a landing while flying between platforms results in a fall to the forest floor and significant damage. Save before attempting long flights between platforms.

⚠ Ravine Drop — One-Way

The drop into the secret mini-boss ravine is one-way. You cannot fly back out — the only exit is a tunnel at the far end of the ravine that leads back to the forest entrance. Be prepared for the boss fight before dropping. There is an Expedition Flag in the ravine, so you can save before engaging.

⚠ High-Density Enemy Packs

The Crimson Forest packs more enemies per square meter than any other Act 3 zone. It is easy to aggro 2-3 packs simultaneously if you are not careful with positioning. Pull enemies one pack at a time — do not rush through the center of the zone.

💎 Pro Tip — Farming Route

The Crimson Forest is one of the best Tier 3 catalyst farming zones in Act 3. A loop from the entrance → cave system → canopy platforms → ravine exit resets enemy spawns and yields 3-5 Tier 3 catalysts per run. Repeatable until you have enough for all weapons to reach Level 28.
