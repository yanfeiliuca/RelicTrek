1. Why You Need It

Music Records unlock tracks in the game's in-game phonograph, accessible at camp. The soundtrack of Clair Obscur was one of the most praised aspects of the game, and several tracks — including pieces tied to specific characters and events — are only accessible by finding their corresponding Record.

Complete collection is required for 100% completion and specific achievements. Several Records are found behind Manor Doors — making the Manor route (see Manor Complete Route guide) a prerequisite for music completion. If you care about the full auditory experience of Clair Obscur, every Record matters.

2. What You're Collecting — Confirmed Record Locations

Each Music Record is hidden in a specific location. Several are behind Manor Doors and require the full Manor Complete Route to access.

Additional Records are found through Gestral Village exploration (early Act 1 records), Verso's Drafts DLC (Thank You Update — new records added), specific side quest completions, and as a reward from completing the dining hall switch puzzle sequence.

3. Prerequisites

  • Manor Complete Route — required for all Manor Door records; see the Manor Complete Route guide as a prerequisite
  • Esquie fly ability — required for the Forlorn record (island southeast of Stone Wave Cliffs Cave) and the Clea! record (Glacial Falls flag area)
  • Esquie dive ability — required for Verso's Drafts DLC records (Thank You Update content)
  • Specific side quest completions — some records are quest rewards
  • Dining hall switch puzzle completion — one record is a puzzle reward

4. Optimal Route — Record-by-Record Retrieval

Follow the Manor Complete Route as the foundational prerequisite — several records are behind Manor Doors and cannot be accessed without it. Then retrieve each record in sequence.

Forlorn — The Overworld Island Record

1With Esquie's fly ability, fly to the small island southeast of Stone Wave Cliffs Cave (overworld).
2Locate and enter the Manor Door on the island.
3Inside: navigate the hidden room with enemy statues to find the Forlorn Record.

Renoir — The Old Lumiere Record

4Travel to Old Lumiere and head to the Manor Gardens expedition flag.
5Enter through the wall crack near the Manor Gardens flag to access the Manor Door.
6Inside: navigate to the Manor bathroom to find the Renoir Record.

L'Amour D'une Mere — The Monolith Record

7Enter the Monolith dungeon and follow the path to the grave path area.
8Locate and enter the Manor Door along the grave path.
9Inside: the door leads to the Manor greenhouse room where the L'Amour D'une Mere Record is found.

Clea! Don't Pull Your Sister's Hair! — The Frozen Hearts Record

10Travel to Frozen Hearts and head to the Glacial Falls expedition flag.
11Navigate past the icicles, use the grapple to cross the gap, then descend.
12Enter the Manor Door to reach the Manor bedroom where the Clea! Record is found.

Additional Records

13Thoroughly explore Gestral Village in Act 1 — early records are hidden in village interiors and side paths.
14Complete the dining hall switch puzzle sequence for a record reward.
15Complete all relevant side quests — several records are quest rewards.
16Enter Verso's Drafts DLC (Thank You Update) for additional records — requires Esquie dive ability.

5. Hazard Guide — Missable and Locked-Out Records

⚠ Missable — Story-Locked Records

Several Records are in areas that become inaccessible after certain story points. If you advance the main story past key thresholds without collecting the Records in the preceding zones, you may be locked out until New Game+ or a post-game revisit.

⚠ Ability-Locked Records

The Forlorn Record requires Esquie fly — if you reach the island location without fly, the Manor Door will be visible but inaccessible. The Verso's Drafts Records require Esquie dive. Ensure you have the required abilities before attempting these locations.

⚠ Manor Door Dependency

All four named Records (Forlorn, Renoir, L'Amour, Clea!) are behind Manor Doors. The Manor Complete Route guide is the prerequisite that unlocks access to every door. Without completing the Manor route, these Records cannot be reached.

Tracking tip: check the camp phonograph regularly. The phonograph menu shows which Records are still missing — use it to track your collection progress and identify which Records remain unfound.
