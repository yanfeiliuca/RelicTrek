Weapon Upgrade Guide — Tier 1 Through Tier 4 Materials, What Each Requires, and Where to Find Them

1. Why You Need This Guide

Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 has a four-tier weapon upgrade system that spans the entire game. Most players understand the basics — kill enemies, collect catalysts, upgrade weapons — but the system has hidden depth that directly determines whether you walk into endgame fights like Simon and Clea with Level 33 weapons or get stuck at Level 28 wondering what you missed.

This guide covers all four tiers from Level 1 to the Level 33 cap. Tiers 1-3 are covered comprehensively here (no dedicated guide exists for those levels). Tier 4 (Perfect Chroma Catalysts) is summarized with a reference to the dedicated perfect-chroma-catalysts guide for full acquisition details.

The difference between a Level 28 weapon and a Level 33 weapon is the difference between a 20-minute Clea fight and a 5-minute Clea fight. Do not skip the final upgrade tier.

2. Upgrade System Overview

Every weapon in Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 follows the same upgrade path. There are no branching upgrade trees, no mutually exclusive paths — the system is linear. What changes between tiers is which catalyst you need and where you find it.

📋 Scope Note

This guide covers Tiers 1-3 in full — the levels that have no dedicated existing guide. Tier 4 (Perfect Chroma Catalysts) is covered in depth by the existing perfect-chroma-catalysts guide. This guide references that content and explains how the tiers connect.

3. Prerequisites — What You Need Before Upgrading

  • Base weapon acquired (story progression, merchant purchase, or boss drop)
  • Corresponding tier catalyst in inventory
  • Access to a Gestral Merchant or upgrade station (available in every major hub)
  • Sufficient currency (cost increases with each tier)

Tiers 1-2 have minimal currency costs. Tier 3 costs increase noticeably. Tier 4 requires a significant currency investment per weapon — plan your economy accordingly before entering the Flying Manor.

4. Optimal Upgrade Route — Tier by Tier

Tier 1 — Standard Chroma Catalyst (Levels 1–10)

Standard Chroma Catalyst

The foundational upgrade material. Widely available from field enemies throughout Acts 1-3, standard Gestral Merchants, and common treasure chests. No special routing is needed — every player accumulates these naturally during normal gameplay.

📍 All Acts — field enemies
🏪 Gestral Merchants
📦 Common treasure chests
⚠ Do Not Sell

Early-game Chroma Catalysts may seem worthless once you pass Tier 1, but they are used for new weapons acquired later. Never sell or discard any catalyst. Inventory space is not limited.

Tier 2 — Dark / Sinister Chroma Catalyst (Levels 10–20)

Dark / Sinister Chroma Catalyst

Named after the Sinister Cave area where they are most densely concentrated. Also found from specific Act 2 optional dungeon Nevrons and hidden chests in the Falling Leaves region.

🕳️ Sinister Cave (Act 2)
🍂 Falling Leaves — hidden chests
⚔️ Act 2 optional dungeon Nevrons
📋 Route Priority

Complete the Sinister Cave thoroughly before moving to Act 3. This dungeon has the highest density of Tier 2 materials in the entire game. A full clear typically yields enough catalysts for 3-4 weapons to reach Level 20.

Tier 3 — Enhanced Chroma Catalyst (Levels 20–28)

Enhanced Chroma Catalyst

The first tier that requires active farming rather than passive accumulation. Found from Act 3 field enemies, Gestral Merchants in Act 3 flying zones (accessible after Esquie fly ability), and from completing side quests in the post-Paintress phase.

🌍 Act 3 field enemies
🦅 Flying zone merchants
📜 Post-Paintress side quests

By the time you reach Act 3, Tier 1 and 2 materials should already be stocked from passive accumulation. Focus farming on Tier 3 during the normal Act 3 exploration phase — you need enough to bring your entire main party to Level 28 before entering the Flying Manor.

Tier 4 — Perfect Chroma Catalyst (Levels 28–33 MAX)

Perfect Chroma Catalyst

The final upgrade tier. Drops exclusively from the four Flying Manor sub-bosses: Goblu, Lampmaster, Évêque, and Dualliste. One catalyst from each — exactly four per playthrough. See the Perfect Chroma Catalysts guide for full acquisition details.

🏰 Flying Manor sub-bosses only
⚠ 4 per playthrough max
⚡ Levels 28 → 33
💎 Routing Tip — Upgrade After Each Sub-Boss

The Flying Manor sub-bosses are prerequisites for the Clea fight. Upgrade your priority weapons immediately after each sub-boss kill, before proceeding to the next. This ensures your weapons reach Level 33 before entering the Clea encounter — the difference between a manageable fight and a brutal one.

5. Hazard Guide — Common Upgrade Mistakes

⚠ Mistake #1 — Selling Early Catalysts

Standard Chroma Catalysts look like junk once you are in Act 3. They are not. Every new weapon you acquire — including endgame weapons like Simoso — starts at Level 1 and needs Tier 1 materials to begin its upgrade path. If you sold all your Standard Catalysts, you will be farming Act 1 enemies at Level 80.

⚠ Mistake #2 — Skipping Sinister Cave

The Sinister Cave is optional. Skipping it means you enter Act 3 with weapons stuck at Level 10 because Tier 2 catalysts are extremely scarce outside this dungeon. The hour you spend fully clearing the cave saves multiple hours of scrounging for scraps later.

⚠ Mistake #3 — Upgrading Too Many Weapons Evenly

Perfect Chroma Catalysts are limited to 4 per playthrough. You cannot bring 6 weapons to Level 33. Choose 3-4 priority weapons (main party) for Tier 4 upgrades. Reserve party members can stay at Level 28 — the gap between 28 and 33 only matters for characters you actively use in the hardest fights.

⚠ Mistake #4 — Entering Clea Below Level 33

The Clea fight is balanced around Level 33 weapons. Entering at Level 28 means roughly 15-20% less damage output — which translates to a significantly longer fight and more opportunities for party-wiping mistakes. Upgrade before you engage.

6. Bonus — Upgrade Priority Order

If you are limited on catalysts, upgrade weapons in this order:

💎 Pro Tip — New Game+ Catalyst Reset

Perfect Chroma Catalysts are limited to 4 per playthrough. If you want to bring all 6 party members to Level 33, your only option is New Game+ — catalyst drops reset, giving you another 4 Perfect Chroma Catalysts in your second run.
