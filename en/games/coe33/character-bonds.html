1. Why Relationship Levels Matter

The relationship system in Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 is not optional flavor text — it gates actual gameplay content. Esquie's dive ability (Level 6) and fly ability (Level 7) are locked behind relationship progression. Maelle's Level 7 bond is the trigger for the Alicia solo fight. Several characters unlock unique Lumina, Pictos, and combat passives at higher relationship tiers.

If you ignore the relationship system, you will complete the game without ever accessing Simon (cannot dive to Renoir's Drafts), Alicia (Maelle not at Level 7), the Act 3 flying zones (no fly), and the hidden Gestral beaches (no swim). Relationship levels are the single most impactful non-combat system in the game, and they are almost entirely dependent on dialogue choices — not combat performance.

Every character can reach Level 7 in a single playthrough, but several bonding events are permanently missable if you progress past their act window. This guide ensures you do not miss a single one.

2. Relationship System Overview

Each character has a relationship level from 1 to 7. Points are earned through:

  • Dialogue choices during story and camp scenes — the primary source of relationship points
  • Side quest completion — specific quests tied to each character grant bonus points
  • Hidden bonding events — optional conversations that appear only under specific conditions (time of day, party composition, location)
  • Combat participation — characters gain small amounts of passive relationship XP when they are in the active party during fights

Dialogue choices are heavily weighted. A single "correct" choice can grant as many points as 10+ hours of passive combat participation. Choose carefully.

3. Prerequisites — How to Not Miss Bonding Events

  • Check your relationship tracker after every major story beat — any character lagging behind is a sign you missed a dialogue opportunity
  • Rotate your active party during exploration — passive combat XP adds up over a full playthrough
  • Return to camp between every major story segment — hidden bonding events trigger at camp, not during exploration
  • Do not rush Act transitions — some bonding events are only available during a narrow window within an act

4. Character-by-Character Level 7 Routes

Verso — The Protagonist

Verso's relationship progression is the most straightforward — as the protagonist, he receives automatic points from main story completion. The key is not missing dialogue opportunities during camp scenes.

Key Dialogue Moments

Verso's relationship hinges on responses that show leadership and emotional honesty. Avoid dismissive or cold dialogue options.

💬 Act 1 camp — choose "We move together"
💬 Act 2 camp — choose "I understand your fear"
⚠ Missable: Act 3 camp scene before the Paintress — must trigger before entering the Monolith

Maelle — The Burst Duelist (Alicia Fight Unlock)

Maelle is the most important relationship to max. Level 7 is the only way to trigger the Alicia solo fight, which drops an exclusive Picto available nowhere else.

Key Dialogue Moments

Maelle responds to strength, resolve, and directness. Hesitation or pacifism loses points. Always choose the option that shows confidence in her abilities.

💬 Act 1 — "You're ready for this"
💬 Act 2 — "I trust you to handle it"
⚠ Act 3 camp scene AFTER Paintress defeat — confirms Level 7 bond and triggers Alicia's appearance at the Monolith
⚠ Critical — Do Not Miss Maelle Level 7

Maelle's Level 7 bond must be achieved BEFORE defeating the Paintress. The post-Paintress camp scene is the final confirmation trigger for Alicia. If Maelle is at Level 6 or below at that point, the Alicia fight is permanently locked for this playthrough.

Sciel — The Healer

Sciel's bond grows through empathetic dialogue choices and by keeping her in the active party during significant story fights. She gains bonus points when she heals party members during boss encounters — actively use her healing abilities rather than relying on items.

Key Dialogue Moments

Choose compassionate responses. Sciel's bond decreases if you consistently choose aggressive or dismissive dialogue with other party members in her presence.

💬 Act 1 — "We need your light"
💬 Act 3 — Side quest at Sirene infirmary (missable if Act 3 progresses too far)

Esquie — Movement Abilities (Critical)

Esquie is the second most important relationship. Level 6 unlocks swim/dive (required for Renoir's Drafts, Simon, and 2 Gestral beaches). Level 7 unlocks fly (required for Crimson Forest canopy, Cliffside Sanctuary beach, and Act 3 flying zone merchants).

Key Dialogue Moments

Esquie's bond is tied to exploration — he gains passive points when you discover new locations. Dialogue choices that encourage curiosity and adventure give the largest boosts.

💬 Act 2 — "Let's see what's out there" (triggers swim ability quest at Level 6)
💬 Act 3 — "The sky's the limit" (triggers fly ability quest at Level 7)
🗺️ Hidden: Discovering all 5 Gestral beaches grants a large bonus bond point pool
📋 Tip — Rush Esquie to Level 6

Prioritize Esquie's relationship early in Act 2. The sooner you unlock swim/dive, the sooner you can access the Act 2 Gestral beaches and Renoir's Drafts. Delaying Esquie's bond progression delays half the game's hidden content.

Lune — Elemental Specialist

Lune's bond grows through elemental combat achievements and specific camp dialogue about the nature of the Gestral and the Continent's history. She responds to intellectual curiosity.

Key Dialogue Moments
💬 Act 2 — Camp discussion about the Gestral — choose the analytical response
⚔️ Bonus points for defeating enemies with elemental weaknesses

Gustave — The Tank

Gustave's bond increases through protective actions — guarding party members in combat and choosing dialogue that prioritizes the group's safety over individual glory.

Key Dialogue Moments
💬 Act 1 — "We protect each other"
💬 Act 2 — Side quest to recover a lost expedition member (missable)
🛡️ Passive: guarding party members during boss fights grants relationship XP

⚠ Act Transition Warning

Before ending any act, check the relationship tracker for every character. If anyone is below the expected level for that point in the game, you missed a bonding event. Reload an earlier save if necessary — the time lost is far less than the time required for a full NG+ replay just to fix missed bonds.

💎 Pro Tip — Max All Bonds in One Playthrough

The key to maxing all 6 bonds in a single run: (1) rotate your active party constantly during exploration, (2) always return to camp between story beats, (3) never skip dialogue — every camp conversation matters, (4) complete every side quest before advancing the act. With this approach, all characters can reach Level 7 by the post-Paintress phase without grinding.
