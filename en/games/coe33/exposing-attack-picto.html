Exposing Attack Picto — The Rarest Miss in Act 1

1. Why You Need It

Out of 210 Pictos in Clair Obscur: Expedition 33, exactly two are permanently missable. Exposing Attack is one of them. Auto Powerful is the other.

Most players meet Noco — the small Gestral merchant — in the Flying Waters area during Act 1. He appears outside The Manor after your party recruits the third member. He's cheerful, chatty, and happy to sell you things. Then the story moves forward, you head toward Gestral Village, and Noco follows. When you find him at the Village, he's no longer a merchant. He just talks. His shop, and everything in it, is gone.

Exposing Attack can only be purchased from Noco. And it can only be purchased from his Flying Waters location — specifically from his expanded inventory, unlocked by beating him in a duel.

If you bought from his normal stock and moved on without fighting him, it's gone. If you walked past him entirely, it's gone. There is no other merchant selling it. There is no enemy dropping it. There is no NG+ recovery. Permanently gone.

Exposing Attack debuffs an enemy after hitting them, increasing the damage they take from the next attack that hits. In burst-damage builds, this means the second hit in a sequence lands harder than it otherwise would.

2. What Exposing Attack Does

Pictos: Exposing Attack
Effect: Your attack exposes the enemy — they take increased damage from the next hit they receive
Application: Works on any attack that connects; the exposed debuff is applied to the enemy, not to your character

This Pictos is especially effective in combination with multi-hit skills, high-damage follow-up characters (Verso, Maelle), and builds that exploit the gap between setup and payoff hits.

3. The Exact Window

Noco is available as a merchant only in Flying Waters, outside The Manor — and only after the third party member has been recruited but before you leave for Gestral Village.

His journey through the game:

  1. Flying Waters, outside The Manor ← ONLY TIME HE SELLS ANYTHING
  2. Gestral Village — present but no longer a merchant (dialogue only)
  3. Camp — arrives during Act 2 after Monoco is recruited; dialogue about a treasure for Verso
  4. Dies during story events
  5. Sacred River (Act 3) — can be resurrected via Monoco's relationship quest at level 7; a nearby merchant sells some items, but NOT Exposing Attack

Do not leave Flying Waters without checking Noco's shop and unlocking his hidden inventory.

4. Optimal Route

1Find Noco. In Flying Waters, after the third party member joins, Noco appears outside The Manor. The nearest checkpoint is marked "Noco's Hut" — look for him near this flag.
2Fight Noco first (required). Noco has a "Fight the Merchant" option like all Gestral merchants. You must beat him in a 1v1 duel to unlock the expanded inventory where Exposing Attack is sold. The fight is straightforward at Act 1 levels.
3Buy Exposing Attack. After the duel, his hidden inventory opens. Purchase Exposing Attack Pictos immediately. While you're here, buy everything else from his normal inventory you want — once you leave this area, his shop closes forever.

5. Hazard Guide

The Only Danger Is Not Knowing the Window Exists

There is no combat hazard — Noco is an easy fight at Act 1 levels. The real risk is time. The window closes the moment you leave Flying Waters for Gestral Village. There's no quest marker. No dialogue suggesting you check his shop. You have to know.

If you've already passed this point on your current save: Unfortunately, Exposing Attack cannot be recovered. It is one of the game's two confirmed permanent missables. The Lumina passive that Exposing Attack unlocks can potentially be obtained through other Pictos that share Lumina nodes — but the Pictos itself is gone.

6. Noco's Full Shop (Flying Waters Only)

CategoryNotes

Noco's exact normal inventory items vary, but his hidden inventory's crown jewel is Exposing Attack. Once unlocked, buy it immediately.
