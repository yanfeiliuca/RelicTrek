Paint Spikes — What They Are, How to Break Them, and What's Hidden Behind Them

What's Behind Them

1. What Paint Spikes Actually Are

Paint Spikes are not a crafting material or a Picto-upgrade currency. They're blue-veined rock formations that block paths across The Continent and inside towns, dungeons, and other locations in Clair Obscur: Expedition 33. Until you unlock the ability to break them, they just sit there looking like scenery — which is why most players walk past dozens of them early on.

Once you can break them, a Paint Spike typically reveals a hidden path, a chest, a Pictos, a weapon, Chroma, Colours of Lumina, or in a few cases an optional boss fight. Breaking one doesn't hand you an item called a "Paint Spike" to spend later — the reward is whatever the broken rock uncovers. [Source: Fextralife — All Paint Spike Locations]

Paint Spikes are a separate system from Paint Cages, which are a different kind of locked collectible with their own unlock method. The in-game tracker keeps the two separate, so don't assume clearing one means the other is done.

2. How You Actually Interact With Them

There's no upgrade menu involved. Once you have the Paint Break ability, you approach a Paint Spike in the field and use your Strike attack on it — the same input you already use in combat — to destroy it on the spot. [Source: Fextralife]

The two systems occasionally intersect — for example, one Paint Spike at Flying Manor is itself a target you need to destroy to open a nearby Paint Cage. [Source: Fextralife]

3. How to Unlock Paint Break

  • Find 4 of the 9 Lost Gestrals scattered across the Continent.
  • Return to Camp and speak to Sastro — he hands you the Paint Break ability as soon as you've turned in the 4th Gestral.
  • Try it immediately on the Paint Spike right next to Sastro at Camp — breaking it drops a Colour of Lumina.
  • Once unlocked, face a Spike in the field and use Strike (Square on PlayStation, X on Xbox, F on PC by default) to break it.
  • Important: Paint Break is removed when you start New Game+ — you'll need to re-collect 4 Lost Gestrals again to regain it. [Source: Fextralife — All Paint Spike Locations]

5. Tips and Common Mix-Ups

📋 Nothing Happened?

If breaking a Spike doesn't seem to reveal anything, turn around and check the area behind or beside where you were standing. The game frequently opens a path in a direction you weren't facing and rarely calls extra attention to it.

⚠ Don't Confuse It With Paint Cages

Paint Spikes and Paint Cages are tracked separately in-game. Clearing every Spike in an area doesn't mean the Cages are also done, and vice versa — check both before moving on.

⚠ New Game+ Resets Paint Break

Starting New Game+ removes the Paint Break ability. You'll need to find 4 of the 9 Lost Gestrals again in that playthrough before you can break Spikes a second time. [Source: Fextralife]
