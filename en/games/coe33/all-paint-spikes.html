1. Why You Need It

Paint Spikes are a separate collectible category from Paint Cages, used specifically for upgrading Pictos to higher ranks. They are found across all acts and are required for 100% completion. Their collection overlaps geographically with Paint Cages — the same areas that have high Paint Cage density also tend to have Paint Spikes — but there are critical differences in distribution that make a dedicated collection route worthwhile.

Understanding the Picto upgrade system: Pictos can be upgraded using Paint Spikes at camp or at specific upgrade points. Higher-rank Pictos have more powerful effects, making Paint Spike collection directly tied to character power level in the late game. Every Paint Spike you collect directly translates into stronger Picto effects and a more powerful party.

2. What You're Collecting — Distribution and Key Differences from Cages

Paint Spikes follow the same act-by-act distribution pattern as Paint Cages, but with important differences that change how you should approach collection.

DifferencePaint CagesPaint Spikes
Primary useUpgrade weapons and PictosUpgrade Pictos specifically
Density patternSpread across overworld and dungeonsHigher density inside dungeons
High-value cachesDistributed across all actsFlying Manor + Crimson Forest (Act 3 only)
TrackerPer-area countPer-area count — distinguishes from Cages

The big takeaway: dungeon thoroughness matters even more for Spikes than for Cages. Paint Spikes appear more frequently in dungeon environments than on the open Continent. If you are a player who tends to rush through dungeons, you will miss a disproportionate number of Spikes relative to Cages.

3. Prerequisites

  • Esquie swim ability — required for Act 2 dungeon island approaches and some underwater Spike locations
  • Esquie fly ability — required for Act 3 secret area caches (Flying Manor adjacent zones, Crimson Forest high ledges)
  • Esquie dive ability — required for Verso's Drafts DLC Paint Spikes (Thank You Update content)
  • In-game collection tracker — distinguishes between Cages and Spikes; use after each dungeon to confirm zero remaining
  • Thorough exploration mindset — dungeon speed-running is the #1 cause of missed Spikes

4. Optimal Route — Act-by-Act Dungeon-First Sweep

Same act-by-act sweep strategy as Paint Cages, but with a dungeon-first priority. The dungeons with the highest Paint Spike density are the ones you should explore most meticulously.

Act 1 — Foundation Sweep

1Sweep all Act 1 dungeons first — Spikes have higher dungeon density. Check every side room and branching corridor.
2Then cover overworld areas — Gestral Village, Stone Wave Cliffs, coastal paths. No special abilities needed for Act 1.

Act 2 — Dungeon Depth

3Prioritize Sirene dungeon and the Monolith dungeon. These have the highest Act 2 Spike density. Explore every branching path — do not take direct routes.
4Sweep Old Lumiere interiors and overworld zones between dungeons.
5With Esquie swim unlocked, return to island locations — Spikes on offshore islands are easily missed.

Act 3 — The High-Value Caches

6With Esquie fly unlocked, target Flying Manor adjacent zones — these contain the highest-value Paint Spike caches in the entire game. Secret area caches in Act 3 are the single most efficient collection target.
7Then sweep the Crimson Forest — focus on high-altitude ledges reachable only with fly.
8Enter Verso's Drafts DLC (Thank You Update) with Esquie dive — additional Spikes added in this content.
📋 Efficiency Tip — Upgrade Immediately

After collecting a Paint Spike cache, upgrade your Pictos at camp immediately. Higher-rank Pictos have more powerful effects — making subsequent dungeon sweeps faster and easier. Do not hoard Spikes — upgrade as you go.

5. Hazard Guide — Why Paint Spikes Are the Most Overlooked Collectible

⚠ Dungeon Speed-Run Penalty

The dungeons with the highest Paint Spike density are the same ones players tend to rush through. Because Spikes are used for Picto upgrades (not weapons), players who focus on weapon upgrades may de-prioritize Spike collection — only to hit a power wall in the late game when Picto ranks become critical. Slow, thorough exploration of every branching path in every dungeon is required.

⚠ Secret Area Misses

The highest-value Paint Spike caches are near secret areas in Act 3 — Flying Manor adjacent zones and Crimson Forest high ledges. These are off the critical path and not marked on the map. If you do not actively seek side paths and hidden ledges in Act 3, you will miss the most efficient Spike caches in the game.

⚠ Tracker Confusion — Cages vs. Spikes

The in-game collection tracker distinguishes between Paint Cages and Paint Spikes — use this distinction. A common mistake is checking the tracker, seeing zero remaining collectibles, and moving on — only to realize later that Spikes are tracked separately and were simply not checked. Verify both Cages AND Spikes before leaving each area.
