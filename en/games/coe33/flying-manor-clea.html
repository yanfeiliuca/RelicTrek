Flying Manor & Clea — Four Trials, One Door, Five Weapons You Can't Get Anywhere Else

1. Why You Need It

There's a glass pyramid floating in the sky above the center of The Continent. If you fly near it, you land in the Flying Manor — and most players leave within ten minutes, having fought a couple of familiar enemy types and grabbed a few items off the floor.

What almost no one realizes is that the Flying Manor is a gauntlet. It contains four bosses you must defeat in any order, and a locked door. The door doesn't open until all four are down. Behind the door is Clea — one of only two secret bosses in the game, and the most demanding test of parry timing in Expedition 33.

Clearing the Flying Manor completely rewards you with five Perfect Chroma Catalysts — enough to max out one weapon for each character in your active party. It also gives you Kralim, one of the best weapons for Lune, and Clea's Life Picto: a Picto that restores 100% of a character's health at the end of each turn where they took no damage. Combined with Cheater (which gives an extra turn), this means a character playing perfectly can self-heal fully every other turn.

Most players find the manor. Almost no one clears it — because they don't know the door can open.

2. What Clea's Life Picto Actually Does

Picto: Clea's Life (Lv. 30)
Effect: Recovers 100% of the character's health at end of turn, if no damage was taken since the previous turn

SynergyDescription
+ Cheater LuminaCheater grants an extra turn. On the bonus turn (where you haven't taken damage), Clea's Life triggers a full heal — effectively giving a free full heal every other turn
+ Defensive buildsCharacters that excel at parrying/dodging can maintain full HP almost indefinitely in sustained fights

Other rewards from full clear:

  • Kralim (Lune weapon) — from Triple Eveque fight; one of Lune's top endgame weapons for elemental builds
  • Perfect Chroma Catalyst × 5 — one from each of the four trial bosses plus Clea; enough to max five weapons
  • Clea Outfit — cosmetic for Maelle/Lune/Sciel, given by the young boy in Central Plaza after the fight
  • Grandiose Chroma Catalyst × 3 — additional upgrade materials from Clea

3. Prerequisites

  • Act 3 started — the Flying Manor requires Act 3 access
  • Esquie's flying ability unlocked — required to reach the floating manor
  • Party level 85–90+ — Clea is a superboss; the four trial bosses are manageable earlier, but don't attempt Clea below 85
  • Prepare anti-stun Lumina — Clea can stun; Anti-Stun Lumina prevents this

4. Optimal Route

This route covers everything from finding the manor to defeating Clea. Total estimated time: ~2–3 hours.

Step 1 — Find the Flying Manor

Open the world map. Locate the Forgotten Battlefield. The Flying Manor floats northwest of it, directly west of a Painting Workshop entrance. Look for the glass pyramid in the sky. Fly there with Esquie and land.

You arrive on a tilted broken ramp. Descend and immediately turn right 180 degrees, walking behind the staircase — a large Nevron statue will be on your left. Walk off the edge in front of you to drop to a lower rocky level.

Make your way to the Central Plaza. Activate the Expedition Flag here. This is your base of operations.

You'll see four glass display cases and a locked door guarded by a young boy. The door opens when all four trial bosses are defeated. The order doesn't matter — clear them however you like.

Step 2 — Four Trial Bosses (any order)

⬦ Lampmaster

Located underneath Central Plaza. Take the staircase to the side that leads to an elevator. Defeat Lampmaster. Reward: Perfect Chroma Catalyst.

⬦ Dualliste

Located across the bridge beside an elevator. Defeat Dualliste. Reward: Perfect Chroma Catalyst.

⬦ Triple Eveque (Flame, Frost, and Thunder)

Located past the floating frames. Jump onto the frames and cross to the distant building. Fight all three Eveques together — only one is vulnerable at a time. Defeat all three. Reward: Perfect Chroma Catalyst + Kralim (Lune weapon, Lv. 28).

⬦ Fourth boss

Continue exploring the remaining wings of the manor. Each area is connected to the Central Plaza; check every path for the remaining boss encounter. Reward: Perfect Chroma Catalyst.

En-route items to pick up:

  • On the left path from the Central Plaza: grapple to find Empowering Break Pictos (Lv. 28)
  • After grapple, follow rope to find a Mime enemy → defeat for Clea Haircut (Maelle cosmetic)
  • The middle path from Central Plaza crosses to the manor building itself → find Colour of Lumina beside three blank canvases
  • Fusoka, a merchant, is past the Bourgeon encounter — sells Lv. 28 weapons including Martenon and Sidaro

Step 3 — Open the Door

1Return to Central Plaza once all four trial bosses are defeated. The door behind the young boy will open. He confirms the path forward. Step through.
2From the Central Plaza Expedition Flag, walk down the broken stairs and grapple across to the next cliff to reach Clea's atelier.

Step 4 — The Clea Fight

3Approach Clea to trigger the encounter. → Full fight strategy in Section 5.
🏆 Rewards

Clea's Life Picto (Lv. 30) + Perfect Chroma Catalyst × 1 + Grandiose Chroma Catalyst × 3 + Recoat × 1

After the fight, return to the young boy in Central Plaza → he gives you the Clea Outfit (for Maelle, Lune, or Sciel).

5. The Clea Fight: Hazard Guide

Recommended level: 85–90+
Weakness: Void damage (also effective: Light)

What makes Clea unique:

She doesn't attack you directly. For each of her turns, she summons a different set of Nevrons to attack in her place. While this happens, she passively heals — but only if your party misses a parry or takes damage. If you play perfectly and avoid every hit, her healing is suppressed.

This makes Clea the game's pure parry test. She's not checking your build. She's checking your reflexes and discipline.

Key attacks from her summons

"Clea summons Nevrons": typically 4 attacks — 3 melee from Lampmaster-type + 1 from Hexga-type. The third attack is slightly delayed from the first two; the Hexga jumps into the air before slamming down. Don't swing early.

Strategy:

  1. Apply Slow to Clea. Reducing her action frequency gives your party more turns between her healing windows.
  2. Stack Rush on your party. More actions per round means more total damage before she can outheal.
  3. Use Cheater Lumina. Extra turns amplify both damage and, if you have Clea's Life, healing.
  4. Burst faster than she heals. Her healing is meaningful — but it has a ceiling. Builds that spike damage (Maelle with Medalum, Verso with Simoso) can outpace her recovery.
  5. Bring Anti-Stun Lumina. Some of her summons can stun your party; this Lumina negates it.
💡 One key piece of advice

Clea is not a fight you endure. She's a fight you race. Get the damage down fast, play clean on defense, and she falls before her healing becomes a wall.
