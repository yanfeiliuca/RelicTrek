Simoso — Verso's Hidden Endgame Sword (And the Hardest Boss That Guards It)

1. Why You Need It

There's a sword in Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 that most players never find. Not because it's hidden behind an obscure puzzle or a missable dialogue choice — but because getting to it requires you to go somewhere the game never points you toward, and then defeat the hardest boss in the entire game.

That sword is Simoso. It's Verso's endgame weapon, and it does something no other weapon in the game does: it makes him functionally unkillable.

While Verso holds Simoso and maintains Rank A or higher, he cannot die. Every attack he lands adds a second hit of Light damage. After four fights, he starts applying Burn stacks on enemies with each Light hit. The weapon arrives at Level 33 — already fully unlocked — the moment you pick it up. No upgrades needed. No catalysts to farm. You defeat Simon, and you walk away with Verso's best-in-slot weapon, already at full power.

The problem is that almost no one knows Simon exists. He's in The Abyss, a location that sits behind a portal in Renoir's Drafts, accessible only after you've unlocked Esquie's ability to swim underwater. The game does not tell you he's there. There's no quest marker, no NPC who says "by the way, something incredible is waiting in the deep water between Sirene and Visages." You either know, or you don't.

If you're reading this, now you know.

4. Optimal Route

This route takes you from the hidden underwater portal all the way to Simon's arena. Total estimated time: ~1–2 hours depending on party preparation.

Step 1 — Find the Underwater Portal

1Travel to the open water between Sirene and Visages. Switch to Esquie and swim underwater. Look for a glowing portal in the open water — it pulses faintly and is easy to miss if you're not scanning carefully.

Step 2 — Navigate Renoir's Drafts

2Enter the portal to arrive in Renoir's Drafts. Work through the first section of enemies.
💎 Bonus — Music Record & Contorso

Look for a grapple point along the upper ledge — it leads to Music Record: Endless Light and the Contorso weapon (Level 31). After defeating the first group of enemies, you'll also receive the Ballaro weapon (Level 31).

Step 3 — Enter The Abyss

3Find and enter The Abyss portal within Renoir's Drafts. Drop through the crawlspace, continue heading north, and you'll arrive at a large circular arena.
💎 Bonus — Save Point

Activate the Expedition Flag: Simon inside the arena. This is your save point and fast-travel anchor. Make sure to save before proceeding — there's no turning back once the fight begins.

Step 4 — The Simon Fight

4Approach the center of the arena to trigger the encounter with Simon. Upon victory, you receive: Simoso (Level 33 weapon), 1,237,500 EXP, Perfect Chroma Catalyst, and the "Peace at Last" trophy.

5. The Simon Fight: Hazard Guide

Recommended: Level 90+ party with Second Chance Lumina equipped on every character. This fight punishes mistakes harder than anything else in the game.

Phase 1 — The Measured Onslaught

⚠ Phase 1 Threats

Simon opens with a 3-hit combo, escalating to a 5-hit combo, then a 6-hit long combo. He also uses an HP-to-1 attack that leaves your character at critical health regardless of defense. Rule: Parry > Dodge in Phase 1. Parrying builds your Perfection rank, which is essential for Simoso's immortality passive.

Phase 2 — Spectral Annihilation

🔴 Phase 2 Threats

At roughly 60% HP, Simon erases your active party — all three front-line members are removed. He summons a spectral phantom sword that attacks independently, and his combos extend to 8 hits. Critical: any downed party member left unattended is permanently erased from the fight. Revive immediately or lose them for good. Rule: Never leave a downed party member on the ground. Drop everything to revive.

Phase 3 — The Red Phantom (Below 25% HP)

🔴 Phase 3 Threats

Simon's phantom sword turns red and deals massively increased damage. Combos now reach 12 hits, and he wipes your party again. This is a DPS race — burn him down before he burns through your reserve members. Rule: Don't panic on the Phase 2→3 transition. The wipe looks worse than it is — your reserve party slots in automatically.

Three Rules to Win

  • Parry > Dodge for Phase 1. Parrying builds Perfection rank, which directly fuels Simoso's power and Verso's survivability.
  • Never leave a downed party member. In Phases 2 and 3, downed members get permanently erased if ignored. Reviving is always the top priority.
  • Don't panic on the Phase 2 transition. The party wipe is scripted. Your reserve members automatically fill in. Stay calm and keep the pressure on.
