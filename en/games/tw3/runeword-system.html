1. Why You Need This

Hearts of Stone added the Runewright enchanting system to The Witcher 3, but it sits behind an optional side-quest chain that many players postpone or skip.

Runewords and Glyphwords are the core idea. If you bring the right three-slot sword or chest armor to the Runewright and use the correct enchantment, you replace three separate rune or glyph bonuses with one combined effect that changes how that gear behaves.

The system is easy to miss, not completely unintroduced. The quest Enchanting: Start-up Costs points Geralt from the Seven Cats Inn notice board to the Ofieri Runewright at Upper Mill. Players who ignore notice boards or expensive optional upgrades can still finish huge parts of Hearts of Stone without investing in it, which is why the feature often feels hidden in practice.

2. How Runewords Work

Requirements:

  • Hearts of Stone installed
  • The optional Upper Mill Runewright questline unlocked
  • A three-slot sword for a Runeword, or a three-slot chest armor piece for a Glyphword
  • Enough crowns to unlock the Runewright's three service tiers

Unlock path:

  • Step 1: Start Enchanting: Start-up Costs, usually from the Seven Cats Inn notice board, and travel to the Runewright at Upper Mill.
  • Step 2: Pay the initial 5,000 crowns to start the system.
  • Step 3: Continue through Enchanting: Quality Has Its Price and pay 10,000 crowns, then 15,000 more, to unlock the higher tiers.
  • Step 4: Use the Runewright's unlocked enchantment options on eligible three-slot gear.

3. Key Enchantments and Their Effects

TypeEnchantmentEffect
Runeword · Level 3ReplenishmentAfter casting a Sign, consumes an Adrenaline Point and charges the next sword attack with that Sign's power
Runeword · Level 3InvigorationAt maximum Vitality, Vitality regeneration becomes added damage on the next strike, up to +50%
Runeword · Level 2SeveranceExtends Whirl by 1.1 yards and Rend by 1.9 yards
Glyphword · Level 1DeflectionArmor deflects all arrows

Highest Priority Enchantments

Replenishment: Usually discussed as a Sign-and-sword hybrid option rather than a default melee pick.

Severance: Makes Rend and Whirl hit a significantly larger area. For combat builds, this is a substantial upgrade.

Invigoration: Scales with max-Vitality sustain rather than Adrenaline. In the right build, it can add meaningful raw damage.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Optional Quest Gate

The Runewright is not part of the automatic Hearts of Stone critical path. If you skip the notice-board lead or never pay the first 5,000 crowns, the system stays locked.

⚠ Three-Slot Requirement

Runewords need a three-slot sword, while Glyphwords need a three-slot chest armor piece. Not every item qualifies, so check the gear before spending crowns or materials.

⚠ Overwriting Runes Destroys Them

Placing new runes in a slot destroys the old one. Before experimenting with combinations, have enough rune materials to try multiple configurations.

⚠ High Crown Cost

Fully unlocking the Runewright costs 30,000 crowns total: 5,000 first, then 10,000, then 15,000. Plan around that money sink before treating the system as a casual detour.

5. En-Route Bonus

💎 Tiered Upgrade Path

The Runewright opens in stages, so you can stop after the first 5,000 crowns if you only want to test the system before committing to the full 30,000-crown route.

💎 Sword And Chest Armor Split

This system covers both Runewords for swords and Glyphwords for chest armor. Treat those as separate planning problems instead of assuming one best enchant fits everything.

💎 Build Planning Value

Once unlocked, the system is best used on long-term gear pieces you expect to keep, because replacing runes or changing enchants consumes materials and money.

6. Why RelicTrek Covers This

This is one of the clearest examples of information asymmetry in The Witcher 3's crafting systems. A full expansion feature exists behind a side-quest chain and a large money sink, so many players delay it long enough that it may as well feel hidden.

This is exactly the kind of hidden system RelicTrek was built to surface. The Runeword system represents everything our site stands for: high-value content that exists in the game, requires assembly and knowledge, and is invisible to the overwhelming majority of players.
