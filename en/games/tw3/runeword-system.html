1. Why You Need This

Hearts of Stone added two major systems to Witcher 3. The first — the new story and characters — is obvious. The second is the Runeword system, which is not.

Runewords work like this: every Witcher gear item has three rune slots. Before Hearts of Stone, you filled them with individual runes. After HoS, if you fill all three slots with the right combination of rune types, a Runeword activates — a special combined effect that no single rune provides and that isn't listed anywhere in the game until you actually do it.

The game does not explain this system. There is no tutorial message, no NPC who teaches it, no tooltip suggesting that rune combinations matter beyond individual rune bonuses. Players who completed Hearts of Stone and never happened to put the correct three-rune combination in the right order have played the entire expansion without ever triggering a Runeword. Most of them have.

2. How Runewords Work

Requirements:

  • Hearts of Stone DLC installed
  • A Witcher gear item with three rune slots
  • The correct three runes in the correct configuration

Obtaining Runic diagrams: Runeword recipes are learned from two sources:

  • Runemaster Keira (Novigrad, after HoS begins): Sells several basic Runeword diagrams
  • The HoS wandering merchant: A specific trader in the HoS area who sells advanced Runeword diagrams

3. Key Runewords and Their Effects

Rune CombinationRunewordEffect
3× Dazhbog RunesReplenishmentCasting a Sign replenishes charges; charged Signs auto-charge on Sign cast
3× Veles RunesInvigorationEvery Adrenaline Point grants 2% of max HP as bonus to attack power
3× Chernobog RunesSeveranceIncreases reach of Rend and Whirl attacks; extended area of effect
3× Stribog RunesDeflectionParrying an attack launches it back at the attacker

Highest Priority Runewords

Replenishment (3× Dazhbog): Transforms Sign-build weapons by allowing continuous charged Sign use without the stamina floor creating gaps.

Severance (3× Chernobog): Makes Rend and Whirl hit a significantly larger area. For combat builds, this is a substantial upgrade.

Invigoration (3× Veles): Scales with Adrenaline investment. In fully developed combat builds, adds meaningful raw damage.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Diagrams Must Be Learned

You don't automatically know Runeword combinations — they must be learned from Runemaster Keira or the HoS merchant. Visit both early in the HoS questline.

⚠ Three-Slot Requirement

Not all weapons have three rune slots. Witcher School gear pieces typically do. Standard crafted items may have fewer. Check item details before buying runes.

⚠ Overwriting Runes Destroys Them

Placing new runes in a slot destroys the old one. Before experimenting with combinations, have enough rune materials to try multiple configurations.

⚠ High Crown Cost

Runeword diagrams are expensive. Budget 5,000–15,000 crowns to purchase all diagrams from both vendors.

5. En-Route Bonus

💎 Greater Runes

Runemaster Keira also sells rare Greater Runes that are difficult to find as loot.

💎 Rare Diagrams Beyond Runewords

The HoS wandering merchant occasionally stocks rare crafting diagrams beyond Runewords — check his full inventory.

💎 Achievement/Trophy

Completing Runeword experimentation unlocks an achievement/trophy.

6. Why RelicTrek Covers This

This is the purest example of information asymmetry in The Witcher 3's crafting systems. A full expansion feature — designed, implemented, balanced — that the game never tells you exists. Most players will never discover it without external guidance.

This is exactly the kind of hidden system RelicTrek was built to surface. The Runeword system represents everything our site stands for: high-value content that exists in the game, requires assembly and knowledge, and is invisible to the overwhelming majority of players.
