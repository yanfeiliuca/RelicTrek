1. Scope and Missable Boundaries

This page covers the Hearts of Stone Viper armor and venomous swords. The two low-level White Orchard diagrams are a separate base-game treasure hunt and are not prerequisites for this gear.

The expansion route has three closing windows: Countess Mignole's four armor diagrams, the venomous steel sword diagram inside the Borsodi vault, and the venomous silver sword in Gaunter O'Dimm's world.

3. Hearts of Stone Route

1Before the auction: speak with Countess Mignole in the Borsodi auction house and enter her trade screen. Buy the four Viper armor diagrams.
2Inside the Borsodi vault: search the chest near Maximilian Borsodi's house and take the Viper venomous steel sword diagram before leaving.
3Expansion finale: choose the path that challenges Master Mirror and enters Gaunter's world. The area is timed; at the later route splits, find the red-glowing point of interest, defeat the specter, and take the silver sword from the stone before solving the main riddle.
4White Orchard: use the dedicated sword guide for the cemetery silver diagram and Amavet Fortress steel diagram. Those crafted weapons have fixed item levels and do not scale with collection time.

4. Facts That Prevent Common Errors

Armor Sale

Countess Mignole is an optional conversation. Leaving the relevant auction sequence without buying the diagrams closes their normal acquisition route for that playthrough.

Two Different Swords

The steel weapon is crafted from a vault diagram. The silver weapon is collected as an actual sword in Gaunter's world; do not plan around a normally obtainable silver-sword diagram.

White Orchard Separation

The base-game steel diagram is at Amavet Fortress Ruins west of Ransacked Village—not at the abandoned mill. Both White Orchard swords have fixed crafting levels.

5. Armor and Weapon Facts

The four armor pieces are level 39 medium Witcher gear and require a Master armorer. Their listed poison-resistance values are 50% on the armor, 30% on the trousers, and 10% each on the boots and gauntlets.

Poison resistance protects against the poisoning status. It does not reduce the toxicity generated by potions or decoctions.

6. Practical Planning

  • Save before the auction and before the expansion finale choice.
  • Buy all four armor diagrams in one visit and verify them in the crafting menu.
  • Search the vault fully before advancing the quest.
  • Do not use poison resistance as a substitute for toxicity-management skills.
  • Compare the White Orchard swords with current drops rather than assuming they remain best through a fixed level range.
