1. Why You Need This

The Manticore School is different from the four main schools in one important way: it has no upgrade tiers below Grandmaster. Blood and Wine adds it through Lafargue's Grandmaster scavenger-hunt chain, so you collect six diagrams and craft the set directly instead of upgrading through Basic, Enhanced, Superior, and Mastercrafted versions.

The old version of this page overstated the set bonus. Manticore does not have a 6-piece bonus that extends decoction duration. The verified 3-piece bonus affects bombs, while the verified 6-piece bonus adds one charge to each alchemy item. Individual armor pieces can add maximum toxicity, but that is an item stat, not the 6-piece set effect.

The armor also has franchise value: Geralt mentions having once worn Manticore armor, and its appearance is likely a visual callback to his basic armor from the first Witcher game.

Manticore is a level 40 Blood and Wine witcher gear set. It does not have lower-tier versions.

3. All 6 Diagram Locations — Practical Route

The verified route follows Merten's trail through five Toussaint locations. Two diagrams, the trousers and boots, are found together at Lebioda's temple.

Stop 1 — Bastoy Prison Ruins (Steel Sword)

1Go to Bastoy Prison Ruins, south of Fort Astre Ruins. If the nearby treasure hunt is still active, expect an alp in the area.
2Climb the standing tower in the northeast part of the ruins. Loot the brick in the wall near the entryway to find the steel sword diagram and Prisoner's journal.

Stop 2 — Cave Southeast of Gélenser Farm (Armor)

3Travel to the cave southeast of Gélenser Farm and southwest of Termes Palace Ruins. A panther may guard the outside.
4Inside, search the prayer notes on the far wall until you find Merten's prayer. Read it to get the armor diagram and the next lead.

Stop 3 — Lebioda's Temple (Trousers + Boots)

5At the temple near the Prophet Lebioda statue, use Aard on the far wall to open the hidden room. Defeat the Great Beggar's Ghost.
6Loot the small container on a table near the stairs opposite the entrance. This container holds both the trousers and boots diagrams plus The Great Beggar's journal.

Stop 4 — Murky Delve (Silver Sword)

7Murky Delve is south of the Palace Gardens and northwest of Seidhe Llygad Amphitheater. Swim in and continue through the hallucination sequence.
8The trial cannot permanently fail, but choices can change which monsters attack. Continue to the hidden chapel and loot the container near the praying figure to find the silver sword diagram and Merten's notes.

Stop 5 — Lake of Cleansing (Gauntlets)

9Go to the Lake of Cleansing northeast of Basane Farm. Bandits may be gathered near the pier.
10Swim just off the pier and use Witcher Senses in the middle of the lake to find the red-marked underwater container. Retrieve the gauntlets diagram and Merten's last letter to complete the quest.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Risk 1 — Old Location Lists

If a guide sends you to Dulcinea Estate, Pereplut, an abandoned Beauclair laboratory, or Sansretour Valley for Manticore diagrams, treat it as stale. The verified quest route follows Merten's notes through the five locations listed above.

⚠ Risk 2 — Murky Delve Choices

Murky Delve is a hallucination trial. There is no hard fail state, but going toward certain illusions can trigger extra monster fights.

⚠ Risk 3 — Lake Search

The final gauntlets container is underwater near the pier. Use Witcher Senses in the lake rather than searching the shoreline only.

⚠ Risk 4 — Bonus vs Set Effect

Do not confuse item stats with set bonuses. Maximum toxicity comes from individual armor pieces; the 6-piece set bonus is alchemy item charges +1.

5. En-Route Bonus Items

💎 The Words of the Prophets Are Written on Sarcophagi

At Lebioda's temple, the skeleton in the hidden room can start this secondary quest.

💎 Lake of Cleansing Valuables

The lake bottom contains jewelry and other valuables near the final Manticore armor piece.

💎 Murky Delve Notes

Murky Delve contains Merten's notes, which are the narrative bridge to the final Lake of Cleansing objective.

6. Build Recommendations

Alchemy Decoction Build

  • Armor: Full Manticore Set (6 pieces)
  • Key Skills: Acquired Tolerance, Heightened Tolerance, Endure Pain, Protective Coating
  • Strategy: Use the armor-piece toxicity stats alongside alchemy skills such as Acquired Tolerance and Heightened Tolerance. The 6-piece bonus helps by adding one charge to each alchemy item, not by extending decoction duration.
  • Useful Against: Longer routes where extra alchemy item charges and potion/bomb management matter.

Bomb Specialist (Alternative)

  • With Manticore 3-piece: Bomb critical hit chance and critical hit damage start applying to bombs, and bombs are thrown without delay.
  • Useful Against: Groups of enemies and encounters where fast bomb throws matter.
