1. Why You Need This

The Manticore School is different from the four main schools in one important way: it has no upgrade tiers. It exists only at Grandmaster level, added entirely by Blood and Wine. There is no basic Manticore, no Enhanced Manticore, no Superior or Mastercrafted Manticore. You find the diagrams and craft Grandmaster immediately.

This makes it accessible — a player who finds all six diagrams can have the full set without the progression grind that the other schools require. But the set's value is specialized: its 6-piece bonus extends the duration of Witcher Decoctions.

Decoctions are alchemy's most powerful effects — longer than potions, more impactful, but with limited uptime. Extending that uptime substantially changes how alchemy builds function. Builds that typically cycle out of decoction effects mid-fight now maintain them through extended combat sequences.

The Manticore set is the correct endgame choice for any player who has invested heavily in the Alchemy skill tree. The armor itself is a direct visual callback to Geralt's gear from The Witcher 1 — for franchise veterans, this is the armor he wore when none of them knew who he was yet.

Note

Manticore exists only at Grandmaster level — no prerequisite tiers needed. Required level: ~44–46.

3. All 6 Diagram Locations — Optimal Route

The Manticore diagrams are spread across Toussaint in locations associated with monsters and abandoned structures — consistent with an alchemy school's focus on beast materials.

Stop 1 — Dulcinea Estate (Steel Sword)

1An old estate on the western edge of Toussaint. The diagram is in the estate's abandoned library on the upper floor. Watch for wraiths in the main hall — use Moon Dust bombs to force them into corporeal form. The library is at the top of the grand staircase, third door on the left. Diagram affixed to a bookshelf.
2💎 Bonus: The Dulcinea estate library also contains a rare book worth 200 crowns to a Novigrad book merchant.

Stop 2 — Blessure Cave (Silver Sword)

3Short cave system in central Toussaint with several Endrega insects. Chest at the far end. Endregas are weak to insectoid oil. The cave has two branches — take the left fork at the junction; the right fork dead-ends.

Stop 3 — Ruins of Pereplut (Chest Armor)

4A standing stone circle area in northern Toussaint. The armor chest is at the central altar. The ruins are guarded by a Golem — use elementa oil and Quen sign. The Golem's slam attacks have a wide radius; dodge backward, not sideways.

Stop 4 — Abandoned Laboratory (Trousers)

5Northeast of Beauclair. The lab belonged to a failed alchemist; his notes and the diagram are in the main work area. The lab is unmarked on the map — look for a stone building with a collapsed roof northeast of Beauclair's city walls. Inside: alchemist's workbench with the diagram.
6💎 Bonus: Failed alchemist's notes contain a formula for Superior Petri's Philter.

Stop 5 — Old Hunting Lodge (Gauntlets)

7Western Toussaint forested hills. The lodge has been taken over by Alghouls — the chest is in the basement storage. Alghouls have an enrage mechanic when bloodied. Open with Necrophage Oil-coated silver sword. The basement entrance is through a trapdoor in the kitchen.
8💎 Bonus: Alghoul marrow from the hunting lodge — rare alchemy ingredient for high-tier potions.

Stop 6 — Sansretour Valley Floor (Boots — underwater)

9Eastern Toussaint. Accessible by descending from the valley rim via a hidden goat trail on the southeastern rim — narrow but passable. The boots diagram is in a submerged chest near an ancient stone marker at the valley floor. The valley floor has high-level monsters; approach at level 42+.
10💎 Bonus: Sansretour Valley floor has several unmarked chests with valuable crafting materials scattered around the ancient markers.

4. Hazard Guide

🔴 Risk 1 — Sansretour Valley Descent

The goat trail isn't obvious from above. Look for it on the southeastern rim — it starts between two distinctive standing rocks. The valley floor itself has powerful monsters appropriate to late-game Toussaint. Approach at level 42+.

⚠ Risk 2 — Alghoul Enrage (Hunting Lodge)

Alghouls enrage when bloodied — they become significantly more aggressive. Prioritize getting the fight under control before any Alghoul enrages. Axii sign can briefly stun them to interrupt the enrage animation.

⚠ Risk 3 — Golem at Pereplut

Elementa oil + Quen sign. Its slam has a wide AoE. Roll backward on the windup — the hitbox extends significantly to the sides. Patience wins this fight; the Golem telegraphs every attack.

⚠ Risk 4 — Wraiths at Dulcinea Estate

Yrden sign forces them into corporeal form where they take full damage. Moon Dust bombs as backup. Wraiths in this location are higher level than typical — don't underestimate them.

5. En-Route Bonus Items

💎 Alghoul Marrow

From the hunting lodge. Rare alchemy ingredient used in high-tier potion crafting.

💎 Superior Petri's Philter Formula

Found in the abandoned laboratory alongside the trousers diagram. One of the rarest alchemy formulas in the game.

💎 Sansretour Valley Chests

The valley floor has several unmarked chests with valuable crafting materials — worth exploring the full area after collecting the boots diagram.

💎 Rare Book (200 Crowns)

The Dulcinea estate library contains a rare book that sells for 200 crowns to the Novigrad book merchant.

6. Build Recommendations

Alchemy Decoction Build

  • Armor: Full Manticore Set (6 pieces)
  • Key Skills: Acquired Tolerance, Heightened Tolerance, Endure Pain, Protective Coating
  • Strategy: Run 3+ decoctions simultaneously using the increased toxicity threshold from the 6-piece bonus. The extended decoction duration means effects last through entire combat encounters without needing to refresh. Combine with Ekimmara + Ekhidna + Troll decoctions for constant HP regeneration.
  • Best Against: Extended fights where decoction uptime matters — boss encounters, monster hunts, and large bandit camps.

Bomb Specialist (Alternative)

  • With Manticore 3-piece: The increased alchemy charges synergize with bomb-focused playstyles. More bombs per fight with the extra charges. Combine with the Pyromaniac and Efficiency skills for maximum bomb output.
  • Best Against: Groups of enemies and enemies with bomb weaknesses (Golems, Elementals).
