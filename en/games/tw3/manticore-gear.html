1. Why You Need This

The Manticore set is the only Witcher school gear that exists solely at Grandmaster level — there's no basic, enhanced, or mastercrafted version to work through. You go straight to the endgame set. You also don't need a previous tier to craft it.

Its set bonuses serve alchemy-focused builds directly: three pieces let your bombs benefit from critical hit chance and damage, and bombs are thrown without animation delay. Six pieces add one extra charge to every alchemy item you carry.

The armor itself is a direct visual callback to Geralt's gear in The Witcher 1 — for franchise veterans, this is the armor he wore when none of them knew who he was yet, before Yennefer, before Ciri, before everything.

The five diagram locations are scattered across the full breadth of Toussaint — a prison tower, a canyon cave, an ancient temple, a hillside lake approach, and a dive to the bottom of a lake.

3. Optimal Route

Stop 1 — Bastoy Prison (Steel Sword)

1Northwest of Beauclair. Head to the small lake northwest of the city, then ride directly west up the hill. The prison tower ruins appear — watch for an Alp lurking inside. Climb the outer wall using the available handholds and then walk along the scaffolding around the tower exterior. At the top: use Witcher Senses to find the steel sword diagram affixed to the stone wall.
2💎 Bonus: The prison basement (accessible via a collapsed section at ground level) contains an old inmate's hidden stash — including a Journeyman's diagram for Enhanced Specter Oil.

Stop 2 — Canyon Cave (Armor + Trousers)

3Southeast of Gelenser Farm, past Francollarts. Look for a narrow canyon entrance in the hillside. Watch for panthers on the approach. Enter the cave. Follow the passage to an inner chamber covered with notes pinned to the walls — Geralt recognizes these as a witcher's research notes. The chest in this chamber contains armor + trousers diagrams.
4💎 Bonus: The cave's research chamber has an unlocked alchemist's kit — three random Master-tier potion ingredients are generated here on each visit.

Stop 3 — Abandoned Temple (Boots)

5North of Prophet Lebioda's Statue construction area. The temple is partially collapsed. Enter, navigate to the deepest room, and search for the chest: boots diagram inside.
6💎 Bonus: The temple's collapsed east wing has a hidden alcove with an offering bowl — contains a Greater Glyph of Warding (armor) and a Soldier Statuette (sell value: 400 crowns).

Stop 4 — Hillside Lake Approach (Silver Sword)

7Northwest of Beauclair, near Plegmund's Bridge. Look for a climbable section of the hillside — exposed rocks form natural handholds. Climb up. At the top of the rise, look for the diagram: Manticore Silver Sword affixed to a stone marker. The reverse of the diagram provides the location of the next piece.

Stop 5 — Lake of Cleansing (Gauntlets — underwater)

8Northeast Toussaint, near Basane Farm. Three level-40 bandits camp on the shoreline. Defeat them. Dive into the lake. Use Witcher Senses underwater to locate a sack on the lakebed. Loot it: Manticore gauntlets diagram.
9💎 Bonus: The lake bottom has two additional underwater sacks near the gauntlets one — containing Dragonfish Scales and Aquatic Mutagen, rare alchemy ingredients.

4. Danger Zones & Bonus Loot

⚠ Danger Zones

🔴 Risk 1 — Alp at Bastoy Prison

Alps are vampires — immune to silver in some configurations. Use specter oil if your silver sword seems ineffective, or switch to steel sword with vampire oil.

⚠ Risk 2 — Panthers at the Canyon Approach

They stagger Geralt with jump attacks and can chain-stun in groups. Axii sign to stun one; kill individually.

⚠ Risk 3 — Underwater Visibility

The Lake of Cleansing has murky water. Witcher Senses underwater highlights the sack with a faint glow, but you need to be within roughly 8 meters to see it. Dive in and swim methodically across the bottom.

💎 Bonus Loot Along the Route

💎 Bonus 1 — No Prior Tier Required

The full Manticore set doesn't require any prior tier of Manticore gear (unlike the other four school sets). This means you can bypass all lower tiers and go straight to the endgame version. If you're a veteran doing a fresh run of Blood and Wine and are already level 40, this is the fastest complete endgame set to acquire.

💎 Bonus 2 — Dragonfish Scales & Aquatic Mutagen

The lake bottom at Lake of Cleansing has two additional underwater sacks near the gauntlets one — containing Dragonfish Scales and Aquatic Mutagen, rare alchemy ingredients used in high-tier potion crafting.

5. Set Bonus & Crafting

Manticore Set Bonuses

  • 3 pieces: Critical hit chance and damage bonuses also apply to bombs; bombs are thrown without delay
  • 6 pieces: Maximum charges of each alchemy item +1

Best Playstyle

Alchemy build with high Toxicity — fill toxicity with multiple decoctions → bomb-focused combat with critical bomb builds → extra alchemy item charges extend combat longevity significantly. Combine with the Acquired Tolerance and Heightened Tolerance skills to safely run 3+ decoctions simultaneously.

6. Build Recommendations

Critical Bomber

  • Armor: Full Manticore Set (6 pieces)
  • Key Skills: Acquired Tolerance, Heightened Tolerance, Pyromaniac, Efficiency (bomb skill)
  • Strategy: Run 3+ decoctions to max toxicity. Open fights with bombs — Grapeshot and Dancing Star benefit from your critical hit stats via the 3-piece bonus. Extra alchemy charges from 6-piece bonus mean more bombs and more potions through long fights. This is the only build where bombs are a primary damage source, not just utility.
  • Best Against: Groups (bandit camps, monster nests) and enemies with bomb-weakness (Golems, Elementals). The critical bomb damage makes short work of clustered targets.

Alchemy Tank (Alternative)

  • With Manticore + Protective Coating: The 6-piece bonus (+1 to all alchemy charges) synergizes with Protective Coating and Endure Pain for extended invulnerability windows during combat. Run Ekimmara + Ekhidna + Troll decoctions for constant HP regeneration, and use the extra potion charges for on-demand healing.
