1. Why You Need This

The Leshen Decoction has the most distinctive visual effect of any alchemy item. When Geralt kills an enemy, a swarm of ravens erupts from the body and attacks the nearest remaining enemies. In a fight with five targets, the first kill instantly creates additional ongoing damage against the surviving four.

In multi-enemy encounters — bandit camps, monster nests, cave systems — the raven swarm extends Geralt's effective damage output without additional attacks. The Leshen Mutagen required drops from Leshens: walking forest entities that are uncommon, non-respawning monsters. Each playthrough has a strictly limited supply.

2. Decoction Effect

When active: each enemy kill (any kill, any weapon) causes a raven swarm to spawn and attack enemies within the raven's detection range. Swarms persist for several seconds dealing moderate but consistent damage.

Best use cases: Bandit camps (multiple humans in sequence), monster nests (grouped Drowners/Nekkers), dungeon corridors (limited space = swarms hit multiple targets).

3. Leshen Locations

Velen

Leshens appear in old forest areas, around remote question-mark locations in the forest interior (not swamp edges). Check forests northwest of Mulbrydale and the Silverton area. One Leshen appears during a specific Velen questline — the most reliably triggered encounter.

Skellige

Leshens on Ard Skellig are found in forested interiors, around ancient stone circles and deep forest shrines. Higher level than Velen — approach after completing most of Velen.

Ancient Leshen (Special)

An "Ancient Leshen" appears during a specific Skellige quest. Significantly harder but drops higher-quality mutagen.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Leshen Weaknesses

Relict Oil on silver sword (essential — Leshens are Relict-type). Dimeritium bombs prevent root-summoning. They summon wolves — kill the Leshen first; wolves become passive when it dies.

⚠ Root Trap

Leshens create root networks that erupt under Geralt. Yrden's slow effect limits root activation range. Staying mobile prevents the trap from fully activating.

⚠ Non-Respawn Warning

Leshens do not respawn. Each playthrough has a limited number. Don't sell or discard the Mutagen — it has no other source.

5. Build Synergy

Best with fast-attack builds where kills happen in rapid succession — each kill triggers the swarm on remaining targets. Pairs well with the Manticore set's alchemy bonuses and the Acquired Tolerance skill to run Leshen Decoction alongside other decoctions simultaneously.

6. Why This Is a RelicTrek Item

The Leshen Decoction requires: knowledge that Leshens exist (uncommon enemy), knowledge that they drop a unique Mutagen (not obvious from loot), knowledge of where Leshens spawn (limited locations, no in-game guide), and knowledge that the resulting Decoction has the raven-swarm effect (the game doesn't preview Decoction effects before crafting). Four layers of information asymmetry for one of the game's most visually spectacular alchemy items.
