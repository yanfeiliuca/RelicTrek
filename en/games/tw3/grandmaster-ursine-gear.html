1. Why You Need This

The Grandmaster Ursine set's 6-piece bonus is one of the most dramatic in the game: all damage dealt by Quen sign abilities increases by 200%. The 3-piece bonus paves the way — when your active Quen shield shatters, there is a chance a new one activates automatically at no stamina cost, with the chance increasing by 5% for each piece of the set worn.

Heavy armor players gravitate toward the Ursine set for its raw defense stats. But the Quen bonus transforms it into something unique: a build where your defense mechanism also becomes your most powerful offensive tool, especially with the Quen Discharge active ability which converts absorbed damage into a damaging explosion.

The two diagram locations follow a common thread: both are underground, both require tracking Bear School symbols to navigate, and both have powerful enemies guarding the witcher corpses that hold the diagrams.

3. Optimal Route

Stop 1 — Flovive Sinking Mansion (Silver Sword + Armor + Gauntlets)

1Travel to Flovive, a small settlement north of Toussaint Prison. Northeast of the village is a striking sight: a large red mansion visibly sinking into the ground, tilted at an angle.
2Circle to the north side of the mansion and find the sinkhole. At the edge, a rock wall blocks you — search for the opening among the rocks (it's low down). Drop in. Follow the underground passage.
3A Giant Centipede ambushes you in the first large chamber — use Yrden sign to pin it when it surfaces; attack the exposed abdomen. Dodge the burrowing attacks.
4After the Centipede: find the witcher's corpse in the next chamber. Loot it: silver sword + armor + gauntlets diagrams.
5💎 Bonus: The centipede's chamber has a secondary tunnel (northeast wall, blocked by a boulder — use Aard). Inside: a chest with Monster Saliva and Monster Bone — rare crafting materials for Grandmaster tier gear.

Stop 2 — Stream Cave northwest of Flovive (Trousers + Steel Sword + Boots)

6From Flovive, head toward the Cockatrice Inn (northwest). Follow the stream east into the inland hills when you reach it. The stream ends at a cave — jump in.
7At the first junction: take the far left corridor (the longer route). It ends in a dead end with a Giant Centipede. Kill it. Loot the witcher's corpse behind it.
8Key navigation trick: follow the Bear School symbols carved into the cave walls. These glowing bear-head symbols appear at key decision points throughout the cave system.
9After the corpse: trousers + steel sword + boots diagrams.
10💎 Bonus: On the way to the stream cave, a bandit camp sits near the Cockatrice Inn. The camp leader carries a "sealed crate key" — the corresponding crate is inside the cave itself (near the entrance), containing a masterwork oil formula.

4. Danger Zones & Bonus Loot

⚠ Danger Zones

🔴 Risk 1 — Giant Centipede Surprise

It burrows under the cave floor and surfaces directly under Geralt. If you're moving slowly through the cave, it will ambush you mid-step. As you approach the large chamber, hold Yrden ready.

⚠ Risk 2 — Cave Navigation Without Cat Potion

Both caves are genuinely dark. The Bear School symbols are your navigation aid, but without Cat potion or sufficient visibility, you can easily miss the correct junction. Drink Cat before entering.

⚠ Risk 3 — Don't Skip the Longer Corridor

Many players take the short path at the junction in Cave 2 and find nothing, then assume the cave is "empty." The diagrams are specifically at the end of the LONGER, less-obvious corridor.

💎 Bonus Loot Along the Route

💎 Bonus 1 — Monster Saliva & Monster Bone

The centipede's chamber in Cave 1 has a secondary tunnel behind an Aard-breakable boulder. Inside: a chest with Monster Saliva and Monster Bone — rare crafting materials for Grandmaster tier gear.

💎 Bonus 2 — Insectoid Oil Ingredients

Both Giant Centipedes can drop Insectoid Oil ingredients upon death (Centipede components). These are used in crafting Specter Oil and White Honey — worth picking up.

5. Set Bonus & Crafting

Grandmaster Ursine Set Bonuses

  • 3 pieces: When Quen shield shatters, chance to auto-cast new Quen at no cost; chance increases 5% per set piece
  • 6 pieces: All damage from Quen sign abilities increased by 200%

Best Playstyle

Full heavy armor tanking with Quen active → Quen Discharge active ability converts absorbed damage into explosive release → 200% bonus amplifies explosion → combine with Greater Quen decoction for maximum absorption. This build turns your defense into your strongest offensive tool.

6. Build Recommendations

Quen Tank Cannon

  • Armor: Full Grandmaster Ursine Set (6 pieces)
  • Key Skills: Quen Discharge, Active Shield, Adrenaline Burst
  • Strategy: Maintain Quen shield at all times. Absorb enemy attacks to charge Quen Discharge. The 200% bonus from 6-piece set turns each Quen explosion into a devastating counter. The 3-piece bonus automatically re-casts Quen when your shield breaks — keeping you protected while you chain explosions.
  • Best Against: Groups of fast-attacking enemies (Nekkers, Drowners, bandit swarms) where constant hits feed your Quen absorption and you can explode repeatedly.

Immortal Tank (Alternative)

  • With Bear School Techniques: The general skill "Bear School Techniques" (equipped in the brown skill tree) grants +25% heavy armor bonus and +5% adrenaline point generation per piece of heavy armor. Since the Grandmaster Ursine set is all heavy armor, this adds an additional +150% armor rating on top of the already massive defense — making you nearly unkillable against physical damage while the Quen bonuses handle the offense.
