1. Why You Need This

The Grandmaster Ursine set's 6-piece bonus is one of the most dramatic in the game: all damage dealt by Quen sign abilities increases by 200%. The 3-piece bonus paves the way — when your active Quen shield shatters, there is a chance a new one activates automatically at no stamina cost, with the chance increasing by 5% for each piece of the set worn.

Heavy armor players gravitate toward the Ursine set for its raw defense stats. The Quen bonus makes it a defensive set with a specific offensive angle, especially if your build already invests in Quen abilities.

The verified route begins at a partially collapsed house between Flovive and Tufo Vineyard, then moves north to Owl Eye Grottos. The second section is the one where Bear School wall symbols guide you through the cave system toward the final diagrams.

3. Practical Route

Stop 1 — Collapsed House near Tufo Vineyard (Silver Sword + Armor + Gauntlets)

1From Flovive, head north toward Tufo Vineyard. Between the village and the vineyard, look for the partially collapsed house tied to Junod of Belhaven's contract.
2Go through or around the house to the backyard. Use Aard to clear the rubble and reveal the small cavern system below.
3Just inside, check the small alcove to the right. The chest there holds the silver sword, armor, and gauntlets diagrams, plus the note that sends the route to Owl Eye Grottos.
4If you continue deeper into the tunnel, there is a guarded treasure at the end, but it is separate from the diagram route.

Stop 2 — Owl Eye Grottos (Trousers + Steel Sword + Boots)

5Travel north to Owl Eye Grottos. Drop into the cave entrance and prepare for a giant centipede almost as soon as you reach the lower floor.
6After the first centipede, return to the waterfall ledge. In the opposite corner, break the rock wall with Aard and read Junod's warning to follow his marks.
7Continue through the cave system, following the Bear School symbols on the walls. The route eventually reaches another river and a ledge drop.
8After the drop, another giant centipede appears down the left path. Kill it, then search the skeleton in the same room for the trousers, steel sword, and boots diagrams.
9This cave overlaps with "Contract: The Tufo Monster." The contract is higher level, so combine the routes only if your build can handle the stronger contract enemies.

4. Danger Zones & Route Notes

⚠ Danger Zones

🔴 Risk 1 — Giant Centipede Surprise

It burrows under the cave floor and surfaces directly under Geralt. If you're moving slowly through the cave, it will ambush you mid-step. As you approach the large chamber, hold Yrden ready.

⚠ Risk 2 — Owl Eye Grottos Navigation

The Bear School symbols matter in Owl Eye Grottos. Without Cat potion or enough visibility, it is easy to miss the marked path and waste time in the cave system.

⚠ Risk 3 — Tufo Monster Overlap

Owl Eye Grottos is also used by "Contract: The Tufo Monster." The contract is higher level than the scavenger hunt; do not merge the two objectives unless you intend to fight the stronger contract enemies.

💎 Route Notes

💎 Guarded Treasure Boundary

The collapsed-house tunnel has a guarded treasure deeper inside, but the Ursine diagrams are in the small alcove chest near the start. Treat any extra loot as optional, not part of the required diagram route.

5. Set Bonus & Crafting

Grandmaster Ursine Set Bonuses

  • 3 pieces: When Quen shield shatters, chance to auto-cast new Quen at no cost; chance increases 5% per set piece
  • 6 pieces: All damage from Quen sign abilities increased by 200%

Best Playstyle

Use the set as a Quen-heavy defensive build: keep Quen active, let the set bonus support Quen abilities, and treat Quen Discharge as an optional synergy if your skill setup already includes it. Avoid presenting the set as universally stronger than every other damage option.

6. Build Recommendations

Quen Tank Cannon

  • Armor: Full Grandmaster Ursine Set (6 pieces)
  • Key Skills: Quen Discharge, Active Shield, Adrenaline Burst
  • Strategy: Maintain Quen during risky trades. The 6-piece bonus supports damage from Quen sign abilities, while the 3-piece bonus gives a chance for a free replacement shield when the active Quen shield breaks.
  • Best Against: Groups of fast-attacking enemies (Nekkers, Drowners, bandit swarms) where constant hits feed your Quen absorption and you can explode repeatedly.

Immortal Tank (Alternative)

  • With Bear School Techniques: The general skill grants +5% maximum Vitality and +5% strong attack damage for each heavy armor piece worn. Only armor pieces count, not swords, so the four Ursine armor pieces add +20% maximum Vitality and +20% strong attack damage.
