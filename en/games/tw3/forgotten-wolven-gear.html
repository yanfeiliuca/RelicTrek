1. Why You Need This

Forgotten Wolf School Gear was introduced with Patch 4.0 alongside the quest In the Eternal Fire's Shadow. The safe core route is split in two parts: complete the Devil's Pit quest to receive the base diagrams, then go to Kaer Morhen keep to read Osmund's notes and Vesemir's note for the higher tiers.

The set's unusual part is its tier structure. Unlike older school sets, the Forgotten Wolf line uses only standard, mastercrafted, and grandmaster tiers in a normal playthrough.

The stable confirmed set bonuses are also specific: the 3-piece bonus increases potion duration, and the 6-piece bonus makes Aard deal extra damage to enemies affected by Yrden.

3. Practical Route

Step 1 — Start at Devil's Pit

1Go to Devil's Pit in Velen and speak to the young Eternal Fire deacon outside the mine to begin In the Eternal Fire's Shadow. He gives Geralt the key to the gate.

Step 2 — Clear the mine and finish the quest

2Work through the Devil's Pit mine, follow Reinald's trail, and complete the Reinald / red miasmal confrontation. The verified reward from the quest is the Forgotten Wolven diagrams.
3The quest page also notes a practical warning: you cannot reenter the mine after defeating the miasmal, so finish your exploration before ending the encounter.

Step 3 — Collect the upgrade notes at Kaer Morhen

4Go inside Kaer Morhen keep. Just inside, the bookshelves are on the left. Climb the nearby ladder to the second level and walk to the far end of the platform. Osmund's notes and Vesemir's note sit on a bookshelf there.
5Read both notes to unlock the mastercrafted and grandmaster levels for the entire set.

4. Route Warnings & Supported Notes

⚠ Danger Zones

🔴 Risk 1 — Quest Giver Confusion

The verified quest giver is a young Eternal Fire deacon outside Devil's Pit. Older summaries that reduce him to a generic priest are imprecise.

⚠ Risk 2 — No Return To The Mine

Once the red miasmal is defeated, the mine cannot be reentered. This is the most important route warning confirmed on the quest page.

⚠ Risk 3 — Kaer Morhen Shelf Is Easy To Miss

The upgrade hunt is real, but the exact bookshelf inside Kaer Morhen keep is easy to overlook if you expect a louder quest marker. The ladder-and-platform route is the reliable cue.

💎 Supported Notes

💎 Bonus 1 — Patch 4.0 Set Identity

The scavenger-hunt page explicitly notes that the Forgotten Wolf armor design is based on Netflix's season 1 and season 2 looks. That part is supported; many extra “best-looking set” claims are opinion, not route fact.

💎 Bonus 2 — Short Upgrade Chain

Compared with older school sets, the normal-playthrough line is shorter: standard, mastercrafted, grandmaster. That makes this set easier to audit than pages that mix in enhanced and superior tiers by habit.
