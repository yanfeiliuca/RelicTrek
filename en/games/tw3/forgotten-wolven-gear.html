1. Why You Need This

The Forgotten Wolven gear was added in the next-gen update of The Witcher 3 — free to all players — and is directly modeled after the armor Henry Cavill wore as Geralt in the Netflix series. If you've never heard of it, you're not alone: the quest that unlocks it starts at an unassuming spot in Velen with a priest nobody seems to talk to.

The set has a unique tier structure: it skips the Enhanced and Superior upgrades entirely, going directly from basic (level 20) to Mastercrafted (level 34) to Grandmaster (level 40). The Grandmaster version matches season 2 of the show — a different visual from the base game version.

Functionally, the set boosts potion duration and increases Aard damage when used with Yrden — a genuinely useful combination for mid-to-late game that holds up as a secondary option even in endgame content.

3. Optimal Route

Step 1 — Prepare supplies

1Before going to Devil's Pit: have Dwarven Spirit and Celandine in your inventory. Both are sold at herbalists and distillers throughout Velen.

Step 2 — Reach Devil's Pit (central Velen)

2Fast travel to the Hanged Man's Tree signpost. Devil's Pit is southeast, near the crossroads. On the road approaching it, you'll encounter a priest of the Eternal Fire — speak with him to start "In the Eternal Fire's Shadow."

Step 3 — Clear the mine

3Enter the mine. Kill the bandits occupying it. Follow the quest marker to find Reinald's remains and the imprisoned spirit.

Step 4 — Brew the potion and confront Reinald

4Use the alchemist's table in the mine to brew a specific potion (Dwarven Spirit + Celandine + other common ingredients). Drink it to see/fight Reinald's spirit. During the dialogue, choose your preferred ending — all choices result in receiving the diagrams.
💎 BonusThe mine has a back tunnel blocked by a wooden barricade. Beyond it: a chest with Rune of the First Ekimmara (a weapon rune with 15% HP steal on hit — one of the best combat runes in the game).

Step 5 — Collect the upgrade diagrams at Kaer Morhen

5Fast travel to Kaer Morhen. Enter the main keep building. Immediately inside, turn left — bookshelves line the wall. Climb the nearby ladder to the second level of shelves. Walk toward the far end of the platform. Two notes sit side-by-side on the shelf: Osmund's notes and Vesemir's note. Read both to unlock Mastercrafted and Grandmaster upgrade diagrams.
💎 BonusWhile at Kaer Morhen, the training yard (outside the main gate) has an unmarked chest containing a Lesser Wolf glyph set — three lower-tier Wolven glyphs that are useful for early-game armor enhancement.

4. Danger Zones & Bonus Loot

⚠ Danger Zones

🔴 Risk 1 — The quest potion

You cannot skip brewing the potion. If you don't have Dwarven Spirit and Celandine, you'll need to leave the mine, buy them, and return. Save yourself the trip — bring them before entering.

⚠ Risk 2 — Reinald's spirit fight

He uses Witcher signs against you. Quen up before the fight. He has predictable patterns — dodge his Aard blast and punish with fast attacks.

⚠ Risk 3 — Kaer Morhen bookshelves are not quest-marked

The game gives you the Scavenger Hunt quest but doesn't place a map marker on the exact shelf location. "Bookshelves to the left of the entrance, climb the ladder, walk to the far end of the platform." No glowing marker. Many players search the wrong area for 20 minutes.

💎 Bonus Loot Along the Route

💎 Bonus 1 — Rune of the First Ekimmara

The back tunnel in Devil's Pit mine contains this weapon rune — 15% HP steal on hit. One of the best combat runes in the game, and completely missable if you don't explore the mine.

💎 Bonus 2 — Grandmaster visual

The Grandmaster Forgotten Wolven set (level 40) has a completely different visual design from the basic version — it matches Henry Cavill's season 2 appearance, which many fans consider even more iconic than season 1.

5. Crafting Guide

Forgotten Wolven Set Skills

  • Increases potion duration — all potion effects last longer with more pieces equipped.
  • Aard sign damage increased when Yrden trap is active in the area — a unique synergy between two signs.

Best Playstyle

Mid-tier hybrid — potion-sustained combat with Aard sign supplementing damage in Yrden zones; transitions well into a Griffin-style sign build before going full Grandmaster.

6. Build Recommendations

Build A — Mid-Game Hybrid (Level 20–33)

  • Weapon: Any Viper or Feline swords available at your level
  • Armor: Full Forgotten Wolven Set (6 pieces, basic)
  • Key Skills: Aard Intensity, Yrden Intensity, Acquired Tolerance

This build leverages the set's dual bonuses: extended potion duration keeps your combat buffs active longer, while the Aard-Yrden synergy lets you control crowds and deal bonus damage simultaneously. Use Yrden to trap enemies, then blast them with Aard for increased damage.

Build B — Grandmaster Transition (Level 40+)

  • Weapon: Grandmaster Wolven or Forgotten Wolven swords
  • Armor: Grandmaster Forgotten Wolven Set (6 pieces)
  • Key Skills: Aard Sweep, Magic Trap, heightened potion tolerance

The Grandmaster version with its season 2 visual is both a cosmetic flex and a functional endgame option. The expanded potion duration combined with Aard damage makes this a respectable secondary set even when you've unlocked other Grandmaster gear.
