Enhanced Feline Gear — The Dark Cave Cat Set

🕐 Route ~2 hrs📦 6 pieces📍 Velen⚠ Difficulty 2/5Level 20⚠ Cat Potion Required📅 Updated 2026-05-31

1. Why You Need This

The Enhanced Feline set is the third tier of the Cat School gear — the step between basic and Superior. Most players need it simply to progress the upgrade chain toward Mastercrafted and eventually Grandmaster.

But it's worth writing about on its own terms because of one specific diagram: the Enhanced Feline Gauntlets are located in a cave so dark that Cat potion is not optional — it's mandatory. Without it, you genuinely cannot see the chest. You can walk past it five times with a torch and miss it because the chest doesn't glow unless your vision adapts to the darkness. This is The Witcher 3 at its most atmospheric, and also at its most opaque for players who don't know that potions can change what you can perceive.

3. The Dark Cave — Gauntlets Route

The cave is northeast of Codgers Quarry, near the road. It looks like an ordinary Velen hillside cave entrance. Inside, the darkness is genuine — no ambient lighting, no lit torches, pure blackness.

1Brew and drink Cat potion before entering. Cat potion grants night vision that persists for the potion's duration.
2With Cat active, the cave interior becomes visible: a simple two-chamber layout. Navigate through the first chamber into the second.
3The chest with gauntlets diagrams is in the second chamber, against the far wall. Without Cat, the chest's outline disappears into the same black as everything else.

💎 Bonus: Hidden Alcoves

💎 Hidden Alchemist Stash

The cave also has two hidden alcoves detectable only with Witcher Senses (not visible even with Cat potion). These contain: alchemist's notes for a unique Ogroid Tropheum recipe, and 2x Vampire Blood — a high-value alchemy ingredient.

4. Danger Zones & Bonus Loot

⚠ Danger Zones

⚠ Risk 1 — Missing the chest without Cat potion

You can walk past this chest five times with a torch and still miss it. The darkness is absolute. Cat potion is not a suggestion — it's the only way to see the chest's outline. Brew it before entering, or you will leave the cave empty-handed.

⚠ Risk 2 — Hindhold ghost enemies

The Hindhold ruins where the armor diagram is located are haunted. Wraiths phase in and out of physical form — without Yrden active, your attacks pass through them. Cast Yrden before engaging and use Specter Oil on your silver sword.

💎 Bonus Loot Along the Route

💎 Bonus 1 — Ogroid Tropheum Recipe (Dark Cave)

The hidden alcove in the gauntlets cave contains an alchemist's notes with a unique Ogroid Tropheum recipe — rare loot that most players never find because the alcove is only detectable with Witcher Senses.

💎 Bonus 2 — Vampire Blood (Dark Cave)

The second hidden alcove contains 2x Vampire Blood, a high-value alchemy ingredient used in several Superior-tier potion recipes. Worth grabbing even if you don't need the gauntlets diagram.

5. Upgrade Path

The Enhanced Feline set is part of the Cat School upgrade chain. You must have crafted the Basic Feline set before you can upgrade to Enhanced. The upgrade requires taking each Basic piece to a Journeyman Armorer or Blacksmith along with the Enhanced diagram and the required crafting materials.

6. Build Notes

The Enhanced Feline set is a stepping stone in the Cat School progression. At level 20, it provides a meaningful upgrade over the Basic set with improved armor ratings and stronger fast attack bonuses. If you're running a fast attack build, crafting the Enhanced set immediately at level 20 gives you a significant power spike for the mid-game content in Velen and Novigrad.

Once you reach level 25, move on to the Superior Feline set — but keep every Enhanced piece in your stash, as each piece is required to craft the Superior version.
