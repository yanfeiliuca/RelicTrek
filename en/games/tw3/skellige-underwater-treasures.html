1. Why You Need This

Skellige is a difficult region. The land quests are extensive, the monsters are powerful, and the sea travel is dangerous. Most players complete Skellige by doing the main islands' question marks, finishing the story quests, and departing.

The sea zone question marks are routinely skipped. This is where the content is.

Skellige's underwater areas contain the densest concentration of Relic-quality weapons of any region in the game. Sunken ships, partially collapsed sea caves, and underwater entrances to cave systems that don't appear on any land map. Drowner corpses in specific underwater locations hold diagrams for equipment that cannot be found anywhere else.

The sea is also where several of Skellige's most unusual treasure hunts begin — triggered by finding a note on a shipwreck that references coordinates leading to a sequence of discoveries.

2. Underwater Regions to Prioritize

Ard Skellig Coastline (North + West)

Multiple underwater ruins along the northern shore. Approach from cliffside paths and dive at the base of the cliffs. Chests and Drowner camps at 5–15 meter depth. Key items: Superior Griffin gear diagram components, Enhanced Bear gear components.

Hindarsfjall Sea Caves

The island of Hindarsfjall has several partially submerged cave entrances only accessible from water. Inside: Drowner packs, Water Hag variants, and chests with Mastercrafted material components.

An Skellig Area (Southeast)

Several shipwrecks in this zone. Drowner corpses on the wrecks drop higher-tier items than equivalent land enemies. Look for wreck structures with distinct hull shapes — they always have loot worth collecting.

Faroe Sea Routes

The narrow channels around Faroe island have sea-floor question marks that appear only in water. Navigate these using boat travel and dive at each marker.

3. Optimal Route

Phase 1 — Ard Skellig coastal sweep: Begin at the northern Ard Skellig shore (Kaer Trolde harbor heading north). Work counterclockwise around the main island, diving at each coastal/sea question mark. This covers the most item-dense zone first.

Phase 2 — Smaller islands: Hindarsfjall → An Skellig → Faroe. Each island has several sea-specific question marks worth visiting.

Phase 3 — Open water shipwrecks: The open sea between Skellige islands has several shipwreck locations that appear only in water zone maps. These require dedicated sailing trips.

4. Hazard Guide

🔴 Sirens

Airborne enemies that attack boats. Use the crossbow for air targets. They cluster in specific sea zones — prepare crossbow bolts before sailing heavily patrolled corridors. Critical: Sirens can destroy your boat if ignored. Kill them before diving, or your boat may not be there when you surface.

⚠ Water Hags in Sea Caves

More dangerous than standard Drowners. Quen shield before entering caves. Water Hags' underground surfacing attack is the primary cause of unexpected damage in enclosed cave spaces.

⚠ Breath Meter Management

Geralt has a breath meter underwater. Long dives require coming up for air. Plan routes to minimize ascent/descent cycles. The Killer Whale potion extends breath duration if you have it.

5. Best Loot to Target

  • Relic-quality silver swords from An Skellig shipwrecks
  • Mastercrafted material components from Hindarsfjall sea caves
  • Superior/Enhanced Witcher gear diagrams from Ard Skellig underwater ruins
  • Unique Skellige-exclusive crafting recipes from deep sea chests

6. The Hidden Second Layer

Skellige's sea isn't empty space between islands — it's a full second layer of content that the game's land-focused map design trains players to ignore. The question marks in the water look identical to the ones on land.

A player who skips the sea question marks has consciously chosen to leave behind some of the game's best equipment. This guide exists so that choice is informed. The four hours spent on this route yield more Relic-quality items per hour than any other region in the game.
