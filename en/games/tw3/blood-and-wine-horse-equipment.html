1. Why You Need This

The safest way to use The Warble of a Smitten Knight as a rewards page is to stick to the items the current source page explicitly names. Earlier versions of this page mixed tournament rewards with unrelated horse-gear claims.

The verified reward structure is narrower but still useful: the race awards a customized saddle, the shooting game awards a customized crossbow, and winning the full tournament awards a customized sword. The item labels change depending on whether Geralt enters as Geralt of Rivia or Ravix of Fourhorn, but the function of each reward slot stays the same.

3. Winning the Tournament Events

Horse Race

The horse race is the explicitly verified source for the saddle reward. If you care about the named mounted reward, this is the event you cannot skip.

Shooting Game

The shooting competition awards the customized crossbow. This is a ranged reward, not horse equipment, and should stay separate from saddle claims.

Full Tournament Completion

Winning the tournament overall awards the customized sword. The tournament page should therefore be treated as a broader reward guide, not as a horse-equipment-only route.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Optional Events Are Easy to Miss

The tournament events are optional branches of the quest. Players who rush the Vivienne storyline can miss the reward events entirely.

⚠ Do Not Mix in Unverified Horse Gear

Current verification for this page supports the saddle, crossbow, and sword rewards listed above. Do not fold in blinders, saddlebags, or other horse items without a dedicated source pass.

5. Related Reward Boundaries

Iris does not belong on this page. Iris is a Hearts of Stone ending-route weapon tied to Olgierd, not a Blood and Wine tournament reward.

Keep the tournament reward page focused on what The Warble of a Smitten Knight explicitly awards, and route unrelated TW3 weapons through their own verified pages.

6. Why Most Players Never Get This

Players often remember the tournament as a colorful side story and not as a reward source. That makes it easy to forget the race, shooting game, and full-tournament item payouts if you never finish every branch.

The bigger lesson from this audit is factual discipline: tournament rewards are real, but they should be listed exactly as verified rather than inflated into a generic "best horse gear" bundle.
