1. Why You Need This

Iris is a Hearts of Stone relic steel sword tied directly to Olgierd von Everec. It is obtained through Olgierd's ending route, not through a Toussaint tournament reward path.

The reason to care is twofold: it is one of the most story-specific swords in The Witcher 3, and it has a distinct combat rhythm. After several fast attacks, including blocked strikes, the blade can flash red. A strong attack then releases stored energy in a powerful blow, but costs Geralt part of his vitality.

The practical application: use fast hits to build charge, watch for the red blade, then spend the charged strong attack only when the trade is safe. Iris rewards timing and route planning more than raw stat chasing.

Iris is obtained during Whatsoever a Man Soweth..., the Hearts of Stone finale, if Geralt saves Olgierd.

2. How the Red Charge Works

  • Iris charges during combat after several fast attacks in a short window.
  • Blocked strikes can still help build the charge.
  • When the blade flashes red, a strong attack releases the stored energy.
  • The charged strong attack costs part of Geralt's vitality, so avoid using it recklessly at low health.

Optimal combat pattern: Build pressure with fast attacks, confirm the red glow, create a safe strong-attack window, then release the charged strike.

3. Obtaining Iris

Quest: Whatsoever a Man Soweth... — the final Hearts of Stone quest.

The route depends on the Olgierd / Gaunter ending branch:

1Progress Hearts of Stone: reach the ending quest Whatsoever a Man Soweth....
2Prepare the intervention route: Geralt must be able to challenge Gaunter O'Dimm instead of simply allowing him to take Olgierd.
3Save Olgierd: intervene for Olgierd and complete Gaunter's challenge.
4Receive Iris: after Olgierd is freed, he gives Geralt Iris as a token of gratitude and departure from his old life.

If you choose the outcome where Olgierd is not saved, this reward is not obtained from that ending.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Wrong Route Assumption

Do not route Iris through the Toussaint tournament. That questline gives its own rewards, while Iris belongs to Hearts of Stone.

⚠ Ending Branch

If you do not save Olgierd during Whatsoever a Man Soweth..., Iris is not awarded in that outcome.

⚠ Vitality Cost

The red charged strong attack is powerful, but it costs vitality. Do not spend it when Geralt is already one mistake away from dying.

5. En-Route Bonus

💎 Olgierd Resolution

The same route resolves Olgierd's bargain and gives the sword narrative weight beyond its combat value.

💎 Relic Steel Sword

Iris is a three-slot relic steel sword and remains useful as a build-defining weapon for players who like strong-attack timing.

6. Build Synergy

Iris shines when fast attacks can safely build charge and a strong-attack opening is available. It is strongest in controlled steel-sword fights where Geralt can decide when to spend vitality for burst pressure.

Against chaotic groups or when Geralt is low on health, hold back. The weapon's ceiling is high, but the vitality cost means poor timing can turn Iris against you.
