1. Why You Need This

The base game's treasure hunt system is well-known: find a lead, follow clues, discover a diagram in a guarded location. It is how most of the Witcher gear is obtained.

Blood and Wine has its own version of this system, entirely separate from the base game quests. The B&W treasure hunts lead to unique equipment specific to Toussaint, including diagram locations for gear that isn't obtainable any other way.

The problem: almost no one knows they exist. The B&W treasure hunts don't appear in most guide compilations because they're easy to conflate with the base game's hunts. They begin differently — some start from notice boards in Toussaint, others from notes found during exploration, others from conversations with specific NPCs.

A player who completes Blood and Wine without ever starting a B&W treasure hunt has missed an entire equipment progression layer built specifically for the DLC.

2. B&W Treasure Hunt Categories

Type 1 — Notice Board Hunts

Posted on Toussaint's notice boards. Easy to miss because players often skip boards in DLC areas. Toussaint has notice boards in Beauclair, several village squares, and outside the Tourney Grounds — check ALL of them on arrival.

Type 2 — Note-Triggered Hunts

Found inside chests or on bodies during Toussaint exploration. A note references a treasure and the quest begins automatically.

Type 3 — NPC-Conversation Hunts

Begin by talking to a specific Toussaint villager, merchant, or vineyard worker who mentions something worth finding.

Type 4 — Witcher Gear Scavenger Hunts (B&W Additions)

The Grandmaster tier additions to each school's gear chain are technically B&W treasure hunts — these are separate from the base game Parts 1–5 and constitute "Part 6" for each school.

3. Key Treasure Hunt Routes

Treasure Hunt: The Warble of a Smitten Knight

While technically a quest, this Toussaint-exclusive event involves a series of challenges across the B&W map and rewards unique equipment including the Iris sword. Begin by reading the tournament notice in Beauclair.

Treasure Hunt: Wine Wars (Series)

A Toussaint-specific questline involving competing vineyards. Completing the full Wine Wars series unlocks unique merchant access and diagram availability at specific Toussaint locations. The questline has multiple phases across different vineyards — see each through to completion.

Hidden Vineyard Caches (Exploration-Triggered)

Several of Toussaint's vineyards contain hidden underground cellars accessible via Aard-breakable walls or hidden door mechanisms. Each typically contains a Relic-quality item or a Mastercrafted diagram:

  • Vermentino Winery underground — Mastercrafted crafting components
  • La Quinta Pettina cellar — Unique ring with Toussaint bonus
  • The Arthach vineyard cave — Relic weapon
  • Sansretour Valley ruins — Note triggering a multi-stage hunt for a B&W-specific Silver Sword

5. Hazard Guide

⚠ Risk 1 — Never Starting

The most common failure is never seeing the B&W notice board posts. Toussaint has notice boards in Beauclair, several village squares, and outside the Tourney Grounds. Check all of them on first arrival in Toussaint.

⚠ Risk 2 — Aard Walls in Vineyards

Multiple Toussaint cellar entrances are behind Aard-breakable walls with no visual indicator. If a vineyard building has unexplored dead-end rooms, cast Aard at suspicious flat walls. One will likely collapse.

⚠ Risk 3 — Quest Order for Wine Wars

The Wine Wars series has multiple phases. Completing them in the wrong order can lock you out of later rewards. Follow each vineyard's quest chain to completion before starting the next.

⚠ Risk 4 — Missable Notes

Some note-triggered hunts begin from lootable items on corpses that despawn after certain story milestones. Prioritize exploration early in the B&W storyline before advancing the main quest.

6. The RelicTrek Advantage

B&W treasure hunts represent the most invisible content in the expansion. The game's UI doesn't distinguish B&W hunts from base game hunts. The notice boards don't glow. The NPCs don't have quest markers until you speak to them.

This information asymmetry is exactly what RelicTrek is designed to break. Every treasure hunt on this page is content that exists in the game, was designed and balanced by the developers, and is routinely missed by the vast majority of players who complete Blood and Wine.

If you find one item from this page that you would have otherwise missed, the four hours spent on the full route have already paid for themselves.
