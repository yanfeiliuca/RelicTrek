1. How Aerondight Works

Aerondight is the reward for There Can Be Only One in Blood and Wine. Geralt must prove Valor, Generosity, Compassion, Honor, and Wisdom, return to Lac Célavy, and defeat the Hermit before the Lady of the Lake awards the sword.

Its combat system has two separate values. Hits build up to ten charges: fast attacks add one, strong attacks add two, and taking damage removes charges unless Quen absorbs the hit. Each charge adds 10% sword damage; at ten charges, attacks always deal critical damage.

A kill at ten charges consumes the stack and permanently adds weapon damage when the current character-level ceiling still has room. The charge cap itself stays at ten. The item description tracks the permanent increase already earned and the maximum currently available at Geralt's level.

That permanent increase can remain useful across later levels and New Game Plus, but Aerondight is not automatically the best silver sword at every moment. Its value depends on how much of the available growth has been unlocked and which secondary bonuses a build needs.

The expansion provides multiple valid opportunities for several virtues. The Hermit at Lac Célavy can report which virtues remain incomplete.

Final Trial

After all five virtues are recorded, return to Lac Célavy and defeat the Hermit. The Lady of the Lake awards Aerondight after this duel.

3. Maximizing Aerondight

Build ten charges before the killing blow. Lower-level human or nonhuman enemies can be practical targets because the silver sword deals reduced damage to them, giving Geralt more hits before the kill.

Do not confuse the ten-charge combat stack with the permanent damage increase. Missing the ceiling at one level does not remove future growth space: higher character levels open more room. Already-earned permanent damage continues into New Game Plus.

Aerondight begins without sockets. The Runewright can add up to three, allowing normal runes or a runeword without changing the charge mechanic.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Five Virtues Are Not the Final Step

Aerondight is not awarded immediately when the fifth virtue is recorded. The Hermit duel at Lac Célavy still has to be completed.

⚠ Taking Damage Removes Charges

A hit can remove the combat stack before the killing blow. Quen can prevent that loss when it absorbs the damage.

⚠ Growth Has a Level-Based Ceiling

A full-charge kill only adds permanent damage while the current level-based allowance has room. Check the item description before farming more upgrades.

5. How Aerondight Compares

Aerondight fills the silver-sword slot and rewards players who can preserve charges and keep its permanent damage near the current level ceiling. Another silver sword can still be preferable when its fixed bonuses better match a specific build.

A steel sword is chosen separately for human and nonhuman enemies. The Iris steel sword is one possible Hearts of Stone option, not a required pairing.

6. When Aerondight Is Worth Maintaining

Aerondight is most useful when its permanent damage increase is kept near the allowance opened by Geralt's level. If that growth falls behind, its current damage may not exceed a newer relic sword with stronger fixed bonuses.

The acquisition route combines choices across Toussaint with a final duel, while the weapon itself rewards clean combat through charge retention. Treat those as two separate checklists: finish the virtues and Hermit route first, then maintain the weapon's level-based growth over time.
