Aerondight — The Silver Sword That Grows Stronger With Every Kill

1. Why You Need This

Aerondight has appeared in multiple Witcher games. It is always in the same place — a lake, a lady, and a question about what kind of witcher Geralt has chosen to be.

In Blood and Wine, that question becomes five concrete acts. Prove valor, compassion, generosity, honor, and wisdom during the expansion's quests. Return to Lac Célavy. The Lady appears. She gives you the sword.

The mechanic is unlike anything else in the game: each hit builds a charge (maximum 10). At full charges, attacks deal bonus damage. Killing an enemy while at full charges permanently raises the maximum charge cap — and with it, the damage ceiling. This permanent increase persists through saves and carries into New Game Plus. There is no reset.

By the end of a full Blood and Wine playthrough, a player who has been using Aerondight will have a sword that deals more damage than any other silver weapon in the game. The more you use it, the more it is worth using.

2. The Five Virtues

VirtueHow to Prove It
ValorFight a difficult monster in Blood and Wine without using any Witcher Signs (no Igni, no Yrden, no Axii). Pure sword combat only.
GenerosityWhen given an opportunity to give money or items to someone in need without obvious reward, choose to do so. B&W has several dialogue moments where this applies — give freely.
CompassionIn a quest involving a cursed or suffering creature, choose the option that breaks the curse rather than the pragmatic option that ends the encounter more efficiently.
HonorIn any duel or combat scenario, when the option to spare a defeated opponent appears, choose to let them live.
WisdomIn a dialogue with an elder NPC or a scholar, choose the option that acknowledges you don't know something, or defer to their expertise rather than claiming authority.
After All Five

Visit Lac Célavy — the lake visible from Corvo Bianco Vineyard (Geralt's estate). The Lady appears and gifts Aerondight.

3. Maximizing Aerondight

Each kill at full charges adds 1 to the permanent maximum. The practical strategy: stay aggressive, maintain combat momentum, and kill enemies whenever the charge bar is full. At higher permanent maximums, each kill-at-max becomes disproportionately powerful.

In New Game Plus, Aerondight carries its accumulated maximum into the new playthrough — it starts at whatever level it reached in the original run. This makes it the only weapon that genuinely improves across multiple playthroughs.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Missed Virtue

The Lady doesn't explain which virtues you've proven. If she says Geralt is "not yet worthy," simply continue B&W quests while being deliberate about the five behaviors. Return to the lake anytime.

⚠ Valor Timing

Signs used unconsciously during intense combat may invalidate that combat for the Valor virtue. Be deliberate when attempting to prove Valor — don't cast anything. Disable Sign shortcuts if necessary.

⚠ Missable Virtue Windows

Some virtue opportunities are tied to specific quests that can be completed before the player knows about the virtue system. The safest approach: whenever a dialogue option involves mercy, generosity, humility, or restraint, choose that option.

5. How Aerondight Compares

Aerondight is not affected by the HoS ending choice (unlike Olgierd's Sword, which locks one weapon per playthrough). It is a pure B&W reward, available regardless of other choices. This makes it the most reliable endgame silver sword for players prioritizing consistency.

Combined with the Iris steel sword from the same DLC, a player can leave Blood and Wine with both a permanently-scaling silver sword and a burst-damage steel sword — a complete endgame weapon loadout.

6. The Only Weapon That Never Stops Growing

Every other weapon in The Witcher 3 is static — you find it, you equip it, its numbers never change. Aerondight is the only exception. Its growth mechanic makes it the closest thing the game has to a "signature weapon" that evolves with the player across the entire Blood and Wine expansion and beyond into New Game Plus.

The five virtues system also makes obtaining Aerondight a narrative accomplishment rather than a combat gate. You don't beat a boss to get this sword. You prove, across the entire expansion, what kind of witcher Geralt chooses to be.
