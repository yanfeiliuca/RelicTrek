1. Why You Need This

The Mastercrafted Feline set is the penultimate tier of the Cat School gear, and the strongest version available without the Blood and Wine DLC. Fast attack builds that aim for the Grandmaster Feline upgrade need this as their foundation — and there's no shortcut.

What most players don't realize: the diagrams are in six different locations spanning Velen and Novigrad, two of which require underwater diving, and one requires a specific blacksmith who is locked behind his own multi-step questline.

Hattori, the master swordsmith in Novigrad, is required to craft the swords. He won't help you until you complete "Of Swords and Dumplings" — a quest where you help him reopen his sword-making business by sourcing steel, dealing with a extortionist gang, and having dinner. If you've never spoken to Hattori and expect to just walk up and have him forge your Mastercrafted swords, you'll be standing in front of a dumpling seller.

3. Optimal Route

PREREQUISITE — Unlock Hattori

0Find Hattori in the Bits district of Novigrad (near the fish market). He's dressed as a dumpling vendor. Complete "Of Swords and Dumplings" — this involves getting iron ore, dealing with the Cleaver gang, and finishing with a meal at his friend's house. After completion, Hattori can forge master-level swords.
💎 BonusDuring "Of Swords and Dumplings," the gang's warehouse has a secondary safe (combination: 1-2-3-4) containing a Superior Feline sword diagram — ahead of schedule.

Stop 1 — Drahim Castle (Steel Sword + Armor)

1Fast travel to Drahim Castle in eastern Novigrad. Enter and descend to the basement level (two ladders). The steel sword diagram is in the corner chest. The armor diagram is in a hidden side room — there's a false wall accessible by pressing the stone switch on the eastern corridor wall.

Stop 2 — Codgers Quarry area (Trousers + Gauntlets)

2Southeast Velen, near Codgers Quarry. The diagrams are in two separate chest locations near the quarry — one inside the quarry building itself, one in a small cave to the east. Both have low-level bandit guards.

Stop 3 — Grassy Knoll shipwreck (Silver Sword, underwater)

3Northwest of the Novigrad docks area. A half-submerged ship lies near Grassy Knoll. Dive down alongside the hull — the chest is against the ship's interior ribs. Loot the silver sword diagram.

Stop 4 — West coast shipwreck (Boots, underwater)

4Novigrad's western coastline has a second sunken ship. Dive to the wreck. Find the chest near the ship's bow.

4. Danger & Bonus

⚠ Danger

⚠ Danger — Hattori as Gatekeeper

The entire Mastercrafted tier of Cat School swords requires Hattori. If you're at level 31 trying to craft these swords and Hattori won't cooperate, you haven't done his quest. Do it first.

💎 Bonus Loot

💎 Bonus — Temerian Armor at Drahim Castle

Drahim Castle's top floor (not the basement route) has a Temerian knight's locked chest requiring a key found on the captain outside — it contains a Temerian Armor set, one of the most visually distinctive non-school armors in the game.

6. Build Recommendations

Build A — Pure Fast Attack (No DLC)

  • Weapon: Mastercrafted Feline Steel + Silver Swords
  • Armor: Full Mastercrafted Feline Set (6 pieces)
  • Key Skills: Whirl, Fleet Footed, Cat School Techniques

This is the strongest fast-attack build available without Blood and Wine. The full set bonuses from the Feline line reward aggressive play — light attacks deal bonus damage and generate adrenaline quickly. Use Whirl to clear groups and light attack chains against single targets.

Build B — Foundation for Grandmaster

  • Weapon: Mastercrafted Feline Swords (keep these)
  • Armor: Full Mastercrafted Feline Set (keep all 6 pieces)
  • Important: Do NOT sell or dismantle any Mastercrafted Feline piece

The Mastercrafted Feline set is the prerequisite for Grandmaster Feline in Blood and Wine. Lafargue in Beauclair will not upgrade your gear unless you still possess every Mastercrafted piece. Keep them all in your stash or inventory.
