1. Why You Need This

Grandmaster Feline is the Blood and Wine upgrade tier for Cat School gear. Its verified set bonuses reward aggressive sword play: the 3-piece bonus makes strong attacks increase fast attack damage for 5 seconds, while the 6-piece bonus makes rear attacks deal 50% more damage and stun at the cost of 1 adrenaline point.

The route itself is compact but easy to mislabel. The verified quest locations are Villa Vedette / Bartolomé de Launay's house, Arthach Palace Ruins, and Pits of Brume. Keep the previous Mastercrafted Feline pieces, because the Grandmaster pieces are crafted as upgrades rather than standalone replacements.

3. Practical Route

Start from the Arthach Palace Ruins signpost in northern Toussaint.

Stop 1 — Villa Vedette / Bartolomé de Launay's House (Armor Diagram)

1Walk east of the signpost along the road. The estate is walled — walk around the perimeter until you find scaffolding to climb over. Inside: defeat the bandits. Enter the red house on the north side of the courtyard. Interact with the painting on the wall. The Grandmaster Feline armor diagram is behind it, along with a letter pointing you to the next location.
2Read the letter to Bartolomé de Launay to move the trail toward Arthach Palace Ruins.

Stop 2 — Arthach Palace Ruins (Trousers + Boots)

3The ruins are a hanse base with a large bandit group and dogs. Prioritize the fire runner before the signal is lit; if the signal goes up, reinforcements can increase the fight dramatically.
4Loot the chest near the signal fire for the boots and trousers diagrams plus the hanse leader's notes. Reading the notes points the hunt to Pits of Brume.

Stop 3 — Pits of Brume Cave (Steel Sword + Silver Sword + Gauntlets)

5Pits of Brume is a guarded cave northwest of the Trading Post signpost. Expect two archespores in tight quarters, with exploding pods making the cave awkward to fight through.
6After the archespores are handled, search the dark corner with the campfire and cot. The nearby container holds the silver sword, steel sword, and gauntlets diagrams plus Lexandre's journal.

Step 4 — Craft at Lafargue in Beauclair

7Return to Grandmaster Smith Lazare Lafargue in Beauclair. Crafting the Grandmaster pieces uses the matching Mastercrafted Feline pieces as components.

4. Route Risks

⚠ Danger Zones

⚠ Risk 1 — Arthach Fire Runner

The hanse base can escalate if the fire runner reaches the signal. Clear enough enemies to loot the signal-fire chest safely, or expect a larger fight.

⚠ Risk 2 — Pits of Brume Archespores

The final cave has two archespores, and the tight space can make their exploding pods more dangerous. Create distance before committing to attacks.

⚠ Risk 3 — Missing Mastercrafted Pieces

Grandmaster crafting consumes the matching Mastercrafted Feline pieces. Keep them until Lafargue upgrades the set.

5. Set Bonus & Crafting

Grandmaster Feline Set Bonuses

  • 3 pieces: Strong attacks increase fast attack damage by 10% per set piece for 5 seconds
  • 6 pieces: Rear attacks deal 50% more damage and stun, consuming 1 adrenaline point

Crafting Note

Each Grandmaster Feline piece is crafted as an upgrade from the matching Mastercrafted piece. Check Lafargue's crafting screen for the exact component list before dismantling stored materials.

Supported Playstyle

Use strong attacks to trigger the 3-piece bonus, then use fast attacks while the short damage window is active. The 6-piece bonus rewards safe rear attacks when enemy positioning allows it.

6. Build Recommendations

Rear Attack Assassin

  • Armor: Full Grandmaster Feline Set (6 pieces)
  • Key Skills: Whirl, Crippling Strikes, Fleet Footed
  • Strategy: Open with a strong attack to trigger the 3-piece bonus, then chain fast attacks while the window is active. Reposition behind the enemy only when it is safe enough to use the 6-piece rear-attack bonus.
  • Works Best Against: Enemies whose attacks leave clear openings for repositioning.

Speed Demon (Alternative)

  • With Cat School Techniques: Each piece of light armor gives +25% critical hit damage and +5% fast attack damage. A full light armor set gives +100% critical hit damage and +20% fast attack damage in total.
