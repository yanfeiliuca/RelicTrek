1. Why You Need This

Grandmaster Wolven belongs to Geralt's own school, and the in-game journal frames the hunt around a previous witcher expedition beneath the Termes Palace Ruins. The practical value is its bleeding-focused set bonus: bleeding instances increase sword damage, and adrenaline points raise how many bleeding effects can apply to one enemy.

The old version of this page overstated the route as a crystal-portal puzzle. The verified route is simpler and more specific: find Isabelle Duchamp's journal at the archaeological campsite, pick up George Herbert's letter and the Purple Ruby near the ruins, use the ruby to disable the underground barrier, then clear the chambers and break walls with Aard.

Patch 4.0 also matters. Grandmaster Wolven crafting used to differ from some other Grandmaster schools, but current wording should assume the matching Mastercrafted Wolven pieces are required components.

2. Part Checklist

All 6 diagrams are found in the underground chambers near Termes Palace Ruins. Entry requires the Purple Ruby found near the ruins; Aard is used later for breakable walls.

3. Practical Route

Step 1 — Find the Archaeological Campsite

1Travel to the Termes Palace Ruins signpost in southeast Toussaint. Use Witcher Senses at the campsite near the signpost and loot the casket containing Isabelle Duchamp's journal.
2Read the journal. It points the hunt north through the ruins and into the underground site.

Step 2 — Find the Purple Ruby

3Near the underground entrance, find George Herbert's letter and the Purple Ruby. The ruby is the key item for the barrier.

Step 3 — Open the Barrier

4Use the Purple Ruby beside the magical barrier to disable it and enter the underground chambers. Prepare specter oil before going in.

Step 4 — Clear the First Chambers

5Behind the barrier are wraiths and barghests. Continue left after the closed door and break the first wall with Aard. A giant centipede attacks in the next area.

Step 5 — Break Through to the Lower Rooms

6Continue until you reach another breakable wall, then drop down. More wraiths and barghests can attack from the adjacent rooms.

Step 6 — Collect Both Diagram Groups

7Search the small dead-end room for a witcher's remains. This body holds three diagrams.
8Go the opposite way and loot the witcher's bag on the floor for the other three diagrams, Rebis, and a journal. After all six diagrams are looted, the quest ends.
9To leave, continue to the next room and take the key near the locked door. A barghest appears on the other side.

4. Route Risks

⚠ Risk 1 — Barrier Key

Do not look for a crystal portal. The verified gate item is the Purple Ruby found near George Herbert's letter at the ruins.

⚠ Risk 2 — Mixed Enemy Rooms

The underground route includes wraiths, barghests, and a giant centipede. Specter oil helps at the barrier, but be ready to adjust once the centipede appears.

⚠ Risk 3 — Aard Walls

Aard is for breakable walls deeper inside the route, not for charging the barrier. If the route seems to stop, look for another breakable wall or adjacent room.

💎 Place of Power

The Termes Palace Ruins route passes near a Quen Place of Power. It is worth collecting before or after the underground section.

5. Set Bonus & Crafting

Grandmaster Wolven Set Bonuses (Next-Gen Updated)

  • 3 pieces: Each bleeding instance applied increases sword damage by 1% per set piece worn
  • 6 pieces: Each adrenaline point increases the maximum number of bleeding stacks applicable to one enemy

Crafting Note

After Patch 4.0, Grandmaster Wolven pieces require the matching Mastercrafted Wolven pieces as crafting components. Check Lafargue's crafting screen before dismantling stored Wolven gear.

Supported Playstyle

The set supports sword builds that can apply bleeding and maintain adrenaline. Keep this as a build direction, not a guarantee that every enemy can be bled or that long stacking always outperforms other schools.

6. Build Recommendations

Bleed Machine

  • Armor: Full Grandmaster Wolven Set (6 pieces)
  • Key Skills: Crippling Strikes, Whirl, and adrenaline-focused combat skills
  • Strategy: Build adrenaline, then use sword attacks and skills that can apply bleeding. The set bonuses reward repeated bleeding applications, but the value depends on enemy vulnerability and fight length.
  • Works Best Against: Longer fights where bleeding can be applied reliably.

Adrenaline Juggernaut (Alternative)

  • Boundary: There is no separate "Wolf School Techniques" general skill in the same style as Cat or Griffin. Treat adrenaline/sign hybrids as optional build experiments rather than part of the Wolven set's documented bonus.
