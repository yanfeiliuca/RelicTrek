1. Why You Need This

The Grandmaster Wolven set belongs to Geralt's own school — the School of the Wolf. Wearing it isn't just about stats. It's the closest thing to Geralt's canonical gear, the equipment of his heritage and his brothers at Kaer Morhen.

The 6-piece bonus, updated in the next-gen patch: each adrenaline point increases the maximum number of bleeding stacks you can apply to a single enemy. Combined with the 3-piece bonus (each bleeding instance applied increases sword damage by 1% per set piece), a maximized adrenaline + bleeding build deals compounding damage that escalates through the fight.

What makes this set's acquisition unique is that all six diagrams are in the same location — but it's a location that requires solving a small environmental puzzle (activate a crystal portal with a specific item + Aard), defeating a wave of monsters, and navigating through two Aard-breakable walls to reach the hidden chamber.

2. Part Checklist

3. Optimal Route

Step 1 — Find the Archaeological Campsite

1Travel to the Termes Palace Ruins signpost in southeast Toussaint (east of Gelenser Farm). Just east of the signpost is an archaeological campsite — tents, scattered notes, equipment. Read the archaeologist's journal here. It points you north to the ruins.
2💎 Bonus: The archaeological campsite has an unlocked supply crate containing Cockatrice Stomach, Berserker Pelt, and 3x Greater Glyph of Mending — valuable crafting materials.

Step 2 — Find the Purple Ruby

3Near the ruins, find the corpse of an archaeologist. Loot it to get the Purple Ruby — this is the key to the portal.

Step 3 — Power the Portal

4At the ruins, find the crystal pedestal. Use the Purple Ruby on it. Then use the Aard sign to channel energy through the crystal — the portal activates. Step through.

Step 4 — Defeat the Monster Wave

5A group of Wraiths and Ancient Foglets appear. Yrden sign makes them corporeal (essential — without Yrden, Wraiths are nearly impossible to hit consistently). Thunderbolt + fast attacks once corporeal.

Step 5 — Break the Two Walls

6After defeating the monsters: find the first Aard-breakable wall. Break it, drop to the lower level. Find the second Aard-breakable wall. Break it.

Step 6 — The Hidden Chamber

7In the chamber: left side contains a witcher's skeleton with armor + trousers + boots diagrams. Right side contains a separate satchel with silver sword + steel sword + gauntlets diagrams.
8💎 Bonus: The chamber has a secondary alcove (northern wall, search with Witcher Senses) containing an enchanted runestone of Binding — one of the more useful non-combat runestones that prevents sword degradation during use.

4. Danger Zones & Bonus Loot

⚠ Danger Zones

🔴 Risk 1 — Wraiths Without Yrden

Wraiths phase in and out of the physical world rapidly. Without Yrden active, your attacks pass through them 50% of the time. Cast Yrden as soon as they appear.

⚠ Risk 2 — Missing the Second Wall

The first Aard wall drop feels like the destination — many players search the lower level and miss the second, slightly hidden wall. Use Witcher Senses to highlight it.

⚠ Risk 3 — Wrong Item for the Portal

The Purple Ruby only appears on the specific corpse near the ruins — not random loot. If you've looted everything but the campsite without finding it, go back to the ruins area and specifically look for an archaeologist corpse.

💎 Bonus Loot Along the Route

💎 Bonus 1 — Place of Power

A Place of Power at the Termes Palace Ruins area grants a Quen-enhanced ability point on first visit.

💎 Bonus 2 — Campsite Supply Crate

The archaeological campsite has an unlocked supply crate containing Cockatrice Stomach, Berserker Pelt, and 3x Greater Glyph of Mending — all valuable for Grandmaster-tier crafting.

5. Set Bonus & Crafting

Grandmaster Wolven Set Bonuses (Next-Gen Updated)

  • 3 pieces: Each bleeding instance applied increases sword damage by 1% per set piece worn
  • 6 pieces: Each adrenaline point increases the maximum number of bleeding stacks applicable to one enemy

Best Playstyle

High adrenaline build (Rend, Whirl, Adrenaline Rush skill) + bleed-inflicting oil + fast attacks to stack bleeding + 6-piece bonus allows bleeding stacks to climb higher as fight progresses. The damage compounds — each new bleed stack makes your sword hit harder, which applies more bleeds.

6. Build Recommendations

Bleed Machine

  • Armor: Full Grandmaster Wolven Set (6 pieces)
  • Key Skills: Whirl, Rend, Adrenaline Rush, Crippling Strikes
  • Strategy: Build adrenaline with fast attacks early in the fight. Once adrenaline is full, the 6-piece bonus maximizes your bleed stack ceiling. Apply bleeds through fast attacks and Whirl. Each bleed stack increases your sword damage via the 3-piece bonus — the longer the fight, the more damage you deal.
  • Best Against: High-HP single targets (Archgriffins, Golems, Elementals, bosses) where the fight lasts long enough for bleed stacks to reach maximum.

Adrenaline Juggernaut (Alternative)

  • With Wolf School Techniques: The general skill "Griffin School Techniques" can be swapped for adrenaline-focused abilities. Combine with Flood of Anger (spend adrenaline to cast signs at full power) for a hybrid bleed-and-sign build that switches between stacking bleeds and finishing with an adrenaline-fueled Igni.
