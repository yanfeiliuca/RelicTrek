1. Why This Tier Matters

Superior potions are the last craftable tier in The Witcher 3. The important distinction is not “bigger numbers everywhere,” but that several potions gain an extra rule, longer uptime, or a new combat edge at tier 3.

The safe conclusion is: treat Superior formulas as late-alchemy cleanup, not as a single route you can clear in one town. The formulas are crafted upgrades, and the pages for the finished potions confirm the stable part that matters most here: the effect changes once you reach the Superior tier.

2. Cat Potion Critical Hit Chance — The Patch 4.0 Numbers

Yes — since Patch 4.0 (the December 2022 next-gen update), all three tiers of the Cat potion line grant critical hit chance that did not exist before: Cat +5%, Enhanced Cat +7%, Superior Cat +10%. It's a separate bonus stacked on top of the night-vision effect the potion always had.

4. Priority Order

  1. Superior Swallow — safest all-purpose sustain upgrade because the regeneration rule changes.
  2. Superior White Raffard's Decoction — strongest panic-heal because it restores full Vitality and adds short damage immunity.
  3. Superior Tawny Owl — best routine pickup for sign-heavy play because it extends stamina utility and never expires at night.
  4. Superior Thunderbolt — high-value damage upgrade, especially when weather lets the storm bonus matter.
  5. Superior Black Blood and Superior Cat — strong situational tools rather than universal first pickups.

5. Hazard Guide

⚠ Do Not Trust Single-Page Formula Routes

This page previously mixed exact Superior-formula locations with overconfident wording. The safer route is to treat Superior formulas as a cross-region cleanup task unless you verify a specific formula separately.

⚠ Toxicity Still Matters

Superior effects are better, but they still sit inside the same toxicity economy. Prioritize the potions that materially change your combat rules instead of trying to rush every formula at once.

⚠ Patch 4.0 Changed Some Potion Details

Superior Cat and Superior Black Blood have Patch 4.0-era behavior notes on the potion pages. If an older guide contradicts them, prefer the newer ruleset.

6. Safe Collection Framework

💎 What To Clean Up First

Finish the tier-3 upgrades that obviously change combat rules first: Swallow, White Raffard's, Tawny Owl, then Thunderbolt.

💎 What To Verify Separately

If you want exact formula locations, verify each formula as its own lookup instead of trusting a bundled “all Superior formulas” route summary. That is the highest-risk part of this topic.

8. Frequently Asked Questions

Does Cat potion give critical hit chance since Patch 4.0?
Yes. Since Patch 4.0 (December 2022), the base Cat potion grants 5% critical hit chance in addition to its night-vision effect. This is a new bonus, not present before the patch.
How much critical hit chance does Superior Cat potion give?
Superior Cat grants 10% critical hit chance since Patch 4.0, the highest of the three Cat tiers (Cat 5%, Enhanced Cat 7%, Superior Cat 10%), plus night vision, hypnosis immunity, and extended duration.
Is the Cat potion critical hit chance bonus new in Patch 4.0, or was it increased from an earlier value?
It's entirely new. Unlike some other potions and decoctions that had existing values increased in Patch 4.0 (for example, Katakan decoction's critical hit chance went from 5% to 10%), the Cat potion line had no critical hit chance effect at all before Patch 4.0.
Does Cat potion critical hit chance stack with other crit-chance sources?
Yes, the Cat potion's critical hit bonus is a flat additive percentage separate from skills, Runewords, or Signs that also boost critical hit chance — it stacks with those sources rather than replacing them.
