Starry Night Over the Pontar — Auction Painting and Avid Collector Route

1. What This Item Really Is

Starry Night Over the Pontar is not a sword and not a Blood and Wine art-forgery reward. It is a Hearts of Stone quest item: a van Rogh painting bought during the Borsodi auction in Open Sesame!. Its value comes from a small missable setup step that can open the Avid Collector sale route, plus a later home-display option if Blood and Wine is installed.

2. Auction Setup

Before the auction: inside the Borsodi Brothers' Auction House, talk to Yaromir near the paintings. Tell him you know art and answer his challenge by identifying the merchant painting by E. van der Knoob. Yaromir then tells Geralt that a van Rogh painting called Starry Night Over the Pontar is worth buying because Marcus T.K. Hodgson admires the artist.

Do not tip off Vimme: Vimme Vivaldi asks what Yaromir told you. If you tell Vimme the truth, he bids against you. Keeping the information to yourself lets you buy the painting for 20 crowns; telling Vimme pushes the price to 60 crowns.

3. Avid Collector Sale Route

The painting is the second auction lot. Buy it, then continue Open Sesame! until Geralt regains free movement. Afterward, go to Hierarch Square in Novigrad and speak with Marcus T.K. Hodgson in his bookshop. If the Yaromir setup was completed correctly, Hodgson buys the painting for 500 crowns and gives the Conserved shaelmaar trophy.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Missable Yaromir Step

Buying the painting alone is not enough for the clean collector route. Speak with Yaromir and answer his painting question before the auction starts.

⚠ Vimme Price Trap

Telling Vimme the truth does not fail the route, but it raises the auction price from 20 to 60 crowns.

⚠ Quest Item Handling

The painting is treated as a quest item, so do not plan around normal stash behavior or permanent New Game+ carryover.

5. Sell It or Keep It

For profit, sell the painting to Hodgson and take the 500 crowns plus trophy. For a collection playthrough, Blood and Wine allows a different choice: hang the painting at Corvo Bianco before selling it. Source records indicate the painting can remain displayed there even after Marcus buys it, but this depends on having the vineyard system available.

6. Practical Verdict

The safest route is simple: pass Yaromir's art check, do not reveal the tip to Vimme, buy the painting cheaply, then decide whether you want the Hodgson sale reward or the Corvo Bianco display. Treat this as a compact Hearts of Stone collector route, not a weapon hunt.
