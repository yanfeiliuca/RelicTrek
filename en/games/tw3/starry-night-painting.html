1. Why You Need This

Blood and Wine's Toussaint is a place of art, wine, and civilised excess. It is also where one of the game's most unusual steel swords is hidden — not behind a monster or dungeon, but behind an art forgery quest. The sword's enchantment scales with Geralt's kills since last meditation, rewarding sustained adventuring between rests. Most players encounter this questline as a flavor piece and complete it for the story, never realizing a relic-quality sword is the reward.

2. The Art Questline

Starting the quest: In Beauclair's cultural district, speak to NPCs near the artists' quarter or the gallery dealing in paintings. A specific contact introduces the painting's history and the question of its authenticity. The investigation leads through Toussaint's cultural establishment.

Resolution: The quest concludes with a decision about the painting's fate. In one resolution path, the sword becomes available — connected to the painting location or given by an involved NPC.

3. Sword Properties

Starry Night Over the Pontar: Relic-quality steel sword · Unique enchantment: damage scales with kills since last meditation · High base damage for B&W content range · Visually distinctive Toussaint aesthetic. Tip: Avoid meditating immediately before difficult fights — let kill count accumulate.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Art Quest vs Combat Expectation

Players focused on combat may dismiss this questline. Engage fully — the sword is at the end.

⚠ Meditation Reset

If the sword scales with kills since meditation, avoid meditating right before key fights. Accumulate kills across a session.

⚠ Quest Visibility

Toussaint has many short quests. This one may not appear prominently. Check notice boards and speak to Beauclair art-world NPCs.

5. B&W's Hidden Weapon Trio

Starry Night joins Iris and Aerondight as Blood and Wine's hidden legendary weapons. Iris requires tournament events. Aerondight requires five virtues. Starry Night requires following an art quest to its conclusion. Together: three swords, three entirely different acquisition paths, all invisible to players who don't know to look.

6. The Most Unlikely Sword Source

A painting forgery quest leading to a legendary steel sword is quintessential Toussaint — the only place where art and violence intersect this literally. It is also the kind of content that is systematically overlooked by players focused on combat rewards. The sword exists, it has unique mechanics, and almost no one finds it without knowing it's there.
