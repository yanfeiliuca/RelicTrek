Griffin School Gear — The First Complete Witcher Set

🕐 Route ~2.5 hrs📦 6 pieces📍 Velen (3 locations)⚠ Difficulty 2/5Level 11📅 Updated 2026-05-31

1. Why You Need This

The Griffin School Gear is the first complete craftable Witcher set most players can access — recommended from around level 11, located entirely within Velen. The armor focuses on sign intensity: each piece boosts your sign power, and medium armor allows for faster stamina regeneration than heavy armor, making it the go-to set for players who want magic (signs) to be their primary combat tool.

The quest can be started by purchasing maps from a specific armorer in Midcopse village — but the maps are optional. Players who skip the maps and go directly to the locations often miss the silver sword and steel sword diagrams because they're in two separate locations outside the main armor cluster.

Dragonslayer's Grotto is the most dramatic part of the hunt: a cavern named for a legendary battle, guarded by Ghouls and Necrophages, where the original four armor diagrams sit in the dark.

3. Optimal Route

Optional Start — Buy maps in Midcopse

The armorer in Midcopse village (central-west Velen) sells two maps: parts 1 and 2 of the Griffin gear Scavenger Hunt. Reading them starts the quest and places markers on your map. Not required — the locations below are precise — but buying the maps gives you the in-game lore about the set's origin.

Stop 1 — Dragonslayer's Grotto (Armor + 3 pieces)

1Northeast of Midcopse, northwest of Blackbough. The cave entrance is marked with a weathered stone archway.
2Inside: Ghouls and Rotfiends. Necromancer Oil recommended on your silver sword.
3Navigate through two chambers to the main cavern — the chest with four armor diagrams is against the back wall.
💎 Bonus — Harvall Unique Sword

The cave's second chamber has a skeleton with a named weapon (Harvall — a unique sword dealing increased damage to humans). Worth grabbing if your level matches.

Stop 2 — Lornruk Lighthouse (Silver Sword)

4Northwest Velen, on the coast. The lighthouse ruins are crawling with Ghouls.
5The silver sword diagram chest is near the top of the lighthouse structure — climb the exterior.
💎 Bonus — Runewright's Journal

The lighthouse interior has a hidden room (requires Witcher Senses to find the wall crack) containing a Runewright's journal with recipes unavailable elsewhere in early Velen.

Stop 3 — Hindhold Castle (Steel Sword)

6Central Velen. The ruins are haunted — Wraiths inside, Ghouls on the exterior.
7Yrden sign essential for Wraiths. The steel sword diagram is in the castle's main hall, in a chest near the fireplace.

4. Danger Zones

⚠ Risk 1 — Ghoul swarm at low level

The main danger for level-11 players is getting overwhelmed by multiple Ghouls. They spawn in groups and chain-stagger if you don't manage spacing. Use Yrden to slow them and engage one at a time.

⚠ Risk 2 — Lornruk lighthouse drop-off

Lornruk lighthouse exterior has a drop-off on the far side — easy to fall and take significant damage if you're navigating the exterior while fighting Ghouls.

⚠ Risk 3 — Missing the sword diagrams

Players who follow only the armor cluster at Dragonslayer's Grotto often leave without the silver sword (Lornruk) and steel sword (Hindhold). These are in completely separate locations — check the map before moving on.

5. Crafting Notes

The Griffin School set is the first Witcher gear most players will craft. All six pieces require only a Journeyman Armorer and Journeyman Blacksmith — no master craftsman needed. These are available in Velen (Crow's Perch, Blackbough, and other settlements).

The set focuses on Sign Intensity as its primary stat. Each piece of armor increases sign power, and the medium armor classification offers faster stamina regeneration than heavy armor — allowing you to cast signs more frequently in combat.

6. Playstyle Notes

The Griffin set is the definitive sign-casting set in the early to mid-game. At level 11, the sign intensity boost means your Igni actually kills enemies (rather than just tickling them), your Quen absorbs meaningful damage, and your Yrden slows enemies enough to control group fights.

This set pairs well with the Griffin School Techniques general skill (yellow skill tree) which grants +20% sign intensity and +5% stamina regeneration for each piece of medium armor worn. With a full Griffin set, that's a massive boost at low level.
