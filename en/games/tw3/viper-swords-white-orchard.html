1. Why You Need This

The Witcher 3 starts you in White Orchard. The game's first area has a tutorial feel — you're level 1, fighting drowners, learning the controls. Most players rush through it toward the main quest.

In that same area, two Viper School weapon diagrams sit in fixed locations that 99% of players walk past. The silver sword diagram is inside a locked crypt at the cemetery — easy to see, easy to assume it's inaccessible without a key. It's not. Aard breaks it open. The steel sword diagram is in the basement of a bandit-occupied mill near Woesong Bridge.

These are level 1 and 2 items, but they carry the Viper School bonus (small XP gain, poison chance) and are significantly stronger than any other early-game craftable weapon. More importantly, the silver sword is the first piece of a set you'll want to complete much later in Hearts of Stone.

3. Route

Stop 1 — Viper Silver Sword (Cemetery)

1From the Mill signpost, ride north for about 90 seconds. The cemetery appears on the right. Inside the cemetery grounds: a small stone crypt building, visibly locked with a wooden-bar door. Cast Aard at the door — it breaks open immediately. Inside: a corpse on the ground. Loot it for the Viper Silver Sword diagram. Craft at the nearest journeyman blacksmith (White Orchard has one near the inn).

Stop 2 — Viper Steel Sword (Abandoned Mill)

2From the Woesong Bridge signpost, head northeast on foot or horseback for 60 seconds. The mill is by the road. 3–4 bandits are outside — kill them. Enter the mill. Find the trapdoor to the basement (in the northeast corner of the ground floor). Down in the basement: the chest at the far end contains the Viper Steel Sword diagram.

4. Danger & Bonus

⚠ Danger

⚠ The Only Real Danger — Missing It Entirely

Nothing in the game points you to either location. There's no quest marker, no NPC hint, no glowing icon. If you don't know to cast Aard on crypt doors, you'll assume it's locked and walk away.

💎 Bonus Loot

💎 Bonus — Hidden Stash in the Mill Cellar

The mill cellar has a second section behind a crumbling wall (breakable with Aard) that contains a hidden stash: a random relic-quality sword that scales to your current level. Worth checking even if you don't want the Viper diagram.

5. Crafting Guide

Crafting Requirements

WeaponCrafter RequiredKey Materials
Viper Silver SwordJourneyman BlacksmithSilver Ingot x2, Leather Straps x2, Monster Brain x1
Viper Steel SwordJourneyman BlacksmithSteel Ingot x2, Leather Straps x1, Monster Brain x1

Both swords require only a Journeyman-level blacksmith — no master craftsman needed. The White Orchard blacksmith near the inn can craft both. Materials are available from the same blacksmith or from scavenging in White Orchard.

6. Build Notes

These are the strongest weapons you can get before leaving White Orchard. The poison chance procs frequently against early-game enemies (drowners, ghouls, bandits) and the XP bonus from kills adds up over the early hours. The silver sword, in particular, is the first piece of the full Viper School set — you'll want the diagrams for the armor and the upgraded steel sword when you reach Hearts of Stone later.

Pro tip: Craft both swords before leaving White Orchard at level 4. The power spike will carry you comfortably through Velen until you can craft Griffin or Feline school gear at level 11.
