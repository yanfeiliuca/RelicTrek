Hattori Master Blacksmith — The Dumpling Vendor Who Forges Legendary Weapons

1. Why You Need This

Mastercrafted weapons are the tier-4 upgrade for all Witcher school gear — stronger than Superior, weaker only than Grandmaster. They require a master blacksmith to forge. In the Northern Kingdoms, there is one master blacksmith whose services can be unlocked: Hattori.

Hattori is currently working as a dumplings vendor in Novigrad. Not by choice — the city's guild system pressured him out of his actual craft. He operates from a stall, sells food, and pretends he has no idea how to forge a sword. He does know.

Without completing his questline "Of Swords and Dumplings," no Mastercrafted weapons can be forged in any Witcher 3 playthrough. The quest is not marked as critical path. Most players encounter Hattori, buy dumplings, and move on — never realizing what they left behind.

2. Quest Steps — "Of Swords and Dumplings"

Step 1 — Find Hattori in Novigrad

Hattori operates from a food stall on Glory Lane in Novigrad. Speak to him about "swords" rather than food — he initially denies any smithing knowledge, then, after pressure, admits his situation.

Step 2 — Find the Truffle Supplier

Hattori needs specific truffles for a cover transaction that will allow him to meet his mentor secretly. Track down the truffle supplier he mentions — a specific merchant location in Novigrad. The supplier also sells rare alchemy ingredients; check his full inventory.

Step 3 — Cover the Food Delivery

Hattori makes a delivery using the truffles as cover. Geralt escorts him through a bandit-heavy district. Light enemies, manageable without preparation. Loot the bandit corpses for crafting materials and coin.

Step 4 — Protect Tang

Hattori's mentor, Tang, is in a compromised position. After the delivery, a confrontation occurs. Protect both Hattori and Tang from attackers — a straightforward fight with several human enemies around level 24.

Step 5 — Hattori's Forge Reopens

After the questline resolves, Hattori returns to his true profession. He can now craft Mastercrafted weapons (tier 4) for all schools. His forge is accessible from his new workshop location. He also sells several rare crafting components not available from other Novigrad merchants.

3. Requirements

  • Recommended level: 24+ (enemies in the Tang fight are ~level 24)
  • Location: Novigrad — Glory Lane food stall → new forge workshop after quest
  • Quest name: "Of Swords and Dumplings"
  • No prerequisite quests required to start

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Tang Encounter

Multiple human attackers in an enclosed space. Use Igni to control the crowd if overwhelmed. Below level 20, bring Swallow potions and a strong silver sword.

⚠ Hattori's Location Change

After the quest, Hattori moves to a forge workshop (no longer at the food stall). He is marked as "Hattori, Master Blacksmith" after the quest — before completing it, he shows as a food merchant only.

5. En-Route Bonus

💎 Rare Alchemy Ingredients

The truffle supplier also stocks rare alchemy ingredients — check the full inventory before leaving.

💎 Bandit Loot

The escort segment passes through a bandit-heavy district; loot corpses for crafting materials and coin.

💎 Post-Unlock Inventory

After unlocking Hattori, he sells rare crafting components not found at other Novigrad merchants.

6. Why This Matters

The information asymmetry here is extreme: Hattori is in one of Novigrad's most-visited areas, selling dumplings, and almost no player realizes he is a master craftsman whose skills are required to craft tier-4 weapons. The questline takes two hours. The reward is permanent access to the best pre-Grandmaster weapon tier for every school set in the game.

This is the single most impactful NPC unlock in the base game that isn't marked as a main quest. Combined with Yoana in Toussaint (who unlocks Grandmaster armor), these two NPCs are the gatekeepers of the entire Witcher gear endgame.
