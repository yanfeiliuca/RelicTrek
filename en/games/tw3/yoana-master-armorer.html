1. Why You Need This

The division of labor is clean: Hattori forges Mastercrafted and Grandmaster weapons. Yoana forges Mastercrafted and Grandmaster armor. Neither one can do the other's work. Both must be unlocked for any Grandmaster gear to be complete.

Yoana operates from Beauclair's smith quarter in Blood and Wine. Her situation when Geralt arrives: her masterwork tools have been stolen. Without them, she cannot work at her highest level. She needs help recovering them.

The questline — "Master Armorers" — is shorter than Hattori's and more straightforward. But it shares the same essential quality: almost no one knows it exists before they need it. Players who arrive in Toussaint with full Grandmaster diagrams and try to craft armor often discover at that moment that they need to complete this quest first.

2. Quest Steps — "Master Armorers"

Step 1 — Find Yoana (Beauclair)

Yoana's workshop is in Beauclair's craftsman district. Approach her as an armorer (not a general vendor) and she will explain that her tools are missing — stolen by a rival or opportunist. She is initially marked only as a general armorer on the map, not as a master craftsman.

Step 2 — Track the Stolen Tools

The investigation leads outside Beauclair to a location in the Toussaint countryside. Geralt uses his Witcher Senses to follow evidence from the workshop to the thief's location. If the trail seems cold, ensure Witcher Senses are active and follow the visible highlighted path.

Step 3 — Recover the Tools

A short combat encounter at the thief's location. Standard human enemies at an appropriate level for Blood and Wine entry (~level 36+). The thief's hideout contains a chest with random Master-tier crafting materials.

Step 4 — Return to Yoana

The tools are back. Yoana returns to full operation and can now craft Mastercrafted and Grandmaster armor for all schools. Her map marker updates from "Yoana, Armorer" to "Yoana, Master Armorer." She also sells rare armor crafting components unavailable elsewhere in Toussaint.

3. The Hattori + Yoana Duo

For Grandmaster gear to be complete, both NPCs must be unlocked:

  • Hattori (Novigrad): Complete "Of Swords and Dumplings" — unlocks Mastercrafted + Grandmaster weapons
  • Yoana (Beauclair): Complete "Master Armorers" — unlocks Mastercrafted + Grandmaster armor

Neither can substitute for the other. Most players complete Hattori's quest naturally during the base game's Novigrad section. Almost no one completes Yoana's without specifically knowing to look for her — making her the single most commonly missed NPC unlock in Blood and Wine.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Finding Yoana

She is not marked on the initial Beauclair map as a master craftsman — only as a general armorer. After completing the quest, her map marker updates to "Yoana, Master Armorer."

⚠ Tracking the Thief

The investigation requires using Witcher Senses (L2 trigger hold) to follow scent/footprint trails from the workshop. If the trail seems cold, re-activate Witcher Senses and check for the highlighted path.

⚠ Level Check

The thief combat encounter is tuned for level 36+. Attempting below level 32 is possible but challenging — bring Swallow potions and a strong steel sword.

5. En-Route Bonus

💎 Thief's Hideout Chest

Contains random Master-tier crafting materials — always worth looting after the fight.

💎 Post-Unlock Inventory

After unlocking, Yoana sells rare armor crafting components not available elsewhere in Toussaint.

6. Why Yoana Matters More Than People Realize

Most players complete Hattori's quest naturally. Almost no one completes Yoana's without specifically knowing to look for her. This makes Yoana the single most commonly missed NPC unlock in Blood and Wine — and the gatekeeper for every Grandmaster armor piece in the game.

A player can have all six Grandmaster Wolf diagrams and be unable to craft any of them because of one uncompleted quest they didn't know existed. Combined with Hattori in Novigrad, these two NPCs are the twin gatekeepers of the entire Witcher gear endgame.
