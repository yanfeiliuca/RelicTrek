Mohgwyn Palace — Two Ways In, One of the Most Hidden Areas in the Game, and the Best Rune Farm

1. Why You Need to Find This Place

Mohgwyn Palace is an underground blood-soaked realm hidden beneath Caelid — an entire legacy dungeon that a surprising number of players never discover. It houses Mohg, Lord of Blood, a Shardbearer boss who drops a Remembrance and 420,000 runes. It also contains the single best rune-farming spot in Elden Ring, capable of producing 50,000-60,000 runes per minute with zero combat skill required.

Inside the palace, a chest before Mohg's boss fog contains the Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone — one of the rarest upgrade materials in the game, required to push any Somber weapon to +10. You can grab it without fighting Mohg.

There are two completely independent routes into Mohgwyn Palace. One requires a questline and can be accessed as early as Liurnia. The other requires reaching the Consecrated Snowfield but involves no quest and no NPC interaction whatsoever.

2. How to Get In — Both Routes Explained

Route A — Varre's Questline (Early Access, from Liurnia)

This route is available as soon as you reach Liurnia of the Lakes and have access to online play or the offline invasion NPC in Altus Plateau.

1Meet White Mask Varre at The First Step (Limgrave) right after the tutorial. Exhaust his dialogue. He will call you maidenless — this is normal.
2After defeating Godrick, Varre moves to Rose Church in southern Liurnia (northwest of the Folly on the Lake site of grace). Speak to him there. He will ask you to join his order.
3Varre gives you Festering Bloody Fingers. You must invade three other players (or the offline NPC Magnus the Beast Claw in Writheblood Ruins, Altus Plateau). You do not need to win. Just invade three times, then return to Varre.
4Varre gives you the Lord of Blood's Favor — a white cloth. Soak it in the blood of a maiden: either kill Irina/Hyetta in Liurnia, or find the dead maiden in the Church of Inhibition (northeast Liurnia, frenzy-flame tower area).
5Return to Varre. He rewards you with the Pureblood Knight's Medal. Use this item from your inventory to instantly teleport to the entrance of Mohgwyn Palace. No combat, no boss, no dungeon required.

Route B — Hidden Consecrated Snowfield Portal (No Quest, Late Game)

1Reach the Consecrated Snowfield (requires both halves of the Haligtree Secret Medallion, accessed via the Grand Lift of Rold). This is late-game territory.
2Starting from the Ordina, Liturgical Town site of grace, head west across the frozen river. Look for a blood-red teleporter tucked against the cliff face in the far northwest corner of the Snowfield. It is unmarked — no map icon, no quest marker. Walk into the swirling red portal.
3The portal deposits you directly inside Mohgwyn Palace, near the Palace Approach Ledge-Road site of grace — which is also the best rune-farming spot.

Inside Mohgwyn Palace — What to Do First

💰 Best Rune Farm in the Game

From the Palace Approach Ledge-Road site of grace, turn around and look across the blood swamp. You will see Albinaurics sitting passively on the cliff edge. Use the Golden Scarab talisman and a bow with Arrow's Reach talisman. Shoot the giant bird (actually a Giant Crow) across the chasm — one arrow will aggro it, and it runs off the cliff for 11,000 runes. Repeat. Then finish the Albinaurics with AOE for another 40,000-50,000. 60,000+ runes per run, under 60 seconds.

Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone — Free +10

💎 Somber ADSS Before Mohg

Before Mohg's boss fog, in the Dynasty Mausoleum Midpoint area, a chest sits in a small alcove guarded by a Sanguine Noble. Kill or run past him. The chest contains the Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone — use it to upgrade any Somber weapon to +10. This is one of only 8 available per playthrough.

Mohg, Lord of Blood — Boss Strategy

1Pre-fight preparation: Obtain the Purifying Crystal Tear from Eleonora, Violet Bloody Finger at the Second Church of Marika (Altus Plateau). Mix it into your Physick. This tear completely negates Mohg's triple Nihil curse — the blood-ring attack that drains your HP three times during the phase transition.
2Phase 1: Mohg swings his trident with wide, delayed sweeping attacks and coats the floor in bloodflame that lingers. Stay close, roll into his swings. Bloodflame pools make the arena dangerous — reposition often.
3Phase transition (~50% HP): Mohg chants "Tres... Duo... Unus..." and impales you with three blood rings (Nihil). If you drank the Purifying Crystal Tear, you take zero damage and Mohg heals almost nothing. If you did not, this sequence is a near-guaranteed death without very high Vigor and flask spam.
4Phase 2: Mohg gains bloodflame wings, faster attacks, and bloodflame trails on every swing. His flying charge is dangerous. The arena is now mostly covered in bloodflame. Stay airborne-averse — his anti-air swipe is brutal. Bleed builds work well; he has high fire resistance.
🏆 Rewards

Remembrance of the Blood Lord + 420,000 Runes + Mohg's Great Rune

3. Hazard Guide — What Kills You Here

The Nihil Without Purifying Crystal Tear Is Brutal

⚠ Do Not Attempt Mohg Without the Tear

Mohg's triple Nihil sequence (the "Tres, Duo, Unus" blood curse) deals massive unavoidable damage and heals Mohg for ~30% of his HP with every ring. Without the Purifying Crystal Tear, you must flask through three consecutive HP-drain waves while Mohg heals. The fight becomes a battle of attrition that most builds cannot win on their first playthrough. Get the tear from the Second Church of Marika — it drops from Eleonora, Violet Bloody Finger, who invades there after progressing Yura's questline or reaching the area naturally.

Varre Questline Kill-Switch

⚠ Do Not Kill Mohg Before Completing Varre's Quest

If you enter Mohgwyn Palace via the portal (Route B) and kill Mohg before completing Varre's questline, Varre will disappear from Rose Church and you permanently lose access to the Pureblood Knight's Medal, Varre's Bouquet, and his armor set for this playthrough. If you want all rewards, complete Varre's quest before fighting Mohg.

Invasion Requirements — Offline Players

⚠ Varre Quest Without Online

If you play offline and cannot invade other players, there is an alternative: Magnus the Beast Claw invades in Writheblood Ruins (western Altus Plateau, near the road to Mt. Gelmir). You can invade his world via the red summon sign on the ground three times — each counts toward Varre's requirement. This was added in a patch specifically for offline players.
