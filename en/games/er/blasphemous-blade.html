Blasphemous Blade — The Secret Manor, Three Assassinations, and the Sword That Heals When It Kills

1. Why You Need It

Volcano Manor is hidden behind the lava fields of Mt. Gelmir, a region most players never fully explore. From the outside, it is a foreboding fortress on a burning mountain. Inside, Lady Tanith offers you a covenant — serve the manor, and you will be rewarded with the power of the Lord of Blasphemy himself.

The covenant requires you to carry out three assassination letters. Each sends you to a different region: Limgrave, the Altus Plateau, and the Mountaintops of the Giants. Red invasion signs wait for you at each location. Find them, defeat the Tarnished targets, and return to Tanith. Complete all three, and she grants you an audience with Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy — a demigod fused with the Great Serpent.

Rykard is a two-phase encounter. Phase one is the Great Serpent — a colossal snake whose body spans the arena, leaving trails of magma that force constant repositioning. Phase two is Rykard himself, wielding a sword of blasphemous flame, calling down skull storms, and unleashing arena-wide fire waves. He is not the hardest demigod, but he has one of the largest HP pools in the game — the fight is designed around a specific weapon you find inside the arena.

Defeat him, and his Remembrance can be traded at the Roundtable Hold for the Blasphemous Blade — a greatsword that heals you on every kill. Not just bosses. Every enemy that falls to its edge restores a chunk of your HP. Its unique skill, Taker's Flames, unleashes a wave of fire across the ground that steals HP from everything it touches. For Faith builds, this is one of the most self-sustaining weapons in Elden Ring. Pair it with the Taker's Cameo talisman — found inside the same manor — and every kill becomes a full heal.

2. Two Paths to Volcano Manor

There are two primary ways to reach Volcano Manor. Route A gives you early access mid-game; Route B is the standard overland ascent through Mt. Gelmir. Both converge at the same front door.

Route A — Rya's Teleport (Early Access, Recommended)

1Find Rya the Scout in Liurnia of the Lakes, near the Laskyar Ruins or the Scenic Isle site of grace. She asks you to recover a stolen necklace from a thief nearby. Buy the necklace back (or take it), return it to Rya.
2Rya next appears at the top of the Grand Lift of Dectus in Altus Plateau. Speak to her there. She reveals she is a servant of Volcano Manor and offers to transport you directly — bypassing the entire Mt. Gelmir climb.
3Accept her teleport. You arrive inside Volcano Manor. Speak to Tanith in the main hall to join the covenant. This route gives you immediate access to the manor's vendors, the Taker's Cameo talisman, the Smithing Stone vendor, and the assassination contracts — all while still mid-game.

Route B — Mt. Gelmir Ascent (Standard Route)

1Enter Mt. Gelmir from the Altus Plateau side, starting at Wyndham Ruins. Take the Seethewater River path north, following it past Fort Laiedd and through the Hermit Village — where the Comet Azur sorcery sits on a corpse near Primeval Sorcerer Azur.
2Climb the lava switchbacks past Man-Serpents, Abductor Virgins, and a Full-Grown Fallingstar Beast guarding the summit crater. This is one of the most dangerous overland routes in Elden Ring — narrow cliff paths, constant environmental damage, and elite enemies with high poise.
3Reach Volcano Manor's front gate. Enter, speak to Tanith in the main hall, and join the covenant. You can access the same vendors and contracts as Route A — you just earned the approach the hard way.
💡 Recommendation

Route A is faster and unlocks the manor far earlier. Complete Rya's necklace quest in Liurnia (under 5 minutes), then take the Grand Lift of Dectus to Altus — she is waiting at the top. Route B is worth experiencing once for the atmosphere and the Comet Azur pickup, but for repeat playthroughs or Faith builds that want the Blasphemous Blade early, Rya is the way.

3. Full Route: Covenants, Letters, and Rykard

Once you join Volcano Manor, the real quest begins. Three assassination letters — each sending you to a different region to hunt a fellow Tarnished. Complete them all, and Tanith gives you what you came for.

Step 1 — Join the Manor

1Speak to Tanith in Volcano Manor's main hall. Accept her offer. She gives you the Drawing-Room Key. Enter the drawing room — first door on the left from the main hall. On the table is your first letter.

Step 2 — Old Knight Istvan (Limgrave)

2Travel to Stormhill in northern Limgrave. The red invasion sign is near the colosseum-like structure north of the Warmaster's Shack. Defeat Old Knight Istvan — he is a heavily armored knight with a greatsword, straightforward by Elden Ring standards. Return to Tanith. Reward: Magma Shot sorcery.

Step 3 — Rileigh the Idle (Altus Plateau)

3Return to the drawing room for the second letter: Rileigh the Idle. Travel to the Bridge of Iniquity site of grace, south of the Shaded Castle in Altus Plateau. The red invasion sign is on the bridge. Rileigh wields dual rapiers and uses quickstep — close distance and stagger him before he can chain dodges. Return to Tanith. Reward: Serpentbone Blade (katana with poison buildup).

Step 4 — Juno Hoslow (Mountaintops of the Giants)

4Return to the drawing room for the third letter: Juno Hoslow, Knight of Blood. Travel to the Ancient Snow Valley Ruins site of grace in the Mountaintops of the Giants. Head northwest through the snowfield. The red invasion sign is near the cliff edge overlooking the frozen river. Hoslow is the hardest contract — he dual-wields Hoslow's Petal Whips, applies Bleed rapidly, and has high poise. Use ranged attacks to whittle him down or bring heavy stagger weapons. Defeat him. Return to Tanith. Reward: Taker's Cameo talisman — restores HP on every kill. This talisman stacks with the Blasphemous Blade's own HP-on-kill effect.

Step 5 — Bernahl's Letter (Optional)

5After completing the three main contracts, speak to Knight Bernahl in the drawing room. He offers his own letter targeting Vargram the Raging Wolf and Errant Sorcerer Wilhelm in Leyndell, Royal Capital. This is optional — you do not need it for the Rykard audience. Completing it rewards the Raging Wolf Armor set and the Gelmir's Fury sorcery. Warning: Bernahl's contract must be completed before defeating Rykard — after Rykard falls, Bernahl leaves the manor permanently.

Step 6 — Audience with Rykard

6After the third letter (or optional fourth) is completed, speak to Tanith. She declares you worthy and transports you directly to Rykard's boss arena. The moment you enter, pick up the Serpent-Hunter great spear from the corpse at the arena entrance. This weapon has the skill Great-Serpent Hunt, which fires a blade of wind on charged R2s — it only functions at full power against Rykard. Upgrade it at a blacksmith before the fight if possible; the upgrades carry over.
7Phase 1 — The Great Serpent: Use charged R2s with the Serpent-Hunter. Each charged heavy fires a wind blade that staggers the serpent. Stay at mid-range to avoid the magma pools. The serpent's bite and body-slam attacks are well-telegraphed — roll into them, not away.
8Phase 2 — Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy: The serpent's body is now wielded by Rykard himself. He uses blasphemous flame sword sweeps, a skull rain attack that fills the arena with homing projectiles, and an arena-wide fire explosion. Stay close to his left side — most of his sword swings miss at close range. The Serpent-Hunter's charged R2s remain effective. The skull rain can be avoided by sprinting laterally. When he raises the sword skyward for the arena-wide blast, run to the far edge of the arena.

Step 7 — Trade the Remembrance

9Upon victory, you receive Rykard's Remembrance and the Lord of Blasphemy's Rune. Fast travel to the Roundtable Hold and speak to Enia. Trade the Remembrance for the Blasphemous Blade. You may also choose Rykard's Rancor (a sorcery that summons exploding spirits). To obtain both, duplicate the Remembrance at a Walking Mausoleum before trading — Elden Ring allows one Remembrance duplication per mausoleum per playthrough.
🏆 Final Reward

Blasphemous Blade: Greatsword. Scales STR D / DEX D / FAI D (base). Unique skill: Taker's Flames — a sweeping wave of fire that steals HP from everything it touches. Passive: restores 4% HP + 40 flat HP on every kill. At high Faith (50+), Taker's Flames can clear entire rooms and return you to full health.

4. Hazard Guide — Where Players Get Stuck or Locked Out

Invasion Signs Not Appearing

⚠ Most Common Issue

Red invasion signs require a non-emboldened state. If you have used a Rune Arc and have a Great Rune active, the invasion signs will not appear. Die once to clear the Rune Arc buff, then return to the sign location. This is the #1 reason players cannot see the assassination targets. Do not be in an emboldened state when hunting contracts.

Serpent-Hunter Is Mandatory for Rykard

⚔️ Do Not Fight Without It

The Serpent-Hunter great spear is placed on a corpse at the arena entrance — you cannot miss it, but you can forget to equip it. Without the Serpent-Hunter, Rykard's HP pool is enormous (nearly 90,000 combined across both phases), and the fight becomes one of the hardest in the game. The weapon is the intended mechanic. Upgrade the Serpent-Hunter at the Roundtable Hold blacksmith before the fight — the upgrades remain effective during the Rykard encounter.

Patches Connection Is Permanently Missable

🔒 Lockout Warning

Patches can appear in Volcano Manor — but only if you encounter him in Murkwater Cave (Limgrave) first and spare his life when he surrenders. If you enter Volcano Manor without meeting Patches, he will never appear there. If you join the manor before completing his questline, he leaves — and his contract request (Great Horned Tragoth) becomes permanently unavailable. This also locks you out of the Bull-Goat Armor set, the highest-poise armor in Elden Ring. If you want Tragoth's contract, do Murkwater Cave first, spare Patches, let him move to the manor, then complete his request before finishing the assassination chain.

Bernahl Leaves After Rykard

⚠ Timing Window

Knight Bernahl offers an optional assassination letter in the drawing room. This must be completed before defeating Rykard. After Rykard dies, Bernahl permanently leaves Volcano Manor and becomes an invader in Crumbling Farum Azula. If you want the Raging Wolf Armor and Gelmir's Fury sorcery, finish Bernahl's contract before speaking to Tanith for the Rykard audience.

Tanith's Quest After Rykard

🎁 Post-Fight Bonus

After defeating Rykard, return to his boss arena. Tanith will be there, consuming Rykard's corpse. If you kill her, her Crucible Knight bodyguard invades you — defeating him drops the Aspect of the Crucible: Breath incantation. This is optional content. If you wait until after completing Rya's full questline (Zorayas), Tanith's fate resolves differently — but the incantation window closes.
