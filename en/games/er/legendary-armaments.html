1. Why This Collection Matters

The Legendary Armaments achievement requires collecting all 9 specific weapons in a single playthrough. Unlike most collectibles in Elden Ring, one of these weapons — the Bolt of Gransax — permanently disappears from the game world when the Erdtree is burned and Leyndell transforms into the Ashen Capital. If you reach the endgame without picking it up, the achievement is locked out for that entire playthrough. There is no way back.

The other 8 weapons are spread across every major region in the Lands Between — Limgrave, Caelid, Liurnia, Altus, the Mountaintops, and Farum Azula. Several are tied to multi-step questlines that can also be broken or missed. This guide covers every weapon, its exact location, and every timing window you must respect to avoid a permanent lockout.

Collecting all 9 rewards the Legendary Armaments trophy/achievement — a mark of a completionist playthrough and a prerequisite for 100% or Platinum.

3. Hazard Guide — What Can Go Wrong

Bolt of Gransax — The Permanent Lockout

⚠ THE Missable

The Bolt of Gransax is the only Legendary Armament that can be permanently lost. It sits on a giant spear shaft in Leyndell, Royal Capital. When you defeat Maliketh in Farum Azula, Leyndell transforms into the Ashen Capital — the spear statue collapses, and the weapon is gone forever. There is no workaround. No shop sells it. No NPC drops it. You must physically climb the statue and pick it up before triggering the Erdtree's burning. If you are unsure whether you have already picked it up, check your inventory before fighting the Fire Giant.

Ruins Greatsword — Festival Timing

⚠ Timing Hazard

The Misbegotten Warrior + Crucible Knight duo in Redmane Castle is unavailable while the Radahn Festival is active. If you arrive at Redmane Castle and the festival is running, you must defeat Radahn first, then rest at the Site of Grace and speak to Jerren to reset the castle to its pre-festival state. The duo boss fight will then be accessible through the castle's main entrance. Do not leave Caelid without the Ruins Greatsword — returning later to clean it up is easy, but forgetting it entirely means a second trip to the castle you have already cleared.

Devourer's Scepter — Bernahl Must Be Alive

⚠ NPC Dependency

If you plan to obtain Devourer's Scepter from Bernahl as an invader in Farum Azula, he must be alive. Killing Bernahl at the Warmaster's Shack early does net you the scepter immediately — but it also removes him from Volcano Manor, locks you out of his assassination contracts, and prevents him from appearing as a summon. If you choose the Farum Azula route, find his invasion sign near the Beside the Great Bridge Site of Grace before fighting Maliketh. Maliketh's death removes all Farum Azula invasions permanently.

Dark Moon Greatsword — Quest Breakage

⚠ Quest Lock

Ranni's questline is one of the longest in the game. It can be broken if you attack certain NPCs — specifically Blaidd, Seluvis, or Iji — before the quest is complete. Do not kill Blaidd at the Redmane Festival. Do not antagonize Seluvis until after you have given Ranni the Fingerslayer Blade. The common failure point is killing Blaidd prematurely, which causes Ranni to disappear from her Rise, locking the entire quest and the Dark Moon Greatsword behind it.

Golden Order Greatsword — Cave Location

⚠ Easy to Miss

The Cave of the Forlorn is hidden on the frozen river in the Mountaintops of the Giants, west of the Freezing Lake Site of Grace. The entrance is a narrow opening in the cliff wall and is easy to ride past. The Misbegotten Crusader inside hits hard with holy damage — stack holy resistance before entering. The sword is a guaranteed drop on defeat.
