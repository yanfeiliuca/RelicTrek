Eclipse Shotel — The Legendary Weapon Left as an Offering in Castle Sol's Church

1. Why the Eclipse Shotel Matters

Castle Sol, in the northern reaches of the Mountaintops of the Giants, is a brutal fortress filled with high-level soldiers and spectral knights. Most players fight through it for Commander Niall and the Haligtree Medallion half — and never find the Church of the Eclipse.

Hidden within the castle is a chapel dedicated to the eclipsed sun. Inside, resting on the altar as a sacred offering, lies the Eclipse Shotel — a legendary curved sword with a unique property: its weapon skill, Death Flare, applies Death Blight buildup on every hit. This is one of the only weapons in the game capable of inflicting the Death Blight status effect, which causes continuous lethal damage that bypasses conventional defenses. Once the buildup crosses the threshold, the target suffers a devastating burst.

No boss is required to collect it. Commander Niall guards the Haligtree Medallion half — not the Eclipse Shotel. You can sprint past every enemy in Castle Sol, enter the church, and claim the weapon from the altar. It requires no key, no quest, and no prerequisite.

2. Route to the Eclipse Shotel

Reach Castle Sol

1Start from the Freezing Lake site of grace in the northwest Mountaintops of the Giants. Head northeast across the frozen lake, avoiding or defeating the Borealis dragon. The path leads upward to Castle Sol's main gate.
2Activate the Castle Sol Main Gate site of grace just outside the entrance. From here, you have two options: fight through the castle normally, or sprint past the soldiers.

Navigate to the Church

3Enter the castle. Head through the main courtyard (watch for the two teleporting spectral banished knights). Take the stairs on the left side of the courtyard going up.
4At the upper level, cross the wooden walkway and look for a building with large circular windows depicting an eclipse motif. This is the Church of the Eclipse — it is a distinct structure within the castle, not the main keep.

Claim the Weapon

5Inside the church, walk to the altar at the far end. The Eclipse Shotel rests on the altar as a glowing item pickup. Interact with the altar to claim it. No boss fight triggers — the weapon is freely lootable.
💡 Optional Bonus

While at Castle Sol, consider defeating Commander Niall at the far end of the castle to obtain the Haligt Secret Medallion (Left) — required to access the Consecrated Snowfield and ultimately the Haligtree. The Eclipse Shotel and the medallion half can be collected in a single visit.

3. Hazard Guide

Spectral Banished Knights — The Castle's Real Threat

⚠️ Teleporting Enemies

Castle Sol is patrolled by spectral Banished Knights who teleport directly behind you mid-combat. They have extremely high poise and deal heavy physical damage. At lower levels, engaging them directly is not recommended — they are as dangerous as most field bosses. Sprint past them if you are underleveled. Use Assassin's Approach or Unseen Form sorcery to reduce aggro range, letting you reach the church with minimal combat.

Rune Level Recommendation

⚡ Recommended RL 100+

The Mountaintops of the Giants are balanced for endgame — enemies hit hard and have large health pools. While the Eclipse Shotel itself requires no boss fight, reaching Castle Sol from the Freezing Lake grace involves navigating past Borealis the Freezing Fog (an optional dragon) and Castle Sol's gate defenses. A rune level of at least 100 is recommended. Below RL 80, expect to die repeatedly to the spectral knights.

Commander Niall — Optional but Rewarding

✅ Kill Niall for Medallion

Commander Niall guards the Haligtree Secret Medallion (Left). Defeating him is not required for the Eclipse Shotel, but doing both in one trip is efficient. Niall summons two spectral banished knights at the start — kill them first, then focus on Niall. His lightning-imbued AoE attacks are his most dangerous move; learn the dodge timing or use lightning-resistant gear.

Death Blight Mechanics

💀 How Death Blight Works

The Eclipse Shotel's skill, Death Flare, applies Death Blight buildup with each hit. Once the buildup meter fills on an enemy (or in PvP), the target takes massive damage instantly — functionally a one-shot against most PvP opponents. The buildup decays over time if not continuously applied, so aggressive follow-up attacks are essential. In PvE, most bosses are immune to Death Blight, limiting its effectiveness to PvP and non-boss encounters.
