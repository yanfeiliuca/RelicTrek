Academy Spell Books — Four Books Scattered Across the Lands Between, and the Full Sorcery List They Unlock

1. Why You Need These Books

When you first defeat Rennala and gain access to her Rebirth and sorcery shop, her inventory is nearly empty. She sells only a handful of basic Glintstone sorceries. Most sorcery players assume this is all she offers — and never discover that four spell books hidden across the Lands Between are the key to unlocking her full catalog.

Give Rennala a spell book, and she adds every sorcery contained in that book to her shop, available for purchase with Runes. Each book unlocks 2-4 sorceries. Collect all four, and Rennala becomes the most comprehensive sorcery vendor in the game — rivaling even Preceptor Seluvis and Sorceress Sellen.

These books contain some of the best sorceries in Elden Ring: Carian Slicer (the fastest melee-range sorcery in the game), Stars of Ruin (undodgeable homing star barrage), and Comet (charged piercing beam for sniping). And none of them require beating a boss to acquire.

2. Spell Book Locations

📜 Royal House Scroll — Limgrave

Unlocks: Carian Slicer, Glintblade Phalanx

📍 Limgrave: Agheel Lake South ⚔️ No boss

From the Agheel Lake South Site of Grace, follow the road southeast toward the bridge. On the south side of the road, look for a large ruin platform with an enemy encampment on top. The scroll sits on a corpse atop the highest platform. Carian Slicer alone makes this the most valuable scroll in the game — it is the fastest melee sorcery, chains rapidly, and costs almost no FP.

📜 Academy Scroll — Liurnia Gate Town

Unlocks: Great Glintstone Shard, Swift Glintstone Shard

📍 Liurnia: Gate Town Bridge area ⚔️ No boss

North of the Lake-Facing Cliffs Site of Grace, at the Liurnia gate where the Sorcerer enemies gather. The scroll is on a corpse near a large gravestone, in the graveyard area just off the main path leading to the Academy gate. Watch for the patrolling Sorcerers — grab the scroll and run if needed.

📜 Conspectus Scroll — Raya Lucaria Academy

Unlocks: Stars of Ruin, Founding Rain of Stars

📍 Academy graveyard, behind the Debate Parlor ⚠️ Academy interior required

Inside Raya Lucaria Academy. From the Debate Parlor Site of Grace, exit north into the large courtyard with rolling boulders. Immediately turn left and follow the path around the exterior wall. The scroll is on a corpse in a small graveyard tucked behind the Parlor building. Stars of Ruin fires 12 homing stars that are nearly impossible to dodge — mandatory for PvP sorcery builds.

🌟 Comet Azur (Free Spell — No Book Required)

Legendary sorcery — obtained directly from Primeval Sorcerer Azur

📍 Mt. Gelmir: Hermit Village ⚔️ No boss

From the Seethewater Terminus Site of Grace in Mt. Gelmir, head north through Hermit Village. At the far end of the village, find Primeval Sorcerer Azur — a crystalline, unmoving figure near the cliff edge. Interact with him to receive Comet Azur directly. This is not a spell book transaction — you receive the spell immediately. Comet Azur is the infamous one-shot boss-deleter when combined with the Cerulean Hidden Tear. The fourth spell book, Ancient Dragon Prayerbook, is in Crumbling Farum Azula — it unlocks lightning incantations from Brother Corhyn or Miriel, not Rennala.

3. How to Give Spell Books to Rennala

Once you have collected any spell book, return to the Roundtable Hold and speak to Rennala (she relocates there after defeating her in Raya Lucaria). Select the "Give Scroll" or "Give Prayerbook" dialog option. Each book you hand over permanently expands her shop inventory.

You can also give certain prayerbooks to Brother Corhyn or Miriel, Pastor of Vows in the Church of Vows. The Ancient Dragon Prayerbook from Crumbling Farum Azula, for example, goes to one of these two — not Rennala — and unlocks dragon-cult lightning incantations.

💡 Progression-Proof

Spell books cannot be permanently missed — they remain accessible until you enter New Game Plus. Even after burning the Erdtree, you can still collect them and turn them in. The only risk is forgetting they exist and never finding them.
