1. Why This Quest Matters

Latenna is a crippled Albinauric archer found at the Slumbering Wolf's Shack in southern Liurnia of the Lakes. She cannot walk. What she offers is her own Spirit Ash — one of the most powerful ranged summons in Elden Ring. But the Spirit Ash is only the beginning.

If you agree to her request and carry her Spirit Ash all the way to the Apostate Derelict in the Consecrated Snowfield, she will reveal a hidden secret. Her summon awakens a massive sleeping Albinauric woman inside the church, and in gratitude, you receive a Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone — one of only 8 available in the entire base game.

Each Somber ADSS lets you take a unique weapon from +9 to +10. There is no farming them. There is no alternate source. Every single one counts — and this one is locked behind one of the longest NPC escort quests in the game, spanning from mid-Liurnia all the way to the endgame snowfield.

The location is guarded by a Walking Mausoleum that patrols directly in front of the church. The area is permanently obscured by a blizzard. And the quest cannot even begin until you have the right half of the Haligtree Secret Medallion.

2. Route

Step 1 — Find Latenna

1Lakeside Crystal Cave — Southwest Liurnia of the Lakes. Requires a Stonesword Key to enter. Navigate through the cave, defeat the Bloodhound Knight boss at the bottom, and exit into the upper cliff area of southern Liurnia.
2Slumbering Wolf's Shack — Directly ahead after exiting the cave. Inside, you will find Latenna sitting beside her dead wolf companion, Lobo. She cannot move and will ask you to take her Spirit Ash to the Apostate Derelict.
🔑 Prerequisite

Latenna will only speak to you if you already possess the RIGHT half of the Haligtree Secret Medallion. The left half is obtained from Commander Niall at Castle Sol (Mountaintops of the Giants). The right half is found in the Village of the Albinaurics (southwest Liurnia), given by Albus — find him disguised as a pot near the cliffs.

Step 2 — Reach the Consecrated Snowfield

3Obtain both halves of the Haligtree Secret Medallion — Right from Albus in the Village of the Albinaurics, Left from Commander Niall at Castle Sol (Mountaintops of the Giants).
4Take the Grand Lift of Rold. At the lift, switch the action prompt to "Hoist Secret Medallion" — this routes you to the Consecrated Snowfield instead of the Mountaintops.

Step 3 — Navigate to Apostate Derelict

5The Consecrated Snowfield is a blinding blizzard zone. From the Hidden Path to the Haligtree site of grace, head northwest across the frozen river. Stay along the western cliff wall to avoid the worst of the blizzard and the roaming enemies.
6The Apostate Derelict is a ruined church in the far northwest corner of the snowfield. A Walking Mausoleum patrols directly in front of it. Wait for it to walk past, then sprint to the church entrance.

Step 4 — Complete the Quest

7Inside the Apostate Derelict, approach the massive sleeping Albinauric woman. Summon Latenna's Spirit Ash inside the church. A cutscene will play — Latenna greets her sister Phillia, and you receive the Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone as your reward.
🏆 Reward

Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone ×1 — one of only 8 in the base game. Takes any unique weapon from +9 to +10.

3. Hazard Guide

⚠ Bloodhound Knight — Level 40+ Recommended

The Lakeside Crystal Cave boss is a Bloodhound Knight in a small arena. At low levels, its rapid dash attacks and bleed buildup will end the fight in seconds. Bring a 100% physical shield or fight mounted if you can. Level 40+ with upgraded weapons makes this substantially easier. The Stonesword Key is consumed on entry — do not waste it if you are underleveled.

⚠ Medallion Prerequisite Is Non-Negotiable

Latenna will not offer her quest — she will not even speak to you — unless you possess the RIGHT half of the Haligtree Secret Medallion. The right half comes from Albus in the Village of the Albinaurics. If you kill Albus without speaking to him, the medallion still drops, but if you miss him entirely and progress too far, this is not missable — he stays in place until you find him.

⚠ Walking Mausoleum Patrol Timing

The Walking Mausoleum in front of the Apostate Derelict patrols in a wide loop. It will one-shot low-vigor players if it steps on you. Wait on the rocks near the church entrance. Watch its patrol path — it walks right-to-left across the church facade, then turns and walks back. Sprint in during the turn window. Once inside the church, the mausoleum cannot reach you.

⚠ Do Not Kill Latenna

Latenna can be attacked at the Slumbering Wolf's Shack. If she dies, the quest is permanently locked for that playthrough. She drops nothing of value. If you are unsure whether an NPC is hostile, lock-on will not target friendly NPCs — this is your test.
