1. Why You Want This Sword

The Ancient Meteoric Ore Greatsword holds the title of highest base damage among all DLC greatswords in Shadow of the Erdtree. For pure Strength builds, nothing in the Land of Shadow hits harder per swing. It is a colossal greatsword forged from ore that fell from the stars — and it looks like it.

Its unique skill, Meteorite, calls down an explosive meteor that strikes overhead with devastating area damage. The skill scales purely with weapon upgrade level and Strength — no Intelligence or Faith investment required. Use it to flatten groups, punish slow bosses, or create space when surrounded.

Best of all: no boss blocks your path to this weapon. It sits in a static ruin, guarded only by ambient enemies and tricky terrain. If you can navigate the southwest valley of the Land of Shadow, the sword is yours.

2. The Route: Moorth Ruins to Starfall Past

Leg 1 — Reach Moorth Ruins

1Start at the Moorth Ruins Site of Grace in southern Scadu Altus. This is your launch point — the ruins are a landmark visible from most of the southern plateau.
2From the grace, head southwest. You are looking for a valley descent — a sloping path that leads down from the plateau into a lower basin. The terrain here is uneven; use Torrent for the descent to avoid fall damage.

Leg 2 — Navigate the Valley

3At the valley floor, follow the basin southwest. You will pass through hostile enemy camps — Stone Golems and scavenger-type enemies patrol this area. You can fight through or ride past them on Torrent.
4The valley narrows as you approach the Ancient Ruins of Starfall Past. The ruins are unmistakable: massive, broken stone structures, star-shaped impact craters in the ground, and a faint blue glow emanating from the central ruin.

Leg 3 — Claim the Sword

5Enter the ruins and head to the southwest side of the central structure. You will see a large, static furnace embedded in the ruin wall — this is not a live Furnace Golem. It is part of the environment.
6At the base of the static furnace, on its southwest side, there is an interaction prompt. This is where the Ancient Meteoric Ore Greatsword is lodged. Interact to claim it — no boss fight, no puzzle, just a hidden pickup at the end of a treacherous journey.
💡 Torrent Recommended

The valley descent and enemy camps are significantly easier to navigate on Torrent. The terrain includes steep drops that a player on foot would need to carefully descend. Do not attempt this without Torrent unlocked for the DLC.

3. Hazard Guide

Enemy Density in the Valley

⚠ Dense Patrols

The valley between Moorth Ruins and Starfall Past is packed with Stone Golems, scavenger packs, and occasional ranged attackers on elevated platforms. At low Scadutree blessing levels, these enemies hit hard. Ride through on Torrent if you are under-leveled — do not waste flasks clearing every camp.

Terrain Navigation

⚠ Tricky Descent

The descent from the Scadu Altus plateau into the valley is steep and has few obvious landmarks. The slope is south of Moorth Ruins, hugging the cliff face. If you find yourself falling to your death, you have gone too far west — the safe descent is farther south. Use rainbow stones to test drops if you are unsure.

Static Furnace — Do Not Confuse

⚠ Not a Furnace Golem

The furnace in the Starfall Past ruins is a static environmental object — it does not wake up, attack, or react to you. This is not one of the roaming Furnace Golems that dominate other DLC regions. If you approach cautiously expecting a boss fight, you will waste time. Walk up, interact, and claim the sword.
