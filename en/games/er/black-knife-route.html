1. Why the Black Knife Set Matters

The Black Knife is not just another dagger. Its unique skill, Blade of Death, fires a projectile that reduces the enemy's maximum HP — a percentage-based debuff that works on bosses, including endgame and DLC encounters. Land it at the start of a fight, and the boss enters the battle with less health than they should have.

The matching Black Knife Armor Set is one of the few armor pieces in Elden Ring that silences your footsteps. Enemies that rely on sound to detect you — including several DLC boss arenas — will not hear you approach. The chest piece alone provides the silencing effect; the full set adds strong physical and holy defense.

And in the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC, the Armor of Night and Rakshasa's Great Katana extend the assassin's legacy. This guide covers the complete Black Knife route: dagger, armor, and the DLC content that carries the theme forward.

2. The Dagger — Black Knife Catacombs

WeaponSkillLocationBoss
Black KnifeBlade of Death (HP reduction projectile)Black Knife Catacombs, northeast LiurniaBlack Knife Assassin

Route

1Travel to the Black Knife Catacombs in northeast Liurnia of the Lakes. The entrance is along the cliffside east of the Frenzied Flame Village, near the Minor Erdtree.
2Navigate the catacombs. This is a standard dungeon: skeleton enemies, guillotine traps, and illusory walls. At the end of the dungeon, pass through the fog door to face the Black Knife Assassin boss.
3Defeat the Black Knife Assassin. The boss is invisible except during attack animations — watch for shimmering distortions in the air. Use wide-swinging weapons or area spells to reveal her position. Upon defeat, she drops the Black Knife dagger.

Route

4Travel to the Sainted Hero's Grave in Altus Plateau. It is located west of the Altus Highway Junction Site of Grace, near the Outer Wall Phantom Tree.
5This is a chariot dungeon — the infamous rolling death machines patrol the main corridors. The layout requires you to use alcoves and timing to avoid being crushed. At the bottom of the dungeon, beyond the chariot gauntlet, you will find the full Black Knife Armor Set on a corpse. No boss guards the armor — the chariots are the gatekeeper.
6The chest piece is the key. Equip the Black Knife Armor (chest) and your footsteps are silenced. This effect works immediately — you will hear the difference. Enemies that aggro on sound will not detect you at normal walking speed. This does not affect visual detection.

4. DLC Connection — Armor of Night & Rakshasa

The assassin's story does not end in the base game. In Shadow of the Erdtree, the Eastern Nameless Mausoleum holds a boss that drops gear thematically linked to the Black Knife assassins.

ItemTypeSource
Armor of NightFull armor set (helm, chest, gauntlets, greaves)Eastern Nameless Mausoleum — Rakshasa boss
Rakshasa's Great KatanaGreat Katana weaponEastern Nameless Mausoleum — Rakshasa boss

Route to the Eastern Nameless Mausoleum

7In the Land of Shadow (DLC), locate the Eastern Nameless Mausoleum. It is in the eastern region of the map, accessible after progressing through the main DLC path. The mausoleum is a small, enclosed dungeon with a single boss arena.
8Defeat Rakshasa — a fast, aggressive boss with extended katana combos and shadow-step teleports. She fights like an evolved Black Knife Assassin: invisible dashes, delayed strike patterns, and rapid slashes. Treat her like a harder version of the base-game assassin.
9Victory rewards the full Armor of Night set and Rakshasa's Great Katana. The Armor of Night does not silence footsteps — instead, it offers strong physical and magic defense with an ethereal aesthetic that mirrors the Black Knife set. The Great Katana is a Strength/Dexterity weapon with a unique multi-slash skill.

5. Hazard Guide

Black Knife Assassin — Invisible Boss

⚠ Teleport-Dash Combat

The Assassin is invisible for most of the fight and teleports between dash attacks. Use weapons with wide horizontal swings (greatswords, curved greatswords) or AoE spells/ashes to reveal her position. The Sentry's Torch from the Hermit Merchant in Altus Plateau reveals invisible enemies when equipped — hold it in your off-hand and the Assassin becomes fully visible. This trivializes the fight.

Sainted Hero's Grave Chariots

⚠ Lethal Environmental Hazard

The chariots in Sainted Hero's Grave kill instantly on contact. Study the timing pattern before running. Each alcove is safe for roughly 3-4 seconds between chariot passes. There are two chariots on separate tracks — listen for both. The armor set is past both chariot gauntlets at the very bottom of the dungeon. There is no Site of Grace near the armor — grab it quickly and use the shortcut lift back up.

Footstep Silence — Chest-Only

✅ Chest Piece Is Enough

Only the Black Knife Armor (chest) is required for the footstep-silencing effect. The hood, gauntlets, and greaves contribute to defense but do not affect sound. If you are using a different head/arm/leg piece for build optimization, you can wear only the chest and still walk silently. This is particularly useful for backstab-focused builds and for navigating DLC areas where sound-aggro enemies are common.

DLC Rakshasa — Faster Than the Assassin

⚠ Aggressive Shadow-Step

Rakshasa's combos are longer and faster than the base-game Black Knife Assassin. She chains 5-7 hit sequences with minimal recovery windows. Stay at mid-range, bait the end of her longest combo string, then punish with a jump attack or charged heavy. Do not trade hits — her katana applies bleed buildup. The Armor of Night and Great Katana are worth the effort, but Rakshasa is a genuine skill check — treat her as a DLC mid-boss, not a throwaway encounter.
