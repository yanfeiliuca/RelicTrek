1. Why This Weapon Is So Easy to Miss

Knight Bernahl is one of the few NPCs in Elden Ring who appears in three completely different regions across the course of a single playthrough. Each encounter is missable. If you skip a region, kill him early, or fail to exhaust a dialogue trigger, the questline breaks — and the Devourer's Scepter, a legendary great hammer, is permanently locked for that playthrough.

The Devourer's Scepter is no ordinary weapon. Its unique skill — Devourer of Worlds — drains HP from all nearby enemies on a charged heavy attack. At +10 with a Strength/Faith build, it is one of the most durable endgame weapons for both PvE mob clear and PvP invasions. The HP-drain mechanic also stacks with talismans like Godskin Swaddling Cloth and Taker's Cameo for absurd sustain.

The weapon drops only from Bernahl's invasion in Crumbling Farum Azula — an invasion that only triggers if you followed his questline through all three regions and never killed him at his earlier locations.

2. The Full Route — All Three Encounters

Step 1 — Warmaster's Shack, Limgrave

1Find Bernahl at the Warmaster's Shack in Stormhill, central Limgrave (northwest of the Gatefront Ruins, along the road toward Stormveil Castle). He sells Ashes of War — buy them now, as his shop inventory moves. Crucially: do NOT kill him. He drops the Devourer's Scepter if killed here, but you want the full questline for the Blasphemous Blade and Raging Wolf armor as well.

Step 2 — Volcano Manor, Mt. Gelmir

2Join Volcano Manor by speaking to Tanith in the main hall. Bernahl will be sitting in a chair in the common room (the hallway with all the NPCs, opposite Diallos). Exhaust his dialogue. He gives you the Letter to Bernahl, which sends you to assassinate Vargram the Raging Wolf and Errant Sorcerer Wilhelm in the Mountaintops of the Giants — a dual invasion sign near the Fortified Manor in Leyndell, Capital of Ash.
3Complete this assassination. Return to Bernahl. He rewards you with the Gelmir's Fury sorcery and the Raging Wolf armor set.

Step 3 — Post-Rykard Dialogue (The Kill Switch)

4Defeat Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy. After the fight, do NOT leave Volcano Manor immediately. Return to the common room and speak to Bernahl one more time. He will remark that Tanith is still eating Rykard's corpse and that his work here is done. This dialogue is the required trigger for his invasion in Farum Azula. Skip it, and he will never invade — no Devourer's Scepter.

Step 4 — Crumbling Farum Azula (The Final Encounter)

5Reach Crumbling Farum Azula (after the Fire Giant, via the Forge of the Giants cutscene). Progress through the level until you reach the Dragon Temple Lift site of grace. From here, go up the lift and head toward the crumbling bridge with the dragon. Just before the bridge, a chest sits on the right side of the walkway. As you approach the chest area, Recusant Bernahl invades — red phantom, wielding the Devourer's Scepter.
6Defeat Recusant Bernahl. He drops the Devourer's Scepter, the Blasphemous Claw (a reusable item that parries Maliketh's black blade attacks), and his armor set — the Beast Champion set.

3. Hazard Guide — Three Ways to Ruin This Questline

Hazard 1 — Don't Kill Bernahl at Warmaster's Shack

⚠ Permanent Lock If Killed Early

If you kill Bernahl in Limgrave, he drops the Devourer's Scepter immediately — but you permanently lose the Blasphemous Claw (Maliketh parry item), the Beast Champion armor set, the Raging Wolf armor set, and the Gelmir's Fury sorcery. The weapon alone is not worth losing everything else. Leave him alive. Complete the questline. You will get the weapon from his Farum Azula invasion plus all the other rewards.

Hazard 2 — The Rykard Dialogue Is a Hidden Trigger

⚠ Most Common Point of Failure

After killing Rykard, Volcano Manor NPCs start to leave. Many players fast-travel away and never speak to Bernahl again. This is the single most common reason the Farum Azula invasion does not trigger. Before leaving Volcano Manor after Rykard, walk back to the common room and exhaust Bernahl's post-Rykard dialogue. He should say something about Tanith's... eating habits. If you do not see this dialogue, reload the area and check again.

Hazard 3 — Concurrent with Blasphemous Blade Quest

⚠ Two Weapons, One Questline

Bernahl's questline runs parallel to the main Volcano Manor assassination chain that rewards the Blasphemous Blade from Rykard's Remembrance. You should progress both simultaneously: do Tanith's assassination letters (for the Blasphemous Blade path) alongside Bernahl's letter (for the Devourer's Scepter path). None of Bernahl's steps interfere with the Tanith/Rykard path — you can complete both in a single playthrough.

💡 Missed It? Check Church of Vows

If you accidentally aggro'd Bernahl but did not kill him, use Celestial Dew at the Church of Vows to reset NPC hostility. This works as long as he is still alive. If he is dead, no absolution can bring him back.
