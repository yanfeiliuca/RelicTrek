1. Why You Need It

Ranni's questline is the longest single NPC quest chain in Elden Ring. It spans 14 distinct steps across 9 separate locations — from the first church you visit to a hidden cathedral beneath the earth — and requires defeating one of the most lethal bosses in the game.

Most players meet Ranni at her rise behind Caria Manor and never see the quest through. Some reach Radahn and stop. Others retrieve the Fingerslayer Blade from Nokron and think they are done. But the real half of the quest — the descent through Ainsel River, the miniature doll that speaks only when you insist, the Baleful Shadow, the Lake of Rot, Astel — is a gauntlet that fewer than 5% of players complete.

The reward at the end is the Dark Moon Greatsword. It is the latest incarnation of FromSoftware's Moonlight Greatsword — a weapon that has appeared in nearly every game the studio has ever made, from King's Field to Armored Core to every Soulsborne title. In Elden Ring, it is locked behind the longest quest chain, and it is also the key to unlocking the Age of Stars ending.

Along the way you collect the Fingerslayer Blade, the Carian Inverted Statue, the Dark Moon Ring, and you cross the Lake of Rot and defeat Astel, Naturalborn of the Void. Each of these is a gate. Miss one, and the quest stops.

2. What the Dark Moon Greatsword Does

Weapon Type: Greatsword (Legendary) · Damage Type: Standard / Magic · Scaling: STR D / DEX D / INT C · Stat Requirements: 38 Intelligence, 16 Strength, 11 Dexterity · Passive: Frost buildup (55) on hit · Weight: 10.0

Moonlight Greatsword (Skill)FP 32Charge the blade with frost magic, then unleash a wave of moonlight with a heavy attack. Buffed state adds 65 Magic damage and increases Frost buildup.
Frost Buildup55 per hitTriggers Frostbite for 10% max HP damage + 20% defense debuff. Devastating against high-HP bosses.
INT ScalingC (base) → B (upgraded)Scales primarily with Intelligence. At 60+ INT, the charged projectile rivals sorcery spells in ranged damage.
Upgrade PathSomber Smithing StonesRequires Somber Stones 1–10. No Ash of War change — the skill is fixed and cannot be replaced.

The skill Moonlight Greatsword coats the blade in frost magic for 60 seconds. During the buff, charged heavy attacks fire a crescent wave of moonlight that travels a significant distance — this projectile costs no additional FP. The blade itself deals split physical/magic damage with frost buildup on every hit. At high Intelligence (60+), the charged projectile alone makes this one of the best ranged options in the game that does not consume FP per swing.

3. The 14-Step Route — From Church of Elleh to the Cathedral of Manus Celes

Ranni's questline is the longest in Elden Ring. Steps 1–8 are the "front half" — reasonably well-signposted. Steps 9–14 are the "back half" — the part 95% of players never see. Here is every step, in order.

Phase 1 — Liurnia and Caelid (Steps 1–5)

1Church of Elleh: After acquiring Torrent from Melina, return to the Church of Elleh at night. Ranni appears under the name "Renna" and gives you the Spirit Calling Bell. This is the first breadcrumb.
2Caria Manor → Ranni's Rise: Clear Caria Manor in northern Liurnia. At the end, enter the Three Sisters area. Ranni's Rise is the central tower. Climb to the top and agree to serve her. Speak to her three advisors: Blaidd (outside), War Counselor Iji (roadside), and Preceptor Seluvis (nearby tower).
3Siofra River Well: Take the Siofra River Well elevator in Mistwood, East Limgrave. Find Blaidd near the cliff edge overlooking the starry sky. He tells you the path forward is blocked — the stars must be moved, and only Radahn holds them.
4Seluvis → Sellen: Return to Seluvis at his tower. He gives you a letter of introduction for Sorceress Sellen. Travel to Waypoint Ruins in Limgrave and speak to Sellen. She reveals that Radahn must be defeated to release the stars — and thus unlock the way into Nokron.
5Redmane Castle — Starscourge Radahn: Travel to Redmane Castle in southeastern Caelid. Defeat Starscourge Radahn. Upon his death, a falling star crashes into the Mistwood, opening the entrance to Nokron, Eternal City.

Phase 2 — Nokron and the Blade (Steps 6–8)

6Nokron, Eternal City: Enter Nokron through the crater in southern Mistwood. Descend through the city, past the Mimic Tear boss, through the Ancestral Woods, and into the Night's Sacred Ground. The Fingerslayer Blade is in a chest beneath the giant skeleton throne.
7Return to Ranni: Bring the Fingerslayer Blade to Ranni at Ranni's Rise. She thanks you, reveals more of her plan, and gives you the Carian Inverted Statue. After she departs, she leaves you — and the next part of the quest is now hidden.
8Carian Study Hall — Inverted: Travel to the Carian Study Hall in eastern Liurnia. Place the Carian Inverted Statue on the pedestal at the entrance. The entire tower inverts — ceiling becomes floor. Traverse the inverted tower, defeat the Preceptor Miriam phantom, and reach the top for the Cursemark of Death and additional lore items.

Phase 3 — The Back Half (Steps 9–14)

9Renna's Rise → Ainsel River Main — THE DOLL: After Ranni departs, Renna's Rise (the northern tower of Three Sisters) unlocks. Climb to the top and take the waygate. You emerge in Ainsel River Main. Follow the path to find a coffin containing the Miniature Ranni Doll. Sit at any Site of Grace, select the doll from your inventory, and talk to it repeatedly — three or more times — until it finally speaks. This is the single most missed step in the entire game. Without it, the quest is dead.
10Nokstella, Eternal City → Baleful Shadow: Continue through Nokstella. Near the end of the area, you are invaded by the Baleful Shadow — a hostile copy of Blaidd. Defeat it to receive the Discarded Palace Key. Do not miss this key. It is required for Step 11.
11Grand Library of Raya Lucaria — Dark Moon Ring: Fast travel to the Grand Library (Rennala's chamber) at Raya Lucaria Academy. Use the Discarded Palace Key to open the locked chest behind Rennala. Inside is the Dark Moon Ring. This ring is essential — without it, you cannot finish the quest.
12Lake of Rot: Return to Ainsel River Main and push deeper. The path leads to the Lake of Rot — a vast scarlet swamp that deals constant Rot buildup. Cross it using Preserving Boluses, Flame Cleanse Me incantation, or simply sprinting to each pressure plate. At the far end, ride the coffin down the waterfall to the Grand Cloister.
13Astel, Naturalborn of the Void: At the end of the Grand Cloister, ride the final coffin deeper underground. You are transported to Astel's arena. Astel uses gravity magic, teleport grabs, and a screen-wide constellation explosion. Stay close to his head. The grab attack is a one-shot for most builds. Defeat him.
14Cathedral of Manus Celes — Dark Moon Greatsword: After Astel, the path opens to an elevator. Ride it up into the Cathedral of Manus Celes — a ruined church under the moon. Approach the hole in the floor. Ranni is there. Put the Dark Moon Ring on her finger. She rises, declares the Age of Stars, and leaves behind the Dark Moon Greatsword. Pick it up, and the quest is complete.
🏆 Rewards

Dark Moon Greatsword + Age of Stars ending unlocked + All en-route items (see Section 5)

4. Hazard Guide — Where Players Get Stuck or Locked Out

Critical Miss — Step 9: The Miniature Ranni Doll

⚠ Most Missed Step in the Entire Game

After picking up the Miniature Ranni Doll in Ainsel River, you MUST sit at a Site of Grace, open your inventory, select the doll, and choose "Talk" repeatedly. The first two or three attempts produce silence. Keep selecting "Talk." On the third or fourth attempt, the doll speaks. If you try once, assume it does nothing, and leave — the quest is dead. There is no in-game hint that you need to try multiple times. This single step fails more Ranni quest runs than every other step combined.

Lake of Rot — Constant Damage Zone

⚔️ Scarlet Rot Accumulation

The Lake of Rot deals continuous Scarlet Rot buildup. Once the bar fills, Scarlet Rot ticks for massive percentage HP damage. Do not try to tank it. Bring Preserving Boluses (buy from the Isolated Merchant in Dragonbarrow), equip the Immunizing Horn Charm, cast Flame Cleanse Me (12 Faith requirement — achievable with the Two Fingers Heirloom talisman), and sprint between the stone pressure plates that raise safe platforms. Torrent is unavailable here — this is all on foot.

Astel, Naturalborn of the Void — Two Attacks to Respect

🔥 Grab Attack

Astel teleports behind you or above you, then grabs. The grab is near-instant and usually a one-shot kill below 40 Vigor. The tell: Astel vanishes, and a faint distortion appears near your position. Roll the moment you see it — not on reaction to Astel reappearing, which is too late.

⭐ Constellation Explosion

Astel rises, creates a field of small glowing orbs that explode across the entire arena. This attack is the #1 cause of death for players who reach Astel. Run to the far edge of the arena — the orbs spread from Astel's center position and lose density at distance. Alternatively, if you are on a melee build, stay directly under Astel's head — the orbs radiate outward and the area directly beneath him is a safe pocket.

Permanent Lockout Checklist

🔒 These Actions Permanently Lock Ranni's Quest

1. Attacking any of Ranni's allies (Blaidd, Iji, Seluvis) before completing their roles. Church of Vows absolution CAN fix some NPC hostility — but not if the NPC is dead.
2. Giving Ranni the Fingerslayer Blade and then attacking her at Ranni's Rise. After she departs, there is no way to restore the quest.
3. Skipping the Miniature Ranni Doll interaction in Step 9. If you leave Ainsel River without exhausting the doll's dialogue, returning later will not fix it — the trigger is location-specific.
4. Missing the Discarded Palace Key from the Baleful Shadow. Without it, the Dark Moon Ring chest in the Grand Library remains locked forever.

5. En-Route Bonus Items — Everything Ranni's Questline Gives You

Ranni's questline is not just about the Dark Moon Greatsword. It is one of the most rewarding NPC chains in the game in terms of side loot. Here is every notable item you collect along the way.

💡 Mimic Tear

The Mimic Tear Ashes are in the same area as the Fingerslayer Blade (Night's Sacred Ground, Nokron), but behind a Stonesword Key door. Do not leave Nokron without it — it is the strongest spirit summon in the game for most builds and trivializes Astel if you summon it with a strong melee or bleed setup.

💡 Black Knife Tiche

Black Knife Tiche is on the Moonlight Altar plateau — the area above the Cathedral of Manus Celes, accessible after Astel. The Ringleader's Evergaol is in the northwest corner of this plateau. Alecto is a difficult fight, but Tiche is worth it: she deals percentage-based destined death damage and is one of the strongest spirit ashes in the game. The Moonlight Altar is also where you find the Moonlight Greatsword sorcery — a separate spell, not to be confused with the weapon.
