Nepheli Loux's Quest — Three NPCs, One Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone, and a Permanently Missable Merchant

1. Why This Quest Matters

Most players meet Gostoc in Stormveil Castle. He seems helpful — he suggests a side path past the main gate, and it works. Then, as you explore the castle, you find him locked in a room. Release him, and he continues to appear, offering goods for sale.

What most players do not realize is that Gostoc is also the NPC who backstabs you — literally. After you die in Stormveil, Gostoc takes a cut of your dropped runes. Many players kill him for it. That is the permanent mistake.

If you keep Gostoc alive and complete Nepheli Loux's full questline, two things happen at the end: Nepheli gives you an Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone as a quest reward, and Gostoc becomes a merchant in Stormveil's throne room who sells a second Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone for 20,000 runes. This is the only purchasable ADSS in the entire game. Kill Gostoc and you lose it forever — there is no second chance and no alternative source.

The quest also interconnects with Kenneth Haight's questline and indirectly with Rogier's — three NPC threads converge on Stormveil's throne room at the end.

2. Complete Route

Step 1 — Do Not Kill Gostoc

1In Stormveil Castle, speak to Gostoc at the main gate and take his suggested side path. Later, find him locked in a room — release him. Never attack him under any circumstances. He will steal a portion of your runes when you die in the castle — absorb the loss. It is a one-time scripted event per area; the runes are negligible compared to what you gain at the end.

Step 2 — Find Nepheli in Stormveil

2Before fighting Godrick, find Nepheli Loux in a side room near the Secluded Cell site of grace. Exhaust her dialogue. She will offer to help in the Godrick fight — summon her if you wish, though it is not required for the quest.

Step 3 — Roundtable Hold

3After defeating Godrick, return to Roundtable Hold. Speak to Nepheli (she will be near the Twin Maiden Husks or by Gideon's study). Exhaust her dialogue. Also speak to Rogier and Gideon about her. Rogier mentions she is the daughter of Hoarah Loux.

Step 4 — Liurnia: Village of the Albinaurics

4Travel to the Village of the Albinaurics in Liurnia (southwestern Liurnia, under the moonlight altar cliff). Find Nepheli sitting near the village entrance, despondent after witnessing the massacre. Exhaust her dialogue. She will return to Roundtable Hold afterward.

Step 5 — Kenneth Haight's Quest

5Find Kenneth Haight in eastern Limgrave (near Mistwood). Clear Fort Haight for him (kill the knight at the top). Then find him again at Fort Haight afterward. Exhaust all dialogue. Kenneth will eventually mention he is searching for a rightful ruler for Limgrave.

Step 6 — Return to Stormveil Throne Room

6After completing both Nepheli's and Kenneth's dialogue chains, fast travel to the Godrick the Grafted site of grace (or walk to the throne room). Inside, you will find Nepheli Loux, Kenneth Haight, and Gostoc gathered together. Nepheli has been crowned as the new ruler of Limgrave.
7Speak to Nepheli: she rewards you with an Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone. Then speak to Gostoc: he is now a merchant selling a second Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone for 20,000 runes. Buy it immediately — it is the only purchasable ADSS in the game.
💎 Quest Yield

Total: 2 Ancient Dragon Smithing Stones — 1 from Nepheli (free reward) + 1 from Gostoc (20,000 runes). No other quest or merchant in Elden Ring offers a purchasable ADSS.

3. Hazard Guide

Gostoc's Betrayal — Scripted, Not Personal

⚠️ Do Not Retaliate

Gostoc is scripted to steal a portion of your runes every time you die in Stormveil Castle. This is not a bug. It is part of his character — he is a scavenger preying on fallen Tarnished. The rune loss is small and temporary. Killing him for revenge permanently removes the only purchasable ADSS in the game. Absorb the backstab. The payoff is worth it.

Quest Interconnection — Do Not Skip Rogier

🔗 Rogier Dialogue Required

After Nepheli returns to Roundtable Hold from Stormveil, you must speak to Rogier about her. Rogier's dialogue triggers the next phase of Nepheli's quest. If you skip Rogier, Nepheli may remain stuck in Roundtable Hold indefinitely. Additionally, Rogier's own questline interconnects with Ranni's — progressing one can advance or lock the other. Speak to everyone at Roundtable Hold before moving forward.

Timing — Do Not Progress Too Far

⏱️ Quest Lock Warning

Certain main-story events — particularly reaching the Mountaintops of the Giants or defeating Maliketh — can lock Nepheli's quest progression. Completing Farum Azula is a known quest-breaking point. Complete the entire Nepheli/Kenneth/Gostoc chain before burning the Erdtree to guarantee all rewards.

Gostoc's Stock — Buy Before NG+

🔄 Does Not Carry Over

Gostoc's ADSS inventory does not restock in NG+. You must buy the stone in each playthrough. If you enter NG+ without purchasing it, that copy is gone forever from that cycle.
