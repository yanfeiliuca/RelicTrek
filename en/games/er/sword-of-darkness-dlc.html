Sword of Darkness — The DLC's Three-Altar Puzzle That Gives You Two Legendary Weapons

1. Why This Puzzle Matters

Deep in the Fog Rift Catacombs, you find a weapon called the Stone-Sheathed Sword. It sits on an altar. You pick it up. You swing it. Nothing special happens — its weapon art does not activate, it has no unique properties, and its damage is unremarkable. Most players stick it in storage and forget about it.

That is the wrong move. The Stone-Sheathed Sword is not a finished weapon — it is the starting piece of a three-altar puzzle that spans the entire Shadow of the Erdtree DLC. Find two more hidden altars in two different regions, and the sword transforms into one of two legendary weapons: the Sword of Light (holy rays, Faith-scaling, holy damage boost) or the Sword of Darkness (darkness AoE, Faith-scaling, reduces enemy holy resistance).

You can switch back and forth between the two forms at any time by returning to the corresponding altar. This means you effectively get two legendary weapons from one puzzle — and you can change your mind whenever you want.

2. Sword of Light vs. Sword of Darkness

PropertySword of LightSword of Darkness
ScalingFaith (primary), Strength/DexFaith (primary), Strength/Dex
Weapon ArtLight — Wide arc of holy raysDarkness — AoE cloud, reduces enemy holy resistance
PassiveBoosts holy damage dealtReduces target holy resistance
Best ForHoly-damage builds, general PvEBoss fights, PvP holy builds
Altar LocationAncient Ruins of Rauh (Altar #3)Ruins of Unte (Altar #2)

Both forms scale primarily with Faith. The Sword of Light excels in general PvE — its holy ray weapon art clears groups and applies a passive holy damage boost. The Sword of Darkness is a boss-killer — its darkness AoE reduces the enemy's holy resistance, making subsequent holy attacks (including your own) deal dramatically more damage. In PvP, the resistance debuff can turn a holy build from mediocre to lethal.

🔄 Switching

Return to the corresponding altar with the sword in your inventory. Interact with the altar to transform it. You can switch forms as many times as you want — there is no cost, no limit, and no lock-in.

3. Full Route — All Three Altars

Altar 1 — Fog Rift Catacombs (Get the Sword)

1Enter Fog Rift Catacombs: From the Scadu Altus region, find the catacomb entrance in the cliff face west of the Moorth Ruins. Descend through the first chamber.
2Navigate the spike trap puzzle: The catacomb's central room has ceiling spikes that slam down in a pattern. Bait the spikes down by stepping onto the pressure plate, then step back. Once the spikes are down, cross the room while they reset.
3Find the hole: After the spike room, look for a broken section of floor — a hole drops into a lower chamber. Jump in. (This looks like a trap. It is not.)
4Claim the Stone-Sheathed Sword: In the hidden chamber below, the sword rests on an altar. Pick it up. This is the starting item. It does nothing yet — keep going.

Altar 2 — Ruins of Unte (Transform to Darkness)

5Reach Shadow Keep's secret path: From the Shadow Keep Main Gate site of grace, take the left path through the barracks. Descend the elevator to the underground waterway.
6Defeat the Furnace Golem: The path to Ruins of Unte is guarded by a Furnace Golem. Use Hefty Furnace Pots (throw into its head basket) or attack its knee weak points with charged heavies to stagger it. Once staggered, riposte the glowing face for massive damage. The Golem is slow but its fire attacks can one-shot low-Vigor builds — stay behind it.
7Find Altar 2: Past the Furnace Golem, enter the Ruins of Unte. The altar chamber is at the far end of the ruins, marked by an unlit brazier on either side. Interact with the altar while holding the Stone-Sheathed Sword to transform it into the Sword of Darkness.

Altar 3 — Ancient Ruins of Rauh (Transform to Light)

8Reach the Ancient Ruins of Rauh: From the Rauh Base site of grace, ascend through the ruins. Take the central elevator shortcut to the upper ramparts.
9Cross the invisible bridge: At the upper ramparts, look east toward a distant tower. There is an invisible bridge connecting the ramparts to the tower. There is NO visual indicator — no shimmer, no glow, no messages. Walk forward into empty air and the bridge will catch you. Trust the geometry.
10Transform to Sword of Light: Inside the tower is Altar 3. Interact with it while holding the sword (in either form — Darkness or the original Stone-Sheathed) to transform it into the Sword of Light.
🗺️ Route Summary

Fog Rift Catacombs (spike trap → hole → sword) → Ruins of Unte (Shadow Keep path → Furnace Golem → Darkness altar) → Ancient Ruins of Rauh (invisible bridge → Light altar). Approximate time: ~2 hours if you know the routes, ~3-4 hours blind.

4. Hazard Guide — What Will Kill You

Spike Trap Navigation (Fog Rift Catacombs)

⚡ Bait, Don't Rush

The spike ceiling kills you in one hit. Do not try to sprint through. Step onto the pressure plate in the center of the room to trigger the spikes, then immediately dodge backward. The spikes slam down and take ~4 seconds to reset. Cross while they are rising. The hole to the secret chamber is on the far side of the spike room, in the back-left corner — jump down into it. Do not skip it — the hole is the only way to the Stone-Sheathed Sword.

Furnace Golem (Ruins of Unte Approach)

🔥 Three Ways to Beat It

Method 1 — Hefty Furnace Pots: Craft Hefty Furnace Pots using furnace visages. Throw them into the Golem's head basket. Two direct hits stagger it for a riposte. Method 2 — Knee Weak Points: The Golem's knee joints glow orange when vulnerable. Charged heavy attacks on the knees build stagger rapidly. Method 3 — Torrent: Mount Torrent, ride circles around the Golem, and slash its ankles. This is slower but safest. Avoid standing in front of it — the fire breath and overhead slam both one-shot low-Vigor builds.

Invisible Bridge (Ancient Ruins of Rauh)

🌉 Walk Forward Into Nothing

This is the single most disorienting puzzle in the DLC. From the upper ramparts of the Ancient Ruins of Rauh, you must walk east into open air toward a distant tower. There is no visual cue — no glass bridge, no floating runes, no shimmer. The bridge simply exists, invisibly. Walk forward at a normal pace. Do not sprint — sprinting can carry you off the edges of the bridge. If you fall, you die. Use Rainbow Stones if you need to test the path: they will land on the invisible surface, confirming you are on the bridge. The bridge is straight for ~40 meters, then makes a 90-degree left turn before reaching the tower.

💡 Co-op Tip

If you are playing co-op, have one player walk the invisible bridge first while the other watches. The walking player's position relative to the tower gives the second player a visual reference for where the bridge is. Once one player reaches the tower, the second can follow the same path.
