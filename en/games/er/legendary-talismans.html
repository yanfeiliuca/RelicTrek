1. Why You Need Them

The eight Legendary Talismans are the most powerful passive equipment pieces in Elden Ring. Between them they cover HP and stamina boosts, stat increases, spellcasting enhancements, physical damage negation, and more. Collecting all eight unlocks the Legendary Talismans trophy — required for 100% completion — and gives you the full toolkit for maximum build optimization.

None of these talismans are permanently missable. You can collect them at any point before the final boss, even after finishing the game and continuing into post-game cleanup. That said, several are hidden behind illusory walls, deep underground zones, or late-game legacy dungeons that no main-quest marker will ever point you toward.

This guide covers every talisman: what it does, where to find it, when to go for it, and what hazards to watch for along the way.

3. Prerequisites & Optimal Route

The eight talismans are spread across the full span of the game. No talisman requires another to access — each is an independent pickup. But the zones they live in have their own progression gates. Here is the most efficient collection order:

Early — Radagon Icon

1Radagon Icon — Raya Lucaria Academy, Liurnia. Reach the Debate Parlor site of grace. Exit north into the courtyard, turn left up the collapsed ramp. Jump across to the upper floor balcony. The chest is in plain sight. No boss required, though you must clear the Red Wolf of Radagon to reach the Debate Parlor in the first place.

Mid — Moon of Nokstella, Godfrey Icon

2Moon of Nokstella — Nokstella, Eternal City. Accessible via the Ainsel River underground route after acquiring the Dark Moon Ring from the Lake of Rot. The talisman is in a chest beneath a giant stone throne in the main chamber. No boss directly guards it, but reaching Nokstella requires navigating the Lake of Rot and defeating Astel, Naturalborn of the Void.
3Godfrey Icon — Divine Tower of Caelid. Take the teleport on the eastern side of the Radahn boss arena (Starscourge Radahn must be defeated). Climb to the top of the tower. The illusory wall is in a small room midway up — roll through it to find the chest.

Late — Radagon's Soreseal, Old Lord's Talisman

4Radagon's Soreseal — Crumbling Farum Azula. From the Dragon Temple Rooftop grace, head east across the crumbling walkways. In the room with the beastmen, roll through the illusory wall on the north side. The talisman is on a corpse inside.
5Old Lord's Talisman — Crumbling Farum Azula. From the Dragon Temple grace, head west through the side chamber with the sleeping beastmen. The chest is in the back corner. Collect both Farum Azula talismans in one pass through the zone.

End — Erdtree's Favor +2, Marika's Soreseal, Dragoncrest Greatshield

6Erdtree's Favor +2 — Deeproot Depths. Accessible via Mohgwyn Palace (drop down after Mohg) or via the coffin in Ainsel River Grand Cloister. Navigate westward through the root corridors. A Crucible Knight guards the talisman, but you can grab it without fighting if you are fast enough.
7Marika's Soreseal + Dragoncrest Greatshield — Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree. Enter via the Prayer Room grace. Dragoncrest Greatshield is in a chest above the elevator shaft after a series of platforming drops. Marika's Soreseal is behind a Stonesword Key fog wall near the Drainage Channel grace. Collect both in one Elphael run — the zone is punishing and you do not want to clear it twice.
💡 Collection Tip

You can collect all eight talismans after defeating the final boss. Elden Ring does not force you into NG+ — choose "continue" after the credits to return to the world and clean up anything you missed.

4. Hazard Guide

Illusory Wall Talismans — Radagon's Soreseal & Godfrey Icon

🔍 Radagon's Soreseal — Farum Azula Illusory Wall

The wall is in a small room northeast of the Dragon Temple Rooftop grace. It looks identical to every other wall in the zone — no messages, no visual tell. Walk up and press the interact button or roll through it. If you are not sure you found the right wall, it is the one directly opposite the entrance door.

🔍 Godfrey Icon — Divine Tower of Caelid Illusory Wall

Halfway up the Divine Tower of Caelid, in the room with a large elevator and a single enemy, the wall on the east side is fake. Roll through to find the chest. The Divine Tower is gated behind Starscourge Radahn — you cannot enter until he is dead.

Elphael Double Run — Marika's Soreseal + Dragoncrest Greatshield

⚠ Two Talismans, One Run

Elphael is one of the hardest zones in Elden Ring. The Dragoncrest Greatshield requires a series of precise platforming drops from the building above the Prayer Room elevator — a single misstep sends you to your death. Marika's Soreseal is behind a Stonesword Key fog wall near the Drainage Channel, after a gauntlet of rot-infested enemies. Bring at least one Stonesword Key. Do not leave Elphael until you have both.

Erdtree's Favor +2 — Root Navigation

🌳 Deeproot Depths Navigation

Deeproot Depths has no path markings and the giant root structures look nearly identical from every angle. From the Deeproot Depths grace, head west through the root corridors. When you reach a large open cavern with a Crucible Knight, the talisman is on a corpse in the back-left corner. You can sprint past the Knight, grab it, and fast-travel out — but if you want the runes and his weapon, fight him.
