1. Why You'll Run Out

21 Ancient Dragon Smithing Stones exist per playthrough — 13 in the base game, 8 in Shadow of the Erdtree. That sounds like a lot until you realize each weapon needs one stone to go from +24 to +25, and you will want to do that for dozens of weapons across multiple builds.

The real problem: of those 21 stones, 5 to 6 are locked behind NPC questlines with permanently missable steps. You miss one dialogue choice, kill the wrong NPC, or progress too far in the story — and that stone is gone until NG+.

Most players who complete the game blind end their first playthrough with 10 to 14 stones. That is enough for 2 to 3 fully upgraded standard weapons but far short of what collectors and build experimenters want. If you want a full armory of +25 weapons for PvP or challenge runs, every single stone counts.

And then there are the Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stones — even rarer. Only 2 of those exist from NPC questlines (Latenna and Millicent). Miss either, and a Somber weapon stays at +9 forever.

3. Priority Route

The order matters. Some questlines break if you progress the main story too far. Here is the priority sequence:

Phase 1 — Early to Mid-Game (Do These ASAP)

1Sellen questline: Complete before reaching the Mountaintops of the Giants. At Raya Lucaria, accept Sellen's offer and help her — do NOT side with Jerren. Finishing her quest yields 1 Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone.
2Nepheli Loux + Gostoc: Requires Kenneth Haight's quest complete, Nepheli alive and given the Stormhawk King ashes, and Gostoc alive (do NOT kill him in Stormveil — many players do for the gate shortcut). Resolve at Godrick's throne room for 2 stones.
3Gurranq 9th Deathroot: Collect all 9 Deathroot across the game and feed them to Gurranq in Caelid. The 9th Deathroot is in the Consecrated Snowfield — off the main path. Get this before burning the Erdtree. Yields 1 stone.

Phase 2 — Late Game

4Latenna questline: Find Latenna in Liurnia (Slumbering Wolf's Shack), then deliver her to the Apostate Derelict in the Consecrated Snowfield. Rewards 1 Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone.
5Millicent questline: Assist Millicent against her sisters at the Haligtree. Choosing to aid her (not invade) yields 1 Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone. This is the second and final Somber stone per run.
6Fixed-location pickups: Grab the Mohgwyn Palace, Elphael x2, Farum Azula x2, Anastasia invasion, Prayer Room, and Raya Lucaria path stones during natural exploration.

Phase 3 — DLC (Shadow of the Erdtree)

7Gravesite Plain: First 3 DLC stones. Get the Furnace Golem drop, the Belurat chest, and the Hornsent Grandam reward. Do not kill Hornsent Grandam prematurely.
8Taylew's Ruined Forge: This is the most hidden forge in the DLC. Navigate the Shadow Keep underground waterways to find the entrance. Inside: an illusory wall on the first floor, and a lava vent puzzle leads to the second stone in the deepest chamber. Bring a torch.
9Scadu Altus / Shadow Keep / Rauh: Final 3 stones. Moorth Ruins encampment, Church District stonesword key gate, and Ancient Ruins of Rauh upper ramparts via the central elevator.
📋 Completion Check

After finishing the route: 13 base-game stones (2 of them Somber) + 8 DLC stones = 21 total. If your count is off, double-check the Gurranq 9th Deathroot and Nepheli + Gostoc — those are the most commonly missed.

4. Hazard Guide — What Goes Wrong

Most Commonly Missed — 9th Deathroot

⚠️ Why Players Miss It

Most players feed 4 or 5 Deathroot to Gurranq and stop — they unlock the Beast Eye, get a few incantations, and assume the questline is done. The 9th Deathroot is in the Consecrated Snowfield, reachable only via the Haligtree Secret Medallion, in a location far from any major landmark. Without a guide, you will not find it. The reward for the 9th is an Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone — miss this, and you lose one of the rarest stones in a run.

Sellen Questline — One Wrong Choice Locks You Out

⚠️ Side with Sellen, NOT Jerren

During Sellen's quest, you will be asked to choose between aiding Sellen or aiding Jerren. If you side with Jerren, Sellen dies and her stone is permanently lost for the playthrough. This is a binary, irreversible choice with no warning dialog. Always choose Sellen — Jerren's reward is replaceable. Sellen's stone is not.

Nepheli + Gostoc — Keep Gostoc Alive

⚠️ Do Not Kill Gostoc in Stormveil

Many players discover that Gostoc steals runes from you on death in Stormveil Castle. The natural response is to kill him. If you do, you are locked out of two Ancient Dragon Smithing Stones — no workaround. Keep Gostoc alive through the entire game. His stones come from the Nepheli + Kenneth Haight questline resolution at Godrick's throne room, which also requires Nepheli to be alive and given the Stormhawk King ashes.

Taylew's Ruined Forge — DLC Navigation Trap

⚠️ Most Hidden Forge in the DLC

Taylew's Ruined Forge is accessed through the Shadow Keep underground waterways — a zone many players skip because it appears to be a dead-end sewer area. The forge entrance is behind a waterfall in the waterway's central chamber. Inside, the first stone requires finding an illusory wall with no telltale signs. The second stone requires navigating a lava vent puzzle that will kill you instantly if misjudged. Bring a torch or the Lantern for visibility, and use Rainbow Stones to test safe footing in the lava puzzle area.

💡 NG+ Reminder

Ancient Dragon Smithing Stones respawn in NG+. The same questlines must be repeated. If you are going for a full +25 armory, plan to collect all 21 per cycle and prioritize the missable questline stones each time.
