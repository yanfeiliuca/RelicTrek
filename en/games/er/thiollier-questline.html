1. Why Thiollier's Questline Matters

Thiollier is one of the most important NPCs in the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC — and his questline is tied directly to Saint Trina, the enigmatic figure connected to Miquella's past. Where the base game left Millicent's story behind, the DLC picks up those threads through Thiollier's devotion to Saint Trina.

Thiollier follows Saint Trina with a singular, almost obsessive devotion. As you advance through the Land of Shadow, his path mirrors yours — moving from Cross site to Cross site, searching for any trace of Saint Trina's presence. The quest is not just about following him; it is about finding what he cannot find alone.

The true prize is Saint Trina's Hidden Needle — a weapon that applies Eternal Sleep, an upgraded form of the Sleep status effect. Where standard Sleep wears off quickly and is easily shaken, Eternal Sleep lasts significantly longer and is much harder for enemies to break out of. Against aggressive DLC bosses that leave no openings, this weapon creates openings that did not exist before. It is one of the strongest DLC-exclusive status weapons in the game.

Thematically, this questline is the DLC's answer to the base game's most poignant side story. If you finished Millicent's quest and wanted more — this is where that story continues, refracted through Saint Trina and the shadow Miquella left behind.

2. Route — Gravesite Plain to Saint Trina's Hidden Needle

Step 1 — First Meeting at Gravesite Plain

1Enter the DLC at Gravesite Plain. Thiollier can be found near the first Miquella's Cross site you encounter. Speak with him. He will express his devotion to Saint Trina and mention that he intends to follow the path of the crosses to find her.

Step 2 — Advance Through the DLC

2As you progress through the Land of Shadow and activate successive Miquella's Cross sites, Thiollier will appear at each one. He moves alongside you through the Cross sites. At each location, exhaust ALL dialogue options — this is critical. His dialogue updates with new information about Saint Trina and Miquella as you progress deeper into the DLC.

Step 3 — Find Saint Trina's Memory

3Deep in the DLC, you will discover Saint Trina's Memory — a key item hidden in a location tied to Miquella's past. The exact location reveals itself as you follow the narrative through the Cross sites. Once you have this item, return to Thiollier at his most recent location.

Step 4 — Give Saint Trina's Memory to Thiollier

4Present Saint Trina's Memory to Thiollier. This triggers a significant dialogue sequence and advances him to the final stage of his questline. He will process what the memory contains and make a decision that locks in the questline's conclusion.

Step 5 — Questline Conclusion

5After the final dialogue, the questline concludes and you receive Saint Trina's Hidden Needle — the quest-exclusive weapon that applies Eternal Sleep. The weapon cannot be obtained through any other means.
💎 Saint Trina's Hidden Needle

Applies Eternal Sleep: upgraded Sleep status with extended duration and higher resistance to being shaken off. One of the strongest DLC-exclusive status weapons. Quest-exclusive — not available through any other means.

3. Hazard Guide — What Can Go Wrong

Concurrent Questline Conflicts

⚠ Runs Concurrently With Leda's Questline

Thiollier's questline runs concurrently with Leda's questline in the DLC. Actions you take for Leda can affect Thiollier's availability and dialogue state. If a step in Thiollier's quest isn't triggering, check your progress on Leda's questline — you may need to advance or pause one to unblock the other. The two questlines are designed to intersect, not block each other, but the ordering matters.

Dialogue Exhaustion Is Mandatory

⚠ Exhaust ALL Dialogue at Every Stage

Thiollier has multiple dialogue branches at each Miquella's Cross site. If you speak to him once and leave, you may miss the trigger that advances his questline to the next stage. At every location, keep talking until he repeats himself. This is the single most common reason players get stuck on this questline — they assume one conversation is enough.

Stuck? Rest and Return

⚠ Rest at Grace Between Stages

If Thiollier is not appearing at the next expected location, rest at a Site of Grace and return. Some questline state updates require a Grace reset to register. If he still does not appear after resting, verify that you have exhausted all dialogue at his previous location — this is almost always the root cause.

💡 Checklist

1) Exhaust all dialogue at every encounter. 2) Check Leda's questline progress if stuck. 3) Rest at Grace and return if Thiollier isn't where he should be. 4) Do not progress past the DLC's point of no return before finishing the questline.
