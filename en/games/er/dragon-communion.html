1. Why Dragon Communion Matters

Dragon Hearts drop from every named dragon in Elden Ring. They are the exclusive currency for Dragon Communion Incantations — some of the most visually spectacular and tactically versatile spells in the game. Ekzykes's Decay inflicts Scarlet Rot. Smarag's Glintstone Breath deals sustained magic damage. Fortissax's Lightning Spear is the highest raw-damage dragon incantation available.

The system has two altars. The Church of Dragon Communion on the Limgrave coast (accessible from the start) only offers 1-heart incantations. The Cathedral of Dragon Communion in southern Caelid (accessible early, dangerous to reach) offers the full catalog — every incantation in the game, from 1-heart basics to 3-heart endgame spells.

Most players collect hearts from dead dragons and never visit either altar. Some never find the Cathedral at all. This guide lists every heart source, every incantation, and the fastest route to unlock the full Dragon Communion arsenal.

Elder Dragon Greyoll outside Fort Faroth in Caelid. She is completely immobile — she cannot fight back. 💎 2-Heart Priority Strategy

With limited hearts, prioritize: Smarag's Glintstone Breath (magic DPS for bosses weak to magic) and Ekzykes's Decay (Scarlet Rot for anything that can be rotted). These two alone cover the majority of boss encounters. After those, Fortissax's Lightning Spear for lightning-weak enemies, then fill out the 1-heart basics as heart surplus allows.

4. The Two Altars — Location and Access

Church of Dragon Communion (Limgrave)

1From the First Step site of grace, head west along the coast. Look for a small island connected by a land bridge. The church sits on this island. Activate the Church of Dragon Communion site of grace inside.
2This altar only accepts 1-heart incantations. Use it early to unlock Dragonfire or Rotten Breath. Its limited catalog makes it a starter altar — do not waste a trip here if you already have 2+ hearts saved.

Cathedral of Dragon Communion (Caelid)

3From the Caelid Highway South site of grace, ride southwest along the road through the swamp. Watch for roaming Kindred of Rot pests and the Putrid Avatar guarding the approach. The Cathedral is a large structure on the southern coast.
4Inside, activate the Cathedral of Dragon Communion site of grace. This altar offers the full catalog — every incantation from 1 to 3 hearts. This is your primary altar for all spending. The Cathedral is also where you trade Remembrances from Fortissax and Placidusax (via Enia at Roundtable Hold first) for their unique lightning incantations.
⚠️ Caelid Route Hazard

The approach to the Cathedral runs through the Swamp of Aeonia. The Putrid Avatar guarding the Cathedral entrance inflicts Scarlet Rot with every attack. Use fire damage against it. Alternatively, approach from the east (coast side) by riding along the beach — bypasses the swamp entirely but requires navigating cliff edges. At low level, the beach route is safer.

5. Hazard Guide

Do Not Waste Hearts — Plan Before Spending

⚠️ Hearts Are Finite Per Playthrough

There are exactly enough hearts from all dragon kills in one playthrough to unlock every incantation — but only if you plan. Wasting early hearts on low-priority 1-heart incantations can lock you out of 2-heart upgrades without farming in NG+. Calculate your spending before visiting any altar: total hearts available = ~18 across all sources (including Greyoll's 5). All ten basic incantations cost 17 hearts total (five 1-heart, five 2-heart). Greyoll's Roar + the two remembrance incantations add 8 more hearts. Prioritize 2-heart spells first, then fill the 1-heart basics as surplus allows.

Greyoll Method — Bleed Is the Key

🩸 Approach from Southwest

Greyoll outside Fort Faroth is surrounded by five smaller dragons that share a health pool with her. Killing the small dragons also damages Greyoll. Do NOT kill the small dragons. Instead, approach Greyoll from the southwest (behind Fort Faroth, using the cliff path). Her tail is exposed and the small dragons cannot reach you there. Use a bleed weapon — Morning Star (buyable from the Weeping Peninsula merchant), any katana with Unsheathe, or the Flail. Bleed procs deal percentage-based damage, bypassing her massive health pool. With a +0 bleed weapon, she dies in approximately 2 minutes. Use a Gold-Pickled Fowl Foot before the final hit for the 74,000 rune boost.

Lichdragon Fortissax — Quest Gate

🔗 Requires Fia's Questline

Fortissax is gated behind Fia's questline in Deeproot Depths. You must: (1) exhaust Fia's dialogue in Roundtable Hold, (2) give her the Cursemark of Death (from Ranni's quest) or let her hold you, (3) defeat Fia's Champions, and (4) enter the Deathbed Dream. The arena has permanent Death Blight buildup on the floor — stay mobile. Fortissax's remembrance gives access to Fortissax's Lightning Spear, the highest burst-damage dragon incantation. Do not skip this fight if you are building a dragon communion character.

Dragonlord Placidusax — The Secret Boss

🐉 Hidden Arena

In Crumbling Farum Azula, from the Beside the Great Bridge site of grace, take the elevator down. Exit and look for a set of floating rubble platforms leading down to a storm-swept arena. You will find a prompt to "Lie down" — this transports you to Placidusax's arena. He drops 2 hearts and his Remembrance can be traded for Placidusax's Ruin, a massive AoE lightning explosion that is one of the most visually impressive incantations in the game.
