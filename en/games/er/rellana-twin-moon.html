1. Why Rellana and Her Rewards Matter

Rellana, the Twin Moon Knight, is the legacy dungeon boss of the Shadow Keep — one of the major strongholds in the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC. She wields two blades simultaneously: one of fire, one of moon. Her fight is a dual-phase spectacle that tests both positioning and adaptability, and she ranks among the most memorable bosses in the expansion.

Her Remembrance offers a choice between two extraordinary rewards. The first is the Twin Moon Blades — a paired Light Greatsword that scales with both Intelligence and Faith, dealing split magic and fire damage. It is widely considered the best Int+Faith weapon in the entire DLC, and one of the strongest hybrid-scaling weapons FromSoftware has ever designed. If your build invests in both casting stats, this weapon is the reason to enter the Shadow Keep.

The alternative reward is the Moon and Fire Swords sorcery — a dual-projectile spell that fires both a magic blade and a fire blade simultaneously. It covers two damage types in a single cast and excels against enemies with split resistances. If you prefer a casting-focused Int+Faith build over a melee hybrid, this sorcery is your pick.

Both rewards are among the best items in the DLC for Intelligence/Faith characters. The question is not whether to defeat Rellana — it is which reward to claim first.

2. Route — Castle Ensis to the Remembrance

Step 1 — First Encounter at Castle Ensis

1Early in the DLC, you will encounter Rellana for the first time at Castle Ensis. This is a required story encounter — she appears as a field boss blocking your path. Defeat her here to continue into the DLC. This first fight is a lighter version of her full moveset.

Step 2 — Reach Scadu Altus

2After Castle Ensis, use the Spiritspring to ascend to Scadu Altus. This is the elevated region of the DLC that houses the Shadow Keep. The Spiritspring is unlocked after the Castle Ensis encounter — you cannot reach Scadu Altus without defeating her first form.

Step 3 — Enter the Shadow Keep Legacy Dungeon

3The Shadow Keep is a full legacy dungeon on Scadu Altus. Navigate through its halls and courtyards, dealing with its garrison of soldiers and knights. The Keep is large — expect exploration, side paths, and multiple Sites of Grace before reaching the boss arena.

Step 4 — Upper Section: Rellana Boss Fight

4Rellana's boss arena is in the upper section of the Shadow Keep. This is her true form — a full boss encounter with both fire and moon phases. Defeat her to earn the Remembrance of the Twin Moon Knight.

Step 5 — Claim Your Reward

5Trade the Remembrance at the Roundtable Hold. Choose the Twin Moon Blades (Light Greatsword, Int+Faith, magic+fire) or Moon and Fire Swords (dual-projectile sorcery). To obtain both rewards, use a Walking Mausoleum to duplicate the Remembrance — this is strongly recommended, as both items are among the best in the DLC for hybrid casters.
💎 Walking Mausoleum Duplication

Use a Walking Mausoleum in the Land of Shadow to duplicate Rellana's Remembrance and claim both rewards in a single playthrough. Both the Twin Moon Blades and the Moon and Fire Swords sorcery are build-defining for Int+Faith characters — it is worth the detour.

3. Hazard Guide — Twin Phase Recognition and Tactics

Fire Phase — Orange Attacks

🔥 Slow but Punishing

Rellana's fire-phase attacks are orange-tinted, slower, and heavily telegraphed. They deal high damage on hit but leave longer recovery windows. Roll through the fire sweeps — not away from them — to position behind her during her recovery. Fire resistance gear helps but is not required; the window to punish is generous if you dodge correctly.

Moon Phase — Blue-Silver Attacks

🌙 Fast and Tracking

Rellana's moon-phase attacks are blue-silver, fast, and feature strong tracking. They cover more distance and can catch you rolling away. Dodge laterally — side rolls, not backward rolls — to break the tracking. The moon slashes come in rapid sequences; do not try to trade or heal mid-combo. Wait for the full sequence to end before any action other than dodging.

Combined Phase — Fire + Moon Combos

⚔️ Dual Element Sequences

Later in the fight, Rellana combines fire and moon attacks in single combos — an orange sweep into a blue-silver tracking slash, or vice versa. These sequences require you to switch dodge timing mid-combo: slow dodge for fire, then fast lateral dodge for moon. The rhythm is the hardest part of the fight to learn. Stay at medium distance to bait the individual-phase attacks rather than the combined combos — medium-distance baiting gives you more telegraph time and reduces the frequency of mixed-phase sequences.

💡 Recommended Strategy

Medium distance is the safest zone. It baits her individual-phase attacks rather than the faster combined combos, giving you clearer telegraphs and safer punish windows. Ranged options (spells, incantations, thrown items) can chip her during phase transitions when melee is too dangerous. If running an Int+Faith build, you are already equipped for this — use ranged spells to create openings, then close distance during her recovery.
