1. Why You Should Collect Every Deathroot

Scattered across Elden Ring are 9 Tibia Mariners — ghostly boat bosses that roam shallow water at night. Each drops a Deathroot. Most players kill one or two in Limgrave, pocket the Deathroot, and never think about them again.

Deathroots are the currency for a hidden questline. At the Bestial Sanctum in northeastern Caelid, the beast clergyman Gurranq hungers for them. Feed him Deathroots, and he rewards you with Bestial Incantations — a full school of Strength/Faith spells that scale with the Clawmark Seal. On the 9th and final Deathroot, he gives you an Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone, one of the rarest upgrade materials in the game.

This questline spans every region of the Lands Between. It is not marked. No NPC tells you where the Mariners are. And at the 4th Deathroot, Gurranq goes hostile — a moment that causes many players to kill him by accident, permanently ending the quest.

2. What Deathroots Are — and the Full Collection Route

How to Reach Gurranq

Gurranq is accessible from the moment you enter Limgrave. Travel to the Third Church of Marika in eastern Limgrave (north of the Mistwood). Behind the church, there is a shallow pond with a hidden teleporter. Step through it — it takes you directly to the Bestial Sanctum in Caelid. Gurranq waits inside. Speak to him to begin the Deathroot questline.

💡 Early Access

You can reach Gurranq at level 1. The teleporter has no prerequisites. The Bestial Sanctum exterior is guarded by a Black Blade Kindred — run past it into the building. It will not follow you inside.

3. Hazard Guide

Gurranq Goes Hostile at the 4th Deathroot

⚠ DO NOT KILL HIM

When you deliver the 4th Deathroot, Gurranq will become hostile and attack you. This is scripted. Do not fight back. He is not bugged, and he is not permanently aggressive. Simply dodge his attacks, sprint away, or exit the Sanctum. After dealing enough damage to him — or waiting long enough — he will calm down and speak to you again. If you kill him, the questline ends permanently for that playthrough with no way to recover it. Run across the room, exit the doors, and wait 30 seconds before re-entering.

Mohgwyn Palace Mariner Requires Access to a Hidden Region

🔒 Locked Behind Quest or Late-Game Portal

The 8th Tibia Mariner is located in Mohgwyn Palace, a hidden underground region not accessible through normal exploration. There are two ways in: (1) Complete White-Faced Varre's questline (obtainable as early as Liurnia — use the Pureblood Knight's Medal he gives you), or (2) Find the hidden teleporter in the Consecrated Snowfield (far west cliff edge, guarded by a Sanguine Noble). Most players will not reach the Snowfield teleporter until the late game — Varre's quest is the faster route.

Tibia Mariners Are Only Aggressive at Night

🌙 Pass Time at a Site of Grace

Six of the nine Tibia Mariners only spawn at night. If you arrive during the day, the boss arena will be empty. Rest at the nearest Site of Grace, select "Pass Time," and choose Nightfall. The Mariner will materialize in the shallow water. The three exceptions are the Mohgwyn Palace, Deeproot Depths, and Altus Plateau Mariners, which are always present.

💡 Optimal Turn-In Route

Collect Deathroots 1-3 (Limgrave + Liurnia), turn them all in at once — no hostility risk. Then collect 4-6 (Altus through Mountaintops) and turn them in together. Be prepared for the hostility at #4, and once he calms, deliver #5 and #6 immediately after. Finally, collect 7-9 (Snowfield, Mohgwyn, Deeproot) for the final push. This minimizes trips to the Sanctum and handles the hostility section in one sitting.
