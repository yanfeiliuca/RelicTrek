Bolt of Gransax — The Only Legendary Armament You Can Miss Forever, and Where to Get It Before You Do

1. Why You Absolutely Cannot Miss This

Leyndell, Royal Capital, has a giant spear embedded in it — the actual bolt that the ancient dragon Gransax hurled at the city during the war against the dragons. Resting on the haft of that massive spear is the Bolt of Gransax, one of the nine Legendary Armaments in Elden Ring.

Here is what makes it unique among all Legendary Armaments: it has a permanent, irreversible deadline. When you agree to burn the Erdtree at the Forge of the Giants — the story event after defeating the Fire Giant — the Royal Capital of Leyndell is destroyed and replaced by the Ashen Capital. The giant spear is gone. The weapon on it is gone. Forever.

No other Legendary Armament works like this. Every other one can be collected at any point before entering New Game Plus. The Bolt of Gransax is the only Legendary Armament with a deadline. If you miss it, your only options are to start over in NG+ or have another player drop it for you — and Elden Ring does not allow trading upgraded weapons above your own highest upgrade level.

This is not a hidden secret area. It is not behind an obscure questline. It is sitting on a giant spear in the middle of the capital, visible from multiple vantage points, and people still miss it because they do not know it will disappear.

2. What the Bolt of Gransax Does

The Bolt of Gransax is a Legendary Spear that scales primarily with Dexterity and secondarily with Strength, and deals Lightning damage. Its unique skill is Ancient Lightning Spear — a massive lightning projectile with exceptional range and high damage output.

Ancient Lightning Spear is what makes this weapon special. It fires a massive bolt of red lightning that travels a long distance and deals very high damage. Against enemies that resist physical damage — golems, heavily armored knights, constructs — the lightning damage pierces through. Against large bosses, the projectile's size ensures you rarely miss. It is one of the strongest ranged options available to melee-oriented Dexterity builds, and it costs FP rather than ammunition.

3. Route — Erdtree Sanctuary to the Bolt (~5-10 Minutes)

Starting Point

1Start at the Erdtree Sanctuary Site of Grace — the main Grace inside Leyndell, Royal Capital, located at the base of the Erdtree's inner sanctum. This is the Grace you unlock after defeating Godfrey, First Elden Lord (the golden shade version).

Descend South

2From the Erdtree Sanctuary Grace, exit south down the stairs. You will pass through the large hall with the Erdtree roots and continue descending through the sanctum's lower levels toward the city exterior.

Continue Southwest Through Leyndell

3Continue moving southwest through the capital streets. The route is mostly straight — follow the descending path through the city. You are heading toward the lower ramparts and the massive structure embedded in the city.

Spot the Giant Spear

4As you approach the southwest side of the capital, you will see an enormous dragon-scale spear embedded into the city structure itself. This is the bolt that Gransax threw at Leyndell. It is unmistakable — a massive, ornate spear shaft angled into the city wall.

Climb Onto the Spear Shaft

5Walk onto the haft of the giant spear. The shaft is wide enough to walk on — treat it as a path. There are enemies on and around it (Leyndell soldiers and pages), but no boss stands between you and the weapon.

Walk Toward the Tip — Collect the Weapon

6Walk along the spear shaft toward its tip. Near the tip, you will find a glowing item on the weapon's surface. Pick it up — this is the Bolt of Gransax.
⏱️ Time Estimate

From the Erdtree Sanctuary Grace to the Bolt of Gransax takes approximately 5-10 minutes. No boss fight. No puzzle. No obscure trigger. It is simply sitting on a giant spear in the capital — the only challenge is knowing it exists and knowing it disappears.

4. Hazard Guide — The Deadline and What Happens If You Miss It

The Point of No Return

⚠ THE DEADLINE — Agreeing to Burn the Erdtree

The exact point of no return is agreeing to burn the Erdtree at the Forge of the Giants — the story event at the Mountaintops of the Giants, immediately after defeating the Fire Giant. Once you agree to this action, Leyndell is destroyed and permanently replaced by the Ashen Capital. The giant spear, and the weapon on it, are gone.

Get It Before the Fire Giant

⚠ Collect BEFORE Entering the Mountaintops

The safest approach: collect the Bolt of Gransax before you even fight the Fire Giant. Leyndell is fully accessible as soon as you defeat the Draconic Tree Sentinel and enter the capital — which happens long before the Mountaintops. You have a large window of gameplay between entering Leyndell and burning the Erdtree. Do not wait. Get the weapon during your first exploration of the capital.

If You Missed It

⚠ NG+ Is Your Only Option

If you have already burned the Erdtree and Leyndell is now the Ashen Capital, the Bolt of Gransax is gone for this playthrough. It cannot be traded between players unless the receiving player already has a weapon at or above its upgrade level. The only reliable way to obtain it after missing the deadline is to start New Game Plus and collect it before burning the Erdtree again. This weapon is also required for the Legendary Armaments achievement — missing it locks you out of that achievement on the current playthrough.

✅ Pre-Burn Checklist

Before you leave Leyndell for the Mountaintops: 1) Collect the Bolt of Gransax from the giant spear. 2) Collect any other Leyndell-exclusive items you want (the capital is rich with unique weapons, ashes of war, and talismans). 3) Verify you have everything. Once the Erdtree burns, there is no return.
