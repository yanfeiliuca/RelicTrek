Sword of Night and Flame — The Locked Room in Caria Manor Most Players Walk Past

1. Why You Need It

Caria Manor has a locked room on its east side. From inside the manor, the door reads as "Does not open from this side." Most players pull on it once, assume they need a key from somewhere deeper in the dungeon, and never come back. The key does not exist — because this door was never meant to be opened from the inside.

The room is accessed from an exterior path that wraps around the outside of the manor's eastern wall. No boss guards it. No key gates it. No NPC questline must be advanced. The path is simply invisible from the manor's interior, and the game never points you toward it.

Inside the room, in a single chest, sits the Sword of Night and Flame — a legendary straight sword that scales with Strength, Intelligence, and Faith simultaneously. Its unique weapon skill, Night-and-Flame Stance, has two follow-up attacks: a light attack fires a Night Comet beam (Intelligence-scaling, sorcery damage, long range), and a heavy attack sweeps a wide Flame AoE (Faith-scaling, fire damage, crowd control). No other weapon in Elden Ring combines sorcery and incantation-style attacks in a single skill.

The stat requirement is steep: 24 Intelligence and 24 Faith, plus 12 Strength and 12 Dexterity. But the payoff is a weapon that can carry a hybrid caster build through the entire game — from Liurnia all the way to the final boss.

2. Optimal Route

Step 1 — Reach Caria Manor

Caria Manor is in northern Liurnia of the Lakes, northwest of the Academy of Raya Lucaria. From the Manor Lower Level site of grace, you are at the starting point.

Step 2 — Cross the Plaza to the East Wall

1From the Manor Lower Level grace, head north through the main courtyard. Ignore the Fingercreepers (spider-hands) — sprint past them. Cross the plaza toward the eastern wall of the manor.
2At the east wall, look for a narrow exterior ledge. This is not a drop-down from a roof — it is at ground level, running along the outside of the wall. Step onto the exterior path.
3Follow the exterior path north along the outer wall. The path is narrow but straight. No enemies patrol this stretch — it is fully safe. Continue until you see a wooden door built into the wall.
4Open the wooden door. It is unlocked. Step inside to find a small room with a single treasure chest.
5Open the chest. Inside: the Sword of Night and Flame.
🗡️ The Weapon

Sword of Night and Flame — Legendary straight sword. Strength E / Dexterity E / Intelligence D / Faith D at base. Upgrades with Somber Smithing Stones. Night-and-Flame Stance skill: light attack fires Night Comet (beam), heavy attack sweeps Flame AoE. Requires 12 Str / 12 Dex / 24 Int / 24 Fai.

3. Hazard Guide

The Exterior Path Is Safe

✅ Minimal Threat

Once you step onto the exterior path along the east wall, there are no enemies — zero. The path is a straight walk to the door. No ambushes. No patrolling guards. No environmental traps.

The Real Danger: Caria Manor Courtyard

⚠ Fingercreepers (Spider-Hands)

The main courtyard of Caria Manor is infested with Fingercreepers — giant spider-hands that burrow underground and ambush from below. They are fast, aggressive, and in some areas they respawn infinitely. Do not fight them. Sprint through the courtyard from the Manor Lower Level grace directly to the east wall. The path from the grace to the exterior ledge takes roughly 15 seconds at a full sprint.

No Boss Required

✅ No Combat Gate

Unlike most legendary weapons in Elden Ring, the Sword of Night and Flame requires zero boss kills. You can acquire it the moment you reach Liurnia — no Radahn, no Rennala, no Loretta. If you can reach Caria Manor and survive a 15-second sprint through the courtyard, the sword is yours.

Low-Level Retrieval Warning

⚠ Liurnia Has a Minimum Level

Reaching Caria Manor requires traversing Liurnia of the Lakes, which is balanced for level 40–60. Low-level characters attempting an early retrieval should stick to the main road, avoid the lobster snipers in the lake, and sprint past the courtyard Fingercreepers without engaging. Torrent is available throughout the overworld route — use him to skip all overworld combat.
