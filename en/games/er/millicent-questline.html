Millicent's Quest — Five Regions, A Daughter of Malenia, and the Choice That Ends It

1. Why This Quest Matters

Millicent is a young woman afflicted with Scarlet Rot — the same rot that consumes Malenia, the Blade of Miquella. Why? Because Millicent is Malenia's daughter, one of several buds born from the Scarlet Aeonia bloomed in Caelid. Her questline spans five regions and ends in one of the most consequential choices in Elden Ring.

At the end of her journey, in the Haligtree, two summon signs appear: one gold, one red. The gold sign summons you to assist Millicent against her sisters. The red sign summons you to invade and kill her.

Choose gold: Millicent lives. You receive the Unalloyed Gold Needle, which can then be used on Malenia's Scarlet Flower after her boss fight to obtain Miquella's Needle — the only item in Elden Ring that can cure the Frenzied Flame ending — plus a Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone.

Choose red: You get a lesser talisman. Millicent dies. The needle is lost forever. There is no second chance. This choice is permanently missable per playthrough.

2. Route

Step 1 — Caelid: Gowry's Shack and the Needle

1Travel to Gowry's Shack in eastern Caelid (south of Sellia, Town of Sorcery). Speak to Gowry. He will ask you to retrieve the Unalloyed Gold Needle from the Swamp of Aeonia.
2Enter the Swamp of Aeonia and defeat Commander O'Neil. Ignore the spectral banished knights he summons — focus entirely on O'Neil. The needle drops upon his defeat.
3Return the needle to Gowry. Rest at the nearby site of grace. Return to Gowry — he has repaired the needle. Take it.

Step 2 — Caelid: Give Millicent the Needle

4Travel to the Church of the Plague (eastern Caelid, above Sellia). Millicent lies collapsed near the grace. Give her the repaired Unalloyed Gold Needle. She uses it to halt the rot's progression.
5Rest at a site of grace, then return. Millicent is now standing and recovered. Exhaust her dialogue. She mentions she will travel.

Step 3 — Altus Plateau: Erdtree-Gazing Hill

6Reach the Altus Plateau (either via the Grand Lift of Dectus or the Ruin-Strewn Precipice). Travel to the Erdtree-Gazing Hill site of grace. Millicent appears nearby on the northern overlook. Talk to her.

Step 4 — Mountaintops of the Giants: Ancient Snow Valley Ruins

7After reaching the Mountaintops of the Giants, find Millicent at the Ancient Snow Valley Ruins site of grace. Exhaust her dialogue again.

Step 5 — Haligtree: Prayer Room

8Reach the Haligtree (via the Consecrated Snowfield and Ordina, Liturgical Town puzzle). Progress through the Haligtree until you reach the Prayer Room site of grace. Millicent is standing just outside on a balcony. Exhaust her dialogue.

Step 6 — The Final Choice (After Defeating Malenia)

9Defeat Malenia, Blade of Miquella in the Haligtree roots arena. Rest at the site of grace that appears in her boss room. Return to the area just before Malenia's arena — two summon signs now appear on the ground.
10CRITICAL: Choose the GOLDEN summon sign (assist Millicent). You will be summoned to help her fight her four invading sisters. Defeat them. Millicent lives.
11After the fight, return to where Millicent was in the Prayer Room balcony. Exhaust her final dialogue. Reload the area — return to her location to find the Unalloyed Gold Needle on the ground. Pick it up.
12Return to Malenia's boss room. Use the Unalloyed Gold Needle on the Scarlet Flower left behind after her defeat. You receive Miquella's Needle + a Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone.
🏆 Rewards

Miquella's Needle (cures Frenzied Flame ending) + Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone ×1 — one of only 8 in the base game.

3. Hazard Guide

⚠ Commander O'Neil — Ignore the Spectrals

Commander O'Neil in the Swamp of Aeonia continuously summons spectral banished knights. Do not fight them — they respawn endlessly. Focus all damage on O'Neil. He is slow and his attacks have clear wind-ups. Scarlet Rot buildup from the swamp water is the bigger threat — bring Preserving Boluses and stay on the small islands of dry land.

⚠ THE FINAL CHOICE — Gold or Red

After defeating Malenia, TWO summon signs appear near her arena. The gold sign lets you assist Millicent — this is the one you want. The red sign lets you invade and kill her — you get Millicent's Prosthesis (a talisman), but you lose the Unalloyed Gold Needle forever. There is no way to get both rewards in one playthrough. Choose the GOLD sign. The Somber ADSS and Miquella's Needle are worth far more than the talisman.

⚠ Do Not Kill Gowry Early

Gowry can be killed at any point after you receive the repaired needle. If you kill him before he repairs the needle, the quest locks permanently. Wait until after you have given the needle to Millicent and exhausted her dialogue at the Church of the Plague before deciding Gowry's fate.

💡 Miquella's Needle — Frenzied Flame Cure

Miquella's Needle has one additional purpose beyond its quest significance. If you have accepted the Frenzied Flame (locking yourself into that ending), you can use Miquella's Needle in the Placidusax boss arena (Crumbling Farum Azula) to purge the flame and restore your ability to choose a different ending. This is the ONLY way to undo the Frenzied Flame ending lock. The needle is consumed on use.
