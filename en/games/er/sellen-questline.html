1. Why Sellen's Quest Matters

Sorceress Sellen was exiled from the Raya Lucaria Academy for forbidden sorcery research. She now teaches in secret from the Waypoint Ruins in Limgrave — a cellar sorcery tutor for Tarnished who find her. Her quest is one of the longest NPC chains in Elden Ring, spanning three regions and ending with a choice that determines whether you receive an Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone or merely a Bell Bearing.

Along the way, the quest gives you Comet Azur — one of the most powerful sorceries in the game — for free. No boss kills it, no shop sells it. You get it by finding Primeval Sorcerer Azur in Hermit Village on Mt. Gelmir, then delivering Sellen's Primal Glintstone to him.

At the very end, you face a choice in Raya Lucaria's Grand Library: a gold summon sign (help Sellen fight Jerren) or a red summon sign (help Jerren kill Sellen). The gold sign rewards an Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone. The red sign gives you Jerren's Bell Bearing and Sellen's armor. There is no way to get both. Pick gold — the ADSS is irreplaceable.

2. Complete Route — Five Steps

Step 1 — Waypoint Ruins: Meet Sellen

1In eastern Limgrave (near Lake Agheel), find the Waypoint Ruins. Defeat the Mad Pumpkin Head boss in the cellar. In the room behind, speak to Sorceress Sellen. She becomes a sorcery merchant. Buy all sorceries you want from her now — she cannot sell them after transforming at quest end.

Step 2 — Witchbane Ruins: Primal Glintstone

2Travel to the Witchbane Ruins in the Weeping Peninsula (south of the Fourth Church of Marika). Enter the underground chamber. You will find Sellen's real body chained to the wall. She tells you her spirit is imprisoned and her Primal Glintstone is elsewhere. She asks you to find it.
3Return to Waypoint Ruins and tell the Sellen projection what you saw. She gives you the Primal Glintstone — a small glowing stone containing her essence. Keep this item; you must now find a new body for her.

Step 3 — Hermit Village, Mt. Gelmir: Comet Azur + Deliver the Stone

4Travel to Hermit Village on Mt. Gelmir in the Altus Plateau (reachable via Seethewater River from the Altus Plateau, winding up the volcano's western face). Near the village's upper edge, find Primeval Sorcerer Azur — a large, crystallized sorcerer sitting motionless. Speak to him and he teaches you Comet Azur for free. This is one of the best spells in the game. Do not miss it.
5After obtaining Comet Azur, interact with Azur again and choose to deliver Sellen's Primal Glintstone. Sellen's essence transfers into Azur's crystallized form. Return to Waypoint Ruins to confirm she has moved.

Step 4 — Raya Lucaria Grand Library

6Travel to the Raya Lucaria Grand Library (the Rennala boss room). You must have defeated Rennala first. The room is now different: Sellen has returned to take over the Academy. Jerren, the witch hunter, is there to stop her.

Step 5 — Make the Choice

7Two summon signs appear on the floor of the Grand Library: a gold summon sign and a red summon sign. Pick the gold sign. This summons you to Sellen's world to help her fight Jerren. Defeat Jerren and Sellen rewards you with an Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone.
⚠️ Wrong Choice Penalty

If you pick the red summon sign, you help Jerren kill Sellen. You receive Jerren's Bell Bearing and Sellen's armor set — but no ADSS. There is no way to obtain the ADSS after making the wrong choice without entering NG+. The Bell Bearing and armor are farmable or replaceable; the ADSS is not.

💎 Quest Yield

Total: 1 Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone + Comet Azur sorcery + access to Sellen's full sorcery inventory. Plus Lusat's armor set and Azur's armor set become available for purchase back at Hermit Village and the Waypoint Ruins body locations.

3. Hazard Guide

Buy Sorceries Before Completing the Quest

⚠️ Pre-Quest Shopping

After Sellen's Primal Glintstone is transferred to Azur's body, she transforms and can no longer sell sorceries. Purchase every sorcery you want from her inventory before delivering the Primal Glintstone to Azur. Her stock includes Glintstone Cometshard, Carian Slicer, Carian Piercer, and several other high-value sorceries that are hard to source elsewhere. If you forget, you must wait until NG+.

Comet Azur Is Free — Do Not Miss It

🌠 Free Legendary Sorcery

Primeval Sorcerer Azur gives you Comet Azur for free when you speak to him in Hermit Village — no boss fight, no purchase, no trade. This sorcery fires a sustained beam that can melt stationary bosses in seconds. You MUST interact with Azur before delivering the Primal Glintstone — if you deliver the stone first, the Comet Azur interaction window may close. Speak to Azur, learn the spell, then deliver the stone.

Watch Jerren's Multi-Hit Combos

⚔️ Jerren Boss Fight

When you pick the gold sign, you are summoned into Sellen's world to fight Jerren. He uses a flamberge with multi-hit combos that can stun-lock you. Keep medium distance and bait his thrust attack — it has a long recovery window. Sellen will cast from range but cannot tank him. Do not let Jerren focus Sellen for too long — if she dies, the fight ends and you cannot retry without reloading the area.

Quest Interconnection — Rogier and Ranni

🔗 Rogier/Ranni Overlap

Sellen's quest indirectly intersects with Rogier's and Ranni's questlines. Rogier mentions Sellen early on and asks you to investigate her. Completing certain steps of Ranni's quest can lock portions of Rogier's dialogue. The safest order: complete Rogier's dialogue about Sellen first, then progress Ranni's quest, then return to Sellen's chain. None of the three quests permanently breaks the others, but dialogue can be lost.
