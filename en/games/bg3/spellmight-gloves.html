Spellmight Gloves — Collect 7 Scattered Clown Body Parts for the Ultimate Caster Gloves

1. Why You Need It

Dribbles the Clown was the star performer at the Circus of the Last Days — until a serial killer dismembered him and scattered his seven body parts across every corner of Baldur's Gate. The quest has no markers. The body parts are hidden in basements, sewer tunnels, locked rooms, and a temple of murder. 99% of players who start this quest never finish it.

But the reward for returning all seven parts to Lucretious at the circus is arguably the strongest caster item in the game: Spellmight Gloves. These gloves let you toggle a passive: +1d8 damage on every spell attack roll, at the cost of -5 to the attack roll. This applies to every individual projectile of multi-hit spells. For a level 10 Magic Missile (8 projectiles), that's 8d8 = 36 bonus damage per cast. For an Eldritch Blast with 3 beams, that's 3d8 extra. The -5 penalty is offset by Advantage (from Blind, Paralysis, etc.) or by spells that auto-hit (Magic Missile).

No other item in BG3 provides this level of per-projectile damage scaling. This single pair of gloves transforms Magic Missile from a utility spell into a boss-deleting tactical nuke.

2. What Spellmight Gloves Do

Slot: Gloves · Rarity: Very Rare · Quest Source: Find Dribbles the Clown, Circus of the Last Days (Rivington, Act 3)

Spellmight (toggleable passive): When casting a spell that requires an Attack Roll, you can take a -5 penalty to the Attack Roll to deal an additional 1d8 damage of the spell's type. This damage applies to every projectile/beam — Magic Missile (up to 8 projectiles), Eldritch Blast (up to 3 beams), Scorching Ray (up to 7 rays), each gets +1d8.

Math: With Magic Missile (auto-hit), the -5 is irrelevant — it's pure +8d8 damage (avg 36). With Eldritch Blast at Advantage, the effective hit penalty is small — net +3d8 damage. With Scorching Ray, a Hexblade dip or Hold Person setup makes each ray auto-crit — net +14d8 damage.

3. Prerequisites

  • Reach Act 3 (Baldur's Gate / Rivington)
  • Visit the Circus of the Last Days in Rivington — find Lucretious near the main stage to begin the quest
  • No combat required for the collection — all 7 parts are ground pickups or container loot
  • Some parts are in areas you'll visit naturally (sewers, Lower City houses, Bhaal Temple)

4. All 7 Body Part Locations

Part 1 — Clown's Severed Hand

1Location: Circus of the Last Days (Rivington). Behind the main stage area, in a hidden alcove accessible through a side passage near where the animal cages are. Landmark: look for a trail of blood leading to a small locked room.

Part 2 — Clown's Severed Foot

2Location: Lower City — in the basement of a house near the Elfsong Tavern. Enter the house (lockpick or key from nearby), go downstairs. The foot is in a chest in the corner of the basement.

Part 3 — Clown's Severed Torso

3Location: Lower City Sewers — in the Undercity Ruins section. Enter from any sewer grate. Navigate to the area near the Murder Tribunal entrance. The torso is on an altar in a side chamber.

Part 4 — Clown's Severed Pelvis

4Location: Lower City — inside a locked house in the Heapside district. Break in through the back window (stealth recommended) or pick the front door lock. The pelvis is on a table in the living area.

Part 5 — Clown's Severed Arm

5Location: Lower City — in a hidden cellar beneath a ruined building northwest of the Counting House. Break through a false wall to reveal the cellar stairs. The arm is on a barrel.

Part 6 — Clown's Severed Leg

6Location: Lower City Sewers — deep in the sewer tunnels near the entrance to the Temple of Bhaal. Follow the sewer path past the Undercity Ruins waypoint. The leg is on a stone slab in an alcove lit by candles.

Part 7 — Clown's Severed Head

7Location: Temple of Bhaal — in Orin's chambers, on a display pedestal in the side room before her main arena. This is the only part that requires progressing the Orin / Murder Tribunal questline to access. It's the last part most players find.

Turn-in

★Return all 7 parts to Lucretious at the Circus of the Last Days. She rewards you with the Spellmight Gloves. Each body part weighs 5 lbs — you'll be carrying 35 lbs of clown parts. Send them to camp between pickups to avoid encumbrance.

5. Hazard Guide

⚠ No Quest Markers — At All

This is the game's ultimate hidden-object quest. Not a single body part location is marked on the map. No NPC gives directions. The quest log says "Find Dribbles" and nothing else. Use this guide as your checklist.

⚠ Parts Are Heavy

Each body part weighs 5 lbs. All 7 together = 35 lbs. That's a significant chunk of your carry weight. Send them to camp after each pickup via the "Send to Camp" context menu option.

⚠ Part 7 Requires Orin Questline

The head is inside the Temple of Bhaal. You must progress the Murder Tribunal → Temple of Bhaal questline to reach it. You can collect parts 1-6 early, but part 7 is gated behind Act 3 main quest progression.
