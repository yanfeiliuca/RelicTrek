1. What Is Here

The Last Light Inn is the Selunite sanctuary in the Act 2 Shadowlands — a lit refuge in a cursed dark landscape. It is one of the most dense concentrations of unique items, companion questline advancement, and NPC interactions in the entire game. Most players use it as a rest point. A thorough visit reveals far more.

2. Key Content

Dammon (Karlach engine upgrade 2): Found in the stable/workshop area. Karlach's Act 2 engine upgrade available only while the Inn stands. Isobel: Critical to Nightsong rescue (see guide). Speak to her but be ready for immediate Marcus ambush. Jaheira: The legendary Harper leader at the entrance. Recruitable companion with her own Act 3 quest arc. Arabella: The tiefling child from Act 1 has survived here. Unique emotional questline. Unique Equipment: Several powerful non-legendary pieces in chests and on NPCs throughout the inn.

3. Complete Route

Step 1: Reach the Inn from Act 2 entry — stay in lit zones or use Moonlantern protection. Step 2: Speak to Jaheira at entrance and pass her skill check. Step 3: Explore every floor: ground floor (common area, vendors, Arabella), upper floor (Isobel's room — hold off until ready), sleeping quarters with lootable containers. Step 4: Find Dammon in the stable area. Upgrade Karlach's engine if she's in party. Step 5: Enter Isobel's room only when fully prepared. Have Karlach, tank characters, and CC ready. Step 6: Explore surrounding Shadowlands: nearby camp with unique items, abandoned wagon with loot, path to Gauntlet of Shar.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Marcus Ambush Timing

The ambush triggers the moment dialogue with Isobel ends. Position party first before speaking to her.

⚠ Inn Destruction

If Isobel dies, the Inn falls into shadow — kills many friendly NPCs and removes all Act 2 trading/resting.

⚠ Jaheira Approval

She respects tactical thinking. Extreme dialogue reduces her approval and affects Act 3 recruitability.

5. The Hub That Deserves a Full Sweep

The Last Light Inn is not a rest stop — it is the densest collection of companion upgrades, quest triggers, and unique loot in Act 2. A systematic sweep before entering Isobel's room means you miss nothing when the chaos starts.
