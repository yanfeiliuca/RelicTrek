Gale Orb Resolution — Three Endings to a Wizard's Fate

1. The Setup

Gale is a wizard who absorbed a Netherese Orb — a fragment of ancient destructive magic — into his chest. It is slowly killing him. Throughout Acts 1-2, he requires magical items to sustain himself (he only needs one or two total and they do not have to be valuable). In Act 3 his questline reaches its conclusion. The orb represents a potential weapon — it can be detonated, theoretically destroying anything nearby including the Netherbrain.

2. Three Ending Paths

Path A — Detonate (Sacrifice): Gale can choose to detonate the orb himself, sacrificing his life to attempt to destroy the Netherbrain. This is a solo resolution and ends Gale's story. Available at the Morphic Pool in late Act 3.

Path B — Restore the Weave: A Netherese artifact can be used to remove and neutralize the orb. This requires finding and using the specific artifact. Gale survives and potentially reaches a peaceful conclusion involving the goddess Mystra. This path requires sufficient approval and following Mystra's guidance.

Path C — Use the Orb as a Weapon: Detonate it at the right moment during the final battle instead. This ending influences the main story conclusion and preserves Gale's life while still weaponizing the orb.

3. Questline Key Requirements

Act 1: Recruit Gale near the Nautiloid crash site. Feed him magical items as needed — he will ask when hungry. Only 1-2 items total are usually needed. He reveals the orb situation after sufficient approval.

Act 2: Gale receives a vision from Mystra (the goddess of magic). She wants him to sacrifice himself using the orb. He can accept or reject this. The decision here influences which Act 3 paths are available.

Act 3: Gale appears near the Morphic Pool area in late Act 3. The resolution choice is presented based on prior decisions and approval rating.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Item Feeding

Gale only needs items from the Uncommon or higher category. Do NOT give him Legendary items — he does not require anything that valuable and the items are permanently consumed.

⚠ Mystra Rejection

If Gale rejects Mystra's Act 2 vision, his questline takes a different tone. Both paths are completable but lead to different endings.

💡 Detonation Is Reversible

The detonation option is reversible if Gale is saved before the actual detonation occurs. Do not panic if the option appears early.

5. A Companion Quest That Shapes the Ending

Few companion quests in BG3 directly influence the main story conclusion. Gale's orb resolution is one of them. Whether he sacrifices himself, restores the Weave, or uses the orb tactically — the choice changes how the final confrontation with the Netherbrain unfolds.
