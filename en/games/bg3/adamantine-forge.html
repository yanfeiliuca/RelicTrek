Adamantine Forge — Kill the Boss With the Hammer, Not Your Sword

1. Why You Need This

Adamantine equipment carries one property nothing else in Act 1 replicates: every attacker who hits an Adamantine-equipped character gains Reeling (disadvantage on next attack roll). This applies to every enemy including bosses. A frontliner in Adamantine Splint Armour creates a consistent accuracy reduction loop across every encounter.

2. Craftable Items

Priority: Adamantine Splint Armour (heavy; attacker Reeling + knocked Prone). Also: Scale Mail (medium), Shield (+2 AC, reflect missiles), Longsword (ignore resistance), Mace (destroy surfaces), Scimitar (finesse). Two Mithral Ore veins = two crafted items.

3. Complete Route

1. Reach Grymforge (southeastern Underdark). 2. Find Mithral Ore (2 veins): west lava shelf near forge + northeast past Duergars. 3. Find Moulds in surrounding rooms. 4. At forge: place Ore in Crucible + Mould in Chamber + pull lever. Grym awakens. 5. Defeat Grym: position character on UPPER WALKWAY with Large Lever. Lure Grym below hammer. Pull lever. 2-3 slams kill him. Alternative: when Grym stands in lava channel, he becomes vulnerable for one turn. 6. Collect crafted item.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Find the Lever

It is on the UPPER walkway, not the forge floor. Move a character upstairs before activating Grym.

⚠ Mould Selection

Cannot change mid-craft. Decide before placing ore. Run twice for two items.

5. Why Most Players Never Craft These

The forge lever is on the upper level where most players never look during combat. Grym's near-immunity to conventional weapons is not explained. Players spend 20 minutes attacking with swords before giving up. The hammer is the intended solution, and the game expects you to figure that out from environmental cues alone.
