Adamantine Forge — Act 1 Crafting System and the Boss You Kill With the Forge Hammer, Not Your Sword

1. Why You Need It

Adamantine equipment carries Reeling — every attacker who hits an Adamantine-equipped character gets disadvantage on their next attack roll. This applies to every enemy including bosses. In Act 1, where enemies lack multi-attack and the action economy is tight, Reeling effectively shuts down a third of incoming damage.

The forge boss, Grym, has near-complete resistance to conventional weapons — slashing, piercing, and bludgeoning are nearly worthless unless the weapon is magical. Most players spend 20 minutes chipping away at Grym with swords while dodging lava damage.

The correct method is built into the arena: the forge has a giant mechanical hammer arm above the central platform, operated by a large lever on the upper walkway. Lure Grym below the hammer zone, pull the lever: 60-100 damage per slam, bypassing all resistances. Two or three slams end the fight.

4. Complete Route — 6 Steps

Step 1 — Reach Grymforge

1Travel to Grymforge in the southeastern Underdark. Three access routes: through the Owlbear Cave, through the Zhentarim hideout, or through Selune Temple. All converge at the Duergar docks. The forge is at the bottom of the structure.

Step 2 — Find Mithral Ore (2 Veins)

2aVein 1: West side of the forge area, on a lava shelf. Jump across the lava channel to reach it.
2bVein 2: Northeast section of Grymforge, past the Duergar encampment. Follow the path near the collapsed tunnel.

Each vein yields one Mithral Ore. Two veins available = two crafted items per playthrough.

Step 3 — Find Moulds

3Explore rooms surrounding the forge: Splint Mould in the NW room, Scale Mail Mould nearby, Shield Mould in NE, weapon moulds scattered in storage areas. Each Mould works exactly once — choose wisely.

Step 4 — Activate the Forge

4At the forge platform: place Mithral Ore in the Crucible (central slot) + Mould in the Mould Chamber (side slot) + pull the Forge Lever on the upper walkway. The platform descends; lava fills the arena; Grym awakens.

Step 5 — Defeat Grym (The Forge Hammer Method)

5Position one character on the UPPER WALKWAY with the Large Lever. With the remaining characters, lure Grym so it stands directly below the giant hammer arm above the central platform. Pull the lever — 60-100 damage per slam, bypassing all resistances. Repeat 2-3 times.

Alternative: When Grym stands in an active lava channel, it becomes vulnerable to all damage types for one turn. Hit it with your hardest spells during this window.

Step 6 — Collect Your Item

6After Grym is defeated, the forge completes the crafting process. Collect your item from the forge output slot. The forge resets and can be used again with a second Mithral Ore + Mould.

5. Hazard Guide

⚠ Find the Right Lever

The forge hammer lever is on the upper walkway, NOT on the forge floor. The floor lever controls lava flow. Mixing them up wastes turns while Grym attacks.

⚠ Mould Selection Is Permanent

You cannot change a Mould mid-craft. If you placed the wrong Mould, you must complete the current craft and run the forge again with a second Mithral Ore.

⚠ Grym Patrol Path

Grym walks a fixed patrol route across the central platform. Instead of trying to force it somewhere, wait for Grym to walk below the hammer naturally, then pull the lever.

💎 Lava Vulnerability Window

When Grym walks through an active lava channel, it gains the Superheated condition — vulnerable to all damage for one full turn. Coordinate your party's hardest-hitting abilities for this window.

6. Bonus: Run the Forge Twice

Two Mithral Ore veins exist in Grymforge, which means two crafted items per playthrough. The optimal combo: Adamantine Splint Armour (first run) + Adamantine Shield (second run). The Splint keeps your frontliner alive; the Shield gives your second melee character the same Reeling protection plus missile reflection. Two characters with Reeling active means better odds of the debuff proccing every round.
