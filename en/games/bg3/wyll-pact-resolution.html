Wyll Pact Resolution — Break the Devil's Deal or Keep Your Power

1. The Setup

Wyll the Blade of Frontiers is a warlock. His patron is Mizora, a devil and servant of Zariel (the Archduke of Avernus). His power comes from the pact. His soul is Mizora's collateral. Throughout Acts 1-3, Wyll navigates this relationship. Act 3 brings the resolution: Mizora demands Wyll renew or break the pact, and his choice — shaped by what he values and what you have helped him discover — determines his final form.

2. Three Possible Resolutions

Resolution A — Save Duke Ravengard + Break the Pact: If Duke Ravengard is rescued from Moonrise Towers in Act 2, Wyll can attempt to break the pact in Act 3. He loses his warlock powers. He becomes a different kind of hero — free but diminished in combat capability.

Resolution B — Continue the Pact: Wyll retains his warlock powers and his soul remains Mizora's collateral. She may grant him a new level of power in exchange for continued service. This preserves his combat effectiveness but at the cost of his freedom.

Resolution C — Zariel Conflict: Depending on specific dialogue and choices, Wyll can challenge Zariel's influence directly through Mizora as intermediary. This path changes Wyll's relationship with his patron significantly and opens unique dialogue options.

3. Questline Key Steps

Act 1: Recruit Wyll at the Druid Grove. He is hunting Karlach (they have prior history). The initial confrontation determines whether both can be in the party simultaneously — do not let Wyll kill Karlach, as Mizora demands. Prevent this confrontation to keep both companions.

Act 2: Help rescue Duke Ravengard from Moonrise Towers (part of the main Act 2 plot). Duke Ravengard's survival is critical — it affects which Act 3 Wyll resolution options are available.

Act 3: Mizora appears with the pact renewal demand. Duke Ravengard's status determines which options are available. The final negotiation shapes Wyll's ending.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Wyll vs. Karlach

If you let Wyll kill Karlach in Act 1 (as Mizora demands), Karlach cannot be recruited and her entire questline is lost. Prevent this confrontation.

⚠ Duke Ravengard Rescue

His rescue in Act 2 is part of main story content but easy to miss if you skip certain Moonrise Towers areas. Explore thoroughly before moving to Act 3.

⚠ Pact Breaking Consequences

Losing warlock powers means Wyll needs to use mundane combat without spell slots if kept in the party. Consider respeccing him at Withers.

5. Freedom or Power — A Defining Choice

Wyll's pact resolution is one of the most personal companion quests in BG3. It's not about a legendary weapon or a hidden boss — it's about whether a hero keeps his soul or his power. The choice you help him make echoes through his ending slides.
