House of Hope — Break Into a Devil Estate, Steal the Orphic Hammer, Walk Out With Four Legendary Items

1. Why You Need It

Raphael the devil has been following your party since Act 1. His home in Avernus contains everything he has acquired — including five items that represent the best equipment density of any single area in Baldur's Gate 3.

Beyond the Orphic Hammer (covered separately), the House of Hope holds:

  • Helldusk Armor: legendary heavy armor with fire immunity and Spell Resistance
  • Hill Giant Gauntlets of Strength: sets Strength to 23 permanently
  • Amulet of Greater Health: sets Constitution to 23 permanently
  • Gauntlets of the Warmaster: on a critical hit, make an additional attack (requires Hope to survive)

Two identical routes exist: sign Raphael's deal at Sharess' Caress and enter, or refuse and break in via Helsik's portal. Either way the House of Hope is accessible, and either way the deal can be destroyed on-site. Clear every room methodically before triggering the alarm — the alarm triggers when you take the Orphic Hammer.

2. Getting In — The Helsik Portal Ritual

Find the Devil Fee shop in the Lower City (northern section). Helsik on the second floor opens a portal in exchange for payment or persuasion (20,000 gold or a powerful artifact).

On the second floor pentagram, place five items at the correct points:

  • Near Helsik desk (NW): Skull
  • NE point: Coin of Mammon
  • Skip one point; next clockwise: Diamond
  • Next: Incense
  • Center: Infernal Marble

All five items are sold by Helsik. The portal opens when placed correctly.

3. Prerequisites

  • Act 3 reached (Lower City accessible)
  • Party level 10+ recommended
  • Access to Devil Fee shop in Lower City
  • Either signed Raphael's contract OR prepared to pay Helsik's portal fee

4. Inside Route — 7 Steps

Step 1 — Speak to Hope

1Speak to Hope at entry — take first dialogue option — gain disguise for free movement throughout the estate.

Step 2 — Get Raphael's Safe Key from Haarlep

2Proceed through dining area to Mystic Force Curtain, interact, turn left. Find Haarlep — get Raphael Safe Key. Do NOT agree to Heart and Soul option = game over.

Step 3 — Open Raphael's Safe

3In the Boudoir: disarm trapped painting on left wall, press hidden button behind it, open safe — get Raphael Notes (contains Archive password needed for the Orphic Hammer).

Step 4 — Loot the Archive (BEFORE the Hammer)

4BEFORE taking the Orphic Hammer: collect Helldusk Armor + both gauntlet-type items from Archive pedestals and nearby cases. The alarm hasn't triggered yet — loot everything.

Step 5 — Take the Orphic Hammer

5Input the password from Raphael Notes at the Orphic Hammer pedestal, deactivate the barrier, take the hammer. ALARM TRIGGERS. Raphael now knows you're here.

Step 6 — Free Hope (Optional)

6Jump from Boudoir balcony to free Hope (defeat 2 Spectators). She joins the Raphael fight as an additional spellcaster and gives Gauntlets of the Warmaster if she survives.

Step 7 — Defeat Raphael

7Raphael: 666 HP + 4 Cambions (96 HP each). Priority: destroy the four Soul Pillars in arena corners — each destroyed pillar weakens Raphael significantly. Collect all rewards after victory.

5. Hazard Guide

⚠ Haarlep Warning

The Heart and Soul option during Haarlep dialogue = immediate game over. Avoid this choice at all costs.

⚠ Alarm Timing

Loot everything in the Archive BEFORE taking the Orphic Hammer. Once the alarm sounds, the estate goes hostile.

⚠ Soul Pillars

Destroy all four Soul Pillars before focusing Raphael. Ignoring them makes the fight unwinnable for most parties. Orphic Hammer's Backbreaker works excellently on pillars.

💎 Raphael Fight Tip

Shadowheart's Divine Intervention refreshes all spell slots mid-fight. Use it when your casters run dry — this fight can be long.
