1. Why You Need This

Raphael the devil has been following your party since Act 1. His home in Avernus contains everything he has acquired. Beyond the Orphic Hammer, the House of Hope holds: Helldusk Armor (fire immunity + Spell Resistance), Hill Giant Gauntlets (STR 23), Amulet of Greater Health (CON 23), and Gauntlets of the Warmaster (crit = extra attack). No other single area has this equipment density.

2. Getting In — Helsik Portal Ritual

Find the Devil Fee shop in Lower City (northern section). Helsik on the second floor opens a portal. On the pentagram, place 5 items (all sold by Helsik): NW near desk → Skull · NE → Coin of Mammon · Skip one clockwise → Diamond · Next → Incense · Center → Infernal Marble. Portal opens when placed correctly.

3. Inside Route

1. Speak to Hope — take first dialogue for disguise. 2. Through dining area to Mystic Force Curtain, turn left. 3. Find Haarlep — get Raphael Safe Key (do NOT agree to Heart and Soul = game over). 4. Boudoir: disarm trapped painting left wall, press hidden button, open safe — get Raphael Notes (Archive password). 5. BEFORE taking Orphic Hammer: collect Helldusk Armor + both gauntlet items from Archive. 6. Input password at Hammer pedestal, take hammer — ALARM TRIGGERS. 7. Optional: free Hope (defeat 2 Spectators) — she gives Warmaster Gauntlets if she survives.

4. Raphael Fight (666 HP)

Enemy: Raphael 666HP + 4 Cambions 96HP each. Priority: Destroy all four Soul Pillars in arena corners — each destroyed pillar weakens Raphael significantly. Shadowheart Divine Intervention refreshes spell slots mid-fight. Orphic Hammer Backbreaker works excellently on pillars. Destroy pillars before focusing Raphael; ignoring them makes the fight unwinnable.

5. Hazard Guide

⚠ Haarlep Warning

Heart and Soul option = immediate game over. Avoid this choice completely.

⚠ Alarm Timing

Loot everything in Archive BEFORE taking the Orphic Hammer. The alarm cannot be undone.

⚠ Contract Destruction

Raphael's contract is found elsewhere in the estate; destroy it to cancel any deal at no penalty.

6. The Best Loot Density in Act 3

The House of Hope rewards more legendary items per hour than any other Act 3 content. The STR23 and CON23 items alone transform any character's stat distribution. Completing the full heist — rather than taking Raphael's deal — earns all five items plus the satisfaction of killing a devil in his own home.
