1. Why You Need This

The Blood of Lathander is available in Act 1 — earlier than the game's normal legendary gear progression. Its passive is the most valuable aspect: once per long rest, when your HP reaches zero, you instead restore 2-12 HP, and allies within 9m also restore 1-9 HP. Most players enter the Creche on Lae'zel's companion quest and leave without ever finding the ceremonial puzzle that unlocks it.

2. Collect 4 Ceremonial Weapons

Ceremonial Longsword: Already placed in puzzle room NW altar. Do not move. Ceremonial Mace: Ground floor, on a Drunk Kobold (pickpocket DC15 or kill). Ceremonial Warhammer: Monastery rooftop, Giant Eagle nest. Defeat eagles or use Speak with Animals. Ceremonial Battleaxe: Upper floor east wing. Jump through broken window, defeat Guardian of Faith with ranged attacks (immune to melee).

3. Dawnmaster Puzzle

Dawnmasters Memorial room, upper floor north. Place each weapon on correct altar (wrong = damage): NW Welkinglory → Longsword (already placed) · SW Vaseid → Battleaxe · NE Seed → Warhammer · SE Stockhold → Mace. All glow when correct. Hidden compartment opens with Dawnmaster Crest inside. Alternative: DC 30 Sleight of Hand on panel near Longsword altar skips weapon gathering.

4. Enter the Creche

Enter Creche Y'llek below monastery. Progress to Inquisitor Chambers. Carrying Dawnmaster Crest makes two statues appear. Rotate both to face each other. Left statue may jam — strike it once with any weapon to unjam. Hidden door opens to trapped passage. Place Crest in altar slot to deactivate traps. Take Blood of Lathander. Without the Crest: four Solar Machines activate, 4-turn countdown to destroy entire monastery.

5. Hazard Guide

⚠ Giant Eagles

Speak with Animals lets you negotiate peacefully for the Warhammer.

⚠ Guardian of Faith

Immune to direct melee; use ranged spells or arrows. It cannot leave the room.

⚠ Jammed Statue

Strike once with any weapon — do not cast spells that destroy objects.

6. The Best Act 1 Safety Net

No other Act 1 item provides a death-prevention mechanic. In Honour Mode, where a single death can end a run, the Blood of Lathander is not optional — it is the most important early-game acquisition available.
