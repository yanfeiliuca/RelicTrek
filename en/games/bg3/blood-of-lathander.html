Blood of Lathander — A Legendary Mace in Act 1, Behind a Puzzle Almost No One Thinks to Solve

1. Why You Need It

The Blood of Lathander is available in Act 1 — earlier than the game's normal legendary gear progression allows. It sits below Rosymorn Monastery in the Githyanki Creche, behind a ceremonial weapon puzzle, a hidden compartment, and a rotating statue mechanism. Nothing in the game tells you it is there.

Its passive is the most valuable aspect: once per long rest, when your HP reaches zero, you instead restore 2-12 HP, and allies within 9 meters also restore 1-9 HP. In Act 1 difficulty spikes, this death-prevention mechanic is worth more than any stat comparison suggests.

Most players enter the Creche on Lae'Zel's companion quest, complete the main objectives, and leave. The ceremonial puzzle is in a separate monastery section that appears decorative. The connection between the puzzle and the legendary weapon below is invisible without external guidance.

3. Prerequisites

  • Act 1 — Mountain Pass accessible
  • Party level 4+ recommended
  • Lae'Zel's Creche questline can run in parallel

4. Complete Route — 5 Steps

Step 1 — Reach Rosymorn Monastery

1Travel through Mountain Pass to Rosymorn Monastery. Heavy jumping and climbing required on exterior.

Step 2 — Collect 4 Ceremonial Weapons

2aCeremonial Longsword: already placed in puzzle room NW altar. Do not move.
2bCeremonial Mace: ground floor, on a Drunk Kobold (pickpocket DC15 or kill).
2cCeremonial Warhammer: monastery rooftop, Giant Eagle nest. Defeat two Giant Eagles or use Speak with Animals to negotiate.
2dCeremonial Battleaxe: upper floor east wing. Door is barricaded inside — find a broken exterior window, jump through, cross broken roof to second window, defeat Guardian of Faith with ranged attacks, take axe.

Step 3 — Solve the Dawnmaster Puzzle

3In the Dawnmasters Memorial room (upper floor north), place each weapon on correct altar (wrong = damage):
AltarDirectionWeapon
WelkingloryNWCeremonial Longsword (already placed)
VaseidSWCeremonial Battleaxe
SeedNECeremonial Warhammer
StockholdSECeremonial Mace

All four glow when correct. Hidden compartment opens with Dawnmaster Crest inside. Alternative: DC 30 Sleight of Hand on a panel near the Longsword altar skips the weapon gathering.

Step 4 — Enter the Creche

4Enter Creche Y'llek below monastery. Progress to Inquisitor Chambers. Carrying Dawnmaster Crest makes two statues appear. Rotate both to face each other. Left statue may jam — strike it once with any weapon (do not destroy) to unjam. Hidden door opens.

Step 5 — Claim the Blood of Lathander

5Navigate trapped passage. Place Dawnmaster Crest in altar slot to deactivate traps. Take Blood of Lathander. WITHOUT the Crest: four Solar Machines activate, 4-turn countdown to destroy entire monastery. Githyanki become hostile.

5. Hazard Guide

⚠ Giant Eagles (Warhammer)

Speak with Animals lets you negotiate peacefully for the Ceremonial Warhammer. No combat needed.

⚠ Guardian of Faith (Battleaxe)

Immune to direct melee; use ranged spells or arrows. It cannot leave the room — exploit this.

⚠ Jammed Statue

Strike the jammed statue once with any weapon. Do NOT cast spells that destroy objects.

💎 No Crest Shortcut

DC 30 Sleight of Hand on the puzzle room panel — Astarion with Expertise can attempt this to skip weapon gathering entirely.

6. Bonus: Titanstring Bow

Also in the Githyanki Creche (same area!) is the Titanstring Bow — adds Strength modifier to arrow damage. Perfect for thrown-weapon builds. Check vendors and loot in the Creche while you're here. Two legendary-quality items in one dungeon run.
