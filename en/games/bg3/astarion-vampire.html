1. The Choice

At the conclusion of Astarion's Act 3 questline, Cazador's ritual is underway. Astarion stands at the center. He can: Complete the Ritual (Vampire Ascension) — become a full Vampire Lord, transforming his combat abilities entirely. He gains: Vampire Bite dealing massive damage, Vampire Charm usable in combat, significantly more powerful physical form, and unique dialogue throughout the rest of the game. HOWEVER: the ritual requires sacrificing the imprisoned vampire spawn present. Morally significant choice. Stop the Ritual (remain a Spawn) — Cazador is defeated without the power being taken. Astarion remains a Vampire Spawn with his existing capabilities. The spawn are freed.

2. Ascension Combat Changes

Ascended Astarion gains: Vampiric Bite — significantly increased damage compared to Spawn version; heals Astarion for a portion of damage dealt. Vampire Charm — charm ability usable in combat. Enhanced stealth and movement abilities. Unique Vampire Lord stat boosts. His entire combat role shifts from a rogue who happens to be a vampire to a genuine supernatural threat.

3. Questline Key Steps

Act 1: Recruit Astarion. He reveals he is a vampire. Maintain his trust by allowing him to feed (he can feed on you or animals) and not betraying his secret unnecessarily. Act 2: Astarion begins having visions. He starts recognizing what the symbol on his back might mean. Follow quest markers for his personal questline. Act 3: Astarion leads the party to Cazador Szarr's palace in the Lower City. Navigate the palace (full dungeon with undead enemies and traps). Reach Cazador in the ritual chamber. The choice presents itself.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Cazador Palace

Multi-level dungeon with Ghouls, Vampire Spawn, and traps. Bring fire-based attacks (undead vulnerability) and plenty of healing.

⚠ Irreversible Choice

The ritual choice cannot be reversed after the decision is made. Both outcomes end the questline; they differ in what Astarion gains.

⚠ Astarion Approval

His questline requires maintaining a positive relationship. He dislikes overtly good and selfless choices. He approves of self-interest and pragmatism.

5. A Companion Transformed

No other companion quest in BG3 offers such a dramatic mechanical transformation. Ascended Astarion is a fundamentally different combatant than Spawn Astarion — the decision is not just narrative, it reshapes how he plays for the rest of the game.
