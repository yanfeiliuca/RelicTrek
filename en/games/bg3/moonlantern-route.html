Moonlantern Route — Survive the Shadow Curse

1. Why You Need This

The Shadowlands in Act 2 are perpetually dark. The shadow curse kills any creature not protected by light. Without a Moonlantern — a magical lantern that projects protective Selunite light — moving through the Shadowlands is a series of health-draining sprints between lit areas, and large zones of the map are functionally inaccessible. Every major Act 2 location (Last Light Inn, Moonrise Towers, Gauntlet of Shar, Reithwin Town) requires crossing shadow-cursed terrain. A Moonlantern is not optional for comfortable Act 2 exploration.

2. Three Acquisition Sources

Source 1 — Kar Hootz the Gnoll: Encountered near the Act 2 entry road. He has a Moonlantern and offers it in exchange for specific help. This is the earliest access point.

Source 2 — Defeated Enemies: Several shadow-cursed enemies and Moonrise Tower guards carry Moonlanterns. These can be looted after combat.

Source 3 — Jaheira and the Harpers: Following Jaheira to a specific location yields a Moonlantern as part of the event. This path requires cooperating with the Harpers at Last Light Inn.

3. The Pixie Option — Almost Always Better

Inside every Moonlantern is a trapped pixie. When you first examine or equip a Moonlantern, you discover this. You have two choices:

FREE THE PIXIE: She gives you a magical blessing that provides shadow protection without needing to carry the lantern. The protection extends to the entire party. She flies off but her blessing persists for all of Act 2. Additionally, the pixie is grateful and provides useful information about the Shadowlands.

KEEP THE PIXIE TRAPPED: The lantern continues to work as a physical item. The pixie remains imprisoned. This means one party member must hold the lantern at all times, limiting equipment options.

✨ Recommendation

Free the pixie. The blessing covers the entire party passively, you keep your off-hand slot free, and there is no downside.

4. Hazard Guide

⚠ Do Not Cross Without Protection

Attempting to cross Act 2 shadow zones without protection at early levels results in severe, consistent damage that will kill your party.

⚠ Reithwin Town Is Dense Shadow

Multiple buildings are in permanent shadow. Plan routes through lit windows and corridors.

⚠ The Lantern Can Break

Do not use Shatter or thunder damage on or near the lantern before freeing the pixie. A broken lantern means no protection.

💡 After Freeing the Pixie

The physical lantern loses its function. Carry a backup lantern or ensure the blessing is active before entering deep shadow zones.

5. The Key to All of Act 2

The Moonlantern is not a weapon or armor piece — it is a zone-access key. Without it, Act 2 shrinks to a handful of safe zones connected by desperate sprints. Free the pixie, gain permanent party-wide protection, and explore the Shadowlands without restriction.
