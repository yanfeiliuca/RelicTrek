Mourning Frost — The Hidden Ice Staff Assembled From 3 Scattered Underdark Pieces

1. Why You Need It

Mourning Frost is a Very Rare staff that does not exist until you assemble it. It is not dropped by any boss. It is not sold by any merchant. It sits across three disconnected corners of the Underdark, on three different NPCs, each requiring a completely different interaction — and if any one of those NPCs despawns before you loot them, the staff is permanently gone for that playthrough.

Once assembled, it becomes the core weapon for any Cold-focused caster: +1 Cold damage on all cold spells, Frostbite inflicted on cold damage (disadvantage on Constitution saves), and the ability to apply Chilled (cold vulnerability — double damage from cold). With the right build — a White Draconic Sorcerer or a Cold-focused Wizard — this staff turns Ray of Frost into a cantrip that applies Frostbite + Chilled simultaneously, setting up doubled cold damage on every subsequent hit.

Most players complete Act 1 without ever knowing this staff exists, because the game never tells you that the three "Icy" junk items in your inventory are actually meant to be combined.

2. What Mourning Frost Does

Weapon type: Quarterstaff (Versatile) · Rarity: Very Rare · Requires: Simple Weapon proficiency

Heart of Ice: Deal an additional +1 Cold damage on all cold-based attacks and spells.

Insidious Cold: Whenever you deal Cold damage, the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or become Frostbitten — disadvantage on all Constitution saving throws.

Ray of Frost (free cantrip): The staff grants the wielder the Ray of Frost cantrip, which synergizes directly with Heart of Ice and Insidious Cold.

Chilled application: When combined with the Wet condition or specific cold synergies, Mourning Frost's Frostbite effect can chain into Chilled — making the target vulnerable to Cold damage (double damage). This is the key that unlocks the Cold-specialist build.

3. Prerequisites

  • Reach the Underdark (Act 1 — via Goblin Camp, Zhentarim Hideout, or Phase Spider Matriarch lair)
  • Must complete Spaw's quest (eliminate the duergar) at the Myconid Colony to access the vault (for the Icy Metal)
  • Must have non-fire, non-thunder damage ready when fighting Dhourn (to avoid destroying his corpse and the Icy Helve)
  • Any character can assemble — right-click any Icy component, select "Combine"

4. Optimal Route

Component 1 — Icy Crystal (Filro the Forgotten)

1Location: Far west Underdark, near the Sussur Tree. Take the Myconid Colony west exit, navigate past the exploding mushrooms and Hook Horrors toward the giant glowing tree. Filro the Forgotten is a drow mage standing near the tree, accompanied by animated armors. He drops the Icy Crystal on death. Note: A Bulette (burrowing monster) patrols the approach — engage Filro after it passes.

Component 2 — Icy Metal (Myconid Colony Vault)

2Location: Myconid Colony — the locked vault behind Sovereign Spaw. Complete Spaw's quest to eliminate the duergar at the Decrepit Village (southwest Underdark shore). After returning, Spaw opens the vault. Inside, loot the Icy Metal from a dead drow body among the treasure piles. Note: If Spaw dies (side with Glut, or the colony is attacked), the vault becomes permanently inaccessible.

Component 3 — Icy Helve (Dhourn the Petrified Drow)

3Location: Northeast Underdark, near the Selunite Outpost. Past the petrified drow statues (spectator fight area), find Dhourn — a named petrified drow. Use a Basilisk Oil or similar petrification cure to unpetrify him. He immediately turns hostile. Kill him to obtain the Icy Helve. Critical: Do NOT knock Dhourn into the chasm. Do NOT use fire or thunder damage that might destroy his corpse. The component is on his body — if the body is destroyed, the staff is permanently lost.

Assembly

4Once you have all three components (Icy Crystal, Icy Metal, Icy Helve), right-click any one of them in your inventory and select "Combine". Drag the other two into the combination window. Craft to create Mourning Frost.

5. Hazard Guide

⚠ Dhourn Corpse Destruction

Dhourn is standing on a ledge near a chasm. Fire spells (Firebolt, Burning Hands) and thunder spells (Thunderwave, Shatter) can knock his body into the abyss or destroy it outright. Kill him with physical damage, cold, or poison. If the body is lost, the Icy Helve is gone forever.

⚠ Myconid Colony Survival

The vault containing Icy Metal is only accessible if Spaw remains in power. Do NOT side with Glut's coup. Do NOT let the colony fall. If Spaw dies before you loot the vault, the Icy Metal is lost.

⚠ Looks Like Junk

The three Icy components appear as generic "white" items in your inventory. They look exactly like vendor trash. Many players sell them to a merchant without ever reading the tooltip. If sold, buy them back immediately — merchants refresh inventory after a long rest, permanently removing sold items.
