1. What the Bell Actually Does

The Fast Travel Bell is not a teleport tool you carry and ring. It is a building component: you spend one Bell plus 20 Wood in Build Mode to place a Fast Travel Point on the coast. Fast travel then works between the points you have built.

  • Two points minimum. One point on its own does nothing on land — you need at least two placed in different locations before point-to-point travel opens up.
  • Ten points maximum. Current guides agree you can have up to 10 active Fast Travel Points at once, so placement is a budget decision.
  • Coast only. Points must be built on a shoreline (near the water, like a dock). You can't put one in the middle of an island.
  • Craftable after the first one. Once you have the recipe, more Bells come from the Workbench, so the first Bell is a starting point, not a one-off relic.
✅ Correction notice (2026-11-14)

An earlier version of this page described a "Pirate Remains" underwater cellar, campfire attunement, and a ring-the-bell teleport menu. None of that matches current published guides, and it has been removed. This page now follows the sources listed at the bottom.

2. Where to Get Your First Bell

Your first Bell is in a Smuggler's Cache close to your initial spawn point on the starting beach. The cache is a hole in the ground disguised under leaves and wood — break the cover to reveal a staircase leading down.

Sources describe the unlock slightly differently. One guide says opening the cache chest unlocks the recipe; another ties the craftable Bell to the early quest "How My Shore Adventure Began" and your first smelted Copper Ingot. Bells can also turn up as loot from small shipwrecks and beach chests on the starter island, and one wiki lists a second pre-placed Bell at an Abandoned Buccaneer Camp. In practice: clear the cache and smelt copper early, and you will have both the Bell and the recipe.

3. Step-by-Step: From First Bell to Working Network

1On the starting beach, look for a patch of leaves and loose wood covering a hole near where you spawned. Break it to expose the stairs.
2Go down with a weapon ready — a Drowned waits inside. It's an early-game fight, not a gear check.
3Open the chest → Fast Travel Bell ×1.
4Smelt your first Copper Ingot (part of the early quest line). Bells are now craftable at a Level 1 Workbench.
5Craft a second Bell: 10 Copper Ingot + 3 Rope. Rope is made at the Workbench from Plant Fiber.
6Open Build Mode (B) → Crafting & Utilities → Utilities → Fast Travel Point (1 Bell + 20 Wood). Place it on the shoreline at your main base.
7Place the second point on the coast of the next spot you'll return to often. With two points up, interact with one and pick the other on the map.

Traveling from Your Ship

While sailing your Ketch you can open the map and fast travel to a point — as long as you're not in combat or being chased. Reports differ on whether this needs one point or two, and one wiki says the whole ship and crew only move when you warp from the helm. In co-op, one player reported traveling alone and having to summon the ship afterwards, so plan around that if friends are aboard.

4. Placement Rules & Free Points

⚠️ Rule 1 — Shoreline only

Multiple guides state Fast Travel Points must be built on a coastline. Scout a flat spot by the water before committing a Bell.

⚠️ Rule 2 — Ten active points

The cap is 10 at a time. One wiki notes that dismantling a point refunds its materials and frees the slot, so early placements can be recycled — 待确认 against your current patch.

⚠️ Rule 3 — Placed by hand

Points are built in the world through Build Mode; you can't place one remotely from the map.

💎 Points You Don't Have to Build

💎 Faction hubs

Some faction hubs — Tortuga and the Buccaneer faction's island are named — have campfire fast travel points that activate once you've visited them for the first time. Visit them early and don't spend your own Bells duplicating them.

Homeward Journey is a separate potion that teleports you to your revival point (your bed or tent). It's a useful bridge before your second Fast Travel Point is up.

6. Tips

  • Copper is the real cost. Every Bell is 10 Copper Ingots. Bank a little copper on each mining trip so a spare Bell is ready when you find a spot worth anchoring.
  • Main base first. It's the point you'll return to from everywhere, including from your ship.
  • Then resource loops. Copper and iron deposits, dungeon entrances and quest hubs you revisit earn a slot; one-off curiosities don't.
  • Carry a kit. A crafted Bell plus 20 Wood in your pack lets you drop a point the moment you land somewhere new.
  • Set a respawn point (bed or tent) before long voyages, and keep a Homeward Journey potion for emergencies.
