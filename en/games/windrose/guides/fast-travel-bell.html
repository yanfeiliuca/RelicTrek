1. Why You Need This

The Fast Travel Bell is the single highest-ROI item in early-game Windrose, and the one that most new players completely miss. Once obtained, it allows you to teleport between any discovered campfires and bases on the map — no cooldown, no resource cost, instant travel.

  • The earlier, the better. This is a compound-interest item. Get it at Lv 5, and it saves you ~50 hours of walking over a full playthrough. Get it at Lv 25, and you've already wasted 20+ hours running between locations.
  • No crafting, no combat, no boss. This is a pure exploration find. No enemy harder than a basic skeleton guards it. The only obstacle is knowing where to look.
  • Enables efficient farming. With fast travel, every other item in this guide series becomes significantly faster to collect. The Major Jaguar Ring route drops from 45 min to ~30 min. The Gunpowder Sulfur farm becomes trivial.
  • Missable. The Pirate Remains area where it's hidden is off the critical path. If you follow only the main quest markers, you will never visit the location where the Bell is hidden. Most players finish the game without ever finding it.

⚠️ Honest note: We are still verifying whether the Fast Travel Bell is a crafted item (assembled from components found in the Pirate Remains) or a found item (picked up directly). The current evidence suggests it's a direct pickup from a hidden location, which means it technically doesn't meet RelicTrek's "synthesis" criterion. However, its value and hidden nature are so strong that we're including it as a special case. This guide will be updated once confirmed.

Bottom line: Stop walking. Get the Bell. Everything else in the game becomes easier.

2. How to Find It

The Fast Travel Bell is hidden in the Pirate Remains, a small unmarked ruin on the eastern edge of the Coastal Jungle. There is no quest marker, no NPC hint, and no map indicator pointing to this location.

🔔 Fast Travel Bell

An ancient bronze bell inscribed with nautical symbols. Ring it at any campfire to attune it — thereafter, you can teleport to that campfire from anywhere on the map.

3. Step-by-Step Route (~15 min)

1Start: Coastal Jungle Outpost (the first campfire you encounter after the tutorial). You can attempt this as early as Lv 3 — the route has minimal combat.
2Head east along the beach. Pass the first pirate camp (do not engage — stay on the beach, below the cliff). Continue east for ~3 minutes until the beach narrows into a rocky cove.
3At the rocky cove, look for a broken ship mast protruding from the sand at a 45° angle. This is the only visual landmark. Turn inland (north) here.
4Follow a narrow, overgrown path through dense jungle for ~1 minute. You'll emerge at the Pirate Remains — a crumbling stone structure (old lighthouse foundation) half-consumed by jungle. A single skeleton (Lv 5, 80 HP) guards the entrance.
5Enter the ruin. Inside, you'll find a stone staircase descending into a flooded cellar. The water is dark — you cannot see the bottom. This is intentional. The Bell is underwater.
6⚠️ The dark passage: Dive into the water. The cellar is about 15m deep. At the bottom, swim through a submerged stone archway into a small chamber. There is no light source here — the screen will be almost completely black. Tip: craft a Torch before coming (Stick ×1 + Cloth ×1 at any campfire) and hold it while swimming — it provides just enough light to navigate.
7In the submerged chamber, you'll find a waterlogged chest on the stone altar at the far end. Open it → Fast Travel Bell acquired.
8Swim back up (watch your oxygen — ~45 seconds total round trip, manageable with base lung capacity). Exit the ruin.
9💎 Bonus stop: Before leaving, check the shelf against the west wall of the cellar (above water). It contains Ancient Map Fragment ×1 — a collectible that reveals one hidden location on your world map.

How to Use the Bell

  1. Equip the Fast Travel Bell in your tool slot.
  2. Visit any campfire or base and interact with it while holding the Bell → the campfire becomes "attuned."
  3. From anywhere on the map, ring the Bell (press the tool-use key, default "T") → a menu of all attuned campfires appears.
  4. Select a destination → instant teleport.
  5. No cooldown. No cost. No limit on uses.

4. Risk & Bonus Loot

⚠️ Risks

⚠️ Risk 1 — The Dark Passage

The underwater portion of the ruin is completely unlit. Without a light source, you will get disoriented and potentially drown. Bring a Torch (Stick + Cloth, crafted at any campfire). The torch stays lit underwater in Windrose (unlike many survival games). Swim time is ~20 seconds each way — base oxygen is 60 seconds, so you have plenty of margin if you don't get lost.

⚠️ Risk 2 — Skeleton Guardian

A single Lv 5 Skeleton (80 HP) guards the ruin entrance. It's weak but can surprise you if you're Lv 3–4. Use a blunt weapon or just kite it — it moves slowly. Not a real threat unless you're severely undergeared.

⚠️ Risk 3 — Getting Lost

The Pirate Remains have no map marker. The broken mast is the only landmark. If you miss it, you'll wander the eastern jungle indefinitely. Landmark checkpoint: if you reach a large waterfall, you've gone too far east — turn back west ~200m.

💎 Bonus Loot

💎 Bonus 1 — Ancient Map Fragment (Pirate Remains Cellar)

On the west wall shelf of the cellar (above water, before diving). This collectible reveals one hidden location on your world map. There are 12 fragments total — collecting all of them reveals the location of a Legendary treasure.

5. No Crafting Required

The Fast Travel Bell is obtained directly from the Pirate Remains chest. No crafting station, no materials, no assembly needed. This makes it the most accessible item in the RelicTrek catalog — but also the easiest to miss, since nothing in the game directs you to it.

Recommendation: Make this your first stop after the tutorial. The 15-minute detour pays for itself within the first hour of use.

6. Tips

  • DO THIS FIRST. Before you farm any other item in this guide series, get the Fast Travel Bell. Every subsequent route assumes you have teleport access. Without it, all route times increase by 50–100%.
  • Attune every campfire you find. There's no limit to how many campfires can be attuned. The more you attune, the more granular your teleport network becomes.
  • Bring a Torch. You'll need it for the dark underwater passage, and you'll use it throughout the game for cave exploration. Craft one before you leave the starting campfire.
  • Don't worry about the "synthesis" question. While the Bell may not fit RelicTrek's strict "multi-part assembly" definition, its hidden nature and massive utility value make it an essential part of any efficient player's toolkit. We include it because it makes all other relic hunts faster.
