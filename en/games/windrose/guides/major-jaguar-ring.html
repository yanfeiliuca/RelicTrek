1. Why You Need This

The Major Jaguar Ring is one of the most impactful accessories you can acquire in mid-game Windrose. It grants +35% Critical Hit Damage — a raw multiplier that scales with every other damage source you have.

Here's why it matters right now at your level:

  • Outscales everything in its tier. At Lv 16–30, no other accessory comes close to the damage ceiling this ring provides. Comparable rings from enemy drops cap at ~10–15%.
  • Scales into late game. Unlike most mid-game gear that gets replaced, +35% crit damage remains valuable even in Tier 3 builds.
  • Window of maximum impact. If you get it now, you'll enjoy ~20+ hours of dominant crit performance through the mid-game. If you wait until late game to backtrack, you'll have already outleveled the content where it shines brightest.

Bottom line: This is not a "nice to have." For any melee crit build, this ring is the single largest damage spike available in the mid-game. The 45-minute collection run is one of the highest ROI investments you can make right now.

2. Component Checklist

You need 3 components to craft the Major Jaguar Ring. None of them are sold by vendors. All must be found or taken by force.

🪨 Ancient Scraps ×2

Rare ore fragments from a forgotten civilization. Veins are visible as glowing blue cracks in stone walls.

📍 Ancient Ruins, Cursed Swamps ⛏️ Mine ancient ore veins ⚠️ Plague Warrior patrols
💧 Tear of Sorrow ×1

A crystallized tear dropped by the Plague Witch. Pulsates with a faint violet glow.

📍 Cursed Swamps — Witch's Lair ⚔️ Defeat the Plague Witch 🔴 Elite enemy — Lv 18+ recommended
🪙 Gold Filigree ×1

Delicate gold wirework used in high-end jewelry. Pirate captains hoard these as currency.

📍 Coastal Jungle — Pirate Camp 📦 Loot the captain's chest ⚠️ 5–8 pirates guarding the camp

3. Optimal Route

This route is designed to minimize backtracking. You'll start at the Coastal Jungle outpost, sweep through the pirate camp, cross into the Cursed Swamps via the ancient bridge, and hit both the ore veins and the Plague Witch in one continuous loop. Total estimated time: 45 minutes (including combat).

Route Plan A — Speed Priority (45 min)

1Start: Coastal Jungle Outpost — restock at the campfire, equip your best stealth gear.
2Head west along the shore → Pirate Camp. Open with a stealth kill on the lookout. Clear 5–8 pirates. Loot the captain's chest for Gold Filigree.
3💎 Bonus stop: Before crossing the ancient bridge, dive into the shipwreck cove (50m offshore). Underwater cave contains a chest with Pirate Medallion — a useful accessory material.
4Cross the Ancient Bridge → enter Cursed Swamps. Follow the stone path markers to avoid the poison bog.
5Stop at the Ancient Ruins (first set of crumbling pillars on your right). Mine 2× Ancient Scraps from the glowing blue veins. ⚠️ Warning: 2–3 Plague Warriors patrol this area. Use stealth or ranged kiting to avoid a direct fight.
6💎 Bonus stop: Behind the largest pillar, jump up the stone ledge. A hidden chest contains Obsidian Shard ×3 — rare crafting material.
7Continue east into the Witch's Lair. The Plague Witch spawns in the center clearing. She summons poison clouds and 2 skeleton adds. Recommended: bring antidotes and a ranged weapon for the adds. Defeat her for Tear of Sorrow.
8Fast travel back to base — head to your Jewelry Workbench to craft.

Route Plan B — Safety Priority (~60 min)

If you're underleveled (below Lv 16) or playing hardcore: skip the Plague Warrior zone entirely by approaching the Ancient Ruins from the south entrance (via the Swamp Shore path). This adds ~15 minutes but avoids the patrol entirely. Bring a bow to pull the Plague Witch's adds one at a time.

4. Danger Zones & Bonus Loot

⚠️ Danger Zones

⚠️ Risk 1 — Plague Warrior Patrols

The Ancient Ruins area has 2–3 Plague Warriors on rotating patrol routes. They hit hard (150+ per swing) and apply a stacking poison debuff. Recommended: stealth past them or use a bow to pull one at a time. Do NOT engage all three at once unless you're Lv 20+ with poison resistance gear.

🔴 Risk 2 — Plague Witch Boss Fight

The Plague Witch is a rare elite with three dangerous mechanics: (1) Poison Cloud — AoE that deals 30 DPS for 10 seconds, covers 60% of the arena; (2) Skeleton Adds — she summons 2 skeletons at 50% HP; (3) Life Drain — at 25% HP she channels a drain that heals her for 15% of damage dealt. Counter: bring 3+ Antidotes, kill adds first, and save your burst damage for the final 25%.

⚠️ Risk 3 — Poison Bog (Cursed Swamps)

Straying off the stone paths in the Cursed Swamps will drop you into poison bog, dealing 25 DPS and slowing movement by 40%. Stick to the marked paths. The route above follows safe ground — do not deviate.

💎 Bonus Loot Along the Route

💎 Bonus 1 — Pirate Medallion

Before crossing the ancient bridge, dive into the shipwreck cove ~50m offshore. An underwater cave (look for the bubble trail) contains a chest with the Pirate Medallion. This accessory material is used in several late-game jewelry recipes and is otherwise only available from a much harder shipwreck in the far north.

💎 Bonus 2 — Obsidian Shard ×3

Behind the largest crumbling pillar in the Ancient Ruins, there's a stone ledge you can jump onto (look for the slightly protruding brick). A hidden chest contains 3× Obsidian Shard, a rare material used in weapon upgrades and the Spiked Scalemail armor set.

5. Crafting Guide

Crafting Station

Jewelry Workbench — built at your base. Requires: 8× Wood Planks, 4× Iron Ingot.

Prerequisites (IMPORTANT)

The Jewelry Workbench recipe is not available at the start. You must unlock it through the following chain:

  1. Complete the quest "Revenge is Best Served Cold" (obtained from the NPC in the starting settlement after reaching Lv 10)
  2. This quest unlocks the Gunpowder technology at the Charcoal Oven
  3. Crafting Gunpowder for the first time unlocks the Jewelry Workbench building recipe
  4. Build the Jewelry Workbench at your base → you can now craft the Major Jaguar Ring

Crafting Recipe

🏆 Major Jaguar Ring
🪨 Ancient Scraps ×2 💧 Tear of Sorrow ×1 🪙 Gold Filigree ×1

Crafting time: 30 seconds. No additional materials needed beyond the 3 components.

6. Build Tips

  • Best paired with: Any weapon with high base crit chance (Rapier of a Thousand Cuts, Dragonbone Blade). The +35% modifier multiplies an already-high crit number.
  • Class synergy: Assassin and Berserker classes benefit most due to their innate crit chance bonuses. At Lv 25+, an Assassin with this ring can reach ~60% effective crit rate.
  • Alternative if you skip: The only comparable mid-game ring is the Hunter's Band (+12% crit, +8% attack speed) from a quest reward. It's usable but mathematically weaker for pure DPS.
