Weapon Ascension — The Hidden Upgrade System Most Players Never Unlock

1. Why You Need This

There are two completely separate upgrade systems in Windrose — and the game barely explains either of them.

The first is the Upgrading Station: a buildable structure separate from all crafting stations that handles level-based upgrades (Level 1 through 8) for weapons, armor, and bags.

The second is Weapon Ascension: a system inside the Weaponsmith Workshop that converts a Rare weapon into an Epic weapon using Tumbaga Ingots, unlocking new passive abilities that the base weapon doesn't have. Epic weapons have fundamentally different combat behavior from their Rare versions.

Most players figure out the Upgrading Station eventually. Almost no one finds Weapon Ascension without a guide — because it requires upgrading the Weaponsmith itself first (which needs an Anvil and Bellows), which requires Mire Metal Ingots, which requires a full swamp farming run. The entire path is invisible unless you know to look for it.

2. Two Systems Explained

System 1 — Upgrading Station (Level Upgrades)

What it does

Increases weapon/armor damage, defense, and power level.

🔧 Built from crafting menu (separate building) ⛏️ Standard ore materials (iron, copper, etc.) 📊 Maximum level: 8

Note: This is distinct from the Weaponsmith Workshop. They are entirely separate buildings.

System 2 — Weapon Ascension (Rare to Epic)

What it does

Converts a Rare weapon to Epic, unlocking its hidden passive ability.

🏭 Weaponsmith Workshop (Ascend tab — only visible after upgrade) 💎 Tumbaga Ingots (varies by weapon) ⚠️ Each weapon can only be ascended once

Warning: Tumbaga Ingots are NOT refunded on disassembly. Commit carefully.

3. Optimal Route

Step 1 — Build the Upgrading Station

In the crafting/building menu, find the Upgrading Station. Place it as a standalone building. Once built, you can upgrade any weapon or armor piece by selecting it and providing the required materials. This system is straightforward.

Step 2 — Unlock Weapon Ascension (The Hard Part)

To see the Ascend tab in the Weaponsmith Workshop, you must first upgrade the Weaponsmith itself. This requires two components:

  • Anvil — crafted at standard crafting stations
  • Bellows — the harder item. The Bellows requires Mire Metal Ingots as a key component

Mire Metal Ingots = Ancient Scraps x2 + Quagmire Powder x1, smelted at the Large Smelting Furnace. Both materials come from the Cursed Swamps.

Step 3 — Farm Tumbaga Ingots

Without Tumbaga Ingots, the Ascend tab exists but you can't do anything with it. Main sources:

  • Ancient Chests inside Cursed Swamps ruins (3–4 per site, one-time only)
  • Stargazer Tower questline: 19 ingots total (the most efficient single source — see our Stargazer Tower guide)
  • POI clearing rewards throughout all biomes

Step 4 — Choose What to Ascend Wisely

Hold onto Tumbaga Ingots until you have a weapon you will keep. Ascending a weapon you will replace in two hours is a waste of the rarest resource in the game. Prioritize:

  • Plague Halberd (unlocks 35% health restore on special attack)
  • Rapier of Devastation (unlocks 40% health restore on mark detonation)
  • Any weapon with a passive you specifically want to activate

Step 5 — Ascend

In the Weaponsmith Workshop, select the Ascend tab. Choose your weapon. Pay the Tumbaga Ingots. The weapon becomes Epic and its hidden passive activates.

💎 Bonus Tip

While farming Tumbaga Ingots in ruins, use Rope (craftable at workbench) to rappel into ruin interiors. Many Ancient Chests are only accessible by descending via rope — players who skip this miss a significant number of ingots.

4. Danger Zones

⚠️ Danger 1 — The Upgrading Station Is NOT the Weaponsmith Workshop

They are different buildings with different functions. Players frequently confuse them and try to find "upgrade" options inside the Weaponsmith, then give up when they can't find it. Build both. Use both.

🔴 Danger 2 — Ascension Is Irreversible and Non-Refundable

Tumbaga Ingots spent on ascension are gone forever. The Disassembly Table refunds base crafting materials but NOT ascension resources. Commit carefully before confirming.

⚠️ Danger 3 — Uncommon Weapons Cannot Be Ascended

Only Rare and higher-quality weapons have an ascension path. If the Ascend tab shows a weapon as ineligible, it is likely Uncommon quality. Check the weapon's rarity before assuming something is broken.
