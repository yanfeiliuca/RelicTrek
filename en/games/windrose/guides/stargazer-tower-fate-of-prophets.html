Stargazer Tower — Solve Three Constellation Puzzles, Earn 19 Tumbaga Ingots

1. Why You Need This

Tumbaga Ingots are the only material that can ascend a weapon from Rare to Epic — permanently changing its power level and unlocking its hidden passive ability. They're the most important rare crafting material in the game for build progression.

The problem is that Tumbaga Ingots are genuinely scarce. Most come from clearing ruins POIs in the Cursed Swamps — three or four per site, at best, from one-time-only Ancient Chests. For players trying to ascend multiple weapons or pieces of ship gear, the supply runs out quickly.

The Stargazer Tower questline — "Fate of the Prophets" — is the single largest one-time source of Tumbaga Ingots in the game: 19 ingots total across three towers and a final boss fight. Most players never complete it because the first tower can be found early but the quest itself only unlocks once you're at the right story progression point, and the second and third towers require pushing deep into the Cursed Swamps against Level 15 Plague enemies.

Every stage of the questline rewards Tumbaga Ingots. Here is the full breakdown:

3. Optimal Route

IMPORTANT NOTE: Because Windrose uses procedural world generation, the exact map coordinates of the three towers vary between playthroughs. There is no fixed "go here" coordinate. The reliable method is thorough exploration of the Cursed Swamps biome using the following landmarks.

1Find the Stargazer Tower. The Stargazer Tower is a named map marker — it appears on your world map once you're close enough. Explore the Cursed Swamps systematically, checking every named POI. The tower is a tall stone structure with an open top. Inside: defeat the Plague Thralls guarding the interior. Open the Ancient Chest to collect 3x Tumbaga Ingots. Important: make sure your bags aren't full when looting the chest — a full inventory can prevent the POI from registering as cleared.
2Solve the stone dial puzzle. On the tower floor, two rotating stone dials control constellation symbols. The solution is carved or illustrated nearby — on the walls, above the doorframe, or on tablets around the base. Look at the reference constellations carefully. Match both dials to the reference. The door or mechanism responds when correct.
3Interact with the Observatory Stele. After solving the puzzle, interact with the Observatory Stele inside the tower. This triggers the "Fate of the Prophets" side quest. Note: you may find the tower early but be unable to interact with the Stele — this is NOT a bug. The questline requires certain main story progression to unlock.
4💎 Bonus: The Stargazer Tower area often has additional Plague enemy patrols outside. These drop Quagmire Powder and Tainted Bile — useful for Ingot Arborum production later. Clear them all.
5Find the Ancient Observatory (Tower 2). The quest log points you southeast of the Stargazer Tower. This observatory uses a different constellation arrangement — the solution from Tower 1 does NOT carry over. Read the reference markings again. Solve, loot, clear.
6Find the Northern Observatory (Tower 3). The quest sends you north. Same process — different puzzle arrangement. Solve, loot, clear.
7Defeat Chick-Chan. After all three towers: the quest log updates, directing you to the "Warlord's Death Site" near the center of the swamp island. Chick-Chan is a massive named Plague Crocodile — enormous health pool but predictable patterns. Target the blisters on its back for bonus damage. Patience wins this fight. Defeating Chick-Chan completes the quest and awards 10 Tumbaga Ingots.
8💎 Bonus: Chick-Chan also drops rare Plague Crocodile materials — including Tainted Bile. The resources from this fight alone partially fund several Ingot Arborum crafts.

4. Danger Zones & Bonus Loot

⚠️ Danger Zones

⚠️ Risk 1 — Level 15 Plague Enemies

The second and third towers are heavily guarded by Level 15 Plague enemies. Don't attempt them under-geared. Your primary weapon should be at least Level 10-15, and bring a large stack of healing potions.

⚠️ Risk 2 — Different Puzzle for Each Tower

Each of the three observatories has a unique constellation arrangement. Looking up a solution online will only give you the solution for the first tower. For the second and third, read the reference markings in the environment — the clue is always physically present.

⚠️ Risk 3 — Quest Lock

If you find the Stargazer Tower early and the Observatory Stele won't interact, you haven't reached the required story progression yet. Complete more main quests, then return.

💎 Bonus Loot

💎 Bonus — Fast Travel Bells

During your farming loops through the Cursed Swamps between towers, place Fast Travel Bells outside the Ancient Debris sites and Frescoed Ruins. The instant teleport dramatically reduces travel time on return trips.

5. How to Spend Your Tumbaga Ingots

19 Tumbaga Ingots is enough to ascend approximately 2-3 weapons or pieces of ship gear from Rare to Epic. Prioritize in this order:

  1. Your primary melee weapon — biggest immediate combat impact
  2. Your primary firearm — if you use a ranged build
  3. Ship cannon or hull gear — for naval combat

Weapons can only be ascended once (Rare → Epic). Choose carefully before committing — Tumbaga Ingots are not refunded on disassembly.

6. Final Tips

  • Do this questline early in your swamp progression. The 19 Tumbaga Ingots will fund your best weapon ascensions right as you start crafting endgame gear — you'll feel the power spike immediately.
  • Bring antidotes and healing potions. The Plague enemies between towers apply poison and disease effects. Don't run out mid-route.
  • Each tower puzzle is solved in-game. There's no need to look up answers. The constellation clues are carved into the walls, doorframes, and tablets around each observatory. Read the environment.
  • Pair with the Enchanting Table. The Tumbaga Ingots from this questline fund ascensions, while the Enchanting Table produces the Ingot Arborum you need to craft the weapons you'll ascend. These two systems are designed to work together.
