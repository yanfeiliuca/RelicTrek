Ingot Arborum — Why You'll Never Have Enough, and How to Fix That

1. Why You Need It

Ingot Arborum is the bottleneck of Windrose's entire endgame. Every top-tier weapon — Plague Halberd, Rapier of Devastation, Arboris Saber, Plague Pistol — requires 5 per craft. Bullet Arborum (the endgame ammunition) consumes more per stack. Upgrading a Plague weapon from Level 11 to maximum Level 15 requires 40 Ingot Arborum in total.

40 Ingot Arborum means:

  • 40 Mire Metal Ingots (= 80 Ancient Scraps + 40 Quagmire Powder)
  • 80 Essence Arborum (= 80 Plague Wood)
  • 40 Tainted Bile (= 40 Plague Crocodile kills minimum)

The Tainted Bile is the real constraint. Plague Crocodiles drop exactly 1 per kill. They're not the most common enemy. And unlike other materials, Tainted Bile doesn't come from any other source.

This guide is specifically about building a sustainable, repeatable farming loop for Ingot Arborum — because most players don't plan for this scale, hit the wall mid-upgrade, and don't know how to break through.

2. Full Material Chain

Every single Ingot Arborum requires three precursor materials, each with its own farming route. Here is the complete chain:

3. Optimal Farming Loop

Set up Fast Travel Bells first. Before optimizing, place Fast Travel Bells at the entrance of: the main Ancient Debris site you've identified in the Cursed Swamps, the Forgotten Ruins (best Quagmire Powder source), and your home base. This cuts transit time dramatically on repeat loops.

The Optimal Single Loop (~90–120 minutes)

1Ancient Debris site — Loot for Ancient Scraps + any Tumbaga Ingots in Ancient Chests (uses Rope to rappel in). Exit.
2Plague Wood zone — Chop Tainted Trees for Plague Wood. Stay until the Wood Plague meter warns you to leave. Kill any Plague Crocodiles encountered for Tainted Bile. Exit zone before full debuff.
3Forgotten Ruins — Clear enemies, loot chests for Quagmire Powder (most efficient single source). Exit.
4Return to base — Smelt: Plague Wood → Essence Arborum. Smelt: Ancient Scraps + Quagmire Powder → Mire Metal Ingots. Craft: Mire Metal Ingot + Essence Arborum + Tainted Bile → Ingot Arborum.
5Repeat.
💎 Efficiency Tip

Plague Crocodiles in the Plague Wood zone can be farmed in the same loop as Tainted Tree chopping. Watch for their distinctive silhouettes near the waterlogged areas within the zone. Kill any you see — each one is one Ingot Arborum closer to your goal.

4. Danger Zones

⚠️ Risk 1 — Tainted Bile Is the Hardest Bottleneck

Everything else in the Ingot Arborum chain can be sped up with better planning. Tainted Bile cannot. Plague Crocodiles are finite encounters per zone per in-game day. Plan accordingly.

⚠️ Risk 2 — Don't Spend Ingots on Bullet Arborum Before Weapons

Bullet Arborum is powerful, but weapon upgrades take priority. Secure your weapon at maximum level before converting Ingots to ammunition.

🔴 Risk 3 — Ingot Arborum Is Not Refundable

The Disassembly Table does not return Ingot Arborum if you break down a weapon. Commit to the weapons you craft.

5. Expected Investment

To craft and max one Plague weapon:

  • Time: ~15–20 hours of focused farming across multiple sessions
  • Plague Crocodile kills: ~40
  • Ancient Debris site clears: ~8–10
  • Forgotten Ruins runs: ~8–10
  • Enchanting Table cycles: 40

This is endgame commitment territory. Plan for it, not against it.
