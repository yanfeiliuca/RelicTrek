1. Why You Need It

Steel Nails are different from every other resource in Windrose. They don't drop from enemies. They don't spawn in chests on land. They don't come from mining or farming. They are looted exclusively from sunken ships on the ocean floor — and they unlock the Combat Repair Kit, an item you use mid-sea-battle to directly repair your ship's hull while under fire.

Most players go through entire playthroughs without finding a single Steel Nail. Not because they're doing anything wrong, but because the game gives no indication that naval salvage is a distinct resource category. The moment you dive to a shipwreck and find Steel Nails for the first time, your crafting Discovery tab lights up with new recipes — and your ship becomes significantly harder to sink.

3. Optimal Route — Dive the Wrecks

This route covers everything from upgrading your diving capability to locating and salvaging sunken ships. Total estimated time: 2–3 hours for a full diving circuit.

Step-by-Step

1Upgrade your diving capability. Windrose limits how long you can stay underwater by default. Before extensive wreck diving: upgrade your breathing talent in the character talent tree (increases dive time), craft or find Diving Bells if available in your version (these extend underwater time), and at minimum, ensure you can survive long enough to reach the bottom of coastal wrecks.
2Identify sunken ship locations. Sunken ships appear in all coastal waters across all three biomes. Look for broken masts or debris visible at the waterline, discolored water patches (the game marks wrecks with subtle visual cues), and treasure notes found on land that occasionally point to underwater stashes near specific wrecks.
3💎 Bonus: While sailing between islands, scan the water surface for wreck indicators. Mark them on your map as custom waypoints. Running a dedicated diving route is far more efficient than stumbling across wrecks randomly.
4Dive and salvage. When you reach a sunken ship on the floor: search the ship's interior — Steel Nails are typically inside the cargo hold or near the ship's frame. Additional loot includes rare strongboxes containing weapon upgrade components, treasure notes leading to hidden island stashes. The Sunken Galleon (a larger, more heavily guarded wreck) rewards higher-tier materials but requires defeating elite enemy guards.
5Craft the Combat Repair Kit. Once you have Steel Nails, the Combat Repair Kit recipe appears automatically. Craft it at the appropriate station. Use it during naval combat to restore hull integrity mid-fight.

4. Danger Zones & Bonus Loot

⚠️ Danger Zones

⚠️ Risk 1 — Drowning

Extended diving without talent upgrades is lethal. Always surface before your oxygen indicator reaches critical. Don't push a single dive too far.

🔴 Risk 2 — Sunken Galleon Elite Guards

The larger wreck sites have elite-tier enemy guards around or inside the hull. Don't mistake a Sunken Galleon dive for a simple resource run — come prepared for combat.

💎 Bonus Loot

💎 Bonus — Treasure Note Chains

Treasure notes found inside underwater wrecks often lead to hidden stashes marked by glowing indicators on nearby islands or reefs. Follow these chains — they frequently reward vanity items, explosive supplies, and rare crafting resources beyond just Steel Nails.
