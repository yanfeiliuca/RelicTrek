Gunpowder — How to Unlock the Firearms Era

1. Why You Need This

Gunpowder is the single most important technology unlock in mid-game Windrose. Until you craft it, you are limited to melee weapons, bows, and thrown items. The moment you produce your first batch of Gunpowder, an entire new combat paradigm opens: firearms.

Here's what Gunpowder unlocks:

  • All firearm recipes — Flintlock Pistol, Musket, Blunderbuss, and later the Repeater Rifle. These weapons deal ranged damage that ignores a portion of enemy armor.
  • Explosive ammunition — Grenades, Bomb Barrels, and Siege Charges. Essential for breaking fortified doors and dealing with grouped enemies.
  • The Jewelry Workbench — Gunpowder crafting is a prerequisite for unlocking the Jewelry Workbench recipe, which gates all high-tier accessories including the Major Jaguar Ring.
  • Ship cannons — Required for late-game naval combat and accessing offshore islands.

Bottom line: Without Gunpowder, you are playing half the game. The 30-minute investment to unlock it pays off for the remaining 30+ hours of your playthrough. This is not optional — it's the gateway to the mid-game.

2. Materials Needed

🟡 Sulfur

Bright yellow mineral deposits found on rocky outcrops. Emits a faint rotten-egg odor when approached.

📍 Foothills — Sulfur Fields ⛏️ Mine from yellow crystal nodes ⚠️ Area unlocked via boss fight
⚫ Ash

Produced by burning wood or organic material in any campfire or furnace. The most basic processed material in the game.

📍 Your Base (any campfire) 🔥 Burn Wood ×3 → 1 Ash 🟢 No risk

3. Optimal Route — From Zero to Gunpowder

This is a two-phase process: first you must unlock the Foothills region (one-time), then you can farm Sulfur freely. Total time for the unlock chain: ~60–90 minutes for the first batch. Subsequent farming: ~15 min per run.

Phase 1: Unlock the Foothills (One-Time)

1Reach Lv 12+. Thomas Richards is a Lv 15 boss. You can attempt at Lv 12 with good gear, but Lv 15+ is safer.
2Travel to the Starting Settlement and speak to Captain Mora (the quest-giver near the central campfire). She gives the quest "Revenge is Best Served Cold" at Lv 10+.
3Follow the quest markers through Coastal Jungle → track Thomas Richards to the Foothills Gate (a fortified camp at the northern edge of the jungle).
4Boss Fight: Thomas Richards. He wields a heavy axe (slow but devastating — 200+ damage per swing) and summons 2 pirate reinforcements at 60% HP. Strategy: kite him around the camp's central tent, focus the adds first, then burst him down with ranged attacks. He is vulnerable to bleed (Rapier of a Thousand Cuts helps tremendously here).
5Defeat Thomas Richards → Foothills Gate opens. The Foothills region is now accessible. The Charcoal Oven recipe also unlocks at your base.

Phase 2: Farm Sulfur & Craft Gunpowder (Repeatable)

6Enter the Foothills through the now-open gate. Follow the main path north for ~2 minutes.
7Turn east at the abandoned mining cart → enter the Sulfur Fields. This area has 8–10 Sulfur nodes. Each node yields 2–4 Sulfur. One sweep = ~20–30 Sulfur.
8💎 Bonus stop: At the far eastern edge of the Sulfur Fields, behind a breakable rock wall (use a pickaxe), there's a chest with Iron Ingot ×5 — useful for building the Charcoal Oven and early firearm crafting.
9Fast travel back to base. Build the Charcoal Oven (8× Stone, 4× Iron Ingot, 2× Clay). Burn Wood to produce Ash.
10At the Charcoal Oven: combine Sulfur + Ash → Gunpowder. Crafting time: 10 seconds per batch.

4. Danger Zones & Bonus Loot

⚠️ Danger Zones

🔴 Risk 1 — Thomas Richards Boss Fight

HP: ~3,500. Attack pattern: Heavy axe swing (frontal cone, 200+ dmg) → Ground slam (AoE, 120 dmg + knockback) → Summon adds (at 60% HP). Strategy: Stay behind him at all times. Use a fast weapon (rapier/dagger) to maximize DPS windows between his swings. Kill the 2 pirate adds immediately when they spawn. Bring 5+ healing items. If you have the Rapier of a Thousand Cuts, the bleed stacking makes this fight significantly easier.

⚠️ Risk 2 — Foothills Bandit Patrols

The Foothills have roaming bandit patrols (2–3 per group). They aggro from medium range and use crossbows. Not individually dangerous, but a patrol + a sulfur node surrounded by wildlife can create an uncontrolled fight. Clear patrols near the Sulfur Fields before you start mining.

💎 Bonus Loot Along the Route

💎 Bonus 1 — Iron Ingot ×5 (Hidden Chest)

At the far eastern edge of the Sulfur Fields, a breakable rock wall hides a chest with 5× Iron Ingot. Use a pickaxe on the cracked wall section. These ingots cover most of the Charcoal Oven construction cost.

💎 Bonus 2 — Bandit Captain's Key (Thomas Richards Camp)

After defeating Thomas Richards, search the back tent of his camp. A lockbox (requires the Bandit Captain's Key, which drops from Thomas) contains Gold Ore ×5 and the Flintlock Pistol Blueprint — your first firearm recipe, unlocked immediately.

5. Crafting Guide

Crafting Station

Charcoal Oven — built at your base (unlocked after defeating Thomas Richards). Cost: 8× Stone, 4× Iron Ingot, 2× Clay.

Recipe

💣 Gunpowder
🟡 Sulfur ×2⚫ Ash ×1

Crafting time: 10 seconds per batch. Each craft produces 1 Gunpowder.

What Gunpowder Unlocks (Full Tech Tree)

  • Immediately: Flintlock Pistol recipe, Musket recipe, Bomb Barrel recipe
  • After crafting first firearm: Blunderbuss recipe, Grenade recipe
  • After crafting 10 Gunpowder: Jewelry Workbench recipe (→ Major Jaguar Ring crafting)
  • Late game: Repeater Rifle, Siege Charge, Ship Cannon

6. Tips & Strategy

  • Farm in bulk. One Sulfur Fields sweep yields ~25 Sulfur. Burn a stack of Wood (50+) at your base to produce enough Ash. Craft 10–15 Gunpowder in one session so you don't need to come back for a while.
  • Flintlock Pistol is the best first firearm. It's cheap (2 Gunpowder + 3 Iron Ingot), has solid range, and the blueprint drops right after the Thomas Richards fight. Craft it immediately.
  • Don't skip the Jewelry Workbench. The 10-Gunpowder threshold for unlocking the Jewelry Workbench is easy to hit during your first farming session. Craft 10 Gunpowder → build the Jewelry Workbench → you can now also craft the Major Jaguar Ring.
  • Coal is not Sulfur. New players sometimes confuse the two. Coal nodes are black and found near campsites. Sulfur nodes are bright yellow and only in the Foothills. Coal cannot substitute for Sulfur.
