1. What This Guide Can Confirm

The Plague Halberd and Rapier of Devastation appear in Windrose weapon discussions, but the public sources checked for this guide do not currently verify their exact blueprint locations, stats, rarity passives, crafting quantities, or tier rankings.

What can be independently confirmed is the surrounding Cursed Swamps resource context. The Cursed Swamps are the final region in the current Early Access build, and public discovery documentation identifies Ancient Scraps, Plague Wood, Essence Arborum, and Tainted Bile as resources connected to late-game crafting.

Evidence boundary

Earlier versions of this page presented exact healing percentages, attack values, mark counts, blueprint routes, farming estimates, and S-tier rankings without reproducible evidence. Those claims have been removed.

2. Verified Cursed Swamps Resources

⛏️ Ancient Scraps

Public discovery documentation places Ancient Scraps around Ancient Ruins landmarks in the Cursed Swamps.

🪵 Plague Wood

Harvested from Tainted Trees and also reported as a Plague Crocodile drop. It is used to make Essence Arborum.

✨ Essence Arborum

Crafted at the Alchemy Table and used in recipes including Ingot Arborum. The exact quantity formula still needs current in-game proof.

🩸 Tainted Bile

Obtained from Plague Crocodiles and used for Ingot Arborum. A fixed drop quantity or exclusive-source claim has not been verified.

3. Safe Verification Route

Until a reproducible blueprint source is available, use the game interface as the primary evidence rather than following a fixed farming route from this page.

  1. Record the game version and Early Access patch shown in your client.
  2. Capture the weapon panel, rarity passive, and blueprint or recipe source text.
  3. Capture the crafting station and every displayed ingredient quantity.
  4. Capture upgrade costs separately from the base crafting recipe.
  5. Only treat a drop location as confirmed after it can be repeated or cross-checked against another direct source.
Useful exploration

Cursed Swamps exploration can still build a stock of independently documented materials: mine around Ancient Ruins, harvest Tainted Trees, and collect Plague Crocodile drops. Do not assume those activities prove a specific weapon blueprint route.

4. Claims That Remain Unconfirmed

Blueprint acquisition

The previously listed Old Skeleton, chest, Tainted Forest, crypt, and flowerbed routes have not been independently verified.

Weapon mechanics

Base attack, scaling, special-attack behavior, mark or charge counts, healing percentages, and Epic rarity passives require current item-panel evidence.

Costs and rankings

Ingot Arborum quantities, upgrade totals, farming-session estimates, best-build recommendations, and S/S+ rankings are not confirmed.
