1. Why You Need This

Two weapons dominate the Windrose endgame meta: the Plague Halberd and the Rapier of Devastation. Both are rated S-tier. Both require Ingot Arborum to craft. Both are hidden in the Cursed Swamps in ways that most players miss entirely.

The Plague Halberd is a Strength weapon with serious reach and a stacking special attack. Ascended to Epic, every special activation restores 35% of your maximum health — making it arguably the most self-sustaining weapon in the game.

The Rapier of Devastation is a Precision weapon. Its plague mark system rewards patient, rhythmic combat: stack 8 marks with light attacks, then detonate with a heavy attack. Get above 5 marks on detonation and you recover 40% of your maximum health on top of the burst damage. Rated S+ by the community.

Neither weapon announces itself. Both require the Enchanting Table to craft. Both are locked behind exploration in one of the most hostile biomes in the game.

3. Optimal Route

PREREQUISITE: Complete the Enchanting Table setup first. Without Ingot Arborum, you can find the blueprints but can't craft either weapon. Build the Enchanting Table before farming these locations.

Farming the Plague Halberd Blueprint

Target: Tainted Forest sub-area of the Cursed Swamps. This zone inflicts Wood Plague buildup on entry — prepare accordingly (potions, breaks to clear buildup).

Specific locations to search:

  • Ancient Ruins POI with Flowerbed — find and open Old Skeleton Chest
  • Tainted Forest skeletal remains — search all pirate skeletons scattered around ruins, crypts, and flowerbeds

The blueprint drops randomly from these sources. Plan for multiple farming sessions. Once you loot the blueprint, the Plague Halberd appears at your Weaponsmith Workshop.

💎 Bonus

While farming the Tainted Forest, you'll also accumulate Plague Wood for Essence Arborum and encounter Plague Crocodiles for Tainted Bile — the exact materials you need for more Ingot Arborum. This is an efficient loop.

Farming the Rapier of Devastation Blueprint

Target: Old Skeleton enemies and Old Skeleton Chests in the Cursed Swamps. Three priority locations:

  1. Cursed Swamps Crypts — clear entire crypt, open all Old Skeleton Chests inside
  2. Tainted Ruins — search thoroughly for Old Skeleton Chests
  3. Ruins with Flowerbed — locate Old Skeleton Chest
💎 Bonus

Crypts in the Cursed Swamps often contain multiple chests — not just Old Skeleton Chests. Check every container. Additional loot includes Tumbaga Ingots (if you find an Ancient Chest) and regular swamp crafting materials.

Crafting Both Weapons

Once you have both blueprints:

  1. Build enough Ingot Arborum at your Enchanting Table (5 per weapon = 10 total minimum)
  2. Craft Plague Halberd at the Weaponsmith Workshop
  3. Craft Rapier of Devastation at the Weaponsmith Workshop
  4. If you have Tumbaga Ingots, ascend both to Epic immediately for the health restore passives

4. Danger Zones & Bonus Tips

⚠️ Danger Zones

⚠️ Risk 1 — Wood Plague Accumulation

The Tainted Forest zone fills your Wood Plague meter while you're inside. At maximum, you receive a damaging debuff. Take breaks outside the zone to let it clear naturally. Do not over-farm in a single session without returning to a safe zone.

🔴 Risk 2 — Blueprint Farming Is RNG-Based

Both blueprints have random drop rates from specific enemy types and chests. Expect 3–8 farming sessions before getting both. Don't give up — they're in the right locations, just not guaranteed on every run.

⚠️ Risk 3 — Ingot Arborum Cost Is Expensive

5 Ingot Arborum per weapon means you need at minimum 10 to craft both. Each Ingot Arborum requires its own farming loop (Mire Metal Ingots + Essence Arborum + Tainted Bile). Plan your Enchanting Table runs in advance.

💎 Bonus Synergy

💎 Bonus — Halberd + Arboris Saber Combo

The Plague Halberd's Epic upgrade pairs extremely well with the Arboris Saber as a secondary. The Saber provides a faster attack loop for building up adrenaline, while the Halberd's special sustains your health during extended fights.
