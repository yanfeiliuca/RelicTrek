Quick Answer

How to Get It

To get the Two Dragons Sword, defeat the Puppet-Devouring Green Monster of the Swamp, keep the Puppet-Devouring Green Hunter's Ergo, then trade that Rare Ergo with Alidoro or Hugo. It is not found in an overworld chest.

The Two Dragons Sword is one of the strongest high-skill Technique weapons in Lies of P. Its value comes from fast slash/stab moves, mobility, Wind of Swords, and a unique charged heavy timing that can function like a perfect-guard counter when mastered.

Step-by-Step Trade Route

1Unlock Alidoro: meet him in St. Frangelico Cathedral Library and keep access to his Rare Ergo trade menu.
2Defeat the Green Monster: clear the Barren Swamp boss fight to receive Puppet-Devouring Green Hunter's Ergo.
3Hold the Ergo: do not consume it and do not rush the alternative amulet purchase.
4Trade for the sword: open Alidoro's or Hugo's boss weapon menu and choose Two Dragons Sword.
5Upgrade carefully: test the moveset before spending rare special-weapon upgrade materials, because its charged heavy timing rewards practice.

Build Notes

Why Technique Players Want It

The sword starts with strong Technique scaling and a fast, mobile moveset. If you like precise positioning, aggressive dodges, and parry-style timing, it can carry a Technique run through late-game bosses.

  • Best for players who can practice charged heavy timing.
  • Works well when stamina and mobility are already comfortable.
  • Technique crank support makes it more focused for dex-style builds.

Why Some Players Skip It

The weapon's strongest feature is not passive damage. It asks you to learn timing, spacing, and the charged heavy counter window. If you want simple heavy stagger damage, another boss weapon may feel easier.

  • Not disassemblable, so no blade/handle experimentation.
  • Requires the Green Monster Rare Ergo.
  • Ghost Walk Amulet competes for the same one-cycle trade.
Charged Heavy Note

The charged heavy has a distinctive timing window. When used correctly, it can counter enemy attacks as the sword flashes, including Fury attacks. That is the reason many players treat Two Dragons Sword as a skill-check weapon rather than just a stat stick.

Common Mistakes

  • Searching for a chest location: Two Dragons Sword is a boss Ergo trade, not a ground pickup.
  • Consuming Puppet-Devouring Green Hunter's Ergo: this removes the trade option for the current cycle.
  • Buying Ghost Walk Amulet first: the amulet may be useful, but it blocks the sword until NG+.
  • Ignoring the charged heavy: the weapon feels ordinary if you never practice its special timing.
  • Expecting Advance scaling: its standard profile is physical and Technique-focused, with no base Advance scaling.

FAQ

Can you get Two Dragons Sword after consuming the boss Ergo?

No, not in the same cycle. If you consumed Puppet-Devouring Green Hunter's Ergo, you need to defeat the Green Monster again in NG+ and keep the Rare Ergo.

Is Two Dragons Sword a boss weapon?

Yes. It is a special boss weapon acquired by trading Puppet-Devouring Green Hunter's Ergo with Alidoro or Hugo.

Is Two Dragons Sword good for Technique builds?

Yes. Its base scaling favors Technique, and a Technique-focused setup makes the most sense for players who like fast physical weapons and precise timing.

What is the alternative reward?

The same Rare Ergo can be traded for Ghost Walk Amulet instead. You cannot get both from one Green Monster kill.

Can Two Dragons Sword be disassembled?

No. Like other special boss weapons, it cannot be disassembled into separate blade and handle parts.
