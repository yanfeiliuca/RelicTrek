1. Quick Answer

Best Completion Route

Start Julian the Gentleman's quest at Rosa Isabelle Street Culvert, find Melody's Wedding Ring, return to Julian, and choose the comforting lie: "I saw the message she left, saying she loves you." This gives the Sad gesture, keeps the Wedding Ring route available, and supports the later Polendina conversation.

This page keeps the old "Julian Juliet" URL for continuity, but the checked game sources identify Julian's wife as Melody, not Juliet.

2. Step-by-Step Route

1Find Julian: travel to Rosa Isabelle Street Culvert and speak with Julian near the gate by the Stargazer route.
2Accept the request: he asks you to find belongings from his wife, Melody.
3Reach Melody: progress back toward Rosa Isabelle Street, cross the bridge route, and find the maid puppet body near the staircase area.
4Pick up the Wedding Ring: interact with Melody's body, then return to Julian.
5Choose your answer: tell the comforting lie for Sad gesture and the Wedding Ring, or tell the truth and route the Bloody Wedding Ring later.
6After King of Puppets: check Polendina's follow-up in Hotel Krat if you kept or recovered ring evidence.

Why The Lie Matters

The quest is one of the cleaner examples of Lies of P's Humanity framing: a compassionate lie can produce a better emotional outcome and connect to Polendina's later question about human-puppet love.

4. Polendina Follow-Up

After defeating King of Puppets, Polendina can ask whether a human and a puppet can love each other. If you have the Wedding Ring route available, use it as evidence in that conversation. Sources checked tie this follow-up to Humanity and Gemini's Iron Protection.

Do This Before Rushing On

Hotel Krat dialogue changes after major story beats. If you are building Humanity for Golden Lie or endings, check Polendina as soon as his follow-up opens.

6. Common Mistakes

Calling Melody Juliet

The old page slug stays for SEO continuity, but the game-source name is Melody.

Choosing truth on a Humanity route

If your route is Golden Lie or Rise of P, the comforting lie is the safer choice.

Forgetting Polendina

The quest does not fully pay off until the Hotel Krat follow-up opens after King of Puppets.

8. FAQ

Where is Julian the Gentleman?

He is found by the Rosa Isabelle Street Culvert route, near the gate by the Stargazer path.

Is Julian's wife named Juliet?

No. The checked sources name her Melody. "Julian Juliet" is retained only as the old URL/search shorthand.

Which answer should I choose for Julian?

For a Humanity, gesture, or Golden Lie route, choose the comforting lie that says Melody left a message saying she loves him.

What happens if I tell Julian the truth?

You miss the comforting-lie reward route. After King of Puppets, sources say Julian can be found dead and you can collect the Bloody Wedding Ring.
