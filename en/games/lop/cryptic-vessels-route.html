Quick Answer

How Cryptic Vessels Work

Bring each Cryptic Vessel to Venigni at Hotel Krat, choose the decrypt option, read the decryption item, then follow the photo or written clue to claim the reward. The six vessels are Crafted, Jeweled, Old, Rusty, Alidoro's, and Mechanical.

The main trap is the Mechanical Cryptic Vessel: its reward requires showing the Frozen Feast special weapon. If you spent Reborn Champion's Ergo on the Arm of God Amulet instead, that solution waits until NG+.

Clean Route Order

1Unlock Venigni: rescue Venigni and return him to Hotel Krat so vessels can be decoded.
2Decode immediately: do not carry encrypted vessels around and forget which clue belongs to which puzzle.
3Clear Crafted and Jeweled early: both send you back to early-game streets and reward useful materials.
4Buy Rusty after full shop expansion: the Rusty Vessel depends on Polendina's fully expanded supply-box inventory.
5Save Reborn Champion's Ergo for Frozen Feast: this keeps the Mechanical Vessel solution available in the same cycle.
6Handle Alidoro deliberately: this vessel is tied to a late-game kill choice and Eugenie's quest resolution.

High-Risk Vessels

Mechanical Vessel

Do not buy Arm of God Amulet first if you want this Quartz and outfit on the same playthrough. You need Frozen Feast, which uses Champion Victor's Reborn Champion's Ergo.

Alidoro's Vessel

This is tied to killing Alidoro after the Relic of Trismegistus events. It affects Eugenie's information route, so decide whether you care more about quest truth, record routing, or keeping choices simple.

Completion Warning

Most vessels can be solved late, but the Mechanical and Alidoro routes depend on one-cycle choices. Put them on your pre-NG+ cleanup list.

Common Mistakes

  • Skipping Venigni decryption: the reward locations usually do not work until the vessel has been decoded.
  • Not buying the Rusty Vessel: Polendina only sells it after the correct shop expansion.
  • Forgetting Check Ground: the Jeweled Vessel requires a gesture, not an interact prompt.
  • Opening the Old Vessel shack carelessly: the floor trap can drop you into an ambush before you loot the chest.
  • Buying the amulet instead of Frozen Feast: this blocks the Mechanical Vessel solution until NG+.
  • Starting NG+ too fast: finish vessel decryption, rewards, and Eugenie's follow-up before moving on.

FAQ

How many Cryptic Vessels are there?

There are six base-game Cryptic Vessels: Crafted, Jeweled, Old, Rusty, Alidoro's, and Mechanical.

Do you need Cryptic Vessels for trophies?

They are tied to the Veteran Explorer completion path, and Alidoro's vessel also intersects with Eugenie's story route. If you are trophy hunting, decode and resolve every vessel before NG+.

Are Cryptic Vessels missable?

Several are recoverable late, but Mechanical can be blocked by choosing the wrong Champion Victor trade, and Alidoro's vessel depends on a late-game choice. Treat both as high-risk.

Can you solve a vessel before Venigni decrypts it?

Usually no. Take the vessel to Venigni first, receive the decryption item, then follow the clue.

Which vessel gives Quartz?

Crafted, Rusty, and Mechanical vessels are the key Quartz-related routes. Track them alongside the all-Quartz checklist.
