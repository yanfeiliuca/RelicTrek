1. Quick Answer

Counting Rule

The base game has eight Legion Arms if you count the starting Left Arm of Steel. It has seven non-default arms: Puppet String, Fulminis, Flamberge, Aegis, Deus Ex Machina, Pandemonium, and Falcon Eyes. Four of those are crafted with Legion Plugs.

For most first runs, upgrade Puppet String for exploration and chase-down utility, then choose Aegis for defensive boss comfort or an elemental arm for your build: Fulminis for Electric Blitz, Flamberge for fire, and Pandemonium for acid.

Source Note

I am not treating the old "all 7" wording as the total count. It is only correct if it means "seven non-default arms." Overture DLC adds separate Legion Arm content, but this page routes the base-game arms only.

3. Unlock Route

1Start: Left Arm of Steel is equipped by default. Do not invest planning time here because it has no upgrade route in the base game sources checked.
2Hotel Krat: meet Eugenie and receive Puppet String.
3After Scrapped Watchman: give Eugenie the Overcharged Storage Battery to unlock Fulminis.
4After King's Flame, Fuoco: give Eugenie the High-powered Flame Amplifier to unlock Flamberge. The Venigni Craft Machine route also becomes your main Legion Arm workbench.
5Find four Legion Plugs: each plug crafts one of Aegis, Deus Ex Machina, Pandemonium, or Falcon Eyes. You can pick the craft order based on build need.

7. Legion Caliber Notes

Legion Caliber is the material used to modify and upgrade Legion Arms at the Venigni Craft Machine in Hotel Krat. Because upgrade materials are limited during a first run, do not upgrade every arm evenly.

Do Not Spread Too Thin

One partially upgraded arm you never use is just stored power. Put early Calibers into Puppet String, Aegis, or the elemental arm your build actually supports.

Carryover

Unlocked Legion Arms and upgrade progress carry into NG+, so a missed optimization can be recovered in a later cycle.

8. Overture DLC Note

Base-game routing above covers the eight original arms. Current post-Overture sources list additional DLC Legion Arms, including Cataclysm and Icarus, and the official Overture page confirms new Legion Arm content. I am not giving exact DLC pickup steps here because this page is scoped to the base-game route.

9. Common Mistakes

Calling the base total seven

Seven is the non-default count. The base-game total is eight when Left Arm of Steel is included.

Crafting before deciding

Each Legion Plug crafts one plug arm. Read the arm role first, then craft for the problem your build actually has.

Ignoring upgrade breakpoints

Some arms become much more useful only after upgrades. Puppet String, Aegis, and Falcon Eyes are the clearest examples from the sources checked.

11. FAQ

How many Legion Arms are in Lies of P?

In the base game, eight if counting Left Arm of Steel, or seven non-default arms if excluding it. Overture DLC adds separate Legion Arm content.

How many Legion Plugs are there in the base game?

Four actual Legion Plug pickups are used to craft Aegis, Deus Ex Machina, Pandemonium, and Falcon Eyes. Fulminis and Flamberge come from story boss materials instead.

Which Legion Arm should I upgrade first?

Puppet String is the safest first upgrade for exploration and general utility. Aegis is the safest defensive upgrade if bosses are the main problem.

Can I miss Legion Arms forever?

The sources checked frame Legion Plug arms as recoverable through exploration and NG+ carryover. If you missed one plug during a run, put it on your NG+ cleanup route.

What upgrades Legion Arms?

Legion Caliber is used at the Venigni Craft Machine in Hotel Krat to modify and upgrade Legion Arms.
