1. Quick Answer

Short Version

Humanity is Lies of P's hidden "becoming human" route state. It rises from lies or compassionate choices, listening to records, answering riddles, completing NPC stories, and a few specific gestures or late-game choices. You need high Humanity for the Rise of P ending path and very high Humanity for the Golden Lie weapon.

The game never shows a numeric Humanity meter. Instead, it gives short on-screen messages after a Humanity gain and reflects your progress through story events, the Portrait of a Boy, and late-game outcomes. If you are aiming for Golden Lie or Rise of P, build Humanity all playthrough rather than trying to fix it at the final boss.

2. Humanity Signals and Messages

When a choice or action increases Humanity, the game shows a short message. Players commonly use the message ladder below as a practical route check, though the game does not expose exact numeric thresholds.

SignalWhat It SuggestsHow to Use It
Your springs are reactingEarly Humanity movement; you are gaining points but still low.Keep taking Humanity sources. This is not enough for Golden Lie or Rise of P by itself.
The Ergo is whisperingMid route progress; your run is moving away from a pure puppet state.Continue records, riddles, and quest cleanup before late game.
You feel warmthHigh-ish Humanity; you are closer to the ending thresholds.Good progress, but keep pushing if you want the safest Rise of P / Golden Lie route.
Your heart is poundingCommonly treated as the strongest visible Humanity signal.Use this as your best practical checkpoint before Sophia if targeting Rise of P.
Portrait of a Boy growthThe Golden Lie route is progressing.Check Geppetto's room after major Humanity gains and before starting NG+.
Important

A few imperfect choices usually do not ruin a Humanity run, but the system is limited. Miss too many records, NPC quests, riddles, or late choices and you may need NG+ to recover.

5. Golden Lie Relationship

Golden Lie Rule

Golden Lie is tied to the Portrait of a Boy and a very high Humanity route. Defeat the Eldest of the Black Rabbit Brotherhood, collect the portrait in the hideout, give it to Geppetto, then keep raising Humanity until the portrait weapon can be claimed.

RequirementWhy It Matters
Portrait of a BoyWithout the portrait in Geppetto's room, your Humanity route cannot produce Golden Lie.
High Humanity before cycle endThe weapon is easy to miss if you start NG+ before checking the portrait.
Records and NPC questsThese are the most reliable extra Humanity sources if your lie choices were imperfect.
Late-game checksCheck Geppetto's room after major Humanity gains, after Sophia, and before NG+.

6. Safe Humanity Route Checklist

1Early game: choose compassionate lies, finish Weeping Woman and Toma-style side content, and buy / collect anything tied to records or gestures.
2Hotel Krat routine: after every major boss, talk to every hub NPC, give Pulcinella supply boxes, ask Venigni to decrypt vessels, and use the gramophone for new records.
3Midgame: finish Cecile, Julian, Polendina / Antonia, Adelina, and other NPC routes before their story windows close.
4Riddles and Sanctums: answer Arlecchino's phones and finish the Trinity Sanctum chain rather than saving it for blind cleanup.
5Golden Lie setup: collect the Portrait of a Boy in Malum District and give it to Geppetto as soon as possible.
6Late game: complete Belle, Eugenie / Alidoro, records, and cryptic-vessel cleanup before the Sophia and Geppetto branch.
7Before NG+: check the portrait, Hotel Krat letters, piano, shop stock, and any missed records before starting another cycle.

7. Common Mistakes

Collecting records but not playing them

Records need to be played at Hotel Krat. For a Humanity route, listening is part of the route, not just flavor.

Entering Sophia's room too early

If you want Rise of P, finish available Humanity sources before the Sophia choice after Laxasia.

Ignoring NPC questlines

Character stories often carry records, gestures, and Humanity choices. They are not optional if you are routing Golden Lie safely.

Starting NG+ immediately

Do not start NG+ until you check Golden Lie, post-ending Hotel Krat rewards, and any late records or letters.

9. FAQ

Is there a visible Humanity meter in Lies of P?

No. The game uses on-screen Humanity messages, story changes, and the Portrait of a Boy / Golden Lie route as practical indicators.

Do you need to lie every time for Rise of P?

No. You can miss some Humanity opportunities and still reach the route if you collect enough through records, riddles, NPC quests, and later choices. A strict truth run is much riskier.

Do records increase Humanity?

Yes. Play new records at Hotel Krat and wait for the game to register them. They are one of the safest ways to support Golden Lie and Rise of P if you missed a few dialogue opportunities.

What does "Your heart is pounding" mean?

It is commonly treated as the strongest visible Humanity message and the safest practical signal before the Sophia choice if you are aiming for Rise of P.

Can Humanity be fixed after the ending?

Only partly. Some postgame checks are still possible, but ending branches and many questline rewards are already decided. For missed Humanity routes, NG+ is the reliable recovery.
