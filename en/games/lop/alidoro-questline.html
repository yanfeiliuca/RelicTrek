1. Quick Answer

Short Version

For completion, unlock Alidoro as the Rare Ergo trader, keep every boss Ergo, then kill Alidoro after the second Black Rabbit Brotherhood fight. Hugo can replace him as the special weapon and amulet trader, while Alidoro's Cryptic Vessel is required for Eugenie's safest late quest route.

The safest classic order is: defeat the second Black Rabbit Brotherhood, exhaust Alidoro's dialogue, kill him if you want the vessel route, take the vessel to Venigni, read the decryption, and then talk to Eugenie. Current patched versions are less punishing for formerly exclusive Records, but this order keeps the story reveal and record checks clean.

3. Chronological Quest Route

1Area IV - Cathedral Library: meet Alidoro after taking the elevator route. Send him to Hotel Krat if you want the simplest trade path.
2After Fallen Archbishop Andreus: visit Alidoro and review your Rare Ergo trades. For completion, prioritize boss weapons first and save amulets for NG+.
3Grand Exhibition / Champion Victor window: keep Eugenie dialogue current, including the glove-related Alidoro check, but do not spend boss Ergo casually.
4Barren Swamp: follow Alidoro's movement and keep Hugo in mind as the later safety net for the trade system.
5Relic of Trismegistus Combat Field: after defeating the second Black Rabbit Brotherhood, continue forward and confront Alidoro.
6Choice point: exhaust his dialogue, then choose whether to spare him or attack him. Select the attack path only when you are ready to resolve Eugenie's vessel route.
7Hotel Krat cleanup: if you killed him, go to Venigni before Eugenie, decrypt Alidoro's Cryptic Vessel, read the decryption, then speak with Eugenie.

Patch-Sensitive Record Note

Older guides and newer post-DLC notes do not always describe the Eugenie records the same way. The official Overture patch says formerly mutually exclusive Records can now be acquired in one playthrough. The safe rule stays the same: preserve a save if possible, decrypt and read Alidoro's vessel before Eugenie, then use NG+ only if Far East Princess or Proposal, Flower, Wolf Part 1 remains missing.

7. Common Mistakes

Talking to Eugenie too early

After killing Alidoro, the cleanest order is still Venigni first. Decrypt the vessel, read it, and then talk to Eugenie.

Spending Rare Ergo for quick levels

The Ergo payout is not worth losing a special weapon or boss amulet on a completion save.

Worrying that Hugo cannot trade

Hugo can cover the special item merchant role after Alidoro's late outcome. The trade system is safer than the record branch.

Expecting both Eugenie outcomes without planning

Because record behavior has varied by patch and branch, verify your inventory before NG+. On current patches, do not assume the opposite branch automatically requires another full cycle.

9. FAQ

Where is Alidoro first found?

Alidoro first appears in Area IV at St. Frangelico Cathedral Library, reached by an elevator route in the upper Cathedral path.

Should I kill Alidoro?

For Alidoro's vessel and Eugenie's full identity route, yes. Killing him late at Relic of Trismegistus unlocks Alidoro's Cryptic Vessel, while Hugo can replace him as the Rare Ergo trader.

Do I lose boss weapon trading if Alidoro dies?

No. Hugo can take over the special weapon and amulet exchange role. What you cannot recover in the same cycle is a Rare Ergo you already consumed or traded for the wrong reward.

What should I do with Alidoro's Cryptic Vessel?

Take it to Venigni at Hotel Krat, decrypt it, read the decryption item, and only then talk to Eugenie.

Can I get both Eugenie records in one run?

Current patched versions allow formerly mutually exclusive Records in one playthrough, but old guides and old saves may behave differently. Follow the safe vessel-first order, check your record inventory, and use NG+ only if a record remains missing.
