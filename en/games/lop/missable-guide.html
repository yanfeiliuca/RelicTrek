1. Quick Answer

Short Version

The most important missables in Lies of P are records, side quests, special boss weapons, Golden Lie progress, ending choices, and early merchant inventory. Clean up each chapter before moving to the next major boss, do not consume Rare Boss Ergo if you want special weapons, and do not start NG+ until you finish Hotel Krat post-ending cleanup.

Lies of P lets you keep exploring after many bosses, but several rewards are tied to character states, dialogue choices, merchant windows, and one-use boss Ergo. Treat every new chapter as a checkpoint: finish available side quests, buy limited stock, play new records at Hotel Krat, and save Rare Ergo before pushing the story forward.

4. Safe Route Through a Playthrough

1Before Parade Master: buy any early merchant weapons you want for collection. This is the first simple missable check.
2After each main boss: return to Hotel Krat, talk to every NPC with new dialogue, check merchant inventory, and play any new records.
3When you receive Rare Boss Ergo: do not consume it. Keep it until Alidoro, Hugo, or your completion plan confirms the trade.
4When side quests update: resolve them before the next major story area. Records and Humanity sources often come from these steps.
5Before Sophia after Laxasia: decide your ending route. High Humanity plus the Sophia choice can change your ending outcome.
6After the credits: do not start NG+ immediately. Return to Hotel Krat for letters, piano, late trades, and any final cleanup.

5. Highest-Risk Missables

Rare Boss Ergo

If your goal is all special weapons, never consume Rare Ergo and do not buy the amulet alternative first. The same boss can be fought again in NG+, but one cycle gives only one trade choice.

Records and NPC Quests

Records are one of the easiest categories to miss because many come from NPC questlines, vendor windows, NG+ merchants, or patch-sensitive dialogue outcomes. Treat records as story-route rewards, not simple collectibles.

Golden Lie

The Golden Lie depends on the portrait route and high Humanity. If you ignore lies, records, and questline Humanity sources, the weapon can remain unavailable in that playthrough.

Ending Choices

The late Sophia choice and final Geppetto choice are the real ending locks. Decide your target ending before walking into the final sequence.

7. FAQ

Can you get everything in one Lies of P playthrough?

No. A full completion route needs NG+ because Golden Records are NG+ vendor purchases, and boss Ergo trade choices are still one reward per Rare Ergo per cycle.

Are boss weapons missable?

Yes. They are missable for the current cycle if you consume the matching Rare Boss Ergo or spend it on the amulet alternative. You can recover in NG+ by defeating the boss again.

Are records missable?

Yes. Records can depend on NPC questlines, dialogue choices, merchants, and NG+ availability. Play new records at Hotel Krat and let the game register them because records also help with Humanity.

Should I start NG+ immediately after the ending?

No. Return to Hotel Krat first. Read available letters, check the piano, finish late trades, and confirm your cleanup list before starting NG+.

What is the safest first-run plan?

For a completion-minded first run, lie often, complete side quests quickly, play new records at Hotel Krat, save every Rare Boss Ergo, buy special weapons before amulets, and delay NG+ until post-ending cleanup is done.
