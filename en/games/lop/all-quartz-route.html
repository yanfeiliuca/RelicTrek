Quick Answer

Short Version

Quartz is the finite P-Organ upgrade material in Lies of P. Current patched base-game runs can reach up to 31 Quartz, with Polendina's shop accounting for 5 total shop Quartz in current community-maintained lists. Older 28-location routes undercount current base-game saves. This checklist covers the base game; Overture DLC Quartz should be tracked separately.

Use this page as a route checklist. The highest-risk Quartz are tied to Trinity Sanctums, Cryptic Vessels, Broken Puppet gestures, shop supply boxes, and the Mechanical Cryptic Vessel reward that requires the Frozen Feast boss weapon.

Why Some Guides Say 28

Launch-era guides commonly list 28 Quartz. Current Patch 1.3-era community-maintained lists show up to 31 in a playthrough, with Polendina's expanded shop accounting for 5 total shop Quartz. Because this page has not verified the exact official shop-addition split from the original patch notes, it tracks the current total and routes every source rather than relying on old 28-count checklists.

Scope Note

This page is a base-game route. If you are playing with Overture installed, treat DLC Quartz as a separate pass until the Overture-specific checklist is expanded.

P-Organ Spending Notes

Quartz is finite, but you do not need to over-optimize every point. Prioritize upgrades that make the whole playthrough easier before chasing niche build bonuses.

Best Early Priorities

  • Extra Pulse Cell uses or stronger Pulse Cell recovery.
  • Better dodge, guard, or recovery options if available on your version.
  • Stagger-window and Fatal Attack support if bosses are the problem.

Completion Priorities

  • Keep enough flexibility for NG+ phases and later P-Organ tiers.
  • Use respec options later if a build path stops helping.
  • Do not spend Rare Boss Ergo for levels just to compensate for weak P-Organ choices.

Common Mistakes

  • Following an old 28-count checklist only: check Polendina's shop and supply-box expansions for current shop Quartz.
  • Buying Arm of God Amulet before Frozen Feast: this blocks the Mechanical Cryptic Vessel Quartz until NG+.
  • Skipping optional Puppets of the Future: the factory and swamp machines are worth clearing.
  • Ignoring Trinity phones: Sanctums hide Quartz and other rare rewards behind riddle progress.
  • Forgetting supply boxes: shop expansions are part of the Quartz route, not just merchant convenience.

FAQ

How many Quartz are in Lies of P?

Current patched base-game runs can reach up to 31 Quartz. Older guides often list 28 because current Patch 1.3-era community lists include additional Polendina shop Quartz beyond launch-era routes.

Can you farm Quartz?

No. Quartz comes from fixed bosses, elite enemies, chests, shops, Trinity Sanctums, Cryptic Vessels, and quest rewards. You collect another set by entering NG+.

Is any Quartz missable?

The most important practical missable is the Mechanical Cryptic Vessel reward because it requires Frozen Feast. If you spent Reborn Champion's Ergo on Arm of God Amulet or consumed it, that Quartz waits until NG+.

Does Quartz reset in NG+?

Locations and rewards become available again in NG+. Your P-Organ progression continues, so NG+ is how you keep expanding beyond a first-run build.

Does this include Overture DLC Quartz?

No. This checklist focuses on the base game and current base-game patch additions. Overture should be handled as a separate DLC route pass.
