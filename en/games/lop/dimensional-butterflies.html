1. Quick Answer

Short Version

Dimensional Butterflies are hidden material enemies revealed by Monad's Lamp glowing red. If one escapes, rest at a Stargazer and return; if you kill it, it stays gone for that playthrough. For From Across the Rift, you need to kill every butterfly type/color, not necessarily every individual butterfly.

The best route is simple: keep Monad's Lamp active while exploring, stop when it turns red, clear nearby enemies, reveal the butterfly, burst it down, then pick up the material before moving on. These drops feed normal weapon upgrades, special weapon upgrades, handle alteration, Legion Arm progress, and late build cleanup.

2. How the Mechanic Works

SignalMeaningCorrect Response
Monad's Lamp glows or pulses redA hidden Dimensional Butterfly is nearby.Slow down, look for shimmer or sound cues, and avoid sprinting past the spawn.
Butterfly appearsThe timer has effectively started.Attack immediately with quick swings, throwables, or a fast Legion Arm if enemies are cleared.
Butterfly teleports or darts awaySome colors move quickly or reposition after being hit.Track the shimmer and keep pressure on it before it disappears.
Butterfly escapesYou missed the current attempt, not the entire item.Rest at a Stargazer and return to the same spot to retry.
Butterfly diesThe drop is secured and the spawn is cleared for the playthrough.Loot the material and mark the area complete.
Do Not Panic

Escaping is not the same as losing the reward forever. Rest and return. The real loss is forgetting the location and moving deep into the next chapter without retrying.

3. Color Types and Trophy Planning

Type / Color LabelBehaviorWhy It Matters
RedCommon, fast, and usually the first type players learn to chase.Usually covers the first From Across the Rift type.
Pink / purpleCan teleport or shift position after damage.Keep watching the shimmer after the first hit; do not swing at the empty original spot.
Blue / rareListed by some trophy sources as a separate higher-rarity type.Kill it when seen, even if another source groups color names differently.
Gold / yellowHigher health and more dangerous pushback behavior.Usually appears later and is commonly the last type needed for the trophy.
Trophy Note

Guides vary in color naming: pink may be called purple, gold may be called yellow, and some lists count blue separately. The safe rule is to kill every new-looking color as soon as you encounter it.

4. Route Priorities

1Inside the House on Elysion Boulevard: treat the first rooftop butterfly as your mechanic tutorial and collect the early Hidden Moonstone reward.
2Workshop Union Entrance and Culvert: chase early upgrade and handle-altering materials such as Motivity Crank and Hidden Moonstone.
3St. Frangelico Cathedral and Malum District: watch for color changes and teleport behavior; this is where many players start missing the faster types.
4Rosa Isabelle Street and Lorenzini Arcade: prioritize later color types for From Across the Rift and rare material cleanup.
5Collapsed Street and Krat Central Station return: grab Half Moonstone and other late normal-weapon upgrade materials while doing endgame cleanup.
6Relic of Trismegistus and Arche Abbey: do not ignore late clusters; some of the best rewards include Full Moonstone of the Covenant and high-tier boosters.
7Overture DLC: keep the same habit in DLC zones, where butterfly rewards can include Quartz, Legion Caliber, Ancient Disk, and other DLC materials.

6. Catching Tips

ProblemFixReason
Enemies interrupt the chaseBack away, clear the room, then trigger the butterfly again.Most failed attempts come from fighting enemies and the butterfly at the same time.
Butterfly escapes too fastUse fast attacks, throwables, or a quick Legion Arm instead of heavy windups.Butterflies punish slow setup more than low damage.
Pink / purple teleportingStop attacking the original spot and follow the new shimmer.Teleport types waste your timer if you do not track them visually.

7. Common Mistakes

Treating red lamp glow as decoration

Monad's Lamp turning red is a mechanical warning. Stop and search before moving on.

Leaving after one escape

Escaped butterflies can be retried after resting. Do not write off the reward until you have returned from a Stargazer.

Ignoring color variety

From Across the Rift is about types. Kill each new color when you see it instead of assuming one red butterfly is enough.

Skipping late butterflies

Late areas can drop higher-value materials needed for special weapon and endgame upgrade plans.

9. FAQ

Are Dimensional Butterflies permanently missable if they escape?

No. Rest at a Stargazer and return to the same spot. The butterfly can reappear until you kill it.

Do killed Dimensional Butterflies respawn?

No. Once killed, that spawn is cleared for the current playthrough.

How do I find hidden butterflies?

Keep Monad's Lamp active and watch for the lamp to glow or pulse red. That means a hidden butterfly is nearby.

Do I need to kill every butterfly for From Across the Rift?

The trophy/achievement asks for every type of Dimensional Butterfly. Because sources vary in color labels, kill every new color you encounter to be safe.

Why should I chase them if I already have the trophy?

Their drops are still valuable for weapon upgrades, handle alteration, Legion Arm upgrades, Quartz routes, and late build cleanup.
