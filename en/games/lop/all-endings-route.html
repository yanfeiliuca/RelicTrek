1. Quick Answer

Ending Summary

Lies of P has three endings. Real Boy comes from giving Geppetto your heart. Free from the Puppet String comes from refusing Geppetto without qualifying for Rise of P. Rise of P requires high Humanity, giving Sophia peace, refusing Geppetto, and defeating Nameless Puppet.

The ending route is mostly decided late, but your Humanity preparation happens across the whole playthrough. If you want the cleanest completion route, build Humanity early through lies, records, riddles, and NPC quests, then decide your ending path before entering Sophia's room after Laxasia.

3. Efficient One-Playthrough Save Plan

If your platform allows manual backup saves, you can reduce repeated playthrough time. If not, route the endings across NG+ cycles.

1Before Sophia after Laxasia: finish available character stories, listen to records, and confirm you have high Humanity if targeting Rise of P.
2Backup point A: make a backup before entering Sophia's room if you want both Sophia branches.
3Free from the Puppet String route: choose Let her live, continue to the end, refuse Geppetto, and defeat Nameless Puppet.
4Rise of P route: reload before Sophia, choose Give her peace with high Humanity, then refuse Geppetto and defeat Nameless Puppet.
5Real Boy route: backup before Geppetto's final request, then choose Give the Heart.
6After any ending: do not start NG+ until you finish Hotel Krat post-ending cleanup.

5. Common Ending Mistakes

Choosing Sophia Without a Plan

The Sophia choice is the major branch. If you are aiming for Rise of P, do not enter that scene before checking Humanity and finishing available cleanup.

Forgetting Nameless Puppet

Refusing Geppetto sends you into the Nameless Puppet route. Giving the heart skips that fight and locks Real Boy.

Starting NG+ Too Early

After the credits, return to Hotel Krat first. Several post-ending interactions and late trades are easy to lose if you immediately start the next cycle.

7. FAQ

Which Lies of P ending is the true ending?

Rise of P is generally treated as the true or best ending because it requires high Humanity, the Sophia peace choice, refusing Geppetto, and defeating Nameless Puppet.

Can you get all three endings in one playthrough?

Only if you use backup saves around the late-game choices. Without backups, plan on NG+ cycles.

Do you need to lie every time for Rise of P?

No, but you need enough Humanity. Lies, records, riddles, and side quests all matter, so a few imperfect choices usually do not ruin the route.

What locks the Real Boy ending?

Giving Geppetto your heart at the final request locks Real Boy and skips the Nameless Puppet ending route.

What should I do after the credits?

Do not start NG+ immediately. Return to Hotel Krat, read available letters, check the piano, and finish any late trades or cleanup first.
