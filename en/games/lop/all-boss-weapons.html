1. Why You Need It

Every main boss drops a Rare Boss Ergo. Each can be exchanged for the Special Weapon OR the Special Amulet — not both. In a single playthrough, you choose one. You need NG+ to get the other.

2. What It Does

Alidoro is the primary trader — first encountered in Area IV (St. Frangelico Cathedral), via elevator at the far back of the cathedral's underground floor. Hugo becomes alternate trader if Alidoro is killed. Klaus is the Overture DLC trader.

3. Prerequisites

  • Must reach Area IV to find Alidoro
  • Must collect Rare Boss Ergos from boss defeats
  • Must not kill Alidoro before trading desired weapons

⚠ Critical
