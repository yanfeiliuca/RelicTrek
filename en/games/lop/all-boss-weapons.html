Quick Answer

Short Version

Do not consume Rare Boss Ergo if you want boss weapons. Each base-game Rare Ergo can be traded for either one special weapon or one special amulet, never both in the same cycle. For a Special Weapon Collector run, buy every weapon first and leave amulets for NG+.

Boss weapons in Lies of P come from Alidoro or, later, Rookie Explorer Hugo. The trade menu is easy to misunderstand because the same boss Ergo also offers an amulet. If you buy the amulet or consume the Ergo, the weapon is locked until you defeat that boss again in a later playthrough.

Unlock Alidoro and Hugo

1Reach St. Frangelico Cathedral Library: after working through Area IV, use the elevator near the upper library route to meet Alidoro.
2Send him to Hotel Krat: this makes the boss weapon and amulet trade menu easy to access after Fallen Archbishop Andreus.
3Check his later locations: after Champion Victor, Alidoro leaves Hotel Krat and later appears around the Barren Swamp route before returning to the broader late-game quest path.
4Use Hugo if needed: if Alidoro is no longer available after his late-game quest outcome, Hugo can take over the Rare Ergo trade role.
Important

The danger is not losing the trader forever; the danger is spending the Rare Ergo. Keep every Rare Ergo in your inventory until your weapon, amulet, and NG+ plan is clear.

First-Run Choice Rules

If You Want All Special Weapons

Buy the weapon every time. This includes weapons you do not plan to equip immediately, because the collection route cares about ownership and the same Rare Ergo cannot be refunded.

  • Do not consume or sell Rare Ergo.
  • Do not buy amulets first on the same save cycle.
  • Save boss amulet collection for NG+ or later.

If You Only Care About One Build

Delay the trade until you have compared the weapon and the verified amulet effect. No single option is best for every build.

  • Check weapon scaling and weight before spending the Ergo.
  • Check the amulet's weight and effect before committing.
  • Keep Frozen Feast if you plan to finish the Mechanical Cryptic Vessel in this cycle.

NG+ Planning

A full base-game boss weapon plus boss amulet collection needs multiple cycles because each boss gives one Rare Ergo per cycle. The cleanest route is simple:

Run 1Buy all boss weapons: keep every Rare Ergo, unlock Alidoro, and choose weapons for every trade.
Run 2Buy amulets: defeat the bosses again in NG+, collect the Rare Ergo again, and choose the amulet alternatives.
Run 3Clean up endings or missed choices: use a third cycle only if you also need ending routes, records, or post-ending collectibles.
Golden Lie Note

Golden Lie is also a special weapon, but it comes from the Portrait of a Boy and high Humanity route, not from a Rare Ergo trade. Keep it on your completion checklist separately.

Common Mistakes

  • Consuming or selling Rare Ergo: either action removes both trade choices for that cycle.
  • Buying an amulet first: it blocks the corresponding weapon until NG+.
  • Trading Reborn Champion's Ergo before planning the Mechanical Cryptic Vessel: that vessel requires Frozen Feast, so choosing Arm of God Amulet delays its reward until a later cycle.
  • Assuming Alidoro is the only safety net: Hugo can cover trading later, but he cannot restore a spent Rare Ergo.
  • Mixing base-game and DLC lists: Klaus and Overture boss trades are separate from the 10 base-game Rare Ergo trades above.
  • Forgetting Nameless Puppet: Proof of Humanity requires Nameless Puppet's Ergo, so Real Boy ending routes that skip the fight do not produce that trade in the same cycle.

FAQ

Can you get all boss weapons in one playthrough?

You can get all base-game boss trade weapons in one playthrough if you defeat the required bosses, keep every Rare Ergo, unlock the trader, and choose weapons instead of amulets. You cannot get every weapon and every amulet from the same Rare Ergo in one cycle.

Where is Alidoro?

Alidoro first appears in Area IV at St. Frangelico Cathedral Library, reached by elevator from the upper library route. Sending him to Hotel Krat makes his trade menu easy to access after Fallen Archbishop Andreus.

What if Alidoro is gone?

Use Hugo once the late-game quest state allows him to take over the trade role. This keeps the trade system available, but it does not restore Rare Ergo that you already consumed or exchanged.

Should I ever consume Rare Ergo?

Only consume Rare Ergo if you are completely sure you do not care about that weapon or amulet on the current save cycle. For completion, trophy, or guide-cleanup runs, keep every Rare Ergo.

Is Golden Lie a boss weapon?

Golden Lie is a special weapon, but it is not a Rare Ergo boss trade. It belongs with Humanity, records, and the Portrait of a Boy route instead.
