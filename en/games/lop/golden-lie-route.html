1. Quick Answer

How to Get Golden Lie

To get the Golden Lie, defeat the Eldest of the Black Rabbit Brotherhood, take the Portrait of a Boy from the Black Rabbit Brotherhood hideout near the Malum District Town Hall Stargazer, give it to Geppetto at Hotel Krat, then raise Humanity through lies, records, riddles, and questlines until the portrait's weapon can be claimed.

Golden Lie is one of the easiest weapons to miss because the game never turns it into a clear quest objective. The portrait quietly changes as your Humanity rises, and if you enter NG+ before checking it, you can lose the chance for that cycle.

3. Step-by-Step Route

1Clear Malum District: defeat the Eldest of the Black Rabbit Brotherhood and activate the Malum District Town Hall Stargazer.
2Take the Portrait of a Boy: inside the Black Rabbit Brotherhood hideout, enter the room on the left side near the entrance/stairs and interact with the portrait.
3Return to Hotel Krat: give the Portrait of a Boy to Geppetto. The portrait will appear in his room and begin tracking your route.
4Build Humanity: continue choosing human/lie outcomes, finish NPC quests, solve Arlecchino riddles, and play newly collected records at Hotel Krat.
5Watch the portrait: the nose length changes as your run progresses. If it is not ready, keep collecting available Humanity sources before ending the cycle.
6Claim Golden Lie: return to Geppetto's room when the portrait is fully developed and interact with it to take the weapon.

6. Troubleshooting

The portrait is not interactable

You probably do not have enough Humanity yet, or you have not returned after enough route progress. Finish remaining Humanity sources before ending the cycle.

I missed the portrait

Return to the Malum District Town Hall / Black Rabbit Brotherhood hideout route and check the room near the entrance and stairs. If the cycle has already moved into NG+, reclaim it in that cycle.

I started NG+

You need to route the portrait and Humanity again in the new cycle. Records you own may carry over, but Humanity progress itself is a run-state goal.

8. FAQ

Where is the Portrait of a Boy?

It is in the Black Rabbit Brotherhood hideout near the Malum District Town Hall Stargazer, after defeating the Eldest of the Black Rabbit Brotherhood. Check the room on the left side near the entrance and stairs.

Do you need to lie every time to get Golden Lie?

No single missed choice automatically ruins the run, but you should treat Golden Lie as a very high Humanity target. Lie often, finish quests, solve riddles, and play newly collected records at Hotel Krat.

Do records matter for Golden Lie?

Yes. Playing records at Hotel Krat is one of the major ways to build Humanity, so collect and register newly obtained records before ending the cycle.

Can you get Golden Lie before the final boss?

It can become available late in the game once your Humanity route is high enough. The safe habit is to check the portrait after major Humanity gains and again before starting NG+.

Can you recover Golden Lie in NG+?

Yes, but you need to repeat the portrait route and build enough Humanity in that new cycle.
