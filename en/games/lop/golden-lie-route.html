Golden Lie — The Secret Weapon That Grows From a Painting as You Learn to Lie

1. Why You Need It

The Golden Lie is a greatsword that does not exist at the start of the game. It grows into existence. Its acquisition is the clearest expression of the game's core theme: a puppet learning to lie its way toward humanity.

The process takes an entire playthrough to complete. Each step is separated from the others by hours of gameplay. The game never labels this as a quest. There is no objective marker, no reminder, no indication that anything is happening. Players who stumble onto the final step by chance, returning to Gepetto's room after the credits, typically don't understand what they're seeing.

It is the single most information-asymmetric item in the game. Of the players who have completed Lies of P, a substantial portion have never obtained it.

2. What the Golden Lie Does

Special Greatsword. Scales with Motivity, Technique, and Advance simultaneously. Unique Fable Art: "Pinocchio." Attack power increases with Humanity level.

The weapon grows from a painting in Gepetto's room at Hotel Krat. As your Humanity increases throughout the game, the nose on the painted Pinocchio grows longer. At maximum Humanity after the final boss, the nose detaches and becomes the Golden Lie.

3. Prerequisites

  • Full playthrough required — all 4 steps must be completed in order
  • Must reach maximum Humanity tier (system message: "Your heart is pounding")
  • Steps: collect painting → hang painting → max humanity → return after final boss
  • No specific boss kills required beyond completing the game

4. Optimal Route

Step 1 — Obtain the Painting (Area V · Krat City Hall)

1Progress through Area V until you reach Krat City Hall. Before leaving the building, find the painting on the wall inside. Interact with it and collect it as a quest item.

Step 2 — Hang the Painting at Hotel Krat

2Return to Hotel Krat (your main hub). Find Gepetto's room. Interact with the empty wall space to hang the painting. It now shows a puppet (Pinocchio) with no nose. This is the beginning.

Step 3 — Raise Humanity to Maximum Tier

3As you progress through the rest of the game, the painting's nose grows longer each time Humanity increases. Humanity increases when you lie during dialogue interactions, complete NPC side quests and questlines, and answer Arlecchino's riddles at phone booths. The final Humanity tier is confirmed when you receive the system message: "Your heart is pounding."

Step 4 — Defeat the Final Boss, Then Return

4After reaching maximum Humanity and completing the game's final boss, return to Gepetto's room in Hotel Krat. The painting now shows the nose detached. Interact with it to receive the Golden Lie.

5. Hazard Guide

⚠ Permanently Missable

If you rush through dialogue choosing truth every time, Humanity will not reach maximum before the final boss. The Golden Lie is permanently missed for that playthrough.

⚠ Easy to Skip Step 1

The painting looks like ordinary environmental decoration. Many players complete Area V without entering the specific room where it hangs.

NG+ alternative: If you missed it, NG+ allows you to keep lying from the start with faster Humanity accumulation, and the painting state carries over.

6. En-Route Bonus Items

Humanity points from NPC questlines (Eugenia, Belle, Julian) and correct riddle answers contribute to reaching max tier.
