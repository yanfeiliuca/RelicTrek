1. Quick Answer

Overture contains 10 weapons: six normal weapons that split into two assembly parts, plus Royal Horn Bow, Death's Talons, Pale Knight, and Monad's Rose Sword as fixed unique weapons. Two weapons consume Rare Ergo choices; Monad's Rose Sword is awarded directly after the final boss.

Rare Ergo Rule

Do not consume or sell Macabre Puppeteer's Ergo or Tortured Guardian's Ergo before checking both the weapon and amulet rewards.

2. DLC Access and Scope

On a first playthrough, reach Chapter 9, repair the Hotel Krat Stargazer, and enter the Forgotten Time. This checklist covers acquisition only: it does not rank weapons or copy patch-sensitive attack values.

4. Assembly Breakdown

The six disassemblable weapons are Lorenzini Bolt, Maniac's Pinwheel, Puppet of the Future's Welder, La Vendetta, Arche's Guardian, and Silent Evangelists' Mace. Royal Horn Bow is a Special Weapon and cannot be disassembled.

5. Klaus and Rare Ergo

Progress from Carnival Garden through the Zoo Tram Station to Eventide Hotel Krat. Klaus is near the Gramophone and provides the DLC Rare Ergo menu. Alidoro may serve as an alternate after switching back to the base-game timeline, depending on quest state. Hugo is not included because Overture Rare Ergo support has not been verified.

6. Verification Boundaries

What This Guide Does Not Claim

The available route sources confirm La Vendetta's post-Coolant-Control-Room backtrack, but not a later permanent cutoff. This page therefore does not label every world pickup as missable or permanently recoverable.
