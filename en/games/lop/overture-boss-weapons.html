1. Quick Answer

Overture has two Rare Ergo reward decisions and one direct final-boss weapon. Trade with Klaus in Eventide Hotel Krat. Each Rare Ergo buys either its weapon or its alternate amulet, not both in the same acquisition.

Do Not Consume Rare Ergo

Keep Macabre Puppeteer's Ergo and Tortured Guardian's Ergo until you have chosen between the weapon and amulet reward.

3. How to Unlock Klaus

1Activate Carnival Garden and obtain the Zoo Tram Station Key.
2Open the station and take the tram to Eventide Hotel Krat.
3Activate the Stargazer and find Klaus in the room left of the Gramophone.
4Use Trade Rare Ergo and confirm the weapon-or-amulet choice.

4. Alternate Trader Boundary

Alidoro is reported as an alternate after returning to the base-game timeline, but availability depends on quest state. Klaus is therefore the primary instruction for this DLC page. Hugo is excluded because no reliable evidence currently proves that his inventory includes Overture Rare Ergo rewards.

5. Reward Planning

  • Death's Talons competes with Morbid Ambition's Amulet.
  • Pale Knight competes with Nightmare's Amulet.
  • Monad's Rose Sword requires no Rare Ergo and does not compete with an identified amulet.
  • This page does not rank the choices; build value depends on playstyle and patch-sensitive balance.

Acquisition-Only Scope
