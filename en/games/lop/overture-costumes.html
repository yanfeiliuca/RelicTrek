1. Quick Answer

The Lies of P: Overture DLC (released June 6, 2025, by Neowiz and Round 8 Studio) adds 15 confirmed new costumes to the game. These outfits are cosmetic only — no stat changes, no combat effects. They fall into four acquisition categories: open-world exploration and chest finds, Black Rabbit Hole Key-locked hideouts, boss kill rewards, cipher machine puzzle completions, and vendor purchases using Ancient Disks.

The Black Rabbit Brotherhood Outfit is obtained through a separate, distinct side quest involving a carnival minigame and a Zoo merchant trade — its relationship to the 15-outfit count is not explicitly confirmed by any single authoritative source and is marked [待确认] in this guide.

✔ What this guide covers

Only outfits with at least one named, linkable external source. Every claim is annotated. This guide deliberately avoids fabricating total counts, NG+ lock conditions, or "permanent missable" warnings that have no source.

2. Verified DLC Context

Overture is a story prequel expansion, not a sequel. As Geppetto's Puppet, a mysterious artifact transports you back to Krat in its final days before the city's fall. The DLC introduces new areas (including the Krat Zoo, the Greenhouse, the Hotel, and Archon Fleet Shipwrecks), new bosses, new weapons, and the winter-season costume set. [Source-B: Fextralife Overture Wiki]

The Fextralife wiki page explicitly states under DLC features: "New Costumes to fit the winter season!" [Source-B: Fextralife] — consistent with the Winter's Hunting Set being obtainable early in the DLC. New content also includes new combat styles (Legion Arms) and over a dozen new weapons. Costumes from this DLC are separate from base-game costume counts and should not be combined.

ℹ DLC Access Requirement

Overture is only accessible after reaching the Golden Stargazer in the base game. Resting at it triggers the cutscene that leads to the DLC starting area. [Source-C: Fextralife user comments]

3. Costume System Overview

As in the base game, Overture costumes are purely cosmetic. Equipping any outfit or piece does not affect Pinocchio's attack, defense, elemental resistances, or any other combat statistic. [Source-B: Fextralife Overture wiki] This means there is no "best" costume mechanically — the choice is entirely visual.

Costumes are collected as individual pieces — some sets come with both a body apparel and a mask or hat in the same container or as a paired boss reward, while others are single pieces. The in-game grouping is not always 1 outfit = 1 item.

5. Black Rabbit Brotherhood Outfit — Separate Side Quest Route

Distinct from the Black Rabbit Hole Key hideout outfits above, the Black Rabbit Brotherhood Outfit is obtained through a multi-step side quest involving a carnival minigame, a trade, and a separate key purchase. This route is documented in a dedicated NoobFeed guide (June 9, 2025). [Source-B: NoobFeed — How To Get Black Rabbit Brotherhood Outfit]

ℹ Count Note

Whether the Black Rabbit Brotherhood Outfit is counted within the 15 outfits above, or is an additional item beyond them, is not explicitly stated in any source reviewed for this guide. Treat it as a guaranteed extra collectible regardless of how it's counted. [待确认]

Step-by-Step Acquisition

1Return to Hotel Krat and find the neighborhood business owner. Purchase as many Sawtooth Wheels as available — you need enough to clear the carnival booth game.
2Go to the nearby carnival booth. Interact with the display and use the Sawtooth Wheels to knock down all the toys. Claim the hidden prize: a unique Teapot.
3Do not sell the Teapot to a regular vendor — the payout will be much lower. Carry it to the merchant stationed in front of the locked tram door at the Zoo.
4Trade the Teapot to the Zoo merchant for approximately 14,000 Ergo — equivalent to roughly one or two level-ups depending on your current stats.
5With the Ergo earned, purchase the Black Rabbit Brotherhood Key from the same Zoo merchant.
6Travel to a Black Rabbit Brotherhood hideout and use the key to unlock the door. Claim the Black Rabbit Brotherhood Outfit from inside.
⚠ Do Not Sell the Teapot Early

If you sell the Teapot to any vendor other than the Zoo merchant, you forfeit the ~14,000 Ergo value and will not be able to afford the Black Rabbit Brotherhood Key. The guide specifically warns against early sale. [Source-B: NoobFeed]

6. Understanding the Key System

Two distinct key types unlock Black Rabbit-related outfits, and they are not interchangeable:

Black Rabbit Hole Keys are found during normal DLC exploration. Multiple copies are hidden in the world, each unlocking a different Brotherhood hideout containing outfit pieces (Piercing Death's, Permeating Death's, Black Death's, and Blazing Death's sets). The exact number of Black Rabbit Hole Keys available in the DLC per playthrough has not been confirmed by a counted, authoritative source. [Source-B: NoobFeed "All 15 Outfits" guide, which references multiple keys used at multiple hideouts]

The Black Rabbit Brotherhood Key (singular, purchased from the Zoo merchant using Teapot Ergo) is a separate, vendor-purchased key that unlocks the dedicated Black Rabbit Brotherhood Outfit hideout. See the Overture Black Rabbit Keys guide for the full key location breakdown.

7. Cipher Machine Puzzle Outfits

The Famed Detective's Coat and Hat (Outfit #8 in the checklist) are tied to the DLC's optional cipher machine puzzles. Three cipher machines are scattered across the Overture areas; solving all three rewards both pieces. [Source-B: NoobFeed "All 15 Outfits"] These puzzles are entirely optional — no story gate forces you to attempt them — but they represent some of the harder optional content in the DLC. See the dedicated Overture Cipher Machines guide for full puzzle solutions.

✔ Optional but Worth It

The Famed Detective's set is the only costume group in the DLC tied to skill-based optional content rather than exploration or boss kills. Don't skip it if you want a complete wardrobe.

8. What Not To Claim Yet

The following are common questions about Overture costumes that cannot be answered from verified sources at time of writing. Do not trust guides (including this one) that provide confident answers to these without linking a primary source:

  • NG+ lockouts: No source reviewed for this guide confirms whether any Overture costume is permanently missable in a given playthrough or locked out in NG+. Do not assume any costume requires NG+ or is forever lost after a story beat without a specific, sourced warning.
  • Exact Black Rabbit Hole Key count per playthrough: NoobFeed's guide references multiple key uses, but does not state the total number available. Fextralife's wiki does not enumerate them.
  • Total combined outfit count (base + DLC): Do not add DLC costume counts to base game counts. They are tracked separately. The base game "all costumes" route is a separate guide.
  • Outfit #11 duplicate: NoobFeed's guide lists a second find of Black Death's Protective Apparel + The Eldest's Gas Mask (under a different key). Fextralife lists these items only once in the Overture costume list. Whether the game gives you two copies of the same items, or this is an editorial inconsistency in the NoobFeed guide, remains [待确认].
  • Black Rabbit Brotherhood Outfit count status: Whether it is within or in addition to the 15-outfit total is not confirmed by any cross-referenced source.

10. FAQ

How many new costumes are in Lies of P: Overture DLC?

Multiple independent guides (NoobFeed, PatchCrazy, GamesFuze) confirm 15 new costumes and accessories. Whether the Black Rabbit Brotherhood Outfit obtained via the Teapot trade route is within or additional to those 15 has not been clarified by a single authoritative source. [Source-B: NoobFeed All 15 Outfits]

Do Overture costumes affect combat stats?

No. All Overture costumes are purely cosmetic, identical to base-game costume behavior. They provide style only. [Source-B: Fextralife Overture wiki]

How do I get the Black Rabbit Brotherhood Outfit?

Buy Sawtooth Wheels from the Hotel Krat neighborhood vendor → use them at the carnival booth to win a Teapot → sell the Teapot to the Zoo merchant near the locked tram door for ~14,000 Ergo → buy the Black Rabbit Brotherhood Key from the same merchant → use the key at a Brotherhood hideout. [Source-B: NoobFeed dedicated guide]

How do I get the Famed Detective's Coat and Hat?

Solve all three optional cipher machine puzzles in the DLC. Both pieces are awarded together upon completing the third puzzle. [Source-B: NoobFeed "All 15 Outfits"]

What are Black Rabbit Hole Keys and where do I use them?

Black Rabbit Hole Keys are found during DLC exploration and unlock secret Brotherhood hideouts containing outfit pieces — including the Piercing Death's, Permeating Death's, Black Death's, and Blazing Death's Hunting Apparel sets and their paired masks. The exact world locations of the keys have not been comprehensively sourced for this guide; see the Overture Black Rabbit Keys guide.
