1. Quick Answer

Short Version

For the safest completion route, accept Red Fox's Malum District offer, give one Gold Coin Fruit at the Grand Exhibition for Quixotic and Beg, then carry two more Gold Coin Fruit into Arche Abbey to spare Black Cat and Red Fox. You still receive their masks without fighting them.

The unique rewards are front-loaded into the friendly route: Quixotic, Beg, Black Cat's Mask, and Red Fox's Mask. Fighting the pair later gives their masks and some Ergo, but it does not add a special weapon, record, or trophy-critical item. If your goal is records, gestures, Humanity support, or a clean NPC route, feed the route instead of forcing the fights.

3. Chronological Route

1Area III - Workshop Union Culvert: meet Red Fox and Black Cat near the Culvert route. There is no major choice here; exhaust their dialogue and continue toward Venigni.
2Area V - Malum District: speak with them near the beginning of the district and accept Red Fox's offer to help with the Black Rabbit Brotherhood route.
3Malum streets: let them accompany you through the early enemy stretch. They eventually stop before the hideout, so finish the route yourself.
4Area VII - after Champion Victor: before taking the tram / monorail toward Barren Swamp, talk to them near the exit and give one Gold Coin Fruit.
5Hotel Krat: play Quixotic at the gramophone and keep gathering Gold Coin Fruit from the tree so you have at least two for the final area.
6Area XI - Arche Abbey Outer Wall: meet Black Cat first. Give him Gold Coin Fruit to avoid the fight and receive Black Cat's Mask.
7Area XI - Arche Abbey Upper route: meet Red Fox later. If Black Cat was spared, give her Gold Coin Fruit to avoid the fight and receive Red Fox's Mask.

The practical route is simple: after Champion Victor, always keep a small Gold Coin Fruit reserve. The fruit also has other uses, but this questline is one of the few places where carrying it directly changes NPC outcomes.

7. Common Mistakes

Leaving the Grand Exhibition without fruit

The Quixotic and Beg reward moment is easy to miss because it appears right after a major boss and before the next area transition.

Entering Arche Abbey empty-handed

Carry at least two Gold Coin Fruit before pushing deep into Area XI.

Killing Black Cat by habit

The fight is optional if you routed the friendly choices. Killing him makes Red Fox hostile later.

Forgetting to play Quixotic

Collecting the record is not the same as using it for route support. Play it at the Hotel Krat gramophone.

9. FAQ

How many Gold Coin Fruit do Red Fox and Black Cat need?

Use three for the clean route: one after Champion Victor for Quixotic and Beg, one to spare Black Cat in Arche Abbey, and one to spare Red Fox later.

Is Quixotic missable?

Yes. It comes from giving Gold Coin Fruit to Red Fox and Black Cat at the Grand Exhibition exit after Champion Victor. If you leave that route unresolved, plan NG+ recovery.

Do you have to fight Black Cat?

No. If the friendly route is available and you give him Gold Coin Fruit in Arche Abbey, he gives Black Cat's Mask and steps aside.

Do you have to fight Red Fox?

No, if Black Cat was spared and the peaceful option is available. Give Red Fox another Gold Coin Fruit to receive Red Fox's Mask without fighting.

Do killing them or sparing them change major endings?

This route is mainly about NPC story outcome, Quixotic, Beg, masks, and optional fights. It is not one of the main Geppetto / Sophia ending switches, but missing Quixotic can affect record completion planning.
