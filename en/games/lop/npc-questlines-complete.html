1. Quick Answer

Short Version

For a completion-minded Lies of P run, check every NPC after each major boss, finish current-area side quests before killing the area's boss, and treat Cecile, Adelina, Belle, Antonia, Eugenie, and Alidoro as the highest-risk questlines. Many NPC rewards are records, gestures, Humanity choices, shop unlocks, or story achievements.

The safest pattern is simple: talk to everyone at Hotel Krat, revisit active field NPCs before the next boss, listen to new records fully, and avoid speaking to Eugenie after Alidoro's late-game reveal until Venigni has decrypted the related vessel and you have read the result.

4. Chronological Safe Route

1Elysion Boulevard: finish Weeping Woman and Toma before treating the early city as complete.
2Cathedral: speak to Sister Cecile, retrieve the Holy Mark, and return it before finishing the Archbishop sequence.
3Hotel Krat midgame: keep Venigni, Pulcinella, Polendina, Antonia, and Eugenie dialogue updated after every major boss.
4Rosa Isabelle Street and Opera House: finish Julian, Old Lady at the Window, and Adelina before moving too far past the King of Puppets arc.
5Grand Exhibition to Station return: recruit Belle when available, then complete her partner request during the later Krat Central Station return.
6Barren Swamp onward: keep visiting Broken Puppet as new gestures unlock; do not leave all gesture cleanup until the final sprint.
7Late game: handle Alidoro, Venigni, and Eugenie in the correct order: vessel first, decryption second, reading third, Eugenie last.
8Before NG+: check Hotel Krat one final time for records, letters, shop stock, and quest rewards.

5. Highest-Risk Questlines

Sister Cecile

Do her request while the Cathedral route is active. If you push the Archbishop sequence without returning the Holy Mark, you risk losing the clean record / gesture route.

Adelina

The Bright Red Apple step belongs before the King of Puppets outcome. Buy the apple from Pulcinella when available and give it to Adelina before clearing the Opera House arc.

Belle

Belle's quest has an early recruitment window and a later Atkinson step. If you want the record route, do not skip her Grand Exhibition interaction.

Eugenie and Alidoro

This is the easiest late questline to mishandle. After obtaining Alidoro's Cryptic Vessel, do not talk to Eugenie first. Decrypt it with Venigni, read it, then return to her.

Polendina and Antonia

Their story depends on midgame Hotel Krat dialogue and cure-related decisions. Keep checking Polendina and Antonia whenever the hotel state changes.

8. FAQ

Which Lies of P NPC questline is easiest to miss?

Sister Cecile, Adelina, Belle, and Eugenie / Alidoro are the biggest risk points because they depend on timing, boss progression, or reading a decrypted item before final dialogue.

Do NPC questlines affect Humanity?

Yes. Some NPC dialogue choices can grant Humanity, and many questlines reward records. Playing records fully at Hotel Krat is one of the main ways to support Humanity and Golden Lie progress.

Can missed NPC quest rewards be recovered?

Some small rewards can be ignored without much damage, but missed records, gestures, and story achievements usually require NG+ or another playthrough.

What should I do after each major boss?

Return to Hotel Krat, talk to every NPC, give Pulcinella any supply boxes, ask Venigni to decrypt vessels, check Eugenie and Polendina, and listen to newly obtained records.

Why is Eugenie's questline risky?

The late Alidoro reveal depends on the order of actions. For the safest completion path, obtain Alidoro's Cryptic Vessel, decrypt it with Venigni, read it fully, and only then talk to Eugenie.
