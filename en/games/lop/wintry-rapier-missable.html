1. Quick Answer

Short Version

Wintry Rapier is safe if you either choose Path of the Bastard: Dexterity at the start or buy Wintry Rapier from the Cerasani Alley Wandering Merchant before Parade Master for 300 Ergo. If you skip that merchant window, the practical recovery is NG+.

This is one of the easiest normal weapons to overlook because it appears before the first real boss. If you are doing Normal Weapon Collector, buy all starter weapons from the merchant before fighting Parade Master instead of assuming they will stay available forever.

Collector Warning

Do not leave Cerasani Alley with only your chosen starting weapon if you care about normal weapon completion. The other starter weapons are cheap, and this is the cleanest time to buy them.

3. Exact Early Route

1Start the game: if you want Wintry Rapier immediately, choose Combat Memory: Path of the Bastard.
2Reach Cerasani Alley: progress through Krat Central Station and the plaza route until you arrive at the Stargazer before Parade Master.
3Find the Wandering Merchant: he stands near the Cerasani Alley Stargazer before the Parade Master arena.
4Buy Wintry Rapier: spend 300 Ergo for the weapon if it is not your starter. For collection, buy the other two starter weapons as well.
5Confirm inventory: check that Wintry Rapier's Blade and Wintry Rapier's Handle are both available before pushing deeper into the story.

6. If You Missed It

If the Cerasani Alley merchant is no longer available and Wintry Rapier is not in your inventory, do not waste time searching late-game shops for a guaranteed current-cycle backup. Put it on the NG+ checklist and buy it immediately when you return to Cerasani Alley in the next cycle.

NG+ Reminder

When the new cycle starts, rush only as far as Cerasani Alley, talk to the merchant, and buy the missing starter weapon before Parade Master.

8. FAQ

Is Wintry Rapier missable?

Yes, if you did not choose Path of the Bastard and you skip the Cerasani Alley merchant before he disappears later. Buy it early for the cleanest route.

Where do you buy Wintry Rapier?

Buy it from the Wandering Merchant near the Cerasani Alley Stargazer before the Parade Master boss. It costs 300 Ergo.

Can you start with Wintry Rapier?

Yes. Choose Combat Memory: Path of the Bastard: Dexterity at the start of the game.

Can you get Wintry Rapier after missing the merchant?

The practical recovery is NG+. Buy it from the same early merchant when the next cycle reaches Cerasani Alley.

Do you need Wintry Rapier for normal weapon collection?

If your goal is all normal weapons, yes. Treat all three starter weapons as early collector items, even if you only plan to fight with one of them.
