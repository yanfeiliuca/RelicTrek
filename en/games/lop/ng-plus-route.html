1. Quick Answer

Short Version

Use NG+ to collect or recover rewards your current cycle did not cover: boss Ergo amulets after buying weapons, Golden Records, alternate endings, missed Eugenie / Alidoro branch cleanup on older or missed-route saves, missed Golden Lie or Humanity routes, and another full set of Quartz. Do not start NG+ until you finish post-ending Hotel Krat cleanup.

New Game Plus starts the story over after an ending while keeping most build progress and equipment. It also raises enemy difficulty, increases Ergo gain, adds stronger item versions, and opens additional P-Organ growth. The real danger is not combat - it is accepting the NG+ prompt before you have checked letters, records, boss Ergo trades, Golden Lie, and ending rewards.

4. NG+ Priority Checklist

1Early Cerasani Alley: buy the first NG+ Golden Record before Parade Master if you are doing Golden Melody cleanup.
2Boss Ergo plan: if Run 1 bought weapons, use Run 2 for amulet alternatives. If Run 1 consumed Ergo, keep every Rare Ergo this time.
3Humanity route: decide early whether this cycle targets Rise of P, Golden Lie, or a lower-Humanity ending branch.
4Records: buy Golden Records as soon as vendors appear and use NG+ to recover any Eugenie / Alidoro branch you missed on an older or unfinished route.
5Quartz and P-Organ: collect another set of Quartz and use new P-Organ phases to smooth late-game combat.
6Ending cleanup: pick the ending that your first run did not cover, then delay the next NG+ until post-ending cleanup is done again.

7. Common Mistakes

Accepting NG+ immediately

The post-credits prompt is not a checklist. Return to Hotel Krat first if you need letters, records, Golden Lie, boss Ergo trades, or late shops.

Forgetting Golden Record vendors

NG+ records are easy to miss because some vendors appear early. Carry Ergo and buy them immediately.

Repeating the same ending route

Before starting the cycle, decide which ending, Humanity branch, and Geppetto choice you are targeting.

Spending Rare Ergo casually

NG+ gives another chance, but each cycle still gives one Rare Ergo per boss. Keep the trade plan visible.

9. FAQ

When does NG+ unlock in Lies of P?

NG+ unlocks after reaching an ending. You can accept the post-credits prompt immediately or return to Hotel Krat and start NG+ later from a Stargazer.

What carries over to NG+?

Level, stats, weapons, amulets, defense parts, Legion Arms, most materials, Ergo, records, gestures, costumes, and P-Organ progress carry over. Story keys and shop-expansion quest items reset.

Should I start NG+ immediately?

No. Check Hotel Krat, letters, piano, Golden Lie, records, late shops, and boss Ergo trades first.

Can NG+ recover consumed boss Ergo?

Yes. Defeat the same boss again in NG+ and keep the new Rare Ergo instead of consuming it.

Do records carry over?

Records carry over, and NG+ adds Golden Record vendor opportunities. Current patched versions also reduce the need to repeat a cycle for formerly exclusive branch records, but NG+ remains the clean recovery route for old saves, missed NPC steps, or alternate endings.
