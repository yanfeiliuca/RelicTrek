1. Quick Answer

Counting Rule

The checked base-game costume sources list 22 costume pieces: 14 outfits and 8 accessories. Several are earned as sets, so the route table has fewer rows than the piece count. Costumes are cosmetic and do not add combat stats.

This page is scoped to the base game. Lies of P: Overture adds separate DLC costume content, but I am not mixing DLC pieces into the base-game count until a dedicated Overture costume pass verifies every pickup.

4. Missable and Conflict Notes

Red Fox Mask vs Peaceful Route

The checked base costume list awards Red Fox's Mask for killing Red Fox. If you prefer the peaceful Red Fox and Black Cat route, put the mask cleanup on another cycle.

Frozen Feast Requirement

Red Fox's Hunting Apparel requires the Mechanical Cryptic Vessel route and Frozen Feast equipped at the merchant. If you traded Reborn Champion's Ergo for the amulet first, this route may need NG+.

Trinity Chain Dependency

Alchemist's Cape depends on the final Trinity Sanctum progression, so do not skip phone booths and prior Sanctums.

5. Overture DLC Note

Overture sources list additional DLC outfit and accessory content. Because this page is a base-game checklist, those pieces are not counted inside the 22 base pieces above. The safe next step is a separate Overture costume table after checking every DLC pickup against current DLC sources.

Why Separate Counts

Mixing base and DLC costumes makes the route harder to follow and can create wrong completion expectations for players who own only the base game.

7. FAQ

How many costumes are in base Lies of P?

The checked base-game costume source counts 22 costume pieces: 14 outfits and 8 accessories.

Do costumes change stats?

No. They are cosmetic and can be changed from the equipment costume tab.

Are all costumes available in one run?

Most base pieces can be routed in one run, but Red Fox's Mask conflicts with a peaceful Red Fox route, and Frozen Feast planning can affect Red Fox's Hunting Apparel.

Does this include Overture costumes?

No. Overture adds separate costume content, but this page keeps the base-game count separate until a dedicated DLC costume sweep is done.
