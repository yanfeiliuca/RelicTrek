1. Quick Answer

Exact Route

Black Steel Cutter is a normal greatsword found in Hermit's Cave, not an Alidoro boss-Ergo trade. Unlock Hermit's Cave through the Rusty Cryptic Vessel route, reach the Hermit's Cave Stargazer, then loot the chest near the large Carcass / elite enemy route to obtain the Black Steel Cutter Blade and Black Steel Cutter Handle.

Its main identity is a fire-damage blade with strong Advance scaling. Use it when you want a normal weapon that can be assembled with other handles, unlike fixed special boss weapons.

3. Chest Route Inside Hermit's Cave

1Open Hermit's Cave: complete the Rusty Cryptic Vessel route and enter from the Barren Swamp Bridge gate.
2Reach the Stargazer: progress into the cave until the Hermit's Cave Stargazer is active.
3Drop down from the Stargazer area: checked route descriptions place the weapon shortly after this point, below or near the Stargazer path.
4Watch for the large Carcass / elite enemy: the chest is near the enemy guarding or hiding by the wooden structure / scaffolding route.
5Open the chest: collect both Black Steel Cutter Blade and Black Steel Cutter Handle.
Cleanup Nearby

Hermit's Cave also contains other completion rewards, including Misty E'Ra and Black Cat's Hunting Apparel / Quartz near the later cave route. Use All Records and All Quartz before leaving the area for good.

6. Common Mistakes

Searching Alidoro's Trade Menu

Black Steel Cutter is not a Rare Ergo trade. Route Hermit's Cave instead.

Skipping the Rusty Cryptic Vessel

If Hermit's Cave is still closed, you likely have not completed the Special Krat Supply Box / Rusty Cryptic Vessel chain.

Ignoring Source Differences

Do not overbuild around a single listed base physical number. The consistent facts are Fire damage and Advance scaling.

8. FAQ

Where do you get Black Steel Cutter?

In Hermit's Cave, from a chest near the large Carcass / elite enemy route after the cave is unlocked.

Is Black Steel Cutter a boss weapon?

No. It is a normal weapon made of Black Steel Cutter Blade and Black Steel Cutter Handle.

Do I need Alidoro for Black Steel Cutter?

No. The route goes through Rookie Explorer Hugo, Polendina's shop expansion, Venigni's decryption, and Hermit's Cave.

Is Black Steel Cutter good for Advance builds?

Yes, if you want a fire weapon with Advance scaling. Keep handle scaling in mind when assembling it with other parts.
