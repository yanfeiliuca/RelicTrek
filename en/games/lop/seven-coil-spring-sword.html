1. Quick Answer

Exact Route

Defeat Parade Master in Krat Central Station Plaza, keep Parade Leader's Ergo, then trade that Rare Ergo with Alidoro or the later base-game boss-weapon trader route for the Seven-Coil Spring Sword. Do not consume the Ergo for currency if you want the weapon in this cycle.

The Seven-Coil Spring Sword is an early special greatsword. It favors Motivity more than Technique, cannot be disassembled, and competes directly with Dancing One's Amulet for the same Rare Ergo.

3. Route

1Beat Parade Master: finish the Krat Central Station Plaza boss and keep Parade Leader's Ergo in your inventory.
2Do not cash it in: Rare Ergo can be consumed for Ergo, but this is the main mistake that blocks the weapon for the current cycle.
3Unlock the boss trader: meet Alidoro, then open the special weapon trade menu.
4Choose the sword: exchange Parade Leader's Ergo for Seven-Coil Spring Sword instead of Dancing One's Amulet if your goal is weapon completion.
5Upgrade only if you will use it: special weapons use Covenant moonstones. Test the moveset before spending limited upgrade materials.

6. Common Mistakes

Consuming Parade Leader's Ergo

This is the main lockout. If you already consumed it, route the sword for NG+.

Expecting a world pickup

The sword is a boss Ergo trade, not a chest or hidden floor item.

Trying to disassemble it

Special weapons are fixed. Use Best Weapon Combos for blade-handle assembly pages instead.

8. FAQ

Where is the Seven-Coil Spring Sword?

It is obtained from the boss-weapon trader by exchanging Parade Leader's Ergo. It is not found as a regular pickup.

Should I choose Seven-Coil Spring Sword or Dancing One's Amulet?

Choose the sword first if your goal is special weapon collection. Choose the amulet only if you already know you value its effect more than collection progress.

Can I get it after consuming Parade Leader's Ergo?

Not in the same cycle from the sources checked. Put it on your NG+ route.

Can the Seven-Coil Spring Sword be disassembled?

No. It is a fixed special weapon, so its blade and handle cannot be separated.
