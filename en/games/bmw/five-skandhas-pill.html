Five Skandhas Pill — The Permanent Stat Boost Hidden in Plain Sight Across All Six Chapters

1. Why You Need It

There's a big reward waiting for players who find all five Skandhas — these optional collectibles don't do anything until you've collected all five, but that prize really is worth it. You'll earn a single Celestial Pill that boosts your Health, Stamina, Mana and more by 10–20%. That's a massive upgrade, but you'll only be able to claim it near the end of the game.

The problem is that most players never piece it together. The Skandhas are a strange collectible — you scavenge them off broken Buddha heads. Until you have all five of them on hand, these items are essentially useless. There are five of them, scattered across chapters that span the entire game. The fifth one can only be obtained by possessing the first four — they're the key to spawning a hidden superboss in Chapter 6 who drops it.

The Five Skandhas Pill gives +20 to every affected stat — Health, Mana, Stamina, Attack, and Defense. That's a +20 boost to everything simultaneously, from a single item, that persists for the rest of the playthrough and into New Game+.

2. What the Five Skandhas Pill Does

Item: Five Skandhas Pill (consumable — use immediately on receipt) · Effect: Permanently boosts all core attributes.

This is equivalent to approximately 5–6 attribute investment points in each stat category — given for free, permanently.

3. Prerequisites

  • Progress through Chapters 1–4 naturally (one Skandha per chapter)
  • Complete Chapter 2 Shigandang optional boss (requires 6 Buddha's Eyeballs)
  • Unlock Zodiac Village (complete Chen Loong's questline in Chapter 3) OR locate Xu Dog in Chapter 2's Cellar Shrine
  • Reach Chapter 6 with all four previous Skandhas to spawn Giant Shigandang

4. All Five Skandha Locations

Each Skandha sits in a broken Buddha head statue. Four of five are straightforward pickups — the second and fifth require boss fights.

Skandha of Form — Chapter 1 (Forest of Wolves)

From the very first shrine, go forward over the wooden bridge into the forest area. Look to the front left corner for a buddha-face rock — it sits just before the Bullguard boss arena. No combat required; simply walk up and collect it.

Skandha of Feeling — Chapter 2 (Yellow Wind Ridge → Shigandang)

This one has the most steps of the first four.

1Collect all 6 Buddha's Eyeballs scattered across Fright Cliff.
2Travel to Rockrest Flat Shrine.
3Turn left into the Stone Vanguard arena and defeat the Stone Vanguard.
4Insert all 6 Eyeballs into the large rock to summon Shigandang.
5Defeat Shigandang to receive the Skandha of Feeling + Heart of Stone.
💡 Note

Shigandang is substantially harder than Stone Vanguard. Prepare for heavy ground-slam attacks. Immobilize creates reliable damage windows. Pillar Stance lets you climb above the ground slams.

Skandha of Thought — Chapter 3 (Valley of Ecstasy)

From the Mindfulness Cliff shrine, head forward to the small wooden plank bridge and drop down. Follow the path up the stairs into a cave, past the rock-carrying enemies. Continue forward, then go right toward the snowy cliffside. Pass through a small cave, then climb the stairs leading to the third statue head. No boss encounter.

Skandha of Choice — Chapter 4 (Webbed Hollow)

From the Purifying Spring shrine, enter the large cavern. Drop into the dark water pond at the center, then turn left. The spider-cocoon-covered shore holds the statue head. No boss encounter — just navigate the dark pond carefully.

Skandha of Consciousness — Chapter 6 (Foothills)

⚠ Conditional Spawn

Giant Shigandang will NOT spawn unless you hold all four previous Skandhas. If you reach Chapter 6 without them, this Skandha becomes permanently unobtainable.

From the Foothills — Verdant Path Shrine, use Somersault Cloud to fly north. Take a right turn into the valley and follow it to a giant rock formation (opposite the waterfall cave). Giant Shigandang will spawn here — but only if you carry all four previous Skandhas. Defeat him, then climb his arm to collect the final Skandha of Consciousness. This is the hardest encounter in the entire questline.

5. Collecting the Pill + Hazard Guide

Turning In the Skandhas

With Zodiac Village unlocked: Return to Xu Dog at Zodiac Village and deliver all five Skandhas. He'll craft the Five Skandhas Pill on the spot. Without Zodiac Village: Find Xu Dog at the Cellar Shrine in Chapter 2. Either way, consume the pill immediately upon receipt — the stat boost is permanent from that moment forward.

Shigandang (Chapter 2) — Boss Hazards

⚔️ Shigandang Threats

Harder than Stone Vanguard by a wide margin. Stone-type enemy with devastating ground slams that cover large areas. Strategy: Use Immobilize to create reliable damage windows. Switch to Pillar Stance — climbing the pillar lets you rise above the ground-slam hitboxes entirely. Keep your distance between Immobilize cooldowns and punish during the locked-down windows.

Giant Shigandang (Chapter 6) — Boss Hazards

🔴 Giant Shigandang Threats

This is an endgame-tier superboss. Come with fully upgraded weapons and your best armor set. Cloud Step is invaluable — it buys you breathing room when the boss chains massive area attacks. Target the glowing weak points on its body to maximize damage. Do not attempt this fight underleveled; treat it as a Chapter 6 endgame challenge.

Permanent Miss Risk

⚠ Critical Warning

Complete this questline before the Chapter 6 final boss. If you advance past the point of no return without collecting all five Skandhas, the Giant Shigandang cannot spawn and the Skandha of Consciousness becomes permanently unobtainable for that playthrough. You'll need a full NG+ cycle to try again.
