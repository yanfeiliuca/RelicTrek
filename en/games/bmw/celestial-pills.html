All 15 Celestial Pills — The Permanent Stat Upgrades Scattered Across Every Chapter

1. Why You Need Them

Each chapter in Black Myth: Wukong hides exactly three Celestial Pills, scattered in small golden containers off the main path. They are easy to ride past at full sprint — and once you move to the next chapter, backtracking ranges from inconvenient to impossible.

Each pill type corresponds to a single stat:

  • Jade Lotus Pill — permanently increases Health by +15.
  • Taiyi Pill — permanently increases Mana by +15.
  • Nonary Pill — permanently increases Stamina by +15.

One of each type per chapter, across Chapters 1 through 5. Collect all 15 and you gain +75 Health, +75 Mana, +75 Stamina — a permanent baseline advantage no armor or weapon can replicate.

Most are found in golden pill containers tucked into dead ends, behind barricades, or along climbable scaffold paths. Two require substantial side quests: the Chapter 4 Stamina Pill is locked behind the Purple Talisman questline, and the Chapter 5 Stamina Pill sits deep inside Bishui Cave after a twin-boss encounter.

Chapter 6 has no Celestial Pills — but the Five Skandhas Pill at the very end of the game upgrades Health, Mana, and Stamina simultaneously by +20 each.

💡 Xu Dog's Crafted Pills

Xu Dog can craft pills with Mind Cores that provide the same stat bonuses as Celestial Pills. However, crafted pills do not count toward the "Medicine Meal" achievement — only the 15 natural Celestial Pills do.

2. All 15 Locations by Chapter

Chapter 1 — Black Wind Mountain

❤️ Jade Lotus (Health) — Guanyin Temple

From the Guanyin Temple Shrine, head up the stairs toward the temple proper. After passing Lingxuzi's arena, check the left side of the courtyard for a golden container.

💙 Taiyi (Mana) — Outside the Cave

From Outside the Cave Shrine, proceed forward across the wooden bridge (watch for Wolf Archers above). Past the Bullguard enemy, the pill container sits on the right side of the path before the next shrine.

🏃 Nonary (Stamina) — Bodhi Peak

Also from Outside the Cave Shrine, ascend the wooden scaffolding toward Bodhi Peak. At the peak, go left under the temple structure — the golden container is tucked against the cliff wall.

Chapter 2 — Yellow Wind Ridge

❤️ Jade Lotus (Health) — Tiger Temple Cave

From the Tiger Temple Entrance Shrine, U-turn and enter the cave behind you. Follow the right wall to a barricaded door. Break through and descend the tunnel to a dead-end — the golden container waits there.

💙 Taiyi (Mana) — Sandgate Village / Valley of Despair

Between Sandgate Village and Valley of Despair, check the side paths branching off the main route. The container is partially hidden behind a rock formation near a kneeling statue.

🏃 Nonary (Stamina) — Valley of Despair Cave

From the Valley of Despair Shrine, head right into the cave entrance. Pass two enemies, then drop down the ledge. The golden container sits in the left corner of the lower chamber.

Chapter 3 — The New West

❤️ Jade Lotus (Health) — Turtle Island Cliffs

From Turtle Island Shrine, head left toward the cliffs. Jump down twice to reach a hidden pathway along the cliff face. The golden container is at the end of this path.

💙 Taiyi (Mana) — Valley of Ecstasy

In the Valley of Ecstasy area, look near the Golden Lotus Pond. The pill container rests on a small stone platform at the water's edge, partially obscured by lotus leaves.

🏃 Nonary (Stamina) — New Thunderclap Temple

From the New Thunderclap Temple Entrance Shrine, enter the Side Hall. To the right of the main staircase, head outside to the frozen pond. The golden container sits on the far bank, near a broken pillar.

Chapter 4 — The Webbed Hollow

❤️ Jade Lotus (Health) — Estate of the Zhu

In the Village of Lanxi, enter the Estate of the Zhu. At the back of the main courtyard, find the altar before dropping into the pit — the golden container sits beside the altar.

💙 Taiyi (Mana) — Cliff of Oblivion

After defeating the Buddha's Right Hand boss, proceed from the Cliff of Oblivion Shrine. Cross the corpse bridge, and immediately look left — the golden container is wedged between two rock formations.

🏃 Nonary (Stamina) — Purple Talisman Questline Reward

This pill is not found in a container — it is a quest reward for completing the Purple Talisman questline, the same chain that unlocks the Chapter 4 secret area. See our Purple Talismans Guide for the full route.

Chapter 5 — Flaming Mountains

❤️ Jade Lotus (Health) — Woods of Ember

In the Woods of Ember area, follow the main route. After crossing the second lava stream, check behind a collapsed stone archway on the left — the golden container is there.

💙 Taiyi (Mana) — Furnace Valley / Field of Fire

Between Furnace Valley and Field of Fire, the main path passes a series of volcanic vents. The golden container sits beside the third vent, partially behind a charred tree.

🏃 Nonary (Stamina) — Bishui Cave

After defeating the twin bosses on the upper floor of Bishui Cave, take the first alcove on the left. The golden container is against the back wall. See our Bishui Cave Guide for the full route.

Bonus — Chapter 6

🌟 Five Skandhas Pill (+20 All Stats)

Chapter 6 has no Celestial Pills, but the Five Skandhas Pill — obtained by collecting all five Skandha items across Chapters 1–4, 5, and 6 and giving them to Xu Dog — permanently increases all three stats by +20 simultaneously. See our Five Skandhas Pill Guide for full collection details.

⚠️ Two Pills Require Side Quests

The Ch.4 Stamina pill requires the full Purple Talisman chain. The Ch.5 Stamina pill sits inside Bishui Cave — both areas are part of their respective chapter's secret area questlines. Do not skip these.

🌟 Five Skandhas Pill (Ch.6)

Chapter 6 has no Celestial Pills, but the Five Skandhas Pill (+20 Health, +20 Mana, +20 Stamina) is obtained by collecting all five Skandha items and giving them to Xu Dog at the Zodiac Village.
