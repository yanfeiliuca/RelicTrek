1. Why You Need It

Chapter 6 brings you back to Mount Huaguo — the birthplace of Sun Wukong and the final chapter of Black Myth: Wukong. Before you can face the final boss, the mountain demands one last trial: collecting the four pieces of Sun Wukong's legendary armor.

Each piece belongs to a different boss scattered across the mountain. The Gold Suozi Armor is held by the Gold Armored Rhino. The Golden Feng-Tail Crown is wielded by Feng-Tail General. The Dian-Cui Loong-Soaring Bracers are guarded by the Cloudtreading Deer. And the leg armor rests with the Emerald-Armed Mantis in Mantis-Catching Swamp.

Collect all four, equip the entire set, and travel to the giant staff embedded in the earth — and the Jingubang (Ruyi Jingu Bang), Sun Wukong's true staff, rises to meet its rightful wearer. The 5-piece set bonus (all four armor pieces + Jingubang) slightly reduces the cooldown of all spells whenever you land a critical hit. You cannot reach the final boss without this armor set — it is story-gated.

The Jingubang does not appear automatically after collecting the armor. You must travel to its specific location on Mount Huaguo with all four pieces equipped. If even one piece is unequipped, the staff will not activate. There are also hidden SECRET set versions of the armor in golden chests, offering an alternate look for the full set.

3. The Four Armor Pieces — Locations

Before hunting armor, complete the early Chapter 6 prerequisite: defeat the Supreme Inspector in a full boss fight. This unlocks the Somersault Cloud spell, which is required to traverse Mount Huaguo's open world and reach all four armor bosses.

Piece 1 — Gold Suozi Armor (Chest)

Gold Armored Rhino

Located northeast of the Foothills region. This boss relies on charging attacks — keep Pillar Stance charged heavies ready to interrupt his charges. Fire resistance helps; his attacks deal incidental fire damage. After defeat, claim the Gold Suozi Armor.

⚔️ Charging attacks🛡️ Fire resistance

Piece 2 — Golden Feng-Tail Crown (Head)

Feng-Tail General

Found in the upper areas of Mount Huaguo, accessible only with Somersault Cloud. This boss attacks with magic projectiles — use Cloud Step to close distance and Pillar Stance for overhead pressure. After defeat, claim the Golden Feng-Tail Crown.

⚔️ Magic attacks☁️ Cloud Step

Piece 3 — Dian-Cui Loong-Soaring Bracers (Arms)

Cloudtreading Deer

Roams the open Foothills area. This boss is extremely fast — swift running attacks that can outpace your sprint. Use Immobilize to lock it down, then burst with charged heavies. Do not chase it on foot; wait for it to charge toward you and counter. After defeat, claim the Dian-Cui Loong-Soaring Bracers.

⚔️ Swift running🔒 Immobilize

Piece 4 — Lotus Silk Cloud-Treading Boots (Legs)

Emerald-Armed Mantis

Found in Mantis-Catching Swamp. The fastest multi-hit attacker among the four armor bosses — its scythe strikes come in rapid flurries. Pillar Stance is highly effective: the vertical reach keeps you above its horizontal slashes. Dodge the initial flurry, then punish during its recovery window. After defeat, claim the leg armor.

⚔️ Fast multi-hit🏛️ Pillar Stance

4. Getting the Jingubang

Once all four armor pieces are collected and equipped, follow this route to claim Sun Wukong's true staff.

1Equip all four armor pieces. Verify every slot is filled — head, chest, arms, legs — with the Wukong set pieces. Even one mismatch prevents the Jingubang from activating.
2Fast travel to the Mantis-Catching Swamp Keeper's Shrine.
3From the shrine, use Somersault Cloud to fly directly upward. Look for a massive waterfall on the mountain face.
4Fly toward the waterfall and drop down behind it into the interior cave. This is a hidden chamber — there is no map marker.
5Inside, approach Sun Wukong's throne. A cutscene triggers automatically. The Jingubang rises from the stone and becomes your weapon.
🏆 Jingubang Reward

Jingubang (Ruyi Jingu Bang) — Mythical rarity staff. Unique moveset with extended reach. 4th Focus Point gauge does not deplete over time. Part of the 5-piece Wukong armor set bonus.

💡 Usage Note

The Jingubang is restricted during the final boss fight (you fight an alternate version of yourself wielding it), but it is excellent for all optional bosses, secret areas, and NG+ runs. Its extended reach and permanent Focus Point retention make it one of the most powerful weapons in the game.

5. Secret Armor & True Ending Connection

Secret Armor — Golden Chest Variant

There is a second, hidden version of Sun Wukong's armor set — identical in stats but with a different appearance — found in small golden chests scattered across the game. One is located in Chapter 6.

1Fast travel to the Verdant Path Keeper's Shrine in the Foothills region of Chapter 6.
2From the shrine, turn left 90 degrees.
3Use Somersault Cloud to fly toward the mountain visible at the end of the open path ahead.
4Land on the mountain ledge — you will find a golden chest containing a piece of the secret Sun Wukong armor set.

This is an open-world secret. There is no quest marker, no NPC hint, and no shrine pointing you toward it. You must explore Mount Huaguo freely with the Somersault Cloud to find it — exactly the kind of hidden reward RelicTrek exists to document.

True Ending Connection

⚠ Do Not Skip the Armor

Collecting all four armor pieces and claiming the Jingubang is a mandatory step for the True Ending. The armor set and Jingubang are required to access the Grand Pagoda secret fight against Erlang Shen. If you finish Chapter 6 without completing this set, you will be locked into the default ending for that playthrough. Complete the armor collection before approaching the final boss.
