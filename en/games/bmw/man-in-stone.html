Man-in-Stone — The Chapter 2 Merchant Hiding Inside a Rock

1. Why You Need It

Northeast of the Squall Hideout Shrine in Fright Cliff, there is a ravine tucked away off the main path. Inside it, a man is trapped inside a boulder — cursed and imprisoned by the Mother of Stones. He has been waiting, unable to move, for someone to break the curse.

Freeing him requires two fights: first, you must track down and defeat the Mother of Stones to obtain the Stone Essence needed to break his prison. Then, once freed, he challenges you to a duel — he tests whether you are truly worthy. Beat him, and two things happen that change the rest of your playthrough.

First, you earn the Azure Dust Transformation — the Rock Guai form, which grants exceptionally high defense at the cost of slower movement. Second, after you rest at a shrine to reload the area, the Man-in-Stone becomes a permanent merchant selling items that are unavailable anywhere else in the game. Among them is the Sobering Stone, a quest item required to complete the Drunk Pig questline and unlock the Wind Tamer vessel — and through it, the Chapter 2 secret area.

2. What You Get — Transformation & Merchant Inventory

Azure Dust Transformation: Rock Guai form · High defense, slow movement · Absorbs hits that would stagger other forms · Ideal for tanking boss phase transitions.

ItemTypeEffect
Sobering StoneQuest ItemRequired for Drunk Pig questline — use it to wake him and unlock the Wind Tamer vessel, which opens the Chapter 2 secret area (Kingdom of Sahali)
Goldflora HairpinCurioIncreases Will dropped by defeated enemies — invaluable for farming currency throughout the game
Iron PelletCurioReduces incoming damage — a universally useful defensive passive for any build

All three items are exclusive to the Man-in-Stone shop. You cannot buy them from any other merchant, and they do not drop from any enemy. If you want the Sobering Stone to access the Chapter 2 secret area, you must complete this questline.

3. Prerequisites & Route

Step 1 — Find the Man-in-Stone

1Fast travel to the Squall Hideout Shrine in Fright Cliff (Chapter 2). From the shrine, head northeast into the ravine — it is tucked off the main path, so watch for a narrow side passage. Inside, you will find a boulder with a man's face embedded in it. Exhaust his dialogue completely — he tells you of the Mother of Stones and the curse.

Step 2 — Defeat the Mother of Stones

2Fast travel to the Rockrest Flat Shrine. From the shrine, backtrack into the cave behind it — do not follow the main path forward. Inside the cave, take the slope up to the right. You will reach the Mother of Stones arena. She does not attack directly; instead, she summons waves of minions. Kill all minions first, then attack the Mother of Stones until she drops the Stone Essence.

Step 3 — Free the Man and Duel Him

3Return to the Man-in-Stone in the Squall Hideout ravine. Offer him the Stone Essence. The boulder shatters and he steps out — but he immediately challenges you to a duel. He uses slow, heavy rock-slam attacks. Stay to his side — his frontal slams have wide hitboxes but leave his flanks exposed. Immobilize works well to lock him down for charged heavies. Defeat him to earn the Azure Dust Transformation.

Step 4 — Unlock the Merchant

4After the duel, rest at any shrine to reload the area. Return to the Man-in-Stone's location — he is now a friendly merchant. His shop sells the Sobering Stone, Goldflora Hairpin, and Iron Pellet. Buy the Sobering Stone immediately if you plan to do the Drunk Pig questline.

Step 5 — Spend Your Will

5Buy the Goldflora Hairpin (+Will drops) and Iron Pellet (damage reduction) as soon as you can afford them. The Hairpin pays for itself over time — the earlier you buy it, the more bonus Will you accumulate across the rest of the playthrough.

4. Hazard Guide

Mother of Stones — Navigation Warning

⚠ Easy to Miss the Cave

The Mother of Stones is located in a cave behind the Rockrest Flat Shrine, not ahead of it. Most players continue forward along the main path and never find her. From Rockrest Flat, turn around and go back the way you came — the cave entrance is behind you.

⚠ Minions First, Then Mother

The Mother of Stones does not fight you directly. She summons waves of rock minions. Killing all minions is the only way to damage her. Focus on clearing each wave quickly — the fight is about endurance, not burst damage.

Man-in-Stone Duel

⚔ Stay Flank, Dodge Slams

The Man-in-Stone's attacks are slow but hit hard. Circle to his side or back after each slam — his recovery is long enough for a charged heavy. Immobilize him and dump your biggest combos. Do not stand directly in front of him; his ground-pound has a deceptively wide shockwave.

Merchant Activation

⚠ Must Rest at Shrine

After the duel, the Man-in-Stone will not become a merchant immediately. You must rest at a shrine to reload the area, then return to his location. If you leave and never return, you lose access to the Sobering Stone — and with it, the Chapter 2 secret area.

Everything the Man-in-Stone questline gives you, plus what those rewards unlock.

🎁 The Real Prize Is the Sobering Stone

The Sobering Stone is the only item in the game that can wake the Drunk Pig in Chapter 2. Without it, you cannot complete the Drunk Pig questline, which means you cannot obtain the Wind Tamer vessel, which means you cannot access the Chapter 2 secret area (Kingdom of Sahali) or fight Fuban. Completing the Man-in-Stone questline is a prerequisite for the true ending.
