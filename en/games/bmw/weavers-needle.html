Weaver's Needle — The Chapter 4 Vessel Hidden Inside a Secret Area You Need to Unlock Twice

🕐 Route: ~1.5 hrs⚔️ Final boss: 3/5 (Duskveil)📍 Purple Cloud Mountain (via Venom Daoist double encounter)⚠️ Scorpionlord permanently missable📅 Updated: 2026-06-01

1. Why You Need It

In Chapter 4's Webbed Hollow, there is a boss called the Venom Daoist. He runs away the first time you defeat him. You have to find him again — in a different area — and defeat him a second time. Only then does he reveal the entrance to a hidden area called Purple Cloud Mountain.

The Weaver's Needle is one of the most powerful Vessels in the game. Its active ability provides a massive window of powerful defense that is particularly effective in Chapter 4's final boss encounter. Purple Cloud Mountain also contains Violet Hail transformation (from Daoist Mi's quest), the Scorpionlord (permanently missable if Duskveil is fought first), and a free-assist fight where Scorpionlord shows up during Duskveil's first phase to help you.

If you skip the double Venom Daoist encounter, you miss an entire hidden zone — three bosses, a Vessel, a transformation, and one of the most atmospheric areas in the game. The Weaver's Needle alone is worth the trek: against the chapter's final boss, Hundred-Eyed Daoist Master, its damage reduction turns a punishing multi-hit onslaught into a manageable fight.

2. What the Weaver's Needle Does

Type: Vessel · Passive: Sustained damage mitigation · Active (full Qi): Massive damage reduction — one of the strongest defensive bursts in the game

Synergy EncounterWhy It Works
Hundred-Eyed Daoist Master (Ch4 final boss)Multi-hit volleys are dramatically reduced — the Needle neutralizes his most dangerous phase
Any sustained multi-hit encounterThe active window is long enough to weather extended combo strings that would otherwise break your guard
Duskveil Phase 2Cursed blood attacks hit in rapid succession; the Needle's damage reduction stacks favorably against curse buildup

The Weaver's Needle does not need upgrades. It arrives fully powered the moment Duskveil falls. Equip it, fill your Qi, and activate it when a boss commits to a long attack chain — the damage reduction window is generous and the cooldown is reasonable.

3. Prerequisites — The Venom Daoist Chain

You must defeat the Venom Daoist twice, in two different locations. He flees after the first fight. The second encounter is what unlocks Purple Cloud Mountain.

Encounter 1 — Pool of Shattered Jade (Webbed Hollow)

1Fast travel to Upper Hollow Keeper's Shrine in Webbed Hollow.
2From the shrine, take the immediate left onto the web path. Follow it across.
3Drop down from the spider sac platform to the lower ledge. Enter the tunnel ahead.
4The tunnel leads to the Pool of Shattered Jade boss arena. Venom Daoist is waiting.
💡 Strategy

Venom Daoist has low HP in this encounter. Burst spells (Immobilize + charged heavy) are highly effective. He will flee when his health drops to a threshold — this is scripted, not a bug. Let him go.

Encounter 2 — Temple of Yellow Flowers

1Fast travel to Court of Illumination Keeper's Shrine — the last shrine before the Chapter 4 final boss.
2From the shrine, go forward through the temple entrance. Do NOT go right — that path leads to the final boss.
3Head southeast through the temple corridors to reach the second Venom Daoist arena.
4Defeat him a second time. Upon defeat, he reveals the entrance to Purple Cloud Mountain.
⚠️ Note

Encounter 2 Venom Daoist is slightly tougher but still a mid-tier boss. If you have reached Court of Illumination, your level and gear should be more than sufficient. The real challenge is what lies beyond the entrance he reveals.

4. Route — Purple Cloud Mountain (ORDER MATTERS)

Once you enter Purple Cloud Mountain, the sequence of bosses you fight determines what you keep — and what you lose forever.

Boss 1 — Daoist Mi (Optional, Recommended)

1Daoist Mi is the first boss you encounter in Purple Cloud Mountain. He is optional but strongly recommended.

Complete his questline to earn the Violet Hail transformation. This transformation grants immunity to poison — invaluable for the fights ahead, especially Scorpionlord. Daoist Mi himself is poison-infused, so bring antidote medicine or high poison resistance.

Boss 2 — Scorpionlord ⚠️ DO BEFORE DUSKVEIL

2The Scorpionlord is perched on the cliffs of Purple Cloud Mountain. Fight him before Duskveil.

Scorpionlord drops the Sky-Piercing Horn — a rare material with only 3 available per playthrough. He is fast, deals high damage, and his poison attacks can end the fight in seconds if you are unprepared. Violet Hail's poison immunity makes this fight significantly more manageable.

⚠️ PERMANENTLY MISSABLE

If you fight Duskveil before Scorpionlord, Scorpionlord will appear as a temporary ally during Duskveil's first phase — and then he is gone from the game forever. No Sky-Piercing Horn. No second chance. The order is: Daoist Mi → Scorpionlord → Duskveil.

Boss 3 — Duskveil (Final, Weaver's Needle Reward)

3Duskveil is the final boss of Purple Cloud Mountain. Defeat it to claim the Weaver's Needle.

Phase 1: Slow, telegraphed attacks. The arena is large — use the space. If you fought Scorpionlord first, he will appear as a temporary ally in this phase, dealing significant damage to Duskveil before falling.

Phase 2: Duskveil shifts to cursed blood attacks. These hit in rapid multi-hit strings and apply curse buildup. The Weaver's Needle — once you earn it — is tailor-made to counter exactly this attack pattern. For now, bring curse-resistance medicine and focus on dodging through the volleys rather than blocking.

🏆 Rewards

Weaver's Needle (Vessel) + Access to Chapter 4 secret area cleared (required for true ending)

5. Hazard Guide

Venom Daoist (Both Encounters)

Low HP, Flees Fast

Both encounters feature a Venom Daoist with relatively low HP. In Encounter 1, he flees at a health threshold — do not over-invest in long-cooldown abilities. Save your heavy hitters for Encounter 2, where the fight must be finished. Immobilize + charged heavy into a full light combo is usually enough to end either fight quickly.

Daoist Mi — Poison Management

☠️ Poison-Infused

Daoist Mi's attacks apply poison buildup. Equip poison-resistance curios before the fight. Antidote powder medicine is effective. If you complete his questline first, the Violet Hail transformation you earn provides poison immunity — but you will not have it for the fight itself unless earned in a prior NG+ cycle.

Scorpionlord — High Damage, Fast

🦂 Glass Cannon

Scorpionlord hits harder and faster than any other boss in this zone. His poison tail attacks can two-shot an unprepared player. Violet Hail transformation (poison immunity) helps significantly if you have it from a prior run. Without it: prioritize poison-resistance gear, keep antidote powder on your quick bar, and respect his tail swipe range — it is longer than it looks.

Duskveil — Two-Phase Gauntlet

Phase 2 Curse Buildup

Phase 1 is slow and telegraphed — conserve resources. Phase 2 introduces cursed blood attacks that hit in rapid succession and apply curse buildup. Curse-resistance gear and medicine are essential. The multi-hit volley is the most dangerous attack: dodge through it (do not block), then punish during the recovery window.

Permanent Miss Warning

⚠️ Scorpionlord Order

If Duskveil is fought before Scorpionlord, Scorpionlord appears as a temporary ally during Duskveil's Phase 1 — and is then permanently removed from the game. You lose: the Scorpionlord boss fight, the Sky-Piercing Horn material (3 per playthrough), and a significant chunk of Purple Cloud Mountain's content. Always fight Scorpionlord first.

6. En-Route Bonus Items (From Purple Cloud Mountain)

Everything you earn across the three bosses of Purple Cloud Mountain.

ItemTypeSource
Weaver's NeedleVesselDuskveil — final boss of Purple Cloud Mountain
Violet HailTransformationDaoist Mi questline — grants poison immunity
Sky-Piercing HornRare MaterialScorpionlord — only 3 per playthrough. Do not sell or discard.
💡 True Ending Requirement

Clearing Purple Cloud Mountain (defeating Duskveil) is one of the five prerequisites for unlocking the true ending and the Erlang Shen fight. The Weaver's Needle itself is a top-tier defensive Vessel that remains useful through endgame and NG+ — it is not a trophy item you equip once and forget.
