1. Why Turtle Island Matters

Turtle Island is where the giant turtle's back becomes explorable in Chapter 3. The story brings you here for one mandatory fight during the turtle ride — and most players clear that fight, disembark, and leave. They miss almost everything.

The same small island holds four significant encounters, and the game does not mark any of them on your map:

  • Kang-Jin Loong — Mandatory story boss during the turtle ride sequence, fought on a frozen lake at Mirrormare. The game transforms you into a bat for this fight. Drops the Starlit Cloud-Bidden Antler, the key material for crafting the Kang-Jin Staff.
  • Kang-Jin Star — Optional boss at Warding Temple, back of the temple area. Drops Cold Iron Leaves (the second material needed for Kang-Jin Staff) and a Mind Core. The Kang-Jin Staff is a prerequisite for crafting the NG+ weapon Stormflash Loong Staff.
  • Cyan Loong — Optional boss at a cliff edge on the island. Appears as a frozen statue. Requires Loong Scales to awaken. The third of four Loong dragon fights. Drops Mountain-Shaking Claw and Loong Pearl.
  • Chen Loong — NPC accessible from North Shore after descending from the turtle. Turn away from the main direction at the shrine, follow the coastal path to its end. Chen Loong is in the water. Gives you the Ruyi Scroll, which unlocks Zodiac Village — the central hub for NPC questlines and upgrades.

Turtle Island is not a side area you can skip. It is the most encounter-dense location in Chapter 3, and what you collect here feeds directly into the NG+ weapon upgrade chain. Leave early, and you will need to come back.

⚠ Kang-Jin Staff Is NOT Optional for NG+

The Kang-Jin Staff is a direct prerequisite for the Stormflash Loong Staff in New Game Plus. Both materials — the Antler from Kang-Jin Loong and the Cold Iron Leaves from Kang-Jin Star — must be collected in the same playthrough. Missing Kang-Jin Star means missing the Stormflash upgrade chain in NG+.

3. Optimal Route — Boss-by-Boss Walkthrough

This route minimizes backtracking and ensures you collect every item in one pass across Turtle Island.

Arrival — The Turtle Ride

1Reach Turtle Island via the Chapter 3 story. The giant turtle comes to life and you ride it across the water. During the ride, you will be attacked — this leads directly into the first boss fight.

Boss 1 — Kang-Jin Loong at Mirrormare

2Mandatory story boss. A cutscene transforms you into a bat for the opening sequence. The fight takes place on a frozen lake — Kang-Jin Loong uses sweeping ice breath and wide-area attacks. Defeat her to receive the Starlit Cloud-Bidden Antler. Immediately craft the Kang-Jin Staff at a Keeper's Shrine if you already have the Cold Iron Leaves. If not, proceed to Warding Temple next.

Boss 2 — Kang-Jin Star at Warding Temple

3Optional boss. After the turtle reaches Bitter Lake and you disembark, explore the island. The Warding Temple is a distinct structure — Kang-Jin Star waits behind the temple building. High-damage lightning attacks, so come prepared. Drops Cold Iron Leaves and a Mind Core. With both materials now in hand, craft the Kang-Jin Staff at any shrine.

Boss 3 — Cyan Loong at the Cliff Edge

4Optional boss. Requires Loong Scales. On the same island, find the cliff edge with a frozen dragon statue. If you have the Loong Scales (obtained from Chapter 2's Loong Scales quest chain), interact with the statue to awaken Cyan Loong. This is the third of four Loong dragon fights across the game. Drops Mountain-Shaking Claw and Loong Pearl.

Descend — Chen Loong at Bitter Lake

5After clearing the island, ride the turtle to North Shore. At the North Shore Keeper's Shrine, turn around — do not follow the main path forward. Follow the coastal path behind you to its very end. Chen Loong waits in the water. Speaking to him triggers a brief exchange, after which he gives you the Ruyi Scroll. Use the scroll from your inventory to enter Zodiac Village — the central hub for all NPC questlines.
✅ Route Complete

If you followed all five steps, you now have the Kang-Jin Staff crafted, the Loong Claw + Pearl for future weapon upgrades, the Ruyi Scroll unlocking Zodiac Village, and a Mind Core. One pass, zero backtracking.

4. Boss Battle Strategies

Kang-Jin Loong — The Mandatory Fight

Ice Breath & Sweeping Attacks

Kang-Jin Loong's ice breath covers wide arcs across the frozen lake. Stay to her sides — not directly in front or behind. Her sweeping tail attacks have long startup animations; dodge sideways, not backward. Positioning matters more than raw offense. The fight is not the hardest on the island, but it punishes greedy players who stay in the danger zones.

💡 First Phase Gimmick

The cutscene transforms you into a bat for the opening sequence. This phase is forgiving — use it to learn her attack patterns before the real fight begins on the frozen lake.

Kang-Jin Star — The Hidden Temple Boss

High-Damage Lightning

Kang-Jin Star hits harder than Kang-Jin Loong despite being optional. Lightning attacks deal significant damage and can chain if you are caught in a combo. Rock Solid and Cloud Step are both effective — Rock Solid to parry her lunges, Cloud Step to escape her AoE lightning bursts. Maintain mid-range: too close and you eat rapid claw swipes; too far and she closes distance with a lightning-charged charge.

Cyan Loong — The Third Loong

Swooping Aerial + Loong Scales Required

Cyan Loong spends much of the fight airborne, swooping down with wide-reaching attacks. Stay close to force grounded attacks, which are easier to punish. If you give him distance, the aerial loop begins and it is hard to break. Bring the Loong Scales — without them, the frozen statue will not awaken and the fight will not trigger. For the full Loong Scales quest chain, see our Loong Scales guide.

Chen Loong — NPC Encounter

📍 Off the Beaten Path

Chen Loong is the easiest encounter to miss on Turtle Island. At North Shore, the game directs you forward toward the next story area. Turn around from the Keeper's Shrine and follow the coastal path in the opposite direction. It is a dead-end path — follow it all the way to the water's edge. Chen Loong is partially submerged. The Ruyi Scroll he gives you unlocks Zodiac Village, which is the central hub for upgrading gourds, curios, armor, and medicine across the entire game. Do not skip this.

6. Beyond Turtle Island — Chen Loong, Zodiac Village & the Loong Questline

Clearing Turtle Island is not the end of this chain — it is the gateway to several interconnected systems that carry into the late game and New Game Plus.

Zodiac Village — The Hub You Did Not Know You Needed

Using the Ruyi Scroll from Chen Loong teleports you to Zodiac Village, a hidden hub where every major NPC gathers as you progress their questlines. Here you can upgrade your gourd (Shen Monkey), refine armor (Yin Tiger), purchase rare curios, and access services that are otherwise scattered across different chapters. If you have been playing without Zodiac Village, you have been playing without access to the game's most important upgrade infrastructure.

The Four Loong Dragons — Where Cyan Loong Fits

Cyan Loong is the third of four Loong dragon fights spread across the game. The full chain:

  • Red Loong — Chapter 1, behind a waterfall near the Forest of Wolves
  • Black Loong — Chapter 2, in a hidden cave near the Sandgate Village
  • Cyan Loong — Chapter 3, Turtle Island cliff edge (this guide)
  • Yellow Loong — Chapter 4, near the Webbed Hollow

All four require the Loong Scales. Defeating all four is required for certain mythical staff upgrades and hidden transformations. Missing Cyan Loong on Turtle Island breaks this chain — you cannot complete the set without returning to this cliff.

🔄 NG+ Connection

In New Game Plus, the Kang-Jin Staff is a prerequisite for the Stormflash Loong Staff — a significantly more powerful weapon. If you miss Kang-Jin Star and fail to craft the Kang-Jin Staff on your first playthrough, you will need to collect both materials again in NG+ before the Stormflash upgrade path unlocks. Doing it now saves an entire NG+ cycle of delay.
