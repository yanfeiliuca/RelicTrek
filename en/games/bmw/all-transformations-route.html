1. Why It Matters

The Destined One can access 10 Transformation spells across the game. Transformations use Might, give a temporary alternate form, and grant that form's moves, resistances, and separate health pool while active.

This page was rebuilt around the verified Transformation category. A Pluck of Many is a Strand spell, and Spell Binder / Forbidden Word is not a transformation. Do not count either one when checking the all-transformations roster.

The correct roster is: Red Tides, Azure Dust, Ashen Slumber, Hoarfrost, Umbral Abyss, Ebon Flow, Golden Lining, Violet Hail, Dark Thunder, and Azure Dome.

3. Category Boundaries and Prerequisites

The biggest risk is not combat difficulty. It is using a wrong checklist. The transformation count does not include A Pluck of Many, Spell Binder / Forbidden Word, Spirits, Vessels, or Curios.

⚠️ High-Risk Windows

Watch three routes carefully: Daoist Mi must be completed before Duskveil for Violet Hail; Ma Tianba must be completed through Chapter 5 for Dark Thunder; Azure Dome requires the true-ending secret-area chain and Erlang route, not a simple Pagoda mural count.

4. Route — All 10 Transformations

1. Red Tides — Chapter 1

Defeat Guangzhi near the Forest of Wolves: Outside the Forest route. This is the earliest transformation and a useful fire form for early bosses.

2. Azure Dust — Chapter 2

1Reach Fright Cliff and find the Man-in-Stone in the Squall Hideout ravine.
2Defeat Mother of Stones and bring the Stone Essence back to him.
3Defeat the freed Man-in-Stone to receive Azure Dust.

3. Ashen Slumber — Chapters 2-3

Follow the Two-Headed Rat Guai route from Chapter 2 into Chapter 3's Pagoda Realm. If the earlier step was missed, use the Lower Pagoda recovery path and interact with the relevant rat corpse after the required Pagoda progress.

4. Hoarfrost — Chapter 3

Defeat Monk from the Sea at New Thunderclap Temple to obtain Hoarfrost.

5. Umbral Abyss — Chapter 3

Continue Chapter 3 until the third Macaque Chief fight. After that encounter, you receive Umbral Abyss.

6. Ebon Flow — Zodiac Village / Yin Tiger

1Progress Chen Loong's quest at Bitter Lake and obtain the medicine from Xu Dog.
2Use the Ruyi Scroll to unlock Zodiac Village.
3Challenge Yin Tiger at Zodiac Village and defeat him to receive Ebon Flow.

7. Golden Lining — Four Loongs Route

Collect the Loong Scales, defeat the earlier Loong bosses, then defeat Yellow Loong in Chapter 4 to obtain Golden Lining.

8. Violet Hail — Daoist Mi

1Unlock Purple Cloud Mountain through the Venom Daoist route.
2Speak with Daoist Mi and farm the requested item from nearby enemies.
3Return to Daoist Mi and defeat him to receive Violet Hail.
4Do this before defeating Duskveil, or the quest can be locked out.

9. Dark Thunder — Ma Tianba

Complete Ma Tianba's five-chapter chain and the Chapter 5 cart interaction. See the dedicated Ma Tianba / Dark Thunder page for exact chapter locations.

10. Azure Dome — Erlang True-Ending Route

1Clear the main secret areas needed to open the Great Pagoda mural path.
2Enter the Erlang route from the Great Pagoda.
3Defeat Erlang, the Sacred Divinity.
4Receive Azure Dome as the Stone Monkey transformation reward.
💡 True Ending Connection

Azure Dome belongs to the true-ending route. The route is broader than a Pagoda mural count: it depends on clearing the relevant secret-area chain and then defeating Erlang.

5. Hazard Guide

Spell Category Confusion

⚠️ Do Not Count Non-Transformations

A Pluck of Many and Spell Binder / Forbidden Word are useful spells, but they are not Transformations. Counting them creates a false 10-item route and pushes real forms like Azure Dust, Umbral Abyss, Golden Lining, or Azure Dome out of the checklist.

Violet Hail Lockout

⚠️ Finish Daoist Mi Before Duskveil

Complete Daoist Mi's quest before defeating Duskveil in Purple Cloud Mountain. Otherwise Violet Hail can be lost for that cycle.

Azure Dome Requirements

⚠️ True-Ending Route

Azure Dome is tied to Erlang, not a simple five-mural collection. Clear the required secret-area bosses, use the Great Pagoda route, and defeat Erlang to receive it.
