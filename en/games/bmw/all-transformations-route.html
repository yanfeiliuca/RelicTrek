1. Why It Matters

The Destined One can access 10 different transformation spells across the game. Each temporarily transforms you into a monster, granting access to that monster's combat abilities and stats. The transformations range from minor combat tools to fight-defining power tools that entirely change boss encounters.

The game contains 10 total transformation spells. Each is obtained differently — some from boss defeats, some from multi-step NPC questlines, some from hidden questlines that span multiple chapters. There is no single exploration route: they are scattered across all six chapters.

Note: 暗雷 transformation is covered in the existing RelicTrek BMW guide. 黯雷 is covered in the Ma Tianba questline guide. This guide covers all remaining transformations and cross-references those two.

3. Prerequisites

Varies dramatically by transformation. Several require multi-chapter questlines with missable steps. The most demanding — the 猿 transformation — requires unlocking 5 specific mural paintings in 浮屠界 (excluding 花果山's mural) AND completing the 瓜田 scene, then re-entering 浮屠界 where 弥勒佛 appears and opens a hidden dungeon leading to 二郎神.

⚠️ Multiple Missable Transformations

Several transformations have permanently missable steps: 鼠 transformation (must free NPC before certain story trigger), 禁字术 (all three steps must be completed in order), 黯雷 (Ma Tianba dialogue chain must be complete before 百眼魔君), and 猿 (prerequisites must be met before certain story progress). Consult the dedicated guides for missable-sensitive transformations.

4. Route — All 10 Transformations

1. 广智 (Guangzhi) — Chapter 1

Obtained through the main story progression in Chapter 1. Cannot be missed — it is part of the critical path through 黑风山.

2. 寅虎 (Tiger) — 六六村

1In Chapter 1, ring three specific bells to unlock the hidden 六六村 village.
2Enter 六六村 and locate 寅虎.
3Defeat 寅虎 in combat to obtain the tiger transformation.

3. 鼠 (Rat) — Chapters 2–3

1Chapter 2: Find the NPC speaking from inside a stone wall and exhaust his dialogue.
2Chapter 3 (浮屠塔/佛屠塔): Find him imprisoned in a cage.
3Defeat 魔将莲眼 near the upper floor flag to free him.
4Speak to the freed NPC and pull out the knife to receive the rat transformation.

Bonus: Collecting all four 魔将 items from the four magical generals also yields a spear weapon.

4. 禁字术/翠笠武师 (Forbidden Word) — Chapter 3

1First encounter: At a temple south of 苦海北岸, find an old man being attacked. Exhaust his dialogue.
2Second encounter: At 罪业塔林, find him sitting on something cold. Use 安身法 beside him to trigger a cutscene. Exhaust dialogue.
3Third encounter: At the 瓜田 (melon field), defeat him in combat. Receive 禁字术.

5. 小黄龙 (Little Yellow Dragon) — Chapter 4

Located in 堕龙壁 near the expedition flag in a small pavilion. A relatively standalone encounter — find the pavilion, fight the dragon, claim the transformation.

6. 藕雹 (Lotus Hail) — Chapter 4

1Speak to 糜道人 (Mi Daoren) in Chapter 4.
2Find 藕雹 material from nearby Daoist monsters (farm them in the area).
3Return to 糜道人 and deliver the material.
4When he attacks you, defeat him. Reward: 藕雹 transformation.

7. 黯雷 (Dark Thunder) — Chapters 1–5

From the Ma Tianba five-chapter questline. See the dedicated guide for the complete route.

8. 暗雷

See existing RelicTrek BMW guide.

9. 皓霜 (Hoarfrost/White Frost)

Chapter-specific location. Check the Chapter with ice/frost environments for this transformation.

10. 猿 (Ape/Sun Wukong) — The Most Complex

1In 浮屠界, unlock 5 specific mural paintings (excluding 花果山's mural).
2Complete the 瓜田 scene (same瓜田 from the 禁字术 questline).
3After both prerequisites, re-enter 浮屠界.
4弥勒佛 appears and opens a hidden dungeon.
5Inside: fight and defeat 二郎神 (Yang Jian).
6Reward: 猿 transformation — the most powerful transformation in the game.
💡 True Ending Connection

Defeating 二郎神 is one of the five prerequisites for unlocking the true ending. The 猿 transformation is a byproduct of pursuing the true ending path — you earn it on the way.

5. Hazard Guide

鼠 Transformation — Lockout Window

⚠️ Missable NPC Free

The imprisoned NPC in 浮屠塔 must be freed before a certain story trigger. If you advance too far in Chapter 3 without freeing him, the opportunity is lost. Prioritize the 浮屠塔 section early in Chapter 3.

禁字术 — Sequential Requirement

⚠️ Must Be In Order

The three 禁字术 encounters must be completed in the exact order: 苦海北岸 → 罪业塔林 → 瓜田. Skipping an encounter or completing them out of order breaks the questline. At each location, exhaust ALL dialogue before leaving.

猿 Transformation — Prerequisite Window

⚠️ Complex Prerequisites

The 猿 transformation is the most missable transformation in the game. Both prerequisites (5 murals + 瓜田 scene) must be completed before certain story progress triggers. If either is missed, the 弥勒佛 encounter never triggers and 二郎神 is inaccessible. This also locks you out of the true ending path.
