Great Sage Armor Set — Four Chapter 6 Bosses, One Complete Set, and the One Piece Hidden Inside a Pig

1. Why It Matters

Chapter 6 (花果山) contains four mid-area bosses, each dropping one piece of the 大圣套装 (Great Sage armor set). The set includes: 凤翅紫金冠 (head) from 凤翅将军, 锁子黄金甲 (chest) from 金甲犀 at 望犀坡, 藕丝步云履 (legs) from 步云鹿 at 见鹿林, and 点翠飞龙釬 (arms) from 碧臂螂 — located INSIDE 猪八戒's belly at 捕螂汀.

Most players defeat three of the four bosses without connecting them to a set system, because the game never labels them as a set until you have all four. The fourth piece — from 碧臂螂 — requires triggering a specific scene at 捕螂汀 to access. Many players miss this entirely.

This guide covers the four-boss collection route. For the 如意金箍棒 (Ruyi Staff) obtained after collecting all four pieces and entering 水帘洞, see the existing 孙悟空神装+如意金箍棒 guide.

3. Prerequisites

Complete the Chapter 6 main route until defeating 王灵官 and unlocking 筋斗云 (Somersault Cloud). The Somersault Cloud is required to access the open-world flying exploration in Chapter 6 — all four boss locations require it to reach. Without 筋斗云, you cannot access 望犀坡, 见鹿林, or 捕螂汀.

💡 Bring the Plantain Fan

The 芭蕉扇 (Plantain Fan) is notably effective against 碧臂螂, the fourth boss inside 猪八戒's belly. Its wide AoE knockback disrupts the boss's rapid attack chains. If you have the fan, equip it before the fight.

4. Route — Four Bosses in Order

Boss 1 — 凤翅将军 (Head Armor)

1From the Chapter 6 starting area, fly via 筋斗云 to the 花果山 main route area.
2Locate and defeat 凤翅将军. Standard boss encounter, no special trigger needed.
3Reward: 凤翅紫金冠 (head armor).

Boss 2 — 金甲犀 (Chest Armor)

4Fly via 筋斗云 to the 望犀坡 expedition flag.
5Defeat 金甲犀 near the flag area.
6Reward: 锁子黄金甲 (chest armor).

Boss 3 — 步云鹿 (Leg Armor)

7Fly to the 见鹿林 expedition flag.
8Defeat 步云鹿 in this area.
9Reward: 藕丝步云履 (leg armor).

Boss 4 — 碧臂螂 INSIDE 猪八戒's Belly (Arm Armor)

10Fly to the 捕螂汀 expedition flag.
11Observe 猪八戒 in the area catching a mantis. This is a scene trigger — watch for it.
12Follow the scene: you will be transported inside 猪八戒's stomach — a unique interior arena.
13Fight and defeat 碧臂螂. Use the 芭蕉扇 if you have it — its AoE is highly effective here.
14Reward: 点翠飞龙釬 (arm armor).

After All Four: The Staff

15With all four pieces collected, fly to 水帘洞 (from 捕螂汀 flag, fly right and up to the cave entrance).
16Enter 水帘洞 with the full set equipped. The 如意金箍棒 acquisition scene triggers. (See existing 孙悟空神装+如意金箍棒 guide for full details.)

5. Hazard Guide

碧臂螂 — Completely Missable

🐷 Don't Skip 捕螂汀

碧臂螂 does not appear in the open world. If you never return to 捕螂汀 and trigger the scene with 猪八戒 catching a mantis, you will never find this boss. The arm piece of the Great Sage set will be permanently missing. After defeating the other three bosses, make sure to visit 捕螂汀 before proceeding to 水帘洞.

Boss 4 Difficulty

⚔️ Interior Arena Challenge

碧臂螂 is fought inside 猪八戒's stomach — a confined arena with limited space. The boss uses rapid multi-hit attack chains that are difficult to dodge in the restricted area. The 芭蕉扇's AoE knockback is the most reliable counter. Without it, bring maximum mobility and be prepared for tight-quarters combat.

Set Bonus — Not Visible Until Complete

🔍 Hidden Information

The game does not label these four pieces as part of a set. There is no in-game hint that collecting all four unlocks anything. Players who defeat three of four bosses and move on never discover that they were one piece away from a complete Mythical armor set. Check your inventory against the table in Section 2 before leaving Chapter 6.
