1. Why Zodiac Village Matters

Armor upgrades in Black Myth: Wukong are not available at standard Keeper's Shrines. The game doesn't tell you they exist. They're in Zodiac Village — a hidden hub area accessible only by completing the Chen Loong questline in Chapter 3.

Zodiac Village gathers four core upgrade NPCs — Yin Tiger, Shen Monkey, Xu Dog, and Chen Loong — into a permanent fast-travel hub. Yin Tiger offers armor upgrades immediately; defeating him unlocks Ebon Flow and an additional Curio slot.

The route has four steps. Find and defeat Chen Loong at Bitter Lake's North Shore, speak to Xu Dog for the requested medicine, deliver it, and use the Ruyi Scroll to enter the village.

3. Chen Loong Questline (Prerequisite)

Four steps to unlock Zodiac Village. This questline becomes available as soon as you reach Chapter 3's Bitter Lake area.

Step 1 — Find Chen Loong

1Fast travel to the North Shore of Bitter Lake Keeper's Shrine in Chapter 3. From the shrine, turn around and follow the coastal path to its end. You'll find an ancient dragon waiting in the water near the shore. Most players walk forward from this shrine — he is behind you.

Step 2 — Defeat Chen Loong

2Defeat Chen Loong, then speak with him until he asks for medicine from Xu Dog. The required quest state is the important part; character-level and spell recommendations vary by build.

Step 3 — Get the Medicine from Xu Dog

3Fast travel to the Crouching Tiger Temple Cellar Keeper's Shrine in Chapter 2. Talk to Xu Dog; he gives you the Special-made Bone-Strengthening Pellet for Chen Loong. It is a quest item, not a shop purchase. If Xu Dog is absent, complete his earlier Sandgate Village encounter first.

Step 4 — Deliver the Medicine

4Return to the North Shore of Bitter Lake. Chen Loong is now standing near the small building on the shore. Give him the pellet. He will thank you and hand over the Ruyi Scroll. Interact with the scroll — you will be teleported inside Zodiac Village. The village is now added to your fast-travel map and can be accessed from any Keeper's Shrine.

4. Navigating Zodiac Village

Once inside, each vendor is positioned in a specific spot. Here is who to visit and in what order.

Yin Tiger (First Priority — Left from Entrance)

1From the village entrance, head left. Yin Tiger is the blacksmith. Talk to him to access armor upgrades. The additional Curio slot is not unlocked by the first conversation: challenge and defeat Yin Tiger, then speak with him again and select the option to equip more Curios.

Shen Monkey (Gourd Upgrades)

2Shen Monkey is the gourd upgrade vendor. He offers the full upgrade tree — bring your Luojia Fragrant Vines. His services are identical to what you'd find at any other shrine, but now consolidated here permanently.

Xu Dog (Medicine & Skandhas)

3Xu Dog relocates to Zodiac Village and keeps his medicine services here. The Five Skandhas hand-in belongs to late-game cleanup: collect all five first, then return for the permanent-stat reward.

Chen Loong (Seed Planting)

4Chen Loong now offers seed planting. Give him seeds you've collected from harvesting plants across the world, and he will grow them into harvestable ingredients. Return periodically to collect — the plants mature over real play time.
📌 Key Tip

Visit Yin Tiger for armor upgrades when you arrive. Treat the additional Curio slot as a separate boss reward: it requires defeating him first.

5. Yin Tiger — Secret Boss Fight

After using Yin Tiger's services, you can challenge him to a fight. This is optional — but highly rewarding.

Boss Profile — Yin Tiger

Yin Tiger is a fast optional duel with delayed swings and long attack chains. Learn the timing rather than relying on a fixed level threshold or a single mandatory spell setup.

Verified Rewards

Ebon Flow transformation spell and access to an additional Curio slot through his post-fight dialogue.

⚠ Fighting vs. Using

Armor upgrades are available without winning the duel. Ebon Flow and the additional Curio slot require defeating Yin Tiger. The duel does not remove his blacksmith services.

6. Quick Tips & Hazard Notes

Chen Loong Safe Spots

During the Chen Loong fight, position yourself directly behind him when he winds up his breath attack. The breath only hits in front. His sweeping water attacks have a tell — he dips his head low before the sweep. Dodge sideways, not backward.

Yin Tiger Timing

His attack animations often delay the final hit. Use several attempts to learn the release timing, and punish only after a chain has clearly ended.

Xu Dog Prerequisite

If Xu Dog is not at the Crouching Tiger Temple Cellar, return to his earlier Sandgate Village encounter and advance that route before requesting Chen Loong's medicine.

💡 Fast Travel Note

Zodiac Village appears as a fast-travel destination under its own category in the travel menu. You can warp to it from any shrine, and warp back to any shrine from the village. It functions as a hub — use it between chapters to restock, upgrade, and plant seeds without breaking your flow.
