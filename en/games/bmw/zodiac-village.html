1. Why Zodiac Village Matters

Armor upgrades in Black Myth: Wukong are not available at standard Keeper's Shrines. The game doesn't tell you they exist. They're in Zodiac Village — a hidden hub area accessible only by completing the Chen Loong questline in Chapter 3.

Zodiac Village also unlocks your third Curio slot (you start the game able to equip two; the third requires Yin Tiger's service), consolidates all major vendors into one fast-travel location accessible from anywhere, and contains a secret boss who rewards you with a powerful transformation spell.

The questline is three steps. Chen Loong is off the main path in Bitter Lake's northern shore. Most players walk past the shrine, turn forward, and never go back the other direction where he's waiting. The Ruyi Scroll he drops when you help him transforms into an entire village — your permanent base of operations for the rest of the game.

3. Chen Loong Questline (Prerequisite)

Four steps to unlock Zodiac Village. This questline becomes available as soon as you reach Chapter 3's Bitter Lake area.

Step 1 — Find Chen Loong

1Fast travel to the North Shore of Bitter Lake Keeper's Shrine in Chapter 3. From the shrine, turn around and follow the coastal path to its end. You'll find an ancient dragon waiting in the water near the shore. Most players walk forward from this shrine — he is behind you.

Step 2 — Defeat Chen Loong

2Chen Loong uses sweeping water attacks and a long-range breath attack. Recommended level 50+. Stay behind him during his breath attack for free damage. Immobilize is effective — use it to stop his sweeping combos. After the fight, talk to him. Exhaust his dialogue. He will tell you he is ill and needs medicine — specifically a pill from Xu Dog.

Step 3 — Get the Medicine from Xu Dog

3Fast travel to the Crouching Tiger Temple Cellar Keeper's Shrine in Chapter 2. Find Xu Dog nearby (he is the medicine vendor you may have encountered earlier). Purchase the Special-Made Bone-Strengthening Pellet from him. If Xu Dog is not here, you need to progress his questline first — he relocates to the Cellar after you meet him in Chapter 2's Sandgate Village.

Step 4 — Deliver the Medicine

4Return to the North Shore of Bitter Lake. Chen Loong is now standing near the small building on the shore. Give him the pellet. He will thank you and hand over the Ruyi Scroll. Interact with the scroll — you will be teleported inside Zodiac Village. The village is now added to your fast-travel map and can be accessed from any Keeper's Shrine.

4. Navigating Zodiac Village

Once inside, each vendor is positioned in a specific spot. Here is who to visit and in what order.

Yin Tiger (First Priority — Left from Entrance)

1From the village entrance, head left. Yin Tiger is the blacksmith. Talk to him to unlock armor upgrades — this is the only place in the entire game where you can upgrade your armor's defense stats. He also unlocks your third Curio slot after you speak with him. You start the game with two Curio slots; the third is exclusive to Yin Tiger's service.

Shen Monkey (Gourd Upgrades)

2Shen Monkey is the gourd upgrade vendor. He offers the full upgrade tree — bring your Luojia Fragrant Vines. His services are identical to what you'd find at any other shrine, but now consolidated here permanently.

Xu Dog (Medicine & Skandhas)

3Xu Dog relocates to Zodiac Village after the questline. He sells medicines, ingredients, and — critically — allows you to redeem the Five Skandhas for a permanent stat-boosting pill. If you have been collecting Skandhas across chapters, this is where you cash them in.

Chen Loong (Seed Planting)

4Chen Loong now offers seed planting. Give him seeds you've collected from harvesting plants across the world, and he will grow them into harvestable ingredients. Return periodically to collect — the plants mature over real play time.
📌 Key Tip

Visit Yin Tiger first. Armor upgrades and the third Curio slot are the most impactful unlocks in the village. Everything else can wait — but those two will change how you approach every fight from Chapter 3 onward.

5. Yin Tiger — Secret Boss Fight

After using Yin Tiger's services, you can challenge him to a fight. This is optional — but highly rewarding.

Boss Profile — Yin Tiger

Yin Tiger is one of the hardest optional bosses in the game. He is fast, aggressive, and has long-range gap-closers that punish passive play. His attacks chain together with very short recovery windows. Unlike most bosses, he has no obvious elemental weakness — raw damage output is more effective than elemental builds.

Build Recommendations

Bring your strongest single-target DPS build. The Fireproof Mantle vessel helps absorb his fire-tinged attacks. Equip the Second Chance curio — Yin Tiger can two-shot players who are underleveled or undergeared. Level 60+ recommended, with at least partly upgraded armor. Pillar Stance is the recommended stance — the charged heavy has long reach and can hit him during his short recovery windows.

Rewards

Ebon Flow transformation spell — one of the most powerful transformations in the game.
Third Curio slot (if not already unlocked via dialogue).
Legendary armor recipe — craftable at Yin Tiger's forge after the fight.
Substantial Will — a significant amount of Will (currency) on defeat.

⚠ Fighting vs. Using

You can use Yin Tiger's services without fighting him. Challenging him is a separate dialogue option. Use his forge first — upgrade your armor, unlock the Curio slot — then challenge him when you're ready. The fight is permanent content; you cannot lose access to his services by fighting him.

6. Quick Tips & Hazard Notes

Chen Loong Safe Spots

During the Chen Loong fight, position yourself directly behind him when he winds up his breath attack. The breath only hits in front. His sweeping water attacks have a tell — he dips his head low before the sweep. Dodge sideways, not backward.

Yin Tiger — Pillar Stance Advantage

Yin Tiger's speed makes Smash Stance charged heavies difficult to land. Pillar Stance lets you charge from a safer distance and the extended reach catches him at the end of his attack chains. Combine with heavy armor (prioritize physical defense over elemental) and Fire/Thunder resistance curios.

Missable — Xu Dog Questline

Xu Dog's availability at the Crouching Tiger Temple Cellar depends on you having met him earlier in Chapter 2 (Sandgate Village). If you skipped him, return to Sandgate Village and find him there first. His questline is missable but not permanently locked — you can backtrack at any point before the final boss.

💡 Fast Travel Note

Zodiac Village appears as a fast-travel destination under its own category in the travel menu. You can warp to it from any shrine, and warp back to any shrine from the village. It functions as a hub — use it between chapters to restock, upgrade, and plant seeds without breaking your flow.
