Mind Cores and Xu Dog's Medicine — The Permanent Stat System Most Players Leave Half-Finished

1. Why It Matters

In Chapter 2, in the cellar beneath Crouching Tiger Temple, you meet an old dog brewing something in a cauldron. His name is Xu Dog. He offers to craft medicine for you. Most players talk to him once, see the menu, and never return. That is a mistake.

Xu Dog crafts permanent stat-boosting medicines using Mind Cores — a resource dropped by almost every Yaoguai King boss in the game. These medicines permanently increase your maximum Health, Mana, Stamina, Stamina Recovery rate, Defense, and elemental resistances. They stack with the 15 Celestial Pills found throughout the world and are a completely separate system.

These crafted medicines do not count toward any achievement, so the game never pushes you to engage with the system. But the stat bonuses are real. A player who defeats every optional boss and crafts every available medicine will have noticeably higher base stats than one who only follows the critical path — more health to survive one-shots, more stamina for extended dodge chains, and elemental resistances that trivialize entire boss fights.

2. How the System Works

Currency: Mind Cores — dropped by Yaoguai King bosses · NPC: Xu Dog — first found in Chapter 2 Crouching Tiger Temple Cellar, later relocated to Zodiac Village with an expanded menu · Will Cost: Each medicine also costs Will (the game's currency) in addition to Mind Cores · Effect: Permanent — once crafted, the stat increase is applied to your character forever.

Xu Dog's menu expands once he relocates to Zodiac Village. If you only talked to him in the Chapter 2 cellar, you have only seen a partial menu. Complete the Chen Loong questline (Chapter 3) to unlock Zodiac Village and gain access to Xu Dog's full medicine list, including the elemental resistance medicines.

3. Priority Order

Mind Cores are finite per playthrough — you cannot farm them infinitely. Craft in this order to maximize survival first.

Step 1 — Unlock Xu Dog in Chapter 2

1Progress through Chapter 2 until you reach Crouching Tiger Temple. Find the cellar entrance. Xu Dog is inside, brewing medicine. Exhaust his dialogue to unlock the basic crafting menu.

Step 2 — Complete Chen Loong for Zodiac Village

2In Chapter 3, complete the Chen Loong questline to unlock Zodiac Village. Xu Dog relocates here automatically, and his menu expands to include elemental resistance medicines and additional crafting options. This is the full version of the system.

Step 3 — Farm Mind Cores from Optional Bosses

3Defeat every optional Yaoguai King across all chapters. Key targets: Shigandang (Ch2), all 4 Loong Dragons (Ch1-4), Mother of Stones (Ch2), Fuban (Ch2), and all Ch4+Ch5 secret area bosses. Each drops 1-2 Mind Cores.

Step 4 — Craft in This Order

4Celestial Nonary Pill (Stamina) first. More stamina means more dodge chains. This is the single highest-value stat increase in the game — surviving one extra boss combo is worth more than slightly more health.
5Celestial Jade Lotus Pill (Health) second. More health prevents one-shots from late-game boss attacks. Essential before Chapters 4-6.
6Iron-Body Brew (Defense) third. Flat defense reduction on every hit. More valuable in long fights than health.
7Elemental Resistance medicines as needed — craft Fire resistance before Chapter 5's volcanic environments, Thunder resistance before Chapter 6's endgame sequence with Erlang Shen.

📊 Story Bosses

Most main-story Yaoguai King bosses drop 1 Mind Core each. Combined with the optional bosses listed above, a thorough playthrough should yield enough Mind Cores to craft every available medicine by the end of Chapter 6.

5. Achievement Note

Crafted medicines from Xu Dog do not count toward the "Medicine Meal" achievement. That achievement only tracks the 15 Celestial Pills found in the world (chests, quest rewards, hidden locations). The two systems are completely independent.

Crafted medicine bonuses stack with found Celestial Pill bonuses. A fully optimized character has both: all 15 found pills plus every crafted medicine from Xu Dog. The difference between a character with both systems completed and one with only found pills is substantial — especially in Stamina and elemental resistances.

⚠ Don't Save Mind Cores "For Later"

There is no benefit to hoarding Mind Cores. They have no other use. Craft medicines as soon as you have enough Mind Cores — the stat bonuses apply immediately and make every subsequent fight easier. A Mind Core sitting in your inventory helps nobody. A Celestial Nonary Pill in your stats helps in every fight from that moment forward.
