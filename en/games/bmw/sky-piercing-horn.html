1. Why You Need It

The Adept Spine-Shooting Fuban Staff requires 4 Sky-Piercing Horns to craft. A single playthrough of Black Myth: Wukong has exactly 3 available. The fourth must come from New Game Plus — specifically, from defeating Fuban again in NG+.

But there is a catch the game never tells you about: if you miss even one of the three first-playthrough Horns, you will not have enough to craft the staff in a single NG+ run. You will need a third playthrough just to catch up.

The most dangerous Horn to miss is Scorpionlord's. If you defeat Duskveil before fighting Scorpionlord in Chapter 4's Purple Cloud Mountain, Scorpionlord disappears permanently from that playthrough. No warning. No second chance. That Horn is gone until NG+.

If you plan to craft every NG+ weapon, you need all 4 Horns. If you plan to use the Fuban Staff as your main NG+ weapon (and you should — it pairs perfectly with the Centipede Armor set), you cannot afford to miss a single Horn.

2. What the Adept Spine-Shooting Fuban Staff Does

Rarity: Mythical (highest tier) · Unlock: NG+ only (crafted) · Unique Ability: Charged Heavy Attacks fire spines at nearby enemies, dealing damage and recovering the player's Health based on Focus consumed.

Material RequiredQuantitySource
Sky-Piercing Horn4Fuban, Scorpionlord, Hundred-Eyed Daoist Master, Fuban (NG+)

This staff pairs especially well with the Centipede Armor set, which amplifies health recovery from charged attacks. Together, they create a sustain loop that turns every charged heavy into both an offensive tool and a defensive reset — you deal damage, you heal, and you keep attacking. In NG+, where enemy damage scales dramatically, this sustain makes a measurable difference in survivability.

Unlike the Triple-Tipped Spear (which is a true ending reward — no crafting required), the Fuban Staff must be crafted at a shrine. And crafting it requires all 4 Sky-Piercing Horns.

3. All 3 Horn Locations (Single Playthrough)

Every Horn source in a single playthrough. If you miss one, you add an entire extra NG+ cycle to your timeline.

Horn 1 — Fuban (Chapter 2, Kingdom of Sahali)

Accessed via the Drunken Pig questline. Fuban is the secret area boss of Chapter 2. Defeat him and the Horn drops automatically. This one is not missable — the Kingdom of Sahali remains accessible as long as you complete the Drunken Pig quest.

📍 Chapter 2 — Kingdom of Sahali✅ Not missable
Horn 2 — Scorpionlord (Chapter 4, Purple Cloud Mountain) ⚠️ PERMANENTLY MISSABLE

Found in Purple Cloud Mountain. You must fight and defeat Scorpionlord before engaging Duskveil. If Duskveil is fought first, Scorpionlord vanishes from the area permanently — no warning, no second chance. This Horn is lost until NG+.

📍 Chapter 4 — Purple Cloud Mountain⚠️ Fight BEFORE Duskveil
Horn 3 — Hundred-Eyed Daoist Master (Chapter 4, Final Boss)

The mandatory Chapter 4 final boss. Unlike the other two, this Horn is unmissable — the Hundred-Eyed Daoist Master is a required story boss. Defeat him and the Horn drops automatically.

📍 Chapter 4 — Final Boss Arena✅ Not missable (mandatory)

4. NG+ Fourth Horn — The Full Timeline

The fourth Sky-Piercing Horn comes from Fuban again in New Game Plus. Same fight, same location (Kingdom of Sahali, Chapter 2), same Drunken Pig questline — you just need to do it all a second time.

Scenario A — You Collected All 3 First-Playthrough Horns

1Collect all 3 Horns in your first playthrough (Fuban, Scorpionlord, Hundred-Eyed Daoist Master).
2Start NG+. Play through to Chapter 2. Complete the Drunken Pig questline again.
3Defeat Fuban in NG+ for the fourth Horn. You now have exactly 4 — craft the Adept Spine-Shooting Fuban Staff immediately.

Scenario B — You Missed One First-Playthrough Horn

1You collected 2 of 3 Horns in your first playthrough. One was missed (most commonly Scorpionlord).
2In NG+, collect the missed Horn. You are now at 3 total (2 from first run + 1 from NG+).
3You still need a fourth Horn — which means reaching Fuban in NG++ (third playthrough). Each missed first-playthrough Horn adds one full playthrough to your timeline.
⚠️ Missed Two or More?

If you missed two or more first-playthrough Horns, wait until you have fully explored all secret areas before attempting the craft. Each missed Horn adds one full playthrough. Two missed means you will not craft the staff until NG+++ (fourth playthrough).

💡 Optimization Tip

If you plan to craft all three NG+ weapons, your NG+ run will span multiple chapters regardless. Collect every Horn you can on every playthrough — they are not consumed across different weapons, but you can only carry a limited number of each material.

5. Hazard Guide — Every Boss That Drops a Horn

Fuban (Chapter 2 — Kingdom of Sahali)

⚠ Large Roll Attack

Fuban's signature move is a large roll attack that covers significant ground. Stay behind him when he winds up — the roll tracks forward only and he cannot turn during it. His sweeping claw attacks have longer range than they visually appear to, so keep a safe distance between openings.

✅ Strategy

Fuban is a large, slow target. Charged heavy attacks to his rear are highly effective. Immobilize works well here — use it to create safe openings for charged Pillar Stance heavies. This fight is less about reflexes and more about positioning. Stay behind him, be patient, and punish after his roll ends.

Scorpionlord (Chapter 4 — Purple Cloud Mountain) ⚠️ Missable

⚠ Fast Poison Attacks + Tail Strike

Scorpionlord is extremely fast with poison-infused attacks that can overwhelm you quickly. His tail strike can one-shot low-Vitality builds — when he raises his tail, create distance immediately. The poison buildup from his regular attacks is no less dangerous: if you get poisoned, the damage-over-time combined with his aggression can end the fight in seconds.

✅ Strategy

The Violet Hail transformation (acquired earlier in Chapter 4) provides temporary poison immunity and is highly effective here. Activate it before engaging and refresh when it expires. Cloud Step is valuable for escaping his rapid combo chains. Focus on charged heavies — Scorpionlord has relatively low stance, so charged attacks can stagger him. Remember: fight him BEFORE Duskveil or lose him permanently.

Hundred-Eyed Daoist Master (Chapter 4 — Final Boss)

⚠ Projectile Volleys

His most dangerous attack pattern is the projectile volley — multiple seeking projectiles fired in rapid succession that can stagger-lock you if you try to tank them. Without the right vessel, these volleys are difficult to avoid consistently and can end the fight before you get into melee range.

✅ Strategy

The Weaver's Needle vessel (acquired from completing the Venom Daoist questline in Chapter 4) can be activated to suppress his projectile phases. Without Weaver's Needle, this fight is significantly harder — complete the Venom Daoist encounters before attempting the Hundred-Eyed Daoist Master. With the vessel active, close the gap and pressure him with charged heavies. He has a large hitbox and limited close-range moves.
