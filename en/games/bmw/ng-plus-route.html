1. Why It Matters

New Game Plus (二周目) in Black Myth: Wukong carries over equipment, currency, and most collectibles. Two major objectives that require NG+ make it worth planning ahead rather than just replaying freely.

Objective 1 — 大力套装 (Mighty Armor Set): This armor set requires 6x 大力铁角 (Mighty Iron Horns). The maximum obtainable in a single playthrough is 4. Two more horns appear in NG+. Most players who want this set don't realize they're already locked out of it until they've completed the game once.

Objective 2 — 驷雨龙法杖 upgrade materials: The 驷雨龙法杖 (Four-Rain Dragon Staff) — already covered in the existing RelicTrek BMW guide — has material requirements that benefit from NG+ carry-over. Certain rare materials needed for max-tier upgrades have higher availability in NG+ areas.

3. Prerequisites

Complete the game once (any ending). Ensure you have collected all 4 first-playthrough 大力铁角 before starting NG+. These 4 horns carry over in your inventory. Any missed first-playthrough horns cannot be recovered — NG+ only adds 2 more, it doesn't respawn the 4 you missed.

4. Route — Combined NG+ Plan

Step 1: Prepare Before Starting NG+

1Verify you have all 4 first-playthrough 大力铁角 in inventory. These carry over to NG+.
2Verify 驷雨龙法杖 is crafted (or materials prepared).
3Complete any remaining first-playthrough objectives you want to carry over.

Step 2: NG+ Efficient Route

1Enter NG+ with your 4 大力铁角 and carried-over gear.
2Chapters 1–4: Re-complete Ma Tianba dialogues (still required for 黯雷 in NG+). Re-unlock 六六村 for remaining collectibles.
3Specific NG+ areas: Collect the 2 additional 大力铁角 that only appear in NG+. These spawn in two specific chapter areas — locations that were empty or had different items in the first playthrough.
4Craft 大力套装 once all 6 horns are collected (4 carried over + 2 new).
5Simultaneous farming: While collecting the NG+ 大力铁角, farm staff upgrade materials in the same areas for the 驷雨龙法杖.
6Collect the two 影神图 characters that auto-unlock in NG+.

5. Hazard Guide

Missed First-Playthrough Horns

⚠️ 4 Horns Is the Maximum — Don't Miss Any

The 4 first-playthrough 大力铁角 are not recoverable in NG+. NG+ adds 2 new horns but does not respawn missed ones. If you only collected 3 in your first run, your NG+ maximum is 5 — and 大力套装 requires 6. Verify your count before starting NG+.

NG+ Boss Scaling

⚔️ Not a Victory Lap

NG+ bosses have scaled HP and damage. Early-chapter bosses that were trivial in the first playthrough can be surprisingly dangerous. Don't assume your carried-over gear makes you invincible — Chapter 1 bosses in NG+ can catch overconfident players off guard.
