1. Why It Matters

Chapter 6 (花果山) changes fundamentally after you defeat 王灵官 and unlock 筋斗云 (Somersault Cloud). The chapter's open world becomes a fully three-dimensional flying exploration space. Dozens of locations that were inaccessible become reachable, including hidden treasure chests, optional boss encounters, and the various pieces of the 大圣套装.

Most players complete the main story of Chapter 6 and miss a majority of this content because: (1) they don't explore by flying off the main path, (2) they don't know the 五蕴丹 collection chain, and (3) several treasure chests are only visible and reachable from the air via specific approach angles. The 凡品 (ordinary-quality) Great Sage armor set — a complete 4-piece set — can be obtained in under 30 seconds after getting the cloud, yet most players never find it.

3. Prerequisites

Defeat 王灵官 and unlock 筋斗云 (Somersault Cloud). This is the non-negotiable requirement — without the cloud, none of the content in this guide is accessible. You should also have the 芭蕉扇 (Plantain Fan) from Chapter 5, as it helps with certain encounters, though it is not strictly required for exploration.

4. Route — Every Hidden Location

凡品大圣套装 — 30-Second Quick Grab

1After obtaining 筋斗云, fly back from 王灵官's arena in a straight line.
2In the middle of the flight path, visible from the air: a large slanted tree trunk.
3Beneath the trunk: a chest containing the complete ordinary-quality (凡品) Great Sage armor set — all 4 pieces.
💡 Zero Combat Required

This is the easiest complete armor set in the game to obtain — no boss fights, no quests, no prerequisites beyond 筋斗云. The only challenge is knowing it exists and spotting the slanted tree trunk from the air. Fly low and slow until you see it.

五蕴丹 Chain — Permanent Stat Boost

1Find 4 "蕴" items hidden across Chapter 6 areas. Explore thoroughly — they are on the ground and in elevated locations.
2Before fighting 大石敢当, ensure you have all 4 蕴 in your inventory.
3Fight and defeat 大石敢当 with the 4 蕴 in your possession.
4After victory: climb to the very top of 大石敢当's back for a cutscene and the 5th 蕴.
5Find NPC 戌狗, show and deliver all five 蕴 items.
6Craft 五蕴丹 — a unique consumable with permanent stat effects.
⚠️ Order Matters

You MUST collect 4 蕴 items before fighting 大石敢当. The 5th 蕴 only appears after the fight if you had 4 in your inventory. If you fight him without the 4 蕴, the chain breaks and 五蕴丹 is permanently lost for this playthrough.

Air-Only Hidden Platforms

1Northeast of 捕螂汀: Mid-air platform with a specific crafting material chest. Fly northeast from the expedition flag and scan for a floating platform.
2Above 见鹿林: Elevated area with a unique armor piece. Fly upward from the 见鹿林 flag and search the airspace above.
3Near 水帘洞 approach: Side cave with additional items before entering 水帘洞. Explore the cliffs around the cave entrance before going in.

四渎龙王 Tribute Route

The four Dragon Kings (四渎龙王) who provided the original armor in the mythology each have associated tribute items in Chapter 6. Fly to their associated locations and complete all four tribute interactions for bonus narrative content and unique items. Each Dragon King's tribute is in a different region of 花果山, reachable only by 筋斗云.

5. Hazard Guide

五蕴丹 Chain — Permanently Breakable

💊 The Most Missable Permanent Buff

Fighting 大石敢当 without the 4 蕴 items permanently breaks the 五蕴丹 chain. There is no recovery — not even in NG+. The buff is permanent and substantial, making this one of the most consequential missables in the game. Always verify you have all 4 蕴 before approaching 大石敢当.

凡品 Set — Hidden in Plain Sight

👑 Most Players Never Find It

The slanted tree trunk with the 凡品 set is not marked, not hinted at, and not referenced anywhere in the game. It's literally in the middle of an open flight path that most players zoom past at full speed. Fly slowly, look down, and watch for the distinctive tree trunk. Once you know where it is, it takes 30 seconds. Finding it blind is near-impossible.
