Dark Thunder Transformation — The Horse Guai You Met in Every Chapter, and What He Left Behind

1. Why You Need It

In Journey to the West, the White Dragon Horse carries Tang Sanzang across the world. In Black Myth: Wukong, Ma Tianba — a horse guai who found his own path to enlightenment — travels in the same direction you do, one chapter at a time. He is not on the critical path. There is no quest marker. No NPC tells you to look for him. And yet, he is there — in five different locations across five different chapters — if you know where to look.

Most players meet Ma Tianba once, maybe twice. They exhaust a dialogue or two and move on, because the game never tells you to do more. But if you find him in every single chapter, and if you talk to him until he has nothing left to say, his story goes somewhere you would never expect.

At the end of Chapter 5, you find his spirit trapped inside a destroyed war cart, guarded by enemies near a lava lake in Furnace Valley. He can no longer speak. He can only wait. Pull the cord to cycle through his dialogue — he will thank you, but the transformation is not yet granted.

After you defeat the Keeper of the Flaming Mountains, return to Ma Tianba's location in Furnace Valley. The dying Ma Tianba gives his whip to the Destined One — and you receive the Dark Thunder Transformation: a lightning whip that inflicts Thunder Bane on enemies and grants immunity to Thunder Damage while transformed. Miss a single chapter, and the quest fails. Find him every time, and Ma Tianba becomes one of the most fully realized supporting characters in Black Myth: Wukong.

2. What the Dark Thunder Transformation Does

Form: Dark Thunder / 黯雷 (weapon: lightning whip) · Element: Thunder · Type: Transformation spell · Passive: Immune to Thunder Damage; inflicts Thunder Bane on enemies. Focus built up before transforming carries over.

MoveInputEffect
Lightning LashesLight AttackSuccessively lash the enemy ahead, building up Focus with each strike
Thunderbolt SweepHeavy AttackSuccessively lash the enemies around, building up Focus with each strike
Lightning InfusionSpell (full Focus)Raise the whip toward the sky — thunder infuses through it, charging you into Thrilled State
Thunder FlurryLight Attack (Thrilled State)Each lash of the whip calls forth a lightning strike to assail the enemy
Thunder DominionHeavy Attack (Thrilled State)Raise your whip to conjure a Thunder Formation, intermittently striking enemies within its reach

Dark Thunder builds Focus through its lashing attacks, then spends that Focus on Lightning Infusion to enter Thrilled State — where both light and heavy attacks add lightning strikes and a Thunder Formation to your swings. The Thunder Damage immunity lets you absorb electrified terrain and Thunder-element boss attacks while transformed.

3. All Five Locations (Prerequisites)

You must find Ma Tianba in all five chapters and exhaust his dialogue in each. Here is every location, in order.

Chapter 1 — Bamboo Grove

Start at the Back Hills shrine. From the shrine, continue forward. Upon seeing the first enemies, turn left — a small path leads to a cave where Ma Tianba is waiting inside.

Chapter 2 — Sandgate Village

Start at the Village Entrance shrine. Turn around and cross the bridge behind you. You will reach a door that cannot be opened from this side. Take the detour to the right, navigate past the enemies, and look near a statue — Ma Tianba is waiting nearby.

Chapter 3 — New Thunderclap Temple

Start at the Temple Entrance shrine. Proceed forward and go through the house. Take the left staircase and turn right. Continue forward, then take the left staircase down. Head into the building in front and go to its second floor. On the second floor, turn left and continue to a long bridge — Ma Tianba is at the end. This is the easiest chapter to miss him in; his location is inside a secondary building off the main path.

Chapter 4 — Webbed Hollow (Bonevault)

After defeating the Centipede Guai boss, proceed forward along the main path. Ma Tianba is in a large cocoon ahead — he is on the main path, not hidden off to the side.

Chapter 5 — Furnace Valley

From the Valley Entrance shrine (reached after defeating the Yin-Yang Fish), continue along the main path until you encounter a shield enemy, then turn right into the lava area. Ma Tianba's spirit is trapped in a destroyed war cart. Pull the cord to cycle through his dialogue — he will thank you, but the transformation is not yet granted.

4. Optimal Route

Rule 1 — Exhaust Every Dialogue

1Every time you find Ma Tianba in a chapter, talk to him until he repeats his last line. A single conversation is not enough. The quest tracks dialogue exhaustion, not encounter count. If you leave the chapter and he still had lines unspoken, that chapter is lost for this playthrough.

Rule 2 — Verify Before Leaving

2Before finishing each chapter, return to his location and confirm his dialogue is fully exhausted. This is especially important for Chapter 3, where his position is off the obvious path. If you finish the chapter without verification, you cannot go back.

Rule 3 — Chapter 5: Two Visits Required

3First visit (before boss): Find the destroyed war cart in Furnace Valley and pull the cord to cycle through Ma Tianba's dialogue. He will express gratitude but remains trapped — this confirms all previous chapters were completed correctly.
4Second visit (after boss): Defeat the Keeper of the Flaming Mountains. Return to Ma Tianba's location in Furnace Valley. The dying Ma Tianba gives his whip to the Destined One — you receive the Dark Thunder Transformation.
🏆 Quest Complete

After pulling forcefully, the Dark Thunder Transformation is added to your spell inventory. No additional steps, no boss fight — just a fully unlocked transformation ready to equip.

5. Hazard Guide

Chapter 3 Is the Hardest to Find

⚠ Biggest Risk — New Thunderclap Temple

Ma Tianba's Chapter 3 location is in a secondary building, accessed via left-side stairs, then west through a connecting building, then right-side stairs to a wooden bridge and open-air balcony. This is the most complex path in the quest. If you follow only the critical path, you will walk right past him without ever knowing he was there. Consult the location directions in Section 3 carefully before leaving Chapter 3.

Chapter 5 — Double Visit Rule

⚠ Do Not Start NG+ Early

After pulling the cord on your first visit, Ma Tianba will thank you — but you do not have the transformation yet. You must defeat the Keeper of the Flaming Mountains first, then return to his location for the second visit. If you start New Game Plus before this second visit, the quest is lost for that cycle. Note: defeating the Keeper before speaking to Ma Tianba at the cart makes the quest incompletable.

Permanent Miss — No Recovery Within a Playthrough

⚠ Miss One Chapter = NG+ Retry

Ma Tianba only moves to the next chapter location after you have cycled through all of his dialogue in the current one. If you advance the chapter without exhausting his dialogue, he will not appear at the next location and the quest cannot be completed that playthrough. The confirmed hard lockout: defeating the Keeper of the Flaming Mountains before speaking to Ma Tianba at Furnace Valley makes the quest incompletable. New Game Plus is the only way to retry.
