Dark Thunder Transformation — The Horse Guai You Met in Every Chapter, and What He Left Behind

1. Why You Need It

In Journey to the West, the White Dragon Horse carries Tang Sanzang across the world. In Black Myth: Wukong, Ma Tianba — a horse guai who found his own path to enlightenment — travels in the same direction you do, one chapter at a time. He is not on the critical path. There is no quest marker. No NPC tells you to look for him. And yet, he is there — in five different locations across five different chapters — if you know where to look.

Most players meet Ma Tianba once, maybe twice. They exhaust a dialogue or two and move on, because the game never tells you to do more. But if you find him in every single chapter, and if you talk to him until he has nothing left to say, his story goes somewhere you would never expect.

At the end of Chapter 5, you find his spirit trapped inside a destroyed war cart, guarded by enemies near a lava lake in Furnace Valley. He can no longer speak. He can only wait. If you pull the rope attached to the cart three times, he will thank you — but nothing else happens. Not yet.

After you kill the Chapter 5 boss, return to the cart. The rope has changed. This time, you pull forcefully. Ma Tianba's spirit is released — and you receive the Dark Thunder Transformation: a lightning whip that inflicts Shock on enemies, grants you full immunity to Shock and Thunder while transformed, and comes with one of the most satisfying counter mechanics in the game. Miss a single chapter, and the quest fails. Find him every time, and Ma Tianba becomes one of the most fully realized supporting characters in Black Myth: Wukong.

2. What the Dark Thunder Transformation Does

Form: Dark Thunder (weapon: lightning whip) · Element: Thunder · Type: Transformation spell · Passive: Immunity to Shock and Thunder while transformed

MoveInputEffect
Lightning DanceLight AttackSlash repeatedly with the lightning whip — fast multi-hit chain that builds Shock on enemies
Fatal ShadowHeavy AttackCharged strike — deals high Thunder damage and a burst of Shock buildup
See Through (Counter)Light → HeavySee through an enemy attack and counter — successful timing grants +1 Focus and briefly staggers the target
Shock ImmunityPassiveImmune to Shock status and Thunder-element damage while transformed — walk through electrified terrain freely
Lightning FlashUnique PassiveIncreased invincibility frames on successful See Through counters — wider window for counter-attacks

Dark Thunder is a transformation that rewards timing. Its counter — See Through — turns enemy aggression into an opening. Lightning Flash extends the invincibility on that counter, giving you room to land a full combo in situations where other transformations would be forced to retreat. The Shock and Thunder immunity makes it uniquely useful against bosses and zones that rely on Thunder-element attacks.

3. All Five Locations (Prerequisites)

You must find Ma Tianba in all five chapters and exhaust his dialogue in each. Here is every location, in order.

Chapter 1 — Bamboo Grove

Start at the Back Hills shrine (near the snake area). From the shrine, head into the temple behind you. Walk past the left side of the large statue, go up the stairs, then down the left path to a building. Climb the stairs inside that building to reach a cave meditation point — Ma Tianba is there.

Chapter 2 — Sandgate Village

Start at the Village Entrance shrine. Turn around and cross the bridge behind you. You will reach a door that cannot be opened from this side. Take the detour to the right, navigate past the enemies, and look near a statue — Ma Tianba is waiting nearby.

Chapter 3 — New Thunderclap Temple

Start at the Temple Entrance shrine. Enter the main building and take the stairs on the left. Head west, then exit that building and enter the next one. Take the stairs on the right side going up. Turn left at the top to cross a wooden bridge, then look for an open-air balcony near the pillars — Ma Tianba is here. This is the easiest chapter to miss him in; the location is inside a secondary building off the main path.

Chapter 4 — Webbed Hollow

After defeating the Centipede Guai boss in Webbed Hollow, search the area for spider cocoons. Ma Tianba is trapped inside one nearby.

Chapter 5 — Furnace Valley

From the Valley Entrance shrine, head toward the right side of Furnace Valley where a lava lake stretches across the area. Look for a destroyed war cart guarded by a group of enemies. Interact with the rope attached to the cart and pull 3 times. At this point, he will only thank you — the transformation is not yet granted.

4. Optimal Route

Rule 1 — Exhaust Every Dialogue

1Every time you find Ma Tianba in a chapter, talk to him until he repeats his last line. A single conversation is not enough. The quest tracks dialogue exhaustion, not encounter count. If you leave the chapter and he still had lines unspoken, that chapter is lost for this playthrough.

Rule 2 — Verify Before Leaving

2Before finishing each chapter, return to his location and confirm his dialogue is fully exhausted. This is especially important for Chapter 3, where his position is off the obvious path. If you finish the chapter without verification, you cannot go back.

Rule 3 — Chapter 5: Two Visits Required

3First visit (before boss): Find the destroyed war cart in Furnace Valley. Interact and pull the rope 3 times. He will express gratitude but remains trapped — this confirms all previous chapters were completed correctly.
4Second visit (after boss): Kill the Chapter 5 boss. Return to the war cart. The interaction prompt now reads "Pull Forcefully." Do it. Ma Tianba's spirit is released, and you receive the Dark Thunder Transformation.
🏆 Quest Complete

After pulling forcefully, the Dark Thunder Transformation is added to your spell inventory. No additional steps, no boss fight — just a fully unlocked transformation ready to equip.

5. Hazard Guide

Chapter 3 Is the Hardest to Find

⚠ Biggest Risk — New Thunderclap Temple

Ma Tianba's Chapter 3 location is in a secondary building, accessed via left-side stairs, then west through a connecting building, then right-side stairs to a wooden bridge and open-air balcony. This is the most complex path in the quest. If you follow only the critical path, you will walk right past him without ever knowing he was there. Consult the location directions in Section 3 carefully before leaving Chapter 3.

Chapter 5 — Double Visit Rule

⚠ Do Not Start NG+ Early

After pulling the rope 3 times on your first visit, Ma Tianba will thank you — but you do not have the transformation yet. You must kill the Chapter 5 boss, then return to the cart for the second interaction ("Pull Forcefully"). If you start New Game Plus before making this second visit, the quest is lost for that cycle.

Permanent Miss — No Recovery Within a Playthrough

⚠ Miss One Chapter = NG+ Retry

If you miss Ma Tianba in any single chapter (or fail to exhaust his dialogue), the quest fails permanently for that playthrough. You will find the war cart in Chapter 5, but the final interaction will not trigger the transformation. The only fix is to try again in New Game Plus. There is no chapter select that lets you go back and fix a missed encounter.
