Triple-Tipped Spear — The Only Non-Staff Weapon, and How to Unlock the Ending That Gives It

1. Why You Need It

Black Myth: Wukong has a default ending. Most players see it, think they've finished the game, and move on. What they don't know is that they only saw half of the story.

The true ending requires completing every secret area across Chapters 1, 2, 4, and 5, plus a side quest in Chapter 3. Once those conditions are met, a door opens in the Grand Pagoda that wasn't there before. Through it is Mount Mei — and on Mount Mei, Erlang Shen is waiting.

Erlang Shen is a very powerful opponent who is almost unaffected by spells. He has a stance bar, which prevents him from taking too much damage. His attacks are infused with Thunder, causing Shock buildup, and he can summon hounds that can disrupt combos and leave players vulnerable. After Erlang, the Four Heavenly Kings fight as a group. After them, Erlang returns in his true divine form for the final confrontation.

Erlang will reveal that he has the sixth relic — Sun Wukong's memory, held in his third eye. It now rightfully belongs to the Destined One.

Defeating this sequence rewards you with the Triple-Tipped Spear — the only weapon in Black Myth: Wukong that is not a staff. Mythical rarity. Thunder element. Exceptionally high critical hit chance. After carrying the same weapon type for the entire game, the true ending hands you something else entirely.

3. Prerequisites Checklist

All five must be completed before the Grand Pagoda door opens:

  • Chapter 1 secret area: Defeat Elder Jinchi in Ancient Guanyin Temple (ring the three bells)
  • Chapter 2 secret area: Defeat Fuban in the Kingdom of Sahali (complete Drunk Pig questline)
  • Chapter 3 Treasure Hunter quest: Find and defeat the Treasure Hunter boss
  • Chapter 4 secret area: Defeat Duskveil (complete Venom Daoist double encounter + purple talisman chain)
  • Chapter 5 secret area: Defeat Bishui Golden-Eyed Beast

Order flexibility: these can be completed in any order. You can even finish the regular ending first, then come back to clean up the prerequisites before the point of no return.

4. Optimal Route

Step 1 — Complete All Five Prerequisites

1Clear every secret area across Chapters 1, 2, 4, and 5, plus the Chapter 3 Treasure Hunter quest. Verify all five conditions from the checklist above are met before proceeding.

Step 2 — Reach the Grand Pagoda

2Fast travel to the Snow-Veiled Trail Keeper's Shrine in Chapter 3 (Pagoda Realm). Head right down the slope to the bridge. Interact with the point of interest at the Grand Pagoda — a door that did not exist before will now be open.

Step 3 — Mount Mei: Three Boss Fights

3Fight 1 — Erlang the Sacred Divinity: The hardest boss in the game. Break his stance bar with charged heavy attacks. Use Thunder resistance gear. Immobilize his hounds when summoned. Spells are largely ineffective — rely on melee pressure and stance-break windows.
4Fight 2 — Four Heavenly Kings: All four simultaneously. Each has distinct elemental attacks. Prioritize the lowest-health King first. Use your best transformation to absorb damage. Cloud Step is invaluable for repositioning.
5Fight 3 — Erlang Shen Divine Form: Erlang returns in his true divine shape. Easier than the first encounter — he is weakened and his moveset is more telegraphable. Finish the sequence to claim your rewards.
🏆 Rewards

Triple-Tipped Spear + Freed Mind Relic + Divine Transformation + "Meet the Match" Achievement

Step 4 — Return for the True Ending

6Return to Chapter 6. Defeat the Great Sage's Broken Shell again. With all six relics now collected, a new ending cutscene will play — the true ending.

5. Hazard Guide

Erlang Shen — Three Rules to Win

Rule 1 — Charged Heavies Break Stance

Erlang has a stance bar that prevents him from taking too much damage. Charged heavy attacks are the only reliable way to break through it. Pillar Stance with the Loongwreathe or Golden Loong Staff is ideal for this fight — the charged heavy has long reach and high stance damage.

Rule 2 — Immobilize the Hounds

When Erlang summons his hounds, they will interrupt your combos and leave you open. Immobilize them immediately — it is one of the few spells that remains useful in this fight. Do not waste Immobilize on Erlang directly; save it for the hounds.

Rule 3 — Thunder Resistance Is Non-Negotiable

Erlang's attacks inflict Thunder buildup. Without resistance, you will be Shocked before you can finish your combos. Equip Thunder resistance curios, medicine, and armor. The Shock status reduces your damage output — in a fight this long, that adds up to a wipe.

Four Heavenly Kings

⚠ Multi-Boss Threat

Each King has a distinct elemental attack type. Target one King at a time — splitting damage across all four prolongs the fight dangerously. Cloud Step is your most valuable tool here: use it to escape bad positioning and to create openings for charged heavies. The fight rewards patience — wait for an isolated King, strike hard, then reposition.

Permanent Miss Warning

⚠ Do Not Progress Too Far in Chapter 6

Certain secret area content can become permanently locked if you trigger specific Chapter 6 cutscenes too early. Complete all five prerequisites before approaching the point of no return in Chapter 6. If you are unsure, complete all prerequisites before fighting the final boss the first time.
