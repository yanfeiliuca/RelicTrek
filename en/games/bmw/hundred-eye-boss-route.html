Hundred-Eye Demon Lord Route — Chapter 4's Final Boss and the Quest You Must Finish Before Fighting Him

1. Why It Matters

The Hundred-Eye Demon Lord (百目魔君/百眼魔君) is the mandatory final Boss of Chapter 4 at the 黄花观-金光苑 area of 盘丝岭. He drops the third and final Sky-Piercing Horn (破天角) — and he's one of only two places in the entire game where this material can be obtained.

What the game never tells you: inside 金光苑, before the open ground where the Hundred-Eye Demon Lord's fight triggers, there is a fourth purple charm (紫色符咒) waiting to be torn down. This charm is the final step of the Fourth Sister questline (四姐支线). The moment you trigger the Hundred-Eye Demon Lord's boss fight — the moment you cross that threshold — the fourth charm becomes permanently inaccessible for the rest of the playthrough.

Most players walk in, trigger the boss, and never know the questline was there. The Fourth Sister questline unlocks a permanent 影神图 (Shadow God Chart) entry for 鹤仙人 and provides unique rewards that cannot be obtained any other way.

2. What You Get — And What You Lose

Boss Reward: 破天角 (Sky-Piercing Horn) — the third one in a playthrough. Combined with previous horns: used to forge enhanced weapons and specific crafting recipes. Only two sources exist in the entire game.

The Fourth Sister Questline: Requires finding and tearing down 4 purple charms scattered around 盘丝岭/盘丝洞:

CharmLocationNotes
Charms 1–3Inside 盘丝洞 areaNo specific order required. The charm near 花间桥 triggers a scene with the Fourth Sister.
Charm 4 (CRITICAL)黄花观-金光苑Same zone as the Hundred-Eye Demon Lord. Tear this down BEFORE walking to the open arena.

Failing to tear Charm 4 before the boss fight: questline fails permanently, character 鹤仙人 never appears in the Shadow God Chart (影神图), and the questline reward is lost for the entire playthrough.

Boss Fight: Two Phases

Phase 1: Standard combat. The Hundred-Eye Demon Lord has moderate HP and uses sweeping melee attacks. Standard combat approach works — save your heavy hitters for Phase 2.

Phase 2 (triggered at ~1/3 HP): He activates a domain (领域) that dramatically increases his damage output and adds area-denial effects to the arena. Key: the 绣花针 (Embroidery Needle) — the reward from defeating 晦月魔君 earlier in Chapter 4 — is used at this exact point to break the domain. If you haven't defeated 晦月魔君 first, the domain fight becomes significantly harder, requiring near-perfect dodging through enhanced attack chains.

3. Prerequisites — Complete in This Exact Order

The order of operations in Chapter 4's endgame is critical. Completing quests in the wrong sequence can permanently lock you out of content.

1Tear down all 4 purple talismans. Charms 1–3 are in 盘丝洞 (any order). Charm 4 is in 黄花观-金光苑 — do this BEFORE approaching the boss arena.
2Defeat 王灵官. Proceed through the main route to confront this boss.
3Defeat 晦月魔君 and claim the 绣花针. This is the Embroidered Needle — the key item that breaks the Hundred-Eye Demon Lord's Phase 2 domain.
4Fight the Hundred-Eye Demon Lord LAST. With all four talismans torn and the Embroidered Needle in your inventory, enter the arena at 金光苑.
⚠️ Critical Order Warning

If you trigger the Hundred-Eye Demon Lord before tearing Charm 4, the Fourth Sister questline is permanently failed. If you fight him without the 绣花针, Phase 2 becomes one of the hardest encounters in the game. The correct order is non-negotiable: charms → 王灵官 → 晦月魔君 → Hundred-Eye Demon Lord.

4. Route — 金光苑 and the Boss Fight

Step 1: Find and Tear the 4th Purple Talisman

1Fast travel to the 金光苑 Keeper's Shrine in 黄花观.
2From the shrine, explore the outer area of 金光苑. Do NOT walk toward the large open arena yet.
3Search the perimeter of the courtyard area. The fourth purple talisman is attached to a structure near the edge of the zone — look for the telltale purple glow.
4Interact with the talisman to tear it down. This completes the Fourth Sister questline.

Step 2: The Hundred-Eye Demon Lord Boss Fight

5Now proceed to the open arena at the center of 金光苑. The boss fight triggers automatically when you enter.
6Phase 1: Standard combat. He uses sweeping arm attacks and occasional charge moves. Immobilize + charged heavy combos work well. Conserve your strongest abilities.
7Phase 2 (at ~1/3 HP): The domain activates. Immediately use the 绣花针 (Embroidery Needle) from your inventory to break it. The domain collapse staggers him — unleash everything during this window.
8After the domain breaks, the fight returns to standard combat patterns. Finish him off to claim the Sky-Piercing Horn.
💡 Strategy Tip

Without the 绣花针, the domain phase requires near-perfect dodging through enhanced attack chains that apply heavy area denial. The needle doesn't just make the fight easier — it's the intended counter. If you're attempting this fight without it, bring maximum healing and be prepared for a prolonged endurance battle.

5. Hazard Guide

Charm 4 — Permanent Miss

⚠️ The Most Common Mistake in Chapter 4

Players naturally walk toward the open arena after reaching 金光苑. The boss trigger radius is large and unmarked. The moment you step into it, the fourth purple talisman vanishes permanently. Always explore the outer perimeter of 金光苑 first. The talisman is not hidden — it's visible if you look before sprinting to the boss.

Phase 2 Without the Needle

☠️ Domain Phase

The Hundred-Eye Demon Lord's domain in Phase 2 is one of the most punishing mechanics in Chapter 4. Without the 绣花针 to break it, you face: enhanced damage on all attacks, persistent area-denial effects that limit arena space, and extended combo chains that are difficult to interrupt. If you skipped 晦月魔君, consider going back before attempting this fight.

Quest Order Recovery

🔁 No Second Chance

There is no NG+ recovery for the Fourth Sister questline. If Charm 4 is missed, the questline's reward and 影神图 entry are lost for this save file. The only fix is a new playthrough or NG+.
