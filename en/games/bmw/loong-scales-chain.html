1. Why This Questline Matters

The Four Loongs is easily one of the most well-hidden questlines in all of Black Myth: Wukong. Not only are the Loongs themselves difficult to find, but the Key Item used to start the quest is even more well concealed.

The Loong Scales look like an unremarkable item at first glance. Their description says they "occasionally flicker with lightning" and seem to be "calling out to their former owner." Most players who stumble upon them try using them near a single waterfall, succeed with the first dragon, and then forget about the rest — never realizing the full chain exists.

There are four waterfalls — and one frozen statue on a turtle's back — all waiting for the Loong Scales to wake them. Each dragon you defeat drops a unique crafting material. Combined in order, they form the upgrade chain that produces the Golden Loong Staff — the best Pillar Stance weapon in the entire game.

If you miss the Loong Scales in Chapter 2, you lock yourself out of four bosses, two unique crafting materials, a spell cooldown pendant, and the strongest staff for one of the three stances. The game never tells you any of this.

2. Weapon Crafting Chain

The Loong weapons form a linear upgrade path. You must craft them in order — each one requires materials from the previous tier plus a new dragon's drop.

WeaponMaterials RequiredStanceNotes
Loongwreathe StaffRed Loong material + Black Loong materialPillar Stance (best)Craft after defeating Red & Black Loong
Golden Loong StaffLoongwreathe Staff + Cyan Loong materialPillar Stance (best in game)Upgraded form; final weapon in this chain

The Yellow Loong drops an additional unique material that is not used in this chain — it is reserved for an NG+ exclusive crafting recipe. Defeat all four to collect everything.

3. How to Get the Loong Scales (Prerequisite)

Everything begins with one hidden item. Without it, none of the four dragons will appear.

Location: Chapter 2 — Yellow Wind Ridge

Fast travel to the Valley of Despair Shrine in Yellow Wind Ridge. This is the boss arena where you fought (or will fight) the First Prince of Flowing Sands. Look along the walls of the arena — you are searching for a section covered in ancient scriptures and faded murals.

1During the First Prince of Flowing Sands boss fight, position yourself so the boss faces the mural wall. The boss's charging attacks will shatter the cracked wall, revealing a hidden passage behind it.
⚠ If You Already Defeated the Boss

If you've already beaten the First Prince and the wall is still intact, equip the Wandering Wight Spirit. Use its headbutt skill against the mural wall to break it open. If you don't have Wandering Wight, any heavy charged attack aimed at the cracked section will work — it just takes longer.

2Enter the secret passage behind the broken wall. Follow the narrow corridor until you reach a small chamber.
3Open the treasure chest inside. You receive: Loong Scales (Key Item).

The item description will now read that they "resonate with the sound of rushing water." This is your cue: waterfalls and hidden dragons await.

4. All Four Loong Dragon Locations

With the Loong Scales in your inventory, four specific locations across four chapters will now trigger boss encounters. You can tackle them in any order, but the recommended sequence follows chapter progression — with one intentional backtrack to Chapter 1.

🐉 Red Loong — Chapter 1 (Backtrack)

1Fast travel back to Chapter 1. From the Outside the Forest Shrine (Forest of Wolves), take the path leading left instead of heading toward the Guangzhi arena.
2You will reach a large waterfall. The Loong Scales will pulse — approach the waterfall and it parts open, revealing Loong Claw Grove behind it.
3Enter the grove to trigger the Red Loong boss fight. Drops: Loongwreathe Staff material.

🐉 Black Loong — Chapter 2

1From the Rockrest Flat Shrine in Fright Cliff, look to the right side of the area. At the top of the stone stairs, you will see a wall of falling sand.
2Approach the sand wall. The Loong Scales react — the sand vanishes, revealing Hidden Loong Cavern.
3Inside, face the Black Loong. Drops: Thunderbone (crafting material) + Tridacna Pendant (reduces spell cooldown time).

🐉 Cyan Loong — Chapter 3

1Travel to Turtle Island in the Bitter Lake region. Make your way onto the giant turtle's back.
2Head to the right side of the turtle's shell. You will find a frozen humanoid statue standing alone. The Loong Scales pulse — interact with the statue.
3The statue shatters and the Cyan Loong resurrects. Drops: essential upgrade material for upgrading Loongwreathe Staff into Golden Loong Staff.

🐉 Yellow Loong — Chapter 4

⚠ Prerequisite

You must defeat Red Loong, Black Loong, and Cyan Loong before Yellow Loong will appear. The Loong Scales will not react otherwise.

1From the Webbed Hollow area, make your way to the Cliff of Oblivion Shrine.
2From the shrine, go down the broken stone hand jutting out over the chasm. Follow the narrow cliffside path downward.
3Look for a drop-down point on the left side that leads to a hanging rope bridge. Cross it.
4On the far side, you will face an extremely narrow wooden beam path lined with slashing spider cocoons. This is the most dangerous traversal segment in the questline — one hit can knock you into the abyss.
5Use Cloudstep to pass through the cocoons safely. At the end you reach the Relief of the Fallen Loong Shrine.
6Yellow Loong waits to the right of the shrine. Drops: unique NG+ crafting material + massive XP.

5. Boss Hazard Guide

Each Loong dragon has a distinct attack pattern. Knowing what to expect before you enter the arena saves lives — and frustration.

🐉 Red Loong — The Fastest

Red Loong is the quickest of the four. His lightning-charged tail sweep comes from your left side and covers nearly 180 degrees — dodge right, not backward. He follows the sweep with a lunge-grab that deals massive damage if it connects.

Strategy: Stay aggressive. Red Loong has the lowest HP pool of the four. A few charged heavy attacks in Pillar Stance will end the fight quickly. Thunder-resistant medicine helps but is not required.

🐉 Black Loong — Area Denial

Black Loong wields two lightning-charged maces and fights on a flat arena. His signature attack is a wide ground shockwave that radiates outward — watch the ground for a pulsing ring and jump or use Pillar Stance to rise above it.

Strategy: Pillar Stance is your best friend here — the elevated position naturally avoids all ground-level shockwaves. When the ground pulses, enter Pillar Stance and wait it out. Counter after each shockwave sequence.

🐉 Cyan Loong — Aerial Swooper

Cyan Loong prefers swooping aerial attacks — he takes flight, circles, then dives. The dive tracks your position aggressively, so dodging early is a trap.

Strategy: Stay close underneath him when he is grounded. His aerial attacks have a harder time hitting targets directly below his takeoff point. When he rises, wait for the audio cue (a sharp screech) — dodge about half a second after you hear it.

🐉 Yellow Loong — The Hardest (Combines All Three)

Yellow Loong is the final exam. He combines Red Loong's speed, Black Loong's area denial, and Cyan Loong's aerial patterns — and adds lightning-infused combos that can two-shot an underleveled Destined One. He is widely considered one of the top five hardest bosses in the game.

Strategy: Use Immobilize liberally — it buys you safe damage windows. Enter the fight with full mana and all cooldowns ready. The Tridacna Pendant from Black Loong helps here by reducing your spell cooldowns. Do not get greedy: two hits, then reposition. The fight rewards patience more than aggression.

⚠ Navigation Warning — Yellow Loong Path

The wooden beam path with spider cocoons leading to Yellow Loong is extremely narrow. Spider cocoons will slash at you on approach — one hit can send you falling to your death. Use Cloudstep (invisibility) to walk past them without triggering their attacks. Do not attempt to fight through them. If you run out of mana for Cloudstep, use Somersault Cloud to bypass the beam entirely if you have unlocked it.
