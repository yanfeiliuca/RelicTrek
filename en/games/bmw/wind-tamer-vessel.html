Wind Tamer Vessel — A Drunken Pig, Three Items, and a Secret Kingdom No One Told You About

1. Why You Need It

Chapter 2 of Black Myth: Wukong has a secret area waiting to be discovered. Instead of ringing three bells, you need to find the Yellow-Robed Squire — a drunken boar hidden somewhere in the back of Rockrest Flat — who will task you with a side quest that, when completed, will unlock the Kingdom of Sahali, a hidden area in Chapter 2.

The quest requires three separate item deliveries across three visits. The boar moves locations between each visit and gives increasingly cryptic hints. Most players who find him assume he's a background NPC, give him the first item, and never find him again.

The Wind Tamer Vessel massively increases damage reduction. Its active ability — triggered with full Qi — temporarily neutralizes wind and sand-based attacks almost entirely. This is especially effective against the Chapter 2 final boss (Yellow Wind Sage), whose sand pillar attacks can be devastating without it.

Beyond the Vessel, completing this quest unlocks the entire Kingdom of Sahali secret area, including Fuban who drops the Sky-Piercing Horn — a rare crafting material with only three available per playthrough, required for a NG+ ultimate weapon.

2. What the Wind Tamer Vessel Does

Type: Vessel · Passive: Damage reduction (constant) · Active (full Qi): Massive damage reduction + wind/sand attack negation · Synergy: Ch2 final boss Yellow Wind Sage (sand pillar attacks negated), any sustained fire/wind fight (passive damage reduction stacks meaningfully)

The Vessel occupies the dedicated Vessel equipment slot — it does not compete with your weapon, armor, or accessory slots. Once earned, it remains a permanent defensive option for the rest of the game.

3. The Drunken Pig Quest — Step by Step

Finding the Drunken Pig

From Rockrest Flat Shrine in Fright Cliff, head north to a small wooden gate leading to a large clearing with Spearbone enemies. A large pig sits by the rocks. He asks you to help him sober up.

Delivery 1 — Sobering Stone

Method A: Buy from the Man-in-Stone NPC (complete his questline first). Method B: Defeat Tiger Vanguard at Crouching Tiger Temple, reach Windrest Bridge Shrine, and look in the hut at the back-left — the Sobering Stone is in a pot.

Give the Sobering Stone to the pig. He sobers up slightly and moves to Crouching Tiger Temple.

Delivery 2 — Jade Lotus

Travel to Crouching Tiger Temple Shrine and talk to the pig again. He requests a Jade Lotus — found in the waterbody near the Village Entrance Shrine, or from any pond in Chapter 2. Give it to him. He mentions needing gold and hints about a rat.

Delivery 3 — Arhat Gold Piece

The First Prince of Flowing Sands drops the Arhat Gold Piece in the Valley of Despair (cave beside the shrine). If you have already defeated this boss, the item is already in your inventory.

Return to the pig's original Rockrest Flat location and give him the Arhat Gold Piece.

The Reveal

He suddenly attacks — revealed as the Yellow-Robed Squire. Defeat him. He deems you worthy and opens the gate to the Kingdom of Sahali. Follow him through, watch the cutscene, and you are transported to the secret area.

4. The Kingdom of Sahali — Route & Bosses

The Kingdom of Sahali is Chapter 2's secret area, accessed exclusively through the Drunken Pig questline. It contains two bosses and unique loot unavailable anywhere else.

Boss 1 — Tiger Vanguard

1Your first encounter inside the kingdom. A direct test of combat fundamentals — clean parries are the most efficient approach. He hits hard but has predictable patterns. Defeat him to proceed deeper.
💎 Pickup — Gold Chest

After defeating Tiger Vanguard, check the left wall just inside the sand gate — a gold chest contains the Knot of Voidness and additional consumables.

Boss 2 — Fuban (Giant Scarab)

2Fuban is a large AoE boss with rolling attacks that cover wide ground. Lateral movement is more effective than dodging backward. He is most vulnerable after his largest attacks — look for the recovery window to unload burst damage. Burn or Poison status effects provide excellent sustained damage throughout this fight.

Fuban Rewards

Upon defeating Fuban, you receive: Wind Tamer Vessel (equippable vessel), Sky-Piercing Horn (rare crafting material, only 3 per playthrough), and the Monastic Insect armor crafting recipe is unlocked.

5. Hazard Guide — What Can Kill You

Yellow-Robed Squire (Ambush)

⚠ Ambush Threat

Transitions immediately from NPC dialogue to combat with no warning. He hits harder than standard Chapter 2 enemies and opens with a fast combo. Use Immobilize to interrupt his first attack chain and gain control of the fight. After the initial burst, his patterns are straightforward.

Fuban (Giant Scarab)

⚠ Arena Boss

Large hitbox with predictable but punishing patterns. His ground-roll covers the full arena — dodge laterally, not backward. Position yourself behind him after the roll completes — you are safe there. Burn or Poison status effects are highly effective for sustained damage throughout this endurance fight.

Quest Lock Warning

🔴 Missable Content

If you trigger the Chapter 2 final boss area (Yellow Wind Ridge) without completing the Drunken Pig questline, the Kingdom of Sahali may become permanently inaccessible for that playthrough. Finish the quest before entering Yellow Wind Ridge's point of no return.

Three Rules to Win

  • Complete all three deliveries before the point of no return. The Kingdom of Sahali can become permanently locked if you enter the Chapter 2 final boss area first.
  • Use Immobilize on the Yellow-Robed Squire ambush. The instant transition from NPC to combat can catch you off-guard — Immobilize buys you the opening.
  • Lateral movement beats backward dodging against Fuban. His rolling attacks cover enormous forward distance but have limited side-to-side tracking.
