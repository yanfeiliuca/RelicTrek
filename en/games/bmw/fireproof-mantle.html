Fireproof Mantle — Three Bells in Chapter 1, and Why You Should Ring Them All

🕐 Route: ~45 min⚔️ Boss difficulty: 2/5 (Elder Jinchi)📍 Ancient Guanyin Temple (via 3 bells)💎 Fireproof Mantle📅 Updated: 2026-06-01

1. Why You Need It

Scattered across Black Wind Mountain in Chapter 1 are three hidden bells that, when rung, unlock access to a secret, entirely optional area called the Ancient Guanyin Temple. The Keeper of the Mountain warns you not to ring them — ignore his advice. Each bell is guarded by a boss or hidden behind a detour. Ring the third, and the game sends you somewhere that doesn't exist anymore: a temple from 270 years in the past.

The Fireproof Mantle is a vessel equipment that grants burn immunity. Specifically, while equipped it provides fire resistance and generates Focus over time — and its active ability (when your Qi gauge is full) grants a significant period of near-complete fire immunity and damage reduction.

This matters in Chapter 5. The Fireproof Mantle will be very helpful in the fight against the Chapter 1 endboss — and in Chapter 5, which features fire damage and lava on the floor, it is nearly essential. Without it, players take significantly more environmental damage throughout the entire fire chapter. Three bells. Three detours. One secret level from 270 years in the past. And one vessel that changes how survivable the entire fire chapter feels.

2. What the Fireproof Mantle Does

Type: Vessel (equip one at a time) · Passive: Fire resistance + Focus generation over time · Active (full Qi): Extended fire immunity + damage reduction for a significant duration

ScenarioSynergyImpact
Ch5 — Flaming MountainEnvironmental lava damageLava floor damage significantly reduced; fire-based enemy attacks far less threatening
Ch1 — Final BossBurn damageBurn buildup and fire damage from the endboss substantially mitigated
True Ending ChainSecret area completionRequired as one of four secret area completions to unlock the true ending path

The Fireproof Mantle is one of the few vessel-type equipments in the game. You can only equip one vessel at a time, but the Mantle's combination of passive Focus generation and on-demand fire immunity makes it the strongest defensive vessel for any fire-heavy encounter.

3. Prerequisites

  • Reach Chapter 1 — Forest of Wolves
  • ⚠️ Defeat the Wandering Wight first — entering the Ancient Guanyin Temple causes him to disappear permanently, and his Spirit is one of the best in the game
  • Defeat Guangzhi (guards Bell 1)
  • Defeat Guangmou (guards Bell 2 area)
  • Defeat Whiteclad Noble (guards Bell 3)

4. Optimal Route — The Three Bells

This route takes you to all three bells in the intended order, collecting everything along the way. Total estimated time: ~45 minutes.

Bell 1 — Guangzhi Boss Arena

Location: Forest of Wolves, Outside the Forest shrine.

1From the Outside the Forest shrine, follow the main path forward. Defeat the wolf guards along the way.
2Cross the wooden bridge. Continue up the path until you reach Guangzhi — a wolf boss wielding a flaming glaive.
3Defeat Guangzhi to gain his transformation spell. The first bell is in his arena — interact with it to ring.
💎 Bonus — Red Tides Transformation

Defeating Guangzhi also grants the Red Tides transformation — one of the earliest and most useful transformations in the game.

Bell 2 — Behind Guangmou

Location: Bamboo Grove, Snake Trail shrine.

1From the Snake Trail shrine, jump left over the cliffs to descend to the lower river area.
2Turn right past the snake enemy and head into the bamboo area. Follow the paved path past two torches.
3At the end of the path, you'll face Guangmou. Defeat him, then ring the bell up the stairs from his arena.

Bell 3 — After Whiteclad Noble

Location: Marsh of White Mist.

1Progress through the Marsh of White Mist until you defeat Whiteclad Noble — a two-phase snake boss.
2After the fight, instead of exiting south toward the next area, head through the small cave to the left.
3Inside the cave, you'll find the third and final bell. Ring it.
🔔 On Ringing the Third Bell

A cutscene plays and you are teleported to the Ancient Guanyin Temple — a secret area set 270 years in the past. There is no way back until you complete it.

5. Hazard Guide — Ancient Guanyin Temple & Elder Jinchi

Once teleported to the Ancient Guanyin Temple, you are committed. The area is linear but contains one critical collectible and one mandatory boss fight.

Collectible: Guanyin Prayer Beads

💎 Guanyin Prayer Beads (Gloves)

Before facing the boss, explore the temple interior. Look for green flames — interacting with them reveals the Guanyin Prayer Beads, a pair of gloves that increase maximum HP and mana. Do not miss these.

Boss: Elder Jinchi

Difficulty: 2/5. His attack patterns are similar to the Wandering Wight — if you defeated the Wight, you already know the basics. The arena is a large temple yard filled with undead enemies.

⚠ Phase 1 — Grounded Combat

Elder Jinchi uses sweeping melee strikes and area-of-effect shockwaves, similar to the Wandering Wight's moveset. Stay mobile, dodge through his swings, and punish after his combos. The yard is full of undead — clear them out when you have breathing room so they don't overwhelm you later.

⚠ Below 50% HP — Absorption Phase

When Elder Jinchi drops below 50% HP, he floats into the air and begins drawing undead toward him. Each zombie absorbed restores his health. This is the fight's core mechanic — if you let him absorb freely, the fight drags on indefinitely.

Strategy to Win

  • Kill zombies during the heal phase. When Elder Jinchi floats up and starts absorbing, switch your focus entirely to killing the undead in the yard. Every zombie you kill is health he doesn't recover.
  • Immobilize works. Use the Immobilize spell to freeze Elder Jinchi and get free damage windows. This is especially useful right as he transitions to the absorption phase — freeze him and burn him down before he can heal.
  • Target nearby corpses during absorption. Position yourself near the undead as they're being drawn in — you can kill them before they reach him, limiting his healing to almost nothing.

After the Fight

Once Elder Jinchi is defeated, you are teleported back to the Marsh of White Mist. Look for the wolf hanging from the tree in front of where the third bell was — interact with it to receive the Fireproof Mantle.
