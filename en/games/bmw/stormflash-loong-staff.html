1. Why You Need It

The Stormflash Loong Staff has 140 base attack — the highest base damage of any weapon in Black Myth: Wukong. It draws Thunder while charging and inflicts Thunder damage on every attack while in the Shocked State. It can only be crafted in New Game Plus.

Here's the problem: whether or not you can craft it on your NG+ run depends entirely on decisions you made in your first playthrough. Specifically, it depends on whether you found and defeated all four secret Loong dragons.

The Stormflash Loong Staff is an upgrade from the Kang-Jin Staff. The Kang-Jin Staff is crafted using materials from the Kang-Jin Star, a mandatory Chapter 3 boss on Turtle Island. That part is automatic.

But the Stormflash Staff itself requires nine Loong Pearls — and Loong Pearls only drop from the secret Loong dragon bosses, which are only accessible if you found the Loong Scales key item in Chapter 2. If you skipped the Loong dragons in your first playthrough, you won't have enough Pearls by the end of your NG+ run to craft the staff. You would need a third playthrough.

This guide tells you exactly what to do in your first playthrough so that NG+ becomes a clean run to the highest-attack weapon in the game.

2. What the Stormflash Loong Staff Does

Rarity: Mythical · Base Attack: 140 (highest in game) · Element: Thunder · Unique Effect: Draws Thunder while charging; inflicts Thunder Damage with every staff attack when in Shocked State.

Shocked State Condition

To trigger Thunder damage on every attack, the Destined One must be in Shocked State — a status applied by Thunder-type enemies. In NG+ environments, this state can be triggered reliably using certain curios or by absorbing Thunder strikes from enemies.

3. First Playthrough Preparation

Complete these steps before finishing your first playthrough. Every step is required for a clean NG+ crafting run.

Step 1 — Get the Loong Scales

1In Chapter 2 — Valley of Despair, after defeating the First Prince of Flowing Sands, search the arena for a cracked wall. Break it open and retrieve the Loong Scales key item inside. Without this, none of the secret Loong dragon bosses are accessible.

Step 2 — Defeat All Four Loong Dragons

2Red Loong — Chapter 1: Backtrack to the waterfall outside the Forest of Wolves. The Loong Scales reveal a hidden passage behind it.
3Black Loong — Chapter 2: At Rockrest Flat, find the sand waterfall and interact with it — the Loong Scales unlock the hidden cave behind it.
4Cyan Loong — Chapter 3: On Turtle Island, near the statue by the shore, the Loong Scales activate a hidden interaction point.
5Yellow Loong — Chapter 4: The strongest of the four. Accessible only after defeating Red, Black, and Cyan Loong. Find him in the Webbed Hollow area near the cliffside shrine.

Step 3 — Craft the Kang-Jin Staff

6Defeat Kang-Jin Star, a mandatory Chapter 3 boss on Turtle Island. She drops the Starlit Cloud-Bidden Antler. Craft the Kang-Jin Staff at any Keeper's Shrine — this staff is required as the base before upgrading to Stormflash.

Step 4 — Collect Gold Tree Core and Kun Steel

7Gold Tree Core drops from Fuban (Chapter 2 secret area) and from certain late-game bosses. You need 4 total. Kun Steel is found in Chapters 5–6 endgame areas. You need 2 total. Both are relatively common in the late game — just make sure not to sell them.

4. NG+ Crafting Route

Once you start New Game Plus, you need to defeat all four Loong dragons again to collect enough Loong Pearls. Each dragon drops approximately 2 Pearls, and Kang-Jin Star drops 1.

BossChapterLoong Pearls
Red LoongChapter 1~2
Black LoongChapter 2~2
Cyan LoongChapter 3~2
Yellow LoongChapter 4~2
Kang-Jin StarChapter 31

Once you have 9 Loong Pearls + 4 Gold Tree Core + 2 Kun Steel + 45,000 Will, visit any Keeper's Shrine and craft the Stormflash Loong Staff.

💡 Route Tip

You do not need to replay the entire game. Rush the main story to unlock each chapter's areas, then detour to each Loong dragon location using the Loong Scales you already have from your first playthrough (key items carry over to NG+).

5. Hazard Guide

Missed Loong Dragons — Permanent Lock

⚠ If You Skipped the Loong Dragons in Your First Run

If you did not defeat all four Loong dragons in your first playthrough, you cannot collect all 9 Loong Pearls in a single NG+ run. Each dragon drops Pearls once per playthrough, and there are exactly enough dragons across two playthroughs (first + NG+) to reach 9. Miss them once, and you will need a third playthrough (NG++) to craft the Stormflash Loong Staff.

Kang-Jin Staff Prerequisite

⚠ Do Not Skip the Kang-Jin Staff

You must craft the Kang-Jin Staff before the Stormflash Loong Staff will appear in the crafting menu. Kang-Jin Star is a mandatory Chapter 3 boss — her material is guaranteed. But you still need to actually craft the staff at a shrine. Check your shrine menu before starting NG+ to confirm you have it.

Shocked State Management

⚡ Thunder Damage Requires Shocked State

The Stormflash Loong Staff's Thunder damage only triggers while the Destined One is in Shocked State. This means you need to manage the tradeoff: being Shocked reduces your damage output from the status itself, but the staff converts that penalty into bonus Thunder damage. Equip Thunder-resistance-reducing armor or use curios that build Shock quickly to keep the state active on your own terms.

Yellow Loong Difficulty

⚔️ Yellow Loong — Hardest of the Four

Yellow Loong (Chapter 4) is significantly harder than the other three. He is fast, aggressive, and has a massive moveset with delayed lightning attacks. You must defeat Red, Black, and Cyan Loong before Yellow Loong becomes accessible. Bring Thunder resistance, keep your distance when he charges his lightning arcs, and punish his recovery windows. His difficulty is 4/5 — second only to Erlang Shen in the base game.
