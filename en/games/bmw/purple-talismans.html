Purple Talismans — Four Seals to Pull, a Boss That Flees, and a Permanent Stamina Upgrade

1. Why This Quest Matters

When you first arrive in the Webbed Hollow, a spider sister is struggling to pull a purple talisman from a tall statue. Every time she tries, the talisman repels her — a burst of energy throws her back. She notices that you can reach what she cannot. She asks for your help: find all four purple talismans scattered across Chapter 4 and remove them from the statues they seal.

These talismans are not random collectibles. Removing all four summons the Supreme Inspector — a winged celestial enforcer with a hand for a head. You do not need to defeat it. Deal roughly 15–20% of its health bar and it flees, leaving behind a golden box. Inside: the Celestial Nonary Pill, which permanently increases your maximum stamina.

Stamina governs dodge availability — and dodging is the core defensive mechanic in Black Myth: Wukong. More stamina means more consecutive dodges, more charged heavies, more pressure windows. This is one of the most valuable permanent upgrades in the game, and it is easy to miss.

⚠️ Critical Miss Window

The fourth talisman is in the Court of Illumination — the final shrine area before the Chapter 4 boss. If you kill the Chapter 4 boss without removing all four talismans, the quest disappears permanently, and the Celestial Nonary Pill is lost for the entire playthrough. Remove the talisman before approaching the boss.

2. What the Celestial Nonary Pill Does

Effect: Permanently increases maximum stamina · Application: Immediate upon pickup, no activation required · Persistence: Carries into New Game Plus · Rarity: One per chapter; Chapter 4's pill requires active pursuit through this quest.

Each chapter in Black Myth: Wukong contains one Celestial Nonary Pill — but most are found along the critical path. Chapter 4's pill is the only one locked behind an optional missable side quest. If you skip the purple talismans, you skip a permanent stamina upgrade that no amount of farming or grinding can replace.

3. All Four Talisman Locations

All four talismans must be removed before the Chapter 4 final boss. The first three can be collected in any order; the fourth requires the first three to be removed first.

Talisman 1 — Verdure Bridge (Middle Hollow)

📍 Verdure Bridge Shrine → Talisman 1

From the Verdure Bridge Keeper's Shrine, cross the wooden bridge and turn left. Enter the lush plant-filled area beyond. The first talisman sits on a stone altar at the far end. Removing it triggers a cutscene with the Second Sister — she explains the history of the talismans and why she cannot remove them herself.

🎬 Cutscene: Second Sister📍 Middle Hollow

Talisman 2 — Cliff of Oblivion

📍 Cliff of Oblivion Shrine → Talisman 2

The easiest of the four. The talisman is very close to the Cliff of Oblivion Keeper's Shrine — visible from where you spawn. Walk up and interact. No enemies, no navigation hazards.

🟢 Easiest📍 Cliff of Oblivion

Talisman 3 — Relief of the Fallen Loong

📍 Relief of the Fallen Loong Shrine → Talisman 3

From the Relief of the Fallen Loong Keeper's Shrine, go behind the shrine and head up the slope made of broken logs. Follow the torch light through a webbed tunnel. Continue climbing to the top and circle around the large tree to the left. Use Somersault Cloud to bypass the spider cocoons blocking the path — dropping platforms and web hazards make this the most navigation-heavy talisman.

⚠ Tricky Navigation📍 Relief of the Fallen Loong

Talisman 4 — Court of Illumination

📍 Court of Illumination Shrine → Talisman 4

The Court of Illumination is the last shrine before the Chapter 4 boss. From the shrine, go around the large tree and up the stairs to the courtyard. Important: An invisible wall blocks the path to this talisman until the first three have been removed. Do not fight the boss before collecting this one — the boss arena is to the right of the shrine; the talisman is up the stairs behind the tree.

⚠ Invisible wall until 1–3 removed⚠ Do NOT fight boss first📍 Court of Illumination

4. The Supreme Inspector Encounter

Once all four talismans have been removed, the Supreme Inspector descends. This is not a standard boss fight — it is a scripted encounter with a unique structure.

Phase 1 — The Descent

1After removing the fourth talisman at the Court of Illumination, a cutscene plays. The Supreme Inspector descends from the sky — a winged celestial enforcer with a hand for a head.

Phase 2 — The Fight (Partial)

2Deal approximately 15–20% damage to the Supreme Inspector. Light combos are sufficient — you do not need optimized burst damage or specific builds. The fight is tuned to be accessible. Dodge his sweeping wing attacks and punish the openings.

Phase 3 — The Flee

3Once the damage threshold is met, the Supreme Inspector disengages and flies away. He is not defeated — he retreats. A golden box appears where he stood.

Phase 4 — The Reward

4Open the golden box to receive the Celestial Nonary Pill. Your maximum stamina increases immediately. The Supreme Inspector is also added to your Portraits collection, which counts toward achievement progress.
💡 He Returns

The Supreme Inspector reappears as a full boss encounter in Chapter 6 — this time, you fight him to the death. The Chapter 4 encounter is a preview; the real fight comes later.

5. Hazards & Missability Warnings

Talisman 3 Navigation Hazard

🕸️ Spider Cocoons & Dropping Platforms

The path to Talisman 3 at Relief of the Fallen Loong is the most navigation-heavy of the four. Spider cocoons block the path, and some platforms drop under your weight. Somersault Cloud is invaluable here — it lets you bypass the cocoons entirely rather than clearing them one by one. If you do not have Somersault Cloud yet, proceed deliberately and clear each cocoon before advancing.

Talisman 4 — The Invisible Wall

🚫 Blocked Until First Three Removed

If you attempt to reach Talisman 4 at the Court of Illumination before removing the first three, an invisible wall blocks the stairs. This is by design — the quest requires sequential completion. Remove Talismans 1–3 first, then return.

Permanent Miss Warning

⚠️ Do NOT Kill the Chapter 4 Boss First

The Court of Illumination shrine serves two purposes: it is the last rest point before the Chapter 4 final boss, and it is the location of Talisman 4. The boss arena is to the right of the shrine. The talisman is up the stairs behind the tree. If you walk right into the boss arena and win the fight, the quest disappears — the talismans become permanently inaccessible, and the Celestial Nonary Pill is lost for the entire playthrough. Go up the stairs first. Always.

💡 Safe Approach

When you arrive at the Court of Illumination shrine, turn around. Walk up the stairs past the large tree to the courtyard. Remove Talisman 4. Watch the Supreme Inspector cutscene. Deal damage until he flees. Claim the pill. Then go fight the boss. This order cannot be reversed.
