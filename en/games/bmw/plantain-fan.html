Plantain Fan — You Get It Automatically in Chapter 5, and You're Probably Using It Wrong

1. Why It Matters

The Plantain Fan (芭蕉扇) is the signature weapon of Iron Fan Princess (铁扇公主), given to the Destined One during the main story events of Chapter 5 (火焰山). Because it's obtained automatically — no quest, no boss, no hidden trigger — most players equip it and treat it like a standard magic weapon.

It isn't. The Plantain Fan operates differently from every other magic weapon in the game. Its mechanics span exploration, combat, and elemental synergy — and the game explains none of them. Players who treat it as just another inventory item leave hidden areas unexplored, miss crowd-control opportunities in boss fights, and never trigger the double-element damage frames that make it one of the most versatile tools in the Destined One's arsenal.

The information gap is wide: most community discussion treats the Plantain Fan as a novelty. The players who have mapped its full potential describe it as a top-three magic weapon when used correctly.

2. What the Plantain Fan Actually Does

Type: Magic Weapon · Obtained: Automatically during Chapter 5 main story · Core Mechanic: Wind burst with three distinct applications the game never explains.

Mechanic 1: Fire-Path Manipulation (Exploration)

In 火焰山's fire zones, the Plantain Fan can extinguish and reignite fire flows, opening paths that appear impassable. The specific locations where this matters are not marked — players who don't experiment with the fan in fire-blocked corridors will never find the hidden areas, collectibles, and optional encounters behind them. Several of Chapter 5's most valuable items are gated behind fire barriers that only the fan can clear.

Mechanic 2: AoE Crowd Control (Combat)

The fan's charged cast has an extremely wide AoE knockback that functions as crowd control on multiple enemies simultaneously. In boss fights, it staggers enemies out of their attack animations more reliably than most other magic weapons. It is notably effective against 碧臂螂 (one of the Chapter 6 bosses in the 大圣套装 route), where the wide-area stagger interrupts the boss's rapid attack chains.

Mechanic 3: Spirit Synergies (Elemental)

Equipping specific fire-element 精魄 (spirits) alongside the Plantain Fan creates damage multipliers that the game never explains. The combination of the fan's wind burst with fire-element spirit active effects creates double-element damage frames — a mechanic that isn't documented in any in-game tutorial but produces some of the highest burst damage numbers available in Chapters 5 and 6.

3. Prerequisites

None. The Plantain Fan is obtained automatically during the Chapter 5 main story. You cannot miss it — it is given to you as part of the narrative progression through 火焰山.

To maximize its exploration utility, you will want to have reached 火焰山's open exploration zones (after the initial story segments). The fire-path manipulation mechanic is only relevant in areas with active fire barriers, which appear throughout the chapter's mid-to-late exploration segments. For spirit synergy optimization, you will need at least one fire-element 精魄 equipped — these become available starting in earlier chapters.

4. Route — How to Maximize the Plantain Fan

Exploration: Fire-Path Clearing

1As you explore 火焰山, watch for fire-blocked corridors and barriers. These appear as walls or streams of flame across paths.
2Equip the Plantain Fan and use its charged cast facing the fire barrier. The wind burst will extinguish the flames, opening the path.
3Explore the revealed areas. Hidden behind fire barriers: unique collectibles, crafting materials, and optional combat encounters that are otherwise completely inaccessible.

Combat: AoE Interrupts

1In multi-enemy encounters, charge the fan fully and release. The wide AoE knockback hits all enemies in a cone in front of you.
2Against bosses, time the charged cast to interrupt their attack windups. The fan's stagger effect is more reliable than most other magic weapons' interrupts.
3In Chapter 6, keep the fan equipped for the 碧臂螂 fight (inside 猪八戒's belly). The fan's AoE is notably effective at disrupting this boss's rapid multi-hit sequences.

Spirit Synergy: Double-Element Damage

1Equip a fire-element 精魄 in your spirit slot (any fire-attribute spirit works).
2In combat, activate your fire spirit's ability first, then immediately follow with the Plantain Fan's charged wind burst.
3The overlapping wind + fire effects create double-element damage frames — a hidden multiplier that significantly increases burst damage during the overlap window.
💡 Don't Swap It Out in Chapter 6

Many players replace the Plantain Fan with other magic weapons after Chapter 5. This is a mistake. Certain Chapter 6 bosses — particularly 碧臂螂 — are significantly more manageable with the fan's AoE crowd control. The fan remains useful through endgame content and into NG+.

5. Hazard Guide

The Plantain Fan has no missable windows or failure conditions — it is an automatic story reward. The only "hazard" is the opportunity cost of not using it to its full potential.

Missed Exploration Content

🔥 Fire Barriers Hide Real Content

The fire-blocked paths in 火焰山 are not decorative. Several contain unique items — crafting materials and collectibles — that are not available anywhere else in the game. The game provides zero hints that the Plantain Fan can clear these barriers. Players who never experiment with the fan in front of fire walls simply never access these areas.

Underestimating Combat Utility

⚔️ More Than a Gimmick

The fan's combat applications are real and significant. Treating it as a novelty item means missing one of the most reliable AoE interrupts in the game. Against multi-enemy encounters and bosses with long attack chains, the Plantain Fan provides crowd control that other magic weapons cannot match.

The Plantain Fan is the most underrated magic weapon in Black Myth: Wukong. It's given away for free, so players assume it's basic. It's not.
