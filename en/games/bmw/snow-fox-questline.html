1. Why You Need It

Just ahead of the Forest of Felicity Shrine in Chapter 3, there is a wooden gate with a body wearing a fox scarf. Most players walk past it — there is no quest marker, no NPC callout, no obvious prompt. The game says nothing.

But if you stop and interact with the body three times, a fox spirit appears. She gives you the Snow Fox Brush — a tool that lets you disguise yourself as a fox. And she asks you to do one thing: find the monk who killed her and make him face her form.

The quest takes about 20 minutes. You equip the brush, fast travel to the Temple Entrance, approach the Non-Void boss arena in fox form, and a cutscene triggers. Defeat Non-Void, return to the fox body, and the brush upgrades into a Curio — the Snow Fox Brush Curio — which extends the duration of every transformation in the game.

This is the only Curio in Black Myth: Wukong that extends all transformation durations. If you use Dark Thunder, Azure Dust, Ebon Flow, or any transformation build, this Curio is permanently slotted.

3. Prerequisites & Route

Step 1 — Find the Fox Body

1Fast travel to the Forest of Felicity Shrine in the Valley of Ecstasy (Chapter 3). From the shrine, head forward along the main path until you reach a wooden gate. A body wearing a fox scarf lies beside it. Interact with the body three times in succession — on the third interaction, the fox spirit will speak to you. She gives you the Snow Fox Brush and asks you to find her killer.

Step 2 — Equip the Brush and Travel to the Temple

2Equip the Snow Fox Brush in your quick-items before proceeding. Fast travel to the Temple Entrance Shrine (New Thunderclap Temple). From the shrine, proceed straight ahead, then take the left path that leads to the Side Hall. Stay in fox form — if you are not in fox form when you approach the boss arena, the encounter will not trigger.

Step 3 — Defeat Non-Void

3Approach the Side Hall in fox form. A cutscene will play — Non-Void recognizes the fox and the fight begins. Non-Void is a ranged attacker who fires projectiles from a distance. Recommended strategy: use Thrust Stance to close gaps quickly, A Pluck of Many to split his attention, and Immobilize to lock him down for charged heavies. The Wind Tamer vessel reduces incoming projectile damage significantly. Reward: Non-Void Spirit.

Step 4 — Return to the Fox Body

4Fast travel back to the Forest of Felicity Shrine and return to the wooden gate where the fox body lies. Interact with it one more time. The Snow Fox Brush will upgrade into the Snow Fox Brush Curio — equippable in any Curio slot, permanently extending all transformation durations.

4. Hazard Guide

Missable Triggers

⚠ Must Be in Fox Form

The Non-Void encounter will not trigger unless you are in fox form when you approach the Side Hall. Equip the Snow Fox Brush before fast traveling to the Temple Entrance. If you arrive without it, you will need to return to the Forest of Felicity, equip it, and travel back — doubling your travel time.

⚠ Three Interactions Required

The fox body must be interacted with three times to activate the quest. After the first interaction, the text changes. After the second, it changes again. Only on the third interaction does the fox spirit appear and give you the brush. If you interact once or twice and leave, the quest will not be active.

Non-Void Boss Fight — Key Tactics

⚔ Ranged Attacker

Non-Void is a projectile-based boss. He will keep distance and fire waves of projectiles. Thrust Stance is ideal — the charged heavy lunge lets you close the gap in a single move. Do not try to trade at range; you will lose.

🛡 Wind Tamer Reduces Projectile Damage

If you have the Wind Tamer vessel (from Chapter 2's Drunk Pig questline), equip it. Its active ability reduces incoming projectile damage — directly countering Non-Void's primary threat.

⚠ Do Not Return Without Defeating Non-Void

If you return to the fox body without defeating Non-Void, the quest will not progress. The brush will not upgrade. You must complete the fight before the final interaction.

🎁 Celestial Nonary Pill

Before or after the Non-Void fight, explore the area to the right of the Side Hall. A frozen pond holds a Celestial Nonary Pill — a permanent stat-boosting medicine. Do not miss it; it is easy to overlook when focused on the boss fight.
