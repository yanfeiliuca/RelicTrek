Ma Tianba Questline — Five-Chapter Dark Thunder Route and Lockout Check

1. Why It Matters

Ma Tianba (马天霸) is an NPC who appears once in each of the first five chapters. He is not a boss. He doesn't appear on any map marker. He doesn't have a quest icon. He simply needs to be found and spoken to — to the point of dialogue exhaustion — in each chapter before advancing to the next.

If a chapter conversation is skipped, return and exhaust it before pushing too far. The sourced hard failure is in Chapter 5: if you defeat the Keeper of the Flaming Mountains before speaking with Ma Tianba at the Furnace Valley cart, the quest can become incompletable.

In Chapter 5, after the Furnace Valley cart step and the Keeper of the Flaming Mountains fight, Ma Tianba's quest rewards Dark Thunder / 黯雷. Earlier references to a separate similarly named thunder transformation were incorrect; Dark Thunder and 黯雷 are the same Ma Tianba transformation.

2. What Dark Thunder / 黯雷 Does

Type: Transformation Spell · Element: Thunder · Source: Ma Tianba questline completion in Chapter 5

Dark Thunder / 黯雷 transforms the Destined One into a horse guai, uses a lightning whip, inflicts Thunder Bane, and grants immunity to Thunder Damage while transformed. Treat all references to Dark Thunder and 黯雷 as the same transformation.

3. Prerequisites — Chapter-by-Chapter Dialogue Tracking

You must speak to Ma Tianba to dialogue exhaustion in each of the five chapters. There is no quest marker. There is no journal entry. You simply need to find him and talk until he has nothing new to say.

Recovery Window

If you miss an earlier conversation, return and exhaust it before advancing the Ma Tianba route. The final documented danger is Chapter 5: do the Furnace Valley cart conversation before defeating the Keeper of the Flaming Mountains.

⚠️ Chapter 5 Lockout

Do not defeat the Keeper of the Flaming Mountains before speaking with Ma Tianba at the Furnace Valley cart. If that step is skipped, the quest can become incompletable and Dark Thunder / 黯雷 may be lost for the playthrough.

4. Route — All Five Chapter Locations

Chapter 1 — Black Wind Mountain / Bamboo Grove

1Ma Tianba is found in Bamboo Grove: Back Hills, inside a small cave off the early route.
2Speak to him until he repeats himself — dialogue exhaustion is the key.

Chapter 2 — Yellow Wind Ridge / Sandgate Village

3Find him in Sandgate Village, near Earth Wolf's area. Defeat Earth Wolf, then exhaust Ma Tianba's dialogue before moving on.

Chapter 3 — The New West / New Thunderclap Temple

4Find him in New Thunderclap Temple, on the side-temple / upper walkway route from Temple Entrance. Exhaust his dialogue.

Chapter 4 — Webbed Hollow / Bonevault

5Found in Webbed Hollow's Bonevault, trapped in a cocoon after the Centipede Guai route.
6Free him and exhaust his dialogue so he can move toward the Flaming Mountains endpoint.

Chapter 5 — 火焰山 (Reward: 黯雷)

7After defeating Yin-Yang Fish, return from the Valley Entrance Shrine and find Ma Tianba at the Furnace Valley cart. Pull the whip / complete the cart interaction before the Keeper of the Flaming Mountains fight.
8After defeating the Keeper of the Flaming Mountains, return to the cart endpoint to complete Ma Tianba's quest and receive Dark Thunder / 黯雷.
💡 Best Practice

Whenever you enter a new chapter, make Ma Tianba your first priority before any other exploration. Find him, exhaust his dialogue, and only then proceed with the chapter's content. This eliminates the risk of forgetting and makes the recovery window irrelevant.

5. Hazard Guide

Missing Ch1–4 Dialogue — Recovery Rules

⚠️ Conditional Recovery

The game allows backtracking to earlier chapter areas, but Ma Tianba's route depends on exhausting each chapter's dialogue. Do not leave the chain unresolved before the Chapter 5 Furnace Valley cart step.

Dialogue Exhaustion — Talk Until He Repeats

💬 Not Just One Interaction

Ma Tianba often has multiple dialogue lines per chapter. Talking to him once is not enough — you must exhaust all his dialogue until he repeats the same line. Players who talk to him once and walk away may think they've completed the step when they haven't. Always talk until the dialogue loops.

No Map Marker — No Journal Entry

🗺️ Completely Unmarked

Ma Tianba has no quest marker, no journal entry, and no in-game tracking of any kind. The game gives zero indication that this NPC exists or that his dialogue chain matters. This is one of the most easily missed questlines in the entire game precisely because nothing tells you it's there.
