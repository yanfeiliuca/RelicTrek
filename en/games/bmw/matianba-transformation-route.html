Ma Tianba Questline — The Five-Chapter Chain That Breaks If You Skip Any Conversation

1. Why It Matters

Ma Tianba (马天霸) is an NPC who appears once in each of the first five chapters. He is not a boss. He doesn't appear on any map marker. He doesn't have a quest icon. He simply needs to be found and spoken to — to the point of dialogue exhaustion — in each chapter before advancing to the next.

If any single chapter's conversation is skipped, the questline does not immediately break. You can return to earlier chapter areas to catch up — BUT only up until you defeat 百眼魔君 (the final boss of Chapter 4). After that point, returning to fix missed dialogues in Chapters 1–4 is no longer possible. The Chapter 5 final step cannot trigger, and the reward is permanently lost.

In Chapter 5 (火焰山), after defeating 火焰山土地, return to Ma Tianba and speak to him. He gives the 黯雷 (Dark Thunder) transformation — a transformation ability distinct from 暗雷 (already covered in an existing RelicTrek guide). 黯雷 (using 黯, meaning gloomy-dark) has different combat properties from 暗雷 (using 暗, meaning secret-dark). These are two separate transformation spells.

2. What the 黯雷 Transformation Does

Type: Transformation Spell · Element: Dark Thunder · Source: Ma Tianba questline completion in Chapter 5

黯雷 transforms the Destined One into a thunder-infused form with unique attack chains and elemental properties. Unlike 暗雷, which emphasizes stealth and quick strikes, 黯雷 provides heavy-hitting thunder attacks with wider AoE coverage. The transformation's combat style is designed for aggressive play — charging into groups and unleashing thunder bursts that hit multiple targets.

3. Prerequisites — Chapter-by-Chapter Dialogue Tracking

You must speak to Ma Tianba to dialogue exhaustion in each of the five chapters. There is no quest marker. There is no journal entry. You simply need to find him and talk until he has nothing new to say.

Recovery Window

If you miss a dialogue in Chapters 1–4, you can return to the earlier chapter area and catch up — as long as you have not yet defeated 百眼魔君 (Chapter 4's final boss). After 百眼魔君 falls, the recovery window closes permanently.

⚠️ The Point of No Return

Defeating 百眼魔君 is the hard lockout. After this boss dies, any missed Ma Tianba dialogues in Chapters 1–4 CANNOT be recovered. The Chapter 5 final step will not trigger. 黯雷 is permanently lost for this save file. NG+ is the only fix.

💡 Best Practice

Whenever you enter a new chapter, make Ma Tianba your first priority before any other exploration. Find him, exhaust his dialogue, and only then proceed with the chapter's content. This eliminates the risk of forgetting and makes the recovery window irrelevant.

5. Hazard Guide

Missing Ch1–4 Dialogue — Recovery Rules

⚠️ Conditional Recovery

Missing Ch1–4 dialogue is recoverable IF you return to those chapter areas before defeating 百眼魔君. The game allows backtracking to earlier chapters. However, once 百眼魔君 is defeated, the recovery window closes. If any of Ch1–4's Ma Tianba conversations were not completed at that point, the Chapter 5 final step cannot be triggered. 黯雷 is permanently lost for this playthrough.

Dialogue Exhaustion — Talk Until He Repeats

💬 Not Just One Interaction

Ma Tianba often has multiple dialogue lines per chapter. Talking to him once is not enough — you must exhaust all his dialogue until he repeats the same line. Players who talk to him once and walk away may think they've completed the step when they haven't. Always talk until the dialogue loops.

No Map Marker — No Journal Entry

🗺️ Completely Unmarked

Ma Tianba has no quest marker, no journal entry, and no in-game tracking of any kind. The game gives zero indication that this NPC exists or that his dialogue chain matters. This is one of the most easily missed questlines in the entire game precisely because nothing tells you it's there.
