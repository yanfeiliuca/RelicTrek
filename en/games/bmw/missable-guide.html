1. Why It Matters

Black Myth: Wukong has no clear "point of no return" warnings. Content locks out with no announcement. The most common form: certain questlines expire permanently when you defeat a chapter's final boss, and some items can only be obtained during specific windows within a chapter's exploration phase.

This guide catalogs every known permanently-missable item, by chapter, with the exact trigger that locks it out. Bookmark this page. Check it before every chapter's final boss. The game will not tell you what you're about to lose.

2. What's At Stake

Permanently missable content falls into several categories: NPC questlines that expire at chapter completion, boss encounters that disappear when you advance to the next area, armor pieces tied to specific in-fight actions, collectible chains with order-dependent steps, and NG+ requirements that cannot be met in a single playthrough.

3. How to Use This Guide

Before fighting each chapter's final boss, consult this chapter's section. Every item listed under a chapter becomes permanently missable when that chapter's final boss is defeated — unless otherwise noted with a specific trigger. The Chapter 4 section is the most critical: it contains the densest concentration of missable content in the entire game.

⚠️ Chapter 4 Is The Danger Zone

Chapter 4 (盘丝岭) has more permanently missable content than all other chapters combined. The final boss 百目魔君 locks out multiple questlines simultaneously. Do NOT fight him until you have verified every item on the Chapter 4 checklist.

4. Complete Missable Checklist — By Chapter

CHAPTER 1 (黑风山)

1六六村 hidden village: Ring three specific bells in Chapter 1 to unlock. Inside: 寅虎 (tiger transformation), unique gear. Must be unlocked before certain story progress.
2旧观音禅院 hidden area: Unlocked by ringing three bells. Contains specific armor crafting unlock and unique boss 金池长老.
3马天霸 dialogue: Talk to him to exhaustion before leaving Chapter 1.

CHAPTER 2 (黄风岭)

1无量蝠 (Limitless Bat): Optional boss who permanently disappears when you enter 极乐谷. Find and defeat him as soon as you reach 苦海北岸 — do NOT advance to 极乐谷 first.
2马天霸 dialogue: Talk to him to exhaustion.

CHAPTER 3 (小西天/流沙河)

1鼠变身 questline continuation: Find the imprisoned NPC in 浮屠塔 before advancing. Defeat 魔将莲眼 to free him.
2禁字术 questline (second step): Find the old man at 罪业塔林 and trigger the scene with 安身法.
3马天霸 dialogue: Talk to him to exhaustion.

CHAPTER 4 (盘丝岭) — MOST MISSABLE-DENSE CHAPTER ⚠️

1四姐支线 第四张紫色符咒: In 黄花观金光苑, tear down the 4th charm BEFORE triggering 百目魔君's boss fight. The charm disappears permanently once the fight triggers.
2黑手道人 arm armor (下毒手): In 盘丝洞-碎玉池, fight 黑手道人. When he performs the ground-thrust (戳地) attack, destroy ALL 4 of his extended arms. If you kill him without destroying the arms first, the armor piece is permanently lost. He appears twice — the arm-destruction window is easier in the first encounter.
3马天霸 dialogue (FINAL RECOVERY WINDOW): If you missed Ch1–3 dialogues, catch up here — still possible before 百眼魔君.
4Defeat 百眼魔君 LAST: He is the chapter final boss. All Chapter 4 missables above must be completed before his fight.

CHAPTER 5 (火焰山)

1马天霸 final dialogue: After defeating 火焰山土地, speak to him for 黯雷 transformation. Only available if Ch1–4 dialogues were completed.

CHAPTER 6 (花果山)

1五蕴丹: Collect 4 蕴 items before defeating 大石敢当. After the fight, climb to the highest point on his back to trigger the final scene and receive the 5th 蕴. Exchange all five with 戌狗 for 五蕴丹.
2影神图 final characters: 袁守诚 requires completing all five chapter interactions with 葫芦仙人 AND having all previous Shadow God Chart entries without question marks. Players with unclear 掌灯狱使 area must do NG+.

GENERAL (Applies to Whole Game)

1大力套装: Requires 6x 大力铁角. Maximum obtainable in one playthrough is 4. Must complete in NG+ for the full set.
2影神图 two final characters: Require NG+ to unlock automatically.

5. Hazard Guide

Chapter 4 — The Density Problem

⚠️ Check Everything Before 百目魔君

Chapter 4 is the most missable-dense chapter by a wide margin. Five separate permanently-missable items all lock out simultaneously when 百目魔君 is defeated. The 黑手道人 arm armor is especially tricky — it requires a specific in-fight action (destroying arms during a specific attack), not just defeating the boss. Many players kill him normally and never realize they missed the armor.

无量蝠 — The Earliest Missable Boss

🦇 Disappears Without Warning

无量蝠 in Chapter 2 disappears permanently the moment you enter 极乐谷. There is no warning, no prompt, and no way to recover him. This is the earliest significant missable in the game and one of the easiest to trigger accidentally — 极乐谷 is a natural story progression area that players walk into without realizing what they're leaving behind.
