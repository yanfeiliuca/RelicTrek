1. Why It Matters

Scattered across all six chapters of the game are 12 plant seeds. They look like ordinary collectibles. The game provides no clear explanation for what to do with them. No quest marker. No tutorial popup. No NPC hint.

The answer: they can be given to 须菩提祖师 (Patriarch Bodhi) at the camp/retreat area. He plants and cultivates them. After a certain story progression, the cultivated plants yield specific items — mostly crafting materials with unique properties, some of which are not obtainable through any other means.

The information gap is extreme. Very few community guides specifically catalog all 12 seed locations AND explain the 须菩提 cultivation system as a unified mechanic. Most players finish the game without ever knowing this system exists.

All 12 can be held in inventory simultaneously and turned in at any point — no time limit, no order requirement.

3. Prerequisites

Access to 须菩提祖师's location — available from early Act 3 or via the camp/retreat area. Chapter 5 seeds require the 芭蕉扇 to clear fire barriers. Chapter 6 seeds require 筋斗云 for aerial access.

4. Route — Seed Locations and Cultivation Process

Collecting the 12 Seeds

Ch.12 seeds hidden in specific parts of the 黑风山 exploration zone. Search thoroughly in side areas off the main critical path.
Ch.22 seeds in 黄风岭 hidden corners. Check areas not directly on the story route.
Ch.32 seeds in 小西天/流沙河 areas, some near optional combat encounters.
Ch.42 seeds in 盘丝岭, some in hidden branching paths off the main exploration route.
Ch.52 seeds in 火焰山. Some are in fire-blocked paths — use 芭蕉扇 to clear barriers and access them.
Ch.62 seeds in 花果山, accessible after 筋斗云 unlock. Fly to reach elevated and hidden areas.

Cultivation Process

1At 须菩提祖师's location, give him seeds from your inventory. He plants them.
2Advance the story — the plants need story progression time to grow.
3Return later and he will have cultivated them into harvestable items.
💡 Patience Required

The cultivation system is not instant. Seeds need story progression to mature. Check back after each major story beat to harvest matured plants. The best rewards come from fully cultivating all 12.

5. Hazard Guide

Fire-Barrier Seeds (Chapter 5)

🔥 Requires芭蕉扇

Some Chapter 5 seeds are behind fire barriers that can only be cleared with the 芭蕉扇. If you don't have the fan equipped or don't realize the barriers can be cleared, these seeds are permanently inaccessible for that playthrough. The game provides no indication that fire barriers hide collectibles.

Obscure Locations

🔍 Easy to Walk Past

Many seeds are in locations that are not obviously important — tucked into corners, behind environmental objects, or on side paths that appear to lead nowhere. The seeds look like ordinary glowing collectibles. Without actively searching, most players will find perhaps 3–4 of the 12 seeds across a full playthrough.

🏆 Hidden Gem System

The Bodhi Patriarch seed system is arguably the most overlooked mechanic in Black Myth: Wukong. It's never tutorialized, never referenced in quests, and never flagged on any map. The rewards are genuinely unique — materials and recipes that cannot be obtained elsewhere. For completion-focused players, this system is essential.
