Prisoner Questline — The Third Prince's Four Lost Captains and the Ashen Slumber Spell

1. Why You Need It

After defeating Kang-Jin Loong, you are transported to Pagoda Realm — a sprawling prison tower that most players treat as a hallway to the next story beat. They light a few shrines and leave. But in one cell, behind bars that look like scenery, sits the Third Prince of Flowing Sand.

He asks you to recover the spirits of four captains — warriors who once served him, killed and scattered across Chapter 3. Each captain died a different death, and their echoes now linger in quiet corners of the realm that most players never check. No quest marker. No journal entry. Just a prisoner's voice and the cell he's trapped in.

Your reward for finding all four is the Ashen Slumber spell — an ability that puts enemies to sleep mid-combat. This works on many bosses, including several Yaoguai Kings. A sleeping enemy cannot act, and the first hit against a sleeping target deals increased damage. In a game where every opening matters, this spell creates free damage windows where none existed before.

Completing the quest also unlocks the Chu-Bai Spear — a forged spear and the only non-staff-tree melee weapon in the base game. It is a completely different moveset from the staff you have carried since Chapter 1.

2. Rewards

Ashen Slumber Spell: Cast to put a single enemy to sleep. Sleeping enemies cannot act. The first hit against a sleeping target deals increased damage — enough to turn many fights. Effective against many Yaoguai Kings. Some bosses and certain enemy types are immune. Cooldown is moderate; use it once per engagement or as an opener.

Chu-Bai Spear: A forged spear weapon. The only non-staff melee weapon available in the base game. Functions as a distinct weapon type with its own moveset — thrust-focused with extended reach. This is not a staff. It changes how Wukong moves in combat.

3. Finding the Prisoner

This quest begins the moment you enter Pagoda Realm:

  • Defeat Kang-Jin Loong — the Chapter 3 opener. After the cutscene, you will be transported into Pagoda Realm.
  • Activate the Lower Pagoda Shrine. Rest. It is directly ahead of where you land.
  • Walk the path opposite the shrine. Most players go up. Go the other way — down the corridor that leads away from the main stairwell.
  • Find the cell of the Third Prince of Flowing Sand. He is behind bars, visible but easy to miss if you are sprinting through. His dialogue is triggered by proximity.
  • Talk to him and accept the quest. He explains the four captains and asks you to reclaim their scattered spirits. Exhaust his dialogue. The quest is now active.
📍 Cell Location

From the Lower Pagoda Shrine, face the shrine and turn around. Walk the corridor that extends away from the central tower structure. The cell is on the ground floor, tucked into the wall. You will hear him before you see him.

4. All Four Captains

Captain Lotus-Vision — Upper Pagoda

1Progress upward through the Pagoda Realm until you reach the Upper Pagoda. Captain Lotus-Vision is a boss encounter on the upper floors. Defeat him in combat — he uses sweeping area attacks; Immobilize followed by charged heavy strikes is effective. This fight is mandatory to access the sealed cells on the upper levels.
2After defeating Lotus-Vision, speak with the Third Prince again (he will have new dialogue about each captain). His spirit will be added to your quest progress.

Captain Void-Illusion — Valley of Ecstasy, Longevity Road

3Progress to the Valley of Ecstasy region. From the Longevity Road path, look for a fork that leads right — away from the main critical route. You will find a giant colossus corpse, partially buried and leaning against a cliff face.
4Approach the corpse and interact to draw out Captain Void-Illusion's lingering spirit. The captain will appear as an echo — no combat. Claim the spirit and move on.

Captain Kalpa-Wave — Valley of Ecstasy

5In the same general region of the Valley of Ecstasy, search the side paths near the giant structures — the towering rock formations and half-buried monoliths that frame the valley. Captain Kalpa-Wave's spirit is tucked into a recess off one of these paths. These areas are not on the critical route; take every branching path you see.

Captain Ice-Bound Heart — Valley of Ecstasy (Deep)

6Further into the Valley of Ecstasy, explore side paths that diverge from the critical route — areas where the main story does not take you. Captain Ice-Bound Heart's spirit rests in a secluded alcove, away from the valley's central pathway. Keep checking side routes; the area is large and easy to miss.

Return to the Third Prince

7Once all four captain spirits have been collected, return to the Third Prince of Flowing Sand in his cell. Exhaust his dialogue. He will reward you with the Ashen Slumber spell and unlock the Chu-Bai Spear for crafting at any shrine.

5. Hazard Guide

Permanent Miss Warning

⚠ Must Complete Before Chapter 3 Final Boss

This quest becomes permanently locked once you defeat the Chapter 3 final boss. The Third Prince's cell will be empty, and the captain spirits will no longer respond to interaction. There is no way to recover this quest in the same playthrough. Complete all four captains and return to the prisoner before you enter the final boss arena of Chapter 3.

Captain Lotus-Vision Boss Fight

⚔️ Mid-Difficulty — Immobilize + Dodge Sweeps

Lotus-Vision uses wide-arcing sweep attacks that cover a large area. Use Immobilize to lock him down, then close distance with charged heavy strikes. Dodge through his sweeps — dodging away will get you clipped by the extended hitbox. This fight is mandatory: you cannot access the sealed cells on the upper floors or speak to the Third Prince about the other captains without defeating him first.

Valley of Ecstasy Navigation

🗺️ Large Branching Area — Take Every Fork

The Valley of Ecstasy is the largest sub-area in Chapter 3. The spirits of the three remaining captains — Void-Illusion, Kalpa-Wave, and Ice-Bound Heart — are all located in quieter, less-traveled corners of this region. If you only follow the main critical route, you will miss all three. Take every side path. Check every alcove. The colossus corpse for Void-Illusion is the most visible landmark; use it as a reference point for exploring the surrounding area.
