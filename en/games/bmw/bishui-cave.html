Bishui Cave — The Chapter 5 Secret Area Behind Five War Carts and a Sacrificial Ox

1. Why You Need This

Chapter 5's main path takes you through the Flaming Mountains. The story bosses are unmissable — you follow the road, you fight them, you finish the chapter. What the game never tells you is that Bishui Cave sits at the end of a side route requiring two things most players will miss: finding a hidden NPC who first appears as a hostile enemy, and locating a fourth war cart that has no map marker.

The Pale-Axe Stalwart. You first encounter him as a mini-boss. Defeat him, and instead of dying, he becomes an ally. He asks for your help finding the Five Element Carts scattered across Chapter 5. Three of them are story bosses — you cannot miss them. The fourth is the problem. It is tucked behind a detour after a rolling ball clears a barrier at the Fallen Furnace Crater. No quest marker. No NPC hint. Just a destroyed wall and a path you have to notice yourself.

When you find and approach the fifth and final cart — the Rusty-Gold Cart — the Pale-Axe Stalwart fights it alongside you. He takes fatal damage during the fight. With his last strength, he smashes open the frozen gate behind the cart, granting you access to Bishui Cave.

Inside: two fire frog bosses, the Bishui Golden-Eyed Beast (a True Ending prerequisite), a Celestial Nonary Pill, and the material needed to craft the Bishui Beast Staff. This is not optional content if you are aiming for the true ending.

3. Five Element Carts — Step by Step

This questline has five carts and multiple missable steps. Follow each one in order.

Step 1 — Defeat and Recruit the Pale-Axe Stalwart

1From the Ashen Pass I Shrine, proceed along the main path. You will encounter the Pale-Axe Stalwart as a hostile mini-boss. Defeat him. After the fight, he does not die — instead, he initiates dialogue. Exhaust all his dialogue. He will ask for your help locating the Five Element Carts. If you walk away before exhausting his dialogue, the quest will not start.

Step 2 — Defeat Three Story Carts (Unmissable)

2Brown-Iron Cart — Found along the main path after Ashen Pass I. Story boss; cannot be missed.
3Gray-Bronze Cart — Encountered in the Furnace Valley area. Story boss; cannot be missed.
4Crimson-Silver Cart — Appears later in the chapter. Story boss; cannot be missed.

Step 3 — Talk to Pale-Axe at Three Locations

5After each cart defeat, the Pale-Axe Stalwart relocates. Find and speak with him at all three locations to progress his questline: Ashen Pass I cliff (after Brown-Iron Cart), Furnace Valley — Emerald Hall (after Gray-Bronze Cart), and Cooling Slope — Field of Fire (after Crimson-Silver Cart). Exhaust his dialogue at each location.
⚠ Miss Warning

If you defeat the Chapter 5 story boss before exhausting the Pale-Axe's dialogue at all three locations, the quest will permanently lock and Bishui Cave will be inaccessible for the rest of this playthrough.

Step 4 — Find the Hidden Rusty-Gold Cart

6From the Fallen Furnace Crater Shrine, backtrack down the path where the rolling ball previously moved. About halfway down, on the left side, a barrier has been destroyed by the rolling ball. Enter the newly opened passage.
7Inside, you will face the Flint Vanguard (optional elite). Defeat him and continue through the passage. This leads to the Cooling Slope Shrine. From the shrine, walk toward the frozen gate — the Pale-Axe Stalwart will be waiting there.

Step 5 — Defeat the Rusty-Gold Cart + Pale-Axe Sacrifice

8The Rusty-Gold Cart is the fifth and final cart. The Pale-Axe fights alongside you. After the battle, he sustains fatal injuries. With his dying strength, he shatters the frozen gate, opening the path to Bishui Cave.
🎁 Before Entering the Cave

Collect the Flame Orb curio from the ground near where the Rusty-Gold Cart fell. Do not enter the cave without picking it up — the area remains accessible, but the curio is easy to overlook in the moment.

4. Inside Bishui Cave — Full Route

Once through the frozen gate, the cave opens into a series of chambers. The layout is mostly linear, but two optional encounters flank the main path.

Optional Elite — Bull Governor

1Shortly after entering, you will encounter the Bull Governor — an optional elite enemy. Defeating him drops his spirit form, which you can equip as a transformation spirit. He is not required for progression, but his spirit is unique to this cave and cannot be obtained elsewhere.

Celestial Nonary Pill — Broken Temple Roof

2Continue through the cave until you reach a chamber with a collapsed temple structure. The Celestial Nonary Pill is under the broken roof, partially buried in debris. Look for the golden glow — it is easy to walk past in the dim lighting of the cave. This pill permanently increases your maximum health.

Optional — Twin Fire Frogs

3In a side chamber before the final boss arena, two fire frog mini-bosses wait. They attack simultaneously and deal heavy fire damage. The fight is optional — you can skip it entirely and proceed to the final boss. If you choose to fight, equip the Fireproof Mantle and take them down one at a time.

Final Boss — Bishui Golden-Eyed Beast

4Level 75+ recommended. The Bishui Golden-Eyed Beast covers the arena in wide fire attacks. Equip the Fireproof Mantle Vessel — its active ability reduces fire damage hugely in this fight. Window it carefully: pop it when the boss begins its charged fire breath or arena-wide flame attacks.
5The boss's aerial fire breath follows a predictable arc — dodge sideways, not backward. At approximately 40% HP, it enters a burrowing phase where it dives underground and erupts beneath you. Watch the ground — the dust trail tells you where it will surface.
6Phase 2: When the boss reaches low health, the Yellow Wind Sage (from Chapter 2) appears in a brief assist sequence and stuns the Golden-Eyed Beast. Use this window to unload everything you have. This is the decisive moment of the fight.
🏆 Clear Rewards

Bull King's Iron Horn (boss drop) + a chest behind the boss arena containing a second Bull King's Iron Horn.

5. Hazard Guide — What Can Go Wrong

The 4th Cart Is Easy to Miss

⚠ Hidden Detour

The Rusty-Gold Cart is behind a destroyed barrier on the rolling ball path from Fallen Furnace Crater. After the rolling ball clears the path, backtrack down that same route. The opening is on the left side, roughly halfway down. It is not marked on the map. If you never backtrack this path, you will never find the fourth cart — and the Pale-Axe questline will stall permanently.

Pale-Axe Quest Is Permanently Missable

⚠ Point of No Return

If you defeat the Chapter 5 story boss before exhausting the Pale-Axe Stalwart's dialogue at all three post-cart locations, his questline ends. The frozen gate will never open. Bishui Cave will be locked for the rest of this playthrough. Before fighting the chapter's final boss, verify that you have spoken to the Pale-Axe at Ashen Pass I, Emerald Hall, and Field of Fire.

Golden-Eyed Beast Without Fireproof Mantle

⚠ Vessel Is Critical

The Bishui Golden-Eyed Beast deals near-constant fire damage across the entire arena. Without the Fireproof Mantle Vessel, you will spend most of the fight dodging fire patches and healing. The Vessel's active ability is at its most impactful here — it negates a large portion of the boss's damage during key phases. If you skipped the Fireproof Mantle in Chapter 1, go back and get it before attempting this fight.
