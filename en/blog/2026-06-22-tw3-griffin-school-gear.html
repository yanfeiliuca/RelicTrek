Griffin School Gear: The Level 11 Velen Diagram Route

The basic Griffin School Gear scavenger hunt is a level-11 route across three Velen locations. Dragonslayer's Grotto contains the four armor diagrams, Lornruk holds the silver sword diagram, and Hindhold holds the steel sword diagram.

The set suits players who want to combine medium armor with frequent sign use, but it is one build option rather than a mandatory first set. The useful part of the hunt is its clear six-diagram chain and upgrade path through Grandmaster gear.

What Makes Griffin Different

The basic Griffin chest armor and both swords carry sign-intensity bonuses, while the armor pieces form a medium-armor set. Its exact value still depends on your skills, equipped pieces, difficulty, and the enemies you are fighting.

The relevant General ability is Griffin School Techniques. Each equipped piece of medium armor adds 5% sign intensity and 5% stamina regeneration. Six medium-armor pieces therefore provide 30% of each bonus when the ability is active.

That pairing supports a sign-focused build without guaranteeing specific Igni, Quen, or Yrden results at level 11.

Three Locations, One Velen Loop

The entire basic Griffin set is spread across three locations in Velen. Edwin Greloff's first and second maps can be purchased from the armorer in Midcopse; reading them adds the relevant scavenger-hunt objectives and map markers, but the diagrams can also be collected directly.

Dragonslayer's Grotto contains all four armor diagrams. Expect Wraiths inside, then use Aard on the weak wall. Beyond it are poisonous gas pockets and an Ekimmara before the chest containing the armor, boots, gauntlets, and trousers diagrams.

Lornruk holds the silver sword diagram. A Wyvern guards the ruins. The reliable entrance is to jump into the water beside the bridge while avoiding the rocks, deal with the Drowners, and follow the underwater cave past a Place of Power. Surface on the far side of the raised bridge, climb the ladder, and reach the diagram chest.

Hindhold holds the steel sword diagram. The site has Endrega workers and warriors around the lower approach and a Harpy nest above. Climb to the upper level, pass through the wooden door, and loot the remains beside the diagram chest.

Practical Tip

Players who go straight to Dragonslayer's Grotto and stop there often leave Velen without the two sword diagrams. The silver sword (Lornruk) and steel sword (Hindhold) are in completely separate locations — mark them both on your map before setting out.

Crafting Without Drama

The basic armor diagrams require a Journeyman Armorer, while the two swords require a Journeyman Blacksmith. No master-level crafter is required for the basic set.

Check the crafting screen before selling shirts, leather components, metal ingots, and monster parts: the exact recipe differs by piece.

The Upgrade Path Is Worth It

The upgrade path continues through Enhanced at level 18, Superior at 26, Mastercrafted at 34, and Grandmaster at 40. The first three tiers use Journeyman crafters, Mastercrafted pieces require master-level crafters, and Grandmaster gear requires the grandmaster smith in Beauclair.

The Grandmaster bonuses are specific. With three pieces equipped, casting a standard sign with stamina makes the next standard sign cast within three seconds cost no stamina. With six pieces, Yrden's trap size increases by 40%; while standing inside Yrden, stamina regeneration rises by 5 per second, sign intensity rises by 100%, and damage taken falls by 20%.

Note

The upgrade quests for Enhanced through Mastercrafted are separate Scavenger Hunt quests. They don't start automatically — look for new maps in merchant inventories as you level up, or keep an eye out for the diagram chests in the upgrade guide locations.

Why This Set Earns Its Place

The Griffin School Gear provides a defined medium-armor and sign-oriented path from level 11 onward. Whether it outperforms other equipment depends on the rest of the build and on how often you use signs.

If you decide to collect it, treat the hunt as three separate access puzzles rather than one cave run: prepare for Wraiths and an Ekimmara in the grotto, a water entrance at Lornruk, and Endrega plus Harpies at Hindhold.
