Yoana Master Armorer: Mastercrafted Armor Route Notes

There's a particular kind of frustration that only The Witcher 3 can produce. You collect the diagrams, gather the materials, and walk up to the armorer at Crow's Perch expecting a clean upgrade path — only to discover that the crafting service you need is still locked behind a side quest.

For base-game Mastercrafted armor, that route runs through Yoana and the "Master Armorers" quest. This note keeps the focus practical: where the unlock starts, what the quest asks for, and how it fits beside Hattori's sword service.

The Master Armorers Route

When you first encounter the armorer situation in Velen, you'll likely speak to Fergus Graem near the notice board at Crow's Perch. He can craft some gear, but base-game Mastercrafted armor sits behind a quest called "Master Armorers." Start it by asking Fergus directly about higher-tier armor work.

What unfolds is one of the more quietly satisfying sidequests in the game. Yoana, who appears to be Fergus's forge hand, is actually the craftsperson with real mastery. She's been working in his shadow, her expertise unrecognized and her status as "helper" something she's clearly tired of. She knows about a set of high-quality smithing tools held at the Old Clan Tordarroch Forge on Undvik in Skellige — tools that would let her work at the level she's always been capable of.

Practical Note

The Tordarroch Forge on Undvik is guarded by an Ice Troll. Bring fire-based Signs or oils and come prepared for a harder fight than the quest's level suggestion implies. The troll hits hard even when you're overleveled.

The Quest Steps, Efficiently

Once you've started "Master Armorers," the progression runs roughly like this. First, you travel to Undvik and fight through to the Old Clan Tordarroch Forge, defeating the Ice Troll guarding it and looting the high-quality smithing tools from a chest nearby. Then you return to Yoana, who — tools in hand — is now ready to demonstrate what she can actually do.

But the quest has one more ingredient: Archgriffin acid. Yoana needs it to forge armor of the quality she's aiming for. This unlocks a hunt for an Archgriffin spotted in the highlands. At around level 24, this is the quest's actual challenge — the Archgriffin hits from the air in ways that can catch unprepared players off-guard. Use Thunderbolt, Grapeshot bombs, and whatever crossbow bolts you have. Staying on the ground and baiting overhead passes is more reliable than trying to chase it.

The final act is a proof-of-craft test. Yoana's armor is pitted against Fergus's, demonstrated on a human subject in a way that makes the outcome unmistakable. After that, Yoana is fully unlocked as a master armorer at Crow's Perch, and her map marker reflects it. She'll also craft a unique cuirass (the Mag Deira) as part of the reward, and she starts selling rare crafting components you won't find elsewhere.

Quest Outcome Check

At the end of the quest, you're asked to agree to the armor demonstration. Refusing the test can fail the quest and may remove Yoana from Crow's Perch. Accepting the test keeps the unlock route intact.

Why Yoana and Hattori Are a Matched Set

The Witcher 3's crafting system divides labor between two master craftspeople that both need to be unlocked independently. Yoana handles armor. Hattori, the swordsmith in Novigrad, handles blades. His unlock requires completing the "Of Swords and Dumplings" quest, which is often encountered during Novigrad play. Yoana's quest, by contrast, is in Velen and requires asking Fergus specifically about higher-tier work.

The practical implication: arriving at late Witcher gear upgrades without Yoana can leave the base-game Mastercrafted armor step blocked. Handle Yoana and Hattori before deep gear cleanup so the armor and sword services are both available when diagrams start stacking up.

Timing Tip

Do "Master Armorers" during your natural Velen/Skellige exploration — don't wait until you have diagrams in hand and need her urgently. The Archgriffin hunt is easiest when you're already traveling through that region anyway. Completing both Yoana and Hattori before late gear cleanup avoids most crafting-service surprises.

What She Unlocks Beyond the Obvious

Most players know Yoana unlocks Mastercrafted armor. Fewer realize she also refreshes her inventory after each time skip or in-game day, stocking rare crafting components — hardened leather, enriched dimeritium, and similar materials — that can be tedious to farm otherwise. If you're running low on something specific for a Witcher armor upgrade, checking her shop is often faster than hunting it in the wild.

She also has a slightly different dialogue posture than most vendors. Yoana is proud of her work in a way that feels earned rather than performative. Talking to her after completing the quest and asking about her craft opens up a handful of exchanges that add texture to what crafting actually means in this world — people with real expertise, working in conditions that don't always recognize it.

The Bigger Picture

Yoana matters for more than the gate she unlocks. Her quest tells a small, complete story about expertise and recognition — the kind of thing The Witcher 3 does quietly throughout its world. She's a better craftsperson than her employer, she knows it, and she needs one witcher's help to prove it to everyone else.

If you've been playing through the Witcher gear questlines and hit an unexpected wall, the answer is probably a dwarf named Fergus at Crow's Perch and the conversation you haven't had with him yet. One quest, roughly two hours, and you'll have access to the Mastercrafted armor service — plus a vendor to revisit when you're short on materials.
