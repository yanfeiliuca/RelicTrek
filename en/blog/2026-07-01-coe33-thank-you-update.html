Clair Obscur: Expedition 33's Thank You Update: Root of All Evil and the Tower Nobody Can Beat

When Sandfall Interactive won Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2025, director Guillaume Broche used part of the acceptance speech to announce something unusual for a studio riding that high: a free content update, dropped the very next day. The Thank You Update (Title Update 1.5.0) went live on December 11-12, 2025, and it is not a token gesture. It adds a full new playable level, fresh music, dozens of new items, and four boss fights that the community is still arguing about seven months later.

For a game that already ended, that is a strange thing to be writing about in July. But the update reframed what "finished" means for Expedition 33, and the discourse around its hardest content is worth understanding before you dive in.

Verso's Drafts and the Root of All Evil

The headline addition is Verso's Drafts, a brand-new environment layered into the postgame. Tucked inside it is a sub-area called Root of All Evil, reached by taking the train near the giant Esquie Head statue by Reverie Path and riding it through a portal. Push down toward the flat ground, find the Main Field Expedition Flag, and climb the stairs to the left of it — that triggers the cutscene that summons the area's boss, Osquio.

Osquio is exclusive to this update and is written as Esquie's cousin, self-declared as the "ultimate cake eater of the universe." It is a small, funny detail sitting on top of what is otherwise one of the more demanding fights added to the game, and that contrast is very on-brand for how Expedition 33 handles its endgame.

Four New Bosses Hiding Inside the Endless Tower

The Endless Tower was already the game's gauntlet mode before the update. Thank You adds four enhanced boss variants, each tucked inside a canvas positioned behind the original gauntlet canvas, and each unlockable only after you've beaten that boss's base version elsewhere in the game. They are not reskins with more health — they carry new phases and damage checks built for parties that already have their builds figured out.

Worth Chasing

Clearing content tied to the Endless Tower now also unlocks cosmetics: a Visages outfit for Verso, and — after clearing all 33 base trials — a Ponytail haircut for Maelle from the Fading Woman standing near the tower's blank canvas.

Thirteen New Weapons and a New Merchant

Beyond the boss content, the update quietly expanded the loot pool: 13 new weapons spread across the cast, plus 16 additional Luminas for build customization. Some of that gear routes through the game's existing Gestral Merchant system, where shops can sell wardrobe pieces, music records, Pictos, weapons, Chromatic Catalysts, and Colour of Lumina — and, as with other Gestral vendors in the base game, a few of them can be fought for extra drops if your best duelist is up to it.

None of this new gear is required to finish the story. It exists for players who already beat the game and want a reason to open their save file again, which is a very different design goal than a paid expansion chasing new-player sales.

How Hard Is "Optional," Really

The update's difficulty has become its own story. Coverage described some of the new bosses as capable of "curb-stomping" experienced players, and player discussion has split into two camps: people frustrated that the fights demand reaction windows that feel unreasonably tight, and people comparing the whole experience favorably to something like Shadow of the Erdtree — brutal, but exactly what a subset of the audience wanted from a postgame.

The important caveat repeated across coverage is that everything in the Thank You Update is optional. You can ignore Root of All Evil, skip the enhanced Tower bosses, and never see Osquio, and the base game you already finished is untouched. The update is there for people looking for a reason to go back in, not a gate blocking anyone else.

Why This Is Worth Your Time

Free postgame content that is actually substantial is rare, and free postgame content designed to be genuinely hard — rather than just padded with bigger numbers — is rarer still. If you already finished Expedition 33 and shelved it, the Thank You Update gives you a real reason to reopen the file: a new area with its own boss and lore hook, a tower that finally tests an optimized party, and enough new gear to justify one more pass through your build. Go in with a level 33 loadout and the expectation that these fights were designed to be earned, not steamrolled.
