Clair Obscur: Expedition 33's Thank You Update: Verso's Drafts, Endless Tower Bosses, and New Gear

After Clair Obscur: Expedition 33's awards-season run, Sandfall Interactive released a free Thank You Update in December 2025. The accessible official and media sources reviewed for this page support a broad, concrete scope: a new playable level called Verso's Drafts, new music, new late-game Endless Tower boss battles, 13 new weapons, 16 additional Luminas, photo mode, and several quality-of-life additions.

For players returning after the credits, the important point is not that the update replaces the base ending. It adds optional postgame routes and build rewards. Some fine-grained route details still need fresh source confirmation, so this version keeps unsupported specifics out of the main advice.

Verso's Drafts Is the Confirmed Headline Addition

The headline addition is Verso's Drafts, a new environment with battles, exploration, music, and unlockables. PlayStation Blog describes it as a major free content addition, while PC Gamer frames it as a new endgame zone that late-game players can access after the required traversal progress.

Endless Tower Gets Harder Boss Content

The Endless Tower was already the game's gauntlet mode before the update. The accessible sources support new late-game boss battles or revamped boss variants tied to the tower. The exact count, unlock canvas locations, and reward conditions need a separate verification pass before they should be treated as route instructions.

Worth Chasing

Use the tower content as a postgame challenge lane, not as a mandatory story gate. Specific cosmetic rewards and trial counts are marked 待确认 in this pass because the accessible sources did not independently verify them.

Thirteen New Weapons and a New Merchant

Beyond the boss content, the update expands the loot pool with 13 new weapons and 16 additional Luminas for build customization. The official PlayStation Blog source supports those counts. Merchant duel specifics and hidden-drop routing are still 待确认 here, so keep this article at the overview level until guide pages are source-checked.

None of this new gear is required to finish the story. It exists for players who already beat the game and want a reason to open their save file again, which is a very different design goal than a paid expansion chasing new-player sales.

How Hard Is "Optional," Really

The update's difficulty has become its own story. Kotaku covered player frustration around the new boss fights, while PC Gamer flagged the Endless Tower additions as harder variants of recognizable encounters. Treat this as a difficulty warning based on media and player discussion, not as proof that every returning player will hit the same wall.

The practical caveat is that this is optional postgame content. You can focus on Verso's Drafts, skip the hardest tower fights until your build is ready, or ignore the new challenge lane entirely if you only want the completed base-game experience.

Why This Is Worth Your Time

Free postgame content that is actually substantial is rare, and this update gives returning players a real reason to reopen the file: a new area, harder tower fights, photo mode, more language support, and enough gear to revisit builds. Go in expecting optional challenge content, then verify exact route and reward details against a dedicated guide before spending a long session on cleanup.
