The Quest Most Windrose Players Abandon — And the 19 Tumbaga Ingots They Leave Behind

There is a particular frustration that visits every Windrose player at roughly the same moment. You've found a weapon you love — a Rapier, a Halberd, something that fits your build perfectly — and you look at the Weapon Ascension table and realize you need Tumbaga Ingots you don't have. Farming them one or two at a time from Ancient Chests in the Cursed Swamps feels punishing, especially when you've already cleared most of those sites.

What a lot of players don't realize is that there's a questline sitting in the swamps — "Fate of the Prophets" — that hands you 19 Tumbaga Ingots at once. Three towers, three constellation puzzles, one enormous Plague Crocodile boss, and nineteen ingots waiting on the other side. It's the largest one-time source of Tumbaga Ingots in the game, and most players either miss it entirely, find the first tower too early before the quest unlocks, or abandon the second and third towers when they hit Level 15 Plague enemies.

This is a guide for the people who want all 19.

Look Up

The Stargazer Tower questline begins in the Cursed Swamps biome at around Level 14–15. The entry point is a named map marker — Stargazer Tower — a tall stone structure with an open top and two rotating stone dials flanking its entrance. The puzzle on those dials has a reputation for stumping people. The solution is simpler than it looks.

Here's the trick: look up. The correct constellation for each dial is carved into a panel directly above the doorway. It's easy to miss if you walk straight in staring at the dials, but the moment you tilt the camera upward, the answer is right there. Match each dial to the symbol overhead, the door unlocks, clear the Plague Thralls inside, and loot the Ancient Chest for 3 Tumbaga Ingots.

Note

If you find the tower early and the Observatory Stele inside won't respond, that's not a bug — the questline is gated behind a certain story progression point. Make a mental note, continue the main quest, and return. The tower will still be waiting.

Interacting with the Stele triggers "Fate of the Prophets" and marks the other two observatories on your map, sending you deeper into the swamps.

Three Towers, Three Unique Puzzles

One thing the quest doesn't explain clearly: every observatory has a different constellation arrangement. The solution from the Stargazer Tower won't apply at the Ancient Observatory or the Northern Observatory. Read the panel above the entrance for each tower independently. Because Windrose uses procedural world generation, your tower configurations may differ from a friend's playthrough — don't rely on someone else's specific answer.

The second and third towers are significantly better-defended than the first. Level 15 Plague enemies patrol the approaches rather than guarding fixed positions, and they can catch you off-guard in the tight swamp terrain. Before pushing deeper, make sure your primary weapon is at Level 10–15 or above, and stock a generous supply of healing potions. Antidotes are worth carrying too — Plague enemies apply poison and disease debuffs, and the stacked damage-over-time can drain health faster than direct hits.

Tip

The swamp paths between towers are rich with Plague-type enemies that drop Quagmire Powder and Tainted Bile — both useful for Ingot Arborum production if you're running the Enchanting Table crafting loop. Take your time and clear them as you go.

The Plague Crocodile at the End of the Road

After all three towers are cleared, the questline sends you to the "Warlord's Death Site" near the center of the swamp island. Waiting there is Chick-Chan — a massive named Plague Crocodile and the true payoff of this questline.

Two things make this fight much more manageable. First, equip a Sun Ring before entering the arena. The surrounding Tainted Forest accelerates Wood Plague buildup, and if the meter fills completely, your character dies instantly regardless of remaining health. The Sun Ring slows that accumulation, giving you more mental bandwidth to focus on the actual fight.

Second, target the orange blisters scattered across Chick-Chan's body. Bursting a blister instantly breaks the boss's posture, triggering a stagger window where you can close in and land your heaviest melee combo before dodging out. Ranged weapons excel here — you can pop the blisters at distance and only sprint in during the stagger. The rhythm of the fight is: burst blister, close in, heavy combo, dodge out, repeat. Never fully drain your stamina. Chick-Chan has delayed slam attacks that punish panic-rolling, and empty stamina at the wrong moment usually means death.

Defeat Chick-Chan and you receive 10 Tumbaga Ingots outright. Combined with 3 from each of the three towers, the full questline pays out 19.

The Only Question Is What to Ascend

Nineteen Tumbaga Ingots is enough to ascend two or three weapons from Rare to Epic. Keep in mind that weapon ascension is permanent and one-way — ingots aren't refunded on disassembly — so think through which weapons you plan to use long-term before committing. As a general rule: prioritize your primary melee weapon first for the biggest immediate combat impact, then your primary firearm if you run a ranged build.

The timing of this questline matters more than most people realize. Complete "Fate of the Prophets" early in your Cursed Swamps progression and the ingots will fund your best ascensions precisely when you're starting to craft endgame gear. You'll feel the power spike within a single play session.

Nineteen ingots. Three puzzles. One boss. It's the best value proposition of any optional content in the Cursed Swamps — and it's sitting there waiting for anyone willing to look up.
