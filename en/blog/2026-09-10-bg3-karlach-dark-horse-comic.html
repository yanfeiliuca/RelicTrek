Karlach Is Getting Her Own Dark Horse Comic — What We Know So Far

Karlach's story is expanding beyond the game itself. At San Diego Comic-Con 2026, Dark Horse Comics announced an official Baldur's Gate 3 comic series, teased with Karlach artwork.

The Announcement

Dark Horse revealed the series as a partnership with Larian Studios and Wizards of the Coast, posting "Hey, Soldier..." alongside featured art by comic artist Becky Cloonan. [Source: x.com/DarkHorseComics] The reveal happened during SDCC on July 23, 2026. [Source: gamerant.com]

What's Still Unknown

Dark Horse has not disclosed a release date, story details, or issue count. The only confirmed elements are the publisher, the licensing partners, and Cloonan's involvement as the featured artist. [Source: gamerant.com]

Why Becky Cloonan Is a Notable Choice

Cloonan is an established comics artist and writer with a career spanning creator-owned and major-publisher work. [Source: en.wikipedia.org] Her attachment to a project that's explicitly centered on Karlach in early art suggests the character will play a central role in the series, even though Dark Horse hasn't confirmed a synopsis.

Worth Watching

No release window has been given. This is a "stay tuned" announcement, not a launch — treat any specific release-date claims elsewhere as unverified until Dark Horse confirms one.
