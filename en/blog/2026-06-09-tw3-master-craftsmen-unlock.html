Hattori: Mastercrafted Weapon Unlock Route Notes

This page is maintained as route notes for unlocking Hattori's Mastercrafted weapon forging service in Novigrad. It overlaps with the dedicated Hattori page, but keeps the focus on the crafting unlock path.

The route runs through dock negotiations, guild pressure, Sukrus, and Ernst van Hoorn's warehouse supplies before Hattori reopens his forge.

Why Mastercrafted Matters

The Witcher school gear system has four tiers: Basic, Enhanced, Superior, and Mastercrafted. Each tier requires the previous one as a component — you cannot skip steps. Mastercrafted is the ceiling of what any craftsman in the base game can produce, and it is the required upgrade path for every school set before you reach Grandmaster tier in Blood and Wine.

The gap between Superior and Mastercrafted gear is significant: you gain additional armor or damage, and set-bonus planning becomes clearer. If you intend to use Witcher gear as your primary build, this service unlock belongs on the route.

Key Fact

Hattori is the base-game blacksmith service to unlock for Mastercrafted weapons. The quest "Of Swords and Dumplings" is not marked as critical, but it gates the weapon upgrade path from Superior onward.

Meeting a Man Who Sells Dumplings

Hattori's stall sits on Glory Lane in Novigrad. When you first speak with him about swords, he deflects with practiced ease — he is a cook, not a blacksmith, and he does not know what you are talking about. Press the conversation toward his actual craft and his resistance softens slightly. He admits the truth: Novigrad's guild system forced him out. The merchant guilds that control the city's trade have made it economically impossible for an unlicensed craftsman of his caliber to operate openly.

His solution is a risky arrangement: meet Tinboy's people at the docks, negotiate a better supply deal, survive the ambush that follows, and then find someone who can protect him while the deal settles. The plan sounds straightforward. It is not.

The Quest That Earns His Trust

The questline for "Of Swords and Dumplings" unfolds across several stages. The initial dock meeting — where Hattori meets his supplier — goes sideways into an ambush. This establishes that operating in Novigrad without guild protection carries real risks, and Hattori needs more than just a witcher escort. He needs a full-time bodyguard.

Enter Sukrus, a Skellige brawler you will find involved in a dispute outside a local inn. Recruiting him into Hattori's service requires resolving a debt dispute: Sukrus's brother-in-law, a Skellige merchant named Ernst van Hoorn, owes money. Your options are to convince van Hoorn to pay up or to find another form of persuasion entirely. Either path eventually leads to the same place — van Hoorn's warehouse.

Tactical Note

The warehouse can be entered by paying 200 Crowns, by using Axii (Delusion level 3 earns bonus XP), or freely if you hold certain save history. Once inside, activate Witcher Senses to locate and mark the crates of crafting supplies along the north-western and north-eastern walls. When van Hoorn confronts you, the fight is unavoidable — but stay within the warehouse area or the quest will fail.

The fight with van Hoorn and his thugs is manageable at the recommended level of 24, though anyone pushing through under-leveled will want Swallow potions in hand. Igni handles crowd control well in the enclosed warehouse space. Your allies in the fight can fall without consequence — they survive regardless — so keep your attention on van Hoorn himself.

After the Dumplings Stop

Once the questline resolves, Hattori closes his food stall and reopens his proper forge. He appears on the map as "Hattori, Master Blacksmith" from this point forward. He also stocks crafting components that can help with later gear upgrades.

As a thank-you, he crafts you a personal gift: Blade from the Bits, a level 21 Relic-quality steel sword assembled from leftover scraps. The larger route value is the forge access itself.

Don't Miss This

The warehouse step is the point where many players lose track of the quest's real requirement: use Witcher Senses to mark all three supply crates before leaving. The later fight matters, but the crates are why Hattori can reopen his forge.

The Broader Picture

Hattori is the weapon half of the base-game crafting gate. His armor counterpart is Yoana at Crow's Perch, unlocked through Master Armorers for Mastercrafted armor. Blood and Wine adds a separate Grandmaster layer later through Lazare Lafargue in Toussaint. Keeping those tiers separate matters for any build centered on Witcher school sets.

The Witcher 3 often places important services behind optional side content. Hattori's route is one of the clearest examples: a vendor conversation in Novigrad becomes a weapon-crafting service later in the gear chain.
