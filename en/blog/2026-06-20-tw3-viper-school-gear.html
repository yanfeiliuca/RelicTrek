Viper School Gear: The Hearts of Stone Missable Checklist

The Hearts of Stone Viper gear is easy to miss because its pieces are split across two main quests and several optional actions. The useful distinction is not whether it is the game's “most missable” set, but exactly which acquisition windows close when a quest moves on.

The Three Hearts of Stone Windows

Armor diagrams: during Open Sesame!, speak with Countess Mignole before the auction and use her trade option. She sells the diagrams for the armor, boots, gauntlets, and trousers. This is the only normal acquisition window for those four diagrams.

Venomous steel sword diagram: later in the same quest, search the Borsodi vault before leaving. The diagram is in a chest near Maximilian Borsodi's house. The vault cannot be revisited after that sequence.

Venomous silver sword: in Whatsoever a Man Soweth..., choose the route that challenges Master Mirror and leads into Gaunter O'Dimm's world. The sword itself—not a normally obtainable diagram—is embedded in a stone at a red-glowing point of interest guarded by a specter.

Before the Auction

Keep roughly 1,200–1,300 crowns available and check the prices shown in your game. The four diagrams are separate purchases, and displayed buy prices can vary.

Do Not Mix It Up with the White Orchard Hunt

The base game has a separate treasure hunt for two low-level Viper sword diagrams. The silver sword diagram is below the White Orchard cemetery chapel. The steel sword diagram is in the ruined watchtower west of Ransacked Village. These diagrams are not prerequisites for the Hearts of Stone armor or venomous swords.

The White Orchard swords have fixed crafting requirements and item levels; they do not become stronger because the diagram was collected or crafted later. Their value is as an early route and a small Viper School story, not as scaling equipment.

What the Verified Stats Actually Mean

The four level-39 armor pieces list poison resistance alongside piercing, slashing, monster, and elemental resistance. Their listed poison-resistance values are 50% on the armor, 30% on the trousers, and 10% each on the boots and gauntlets.

Poison resistance protects against the poisoning status; it does not reduce the toxicity generated by potions or decoctions. Treat toxicity management as a separate alchemy system.

Both venomous swords list +75% critical-hit damage, +10% critical-hit chance, +25% Aard Sign intensity, and a 15% chance to poison. The steel sword also grants bonus experience from humans and nonhumans; the silver sword grants bonus experience from monsters. Runestones and skills can alter a build, but their effects should be evaluated separately rather than presented as an automatic set bonus.

Finale Route

Gaunter's world is timed. Follow the path until the later splits, look for the red-glowing point of interest, defeat the nearby specter, and take the sword from the stone before solving the main riddle.

Short Checklist

  • During Open Sesame!, talk and trade with Countess Mignole before the auction.
  • Buy all four armor diagrams before leaving that opportunity.
  • Loot the venomous steel sword diagram before leaving the Borsodi vault.
  • In the expansion finale, challenge Master Mirror if you want access to the venomous silver sword route.
  • Collect the two White Orchard diagrams separately; they are base-game, low-level swords.
