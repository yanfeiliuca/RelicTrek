Black Myth: Wukong's Celestial Pills: The Upgrade Trail Most Players Rush Past

Why the Celestial Pills Still Matter

Most players chasing Black Myth: Wukong's endgame gear spend their time comparing armor sets and weapon stances, and it's easy to sprint straight past the small golden pill containers tucked off the main path in every chapter. That's a mistake. The 15 Celestial Pills scattered through Chapters 1 through 5 are a permanent stat investment that no armor swap or respec can match — once collected, the bonus is baked into the character forever.

Community discussion around the game consistently flags the pills as one of the most overlooked systems in a first playthrough, mostly because they sit in dead ends, behind barricades, or along optional scaffold climbs rather than on the critical path.

What Each Pill Actually Does

The pills split into three named types, each tied to a single attribute: the Celestial Jade Lotus Pill raises maximum Health, the Celestial Taiyi Pill raises maximum Mana, and the Celestial Nonary Pill raises maximum Stamina. Guides from GameSpot and PowerPyx both confirm the same pattern — roughly three pills per major region, one of each type, spread across the game's first five chapters.

Collect the full set and the payoff is a flat, permanent boost across all three core resources, stacking on top of whatever gear and Spirit build you're running that patch.

Tip

If a pill container looks slightly out of reach, check for a nearby climbable ledge or scaffold before assuming it's inaccessible — several of the trickier ones sit just off a jump path rather than requiring a hidden switch.

The Two Pills People Think They Missed

Two locations cause most of the confusion. One Stamina pill is gated behind a side-quest chain that has to be finished before a chapter's final boss fight, which means players who rush the main story can genuinely lock themselves out of it — for that playthrough. The other sits deep inside an optional cave area, past a boss encounter, in a spot that's easy to walk by if you're beelining for the exit.

The good news, confirmed across several trophy and achievement guides: New Game+ carries your pill progress forward, and pill containers respawn on a fresh run. So even if a Stamina pill slips past you the first time, picking up any other Stamina pill in New Game+ still credits the achievement tied to collecting one of each type.

Crafted Pills Don't Count — Why That Trips People Up

Xu Dog, the game's crafting vendor, can produce pills with the same stat bonuses using Mind Cores as a crafting material. It's a legitimate way to boost your build, but it does not count toward the "Medicine Meal" achievement or trophy, which specifically tracks the 15 naturally placed Celestial Pills. Players chasing 100% completion have flagged this distinction repeatedly in community threads, since the crafted version looks identical in the stats menu but doesn't register the same way.

Worth the Ten Minutes Per Chapter

None of this requires a detour build or a wiki open at all times — just a habit of checking dead ends, cave alcoves, and side paths before moving on from a chapter. Fifteen small stops across a full playthrough buy a permanent baseline no gear swap replicates, and the two "hard" pills are quest rewards rather than secrets, so they come naturally if you're already following the game's optional content.
