The Witcher 3's Doppler Decoction Is a Backstab Buff — and It's Locked Behind One Conversation

Most of The Witcher 3's decoctions come from monsters you're going to kill anyway on the main path. The Doppler Decoction is different — the creature that drops its mutagen is a side-quest shapeshifter who spends most of the encounter trying to talk his way out of dying, and the game only gives you one shot at the conversation. Get the ending you want and the decoction is yours for every future playthrough on that save. Get the other ending and there's no second doppler waiting somewhere else to make up for it.

What the Decoction Actually Does

The Doppler Decoction's effect is a critical-hit-damage buff specifically for attacks landed from behind a target — it doesn't touch your Fast or Strong Attack skill trees directly, it just makes backstab crits hit noticeably harder while it's active. Like other decoctions, it comes with a real Toxicity cost and a long base duration (around 1,800 in-game duration units, roughly half an hour), and every point you put into the Alchemy skill line stretches that duration further, so a committed alchemist build barely has to re-drink it between fights.

That makes it a natural pairing for rotations built around flanking — Whirl into a repositioned strike, Feline school's mobility, or any playstyle where you're circling behind an enemy rather than trading blows head-on. It's a niche pick next to bread-and-butter decoctions like Katakan or Ekhidna, but for a backstab-heavy build it's a straightforward damage spike with no downside beyond the toxicity you're already managing.

An Elusive Thief: The Quest That Decides It

The only route to the Doppler Mutagen is the Novigrad contract "An Elusive Thief," picked up from the notice board in Hierarch Square and handed out by a merchant named Sylvester Amello, who's had a shapeshifting "imp" raiding his stall. Witcher Senses carry you through a genuinely fun little detective chain — tracks, dropped food, a letter confirming it's a doppler, a perfume trail leading to a guard harassing an elf who's actually the doppler mid-disguise. Once he bolts, you have to stay close; fall too far behind and the contract fails outright.

The fight itself is a formality — he copies Geralt's moveset but not Geralt's gear, so knocking him to a quarter health is quick work. What matters is what happens next: he surrenders and asks to be spared. That's the entire decision. Kill him and you get the Doppler Mutagen, his trophy (a passive +5% gold gain), and the full contract reward from Sylvester. Spare him and he hands over some coin and a weapon diagram before fleeing for good — no mutagen, no trophy, and only half the payout from the merchant, since Geralt has no proof to show him.

There's no do-over. Per-playthrough, this is the only doppler that drops the mutagen ingredient — other dopplers encountered elsewhere in the story don't carry it. If you let this one go, the decoction is off the table for that save.

Brewing It: What You Need

Beyond the mutagen, the recipe calls for one Dwarven Spirit, one Han Fiber, and one Longrube — none of them rare, all easy to have stockpiled by the time you're deep into the Novigrad questline. The formula itself doesn't require finishing the contract first; it can be found as a diagram well before you ever meet the doppler, including a spot in White Orchard near the abandoned campsite east of the cemetery and another tucked into the elven ruins library in the Byways. Picking it up early just means one less thing to think about when the contract's moral fork actually arrives.

Worth the Trade-Off?

The Doppler Decoction won't reshape a build the way a toxicity-focused mutation or a core Sign investment will — it's a targeted damage tool for a specific angle of attack. What makes it worth flagging isn't the power level, it's the shape of the choice: a small, well-written side quest where the "good" ending and the "optimal" ending point in opposite directions, and the game trusts you to only find out which is which once. If backstab damage matters to your build, that's reason enough to pick the fight.

Tip: Grab the recipe diagram in White Orchard or the Byways early, so by the time Sylvester Amello's contract shows up in Novigrad you already know exactly what the fight is worth to you.
