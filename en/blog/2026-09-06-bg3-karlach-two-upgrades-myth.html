The Karlach 'Three Upgrades' Myth: What Dammon Actually Does to Her Engine

Search for Karlach's infernal engine and almost every guide — RelicTrek's included, until today — describes "three upgrades across three acts." It's a useful shorthand, but it's not quite what the game actually does. Dammon only performs two real upgrades. The third act isn't a third upgrade; it's where the second one usually happens if you're running behind schedule.

What the Quest Data Actually Says

According to bg3.wiki's breakdown of the "Forging a Heart" quest, "Karlach's engine can receive two upgrades from Dammon," who can be met in a new location during each act — the Emerald Grove in Act One, Last Light Inn in Act Two, and the Forge of the Nine in Act Three. [Source: bg3.wiki] Three possible meeting points, two actual upgrades.

Why the First Meeting Doesn't Always Count

Depending on when you first find him, that first conversation can be more assessment than repair. [Source: bg3.wiki] If you meet Dammon for the first time at Last Light Inn or later, both upgrades can sometimes be delivered there in a single Long Rest instead of being spread across two separate visits. [Source: bg3.wiki]

The Hard Ceiling

Two is the maximum. Dammon's repairs can't fully fix the engine — they can only postpone Karlach's fate long enough to reach the endgame choice. [Source: orbispatches.com]

Why This Distinction Matters

If you're chasing "the fully upgraded Karlach" on a fast playthrough, you don't need three separate Dammon conversations spread across three acts — you need two successful repairs, which can happen faster than most walkthroughs imply. Understanding the actual mechanic changes how you plan your route through Acts One and Two.
