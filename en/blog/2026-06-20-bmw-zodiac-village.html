Zodiac Village Route Notes: Chen Loong, Ruyi Scroll, and Yin Tiger

This page is maintained as route notes for unlocking Zodiac Village. The key check is simple: from the North Shore of Bitter Lake shrine, turn back along the water's edge to find Chen Loong and begin the Ruyi Scroll chain.

That dragon is Chen Loong, and completing his request unlocks Zodiac Village. The village gathers four core upgrade NPCs in one fast-travel hub, opens Yin Tiger's armor-upgrade service, and gives you access to his optional duel.

What Zodiac Village Provides

Zodiac Village is a reusable fast-travel hub for Yin Tiger, Shen Monkey, Xu Dog, and Chen Loong. Yin Tiger upgrades armor, Shen Monkey handles gourd-related upgrades, Xu Dog provides medicine services, and Chen Loong manages seed planting. The Five Skandhas reward is later cleanup: it requires collecting all five before returning to Xu Dog.

The important distinction is timing. Speaking with Yin Tiger gives access to armor upgrades. The additional Curio slot is a separate reward: challenge and defeat Yin Tiger, then speak with him again and choose the option to equip more Curios.

Finding Chen Loong: The Questline Walkthrough

The chain has four steps. Reach Chapter 3's Bitter Lake area and fast-travel to the North Shore of Bitter Lake Keeper's Shrine. From there — and this is the part players miss — turn around and follow the coastal path away from the main route. Chen Loong is at the water's edge. He looks formidable, and he is: he's your first obstacle, and defeating him is required to start the questline.

Defeat Chen Loong, then continue the conversation until he asks for medicine from Xu Dog. The quest does not require a fixed character level or a specific spell setup.

Where to Get the Medicine

Fast-travel to the Crouching Tiger Temple Cellar Keeper's Shrine in Chapter 2. Speak with Xu Dog to receive the Special-made Bone-Strengthening Pellet; it is a quest item, not a shop purchase. If he is absent, complete his earlier Sandgate Village encounter first.

Return to Chen Loong with the pellet. He recovers, thanks you, and hands over the Ruyi Scroll. Use the scroll and you'll be pulled into Zodiac Village, which then appears in the travel menu as a hub for these vendors.

Making the Most of Your Time in the Village

When you first arrive, go left to Yin Tiger to access armor upgrades. If you want the additional Curio slot, use the separate challenge dialogue, defeat him, and speak with him again afterward.

Xu Dog remains useful for medicine services. The Five Skandhas hand-in cannot be completed early in Chapter 3; return only after collecting all five later in the game.

Chen Loong's seed garden is a slower investment. If you've been picking up seeds during your travels, plant them here. They grow over real playtime and provide ingredient returns when you revisit — useful for crafting medicine and pills in bulk. It's not urgent, but it's worth starting early so the plants are ready when you need them.

Yin Tiger: The Optional Boss You Should Attempt

Once you've used Yin Tiger's forge, you can challenge him to a fight. Winning grants the Ebon Flow transformation spell and access to an additional Curio slot through his post-fight dialogue. His armor-upgrade service remains available.

Yin Tiger uses delayed swings, long attack chains, and fast gap-closing moves. No single spell or stance is mandatory. Focus on learning the release timing of his delayed attacks and wait for a chain to finish before committing to a counterattack.

Dodge Timing

Several attacks delay the final impact. Watch a full chain before reacting, and use clear recovery windows instead of relying on an unsupported fixed-level recommendation.

A Hub That Rewards Curiosity

Zodiac Village changes the rest of the route by centralizing four important NPCs. Armor upgrades are available immediately; Ebon Flow and the additional Curio slot remain tied to winning the optional Yin Tiger duel.

The route begins with an optional dragon encounter behind a shrine on the North Shore of the Bitter Lake. Completing Chen Loong's request unlocks the village hub, while Yin Tiger's duel remains a separate optional reward step.
