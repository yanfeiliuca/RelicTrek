Clair Obscur: Expedition 33's Thank You Update: What's Actually in Verso's Drafts

A GOTY speech turned into a free expansion

Most post-launch content gets teased in a trailer. Clair Obscur: Expedition 33's Thank You Update was announced on stage, during Game Director Guillaume Broche's acceptance speech after the game won Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2025. That origin story matters because it set expectations high, and the update — released free on December 12, 2025 as patch 1.5.0 — mostly delivers on them. Nearly a year after launch, Lumiere is still worth a return trip.

Quick context

The Thank You Update is free for everyone who owns the base game. No separate purchase, no season pass — just a patch.

What's actually inside Verso's Drafts

The headline addition is Verso's Drafts, a new playable area found east of Lumiere. You'll need Esquie's underwater diving ability to reach it, which places it firmly in Act 3 territory rather than something you can rush into on a fresh save. Once you're in, expect roughly five hours of new environments, battles, and music if you're exploring thoroughly rather than beelining the exit.

Tucked inside Verso's Drafts is a sub-zone called Root of All Evil, which houses the DLC's final boss encounter. Across the whole area there are six Expedition Flags to track down, and altogether the update adds around 149 new items to hunt through — weapons, Luminas, and miscellaneous collectibles included.

New weapons and Luminas worth building around

Combat-focused players get 13 new weapons out of the update, including a candy scythe that's become one of the more talked-about additions for its look alone. On the customization side, there are 16 additional Luminas to unlock, which is a meaningful bump for a game whose endgame builds already lean heavily on Lumina stacking.

The more practically useful change might be Lumina Sets: you can now save up to 50 different Lumina loadouts. If you've been manually rebuilding your setup every time you swap from a boss-killer build to an exploration build, this alone is worth updating for.

The Endless Tower got meaner on purpose

If you cleared the Endless Tower before the update and haven't been back, there's a reason to return. The tower's red paintings — previously decorative — now double as portraits for four new boss encounters: Chromatic Lampmaster, Duollistes, Clea Unleashed, and Simon, the Divergent Star. Each is a harder variant of an existing boss with new attack patterns, and each only unlocks after you've beaten its original counterpart, so this is deliberately gated toward players who already know the fights.

Player-reported approach

Community discussion around the update suggests treating the tower as a slow burn rather than a single push: clear easier floors first, farm the trash enemies for upgraded weapon drops, and save the harder new boss variants for later visits. The tower's difficulty scales as you go back to it, so there's no rush to finish it in one sitting.

Quality-of-life additions that quietly matter

Beyond new content, the update adds a full Photo Mode, a battle abandon option for encounters you'd rather bail on, and additional language support. None of these move the needle on their own, but together they're the kind of polish that tends to show up in a "thank you" patch specifically — the team clearly used this as a chance to fix small friction points players had been asking about since launch.

Why this is worth your time

Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 could have coasted on its Game of the Year win. Instead, the Thank You Update gives returning players a genuine reason to reopen a finished save: a new area with real content behind it, gear worth planning builds around, and a harder endgame challenge for anyone who thought they'd already seen everything Lumiere had to offer.
