The Witcher 3 Mastercrafted Feline Gear: Why the Cat School's Best No-DLC Set Is Worth the Chase

There is a moment in most Witcher 3 playthroughs when you realize you have been sleeping on the Feline school. You are somewhere in Novigrad, watching your light attacks bounce off half the enemies in the area, and someone in a community post offhandedly mentions the word "Mastercrafted." That's the moment this guide is for.

The Mastercrafted Feline set is the strongest Cat School gear available without the Blood and Wine expansion — six pieces spread across Velen and Novigrad, requiring roughly three hours to collect if you know exactly where you are going. It is also, quietly, one of the trickier treasure hunt setups in the base game, because one of its most essential crafters is hiding behind a disguise and a multi-step side quest.

The Hattori Problem — And Why You Should Solve It First

Before you touch a single diagram, go find Hattori. He is in the Bits district of Novigrad, dressed as a dumpling vendor near the fish market. He used to be the finest swordsmith in the city, and now he sells dumplings because it is safer. The quest that restores his craft — "Of Swords and Dumplings" — involves sourcing steel, dealing with the Cleaver gang, and sitting down for a meal. It takes maybe an hour, and it is genuinely one of the better side quests in Novigrad.

Why does this matter so much? Because Hattori is the only blacksmith in the base game capable of forging Mastercrafted-tier Witcher swords. Every other crafter will turn you away. Players who collect all six diagrams and then walk up to a random armorer expecting them to forge the swords are in for a rude surprise. Do the quest first, unlock Hattori, and everything else flows cleanly from there.

Bonus During Hattori's Quest

During "Of Swords and Dumplings," the gang's warehouse has a secondary safe — combination 1-2-3-4 — containing a Superior Feline steel sword diagram. It is ahead of schedule for where you likely are in the Cat School progression, and well worth grabbing.

Six Diagrams, Two of Them Underwater

The diagram hunt works best as a two-zone sweep: Novigrad first, then Velen. Start at Drahim Castle in eastern Novigrad. Descend to the basement — two ladders — and you will find the steel sword diagram in a corner chest. The armor diagram is nearby in a hidden side room accessible by pressing a stone switch on the eastern corridor wall. That false wall moment is the one detail that sends players hunting through forum posts, so now you know.

From Drahim, head to the western coast of Novigrad for the first underwater diagram. A sunken ship rests near the coast with the boots diagram in a chest near the bow. Then move to the Grassy Knoll area where a second shipwreck sits half-submerged — dive alongside the hull to find the silver sword diagram in a chest against the ship's interior ribs. Neither dive requires anything special beyond Geralt's basic swimming ability.

The final two pieces — trousers and gauntlets — are in the Codgers Quarry area in southeastern Velen, split between the quarry building itself and a small cave to the east. Both are guarded by bandits that are manageable at level 31.

Tip — Drahim Castle Bonus Loot

The upper floors of Drahim Castle have a locked chest belonging to a Temerian knight. The key is on the captain outside. The chest contains the Temerian Armor set — one of the most visually distinctive non-school armors in the entire game.

The Two Crafters You Cannot Skip

Getting all six diagrams is half the work. Crafting requires two specific masters: Hattori in Novigrad for both swords, and Yoana at Crow's Perch in Velen for the four armor pieces. Yoana becomes a master armorer after you complete her quest. If you have been playing a Cat School build from the start, you likely already know her — but if you came to this tier from a different direction, check whether you have unlocked her before hauling the materials across the map.

The crafting materials are consistent across the tier: Dimeritium Ore and Ingots, Cured Leather, Monster Carapace, Monster Bone, and Monster Brain. Dimeritium is the bottleneck — buy it from merchants in Novigrad and Oxenfurt if you have not been stockpiling it. The other materials drop reliably from the monsters you have been fighting throughout the main game.

Building Around the Feline Set

The Cat School bonus rewards fast, aggressive play. Light attacks deal bonus damage and build adrenaline quickly, which feeds into skills like Whirl for crowd control and Fleet Footed for defense through movement. The community consensus for a pure fast-attack build leans into Muscle Memory, Precise Blows, and Cat School Techniques — the last of which requires wearing exclusively light armor to function, so the full Feline set is essential, not optional.

For players who want to mix in signs, the Feline set's fast stamina regeneration also enables brief but effective Igni and Aard bursts without interrupting your attack rhythm. It is a more versatile foundation than it first appears.

One Rule Before You Move On

If you have Blood and Wine, or plan to play it eventually, there is one thing you absolutely cannot do: sell or dismantle any Mastercrafted Feline piece. The Grandmaster Feline set in Toussaint requires that you present all six Mastercrafted pieces to the craftsman there. No pieces, no upgrade path. Stash everything in your storage chest once you reach Blood and Wine, and leave it there until you are ready to upgrade.

The Mastercrafted Feline set is worth the chase not just as a build endpoint, but as a foundation. Two crafters to unlock, six locations to visit, two dives to take — and at the end of it, the strongest fast-attack setup the base game offers. The cat is patient. So can you be.
