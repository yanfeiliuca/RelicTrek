The Five Skandhas Pill: Black Myth: Wukong's Reward for Patient Explorers

Most rewards in Black Myth: Wukong arrive the moment you earn them: a boss falls and you get a spell, you open a chest and you get a gourd. The Five Skandhas Pill works differently. It asks you to carry a promise across almost the whole game. Five odd stone fragments, picked up from broken Buddha heads in five different chapters, do nothing at all until the last one is in your hands. That's why so many players finish the game without ever seeing the pill. It's also why the questline is worth a closer look, especially now that many players are returning for New Game+ runs and completion passes.

A Reward Built on Patience

The Skandhas are named after the five aggregates of Buddhist thought: Form, Feeling, Thought, Choice, and Consciousness. Together they describe what makes up a living being. The game turns that idea into a collection route, and it fits well. You can't take a shortcut to Consciousness. You get there only by carrying the other four with you. In practice, the fifth Skandha won't appear at all unless you already hold the first four, so the whole chain only works if you've been paying attention since the Forest of Wolves.

When you bring all five to Xu Dog in Zodiac Village and choose the option to show him the Skandhas, he gives you the Five Skandhas Pill. It is a celestial medicine that permanently raises your core vitals (Health, Mana, and Stamina) in one go. Our full guide records it as roughly +20 across the board. That is about the same as several attribute points' worth of investment, and you get it free on top of your build.

The First Four: Easy to Walk Past

The early Skandhas aren't difficult to get. They're just easy to miss. Form sits at a Buddha-face rock in Chapter 1's Forest of Wolves, a short walk from the first shrine. If you've already fought the Bullguard, you've gone past it. Thought is at the end of a hidden cave route below Mindfulness Cliff in Chapter 3's Valley of Ecstasy, on the same detour that leads to a Fiery Gourd chest. Choice is in Chapter 4's Webbed Hollow, past the Purifying Spring shrine, on a cocoon-covered shore at the far end of the dark lake.

Feeling, from Chapter 2, is the one that takes real work. You'll need to collect the six Buddha's Eyeballs scattered around Fright Cliff, set them into the great rock near Rockrest Flat, and then defeat Shigandang, a stone guardian much tougher than the Stone Vanguard you fought nearby. Immobilize gives you safe damage windows, and Pillar Stance lifts you above his ground slams.

Explorer's Tip

Look for broken Buddha heads as you move through each chapter. Any stone face resting on the ground, by a riverbank, or above dark water deserves a second look. The Skandhas are always tucked just off the main path, never in plain view.

Giant Shigandang: The Exam at the End

Chapter 6 is where all that patience pays off. In the Foothills, a large rock formation in a side valley stays quiet unless you're carrying the first four Skandhas. If you are, Giant Shigandang rises out of it. He's one of the more unusual fights in the game. He can't move from his spot, so you can stay out of range for most of his attacks. When he's tired his arms rest on the ground, and that's your opening. The blue crystals on his hands take heavy damage, so aim for them.

Be careful as his health runs out, because he finishes with a burst of shockwaves. Hold a Transformation in reserve for that moment, or save your stamina for well-timed dodges. When he finally falls, climb up his arm to take the Skandha of Consciousness. It's a fitting image: you climb the stone giant to reach the last piece.

Don't Cut It Close

Treat the Skandhas as a running checklist, not something to clean up at the end. If you push through the finale without the first four, Giant Shigandang never appears, and you'll need another cycle to finish the set.

New Game+ and the Long Game

Many returning players don't realize the questline can be repeated in later cycles. Community guides report that a second pill doesn't stack another vitals boost. Instead, the reward converts into Will (the game's currency). So the real value comes on your first clear, where the stat boost carries into every fight after it, including the toughest late-game encounters. If you're starting a fresh save, plan for it from Chapter 1. If you're already in NG+, think of it as a nice extra rather than a requirement.

Why This Is Worth Your Time

The Five Skandhas Pill is a good example of what makes Black Myth: Wukong rewarding to explore slowly. It pays off curiosity that lasts across the whole journey, not just quick reactions in one fight. The stat boost is real and permanent, and Giant Shigandang is a memorable boss you'd otherwise never meet. Collecting the set also gets you looking at the edges of each chapter, where the game hides many of its best moments. Pick up Form in the Forest of Wolves, and let the rest of the route come to you.
