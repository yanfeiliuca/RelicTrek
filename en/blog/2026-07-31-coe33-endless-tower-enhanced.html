The Thank You Update Made Expedition 33's Endless Tower the Hardest Fight in the Game

When Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 won Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2025, director Guillaume Broche used the acceptance speech to tease something for the people still playing: a free update, no strings attached. That update, the Thank You Update, shipped on December 12, 2025, and buried inside it is the single hardest stretch of combat the game has ever asked of anyone — four bosses hiding behind the Endless Tower's original gauntlet.

A Second Gauntlet, Locked Behind the First

If you already cleared the base Endless Tower, you know the rhythm: floor after floor building toward a boss gauntlet at the top. According to the Expedition 33 wiki, the Thank You Update tucks a second gauntlet directly behind that one, inside its own canvas, and it stays closed until you've beaten the original. Waiting on the other side are four remixed versions of encounters you already know: Chromatic Lampmaster, Duollistes, Clea Unleashed, and Simon, the Divergent Star.

These aren't stat-padded reskins. Per the individual boss pages on the Expedition 33 wiki, each fight was rebuilt with new attack patterns and mechanics layered onto the original's foundation, meaning the muscle memory you built fighting the base versions only carries you partway through.

How the Four Actually Stack Up

Community consensus, still forming since the update is only weeks old, is fairly consistent: Duollistes is the entry point. It's the least punishing of the four, and by multiple accounts it's farmable — a clean, repeatable clear rather than a one-shot gauntlet piece. Simon comes next, followed by Chromatic Lampmaster, with Clea Unleashed sitting at the top as the fight most players are still working to close out.

That ordering should shape how you spend your resources. Don't burn your best revival items and buffs on Duollistes just because the game puts it first — save your deepest reserves for Clea.

Reward Math

Each of the four enhanced bosses drops a Perfect Chroma Catalyst on defeat — the same material used to push weapons to their final upgrade tier. If your party's weapons aren't maxed yet, clearing this gauntlet is currently one of the more reliable ways to close that gap.

What Else the Update Quietly Fixed

The Thank You Update wasn't only about punishment. Coverage from TechRadar notes it also added a full photo mode, new costumes, and additional weapons and Luminas to diversify builds. The most useful addition for anyone actually attempting these four bosses is Lumina Sets — the ability to save a full Lumina loadout and swap to another instantly, instead of manually rebuilding your setup before every attempt.

That feature changes how preparation should work here. Build one loadout tuned for sustained AP output and survivability, a second tuned for burst damage, and swap between them boss to boss rather than forcing one setup to cover four very different fights.

Why This Is Worth Your Time

If you finished Expedition 33 months ago and shelved it after cleaning up the base game's toughest optional fights, this is the first content since launch that gives a maxed-out party something to genuinely worry about. Four remixed bosses, gated behind a gauntlet you already conquered once, aren't filler — they read like a team rewarding the players who stuck around after the credits rolled. And with a Perfect Chroma Catalyst waiting behind every one of them, there's a concrete reason to go back in beyond bragging rights.
