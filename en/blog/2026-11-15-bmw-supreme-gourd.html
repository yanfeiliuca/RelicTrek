Ten Charges Is Only the Start: Building Around the Supreme Gourd in Black Myth: Wukong

Most talk about the Supreme Gourd stops at the moment you get it: a hidden room in Furnace Valley, a jump across molten ground, a flame that turns your fully upgraded gourd into a ten-charge vessel. That is the right place to start, and our guide walks that route step by step. But late in Chapter 5 and into Chapter 6, the more useful question is a different one. Ten charges of what? The gourd is only the container. What goes inside it, and when you trade it for something else, decides how many of those ten sips actually save a fight.

What the Supreme Gourd Is, and What It Isn't

The Supreme Gourd is the end of the Old Gourd's upgrade line. You raise the Old Gourd through its tiers with Luojia Fragrant Vines at Shen Monkey until it reaches nine charges as the Medicine Buddha Gourd, then carry it to the fire in the small building across the lava in Furnace Valley, reached from the Rakshasa Palace Shrine by the easy-to-miss left-hand stairs. The flame gives you the tenth charge.

The detail people overlook is what you don't get. The Supreme Gourd carries no special effect at all. It is the highest charge count in the game, and that is its entire identity. Every other gourd in the game trades charges for a perk. Knowing that changes how you should think about it: it is the safe default, not automatically the best tool for every boss.

Before You Cross the Lava

The flame only works on a gourd that already has nine charges. If you arrive short, the room is not lost. You can fast travel back to the Rakshasa Palace Shrine later, once your vines are in.

The Drink Matters More Than the Tenth Charge

Each sip heals a share of your maximum health, and the drink in the gourd sets that share. The starting Coconut Wine heals 55% per sip once upgraded. A Thousand Days of Inebriation pushes that to 60%, but the drunkenness briefly slows your movement, which is a real cost against fast bosses. Loong Balm heals only 30%, but it powers up your next attack. Pine Brew heals 35% and grants Focus when you drink below half health. Lambbrew splits the heal into a small instant portion and a larger heal over time.

Do the arithmetic and the tenth charge looks smaller. One extra sip of a 55% drink is roughly half a health bar across a whole fight. Swapping from a weak drink to a strong one improves every one of your ten sips. If you have spare Awaken Wine Worms, spending them on your main drink at Shen Monkey often does more for survival than anything else you can do in Chapter 5.

Worm Budget

Fully upgrading every drink takes more Awaken Wine Worms than one playthrough provides. Pick one main drink and upgrade it first, rather than spreading worms thinly across several.

Soaks Turn a Heal Into a Move

Soaks are the ingredients you steep in your drink, and upgraded drinks open more soak slots, up to four on the best ones. They are where the healing system becomes a combat system. Iron Pellet makes your drinking impossible to interrupt and adds strong damage reduction, which fixes the most common way players die: drinking at the wrong moment and getting hit mid-sip. Flower Primes clears all four Banes, burn, shock, poison and chill, at once. Turtle Tear restores Mana if you drink at full health. Goat Skull briefly raises your maximum health.

Good combinations depend on the fight. Against a boss that stacks a Bane, Flower Primes turns your gourd into a cure as well as a heal. Against a boss that punishes every opening, Iron Pellet keeps your sips safe. With ten charges available, you can afford to treat some sips as tools rather than emergencies.

When to Put the Supreme Gourd Away

Because you can swap gourds from the pause menu at any time, the Supreme Gourd does not have to be permanent. The Xiang River Goddess Gourd holds eight charges and gives burn resistance plus brief immunity to lava-covered ground, which is a natural pick for Chapter 5's fire-heavy areas. The Fiery Gourd holds seven and adds chill resistance and stamina. The Jade Guanyin Gourd also holds eight. At the far end, the Plaguebane Gourd holds just five and halves its healing, but it boosts your attack after each sip, which suits players who would rather end fights fast than outlast them.

A sensible habit: keep the Supreme Gourd as your exploring and learning gourd, when you are still reading a boss and need the most room for mistakes. Once you know a fight well, try a specialist gourd that answers that fight's main threat. Two fewer charges often cost less than the perk saves.

Why This Is Worth Your Time

The trip to Furnace Valley is short and the reward is permanent, so the Supreme Gourd belongs in every run. The larger payoff comes from treating it as the base of a kit: one strong drink, soaks chosen for the fight ahead, and the judgment to swap to a specialist gourd when a boss calls for it. That turns a quiet upgrade into the most flexible tool you carry through the back half of the game.
