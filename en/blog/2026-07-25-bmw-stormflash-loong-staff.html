The Highest-Attack Weapon in Black Myth: Wukong Only Exists on Your Second Run

Most "best weapon in the game" conversations end with a farming route: kill the right boss enough times, grind the right currency, and eventually the gear is yours. The Stormflash Loong Staff doesn't work that way. It carries 140 base attack — the highest of any weapon in Black Myth: Wukong — but the crafting menu for it doesn't even exist until New Game Plus, and the ingredients trace back to a set of optional bosses that a lot of players never bother fighting the first time through. If you want it, the planning starts now, not in NG+.

What 140 Attack Actually Buys You

The Stormflash Loong Staff is a Mythical-rarity weapon with 140 base attack and a 9% critical hit chance, drawing Thunder element while charging. Its unique effect inflicts Thunder Damage with every staff attack once the Destined One is in Shocked State — which flips the usual logic of avoiding status effects on its head. Instead of playing around Thunder attacks from enemies, this staff wants you stepping into them on purpose, since the Shocked State it triggers becomes the source of bonus damage rather than a debuff to dodge.

It doesn't stand alone, either. Stormflash is the upgraded form of the Kang-Jin Staff, and the crafting menu only unlocks once you already hold that base weapon in your inventory.

The Real Gatekeepers Are Four Dragons You Might Have Skipped

Before any of the Loong bosses are even reachable, you need the Loong Scales key item, tucked inside Chapter 2's Yellow Wind Ridge. It's found in the same arena where you fight the First Prince of Flowing Sands — crack open the wall there and a treasure chest is waiting behind it. Without the Scales in your inventory, the four Loong dragons simply aren't accessible.

Those four are Red Loong, Black Loong, Cyan Loong, and Yellow Loong, spread across Chapters 1 through 4. Yellow Loong is the one to prepare for: it only becomes fightable after the other three are dead, and by most accounts it's noticeably faster and more aggressive than its siblings, with a moveset built around delayed lightning arcs that punish players who commit to attacks too early. Hunting all four pays off twice over, too — beyond feeding into Stormflash's crafting chain, defeating the full set of Loong bosses is also what opens the door to the Golden Loong Staff, so this isn't wasted effort even if New Game Plus isn't on your mind yet.

Before You Finish Your First Run

Craft the Kang-Jin Staff at a Keeper's Shrine before you close out your first playthrough. The Stormflash upgrade won't appear in the crafting menu at all until Kang-Jin Staff is already sitting in your inventory, so this is the one step that's easy to forget and expensive to go back for.

Why the Crafting Bill Only Makes Sense in NG+

The full recipe is steep: 45,000 Will, 9 Loong Pearls, 4 Gold Tree Cores, and 2 Kun Steel, and the option to craft it is locked to New Game Plus entirely — there's no route to it on a first playthrough no matter how much you grind. Gold Tree Core and Kun Steel are the more forgiving pieces; both show up commonly enough in late-game areas that most players accumulate them without specifically farming. Loong Pearls are the bottleneck, since they trace directly back to the dragon fights and the mandatory Kang-Jin Star boss rather than any general drop table.

Planning Your Run Instead of Reacting to It

The practical takeaway is less about any single fight and more about sequencing. Treat the Loong Scales pickup and the four dragon fights as checklist items alongside your normal Chapter 1–4 progress, the same way you'd track a missable side quest, rather than something to circle back for later. By the time you reach New Game Plus, the goal isn't to start hunting dragons from scratch — it's to already have the Kang-Jin Staff crafted and the dragon fights fresh in memory, so the NG+ pass toward Stormflash becomes a matter of repeating known fights rather than relearning the whole chain under time pressure.

Why This Is Worth Your Time

Black Myth: Wukong rarely announces which optional content is going to matter later, and the Stormflash Loong Staff is a clean example of that pattern paying off for players who explore anyway. The highest-attack weapon in the game isn't hidden behind reflexes or luck — it's hidden behind whether you bothered to fight four dragons you didn't strictly have to fight. Do that once, and New Game Plus stops being a second grind and starts being the victory lap.
