Witcher 3's Cat Potion Got a Crit Buff — Is Superior Cat Worth Brewing?

For most of the last decade, the Cat potion line in The Witcher 3 had exactly one job: let Geralt see in the dark. Nobody brewed it for damage. Then Patch 4.0, CD Projekt Red's next-gen overhaul from December 2022, quietly bolted a critical hit chance bonus onto every tier of the line — and at the top of that line, Superior Cat now grants +10% crit chance on top of the night vision and hypnosis immunity it always had. Years after launch, that's still a change worth understanding if you're running a crit build.

A Buff Nobody Asked For, But Everyone Can Use

The Cat line's crit bonus scales cleanly across its three tiers: Cat sits at 5%, Enhanced Cat at 7%, and Superior Cat tops out at 10%. These are flat additive percentages, not multipliers, and there's no pre-Patch-4.0 baseline to compare them against — the crit chance simply didn't exist before the update. It's worth not confusing this with the Katakan decoction, a separate item whose existing crit bonus was doubled from 5% to 10% in the same patch. Two different items got crit buffs in Patch 4.0, and it's easy to mix them up if you're going off memory of an old build guide.

Why This Matters Now

Plenty of crit-build guides for The Witcher 3 predate Patch 4.0 and never account for this. If you're rebuilding a Cat School crit setup from an older writeup, the potion math has changed under you.

Where It Actually Fits Into a Crit Build

Superior Cat isn't a build on its own — it's a stacking layer. Community-tested Cat School crit builds generally lean on light armor plus Cat School techniques from the neutral skill tree, aiming for roughly 30% critical hit chance on light attacks and 35% critical damage on heavy attacks before potions even enter the picture. Against that baseline, Superior Cat's +10% is a meaningful top-up with essentially no downside beyond the toxicity cost, since it stacks separately from skill- and gear-based crit bonuses. If your build doesn't scale off crits at all, the potion is still useful for its original job — you just won't notice the extra 10% doing much.

Practical Tip

Because the crit bonus is additive and stacks outside your skill tree, it's most noticeable on builds already sitting in the 25–35% crit chance range from gear and skills — that's where an extra 10% starts turning near-misses into criticals rather than just nudging a number that was already low.

Brewing It: What You Actually Need

Superior Cat is crafted through Alchemy after learning its manuscript, and it upgrades directly from a brewed Enhanced Cat rather than from raw ingredients alone. The full craft requires White Gull ×1, Enhanced Cat ×1, Berbercane Fruit ×4, Cortinarius ×4, Moleyarrow ×1, Allspice ×1, and Aether ×1 — so budget time to brew the Enhanced tier first if you haven't already. Manuscripts for potions like this typically show up in guarded treasure or from herbalists and alchemists in major settlements; one community-reported possible stock location is Otto Bamber at the Herbalist's Hut northeast of Oxenfurt, though stock varies by playthrough, so treat that as a lead rather than a guarantee.

Worth Your Time?

If you're already committed to a crit-focused Cat School build, Superior Cat is close to a no-brainer — it's a free stacking bonus that costs you nothing but a toxicity slot you were probably filling with a Cat potion anyway. If critical hits aren't a pillar of your build, the potion still earns its keep the old-fashioned way, lighting up caves and crypts and shrugging off hypnosis, with the crit chance riding along as a quiet bonus rather than the reason to brew it. Either way, it's a small but real example of how a five-year-old game's next-gen patch reshaped builds that predate it — worth double-checking against any crit-build guide you're still using from before December 2022.
