The Five Skandhas Pill: Black Myth Wukong's Best-Kept Permanent Upgrade

There's a category of secret that Black Myth: Wukong does especially well — the kind where the individual pieces seem meaningless, but the whole is extraordinary. The Five Skandhas questline is perhaps the finest example. Five small orbs, collected from broken Buddha heads across five chapters. Each one useless on its own. Together, they unlock a permanent +20 boost to every core stat in the game. And a surprising number of players finish the story without ever knowing it existed.

What Makes This Upgrade Different

Most permanent upgrades in Black Myth: Wukong require you to choose — more Health means less investment in Mana, more Attack means less Defense. The Five Skandhas Pill sidesteps that calculus entirely. It grants +20 to Health, Mana, Stamina, Attack, and Defense simultaneously. That's the equivalent of investing five to six attribute points into each stat category, all at once, for free — and it carries over into New Game+.

The catch is that nothing in the game telegraphs this clearly. You find a glowing orb in a broken statue and the description says only that it's one of the Five Skandhas of Buddhist philosophy — form, feeling, thought, choice, consciousness. If you're not already aware that collecting all five triggers a reward, it reads as lore flavoring. Many players pocket them, forget about them, and only discover the questline exists after their first playthrough ends.

Context

The Five Skandhas (五蕴, Wǔ Yùn) are a Buddhist concept describing the five aggregates of human experience. Game Science wove this philosophy directly into gameplay — each Skandha you collect represents one layer of consciousness, and only when all five are gathered can the Destined One transcend limitation. It's one of the game's more elegant thematic gestures.

The Route, Chapter by Chapter

There is one Skandha in each chapter except Chapter 5. Four are relatively painless pickups; two involve boss fights. Here's what to expect at each stop.

Chapter 1 — Skandha of Form (Forest of Wolves): Cross the wooden bridge at the start of the chapter and enter the open forest. Veer left toward the tree line before the Bullguard arena. A broken Buddha head sits in a quiet corner — interact with it and the Skandha of Form is yours. No combat, no puzzle. Think of it as the game's handshake with the questline.

Chapter 2 — Skandha of Feeling (Yellow Wind Ridge): This one earns its place. First, collect all six Buddha's Eyeballs scattered across Fright Cliff — they're hidden in alcoves, on ledges, and behind enemies throughout the area. Once you have them, head to Rockrest Flat, defeat the Stone Vanguard in the arena to the left of the shrine, then insert all six Eyeballs into the large rock formation. This summons Shigandang, a substantially tougher stone guardian who drops the Skandha of Feeling along with the Heart of Stone. Immobilize creates clean damage windows; Pillar Stance lets you rise above his devastating ground slams entirely.

Chapter 3 — Skandha of Thought (Valley of Ecstasy): From the Mindfulness Cliff shrine, cross the wooden plank bridge and drop down. Follow the path up through the cave, past the rock-carrying enemies, then continue right toward the snowy cliffside. Pass through a small cave and climb the stairs to the statue head. The path is a little winding but involves no boss encounter — just careful navigation through a quiet, atmospheric corner of the chapter.

Chapter 4 — Skandha of Choice (Webbed Hollow): From the Purifying Spring shrine, enter the large spider cavern and drop into the dark water pond at the center. Turn left along the shore — you'll find the statue head among the spider cocoons. The disorienting darkness is the main obstacle here. No boss, just darkness and patience.

Chapter 6 — Skandha of Consciousness (Foothills): This is where the questline becomes a genuine challenge. From the Verdant Path shrine, use Somersault Cloud to fly north, then turn right into the valley leading away from the waterfall cave. Giant Shigandang spawns here — but only if you're carrying all four previous Skandhas. Without them, the boss simply doesn't appear and the fifth Skandha becomes unobtainable for that playthrough.

Giant Shigandang Strategy

Unlike most bosses, Giant Shigandang can't move freely — his bulk anchors him in place. The key is patience: stay at mid-range, watch for the arm-raise that telegraphs his ground slams, and jump over the shockwaves that radiate outward. When his fists are resting at center, that's your attack window. Pillar Stance is excellent here for staying above shockwave range. Target the glowing weak points on his body to maximize damage, and bring your best gear — this is a Chapter 6 endgame encounter in every sense.

Claiming Your Reward

With all five Skandhas in hand, find Xu Dog. If you've completed Chen Loong's questline in Chapter 3, Xu Dog is available at Zodiac Village for the rest of the game. If you haven't unlocked the village, he can also be found at the Cellar Shrine in Chapter 2. Either way, select "Show the Five Skandhas," then "Make Five Skandhas Pill." The pill appears in your inventory — consume it immediately for the permanent stat boost.

There's no reason to hold it for later. The effect is permanent and applies to everything that follows, including New Game+. The sooner you claim it, the more of the game you play with those extra stats.

The One Thing You Must Not Forget

The questline has a hard deadline. Once you pass the point of no return in Chapter 6 — the final boss approach — Giant Shigandang can no longer be spawned and the Skandha of Consciousness is gone for that run. If you've collected the first four and haven't yet attempted Chapter 6's optional content, carve out time before the finale. The fight is one of the game's most memorable encounters, and the reward is permanent.

The Five Skandhas questline is the kind of thing Black Myth: Wukong does quietly and confidently — it trusts the player to be curious, to pick up strange orbs and wonder what they're for, and to eventually connect five dots scattered across a very long journey. If you're the kind of explorer who leaves no statue uninspected, this questline was made for you.
