Baldur's Gate 3's New Three-Way Karlach Choice Nobody's Talking About Yet

Buried inside Patch 2's Karlach changes is a small piece of new dialogue that most players are going to miss the first time it comes up — because it only appears in the final minutes of the game, and only if specific conditions are already met.

The Setup

If Wyll has taken the Blade of Avernus path and Karlach is set to return to the Nine Hells at the end of the game, the game now asks you directly what your character does about it. [Source: kotaku.com]

The Three Options

You can now choose to go with the Blade of Avernus as a group, go to Avernus alone with Karlach without Wyll, or let the two of them go without you. [Source: dualshockers.com] The "alone with Karlach" branch specifically opens up to players who have romanced her. [Source: dualshockers.com]

Condition to Watch For

The romance-specific branch requires an active relationship with Karlach going into the epilogue — it won't appear for a platonic playthrough. [Source: dualshockers.com]

Why This Is Easy to Miss

Because this choice sits at the very tail end of the campaign, most players who already beat the game before Patch 2 won't stumble into it without a fresh ending sequence or a late save. It's not flagged as a major decision point the way earlier act-defining choices are — it just appears in dialogue.

Why It's Worth Planning For

If you're romancing Karlach and want the most personal version of her ending, this is the moment that determines whether you experience it solo with her or as part of a three-person group with Wyll. Know which one you want before you get there.
