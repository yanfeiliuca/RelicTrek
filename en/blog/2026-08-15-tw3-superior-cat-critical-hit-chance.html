The Witcher 3's Superior Cat Potion Quietly Became a Crit-Build Staple

Cat potion has always been a utility drink — brew it before a night ambush or a hunt through the sewers, get night vision and immunity to hypnosis, forget about it once the fight's over. Since the next-gen Patch 4.0 update, that changed for the top tier of the line without much fanfare: Superior Cat now also grants a flat +10% critical hit chance. It's easy to miss because nothing in the potion's description or its old reputation prepares a player to look for it, but for anyone running a crit-focused build, it's turned a situational item into something worth keeping brewed permanently.

A Free Bonus Nobody Had to Ask For

The three tiers of Cat now scale their critical hit bonus alongside their other effects: base Cat sits at +5%, Enhanced Cat at +7%, and Superior Cat tops the line at +10%. There's no pre-patch number to compare it against — this is a new effect stapled onto potions that previously did nothing for offense at all, purely a night-vision and status-immunity tool. That's worth underlining because players who looked Cat up years ago and mentally filed it under "exploration potion" have no reason to have revisited that judgment since.

Don't Mix It Up

Superior Cat's new crit bonus is a separate change from the Katakan decoction, whose own pre-existing critical hit chance was doubled from 5% to 10% in the same patch. Same patch, same final number, two completely different items with two different mechanics behind them.

Where +10% Actually Earns Its Slot

On its own, +10% critical hit chance sounds modest next to gear pieces that promise 20% or 25% critical damage. What makes it worth a potion slot is that it's additive to everything else already stacking a crit build: Cat School techniques, Manticore or Feline armor pieces, and skills that scale off fast attacks all push toward the same number, and Superior Cat's contribution costs nothing but a Toxicity slot and three minutes of brewing prep. Community builds organized around the so-called Feline Euphoria setup — heavy alchemy investment, Cat School gear traded for offense instead of armor rating, Ekimmara and Katakan decoctions for sustain and crit — treat Cat-line potions as a baseline rather than an afterthought, because the whole build is designed to delete enemies before they get a real swing in. Quen tends to be the actual survivability plan in those builds, since the armor itself is deliberately sacrificed.

None of that requires committing to a full glass-cannon respec. A more moderate crit-leaning build — say, Manticore gear for the damage bonuses plus a couple of fast-attack skill nodes — still benefits from Superior Cat the same way: it's a flat number added on top, no synergy requirements, no wasted potential if the rest of the build isn't fully optimized around crit.

Cat, Enhanced Cat, and Superior Cat Aren't Interchangeable Anymore

Duration and dose count scale up the ladder too, which matters more than it sounds. Base Cat lasts 60 seconds across 3 doses, Enhanced Cat stretches to 120 seconds and 4 doses, and Superior Cat holds for a full 180 seconds across 5 doses. For a crit build that wants the bonus active through an entire extended fight rather than reapplying it mid-combat, that duration gap is the real argument for pushing all the way to the top tier rather than settling for Enhanced Cat once it's unlocked.

Brewing It Without Blowing the Toxicity Budget

Superior Cat upgrades from an existing Enhanced Cat and calls for White Gull, Berbercane Fruit, Cortinarius, Moleyarrow, Allspice, and Aether — all standard alchemy ingredients rather than anything tied to a specific quest chain, so there's no missable window here. The manuscript needed to unlock the recipe has been reported at a herbalist's hut northeast of Oxenfurt, though vendor stock is known to vary between playthroughs, so that's worth treating as a lead to check rather than a guarantee.

Tip

Because Superior Cat only costs 15 Toxicity, it slots comfortably alongside a decoction and one or two combat potions without pushing a crit build past the Toxicity threshold where side effects start kicking in — worth checking against whatever else is already brewed before a big fight.

Why This Is Worth Your Time

A patch-era addition to an old, familiar item is easy to overlook, especially when the item's whole reputation was built on something else entirely. But for a game nine years past release, a free stacking crit bonus with essentially no downside is the kind of detail that rewards players who go back and re-check items they'd already decided they understood. If a crit build is already in progress, Superior Cat has quietly become one of the cheapest ways to push it further.
