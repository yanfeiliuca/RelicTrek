Crimson Forest Source Check: Sword Puzzles, Chevalam, and Open Questions

Crimson Forest is currently useful to treat as a late-game optional area tied to Verso's Chevalam weapon, but several route details that circulated in the first RelicTrek draft were too specific for the sources we could verify. Polygon's Verso build guide states that Chevalam comes from the Chromatic Gold Chevaliere and that this boss is accessed by completing sword puzzles in the Crimson Forest. [Source: https://www.polygon.com/clair-obscur-expedition-33-guides/601154/best-verso-build-attributes-skills-weapon-luminas-pictos]

What should not be treated as confirmed from this pass: exact statue coordinates, a forced "fall from two planks" solution, companion adds named Clair and Obscur, elemental weaknesses, precise reward quantities, or claims that the zone is one of the highest-density farms in Act 3. Those details remain unverified until they are backed by a current wiki page, guide video, screenshot set, or in-game capture.

What We Can Safely Say

Chevalam is positioned by Polygon as Verso's strongest late-game weapon because it starts him at maximum Perfection rank, while also making defensive play important because he cannot rely on healing or shields in the same way. [Source: https://www.polygon.com/clair-obscur-expedition-33-guides/601154/best-verso-build-attributes-skills-weapon-luminas-pictos]

The same source frames Verso around Agility, Luck, Perfection rank, and defensive execution. It also lists Perilous Parry among useful Pictos for his build, so Crimson Forest is best framed as part of a Verso optimization route rather than as a fully mapped loot farm until more direct area evidence is available. [Source: https://www.polygon.com/clair-obscur-expedition-33-guides/601154/best-verso-build-attributes-skills-weapon-luminas-pictos]

Verification Boundary

Use this page as a source-checked pointer: Crimson Forest → sword puzzles → Chromatic Gold Chevaliere → Chevalam. Treat exact puzzle layout, boss weakness, support enemies, and drop tables as pending confirmation.

For broader game mechanics, independent summaries confirm that Expedition 33 uses Pictos/Luminas for character customization, Chroma Catalysts for weapon upgrades, Expedition Flags for rest and fast travel, and Esquie traversal abilities to access optional zones. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clair_Obscur%3A_Expedition_33]

Open Questions Before a Full Route

The prior version of this article described a specific three-statue path, a two-companion boss setup, Light/Dark weaknesses, Fire/Ice resistance, a level 50 recommendation, and fixed rewards. Current search did not produce independent support for those exact claims, so they have been removed rather than repeated as fact.

If you are routing this in-game, record the statue count, map entry point, boss name, reward screen, and any elemental test results before converting this into a step-by-step guide. Until then, the safe recommendation is simple: pursue Crimson Forest when you are specifically chasing Chevalam for a Verso build, and keep a manual save before puzzle and boss attempts.
