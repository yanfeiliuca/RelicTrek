Medalum: Gestral Arena Reward, Passives, and Recovery Route

The Gestral Village tournament is part of the story route, but its final reward depends on who defeats the Stranger in the fourth duel. If Maelle wins, she receives Medalum. If another character wins, that character receives a different weapon reward or an upgrade to a weapon already owned.

Missing Medalum in the tournament is recoverable: Maelle can later earn it by defeating Golgra in a separate one-on-one fight.

Why Virtuose Start Changes Everything

Maelle's combat system revolves around switching stances. Virtuose is her high-damage stance, and many rotations normally need a skill or stance transition before they can use it.

At weapon level 4, Medalum starts Maelle in Virtuose stance. This removes the opening stance transition and makes first-turn Virtuose skills immediately available.

That is a meaningful tempo advantage. Actual damage still depends on weapon level, attributes, skills, Pictos, Luminas, enemy defenses, and the current game patch.

The Stats That Make It Scale

Medalum is a physical weapon with Defense C and Agility D scaling. Those ratings mean both attributes contribute to its weapon power, while Defense and Agility also retain their normal character-level functions.

At level 10, every Burn applied while Maelle is in Virtuose stance is doubled. At level 20, Burn deals double damage while she is in Virtuose stance. These effects support Burn-focused rotations; they do not mean Agility alone guarantees acting before every enemy.

Virtuose-compatible skills can take advantage of the level-4 opening immediately. Build rankings and exact damage claims should remain conditional, especially because post-launch patches changed prominent Maelle damage setups, including Stendhal and Medalum-related Burn behavior.

How the Tournament Actually Works

The Gestral Village tournament has four one-on-one rounds. The first three can be completed with any available fighter. The fourth fight against the Stranger determines the character-specific reward: choose Maelle and win to receive Medalum.

The selection matters because the final winner receives the reward. If Medalum is the goal, use Maelle for the fourth round and prepare her for a solo fight.

Reward Rule

Winning with another character gives that character's arena reward, or upgrades it if already owned. Medalum is awarded from this route only when Maelle wins the final duel.

If You Already Missed It: The Golgra Duel

After the tournament, Maelle can obtain Medalum by defeating Golgra in a separate one-on-one challenge.

Golgra is a much harder solo opponent than the arena finale, so this is a recovery route rather than the easiest acquisition path.

If the tournament opportunity is already gone, return after improving Maelle's weapon, attributes, and solo-fight tools. A save from before the final arena round is the shorter route when one is available.

Tip

If Medalum is part of the plan, use Maelle for the Gestral Village tournament's final round. This avoids relying on the substantially harder Golgra recovery fight.

Making the Most of Medalum

Level 4 is the key first breakpoint because it unlocks the Virtuose start. Levels 10 and 20 add the two Burn effects. Attribute allocation should consider Medalum's Defense C and Agility D scaling alongside survivability, turn order, and the rest of the build.

Opening-turn and AP-support Pictos can complement Medalum, but the correct selection depends on the available Lumina budget and whether the build prioritizes immediate Virtuose damage, Burn application, defense, or longer encounters.

Medalum remains a useful option when a route values immediate Virtuose access or its level-10 and level-20 Burn effects. It is not mandatory for every Maelle setup, and later weapons or patch-specific combinations can support different strategies.

The Gestral Village tournament is the earliest and simplest Medalum route. Choosing Maelle for the final duel secures the weapon immediately; the Golgra challenge remains available if another character received the arena reward.
