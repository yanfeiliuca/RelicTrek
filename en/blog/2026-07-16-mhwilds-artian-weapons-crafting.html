The Gogmazios Update Just Made Artian Weapons Non-Optional in MH Wilds

Monster Hunter Wilds' Title Update 4 landed with Gogmazios as a permanent event quest, and it quietly changed the calculus on Artian Weapons. They were already the game's best-in-slot endgame crafting system for most weapon types. Now a fully reinforced Rarity 8 Artian weapon isn't a finish line — it's the entry ticket to Gogma Artian Weapons, a second layer of crafting built on top of the one you've been ignoring since Chapter 4. If you've been putting off the Tempered Monster grind, this is the update that makes it non-optional.

Parts, Not Loot: How Artian Crafting Actually Works

Artian Weapons unlock after the Chapter 4 main story quest "Wyvern Sparks and Rose Thorns," which rewards a Damaged Weapon Shard and opens Tier I crafting at the smithy. From there, every one of the 14 weapon types is built from three parts — Blades, Discs, Tubes, or Devices — pulled from a fixed combination table. A Long Sword always wants one Blade and two Tubes; a Charge Blade always wants Blade, Disc, Device. There's no substitution and no partial credit.

The part rarity you farm matters more than anything else in this system. Rarity 6 and 7 Artian weapons exist, but they cap out lower on attack, carry fewer decoration slots, and never reach white sharpness. Nobody building for endgame should spend real farming time on anything below Rarity 8.

The Only Farming Route Worth Running

Rarity 8 parts drop exclusively from 6-star and 7-star Tempered Monsters — Gore Magala, Arkveld, Uth Duna, Jin Dahaad, Rey Dau, and Nu Udra all qualify. Arkveld sits at the top of that list and is generally treated as the core farming target, but the real efficiency gain comes from Investigations that pair two Tempered monsters in a single quest. Community reporting puts the ceiling at up to 20 parts per run when a secondary Tempered monster is present, which is why the Gore Magala plus Arkveld double-Investigation has become the de facto farming loop.

Tip: Check an Investigation's bonus reward slots before you queue. Gold slots carry the best odds of producing Rarity 8 parts, and a three-Gold Investigation will out-farm a Bronze-heavy one by a wide margin over an evening of hunts.

Once you hit HR 100, the Melding Pot at Suja Village opens a "Meld Relics" option that trades 120 Melding Points for any specific Rarity 8 part you name. It doesn't replace farming — the point cost adds up fast — but it's the right tool for finishing a weapon when you're one stubborn part short.

Reinforcement Is a Reroll Game — Play It Like One

Every Artian weapon gets five reinforcement slots, and each one lands a random bonus: Attack, Affinity, Sharpness, Element, or Defense. Players comparing notes on this system generally agree a true "god roll" — five Attack in a row — is closer to a 1-in-200 outcome than something you should plan around. The more realistic target most hunters converge on is three Attack, one Sharpness, and one flexible slot, since Artian weapons without sharpness investment simply aren't viable in practice.

The mechanic that makes this manageable is the seed: reinforcement outcomes are fixed for your current session the moment you craft a weapon, not rolled fresh at each level-up. That means you can craft a cheap, disposable Rarity 6 or 7 weapon first, check its reinforcement preview, and see the pattern your session is about to produce before committing your real Rarity 8 weapon. If the preview is bad, quit without saving and try again.

Don't skip the dummy craft. Reinforcement materials — Drearisite, Specklite, Argecite, and Oricalcite — come from Festival Shares on a refresh schedule. Burn them on an unchecked bad roll and you're waiting for the next cycle to try again.

What Comes After Rarity 8: Gogma Artian Weapons

This is the part that changes the whole equation. Title Update 4's Gogma Artian Weapons fuse Artian technology with materials farmed from Gogmazios, and they're only reachable if you already own a Rarity 8 Artian weapon of the type you want to upgrade. Defeat Gogmazios and a new "Upgrade to Gogma Artian Weapons" tab opens at Gemma's smithy. Gogma Artian weapons add their own layer of RNG on top — rolling for Set Bonus Skills and Group Skills — but none of it is accessible without a finished Rarity 8 base first.

In other words, the farming route and reinforcement seed-fishing described above aren't just an endgame side quest anymore. They're the prerequisite for the update everyone's actually here for.

The Artian system rewards patience over luck-chasing: pick your weapon combo, run Gold-slot Investigations against paired Tempered monsters, and test every reinforcement seed with a throwaway craft before you spend materials for real. Do that consistently and a Rarity 8 weapon stops being a grind — it becomes the base you build your Gogma Artian weapon on.
