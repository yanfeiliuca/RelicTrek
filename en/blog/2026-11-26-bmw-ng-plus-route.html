Black Myth: Wukong NG+ Route: Count Your Horns Before the Credits Roll

Most people who finish Black Myth: Wukong do one of two things: they put the controller down satisfied, or they press "begin anew" and start swinging the staff again with no plan at all. There's a better third option. New Game Plus is where two of the game's most coveted rewards actually become reachable, and a little forethought before that final credits roll can save you an entire extra cycle of frustration.

With the late-year sale season bringing a fresh wave of players to their first ending, now is the perfect moment to talk about how to make your second journey count.

Why Your Second Cycle Is Not a Victory Lap

The instinct on entering NG+ is to treat it as a cleanup run. You keep your equipment, your currency and your build, so surely Chapter 1 will melt? It won't — not entirely. Bosses in the new cycle have scaled health and damage, and early encounters you breezed past at the end of your first run can punish a careless dodge. Players returning after a long break often report that the first few bosses feel sharper than they remember, simply because muscle memory has faded while the numbers have gone up.

Treat Chapter 1 as a warm-up rather than a formality. Spend a few fights re-learning timings before you start speeding through areas.

Remember

What carries over is your power — gear, currency and most of your collection. What resets is the world: questlines, area progress and access to hubs such as 六六村 all have to be re-earned.

The Six-Horn Problem Behind the Mighty Armor Set

The headline reason to plan NG+ is the Mighty armor set (大力套装). It needs six Mighty Iron Horns, but a single playthrough only offers four. The final two appear only in the new cycle, in areas that held something different the first time around.

The catch is brutal in its simplicity: NG+ adds two horns, it does not respawn the four you missed. If you only picked up three in your first run, your ceiling is five — and the set stays forever out of reach on that save. So before you face the final chapter, open your inventory and count. Four horns in hand is the single most important checkpoint of the entire plan.

Tip

Make the horn count part of your pre-final-boss ritual, alongside topping up your gourd and checking your spirit loadout. It takes ten seconds and protects dozens of hours.

Combining Objectives: One Route, Two Rewards

The second goal worth folding into the same cycle is fully upgrading the Siyu Dragon Staff (驷雨龙法杖). Several of its rarer upgrade materials are easier to come by in NG+ areas, and — conveniently — some of the same regions you'll visit for the extra horns are good places to farm them.

The efficient rhythm looks like this. Enter NG+ with your four horns and the staff either crafted or its base materials prepared. Move through Chapters 1 to 4 at a steady pace, re-completing the Ma Tianba dialogues along the way, since they're still required for the Dark Thunder (黯雷) transformation in the new cycle. Re-unlock 六六村 when you reach it. Then, as you collect the two new horns, farm staff materials in the same zones instead of backtracking later. Craft the armor the moment you have six horns, and you'll carry its benefits through the rest of the cycle.

Along the way, keep an eye on your Journal: two new entries unlock automatically in NG+, which makes the second cycle a quiet boost for completionists too.

Community Wisdom From Second-Cycle Veterans

Discussion among returning players tends to land on a few recurring lessons. First, NG+ is the natural place to chase whichever ending you didn't see, so think about which path you want before you start rather than discovering requirements halfway through. Second, a carried-over build is a chance to experiment — many players use the second cycle to try spells and stances they ignored the first time, since the safety net of a strong kit makes failure cheap. Third, don't rush the world. Because questlines reset, side stories you only half-followed are fully available again, and they're often richer on a second, more attentive viewing.

Why This Is Worth Your Time

A second cycle without a plan feels like repetition. A second cycle with one feels like a reward hunt — a focused trek toward an armor set most players never complete and a staff at its full potential, with a deeper read of the story as a bonus. Count your horns, prepare your staff, respect the scaled bosses, and the journey anew becomes one of the most satisfying stretches of the game.
