Beating Clea in Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 — The Secret Boss That Punishes Every Missed Parry

Most players who fly to the Flying Manor never realize there's a second layer to it. They land, fight a handful of familiar enemies, grab some loot off the floor, and leave — never noticing the locked door in Central Plaza, guarded by a boy who won't open it until four trial bosses are dead. Behind that door is Clea, Verso and Alicia's sister, and one of only two secret bosses in the entire game.

She's also, by the numbers the community has pulled from the game's own data, an enemy whose health climbs into the tens of millions on higher New Game+ tiers — a boss that makes clear this is not a fight you out-level. It's one you out-play.

The Mechanic That Breaks Most First Attempts

Clea never attacks you directly. On each of her turns she summons a themed wave of Nevrons instead — "creepy Nevrons," "musical Nevrons," "violent Nevrons," and so on — and every wave is a scripted sequence of three to seven attacks that has to be parried or dodged in full. Community-sourced boss data collected on the Expedition 33 wiki (credited to Reddit contributor ElNinoFr) puts her self-heal on a missed parry at roughly 450,000 HP in a single tick — enough to undo several turns of party damage instantly.

💡 What this actually means

Clea isn't checking your build or your gear score. She's checking whether you can hold a clean parry streak through a six-hit combo without your attention slipping once.

Learn the Waves Before You Chase the Damage

Six wave types recur throughout the fight, and each behaves like its own rhythm pattern worth drilling in isolation. The four-hit "creepy Nevrons" wave ends with a delayed Hexga slam that catches players who swing their parry timing too early. The seven-hit "violent Nevrons" wave is the longest single sequence, mixing sword strikes that need a standard parry with jump attacks from Stalacts that require a jump counter instead — timing the wrong response on either one breaks the chain. Treating each wave as its own pattern to memorize, rather than reacting on instinct, is what separates players who clear Clea from players who get stuck on her for hours.

The Damage Race: What Actually Works

Once your parries are reliable, the fight turns into a race against the healing you'll still occasionally give up. A few approaches keep showing up in community strategy write-ups:

  • Slow on Clea, Rush on the party. Fewer of her turns means fewer heal windows to survive in the first place.
  • Break-stun chains. Lune's Crustal Crush or Terraquake, and Monoco's Stalact Punches, build Break fast enough to skip an entire heal phase outright. Pairing this with the Quick Break Lumina lets whoever lands the stun immediately act again.
  • Sciel's Foretell burst. Entering Twilight with a Gradient charge active, opening with Shadow Bringer to slam all 20 stacks of Foretell onto Clea, then following up in the same turn with Phantom Blade to consume it, is reported by the wiki's community contributors to deal enough damage in one combo to outweigh a full missed-parry heal on its own.
  • Elemental weapon choices. Since Clea is weak to Void and Light damage but resists Physical, Verso players are pointed toward weapons like Blodam, Confuso, Delaram, or Demonam, while Maelle's Barrier Breaker or Chalium make better picks than a physical-focused loadout.

A smaller number of community reports describe skipping the marathon entirely — a heavily specialized Maelle-and-Sciel setup built around Maelle's Stendhal ability dropping Clea in a single hit. It's not the reliable route for most parties, but it's out there as proof of how far the fight's math can be pushed.

Why This Is Worth Your Time

Clea won't show up on any required checklist, and nothing forces you to find that locked door. That's exactly why beating her feels different from every other fight in Expedition 33 — the game never makes you prove you can do this. The reward, Clea's Life, is a Picto that turns a clean, damage-free turn into a full heal. It only becomes useful once you've already shown you can string clean turns together against the game's harshest parry check. If you've cleared the Flying Manor's four trials and walked past that door without going through it, she's still waiting.
