Ten Catalysts, Ten Decisions: Mastering Perfect Chroma Catalysts in Expedition 33

There is a moment in every Expedition 33 playthrough when the adventure shifts. The credits-roll story is done, the world is quieter, and what remains is the question that every endgame in a great RPG eventually asks: what does your party actually become? For most players, that question has a crisp answer — Level 33 weapons. And to get there, you need Perfect Chroma Catalysts, the rarest upgrade material in the game.

With only ten available in a single playthrough before New Game Plus, every catalyst is a genuine decision. Spend wisely, and you'll field a party capable of taking on Expedition 33's most demanding hidden challenges. Spend carelessly, and you may find yourself wishing you had waited. This guide walks through where to find all ten, which source you can actually farm, and how to make every choice count.

What a Perfect Chroma Catalyst Actually Does

Weapons in Expedition 33 are upgraded through a tiered system of Chroma Catalysts. Regular and Superior Catalysts carry you comfortably to Level 32 — more than sufficient for the main story and most early endgame encounters. But the jump from Level 32 to Level 33 is something else entirely. A weapon at maximum level nearly doubles its base power, pushing past 8,000 before accounting for your character's Attribute bonuses and Traits. With S-tier and A-tier Attributes both at 99, a fully maxed weapon represents one of the most dramatic stat leaps available anywhere in the game.

That transformation is the promise behind every Perfect Chroma Catalyst. The cost is scarcity — and permanence. Once applied, the upgrade cannot be reversed.

Where to Find All Ten

The ten Perfect Chroma Catalysts are spread across Expedition 33's most challenging late-game and endgame zones. None are handed over lightly. Here is where each one lives:

The Flying Manor holds four of them, one apiece from its major boss encounters: Dualliste, the Lampmaster, the Eveque trio, and Clea. These fights rank among the toughest in the game, and clearing the Manor end-to-end is essentially a prerequisite for any player serious about maxing their party's arsenal.

After defeating the Lampmaster in the Painting Workshop, speak to the boy in that area — he'll hand over an additional catalyst as thanks for the deed. Easy to overlook in the post-fight momentum, worth a moment to collect.

Two more catalysts come from Renoir's Drafts. The Chromatic Creation drops one on defeat, and reaching The Abyss deep within the Drafts to beat Simon awards two at once — making Simon one of the most catalyst-generous single encounters in the entire run.

One catalyst is available from completing Endless Tower stage 11, level 3. And the Serpenphare, encountered on the Continent, drops one as well.

Finally, there is a missable catalyst: the Trash-Can Man in Lumiere will offer one — but only if you found him during the Prologue. This is the most time-sensitive pickup in the collection, and easy to lose permanently by skipping past him in the opening hours.

The One Source You Can Farm

Ten catalysts spread across a party of six or seven characters creates natural tension. Fortunately, one source is renewable: the Chromatic Creation in Renoir's Drafts respawns after defeat, meaning you can return to farm additional Perfect Chroma Catalysts once you've made your initial clear. It is not a trivial grind — the Chromatic Creation is a demanding fight — but it removes the hard ceiling on your catalyst total if you're willing to invest the effort.

Farming Tip

The four new superbosses added to the Endless Tower each drop a Perfect Chroma Catalyst on death. For players deep into endgame content, they offer a challenging but consistent secondary farm route.

Spending Wisely: The Only Decision That Really Matters

Because the upgrade is permanent, the most important thing you can do before committing a Perfect Chroma Catalyst is slow down and think it through. Test candidate weapons at levels 25 to 30 to get a genuine feel for their combat performance. Then take stock of every weapon available to your full roster — in particular Lithum and Joyaro, which are tied to character questlines and easy to miss if you complete the main story before finishing everyone's personal arc.

Worth Knowing

Don't commit a catalyst to any character until you've seen every weapon available to them. Some of the strongest options are locked behind late-game questlines, and maxing a weapon you'll later overshadow is a quietly painful lesson in restraint.

In practice, many players begin with Maelle, who scales into one of the game's strongest damage dealers by the story's end and benefits enormously from the Level 33 jump. But there is no single correct answer — the right call depends entirely on how you've built your party and what content you're still planning to tackle.

Why This Is Worth the Journey

Hunting Perfect Chroma Catalysts is one of the best reasons to explore every corner of Expedition 33's endgame. The chase takes you through the Flying Manor's haunting, painterly rooms, into the stranger depths of Renoir's Drafts, and up the demanding vertical of the Endless Tower — experiences that are rewarding in themselves, well beyond any material payoff. And when you finally apply a catalyst to the weapon you've been building toward all game, the result is unmistakable. Level 33 is not a footnote. It is the full stop at the end of the build you've been writing since the expedition began.
