The Sky-Piercing Horn Grind Nobody Warns You About

Black Myth: Wukong · 2026-07-05

If you've spent any time in NG+ theorycrafting threads for Black Myth: Wukong, you've probably seen the same weapon come up again and again: the Adept Spine-Shooting Fuban Staff. It's not the flashiest name on the weapon list, but the way it's gated has made it one of the more talked-about crafting chains in the game — mostly because so many players find out too late that they're short one material.

A Recipe That Punishes Guesswork

According to the item's own wiki entry, crafting the staff costs four Sky-Piercing Horns, four Gold Tree Cores, two Kun Steel, and 45,000 Will (Source: Black Myth: Wukong Wiki on Fandom). The Horns are the bottleneck. A single playthrough only produces three of them — one from Fuban in the Kingdom of Sahali, one from the Scorpionlord in Purple Cloud Mountain, and one tied to Chapter 4's story progression. The fourth doesn't exist until you start New Game Plus and beat Fuban again (Source: Dexerto crafting recipe writeup).

That's the part that trips people up. The staff is explicitly an NG+ weapon — you cannot craft it on your first run no matter how many Horns you've stockpiled, because the fourth simply isn't available yet.

The One Fight Everyone Talks About

Search any Black Myth: Wukong discussion board for "Scorpionlord missing" and you'll find the same story repeated: players reach Duskveil at the end of Purple Cloud Mountain, beat it, then realize the Scorpionlord is nowhere to be found. Steam Community discussion threads confirm the sequencing matters — engage Duskveil before the Scorpionlord and the Scorpionlord fight is gone for that playthrough (Source: Steam Community discussion, Black Myth: Wukong forum).

Community-Reported Recovery Trick

Some players in that same Steam discussion report that if the Scorpionlord seems to have vanished, traveling to the Zodiac Village and then returning to Purple Cloud Mountain can cause it to reappear. It's not a guaranteed fix, and it's not officially documented, but it's worth trying before you write off the fight as lost for that run.

Why the Math Gets Worse With Every Miss

Because the recipe needs exactly four Horns and a single playthrough plus one NG+ cycle produces exactly four (three from run one, one from NG+ Fuban), there's zero slack in the system. Skip the Scorpionlord once, and you're not just delayed by a farming session — you're delayed by an entire additional playthrough, because the missed Horn can only come back the next time you reach that boss again.

This is part of why the staff shows up so often in min-maxing conversations: it's less about difficulty and more about sequencing discipline. Players who plan their NG+ builds around it tend to treat the Scorpionlord fight as a hard checkpoint, not an optional detour.

What You Get for the Trouble

The payoff, per multiple weapon-guide breakdowns, is a staff whose charged heavy attacks fire spines at nearby enemies while recovering health based on Focus spent — effectively turning aggressive play into sustain. Guides covering NG+ weapon tier lists describe it as versatile for both add-clearing and boss fights, and note it pairs well with the Centipede Armor set for an even steadier health-recovery loop.

Why This Is Worth Your Time

The Fuban Staff isn't a secret superweapon locked behind an obscure puzzle — it's a lesson in respecting boss order. If you're currently in Chapter 4 and haven't touched Purple Cloud Mountain yet, that's the moment this matters: fight the Scorpionlord first, bank the Horn, and save yourself a full extra playthrough down the line.
