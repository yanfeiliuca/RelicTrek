Black Myth: Wukong's Man-in-Stone: The Quest That Gates Chapter 2's Secret Area

Most side content in Black Myth: Wukong hands you a curio or a bit of extra gear. The Man-in-Stone questline in Fright Cliff is different — clear it, and you don't just walk away with a transformation spell, you unlock a merchant who sells the one item standing between you and Chapter 2's secret area.

A Curse Hiding in a Ravine

From the Squall Hideout Shrine, a side ravine off the main path leads to a man trapped inside a boulder. He's been cursed by something called the Mother of Stones and can't move on his own. Talk to him and he points you toward a nearby cave where the curse originates. According to GameSpot's quest breakdown, at the end of a tunnel lined with bluish crystals, striking a rock formation reveals the Mother of Stones boss itself.

Two Fights, Not One

The Mother of Stones fight is manageable once you know the pattern: she's stationary and doesn't attack directly. Instead she summons waves of Rock Guai minions at certain HP intervals, and clearing those waves is the only way to keep damaging her. Win, and she drops Stone Essence.

Bring the Essence back to the Man-in-Stone and the quest turns. Per Fandom's Man-in-Stone page and several other guides, he tries to hang onto the Essence instead of thanking you for it — that's your cue to attack, which triggers a full boss duel. His moveset is slow but hits hard; circling to his flank after each slam opens a window for a charged heavy.

Tip

Winning the duel doesn't hand you the Azure Dust transformation automatically — interact with him again afterward to actually receive it.

What Azure Dust Actually Does

Azure Dust turns you into a Rock Guai, the same enemy type you've been fighting through the questline. It centers on a ram attack that deals heavy damage on contact, and per the Black Myth: Wukong Fandom wiki, it also grants immunity to all Four Banes — the game's status ailments — while active. That makes it a panic button against status-stacking enemies, not just a damage cooldown.

The Sobering Stone Is the Real Prize

After the duel, rest at any shrine to reload the area, then head back to where the Man-in-Stone stood. He's now a permanent merchant. The headline item in his shop is the Sobering Stone, priced at 6,480 Will according to GameSpot. That single item is required to unlock Chapter 2's secret area — there's no other route in. Skip this questline and that content stays locked for the rest of your playthrough.

Note

The merchant doesn't appear the instant the duel ends. You have to rest at a shrine first, then return — walking away without resting can make it look like the quest didn't register.

Worth Your Time

Next to some of Black Myth: Wukong's longer detours, Man-in-Stone is compact — two fights and one shrine rest, done in well under half an hour. What makes it worth prioritizing is that it's a gate, not just a bonus: the Sobering Stone it sells is the only route into a chunk of Chapter 2. If you're chasing full map completion, this is one of the few side quests that actually blocks progress elsewhere if you skip it.
