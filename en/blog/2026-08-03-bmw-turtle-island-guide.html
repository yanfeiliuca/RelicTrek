Why Turtle Island Is Black Myth: Wukong's Most Overlooked Chapter 3 Detour

Most players who reach Turtle Island in Chapter 3 do exactly what the story pushes them to do: survive the mandatory turtle-ride boss fight, step onto the shore, and keep moving toward the next objective. That instinct is understandable — the game never marks the island's other three encounters on the map. But skipping them means walking away from gear that feeds directly into New Game+, and recent community discussion around the area is worth folding into any route plan.

The Encounter Density Nobody Expects

Turtle Island crams four distinct fights and one hub-unlocking NPC into a single small landmass: the mandatory Kang-Jin Loong fight during the turtle ride, the optional Kang-Jin Star behind Warding Temple, the frozen Cyan Loong statue at the cliff edge, and Chen Loong hidden in the water off North Shore. Fandom's wiki entry on Cyan Loong confirms the boss sits in Bitter Lake's Turtle Island sub-area and only becomes fightable after Kang-Jin Star has been dealt with — so the order you clear these fights in isn't arbitrary.

Gear Up Before You Fight Cyan Loong

The clearest piece of community-verified advice for this leg of the island: don't walk into the Cyan Loong fight with whatever staff you happen to be holding. Per the Black Myth: Wukong Fandom wiki, the Kang-Jin Staff carries high base damage and an increased critical hit chance, and it adds Thunder Damage to your combos once the Punishing Downpour skill is unlocked — which lines up neatly against a Loong that fights with electrical attacks. The Loongscale Set, craftable after Kang-Jin Star falls, reduces Shocked-state duration and boosts your own Thunder Damage output, according to the same source.

Community Tip

PowerPyx's weapon-location guide treats the Kang-Jin Staff as a mandatory waypoint on the road to the game's later Loong staffs — craft it the moment you have both materials rather than saving the trip for later.

What Cyan Loong Actually Drops

The Fandom entry lists the Cyan Loong reward pool as Fine Gold Thread x2, a Mind Core, a Loong Pearl, and a separate chest containing the Mountain-Shaking Claw and a Breath of Fire — and defeating him also unlocks crafting for the Golden Loong Staff. That's a wider drop table than a single-glance summary suggests, and it's worth clearing the chest separately from the boss kill so nothing gets left behind on the cliff.

Chen Loong's Ruyi Scroll Is a Permanent Fast-Travel Node, Not a One-Time Item

KeenGamer's walkthrough of the Chen Loong questline adds a detail that's easy to miss on a first pass: the Ruyi Scroll doesn't just open Zodiac Village once. It adds a permanent "Ruyi Scroll – Zodiac Village" option to every Keeper's Shrine's travel list, and it can also be used directly from the inventory to warp there from anywhere on the map.

Why This Matters

Zodiac Village is the hub for upgrading curios, armor, and medicine for the rest of the game — treating it as a one-time detour rather than a standing fast-travel option costs you return trips later.

The NG+ Payoff: Stormflash Loong Staff

The reason Turtle Island keeps coming up in New Game+ planning threads is the Kang-Jin Staff's upgrade path. Per Fandom's Stormflash Loong Staff page, the Kang-Jin Staff is the direct precursor to the Stormflash Loong Staff, which draws Thunder while charging and applies Thunder Damage to every staff attack landed while an enemy is Shocked. Sportskeeda's crafting breakdown lists the cost as 9 Loong Pearls, 4 Gold Tree Cores, 2 Kun Steel, and 45,000 Will — and notes the weapon can't be crafted until all three Loong brothers are defeated in NG+, alongside Kang-Jin Loong and Kang-Jin Star from the base run. Every Loong Pearl collected on Turtle Island this playthrough is one less to farm in NG+.

Why This Is Worth Your Time

Turtle Island isn't a side attraction you can safely skip — it's a staging area. One clean pass nets a crafted staff, a permanent shortcut to your upgrade hub, and NG+ crafting materials you'd otherwise have to hunt down later. Clear all four encounters in one visit and the rest of the Loong staff chain gets noticeably shorter.
