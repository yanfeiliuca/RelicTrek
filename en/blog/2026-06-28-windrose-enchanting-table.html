Windrose Enchanting Table: Verified Build Costs and Arborum Notes

The Enchanting Table is a Windrose crafting station tied to the Cursed Swamps and Arborum-tier materials. Current guide sources agree that it matters for Ingot Arborum progression, but they do not justify treating every weapon route, upgrade cost, or combat value as settled for all Early Access patches.

This page keeps only the claims that can be tied to the sources below: the listed build materials, the roof and Bonfire placement requirement, and the broad connection between Enchanting Table crafting and Arborum materials. Exact unlock triggers, one-kill drop counts, weapon crafting totals, and Plague Halberd combat values are marked 待确认 until verified from current game UI or stronger sources.

Unlock Trigger: Confirm the Current Patch

Sources do not describe the unlock trigger in exactly the same way. One GAMES.GG guide connects the recipe unlock to Ancient Scraps, Method.gg frames enchanting around Plague Wood and Essence Arborum, and GAMES.GG's Ingot Arborum guide says the first Essence Arborum unlocks the Enchanting Table recipe. Treat the precise trigger as 待确认 unless you are checking it in-game.

The low-risk route is to collect Ancient Scraps and Plague Wood while exploring the Cursed Swamps, then check whether Essence Arborum and the Enchanting Table appear in your build or crafting menus. Current sources support that these materials belong to the same late-game crafting chain, but the exact menu-unlock moment needs direct confirmation.

If your build menu still does not show the table, do not assume a fixed "one chest" solution. Re-check the Cursed Swamps resource chain first: Ancient Scraps, Plague Wood, Essence Arborum, and the relevant furnace or table recipes are the current source-backed checkpoints.

What You Actually Need to Build It

The supported build cost is 3x Mire Metal Ingot, 10x Hewn Stone, 10x Plant Fiber, and 2x Essence Arborum. GAMES.GG, Method.gg, and Destructoid all list that same top-level material set.

For the inputs behind that list, Destructoid and GAMES.GG connect Mire Metal Ingot to Ancient Scraps and Quagmire Powder, while multiple sources connect Essence Arborum to Plague Wood. Exact enemy drop rates and route yields are not confirmed here.

Important

The table also has placement requirements in the checked sources: it needs a roof or sheltered status and must be near a Bonfire / heat radius. Build it in a covered workshop and re-check the status indicator if it does not activate.

The Bottleneck: Tainted Bile and Ingot Arborum

Once the table is running, the main source-backed material to track is Ingot Arborum. Current sources connect that recipe to Mire Metal Ingot, Essence Arborum, and Tainted Bile, and GAMES.GG connects Tainted Bile to Plague Crocodiles. Fixed drop exclusivity and fixed per-kill quantities are not treated as confirmed here.

Plan with a safety margin because the exact Ingot Arborum costs are source-sensitive. Method.gg lists several plague weapons and Bullet Arborum as Ingot Arborum users, but this article does not confirm per-weapon totals, upgrade totals, disassembly refunds, or fixed session yields. What the Enchanting Table Actually Unlocks

Sources differ on the exact output set. GAMES.GG and Destructoid list Ingot Arborum, Fabric Arborum, and Hide Arborum, while Method.gg's enchanting guide focuses on Ingot Arborum. Verify the current recipe list in your own table before treating Fabric or Hide Arborum as guaranteed outputs.

The Plague Halberd and other plague weapons are useful propagation checks because multiple pages link them to Ingot Arborum.
