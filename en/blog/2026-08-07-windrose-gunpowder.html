Windrose Gunpowder: Why You Shouldn't Wait for the Foothills

Windrose treats Gunpowder as a two-track resource: something you loot in the opening hours, and something you manufacture only after you clear the Foothills gate. Most new players hear "Gunpowder" and mentally file it as a mid-game unlock, which means they miss the earlier track entirely — and arrive at the Foothills with an empty pouch just as a locked chest starts calling for a Blunderbuss shot.

You Can Find Gunpowder Before You Can Craft It

Before any crafting station exists in your world, Gunpowder already shows up as loot. Community guides point to Blackbeard Pirate Camps on the second and third islands as the most reliable source, alongside supply crates scattered across the early map. It appears to be a finite stash by design — the opening hours are built around rationing what you find, not farming a renewable node. Search every container on the starting islands rather than rushing past them; a handful of saved shots can matter far more later than the ten minutes it takes to loot now.

Those Early Shots Earn Their Keep Against Thomas Richards

Crafting doesn't open up until you beat Thomas Richards during the "Revenge is Best Served Cold" questline and the Foothills region unlocks. That fight is exactly where looted Gunpowder pays off. Ranged weapons like the Blunderbuss let you kite the fight and pick him apart from a distance instead of trading blows in melee, and players who go in with even a small stockpile of shots report an easier time landing headshots at range rather than absorbing his combos directly.

Clearing the Foothills Gate Changes Everything

Once Thomas Richards is down, sulfur becomes farmable. Look for tall stone boulders scattered across the Foothills islands — sulfur shows up as breakable yellow-green veins directly on the rock face. You'll need an Iron Pickaxe to break them open; a starting pickaxe won't do the job, so it's worth upgrading before you make the trip out.

Ash, meanwhile, comes from a Charcoal Kiln. Burn Wood into Charcoal and Ash drops out as a byproduct, so a steady habit of wood-burning back at base builds your Ash stock passively while you're off doing other things — no dedicated farming run required.

Heads Up

Community sources don't fully agree on the exact conversion rate yet. Some guides cite 10 Sulfur plus 20 Ash for a batch of 15 Gunpowder at a Millstone-type station, while others describe smaller ratios closer to 2:1 at different crafting benches. Treat any specific number as unconfirmed until you check the recipe at your own station — Windrose has seen enough post-launch changes that older numbers may no longer hold exactly.

Tip

Don't treat the Thomas Richards fight as an all-or-nothing gunfight. Guides note that a Blunderbuss and steady kiting are the reliable path if your Gunpowder stash is thin, while a bigger stockpile just makes the fight faster by letting you land more headshots at range. Either approach clears the gate — the ammo you looted earlier just decides how comfortable the fight feels.

Why This Is Worth Your Time

Gunpowder is one of those systems where a little advance planning compounds. Loot aggressively on the starting islands instead of beelining the main quest, hold onto what you find for the Thomas Richards fight, and by the time you're standing in front of the Foothills' sulfur veins with an Iron Pickaxe in hand, you'll already understand the rhythm the game is trying to teach you: scavenge first, fight smart, then industrialize. That's a more interesting loop than treating Gunpowder as just another resource node to grind — and it's exactly why it's worth tracking down early instead of waiting for the game to hand it to you.
