The Cave Nobody Warns You About: Getting First Offensive in Clair Obscur: Expedition 33

Abbest Cave is a small optional location north-northwest of Spring Meadows on The Continent, marked by an Expedition Flag at its entrance. Players can reach it during Act 1, but the Chromatic Abbest inside is much stronger than the nearby regular enemies.

Defeating it awards the First Offensive Pictos. Its Lumina costs 5 points and increases both the first hit dealt and the first hit received by 50%.

What First Offensive Does

First Offensive does exactly one thing: your first hit in any fight deals 50% more damage. That is the entire effect. The symmetrical drawback — the first hit you receive also deals 50% more damage — is real, but manageable with the right setup.

The 5-LP cost leaves room for other Luminas, but the incoming-hit penalty means it still needs a defensive plan.

Finding the Abbest Cave

Look for Abbest Cave north-northwest of Spring Meadows on The Continent. Activate the Expedition Flag at the entrance before testing the encounter.

The Chromatic Abbest is available early but is much stronger than the nearby regular enemies. It resists Physical damage and is weak to Dark and Light. Activate the flag, test the encounter, and return after upgrading if its attacks remove most of a character's health or your damage barely moves the fight.

Verified Reward

Defeating the Chromatic Abbest awards First Offensive at level 10 and five Colours of Lumina.

Beating the Chromatic Abbest

The Chromatic Abbest is weak to Dark and Light damage and resists Physical attacks. Bring either vulnerable element, keep healing or revival available, and avoid relying on a Physical-only damage plan.

Watch a full attack sequence before committing to a counter. If the parry rhythm is not consistent yet, dodge first and preserve healing or revival options.

Tip

The glowing orb in the Chromatic Abbest's forehead is a weak point accessible with Free Aim shots. Target it when the boss gives you a safe opening.

Building Around the 50% Opening Strike

Shields or other defensive effects can reduce the risk of the first incoming hit, while Speed can help the equipped character use the offensive bonus earlier.

Use the bonus on a strong opening skill and pair it with a defensive plan for the first incoming hit. The low 5-LP cost leaves room to adapt the rest of the build instead of forcing one specific endgame combination.

Why This Is Worth Your Time

First Offensive stands out because Abbest Cave is accessible during Act 1 and does not require an Esquie traversal ability. The limiting factor is whether your current party can handle the Chromatic Abbest.

If a character has a strong opening action and a way to survive the first incoming hit, First Offensive is a practical 5-LP option rather than an automatic choice for every build.

The Expedition Flag at the Abbest Cave entrance is still there. It was there when you walked past it the first time. It will be there when you go back.
