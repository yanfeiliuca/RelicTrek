Manticore School Gear: Verified Blood and Wine Diagram Route and Set Bonuses

There's something quietly poetic about how you find the Manticore School gear. No lengthy quest chain, no master witcher to track down for each upgrade tier — just six diagrams scattered across the sun-drenched fields and forgotten ruins of Toussaint, waiting for a witcher who knows where to look. For longtime franchise fans, the moment you first equip the full set carries extra weight: this is the armor Geralt wore in the original Witcher game, a visual anchor to the version of him that most of the world hadn't met yet.

But aesthetics aside, the Manticore set earns its place on a practical level too. It supports alchemy and bomb play without requiring any lower-tier gear first. One correction matters: Manticore does not extend decoction duration as a set bonus. Its verified 3-piece bonus improves bombs, and its verified 6-piece bonus gives every alchemy item one extra charge.

What Makes Manticore Different

Every other witcher school set — Feline, Griffin, Bear, Wolf — follows the same progression: you start at basic, work through Enhanced, Superior, Mastercrafted, and finally Grandmaster. It's a satisfying grind, but it's a grind. The Manticore set doesn't work that way. It exists only at Grandmaster level. You find the diagrams, you gather the materials, and you craft the full endgame set in a single step. No prerequisites, no earlier tiers.

This makes it unexpectedly accessible. A player who arrives in Toussaint at level 40 and spends a focused afternoon tracking down all six diagrams can have a complete Grandmaster witcher set by nightfall. The other schools demand you've done the homework. Manticore just asks that you explore.

The set's real power sits in its bonuses. Three pieces give your bombs a meaningful upgrade: critical hit chance and critical hit damage start applying to them, and throws lose their delay. Six pieces unlock the alchemy utility effect: every alchemy item gets one extra charge. Individual armor pieces can also add maximum toxicity, but that is an item stat, not the 6-piece set effect.

Why Charges and Bombs Actually Matter

Decoctions remain the backbone of high-level alchemy play in The Witcher 3, but the Manticore set helps them indirectly rather than by extending their timers. The armor-piece toxicity stats can support a higher toxicity ceiling, while alchemy skills such as Acquired Tolerance and Heightened Tolerance determine how comfortably you can maintain multiple effects.

The 6-piece bonus matters because one extra charge on each alchemy item changes long routes and repeated encounters. You get more use from potions, bombs, and other consumables before resting. The 3-piece bonus also gives bomb builds a concrete combat payoff, since bombs can benefit from critical hit chance and critical hit damage and can be thrown without delay.

So the correct pitch is narrower but still useful: Manticore is not a magic decoction-duration set. It is a no-prior-tier Grandmaster set for players who want extra alchemy charges, useful armor-piece toxicity stats, and stronger bomb handling.

Gathering the Diagrams: Five Stops Across Toussaint

The verified Manticore hunt follows Merten's trail through five Toussaint locations. Start at Bastoy Prison Ruins, south of Fort Astre Ruins. The steel sword diagram is hidden in a brick in the wall near the entrance of the standing tower in the northeast part of the ruins.

Next, go to the cave southeast of Gélenser Farm and southwest of Termes Palace Ruins. Search the prayer notes on the far wall until you find Merten's prayer; reading it gives the armor diagram and the next lead. From there, head to Lebioda's temple near the Prophet Lebioda statue, use Aard on the far wall to open the hidden room, defeat the Great Beggar's Ghost, and loot the small container on the table near the stairs. That one container holds both the trousers and boots diagrams.

The silver sword is in Murky Delve, south of the Palace Gardens and northwest of Seidhe Llygad Amphitheater. Swim in, continue through the hallucination sequence, and loot the container near the praying figure in the hidden chapel. The final gauntlets diagram is underwater at the Lake of Cleansing northeast of Basane Farm, in the red-marked container just off the pier.

Practical Tip

If an older guide sends you to Dulcinea Estate, Blessure Cave, Pereplut, an abandoned Beauclair laboratory, an old hunting lodge, or Sansretour Valley for Manticore diagrams, treat that route as stale. The current verified route is the five-location Merten trail above.

Who Should Actually Use This Set

The Manticore set's value is proportional to how often you use alchemy items and bombs. If you're running a pure sign build or a fast attack Feline setup, the set's bonuses may not move the needle much. But if your Geralt rests around potion, bomb, oil, and decoction planning, the extra alchemy charge from the full set and the toxicity stats on armor pieces are useful without forcing you through a lower-tier gear grind.

There's also a less optimized but equally valid use case: the bomb specialist. The 3-piece bonus (critical hits applying to bombs, no throw delay) makes a bomb-heavy playstyle significantly more responsive. Combine it with Pyromaniac and Efficiency for a build that throws bombs faster and harder than the game's combat system usually allows.

Note

Manticore's set bonuses are easy to misquote because armor-piece stats and set effects are often discussed together. Keep them separate: toxicity is on the armor pieces; the 6-piece set bonus is alchemy item charges +1.

A Set Worth Its Route

Blood and Wine's Toussaint is one of the most beautiful regions CD Projekt RED ever made, and the Manticore route gives the set a compact story of its own through Merten's trail. The five-stop hunt takes roughly one to two hours at a measured pace. The reward is a Grandmaster set that requires no prior grind, looks like Geralt's original armor, and gives bomb and alchemy players a real endgame tool. That's a solid afternoon's work.
