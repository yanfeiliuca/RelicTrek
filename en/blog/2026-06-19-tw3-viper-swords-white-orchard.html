The Secret Swords That Change Your First Hour of The Witcher 3

White Orchard is designed to feel small. You arrive as a level-1 Geralt chasing a trail of burnt grass, fight a handful of drowners, and learn the mechanics. The game gently nudges you toward the main road and the wider world beyond. Most players spend maybe an hour here before moving on.

Hidden in that opening zone are two low-level Viper School sword diagrams. They belong to a treasure hunt with documents that connect the two sites, so the useful lesson is the route—not an unverifiable claim that nearly everyone misses them.

They're called the Viper School swords, and if you know where to look, you can walk out of White Orchard significantly more dangerous than you walked in.

Why the Viper Swords Are Worth Your Time

Both swords have poison chance and kill-experience bonuses. The level-1 silver sword also adds Aard intensity and monster experience, while the level-2 steel sword adds experience from humans and nonhumans.

Hearts of Stone later adds Viper armor and venomous swords, but those are separate expansion acquisitions. The White Orchard diagrams are not prerequisites for the DLC set.

How long either sword remains useful depends on your difficulty, drops, and quest rewards. Compare the crafted result with your current weapon instead of following a fixed replacement level.

Where to Find Them

The silver sword diagram is below the chapel at White Orchard cemetery. Deal with the wraith, use Aard to open the entrance, descend into the basement, and loot the skeleton beside Kolgrim's letter.

The steel sword diagram is not in a mill cellar. Go west of Ransacked Village to the Amavet Fortress Ruins, climb onto the plateau, defeat the deserters, and loot the chest inside the ruined watchtower. The same chest contains an interrogation report that points toward the cemetery.

Crafting Note

Both diagrams require an Amateur blacksmith. The silver sword uses two silver ingots, leather straps, emerald dust, and venom extract; the steel sword uses an iron ingot, leather straps, emerald dust, and venom extract.

What the Game Doesn't Tell You

This is a recorded treasure hunt rather than an entirely untracked secret. Reading Kolgrim's letter or the interrogation report points Geralt toward the second site.

The game is, in a sense, quietly testing whether you'll Aard a door that looks locked. The answer should always be yes.

Aard Tip

Aard sign is the key to more than just this crypt. Throughout The Witcher 3, many doors, walls, and structures that appear inaccessible can be forced open with Aard. If something looks locked and there's no obvious key, try the sign first.

When to Craft and How Long They Last

The treasure hunt itself lists a suggested level of 6, while the crafted swords require levels 1 and 2. You can collect the diagrams earlier if you can handle the wraith and deserters.

Neither sword scales automatically. Replace it when another weapon gives better damage or more useful bonuses for your build; there is no universal ten-hour or level-10 cutoff.

A Note for New Players

If this is your first time in White Orchard, the cemetery and fortress are worth visiting because they combine an early crafting route with a small Viper School story.

Collect both documents and diagrams, then decide whether the material cost makes sense for your current loadout before leaving White Orchard.
