Turn Around at Bitter Lake: Unlocking Zodiac Village in Black Myth: Wukong

Ask a group of Black Myth: Wukong players when the game finally "clicked" for them, and a surprising number will name a quiet coastal path in Chapter 3 rather than any boss. That path leads to Zodiac Village — a hidden hub the game never advertises, where armor upgrades, a full gourd service, seed planting and one of the most satisfying optional duels in the game all live under one roof. With the game still drawing new players and returning NG+ runs, it is worth knowing exactly how to get there before you burn hours wondering why your armor never improves.

Why a Village Matters More Than a Boss

Keeper's Shrines handle a lot, but they do not upgrade armor. That service sits with Yin Tiger, the blacksmith of Zodiac Village, and the only way in is the Chen Loong questline. If you have been carrying the same defensive numbers since Chapter 2, this is almost certainly why.

Once unlocked, the village becomes a permanent fast-travel destination with its own category in the travel menu. You can warp in from any shrine and back out again, which turns it into a natural rest stop between chapters: restock, upgrade, plant, and return to the trail without breaking momentum.

Following the Dragon: Chen Loong's Questline

The route has four beats. Start by warping to the North Shore of Bitter Lake shrine in Chapter 3. Here is the detail that trips most people up: Chen Loong is behind you. Turn around from the shrine and follow the coastal path to its end, where an ancient dragon waits in the water.

Defeat him, then keep talking until he asks for medicine from Xu Dog. Fighting him is far kinder if you respect two tells. His breath attack only covers the area in front of him, so slipping behind him during the wind-up is your safest window. His sweeping water attacks are telegraphed by a low dip of the head — dodge sideways, not backward, or the sweep will still catch you.

Explorer's Tip

If Xu Dog is missing from the Crouching Tiger Temple Cellar in Chapter 2, you have skipped his earlier Sandgate Village encounter. Advance that first, and the medicine step will open up.

At the Crouching Tiger Temple Cellar, Xu Dog hands over the Special-made Bone-Strengthening Pellet — a quest item, not something you buy. Carry it back to Bitter Lake, where Chen Loong now stands near a small building on the shore. Give him the pellet and he rewards you with the Ruyi Scroll. Interact with it, and you are inside the village.

Who Lives in Zodiac Village

Four familiar faces gather here, and the order you visit them matters a little. Head left from the entrance to find Yin Tiger first; his armor upgrades are available immediately and are the biggest power spike the village offers. Shen Monkey runs the full gourd upgrade tree, so bring any Luojia Fragrant Vines you have been hoarding. Xu Dog keeps his medicine services here and is the eventual destination for the Five Skandhas hand-in — a late-game reward that only pays out once you have all five.

Then there is Chen Loong himself, retired from dragon duty to tend a garden. Hand him seeds gathered from plants around the world, and he grows them into harvestable ingredients that mature over play time. It is easy to forget, so make a habit of checking in whenever you warp through — a small, steady income of materials for very little effort.

Worth Knowing

The extra Curio slot is not handed over in your first conversation with Yin Tiger. It is a separate reward that requires beating him in a duel first.

The Duel With Yin Tiger

After you have used his services, Yin Tiger will accept a challenge. It is optional, and losing costs you nothing — his blacksmith work stays available either way. Winning, however, grants the Ebon Flow transformation and unlocks an additional Curio slot through his post-fight dialogue. For build-focused players, that extra slot alone justifies the effort.

The fight itself is a test of patience. Yin Tiger is fast, strings together long attack chains, and likes to delay the final swing just long enough to punish an early dodge. Community advice converges on the same point: there is no magic level threshold or mandatory spell. Spend your first few attempts simply watching. Learn where his chains truly end, and only commit to a punish once you are certain the last hit has landed. Players who treat him as a rhythm puzzle rather than a damage race tend to win far sooner.

Why This Is Worth Your Time

Zodiac Village is the kind of secret that rewards curiosity — a game literally hiding its upgrade system behind the instinct to turn around. Unlocking it fixes the stalled-armor problem, centralises four essential services, quietly farms ingredients for you, and offers a duel that pays out in permanent build flexibility. If you are anywhere in Chapter 3, make the detour today. Your future self, facing the late-game bosses with better armor and one more Curio, will thank you.
