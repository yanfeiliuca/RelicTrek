Five Chapters, One Horse, One Transformation: The Story Behind Dark Thunder

Ma Tianba appears in five locations across the first five chapters of Black Myth: Wukong. His optional dialogue chain ultimately leads to the Dark Thunder Transformation, but the game does not provide a conventional quest log for tracking every encounter.

The reliable approach is simple: find him in each chapter, continue talking until his dialogue repeats, and close the Chapter 5 cart interaction before defeating the Keeper of the Flaming Mountains.

The Horse Who Keeps Appearing

Ma Tianba is a horse guai — a spirit who has found his own path toward something like enlightenment. In a game full of demons pursuing power, he is quietly doing something else. Every chapter, he turns up somewhere slightly unexpected: meditating in a cave, trapped behind a door, wrapped in a spider's cocoon. Each time, he has a few things to say — about the journey, about the world around him, about what he has seen. And each time, you need to hear all of it before you move on.

That last part is the key. A short conversation is not enough: continue interacting until his final line begins to repeat. Because the game does not clearly display progress for this chain, checking each encounter before moving deeper into the story is safer than relying on a later recovery window.

Quest Rule

Talk to Ma Tianba until he repeats himself. Every chapter. If he still has new lines, you haven't finished — keep pressing the interact button.

Why Chapter 3 Deserves an Extra Check

Ma Tianba's Chapter 3 position at New Thunderclap Temple is away from the direct route through the area. That makes this encounter easy to overlook even when the main chapter progression feels complete.

Before finishing a chapter, return to his location and confirm that his dialogue repeats. If an earlier encounter is still available, complete it as soon as possible. The clearly documented hard lock occurs in Chapter 5: defeating the Keeper of the Flaming Mountains before handling Ma Tianba's Furnace Valley cart and whip interaction can make the quest impossible to complete in that playthrough.

Chapter 5 Reminder

At Furnace Valley, locate Ma Tianba at the damaged cart and complete the available cart or whip interaction before defeating the Keeper of the Flaming Mountains. After the boss fight, return to the cart to finish the sequence and obtain Dark Thunder.

What Is Currently Verified About Dark Thunder

Dark Thunder changes the Destined One into a horse guai wielding a lightning whip. The available reference lists Thunder Damage immunity and attacks that can inflict Thunder Bane.

The currently documented move set is Lightning Lashes, Thunderbolt Sweep, Lightning Infusion, Thunder Flurry, and Thunder Dominion. Earlier claims on this page about a See Through counter, Lightning Flash, Focus gain, stagger, or extended invincibility were not supported by the checked source and have been removed pending direct in-game evidence.

Those verified traits make Dark Thunder a distinct lightning-focused option. Damage rankings, universal boss recommendations, and broader Shock-status immunity should not be inferred from the Thunder Damage immunity alone.

A Quest Worth Doing for Its Own Sake

What's worth noting about Ma Tianba is that his story is one of the more fully realized in a game that tends to tell its best stories through environmental detail and optional dialogue. You watch a character travel alongside you across the entire arc of the game — sometimes ahead of you, sometimes trapped, sometimes barely holding on — and the transformation you receive at the end feels like a proper conclusion rather than a reward dropped from a loot table.

The quest is also one of the clearer examples of an optional character story continuing across several chapters. Completing it preserves that narrative thread and adds Dark Thunder to the transformation collection without relying on unsupported combat rankings.
